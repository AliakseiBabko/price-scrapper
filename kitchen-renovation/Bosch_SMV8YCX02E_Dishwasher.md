## 🎯 Why It Was Selected

1. **Zeolite Drying (Perfect Plastic Drying)**:
   - **Reasoning**: Standard dishwashers dry via condensation, leaving plastic cups, containers, and baby dishes wet. **Zeolite drying** uses natural minerals that absorb moisture and release intense dry heat during the drying phase. This ensures that plastic children's cups and lunchboxes come out 100% dry and ready for the cupboard.
2. **Ultra-Quiet Operation (Kitchen-Living Room Setup)**:
   - **Reasoning**: If your new apartment has an open-plan kitchen and living room, dishwasher noise is a major issue. At **43 dB** (dropping to 41 dB in Night Mode), the Serie 8 is whisper-quiet. You can run it in the evening without disrupting family conversations or TV time.
3. **Daily Quality of Life**:
   - **Reasoning**: The dishwasher is the most frequently operated appliance in a family home. We opted to keep the premium Serie 8 model here because its adjustable racks, third-drawer cutlery tray, and high cleaning efficiency significantly reduce daily chores.

## ⚠️ Concerns and Technical Risks

* **Zeolite Chamber Complexity**:
   - **Detail**: The Zeolite minerals are stored in a specialized chamber in the base of the machine. If water or detergent leaks directly into this chamber (e.g. if the heating element seal fails), it requires a costly professional repair.
* **Sensitivity to Hard Water**:
   - **Detail**: Belarus/local municipal water can be hard. To prevent lime deposits from clogging the Zeolite system and heating elements, you must strictly monitor salt levels and use high-quality water softener salt.
* **Expensive Replacement Parts**:
   - **Detail**: As a Serie 8 machine, it features a complex control board and premium components. Out-of-warranty repairs are substantially more expensive than on Serie 2 or 4 models.
