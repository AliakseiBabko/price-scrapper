# Kitchen Waste Disposers (измельчители)

Whether to fit one, what it is actually for, and the under-sink conflict it creates that nothing else in this vault records.

**This page was created empty and is populated here from its first substantive source (2026-09-03, Round 6).**

## ⚠️⚠️ A Strong Endorsement, With the Scope Stated — It Is a SCRAP-CLEARING Device, Not a Waste-Processing One

**Александр Синчуков, on the disposer in his own kitchen one month into occupancy: button-operated, «вся грязь мы туда заталкиваем, это прямо вообще ИДЕАЛЬНО».**

**And he answers his own comment section on the standard objection, which is what makes this usable:**

**«Многие писали, что НЕЛЬЗЯ ЧИСТИТЬ КАРТОШКУ в него… КОНЕЧНО ЖЕ Я ЭТОГО НЕ ДЕЛАЮ. Я ЧИЩУ КАРТОШКУ В МУСОРНОЕ ВЕДРО, а здесь остаются какие-то МЕЛКИЕ ОСТАТКИ пищи, которые для измельчителя НЕ СТРАШНЫ [и не страшны] для вашей КАНАЛИЗАЦИИ.»**

- → **A disposer handles FINE SCRAPS left on plates and in the bowl. Peelings and bulk vegetable waste go to the bin.** Used within that scope he reports no drainage problems; used outside it, the objection stands.
- → **So the disposer does not replace the bin — which is what makes the conflict below matter.**

## ⚠️⚠️ The Conflict Nobody Mentions: the Disposer Eats the Bin's Volume — and There Is a Specific Fix

**«Мусорное ведро вот такого размера — это МАКСИМАЛЬНОЕ, что сюда влезает, ПОТОМУ ЧТО У МЕНЯ ЕСТЬ ИЗМЕЛЬЧИТЕЛЬ.»** The disposer body hangs directly below the waste outlet and occupies the centre of the under-sink cabinet — which is exactly where a full-size pull-out bin would go.

**⚠️⚠️ THE FIX HE SAW ELSEWHERE, flagged by him as «это не реклама, это просто на задуматься»: a sink whose WASTE OUTLET IS DELIBERATELY OFFSET TO A CORNER of the bowl, so the disposer sits in the corner of the cabinet too — «Это я считаю СУПЕР КРУТОЕ РЕШЕНИЕ, потому что тогда бы у меня ВЛЕЗЛО БОЛЬШОЕ ВЕДРО».**

- → **⚠️ Offsetting the waste outlet to a corner frees the middle of the cabinet for a full-size bin. This is decided at SINK SELECTION and cannot be changed afterwards — the outlet position is a property of the bowl.**
- → **The question to ask when specifying a sink alongside a disposer: where is the waste outlet, centre or corner?** Nothing else in this vault raises it, and the consequence is a permanently undersized bin.
- ⚠️ *The showroom brand is garbled in the caption track and is deliberately left unnamed rather than guessed.*

## ⚠️ The Adjacent Constraint — the Same Cabinet Holds Everything Else

In his own kitchen the under-sink cabinet also carries **the water filter, a leak sensor, a soap dispenser feed, and two hooks for the sponge and cloth.** → **The under-sink cabinet is the most contested volume in a kitchen, and a disposer is the largest single claimant on it.** See [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Storage_and_Hardware|Storage and Hardware]] and [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Leak_Protection_Systems|Leak Protection Systems]].
