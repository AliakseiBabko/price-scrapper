# 🍳 Appliance Preferences & Design System

This document captures your specific physical preferences, sizing details, color aesthetics, and appliance brand requirements for the kitchen and laundry sets.

---

## 🍳 1. Kitchen Appliance Preferences

### 🎨 Aesthetics & Color Matching
- **All-Black Glass Fronts**: Every visible kitchen appliance (oven, microwave, induction hob, and hood) must have a **fully black glass** face. There should be no visible stainless steel, chrome, or silver borders/trim when the devices are closed or installed.
- **Set Cohesion**: Set components should belong to matching brand ecosystems where possible (e.g., all-Bosch or all-Gorenje) to ensure uniform black glass tints and UI styling.

### 🍕 Cooktop (Hob)
- **Standard Round Coils Only**: Avoid paying a premium for rectangular flex/bridge induction zones, as the family does not fry in rectangular pans and Flex Zones can cause uneven heating for standard round cookware.
- **Round Pots/Pans Focus**: All daily cookware (pots, pans, cast iron, and thick-bottomed aluminum) is round.
- **Large Circular Burner**: A large circular burner (e.g., 28 cm) is highly desired to accommodate massive pans and occasional pilaf cooking.

### 🍿 Microwave & Oven Stack
- **Solo Microwave**: The family does not use microwave grill elements. Solo (microwaves-only) models are preferred if they provide a cleaner vertical stack aesthetic or better series matching with the oven.
- **Stacked Alignment**: When stacked in a vertical column, the microwave is positioned directly above the oven. In comparison tables, the upper device is bottom-aligned and the lower device is top-aligned to simulate their real-world stack.

### 🍽️ Dishwasher
- **Size**: Fully integrated 60 cm models only.
- **Floor Indicators**: Must feature a floor light indicator projection. Either a simple floor projection dot (**InfoLight**) or remaining cycle time in minutes (**TimeBeam**).

### 💨 Hood
- **Manual Control Preference**: Telescopic or built-in hoods should have mechanical button speed regulation (speed buttons/sliders) that can be operated manually. Do not select wireless automation (WiFi or Hob2Hood) as manual power adjustment is preferred.

### ❄️ Refrigerator Options
- **Built-in Side-by-Side Options (Liebherr)**:
  - **Large Option**: **Liebherr IXRFS 5125 Plus** (500 L, Height 177 cm, BioFresh/NoFrost, ~9,700–11,336 BYN).
  - **Compact Option**: **Liebherr IXRF 4555 Prime** (374 L, Height 139.5 cm, BioFresh/NoFrost, ~14,887–16,417 BYN).

## 🧺 2. Laundry Appliance Preferences

### 🧼 Washer & Dryer
- **Drum Capacity**: Large capacities (10–13 kg washing, 9–10 kg drying) are required to wash double bedding sets and high volumes of children's clothes in single runs.
- **Self-Cleaning Condenser**: Dryer must feature a self-cleaning heat exchanger to handle high lint loads automatically.
- **Aesthetic Matching**: Dryer and washing machine must belong to the same product generation and share identical control panel interfaces and body styling.
- **Stacking Configuration**: Dryers are always stacked on top of washing machines. The stack must list the compatible stacking kit (e.g., the **LG KSTK1** kit for LG pairs).

---

## 📊 3. Appliance Sets & Sorting Rules
- **Sequential Set Naming**: Sets must be named sequentially from left to right: `Set 1`, `Set 2`, `Set 3`, `Set 4`.
- **Budget Sort Order**: Sets must be sorted from left to right in **strictly descending order of their total budget**:
  - **Set 1 (Bosch Touch)**: Premium set (~13,883 BYN)
  - **Set 2 (Electrolux Touch)**: All-touch interface (~9,048 BYN)
  - **Set 3 (Bosch Manual)**: Balanced dials/LCD interface (~8,515 BYN)
  - **Set 4 (Gorenje Manual)**: Economy brand cohesion (~7,202 BYN)
- **Currency Format**: Always display prices and budgets in **BYN** (never use "rubles" or "р.").
