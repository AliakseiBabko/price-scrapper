### Fridge Types

- **Freestanding** — typically 60cm wide, the most popular type, fits any interior style,
  widest color/style range.
- **Built-in** — integrates into kitchen cabinetry behind a matching furniture facade; chosen
  for aesthetic/style unity. Costs more, involves real compromises, and is far more demanding
  of correct installation quality and ventilation than a freestanding unit.
- **⚠️ Side-by-side, with a real sizing "trick" flagged**: looks narrower on paper (~90cm)
  than two separate 60cm units (120cm combined), but manufacturers require a 10-15cm
  clearance gap on *each* side for the doors to open properly — once both clearances are
  added, total footprint is ≈120cm, identical to two standard 60cm units. Mainly chosen by
  buyers with large apartments/houses and no space pressure, largely for styling and a
  built-in ice-generator tray; two independent single-door fridges are called more practical/
  efficient in actual use.
- **Dimension ranges**: freestanding — height 83-216cm, width 45-91cm; built-in — height
  82-212cm, width 54-70cm; side-by-side — height 170-193cm, width 78-122cm (before adding
  door-swing clearance).
- **Freezer placement flexibility**: the freezer compartment can be positioned above, below,
  or omitted entirely (all one continuous fridge interior in that case), or paired as a
  same-family freezer-only column alongside a fridge-only column — yielding meaningfully more
  combined volume than an equivalent side-by-side unit.

### Cooling Systems

- **⚠️ A real budget-tier trap**: drip/capillary cooling (simplest, cheapest) is actually the
  *better* choice for the fridge compartment specifically — keeps interior humidity high
  (~70%), benefiting herbs, raw meat/sausage, etc.; downside is uneven top-to-bottom/
  front-to-back cooling. **No Frost** (fan-circulated air) isn't essential for the fridge side
  but is a hard requirement for the freezer side (no periodic ice-buildup defrosting needed,
  more even cooling). **Full No Frost / "multi airflow"** (same concept, different marketing
  names per brand) applies No Frost to both compartments — **a real advantage only in
  expensive/premium models, and a real negative in budget models**: a budget Full No Frost
  fridge compartment pulls humid air out via the fan, dropping humidity below ~30% (harmful
  for humidity-sensitive foods), because budget models don't actively re-humidify. **Explicit
  recommendation: don't buy an economy-tier model advertised as "Full [No Frost]" — prefer
  plain drip-cooled fridge + No-Frost-only freezer.**
- **⚠️ Humidity-controlled crisper-drawer mechanism, the cause behind the trap above**:
  fridges typically have a "zero-zone" drawer (near-0°C) and a "high-humidity" drawer. With
  plain drip cooling and no air circulation, the lower chamber is naturally more humid than
  the top (basic physics, no active mechanism needed). Add a fan for **budget** Full No Frost
  and that natural humidity gradient is destroyed with no compensating humidification — the
  drawer is still labeled "high-humidity" but doesn't function as one. **Expensive** Full No
  Frost models actively pump humidified air into these drawers, so the labeled function
  genuinely works. Confirms: only buy Full No Frost at the premium price point.

### Compressor

- **⚠️ Inverter payback period, specific to Russian electricity pricing**: compressor type is
  the single most important factor for service life, noise, and time-to-first-repair. Inverter
  compressors are quieter, start up more smoothly, and are more energy-efficient — but the
  price premium (from ≈10,000 RUB/≈$130 and up) takes roughly **5-7 years** to pay back via
  electricity savings in Russia's low-electricity-price context (unlike Europe's much higher
  per-kWh rates, where the same gain pays back faster). **Compressor quality depends on the
  compressor manufacturer, not the fridge brand** — two units of the same model side-by-side
  in a store can have different compressor suppliers, with no way for a buyer to control for
  this at purchase time.
- **⚠️ Manufacturer-recommended annual power-cycling rule**: virtually every manufacturer
  states you should unplug the fridge for at least ~2 hours once a year — mechanism unclear
  even to the source, but confirmed as a universal manufacturer recommendation, not folklore.
  Practical tip: combine this with the fridge's periodic cleaning/washing.
- **⚠️ Energy-efficiency class is explicitly irrelevant in Russia**: unlike markets with very
  high electricity prices (the source cites $600-$1,000/month bills as the context where it
  matters), Russia's electricity pricing makes chasing an energy-class rating pointless for
  any household appliance — recommends ignoring this spec entirely in this market.

### Interior, Controls, and a Design-Philosophy Note

- **Interior ergonomics** — shelf count/adjustability, drawer count, telescopic drawer rails —
  affect comfort, not build quality. **A stated cultural contrast**: Chinese-made models invest
  disproportionately in interior polish/lighting/flourishes (a "wow" effect); European
  manufacturers focus primarily on core build quality, sometimes at the expense of these
  interior extras — framed as a genuine design-philosophy difference, not a quality hierarchy.
- **Controls**: mechanical, electronic/touch, hybrid, or Wi-Fi/app-connected (including a named
  Zigbee-protocol example). Wi-Fi/app control called "completely useless" by the source's own
  opinion — recommends mechanical or touch as the baseline: "simpler is more reliable."

## ⚠️⚠️ Three Fridge Findings From One Practitioner in Five Days — Including Both Sides of the Built-In Trade-Off (Александр Синчуков, 2021-01-27 and 2021-02-02)

### ⚠️⚠️ Side-by-side fridges are a myth — a strong contrarian claim

**«Меня постоянно заказчики просят поставить им огромный холодильник САЙД-БАЙ-САЙД… поверьте мне, это САМОЕ ГЛУБОКОЕ ЗАБЛУЖДЕНИЕ — в этих холодильниках НИЧЕГО НЕ ВЛЕЗАЕТ… посмотреть вживую, они ОЧЕНЬ МАЛЕНЬКИЕ и ОЧЕНЬ КОМПАКТНЫЕ, и туда МАЛО ЧТО ВЛЕЗАЕТ. Кроме того они ещё и стоят СИЛЬНО ДОРОЖЕ.»**

His alternative: **TWO SEPARATE COLUMNS — one refrigeration module and one freezer module — «вот тогда у вас точно всё влезет».** ⚠️ *The module width is ASR-garbled and is not resolved by guessing; 60 cm is the plausible reading.*

- → **Route as a Perspectives counter-claim against this page's buying-guide content, with the dimension flagged. It is checkable in any showroom, which is exactly what he recommends: «посмотреть вживую».**

### ⚠️⚠️ Built-in versus freestanding — both sides from one practitioner within a week, plus his own resolution

- **2021-01-27, the CAPACITY side: «встроенный холодильник ЗНАЧИТЕЛЬНО МЕНЬШЕ отдельностоящего… встройка — это другая эстетика, но она всё равно ЗНАЧИТЕЛЬНО МЕНЬШЕ. ЭТО ФАКТ.»** (with the American-sitcom chrome fridge as the cultural contrast). [source: [[_Sources/YT_wvlr2aGDMCc_nsdsgn_five_kitchen_mistakes|YT_wvlr2aGDMCc]]]
- **⚠️⚠️ 2021-02-02, the CLEANING side, and he decides AGAINST capacity: «В СЛЕДУЮЩЕЙ моей квартире НЕ БУДЕТ отдельностоящего холодильника, потому что вот это всё В ГРЯЗИ — когда я жарю СТЕЙК, здесь ЛЕТИТ ЖИР, это очень тяжело отмывается. Могу сказать, что это ХУЖЕ ОТМЫВАЕТСЯ, ДАЖЕ ЧЕМ ЧЁРНОЕ СТЕКЛО. Также есть вот такие ЩЕЛИ, куда что-то может УПАСТЬ, ЗАВАЛИТЬСЯ, и чтобы это отмыть, надо ХОЛОДИЛЬНИК ВЫНИМАТЬ — я конечно НЕ БУДУ это делать каждый раз. Я ЛУЧШЕ ПОСТАВЛЮ ВСТРОЕННЫЙ, в который влезет МЕНЬШЕ ПРОДУКТОВ — это важный момент — но при этом это будет ОЧЕНЬ ПРОСТО В УБОРКЕ. Потому что этот холодильник — огромный ПЫЛЕСБОРНИК, особенно СВЕРХУ… рано или поздно мне придётся туда залезть и всё вычищать, и я С ОТВРАЩЕНИЕМ ЖДУ ЭТОГО МОМЕНТА.»**
  - → **⚠️⚠️ A DATED TRADE-OFF, KNOWINGLY DECIDED: LESS CAPACITY IN EXCHANGE FOR CLEANABILITY — and he had stated the capacity cost himself five days earlier. The vault gets both sides and his own resolution.**
  - → **⚠️ The top of a freestanding fridge is a GREASE-AND-DUST LEDGE — the same argument that drives building kitchens to the ceiling, applied to an appliance. And the calibration is memorable: fridge-side grease is HARDER to clean than black gloss glass.**

### ⚠️⚠️ Ventilation for a built-in fridge — a one-to-two-year service-life penalty, cheap to prevent

**«ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬНО делайте В ЦОКОЛЕ вентиляцию, также НАД холодильником РЕШЁТОЧКУ для вентиляции — потому что если холодильник будет сильно ЗАЖАТ в нише снизу и сверху, там НЕ БУДЕТ ЦИРКУЛЯЦИИ ВОЗДУХА, и он ПРОСЛУЖИТ НА ГОД, НА ДВА МЕНЬШЕ. Это ОЧЕНЬ ЛЕГКО УСТРАНЯЕТСЯ, но сэкономит вам очень много денег и времени.»**

- → **A plinth vent plus a top grille. Invisible once built, and the penalty is a named fraction of the appliance's life — which makes it a checkable item on a furniture drawing.**
