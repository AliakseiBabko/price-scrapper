## 🎯 Why It Was Selected

1. **Bespoke Satin Greige Finish**:
   - **Reasoning**: Outstanding matte aesthetic that blends seamlessly into modern interiors if placed in a master bathroom or visible hallway.
2. **AI Wash & i-DOS Auto-Dosing**:
   - **Reasoning**: The machine automatically senses the weight and fabric type, then dispenses the exact required amount of water and detergent. Saves detergent and protects fabrics from over-washing.
3. **SpaceMax Capacity (11 kg)**:
   - **Reasoning**: Utilizes thin-wall technology to provide a larger 11 kg drum capacity within standard external depth dimensions.
4. **Ecobubble™ Technology**:
   - **Reasoning**: Mixes detergent with air and water to create active bubbles before the cycle starts, enabling effective cleaning even at low temperatures (15°C).

## ⚠️ Concerns and Technical Risks

* **Plasticky Outer Accents**:
   - **Detail**: The control knob and screen panel are finished in plastic, which feels slightly less premium than Bosch's glass/metal dials.
* **Smart Home Dependency**:
   - **Detail**: To unlock full optimization and custom cycle links with the dryer, it requires SmartThings app integration and a stable Wi-Fi connection.
