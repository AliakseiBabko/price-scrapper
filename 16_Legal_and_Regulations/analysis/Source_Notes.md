## ⚠️ Rejected and quarantined material

**An English-language AI summary with web citations was supplied alongside the PDF on 2026-09-08 and was NOT admitted as a source.** It was used only as an index of claims to verify. Checked against the primary text: **three claims confirmed, two confirmed with a material correction, two wrong, seven not addressed by № 164 at all.** The full claim-by-claim verdict is in the [[_Sources/DOC_postanovlenie_164_2026_pereustroystvo_pereplanirovka|source note]].

**Its two outright errors are worth naming here**, because both circulate widely:

- **«№ 384 is the parent regulation updated by № 164»** — no: **№ 384 is repealed.**
- **«You must obtain технические условия from the power utility for high-wattage appliances»** — no: **§6 requires ТУ only for gas and central heating.** The real constraint is §4's prohibition on exceeding the premises' design calculated loads.

**Claims from that summary that remain UNVERIFIED and are therefore not in this folder**: СН 2.02.05-2020 fire ratings; **СН 3.02.01-2020 wet-zone placement**; постановление № 399 and the 07:00–23:00 / night-silence regime; Закон № 271-З and the waste segregation/carrier/self-haul procedure; КоАП penalty amounts; the sound-insulation court remedy; and the «госстройэкспертиza no longer required» claim.
