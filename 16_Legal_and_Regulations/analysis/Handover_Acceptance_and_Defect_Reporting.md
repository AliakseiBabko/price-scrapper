# Handover, Acceptance Inspection & Defect Reporting — Belarus

## Developer Defect-Remediation Practice (first qualifying source, added 2026-08-24)

Pavel Sidorik (individual practitioner, own new-build apartment renovation) states directly and unambiguously that this apartment/developer relationship is in Belarus, in the specific context of defect remediation: *"В Беларуси это не прокатит... В Беларуси застройщик скажет: я буду исправлять сам и соответственно растянет на длительное время. Либо вы просто это исправляете за свой счет."* — clearing this page's own level-1 bar (a first-hand Belarus practitioner/homeowner account naming Belarus directly).

- **A Belarusian developer will not pay a cash penalty or apartment-price discount in lieu of fixing a flagged defect** — contrasted by the speaker against a practice he describes as sometimes available in Russia. In Belarus the developer's only two postures observed: fix it themselves (typically drawn out over a long time), or leave the buyer to fix it at their own expense.
- **Realistic defect-remediation timeline, real example**: a window/sash replacement took roughly 1.5 months from complaint to resolution; the speaker's broader stated experience is that developers tend to stretch minor-defect fixes over multiple months even without a fixed timeline forcing it.
- **A screed crack is not a screed-replacement claim** — at most the developer will patch/fill the crack itself, not replace the screed.
- **An unqualified/uncredentialed buyer's own acceptance inspection carries the same legal standing as one performed by a credentialed specialist** — "a defect exists or it doesn't," independent of who identifies it or what training they have.
- **Deep glass-pane chips/scratches are grounds for full sash/glazing-unit replacement** — confirmed with a real outcome: two of this apartment's largest glazing units were replaced by the developer after being flagged at acceptance.

## Scope

The process of taking possession of a new-build apartment from the developer in Belarus:

- What to physically check during the handover walkthrough — structural issues, plumbing/electrical rough-in, window/door seals, floor/wall/ceiling finish defects (relevant to this project's own `WhiteBox`/rough-finish delivery condition — see `nd5WfYyjelg`/`suY0GGTOG9E` in the Kruglov/Ontario intake once processed for general technique, though those are not Belarus-specific and won't qualify for *this* page).
- How to formally document and report deficiencies — the acceptance act (`акт приёма-передачи`) and its legal weight, what happens if you sign it with or without noted defects, and what recourse exists if the developer disputes a defect.
- Developer warranty periods and claim procedure for defects discovered after handover.
- Realistic timelines for defect remediation before/after taking possession.
