# Renovation Permits & Approvals — Belarus/Minsk

**Evidence bar for this page (and this whole folder): level 1 only.** A source qualifies only if it names Minsk/Belarus directly and specifically in its own content — not via channel branding, tags, or title alone (level 2), which does not qualify.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **⚠️⚠️ 2026-09-08 — this page now rests on primary law, not only on practitioner accounts.**
> **[Постановление Совета Министров РБ от 03.04.2026 № 164](../../_Sources/DOC_postanovlenie_164_2026_pereustroystvo_pereplanirovka.md)** was read in full and is the governing act. **It repeals постановление № 384 of 2013 outright** — so every older guide, article or video citing «постановление 384» is out of date, and any claim traced to it needs re-checking against № 164.
> **It is a Council-of-Ministers постановление under Жилищный кодекс ст. 5, not a presidential decree.** Secondary English summaries calling it "Decree No. 164" are wrong in kind.
> **Commencement**: published 07.04.2026; the two Положения and the repeals take effect **three months later, in early July 2026** (7 or 8 July depending on the count — unresolved, and nothing here turns on it).

## ⚠️⚠️ §3 — what legally counts as переустройство/перепланировка, and what does not

**The list is closed.** Works that ARE переустройство and/or перепланировка:

- **замена или перенос систем газоснабжения, центрального отопления**;
- **устройство гидро-, паро-, звукоизоляции**;
- **изменение площади, количества жилых комнат и подсобных помещений в квартире** за счёт разборки существующих и (или) устройства новых **перегородок**;
- изменение площади и количества помещений **в изолированных нежилых помещениях** by the same means;
- **устройство, увеличение проёмов в ненесущих стенах и перегородках**.

> **«Иные работы не являются работами по переустройству и (или) перепланировке.»** (§3, final sentence)

**→ The most useful line in the act.** Finishes, tiling, a screed poured over an existing screed, replacing sanitaryware in place, sockets, lighting, furniture — **none of it is перепланировка, and none of it needs согласование under this Положение.**

**⚠️ Two cautions on that, and they matter more than the exemption:**

1. **It exempts the work from the permit, not from the rules.** §4's prohibitions stay live regardless, and fire and sanitary requirements are incorporated by reference.
2. **⚠️ «Устройство гидро-, паро-, звукоизоляции» IS on the list.** On the face of the text, **laying acoustic underlay or applying waterproofing is a work requiring согласование** — which is counter-intuitive, since most people would file both under "finishing". Worth confirming with the исполком before assuming a wet-room membrane needs a permit.

**Also outside the definition** (п.2 of the постановление, not the Положение): **glazing balconies and loggias**, and **replacing window and door infills** — see [[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Facade_AC_and_Glazing_Approvals|Facade, AC & Glazing Approvals]], which carries their own separate approval regime.

## §4 — the prohibitions

Переустройство/перепланировка is **запрещено** where it:

- violates **строительные, противопожарные, санитарно-эпидемиологические** requirements, or legislation on culture and on architectural/urban-planning/construction activity;
- **reduces the bearing capacity** of foundation soils, foundations, columns, walls, beams, floor slabs, supports or roofs — **or impairs the hydro-, vapour-, thermal- or sound-insulation, or the bio- and fire-resistance, of load-bearing and enclosing structures**;
- **disrupts the operating regime of engineering systems**, or degrades the working parameters of the building's or individual premises' engineering equipment;
- affects **ventilation and smoke shafts and channels**;
- converts **balconies and loggias into heated premises**;
- creates living rooms **without natural light**, or places a **partition into a window opening**;
- adds **central-heating, hot-water or electrical equipment that would exceed the premises' design calculated engineering loads**;
- changes the **architectural or colour solution of the facade**;
- reduces the operational suitability of the building's structures;
- applies to a house declared unfit for habitation and beyond restoration.

### ⚠️ Two of these bear directly on this project

- **The лоджия cannot become a heated room.** Not a design preference — a prohibition. See [[00_Master/project_decisions|Project Decisions]] and [[10_Balcony/Balcony_Guide|Balcony]].
- **⚠️ The real constraint on electric underfloor heating and heavy appliances is the DESIGN LOAD, not a permit.** A widely repeated claim says you must obtain технические условия from the power utility for high-wattage equipment. **№ 164 does not say that** — §6 requires ТУ only for gas and central heating. What it does impose is a flat prohibition on **exceeding the calculated engineering load for the premises**, which is a different obligation with a different remedy: you check the design figure, you do not apply for a document.


> [!IMPORTANT]
> **✅⚠️ THIS BUILDING HAS NO NATURAL GAS — electric stoves only (owner, 2026-09-08). That collapses the §6 track to a single trigger.**
>
> §6 catches **gas supply OR central heating**. The gas limb can never apply here, so **the only thing that can put this project into the проект + технические условия track is touching CENTRAL HEATING — moving a radiator.** Everything else this project plans (partition changes, openings in non-load-bearing walls, the кухня-into-living merge, plumbing within the wet block) sits in the §7 **согласование-only** track.
>
> **→ So "do not move a radiator" is no longer one caution among several. It is the single line between a designer's fee and none.** ⚠️ The unresolved §102 tension below is about exactly this boundary and is not settled by the gas answer.
>
> **⚠️ And the same fact makes the design-load constraint above MORE binding, not less.** With no gas, the **entire cooking load** sits on the electrical figure — hob and oven — alongside any electric underfloor heating and the appliance set. **Obtain the premises' calculated engineering load before committing to hob + oven + underfloor heating together.** There is no permit to apply for and no document to be refused, which is precisely why this one gets skipped: the failure shows up as tripping breakers after the money is spent.
## ⚠️⚠️ §5–7 — two approval tracks, and which one this project is on

| Works | What is required | Approving body |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Replacing or relocating gas supply or central heating** (§6) | **согласование + технические условия + a designed проект** | исполком; проект then agreed with the **территориальное подразделение архитектуры и градостроительства** |
| **Insulation; partition changes in a flat; openings in non-load-bearing walls** (§7) | **согласование (разрешение) — NO проект** | **district/city исполком**, or the local administration of a district within a city (§5) |

> **→ For this flat — external walls non-load-bearing, partitions already built by the developer — partition changes and new openings need исполком согласование but NOT a designed project.**
>
> **⚠️ And the trip-wire: moving a radiator is «перенос систем центрального отопления».** It flips the whole job into the проект track, with технические условия and a paid designer. **The heating layout is therefore a permitting decision, not just an engineering one** — see [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Heating_Placement_Rules|Heating Placement Rules]].

## §8–12 — the procedure, and what the permit itself must say

- **Citizens** file an application plus the documents listed in **подпункт 1.1.21 пункта 1.1** of the schedule of administrative procedures approved by **Указ Президента № 200 of 26 April 2010.** ⚠️ **That list is by reference and is not reproduced in № 164** — see Open Questions.
- **The list given for legal entities and IP** is a useful proxy for what will be wanted: **технический паспорт** (for an owner) **only if issued before 1 January 2023**; **план-схема или перечень (описание) работ**; the owner's written consent if the premises are leased or held in free use; **written consent of all co-owners** in shared ownership; consent of the организация застройщиков where the applicant is a member but not the owner.
- **§9 — an inspection, with an access obligation.** At a time agreed with them, the initiator must admit representatives of the housing-operating organisation, **the energy and gas supply organisations**, and the исполком. An **акт технического осмотра** is drawn up **in free form**, recording whether the existing layout matches the technical passport and the condition of structures and engineering equipment; signed by the initiator and the authorised official, **approved by the исполком representative**.
- **§10 — a decision within one month** of the application. **⚠️ And the согласование must itself state:** the initiator; the types of works; whether a проект is needed; **the dates of completion and of acceptance**; **the conditions for storing building materials and the procedure for removing their waste**; plus any other requirements from technical regulations.
  > **→ Your own permit sets your waste rules and your acceptance date.** General waste legislation is the backdrop; the operative rule for a specific renovation is the line written into its разрешение. See [[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Works_Conduct_and_Waste|Works Conduct & Waste]].
- **§11 — refusal grounds, and there are only three**: false information in the submitted documents; the works being prohibited under §4; **a risk of collapse of the premises' structures.**
- **§12 — appeal** to the superior body, then to court.

## ⚠️ §14 — you may do the work yourself

**«Работы по переустройству и (или) перепланировке могут выполняться самим инициатором либо с привлечением лиц, основным видом деятельности которых является строительная деятельность.»**

**→ Self-performance is expressly permitted, and the law does not require a licensed contractor for these works.** Where a contractor *is* engaged, **construction must be its main activity** — which is a real screening criterion when hiring, and a reason to check a candidate's registered activity rather than only their portfolio. See [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Contractor_Vetting_and_Selection|Contractor Vetting]].

## Chapter 4 — самовольная перепланировка, and the route back

- **§22 — самовольные переустройство и (или) перепланировка запрещены.**
- **§23 — retro-approval is possible**, and is granted **only if the work does not affect the safety of the building in service and was carried out in accordance with technical regulations, as confirmed by a техническое заключение.** That заключение is based on the **ведомость технических характеристик** and on a general — or, if needed, detailed — survey of structures and engineering systems, performed by a legal entity or IP **entitled to that activity**, **at the offender's own order and expense**.
- **⚠️ §28 — if retro-approval is refused, the premises must be restored to their previous state**, by or at the expense of whoever did the work, **within a deadline set by the исполком** — **and the restoration must itself go through the same production-and-acceptance procedure.** So an unapproved change can cost the work twice plus a survey.
- **§29 — liability under legislative acts.** ⚠️ **№ 164 does not quantify penalties**; the КоАП figures circulating in secondary summaries are not verified here.

### What they add

**Relocating a "wet point"** — moving a kitchen to a different room than the developer's layout — **required a technical replanning project and исполком approval** before work could start. `confirmed`, level 1. Episode 2 corroborates from the same project: handling the approval process without professional help cost roughly **a month** of delay.

> **⚠️ Reconcile with № 164 rather than treating this as the rule.** §7 puts partition changes in the *no-project* track; a проект is required by §6 only for gas and central heating. **A wet-point move that also relocates heating or gas would need a проект; one that only moves partitions and plumbing, on the face of § 6–7, would not.** The practitioner's project may have included heating work, or the requirement may have come from a technical regulation outside № 164. **Recorded as an unresolved tension, not smoothed** — and worth asking the исполком directly, since it is the difference between a designer's fee and none.

**Merging two separate bathrooms, each on its own riser, typically means one riser is capped** («глушится») while the other absorbs the combined load. `confirmed`, level 1.

**A height threshold exists above which a suspended/wall-hung fixture cannot be raised without going through replanning approval** — the existence is `confirmed` as the host's practical experience; **the value was never stated.** See Open Questions.

**⚠️ A developer cannot legally hand over a new-build apartment without a screed already poured.** A buyer wanting a different build-up must accept the unit with the developer's screed, then demolish and redo it as extra work — «по белорусскому законодательству застройщик не может сдать квартиру без стяжки». `confirmed`, level 1. **Directly relevant here**: this flat's handover includes a screed, and replacing it is chargeable rework, not a variation.

## Open Questions

- **⚠️ Which works are exempt from the ведомость технических характеристик?** §18 and §23 exempt «абзацы второй и третий части первой пункта 3». Under the standard Russian drafting convention the introductory phrase counts as абзац первый, which makes the exempt works **gas/central-heating replacement and insulation** — but read as list items they would be **insulation and flat-partition changes**. **The difference decides whether this project needs one. Unresolved; ask the исполком.**
- **The citizen's document list** sits in Указ № 200, п.1.1.21 (and 1.1.211 for retro-approval, 1.1.212 for approving the acceptance act, 1.15.3 for project approval). **Not yet read.**
- **The wet-zone placement rule** — no bathroom above a neighbour's living room or kitchen — is widely repeated and **is not in № 164**. It needs **СН 3.02.01-2020** itself before it goes into this vault as a rule.
- **Fire-resistance requirements for new partitions** likewise: §4 incorporates «противопожарные требования» by reference, but the specific **СН 2.02.05-2020** is not cited here.
- **Penalties** for самовольная перепланировка — not quantified in № 164.
- **The height threshold** for suspended fixtures (from the practitioner account) — still unknown.
- **Whether «устройство звукоизоляции» genuinely requires согласование in practice**, given it appears in §3's closed list.
- **Whether госстройэкспертиза is still required** for anything in this scope. № 164 does not mention экспертиза; it repeals п.11 приложения 2 to постановление № 266, which is plausibly the mechanism — but that is inference.
