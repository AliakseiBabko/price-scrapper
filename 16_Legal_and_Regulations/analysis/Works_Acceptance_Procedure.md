# Acceptance of Completed Переустройство/Перепланировка Works — Belarus

**Level 1 only.** Created 2026-09-08 from primary law: **[Постановление Совмина РБ от 03.04.2026 № 164](../../_Sources/DOC_postanovlenie_164_2026_pereustroystvo_pereplanirovka.md)**, Положение 1, §§17–21 and its annexed form.

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Do not confuse this with developer handover.** This page is the state acceptance of **your own** replanning works, at the end of the job. **Accepting a new-build flat FROM the developer** is a different procedure with different parties and remedies — [[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Handover_Acceptance_and_Defect_Reporting|that page]] is still a placeholder with no qualifying source.

## ⚠️⚠️ The schedule fact that has to be planned around

> **The acceptance commission and its chair are appointed by the исполком NOT LESS THAN 30 CALENDAR DAYS before it begins its acceptance work** (§17).

**And the initiator sets the commission's start and end dates himself**, within the acceptance deadline that the **разрешение** already fixed (§10, §17).

**→ So the last month of the programme is administrative, not constructional.** A project that finishes on site and only then thinks about acceptance has added roughly a month. **Trigger the appointment while the finishing trades are still working**, and treat the acceptance date in the permit as a hard milestone the works must land before — see [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Project_Duration_and_Scheduling|Project Duration & Scheduling]].

## Who sits on the commission (§17)

- **The contractor and/or the initiator**, or their representatives;
- the **housing-operating organisation** (and/or the provider of housing-and-communal services);
- the **energy and gas supply organisations**;
- the **исполком**;
- **the designer, if required**;
- and, by agreement with them, representatives of other organisations may be added.

**⚠️ Note that the energy and gas organisations attend the acceptance even where the works required neither ТУ nor a проект** — the composition is not conditioned on the track. Plan on coordinating five parties' availability, not two.

> [!NOTE]
> **❓ THIS BUILDING HAS NO NATURAL GAS — electric stoves only (owner, 2026-09-08). Whether that removes a party from the commission is genuinely unresolved.**
>
> §17 lists «the energy and gas supply organisations» **unconditionally** — the text does not condition the list on whether the building is gasified. With no gas connection there may be no such counterparty to invite, which would cut the coordination from **five parties to four**.
>
> **Do not plan on four.** Assume five until the исполком says otherwise, because the failure mode is asymmetric: over-inviting costs a phone call, while discovering a missing signatory at the acceptance costs another appointment cycle in a month that is already administrative. **Worth asking when the §5 согласование application goes in** — it is free to ask at that point.

## §18 — the documents to present

| Document | When |
| :--- | :--- |
| The developed and duly agreed **проект** | * only if a проект was required for the works |
| **Акты на скрытые работы** (hidden-works certificates) | * only if such documents are required for the works |
| **Ведомость технических характеристик на изолированное помещение**, produced by the state real-estate registration organisation, **at the initiator's order and expense** | see the exemption below |

*(The asterisked two are marked in the act as «представляются, если такие документы предусмотрены для производства работ».)*

**⚠️ Hidden-works certificates are worth reading as a design constraint, not paperwork.** If they are required for your works, then **anything that gets covered up has to be documented before it is covered** — which is exactly the discipline this vault's practitioner sources apply anyway: pressure-test before filling the chases, photograph the services before boarding. See [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Pressure_Testing|Pressure Testing]] and the pre-closure documentation rule on [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Rough_Plumbing_Sequencing|Rough Plumbing Sequencing]]. **The legal requirement and the good-practice habit point the same way; the difference is that one of them gates your acceptance.**

### ⚠️⚠️ The ведомость exemption is ambiguous, and it decides a real cost

§18 says the ведомость is **not** required for the works «указанных в абзацах втором и третьем части первой пункта 3».

- **Under the standard Russian drafting convention**, the introductory phrase «К работам… относятся:» is **абзац первый**, making абзацы второй и третий = **gas/central-heating replacement** and **insulation works**.
- **Read instead as list items**, they would be **insulation** and **partition changes in a flat**.

**→ Only the second reading exempts this project.** Since the ведомость is ordered from and paid to the registration organisation, **the difference is a real line item.** **Unresolved — ask the исполком, and do not assume the favourable reading.** The same exemption clause reappears in §23 for the самовольная route.

## §19 — the act itself

- **Signed by ALL members** of the commission.
- **Drawn up in five (four) copies**: **two to the исполком**, **two to the initiator**, **one to the contractor** (where there is one).
- The annexed form records: the commission's composition; the address; the decision the works were carried out under; the contractor/initiator and the types of works performed; the designer and the documentation, and the decision approving the проект; **the start and finish dates of the works**; and the post-works characteristics — **общая площадь, жилая площадь, количество жилых комнат**.
- It has an explicit slot for **«допущенные отступления от проекта (другие выявленные недостатки)»** and, if acceptance is refused, for **the reasons and the deadline for remedying them.**

**⚠️ The form asks for areas and a room count as the outcome of the works.** That is a documentary reason to have a defensible area figure at the end — and a reminder that this project's own standing rule is that **areas are not evidence and linear dimensions are** (see [[00_Master/Geometry_Variance_Study|Geometry Variance Study]]). The two are not in conflict: the ведомость/registration organisation produces the figure that goes on the act; your model should not be the source of it.

## ⚠️ §20 — the план-схема becomes the acceptance standard

> Works are **NOT accepted** if they were carried out **with deviations from the проект, the план-схема, or the перечень (описание) работ** — or in breach of technical regulations. Acceptance becomes possible **after the remarks recorded in the act are remedied.**

**→ Whatever you submitted at application stage is what you will be measured against.** Two practical consequences, and they pull in opposite directions:

- **Do not over-specify the план-схема.** Every detail you commit to is a detail you can deviate from.
- **Do not under-specify it either**, because §3's closed list and §20's deviation test together mean the submitted description defines the permitted scope.

**⚠️ This is the clause that makes late design changes expensive in a way that has nothing to do with construction cost.** A change of mind after согласование is a change to the document you will be accepted against. For a project that explicitly wants to stay flexible late, **the план-схема should be scoped to what is genuinely decided, and the flexible decisions kept outside the permitted-works description where the law allows** — noting that finishes and fixtures are not перепланировка at all (§3), so most late-changing decisions fall outside it anyway.

## §21 — then the paperwork catches up with reality

**Preparation of the technical passport for the property, and state registration of the change to the property resulting from the переустройство/перепланировка, are carried out by the state real-estate registration organisations** under the registration legislation. See [[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Property_Registration|Property Registration]].

**To have the acceptance act approved**, a citizen applies to the исполком with the documents listed in **подпункт 1.1.212 пункта 1.1** of the schedule of administrative procedures (Указ № 200). **⚠️ That list is by reference and not reproduced in № 164.**

The исполком's approving decision must contain the premises' address and **inventory number per the unified state register**, the initiator, and the date of the acceptance act.

## Open Questions

- **⚠️ Which works are exempt from the ведомость технических характеристик** — the §18/§23 ambiguity above. **The one item on this page worth resolving with a phone call.**
- **Whether акты на скрытые работы are «предусмотрены» for the no-project track.** The act conditions them on the works, not on the track, and does not say which works require them. **If they are required, the hidden-works documentation has to start on day one.**
- **The document list for approving the acceptance act** (Указ № 200, п.1.1.212) — not yet read.
- **Who prepares акты на скрытые работы when the initiator performs the works himself**, given §14 permits self-performance and these certificates are normally a contractor's document.
- **What «пять (четырёх) экземплярах» turns on** — presumably the presence of a contractor, but the act does not say so explicitly.
- **Whether the commission can be convened before the works are fully finished**, given the initiator sets its dates and the 30-day appointment lead time.
