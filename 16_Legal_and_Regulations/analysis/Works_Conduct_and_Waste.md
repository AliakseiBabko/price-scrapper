# Conduct of Works, Noise Hours & Construction Waste — Belarus

**Level 1 only.** Created 2026-09-08 from primary law: **[Постановление Совмина РБ от 03.04.2026 № 164](../../_Sources/DOC_postanovlenie_164_2026_pereustroystvo_pereplanirovka.md)**, Положение 1, §§10, 15, 16.

**Scope**: the rules that bind you *while the work is running* — when you may make noise, what you may not block, how materials and debris move through the building, and what happens if something breaks. The approval that precedes all of it is on [[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Renovation_Permits_and_Approvals|Renovation Permits & Approvals]]; the acceptance that ends it is on [[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Works_Acceptance_Procedure|Works Acceptance]].

## ⚠️⚠️ §15 — the three prohibitions during works

**In multi-apartment and blocked residential buildings, during переустройство/перепланировка, it is prohibited to:**

### 1. Work noisily outside a narrow window

> **«производить в выходные и праздничные дни работы по переустройству и (или) перепланировке, создающие шум или вибрацию, а также начинать такие работы ранее 9 часов и заканчивать их позднее 19 часов в рабочие дни»**

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Permitted** | **Working days only, 09:00–19:00** |
| **Prohibited** | **Weekends and public holidays, entirely** |
| **What it catches** | Works «создающие шум или вибрацию» — so a demolition hammer, a wall chaser, a concrete saw |

**⚠️ Note the scope limit precisely: this is the rule for переустройство/перепланировка works.** Because §3's definition is closed, **work that is not перепланировка is not covered by §15** — and a separate act («Правила пользования жилыми помещениями», widely cited as постановление № 399, with a 07:00–23:00 window and mandated night silence) is said to govern ordinary noise. **⚠️ That act has NOT been verified in this vault.** Until it is, the safe planning assumption is the narrow window, and the open question is recorded below.

**Practical consequence for a self-managed project**: **the noisy trades have a 50-hour week and no Saturdays.** Demolition, chasing and drilling all compete for the same window, which makes their sequencing a schedule constraint rather than a preference — see [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Project_Duration_and_Scheduling|Project Duration & Scheduling]].

### 2. Clutter or dirty the common parts

> **«загромождать и загрязнять строительными материалами и их отходами коридоры, лестничные марши и площадки, проходы, запасные выходы и другие вспомогательные помещения жилого дома»**

Corridors, stair flights and landings, passages, **emergency exits** and other auxiliary premises. **Both cluttering and dirtying are named** — so tracked-in dust is a breach, not only a stack of bags.

### 3. ⚠️ Use passenger lifts for unpackaged materials or waste

> **«использовать пассажирские лифты для транспортировки строительных материалов и их отходов без упаковки»**

**Read it precisely: the prohibition is on carrying them UNPACKAGED, not on using the lift at all.** Packaged material in a passenger lift is not prohibited by this clause. **That is a materially different rule from the blanket ban asserted in secondary summaries**, and it makes packaging a compliance item with a cost attached — worth pricing alongside the protection materials on [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Demolition_Site_Management_and_Waste|Site Management & Waste]].

## ⚠️⚠️ §10 — your own permit sets the waste rules

The **согласование (разрешение)** must state, among other things:

> **«условия хранения строительных материалов и порядок вывоза их отходов»**

**→ The operative waste rule for a given renovation is the line written into its own permit**, alongside the completion and acceptance dates. General waste legislation is the backdrop; **this is the enforceable instruction, and it is issued to you by name.**

**⚠️ So read the разрешение for it, and plan the skip or the haulage against what it actually says** rather than against a general article. Two consequences worth planning for:

- **The permit also fixes your acceptance date**, so the waste and storage arrangements have to survive until then.
- If storage conditions are specified and you cannot meet them (no courtyard space, no permitted store), that is a problem to raise **before** the permit is issued, not after.

**What this page does NOT yet carry**: the general waste-management regime — segregation duties, hiring a certified carrier such as UP «Экорес» in Minsk, self-hauling to a registered landfill, and the paperwork for a haulage contract. All of that was supplied in a secondary summary citing **Закон № 271-З** and Минжилкомхоз rules, and **none of it is in № 164.** It is plausible and probably right, but it is **not verified** and does not meet this folder's bar. See Open Questions.

## §16 — the damage protocol, and it is a stop-work rule

**If, during the works, the contractor or the initiator damages the building's engineering systems, or deformations, cracks or other changes appear in load-bearing or enclosing structures:**

1. **Stop the works immediately** and take measures to prevent the deformations worsening.
2. **Inform** the территориальное подразделение архитектуры и градостроительства, or the housing-operating organisation.
3. **The damage and its consequences are remedied at the expense of whoever caused them.**
4. **If it cannot be remedied at once, ALL the works are suspended** — not just the affected part.
5. **A commission is formed no later than the first working day after discovery**: the contractor and/or initiator, plus a representative of the исполком, plus the housing-operating organisation, plus the designer if needed.
6. **The commission decides within two working days** whether the works may continue after remediation, or **proposes to the исполком that they be prohibited** and restoration works carried out.
7. A prohibition **can later be lifted** by the исполком once its cause is removed.

> **⚠️ The asymmetry worth internalising: discovering a crack costs you at minimum a full stop plus three days of process, and at worst a prohibition and restoration.** That is a strong argument for the survey-and-record discipline this vault already applies to structures — see [[00_Master/Evidence_Reading_Discipline|Evidence Reading Discipline]] — and for photographing the existing state before starting, so a pre-existing crack is not attributed to your works.

**⚠️ And note who is liable: «лиц, допустивших такие повреждения» — whoever caused it.** In a self-managed project with per-trade specialists, **that is a contractual question you should have settled per trade before anyone starts**, because the act allocates it to the causer and says nothing about how you prove which trade that was.

## Open Questions

- **⚠️ The noise regime for work that is NOT перепланировка.** «Правила пользования жилыми помещениями» (reportedly постановление № 399: 07:00–23:00, silence 23:00–07:00) is **unverified**. If it holds, cosmetic work has a much wider window than перепланировка and **the two regimes must be applied per task, not per project** — which would be a genuinely useful scheduling lever. **Needs the act itself.**
- **The general construction-waste regime**: Закон № 271-З, segregation categories, certified carriers and tariffs, self-haul, and the documents needed for a haulage contract. **Unverified; not admitted to this folder yet.**
- **Penalties.** № 164 §29 refers only to «ответственность в соответствии с законодательными актами». The КоАП articles and amounts circulating in secondary summaries are **not verified**.
- **Whether public-holiday dates for this purpose follow the standard national list**, and whether a working Saturday declared by government (a transferred working day) counts as a рабочий день for §15.
- **Whether the housing-operating organisation can impose stricter hours** than §15 by its own rules, and whether such rules bind an owner.
