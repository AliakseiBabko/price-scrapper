  - **⚠️ Recorded that № 164 REPEALS постановление № 384 of 2013 in full** (plus eleven partial repeals). Everything written about Belarusian replanning on the basis of № 384 is superseded — and № 384 is what most secondary material still cites.
  - **⚠️ Recorded that it is a Council-of-Ministers постановление under ЖК ст. 5, not a presidential decree.** English summaries calling it "Decree No. 164" are wrong in kind, which matters because it sits under the Housing Code and can be replaced by another постановление.
  - `Renovation_Permits_and_Approvals.md` **rewritten with a primary-law layer above the retained practitioner accounts**: the closed §3 definition and its «иные работы не являются» sentence, the §4 prohibitions, the two approval tracks (§§5–7), the procedure and one-month decision, what the permit must itself contain (§10), express permission to self-perform (§14), and the самовольная route (§§22–29). **Two tensions with the practitioner accounts left OPEN rather than smoothed** — a wet-point move requiring a проект, against §§6–7 putting partition work in the no-project track.
  - `Works_Conduct_and_Waste.md` **created**: §15's three prohibitions (09:00–19:00 working days only, no weekends or holidays; common parts kept clear and clean; passenger lifts not for **unpackaged** materials — narrower than the blanket ban secondary sources assert), §10's requirement that the permit itself set the waste and storage rules, and §16's damage protocol.
  - `Works_Acceptance_Procedure.md` **created**, and deliberately kept **separate from developer handover** — different procedure, different parties. Carries the **30-calendar-day commission appointment lead time**, the five-party composition, the document list, the annexed act form, and §20's rule that the submitted план-схема is the standard you are measured against.
  - `Facade_AC_and_Glazing_Approvals.md` **created**. **⚠️ It CLOSES an open question that had stood since 2026-08-24**: Положение 2 §2 names «оборудование, применяемое для кондиционирования воздуха» in its own definition, so **a facade or roof AC outdoor unit requires согласование from the территориальное подразделение архитектуры и градостроительства — not the исполком.** Also the glazing and window-infill regime from п.2.1–2.3, including the obligation to dismantle glazing for the duration of facade repairs.
  - `Property_Registration.md` **half filled**: the replanning-change side now rests on §21 (the registration organisations, not the исполком, produce the technical passport and register the change), the 1 January 2023 technical-passport cut-off, and the ведомость технических характеристик at the initiator's expense. **Registration of ownership deliberately left a placeholder** — § 21 defers to the registration legislation, which has not been read.
