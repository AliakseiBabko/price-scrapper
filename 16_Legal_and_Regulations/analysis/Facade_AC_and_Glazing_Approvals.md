# Facades, Air-Conditioning Units, Antennas & Glazing — Approvals in Belarus

**Level 1 only.** Created 2026-09-08 from primary law: **[Постановление Совмина РБ от 03.04.2026 № 164](../../_Sources/DOC_postanovlenie_164_2026_pereustroystvo_pereplanirovka.md)** — п.2.1–2.3 of the постановление itself, plus **Положение 2**, «об условиях и порядке установки на крышах и фасадах многоквартирных жилых домов индивидуальных антенн и иных конструкций».

**Why this is a separate page**: none of it is переустройство/перепланировка. It has **its own approval regime and a different approving body**, and conflating the two sends you to the wrong office.

## ⚠️⚠️ An air-conditioning outdoor unit needs facade approval — and this closes a standing open question

**Положение 2, §2 defines «индивидуальные антенны и иные конструкции»** as:

> any kinds of individual antenna equipment, including that used for receiving terrestrial and satellite television broadcasts, **«оборудование, применяемое для кондиционирования воздуха»**, and other constructions.

**→ An AC outdoor unit mounted on the roof or facade of a multi-apartment building is an «иная конструкция», and its installation requires согласование.** From the definition itself, not by inference.

> **⚠️ This answers a question that had been open on [[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Renovation_Permits_and_Approvals|Renovation Permits & Approvals]] since 2026-08-24** — "what body actually governs AC external-unit placement in Minsk apartment buildings" — and it answers it with primary law rather than with the level-2 anecdote that was deliberately excluded at the time.

### And the body is NOT the исполком

| | |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Approving body** | **территориальное подразделение архитектуры и градостроительства** (§3) |
| **Not** | the исполком (which handles перепланировка), and not the building management |
| **Citizen's documents** | per **подпункт 1.15.1 пункта 1.15** of the schedule of administrative procedures (Указ № 200) — ⚠️ by reference, not reproduced in № 164 |
| **Decision** | согласование or a reasoned refusal, in **two copies** — one to the initiator, one retained by the architecture subdivision (§5) |
| **Appeal** | superior body, then court (§7) |

### §6 — refusal grounds

- Documents or information **not compliant with legislation, including forged, falsified or invalid** documents;
- the installation **reducing the building's fitness for use or creating a risk of collapse** of its structures;
- **the исполком having prohibited such installations** on the facades of houses **on the State List of Historico-Cultural Values**, or on facades **giving onto main streets and squares**, whose list the исполком determines.

**⚠️ So a facade-level ban is a real, lawful possibility for a specific building or street** — which is what the previously-excluded anecdote about a building banning facade AC units was probably describing. **Check the исполком's main-streets list for this address before specifying an outdoor unit position.** See [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/AC_Key_Concepts_and_Placement|AC Key Concepts & Placement]].

### §10–11 — continuing duties after installation

- **Keep the unit in technically sound condition and repair it in time. «Неисправные… подлежат демонтажу»** — a faulty unit must be dismantled.
- If a breach of technical regulations committed at installation comes to light in service, **remedy it or dismantle.**
- **⚠️ §11 — dismantle for the duration of roof or facade repair works** carried out by the housing-operating organisation, where required.

**→ Two of these are ongoing obligations attaching to the owner, not one-off conditions**, and the dismantle-for-repairs duty is the one that will actually arrive unannounced.

### §12–14 — unauthorised installation

**Prohibited**, and whoever did it **must obtain the согласование retroactively**, which is granted if none of §6's refusal grounds applies. **Laundry-drying constructions are excluded from the самовольная definition** as well as from approval.

### §3 — the one exemption

**Constructions for drying laundry need no согласование** — **but «данные конструкции могут устанавливаться только в пределах внутреннего пространства балкона и (или) лоджии».** Inside the balcony or loggia only. **A drying rack fixed to the outside of the balcony rail is not covered by the exemption.**

## Glazing and window/door infills — not перепланировка, but not unregulated

From **п.2 of the постановление** (in force on publication, 07.04.2026, unlike the Положения):

### п.2.1 — balcony and loggia glazing

- **Glazing balconies and loggias in multi-apartment buildings is NOT переустройство/перепланировка.**
- **But it must follow variants approved by the территориальное подразделение архитектуры и градостроительства** — **with colour, materials and configuration specified** — and those variants are **published in publicly accessible places and on the исполком's official website.**
- **⚠️ A continuing obligation few would expect**: where the housing-operating organisation carries out **repairs to the facade, balconies or loggias**, the person who initiated the glazing **must, if necessary, arrange the dismantling of the glazing elements for the duration of those repairs.**

> **⚠️ Directly relevant to this project: the лоджия glazing is part of the developer's handover.** Two consequences worth checking rather than assuming. **First**, if the glazing was installed by the developer as part of the building, the dismantle-for-repairs duty attaches to «гражданин… по инициативе которых выполнялось остекление» — **arguably not the buyer, who did not initiate it.** **Second**, any *change* to that glazing would have to land within the approved variants for this building. **Neither is resolved here.** See [[10_Balcony/Balcony_Guide|Balcony]] and [[00_Master/project_decisions|Project Decisions]].

### п.2.2 — window and door infills, and main-street frontages

**Replacing window and door infills**, and **glazing balconies/loggias that face main streets and squares** (list determined by the исполком), is **not перепланировка** — **but must be carried out preserving the configuration and colour of the existing infills and glazing.**

**→ Read together with §4's prohibition on «изменение архитектурного и цветового решения фасада»**: on a main-street frontage, a window replacement is a like-for-like replacement, not a design opportunity. **Colour and configuration are fixed by what is already there.**

### п.2.3 — historico-cultural values

For houses **entered in the State List of Historico-Cultural Values of the Republic of Belarus**, window and door infill replacement follows variants approved by the architecture subdivision **in agreement with the Ministry of Culture.**

## Open Questions

- **Is this building on the исполком's list of main streets and squares?** Decides whether window/glazing work is like-for-like only, and whether facade AC units can be refused outright. **Address-specific; checkable.**
- **What are the approved glazing variants for this building/street**, and where are they published? п.2.1 says publicly and on the исполком site.
- **⚠️ Does the dismantle-for-facade-repairs duty run with the flat or with the person who initiated the glazing?** The wording says the initiator; for developer-installed glazing that is not the buyer. **Unresolved, and it is a real long-run liability.**
- **The citizen's document list** for facade/AC approval sits in Указ № 200, п.1.15.1 — not yet read.
- **Whether an AC unit sited entirely within the loggia's internal space** (rather than on the facade or roof) falls outside Положение 2 altogether — the drying-rack exemption suggests internal-space siting is treated differently, but the act does not say so for AC equipment. **A potentially significant design workaround, unverified.**
- **Whether the ban applies to the condenser only, or also to the pipework and its facade penetration.**
