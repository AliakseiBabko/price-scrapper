# Legal & Regulations (Republic of Belarus)

Everything in this folder is scoped to **Republic of Belarus law, procedure, and practice only** — renovation permits/approvals, conduct of works, acceptance, facade approvals, property registration, and new-build handover. It is the legal-procedural counterpart to `11_Budget_and_Planning` (money/technique) and `12_Engineering_and_Systems` (how things work); this folder is about what you are legally required or entitled to do, and which body you deal with to do it.

> [!WARNING]
> **Hard scope rule — stricter than the rest of this vault.** Every other folder treats Russian-Federation sources as usable secondary reference for pricing and technique, clearly labelled. **This folder does not extend that allowance.** Law, permitting bodies and registration procedure are jurisdiction-specific by nature — a Russian исполком procedure is not "weaker evidence", it is **a different country's law**, and would be actively misleading here. **Only content naming Belarus (or a Belarus city/region) directly in its own source material qualifies — level 1.** Level-2 association via channel branding, tags or title does not qualify.
>
> Russian-sourced legal content elsewhere in this vault (e.g. `14_Furniture/analysis/Furniture_Dispute_Legal_Recourse.md`) **stays where it is and is explicitly not part of this folder.**

## ⚠️⚠️ Current state: primary law is now in, as of 2026-09-08

**The folder is no longer a scaffold.** **[Постановление Совета Министров РБ от 3 апреля 2026 г. № 164](../_Sources/DOC_postanovlenie_164_2026_pereustroystvo_pereplanirovka.md)** — «О переустройстве и перепланировке, установке на крышах и фасадах многоквартирных жилых домов индивидуальных антенн и иных конструкций» — has been read in full (15 pp., primary PDF from the National Legal Internet Portal) and is now the governing layer of four pages.

**Three things to know before reading anything older about Belarusian replanning:**

1. **⚠️ № 164 REPEALS постановление № 384 of 2013 outright.** Every guide, article or video citing «постановление 384» is out of date.
2. **⚠️ It is a Council-of-Ministers постановление under Жилищный кодекс ст. 5 — not a presidential decree.** English summaries calling it "Decree No. 164" are wrong in kind.
3. **Commencement**: published 07.04.2026; the two Положения and the repeals take effect **three months later, early July 2026**.

**Still genuinely empty**: **registration of ownership** (№ 164 defers to the registration legislation, unread), and **new-build handover from the developer** (no qualifying source at all).

## Renovation Permits & Approvals

**§3 of № 164 is a closed list, and its last sentence does the most work: «Иные работы не являются работами по переустройству и (или) перепланировке.»** Finishes, tiling, screed over screed, sanitaryware swapped in place, sockets, lighting and furniture are **not** перепланировка and need no permit — though §4's prohibitions still apply to them.

**Two approval tracks.** Gas or central-heating relocation needs **согласование + технические условия + a designed проект**. Insulation, partition changes in a flat, and openings in non-load-bearing walls need **согласование only, no проект** — from the **district/city исполком**. **⚠️ Moving a radiator flips the job into the project track.**

**⚠️ And §14 expressly permits doing the work yourself**; where a contractor is used, **construction must be its main activity.**

## Conduct of Works, Noise Hours & Waste

**Noisy replanning work: working days only, 09:00–19:00. Weekends and public holidays are prohibited entirely** (§15). Common corridors, stairs, landings and emergency exits must be kept clear **and clean**; **passenger lifts may not carry materials or waste «без упаковки»** — unpackaged, which is not the blanket ban secondary sources assert.

**⚠️ And your own разрешение sets your waste rules**: §10 requires it to state the conditions for storing materials and the procedure for removing their waste, alongside the completion and acceptance dates.

→ **[[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Works_Conduct_and_Waste|Full detail]]** — the three §15 prohibitions and what they cost a schedule, the permit-sets-the-waste-rule point, and the §16 damage protocol (immediate stop, a commission by the next working day, a decision in two)

## Acceptance of Your Own Works

**⚠️ The acceptance commission is appointed by the исполком not less than 30 calendar days before it starts** — so the last month of the programme is administrative, not constructional. Five parties sit on it, including the energy and gas organisations, regardless of track.

**⚠️ And §20 makes the план-схема you submitted the standard you are measured against**: works are not accepted if they deviate from it.

→ **[[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Works_Acceptance_Procedure|Full detail]]** — the 30-day lead time, commission composition, the documents (проект, акты на скрытые работы, ведомость технических характеристик), the annexed act form and its five copies, and the ведомость exemption ambiguity that is the one item here worth a phone call

## Facades, AC Units, Antennas & Glazing

**⚠️ An air-conditioning outdoor unit on a facade or roof requires согласование — from the территориальное подразделение архитектуры и градостроительства, not the исполком.** Положение 2 §2 names «оборудование, применяемое для кондиционирования воздуха» in its own definition. **This closes an open question that stood on the permits page from 2026-08-24.**

**Balcony/loggia glazing and window/door infill replacement are NOT перепланировка** — but they must follow **approved variants** (colour, materials, configuration) published by the architecture subdivision, and on main-street frontages must **preserve existing configuration and colour.**

→ **[[16_Legal_and_Regulations/analysis/Facade_AC_and_Glazing_Approvals|Full detail]]** — the approving body and refusal grounds (including lawful street- and building-level bans), the continuing duty to maintain or dismantle, the dismantle-for-facade-repairs obligation, and the drying-rack exemption that only applies inside the balcony

## Property Registration

**Half filled.** № 164 §21 establishes that **the technical passport and the state registration of the change are performed by the state real-estate registration organisations**, not the исполком — and that the procedure is set by the registration legislation, which has not been read. Also: the **1 January 2023 technical-passport cut-off**, and the **ведомость технических характеристик** ordered and paid for by the initiator.
