# Legal & Regulations (Republic of Belarus)

Everything in this folder is scoped to **Republic of Belarus law, procedure, and practice only** — renovation permits/approvals, property (ownership) registration, and new-build handover/acceptance/defect-reporting. It is the legal-procedural counterpart to `11_Budget_and_Planning` (money/technique) and `12_Engineering_and_Systems`+ (how things work); this folder is about what you're legally required or entitled to do, and which government/developer body you deal with to do it.

> [!WARNING]
> **Hard scope rule — stricter than the rest of this vault.** Every other folder in this project treats Russian-Federation sources (the majority of processed YouTube channels) as usable secondary/comparative reference for pricing and technique, explicitly labeled as such. **This folder does not extend that allowance.** Law, permitting bodies, registration procedure, and consumer-protection/warranty rules are jurisdiction-specific by nature — a Russian исполком procedure, a Russian property-registration step, or Russian consumer-protection statute is not just "less relevant," it is a **different country's law** and would be actively misleading if presented here as if it applied in Belarus. **Only content that names Belarus (or a Belarus city/region) directly and specifically in its own source material qualifies for this folder — level 1 attribution, per this project's existing evidence-tier framework.** A source whose Belarus association comes only from channel branding, tags, or title (level 2) does not qualify either. This is a stricter bar than the general project's location-attribution rule, not a new one — see `renovation_regulations_belarus_knowledge_store.md`'s own note, which already established it.
>
> This also means: content already in this vault that discusses legal/regulatory topics but is Russian-sourced (e.g. `14_Furniture/analysis/Furniture_Dispute_Legal_Recourse.md`, built on a Russian consumer-protection lawyer's on-camera explanation) **stays where it is and is explicitly NOT part of this folder** — it is not Belarus law and must not be cited here or treated as applicable to this project's own Minsk-area renovation.

## Renovation Permits & Approvals

What's confirmed so far, from a real Minsk replanning project: relocating a "wet point" (e.g. moving a kitchen to a different room than the developer's layout) **requires a technical replanning project and исполком (executive committee) approval** before work can start — not optional paperwork, it gates whether work can proceed at all. Merging two bathrooms on separate risers typically means one riser gets capped. There's also a height threshold above which a suspended/wall-hung fixture can't be freely approved — the threshold's actual value was never stated by this source, a known gap.
