# Furniture — Bed Frames and Bases

Detail page for [[14_Furniture/Seating_and_Tables|Seating & Tables]].

**Bed widths and clearances, what to inspect on the frame, and the base the mattress actually sits on — including building one.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. A genuine disagreement in this vault about bed HEIGHT

**Three sources, two positions, and they pull opposite ways.**

| Source | Position | Kind of argument |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Шеврина / SMBUREAU** ([[14_Furniture/analysis/Loose_Furniture_Selection_Principles\|principles]]) | *"A bed — and any soft furniture meant for rest — **should not exceed 40 cm** from the floor"* | **Compositional** — low rest furniture reads better in a room |
| **Кузина** | **Standard Russian bed height IS up to 40 cm**, explicitly contrasted with American beds | **Observational** — a market fact, not a prescription |
| **⚠️⚠️ ARMOS** | **Optimal combined bed + mattress height = the bend of your knee, ± a palm's width**; and *«если совместная высота значительно НИЖЕ вашего колена, вам такую кровать будет использовать не очень удобно»* | **Ergonomic** — you have to get out of it |

> **⚠️⚠️ FOR MOST ADULTS THE KNEE BEND IS ROUGHLY 45–50 cm, SO THESE GENUINELY CONFLICT.**
>
> **And Кузина's observation explains the shape of the problem: if the Russian market norm really is ≤40 cm, then THE MARKET NORM AND THE DESIGN RULE AGREE WITH EACH OTHER AND BOTH SIT BELOW THE ERGONOMIC OPTIMUM.** **The cost is paid by exactly the people the mattress sources name — older users, and anyone who finds a low bed hard to rise from.**
>
> **→ NOT RESOLVED. It depends on who sleeps here and for how many years, and it should be decided deliberately rather than inherited from whichever source was read last.** ⚠️ **Note it compounds with mattress height: a thin mattress on a low frame fails both rules at once, and [[14_Furniture/analysis/Mattress_Construction_And_Selection|the mattress page]] §7 gives 17–18 cm as a springless floor.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Widths, and the arithmetic underneath them

| Case | Width |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Child, single** | **80 or 90 cm** (standard) |
| **Adult, single** | **better 120 cm** |
| **Couple** | **from 140 cm — the MINIMUM**; better **160–180**, or 200 |

- **⚠️⚠️ THE RULE THAT GENERATES THEM: from each person to the edge of the bed, or to their partner, ideally 20 cm or more.** **→ Turns "how wide" from taste into arithmetic.**
- **⚠️⚠️ PASSAGES: 50 cm or more** around the bed. ***«Замерьте место, где будет стоять кровать — это будут максимально возможные габариты вашей кровати.»*** **→ The room sets the ceiling; the 20 cm rule then says whether what fits is enough.**
- ⚠️ **And because *«большинство из нас живёт не во дворцах»*, passages stay tight — which is why the next item matters.**

## ⚠️ 3. What to inspect on the bed

- **⚠️⚠️ Trauma safety, specifically**: corners **soft and rounded**; **legs must not protrude — ideally set slightly INWARD from the bed's outline**, which is what stops you kicking them. **Directly relevant to [[06_Small_Bedroom/Small_Bedroom_Guide|a tight bedroom]].**
- **⚠️⚠️ ASK TO SEE THE INTERNAL CONSTRUCTION** — in a shop from the seller, **online by asking for photographs.** **Assembly should be on BOLTS with metal fittings. Ideal: a metal frame.**
- **The lamellae base (ортопедическая решётка) ideally solid-welded and non-dismountable** — the guarantee against squeaks and against it working loose. ⚠️ **A dismountable frame gives no such guarantee.**
- **⚠️ If the lamellae sit on a separate metal frame, check for SOFT DAMPING ELEMENTS where it rests on the bed** — they prevent knocking, **and this is flagged as especially important on a lift-mechanism bed.**
- **Lift mechanism on gas struts** plus a linen box — practical in a flat.
- **⚠️ Ground clearance is a cleaning decision**: no legs = no dust underneath **but the plinth is vulnerable to a vacuum cleaner**; high legs = **a robot vacuum passes under**.

**⚠️⚠️ UPHOLSTERY — and this corroborates the vault independently:** **экокожа is unsuitable if you want 10–15 years**; **with pets, рогожка is unsuitable however expensive, because interlacing fibres catch claws — velour or flock give a claw nothing to grip.**

> **→ SECOND INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL. [[14_Furniture/analysis/Loose_Furniture_Selection_Principles|Loose Furniture Selection Principles]] already records the same mechanism from Шеврина about bouclé and ribbed velvet. Two unrelated sources, different fabrics named, same physical reason.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. The base: 7–8 cm, and the mattress is what pays for getting it wrong

**Lamellae are springy elements that *«работают вместе с упругими элементами матраса, усиливая его анатомические свойства»* — the base is part of the suspension, not a shelf.**

> ***«Самое главное, чтобы расстояние между ними не было больше чем 7–8 сантиметров, чтобы ПРУЖИНЫ МАТРАСА НЕ ПРОВАЛИВАЛИСЬ в эти промежутки — тогда срок службы матраса существенно сократится.»***

- **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE MECHANISM IS NAMED: individual springs drop into an over-wide gap and work outside their design range.** **The victim is the MATTRESS, not the base** — the same asymmetry as a bonnell block abrading the settling layers above it.
- ⚠️ **The flagship says "not more than 8 cm", the base video "7–8". Recorded as 7–8 cm with 8 as the outer limit.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Building a base — and the pallet trap

1. **Buy a ready metal frame and supports**, then make the suspension, sides and headboard.
2. **Build from ЛДСП, sanded plywood or similar sheet material.** ***«Главное — достаточно большое количество РЁБЕР ЖЁСТКОСТИ и дополнительной опоры. Заложите ИЗБЫТОЧНУЮ ПРОЧНОСТЬ — это гарантирует вас от поломок, скрипов и проблем.»***

> **⚠️⚠️ THE PALLET WARNING follows directly from §4: the gaps between pallet boards are far larger than 7–8 cm, so a pallet base *«не подходит для современных матрасов»*.** **→ It must be levelled and covered with sheet material — ЛДСП or sanded plywood — before a modern mattress goes on it.**
>
> ⚠️ **Note a tension the source does not resolve: a solid sheet base is accepted here, while the same source stresses mattress ventilation elsewhere.** **Unresolved, and flagged rather than smoothed over.**
