# Furniture — Pillows and Toppers

Detail page for [[14_Furniture/Seating_and_Tables|Seating & Tables]].

**What goes on top of the mattress: pillow height by sleeping position, and the two opposite kinds of topper.**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ⚠️⚠️ **The pillow material is presented by a named osteopath (Станислав Жашкевич) — and *«все подушки для сюжета предоставлены фирмой Armos»*, on ARMOS's channel, closing with a recommendation to buy from ARMOS.** **That is a SPONSORED clinical opinion.** **→ The positional reasoning transfers and is checkable against any independent source; the RANKING of pillow types is not independent.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. Pillow height follows sleeping position

> **Back sleepers → LOWER pillow. Side sleepers → HIGHER pillow.**

**Applied consistently across every type he shows, which is what makes it the finding rather than the product notes.** ⚠️ **The mechanism is implicit: on your side the gap between head and mattress is a shoulder deep; on your back it is not. He never says it that plainly — this is the vault's reading of a rule he uses four times.**

| Type | Position |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Memory foam** | *«Практически эталонный выбор»* — universal for back AND side, **provided height matches the position** |
| **Latex** | **Natural, hypoallergenic** — good where dust or odours provoke allergy. Low samples suit back sleepers; **side sleepers need a taller one** |
| **⚠️⚠️ Rigid orthopaedic foam** | **Strong neck fixation; near-ideal for SIDE sleeping IF there are essentially no cervical problems.** ⚠️⚠️ **On your BACK with cervical-lordosis issues the rigid curve *«будет лишь вредить вашему сну»* — risk of waking with headache and neck pain** |
| **Лебяжий пух (synthetic)** | **⚠️ NOT an orthopaedic pillow**, and he says so — different shape. Hypoallergenic despite the name |
| **Three-layer adjustable** | Memory / latex / memory under a cooling cover — **layers removable and reorderable** |

**⚠️⚠️ THE SYNTHETIC-DOWN TECHNIQUE COSTS NOTHING AND MOST PEOPLE GET IT WRONG:**

> **On your back, HEAD AND SHOULDERS must BOTH be on the pillow.** *«Если вы подбиваете подушку и пытаетесь лечь на спину, а плечи ваши лежат ВНЕ подушки, ваша шея будет ПЕРЕГРУЖАТЬСЯ.»* **On your side you can simply crush it to height.**

⚠️ **The adjustable pillow's real merit is conceptual: it converts height and firmness from a purchase decision into an adjustment** — which is the honest answer to §2.

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Two rules that generalise past pillows

1. **⚠️⚠️ *«Каждому человеку надо подбирать подушку ИНДИВИДУАЛЬНО — даже с одинаковым типом сложения два разных человека могут испытывать разные ощущения от одной подушки.»*** **→ Build does not predict the answer, so no recommendation — including his — substitutes for trying it.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ THE TEST-CONDITION RULE:** *«Запомните ЖЁСТКОСТЬ ВАШЕЙ КРОВАТИ и протестируйте подушку на точно такой же кровати с точно такой же жёсткостью. В противном случае вы рискуете прийти домой и испытать другие ощущения.»*
   **→ A pillow cannot be tested in isolation: how far you sink into the mattress changes the pillow height you need.**

> **⚠️⚠️ PAIR IT WITH THE TEN-MINUTE MATTRESS TEST on [[14_Furniture/analysis/Mattress_Construction_And_Selection|Mattress Construction and Selection]] §5. Together they say: TEST BOTH, ON EACH OTHER, BEFORE BUYING EITHER.** **That is the single most useful procedural finding of the round, and it falls out of two videos that never mention each other.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Toppers — and the first thing is what a topper is NOT

> ***«Топперы, или тонкие матрасы — НЕ ПУТАТЬ с наматрасниками или защитными чехлами.»***

**Purpose: rescue a sagging mattress or an uncomfortable sofa when a proper bed and mattress are not possible.** *«Логично, что это должна быть достаточно бюджетная покупка.»*

**⚠️⚠️ TWO OPPOSITE GROUPS — and buying from the wrong one is the failure mode:**

| Group | Material / thickness | What it does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **① RAISE FIRMNESS + LEVEL** | **Coconut coir 1 cm or 3 cm**; **6 cm coir ≈ a thin or child's mattress, very firm** | **Levels ANY sagging mattress.** ⚠️⚠️ **CANNOT be folded — folding breaks the structure and leaves a permanent crease** |
| **② RAISE COMFORT** | Foam 3 or 6 cm; **memory foam**; latex foam (denser, dearer) 2–3 cm | Levels less, changes the feel. **Memory foam distributes pressure, reduces soft-tissue numbness, less tossing** |
| **Combination** | **2 cm coir + 2 cm foam**; or struttofaiber + coir at 6 cm, reversible | **Levels and firms while the foam preserves comfort** |

> **⚠️⚠️ THE SOFA CASE IS THE MOST SPECIFIC ITEM: a 3 cm FOAM topper on a fold-out sofa.** It **closes the gaps at the joints of the opened sofa**, raises comfort, and means *«вы спите не на том, на чём сидите»* — cheap, foldable any way, hides inside or behind the sofa.
>
> **→ ⚠️ AND THIS IS WHY THE TWO-GROUP SPLIT MATTERS: a coir topper would fix the same sagging but CANNOT be folded, so it is the wrong tool for a sofa specifically.**

⚠️ **His memory-foam case**: worth it *«если есть проблемы с качеством сна, особенно на диване»* — **specifically where the sofa's sleeping surface is already FLAT** (he names an accordion mechanism), i.e. where levelling is not the problem and comfort is.

## ⚠️ A third use for a topper: joining two mattresses (Круглов, 2026)

**Where two partners differ in weight by more than about 60 kg, no single off-the-shelf mattress works** — see [[14_Furniture/analysis/Mattress_Construction_And_Selection|Mattress Construction and Selection]] §9. **One of the two solutions is two separate 80 cm mattresses on a 160 cm bed, and the thing that makes that liveable is laid on top:**

> **A наматрасник or a thin «микроматрасик» spanning both, to make a single plane** and hide the join.

- **→ ⚠️ So the levelling group in §3 has a use beyond rescuing a sagging mattress: it is also what turns two mattresses into one bed.** ⚠️ **He calls the arrangement *«сильно простой вариант, но менее удобный»*** than a purpose-made split mattress.
- **⚠️ And a separate endorsement of the наматрасник in its ordinary role**, which this page previously only distinguished FROM a topper: it protects the mattress cover from perspiration, **reduces the likelihood of dust mites**, and reduces wear on the top layer.
