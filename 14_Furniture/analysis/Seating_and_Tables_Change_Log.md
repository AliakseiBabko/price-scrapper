# Seating & Tables — Change Log

- 2026-08-30 — **Pages created** from the Shevrina/SMBUREAU Round 2 batch. This vault had no loose-furniture content at all before today: `14_Furniture` held only wardrobe and storage pages, and `Furniture_Index.md` had explicitly flagged the gap ("expected to grow as more furniture categories — seating, tables, kitchen-adjacent furniture — get processed").
  - Created as a compact guide (`Seating_and_Tables.md`) plus three `analysis/` pages, matching the shape the Wardrobes content in this folder already uses: `Loose_Furniture_Selection_Principles.md` (cross-cutting), `Seating_Selection.md`, `Table_Types_and_Dimensions.md`.
  - Three sources crossed this project's 3-source page-creation threshold in one round — but **all three are the same channel**, so the pages are `single-account` and carry that caveat prominently rather than reading as settled practice.
  - **Deliberate extraction decision**: the three source videos are largely brand-by-brand surveys of Russian furniture SKUs, with per-model aesthetic verdicts ("10 из десяти", "вызывают нервный тик"). **None of those verdicts were recorded** — they are taste, market-specific and time-decaying, and fail this project's value criteria. Only the reasoning underneath was extracted. This is why three videos totalling ~55 minutes produced a fairly compact set of pages.
  - **Two internal refinements worth noting**, where a later video corrected an earlier overstatement: the leg rule is not "no angled legs" but "**a backward rake alone is fine; a simultaneous sideways splay is the failure**" (from the chairs video); and the "why a piece reads designed" causes list is stated most completely in the tables video, then restated from the positive side in the sofa video.
