Triage: [`armos_channel_triage_20260914.md`](../../_Inbox/planning/armos_channel_triage_20260914.md). **FIVE THINGS WORTH CARRYING:**
  1. **⚠️⚠️ THE VAULT HAD NOTHING HERE.** `независимые пружины`, `боннель`, `топпер`, `латекс` and `memory foam` were at **ZERO files**; `матрас` at one. **Unlike the two channels before it, every topic here was new ground rather than saturated** — which is also why nothing on these pages can be cross-checked internally.
  2. **⚠️⚠️ FIRMNESS LABELS ARE NOT COMPARABLE BETWEEN MANUFACTURERS**, because there is no standard — *«матрас средней жёсткости одного производителя будет вам казаться мягким»*. **A cross-vendor warning from a vendor, and it makes an online comparison on firmness meaningless.** ⚠️⚠️ **The antidote is procedural and falls out of TWO videos that never mention each other: lie on the mattress ten minutes in your own sleeping position, and test a pillow on a bed of YOUR bed's firmness. → TEST BOTH, ON EACH OTHER, BEFORE BUYING EITHER.**
  3. **⚠️⚠️ DO NOT BUY ON SPRING COUNT, AND THE REASON IS PHYSICAL.** However many springs per m², each is DISCRETE and cannot conform to fine contours; a spring is small-diameter wire and will press on soft tissue regardless. **512/m² is not twice as good as the 256/m² industry standard — the higher count is bought with THINNER WIRE.** → **The comfort layers and the cover are what make a spring block usable, not extras on top of it**, and *«чем лучше матрас, тем толще слой-изолятор»*.
  4. **⚠️⚠️ A DISAGREEMENT INSIDE THIS FOLDER ABOUT BED HEIGHT, NOW RECORDED RATHER THAN SMOOTHED.** Шеврина's rule — rest furniture **≤ 40 cm** — is COMPOSITIONAL. ARMOS's is ERGONOMIC: **bed plus mattress to the bend of the knee, ± a palm, roughly 45–50 cm.** ⚠️ **And Кузина observes the Russian market norm IS ≤40 cm → the market and the design rule agree with each other and BOTH SIT BELOW THE ERGONOMIC OPTIMUM, with the cost paid by older users and anyone who struggles to rise from a low bed.** **Not resolved; it depends on who sleeps here and for how long.**
  5. **⚠️⚠️ LAMELLA SPACING 7–8 cm MAXIMUM, and the victim of getting it wrong is the MATTRESS** — individual springs drop into an over-wide gap and work outside their design range. **Same asymmetry as a bonnell block abrading the settling layers above it: the cheap component survives and the expensive one wears out.** → **And it kills the pallet-bed idea outright unless the pallets are levelled and sheeted over.**
  **Also**: **spring versus springless is a TASK question and NOT a budget lever** — at equal height, load capacity and cover they cost about the same, with springless *«король тонких матрасов»* for sofas, children and anything that must roll up. **⚠️⚠️ A LATEX WARNING: latexed foams lack air permeability and an all-springless mattress has no internal air — avoid latex in a damp room or with heavy perspiration**, and the sponge-left-unwrung analogy makes the point better than a spec sheet. **⚠️ Load capacity silently changes the firmness you FEEL** — a 100 kg user on a 90 kg mattress will not find it firm, and it fails early. **⚠️ What a higher price buys is DEFORMATION HEADROOM: a taller spring block deforms less under the same load, so service life multiplies** — checkable reasoning rather than a materials claim. **⚠️ Coconut coir is a REINFORCING layer, not a comfort one.** **⚠️ On your back, head AND SHOULDERS must both be on a synthetic-down pillow or the neck is overloaded.** **⚠️ Toppers split into two OPPOSITE groups and the wrong one is the failure mode — coir levels but cannot be folded, so a 3 cm foam topper is the fold-out-sofa answer.** **⚠️⚠️ SECOND INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL on upholstery: рогожка catches pet claws and velour or flock do not — the same mechanism Шеврина gave for bouclé and ribbed velvet, from an unrelated source.** **No price transferred (RUB Russia 2022–2023, rule 2).   2. **⚠️⚠️ THE FIRMNESS FINDING IS NOW ESTABLISHED, NOT ASSERTED.** Two unrelated sources with opposite commercial positions agree there is **no firmness standard anywhere** — so labels are not comparable between makers. **And the firmer-is-better MYTH is debunked with its origin: it dates from when only dependent-spring mattresses existed, where firm beat a bad soft option rather than being good in itself.**
  3. **⚠️⚠️ FOUR CORRECTIONS, AND ON EACH THE MANUFACTURER'S ANSWER WAS THE ONE THAT FAVOURED A SALE.** The ten-minute shop test is **not sufficient** — one to three nights, bought on a return policy, and a warm showroom lies because foam and latex stiffen when cool. **Height is not a quality signal, DENSITY is.** **The cover is *«70% маркетинг»* and is exactly what a bad maker uses to disguise bad filling** — which inverts ARMOS's use of it as a class marker. **And 8 cm between lamellae is already too much**, with the real rule being *small relative to the ~3 cm spring diameter*.
  4. **⚠️⚠️ THE BED-HEIGHT PERSPECTIVE SHIFTED: two independent ERGONOMIC sources now stand against one COMPOSITIONAL rule.** Круглов's *«угол коленей не менее 90° когда садитесь»* is also **more checkable than ARMOS's "palm's width"**. → **Treat Шеврина's ≤40 cm as the LOOK and the knee test as the FUNCTION.**
  **Also entirely new**: **partner weight-difference thresholds — 25 kg / 25–30 / 30–60 / and over 60 kg where NO off-the-shelf mattress exists**, answered by a factory split mattress under one cover or two 80 cm mattresses joined by a topper. **⚠️⚠️ "Ортопедический" is marketing, and a mattress does NOT treat a back — it is preventive and a wrong one can worsen things, so an existing back problem means seeing a doctor first.** **⚠️ Service life by segment (3–5 / 5–8 / 10–15 years) with a usable death criterion: it dies when it stops SUPPORTING, not when it breaks.** **⚠️ A care regime claimed to add 50%+: rotate head-to-foot every 2–4 weeks for three months then every 3–6, air the bed 20–30 minutes on waking, vacuum monthly, never work propped against the headboard.** **⚠️ Latex smell never airs out, and artificial latex has a much shorter life.** **⚠️⚠️ And the sentence that outranks every price band: *«Неправильно подобранный матрас в ПРЕМИУМ-сегменте намного хуже, чем ПРАВИЛЬНО подобранный в ЭКОНОМЕ.»*** **No price transferred (RUB 2026, and sourced from one maker).   1. **⚠️⚠️ THE SPRING COUNT IS QUOTED IN A GAMED UNIT, WHICH NEITHER PRIOR SOURCE MENTIONED.** Both said *don't chase spring count*; **neither said the UNIT is manipulated.** A sleeping place of 80×200 cm is **1.6 m², not 2** — yet makers take a true 256/m², multiply by two, and publish «512 на спальное место». **→ Ask "per square metre or per sleeping place" BEFORE asking the number: two comparable-looking figures are in different units and the larger is the lower density.** ⚠️ **The multiplication is also wrong on its own terms — at 256/m² over 1.6 m² the true count is about 410, so the published figure overstates by roughly a quarter. НЕ ФАКТ»*. **→ The cover is a real cost driver and a poor quality proxy; where budget forces a choice, the FILLING wins.** ⚠️ **She is fairer than the designer and does NOT call it a scam — it is a legitimate comfort purchase, simply not evidence of what is underneath.**
