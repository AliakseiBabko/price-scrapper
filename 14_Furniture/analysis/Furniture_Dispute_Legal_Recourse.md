# Furniture — If a Contractor's Work Turns Out Defective

This is Russian consumer-protection law as explained by a named guest lawyer in one source — **not an independently verified citation of the actual statute text**. Treat the mechanism/process as plausible and worth knowing, but verify the specific percentages against the current law before relying on them in a real dispute. Part of [[14_Furniture/Wardrobes_and_Storage|Wardrobes & Storage]]. [source: [[_Sources/YT_pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_dispute_legal_process|extraction note]]]

- **Two legal-expertise categories determine what can be claimed**: **товароведческая экспертиза** (product/goods expertise) applies to a free-standing furniture purchase; **строительно-техническая экспертиза** (construction-technical expertise) applies when the item is built-in and its removal affects the apartment's own interior finishing — the latter additionally allows claiming finish-restoration costs a goods-only claim wouldn't cover. [source: [[_Sources/YT_pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_dispute_legal_process|pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_di]]]
- **The other party must be formally notified of a scheduled expert inspection's date/time in advance** (e.g. via registered telegram) to preserve their documented right to attend — skipping this lets them later contest the report's validity in court. [source: [[_Sources/YT_pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_dispute_legal_process|pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_di]]]
- **Recommended sequence**: engage a lawyer first (to identify contract type — work vs. goods, since the law treats these differently) → determine built-in vs. free-standing → formulate specific questions for the formal expert examination → notify the other party → conduct the examination → calculate the claim. [source: [[_Sources/YT_pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_dispute_legal_process|pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_di]]]
- **An individual entrepreneur (IP) in Russia is personally liable with their own assets** — ceasing IP status doesn't extinguish an existing debt, which can be pursued against the person privately instead. Relevant when evaluating recourse against a small IP-structured contractor specifically. [source: [[_Sources/YT_pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_dispute_legal_process|pVMvsuhrwWs_furniture_di]]]
- If unresolved via a pre-court settlement demand, Russian consumer-protection law reportedly adds a **50% penalty on the combined goods/damages/moral-harm total**, plus a **3%/day contractual penalty (неустойка)** on the contract value, capped at a stated maximum — `unverified` against the actual statute text, single secondhand account.

## ⚠️ Prevention — What to Check Before Signing, From a Manufacturer (FLAT, added 2026-09-02)

The page above is the recourse route once work is defective. **This section is the upstream half: the checks that keep you out of it**, from a furniture manufacturer's own channel (Михаил, owner of FLAT, St Petersburg, 11th year, premium segment).

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **`promotional_ratio: medium` — the subject is inherently self-serving ("how to choose a furniture maker," from a furniture maker), and it closes on "обращайтесь в проверенные компании." That recommendation is not recorded as neutral advice.** What makes the rest extractable is that **two of his five points demolish claims his own industry sells with, including one that would favour a company of his size** — see the myths below. **Russian jurisdiction throughout; flagged, and not routed to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`** (Belarus-only). [source: [[_Sources/YT_TNMzWzhAqqI_flat_choosing_furniture_maker|TNMzWzhAqqI]]]

**The failure this is written against**: furniture ordered in parallel with a renovation, 4–6 months pass, move-in is due — **and the company asks you to wait an unknown length of time, "может быть месяц, может быть два," or nobody answers the phone at all.** He states plainly this is not a myth and that saying so is a professional duty.

### ⚠️ Entrusting a large project to a private craftsman (частный мастер)

- He may lack the resources to deliver, **and material liability is effectively unrecoverable from an individual — "взыскать потом что-либо с физического лица будет невозможно."** **This is the practical counterpart to the page's existing note that a Russian IP is personally liable with their own assets: an IP can be pursued, an undocumented private individual realistically cannot.**
- **⚠️ If proceeding anyway, his minimum diligence is specific: check the person on the FSSP (bailiff service) registry for debts; verify they have actually done comparable projects, where and how; and treat control as mandatory — "нельзя просто так отдать ему кучу денег и ждать 6 месяцев." Require a photo report of the materials actually purchased.**

### ⚠️ The contract, and the specification that is the document that actually protects you

- **"Без договора доказать факт сделки будет невозможно — договор ваша страховка."**
- **⚠️ Read it, not just the deadline.** Check it is not one-sided — obligations all on the buyer, none on the maker. **His real example is a pattern to look for: a clause providing that if the buyer failed to prepare the premises for measurement by a given date, the prepayment for drawings was forfeited and had to be paid again.** Test the contract for reasonableness and ask about anything you dislike.
- **⚠️ The specification is a separate mandatory annex and is the one that prevents the dispute: it must state exactly what is being bought — not "кухня за миллион" but a sketch with dimensions, materials, hardware and everything else.** **Without it the failure mode is "а мы с вами не договаривались… доплатите ещё 20%." "Договариваться об этом нужно на берегу."**
- **⚠️ Read this against the page's existing expertise distinction: a built-in item triggers construction-technical expertise and allows claiming finish-restoration costs. A specification with dimensions and materials is what makes such a claim provable — without it there is no agreed baseline to measure the defect against.**

### Verification, even of a recommendation

- **His case: a foreman you met only on your own site says "есть нормальные ребята," and you transfer your own responsibility without doing the elementary checks.** He is explicit that recommendations are good — "этим наша отрасль живёт" — **but verify anyway, "это много времени не занимает."**
- Checks: social media presence, some website, reviews. **⚠️ And if being a manufacturer rather than a reseller matters, go and physically look at the production — "не доверяйте просто словам 'вы производители'… доедьте и посмотрите." He reports cases of people showing production facilities that were not theirs, taking the money and disappearing.**

### ⚠️ The price-spread rule, and two myths demolished

- **⚠️ For an identical specification the honest market spread is small: "адекватный разброс в пределах 10%, причём даже 10 иногда это много."**
- **⚠️ A large price imbalance therefore means one of two things, both of which become your problem: someone counted carelessly, or a premium project has been mis-scoped as economy — "они сами его идентифицировали неправильно."** **Directly actionable: build one identical specification, send it to several makers, and treat a materially cheaper number as evidence the specification was not priced, not as a saving.** **Same logic as the chain-hypermarket spec-downgrade finding already recorded in this vault for flooring, arriving from a different trade.**
- **⚠️ Myth one — "we have special supplier access": "нету ни у кого никаких выходов на поставщиков."** Furniture-hardware and panel-material suppliers are well known and few in the country, and nobody buys those materials substantially cheaper. **"Это миф — забудьте."** Only a very large company can obtain a real advantage (not quantified).
- **⚠️ Myth two, and this one runs against his own scale: "если ты крупный производитель, у тебя дешевле — тоже отчасти миф. Нет: содержать большое производство стоит много денег." Scale buys quality, lead time and service — not a lower price.** **A manufacturer stating that his own size does not make him cheaper is the reason the rest of this source is worth trusting.**
- **⚠️ Do not work on 100% prepayment** unless you genuinely know the company and have worked with it before. "Отдали 100% — вот можно забыть про них. Не всегда, но…"

**His own candid note on the industry, recorded as observation: "можете зайти в отзывы почти любой мебельной компании большой — уши в трубку свернутся."** He is not arguing for paranoia — "происходит какая-то жесть — во-первых, она реально происходит, нужно себе в этом отчёт отдавать" — the elementary steps above are what protect you.
