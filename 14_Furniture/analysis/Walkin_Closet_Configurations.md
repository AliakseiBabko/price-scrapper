# Furniture — Walk-In Closet Width by Configuration

Part of [[14_Furniture/Wardrobes_and_Storage|Wardrobes & Storage]].

**Single-sided minimum: 135cm** = 10cm door-trim standoff + 70cm rough opening (for a 60cm door) + 55cm zone depth (finishes to 60cm). Reducible to **125cm** (drop the standoff, if no adjacent wall-trim conflict) or **120cm** (standoff dropped + zone shortened 45-50cm using 30-40cm shelving instead) with a still-usable 60cm aisle. **Maximum before double-sided applies: 145cm** (150cm allows an opposite 30cm shelf; 175cm+ upgrades to a proper double-sided layout — 55cm aisle + two 60cm zones). [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_RyHzFDGgqKA_wardrobe_closet_layout_zones|RyHzFDGgqKA_wardrobe_clo]]]

**Don't shrink a bedroom to carve out a single-sided walk-in** — a plain wardrobe in the same footprint holds the same clothing volume without losing the ~70cm an aisle costs. **Minimum room length to justify one without sacrificing other function: 5m** (at 3m room width) — shorter rooms should default to a plain wardrobe instead. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_Xh7uwbKVmfA_two_wardrobes_beat_walk_in|Xh7uwbKVmfA_two_wardrobe]]]

**Double-sided doors typically swing outward**, except into a narrow corridor, where inward-swing is chosen for safety over comfort. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_RyHzFDGgqKA_wardrobe_closet_layout_zones|RyHzFDGgqKA_wardrobe_clo]]]

**Three-sided minimum leg length: 80cm.** Shorter legs force overlapping zones that become impractically hard to reach. **Overlap counter-clockwise** (right zone placed first, front overlaps it, left overlaps the front) — most people are right-handed, so compressing hung clothes with the stronger hand while retrieving with the free hand is more comfortable; the same logic extends to reaching into far shoe-storage corners. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_RyHzFDGgqKA_wardrobe_closet_layout_zones|RyHzFDGgqKA_wardrobe_clo]]]

**Four-sided minimum width: 260cm** (auxiliary zone left) or **280cm** (auxiliary zone right); 300cm+ opens further configurations, more so above 360cm. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_u28Gqg_V7eE_walkin_access_from_hallway_not_bedroom|u28Gqg_V7eE_walkin_acces]]]

**Worked layout for the common ~1.90×1.05m developer-supplied storage room**: only one configuration is arithmetically workable — 60cm zones on both left and right walls with a shallow (~33cm-deep) shoe rack centered at the far end. L-shaped and back-wall-only layouts both fail (leave only 25-45cm for storage after door-opening/aisle allowances are subtracted). [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_Xh7uwbKVmfA_two_wardrobes_beat_walk_in|Xh7uwbKVmfA_two_wardrobe]]]

**A mirrored walk-in needs a minimum width of 1.7m**: 1m mirror-standing clearance + 60cm clothing zone + 10cm sliding-track allowance. Below that, don't build a walk-in with an internal mirror at all — see [[14_Furniture/analysis/Wardrobe_Common_Mistakes|Common Mistakes]] for why the mirror can't just be skipped or relocated. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_kj503mBQXq8_small_closet_layout|kj503mBQXq8_small_closet]]]
