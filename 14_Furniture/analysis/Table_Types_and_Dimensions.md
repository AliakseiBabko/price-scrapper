# Table Types & Dimensions

## Scope

## The Taxonomy — Two Objects People Conflate

**⚠️ Her central point: "журнальный" and "кофейный" are functionally different objects, and the standard failure is a low table that is simultaneously big *and* tall** — which serves neither purpose and clutters the room.

| | **Журнальный** (low table) | **Кофейный / приставной** (side table) |
|---|---|---|
| **Height** | **≤ 30–35 cm** | **~50 cm and above** |
| **Top size** | Large — ~100 cm on the long side is normal | Small — small diameter or footprint |
| **Mobility** | Largely static, sits in front of the sofa | Mobile, moved to where it's needed |
| **Actually for** | Magazines, remotes, sweets, a fruit bowl; board games played sitting on the floor around it. **Not** for repeatedly setting a mug down | Drinking tea or coffee, and eating. Can go on **both** sides of a sofa — her example is a couple each wanting their own |

- **⚠️ Never let a low table approach the sofa's seat height.** She reports this happening routinely — "ещё чуть-чуть, и он уже будет выше сидушки дивана. Зачем такой?" On one model she says being **15 cm lower** would have fixed it entirely.
- **⚠️ Tables with a massive base are held to the stricter end: 30 cm, 35 cm absolute maximum** — otherwise they read heavyweight. Worst case named: a small living room that also has low ceilings.
- **A `приставной` should be able to slide its base under the sofa**, so the top can be pulled in over your lap. Worth checking the base geometry allows it.

## Pairing Two Tables

**⚠️ Use two, and differentiate them: one bigger and lower, one smaller and higher.** A matched "set" of two similar tables defeats the point, because the point is to cover both functions.

**Her worked sizing**: a **32 cm high × 90 cm diameter** low table plus a **45 cm high** small one.

## Scaling Against the Sofa

**⚠️ A real proportional constraint, not a preference**: a small-diameter side table suits a sofa of roughly **180–190 cm**. Against a **2,400–2,500 mm** sofa the same table reads out of scale — "вопрос несоразмерности."

Cross-reference: sofa sizing itself is covered in [[04_Living_and_Dining_Room/analysis/Living_Room_Layout_and_Furniture_Sizing|Living Room Layout & Furniture Sizing]].

## Market Note (context, not technique)

She states the Russian market for tables and chairs is **markedly weaker in design terms than the upholstered-furniture market**, and characterises the problem in three parts: pieces too "designerly" or bright to be livable; an absence of simple base-level designs to fall back on; and heavy repetition of a few templates — "если это столик, то это обязательно круглая столешница и три или четыре ножки."

Recorded to explain why her brand survey is so negative, and as a practical hint that a plain low table may be easier to have made than to buy.
