### Lighting

| Segment | Reference |
| :--- | :--- |
| Mid | Technical/functional light fixtures: Russia or China-sourced; decorative light: mostly "replicas" (non-original/licensed reproductions) |
| Premium | Technical light starts Russia (higher quality tier) at the entry end, moves to high-end Europe at the top end — key differentiator is optics (lenses, light distribution, scene/effect control); decorative light becomes originals, no replicas |
| Luxury | Top-tier European technical-light brands (source cites a brand rendered as "модулара"/"эрка" — likely ASR-mangled, not resolved) with advanced optics; decorative light from premium contemporary brands (a Portuguese brand rendered "Сирип," a French brand rendered "Роше Бабл" — both ASR-uncertain) or rare/auction-sourced vintage fixtures |

### Furniture (Cabinetry / Soft Furniture)

| Segment | Reference |
| :--- | :--- |

## 3. ASR Uncertainty Notes

Several brand names in §2 are rendered ambiguously or inconsistently by the ASR transcript (Italian/French/English brand names transliterated phonetically into Russian, then auto-captioned back into Russian text) — these are flagged inline above rather than silently "corrected" to a guessed real brand name. Treat every ASR-uncertain brand name as approximate, not a confirmed spelling.

## 4. Relevance to This Project

**Not a direct budgeting benchmark for this project's self-managed plan** — this is turnkey pricing at the top of the market (mid-segment floor alone exceeds this store's previous broadest turnkey figure). Its value here is **calibration**: understanding what a "mid," "premium," or "luxury" quality tier actually buys, category by category, helps interpret where other sources' choices and prices sit on the same spectrum — including this project's own planned self-managed budget, which should be understood as sitting well below even this source's "mid" segment in absolute terms, though the *category structure* (plumbing/tile/flooring/lighting/furniture/kitchen/engineering/textile) is a reasonable checklist regardless of budget level.
