# Technical Supervision (технадзор) — What It Buys, and How It Fails

Part of [[11_Budget_and_Planning/Budgeting_Guide|Renovation Budgeting Master Guide]]. Durable, checkable content on independent technical supervision as a purchased role: what it is for, what it costs you when it goes wrong, and what it must be governed by.

> **⚠️ Keep the two supervision roles distinct throughout.** **Авторский надзор** is the project author's own supervision, and its purpose is *design decisions in flight* — see [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Design_Services_and_Fees|Design Services & Fees]]. **Технадзор** is independent *technical* inspection against a standard. This page is about the second.

## Why it exists — the problem it is bought to solve

**The client cannot read an estimate.** «Человек, для того чтобы разбираться в этой смете, должен быть экспертом» — the average client looking at one quote at 1.8M and another at 2.5M «не понимает вообще, что происходит». **And the designers who are usually asked to arbitrate say plainly that it is not their job: «это должен делать человек, который в этом разбирается».** [source: [[_Sources/YT_SEdNCGA0Ttg_nsdsgn_choosing_builders_roundtable|YT_SEdNCGA0Ttg]]]

What the role actually evaluates, per the same source: **paint category, how the electrics have been executed, what is and is not counted in the estimate, and waste handling.**

**⚠️ The framing that justifies the fee is timing, not fault-finding.** «Хорошо, что у тебя есть технадзор и это выяснилось на этапе, когда у тебя не зашито ничего. А когда у тебя не горит светильник — там уже хуже.» **The value is catching things while nothing is concealed** — which is why the same source says to engage as early as possible, ideally at the first stages, since a supervisor can «мосты построить или наоборот сжечь».

## Where to find one, and why self-supervision does not count

- **Технадзор is essentially the same trade: «это по сути те же строители — компетентный человек».** Found by recommendation.
- **⚠️ Self-supervision is not the same product: «если строитель контролирует сам себя, ты должен очень сильно доверять этому строителю».** Serious construction companies hire a separate supervisor for themselves as an internal audit — which is the tell that it is a real function.
- **⚠️ And an internal supervisor decays, with a stated mechanism and a timescale.** One panellist ran his own in-house технадзор while he was still doing renovations, and **it stopped working after roughly the first six months: «потом они с парами уже там друзья туда-сюда».** The supervisors and the crews became friendly. **A concrete organisational finding, and an argument that independence has to be structural rather than contractual.**

## ⚠️ The failure modes — this is the half that is usually missing

**1. The market selects for adversarialism rather than measurement.**
Mostly ex-прорабы repositioning. Searching Avito he found listings promising **«выбью с ваших строителей все деньги», «уничтожу их»** — selling punishment, not inspection. Recruiting a genuinely good one was «огромная работа».

**2. ⚠️ A worked case where the supervisor's instruction was technically wrong and financially motivated.**
A client-appointed технадзор told the client that **all the electrics should be redone on скрутка (twisted joints) instead of Wago clamps.** Two things about it:

- **The technical rebuttal, which is correct and checkable: under a thermal imager a скрутка has MORE contact area and therefore more heating — «и вот это минус».** The claim that скрутка beats clamps circulates widely and on YouTube; it does not survive measurement.
- **⚠️ The incentive: «поставить на клемму или сделать скрутку — это стоит 500 руб либо 1.000 руб за соединение»** (≈$6 versus ≈$10 per joint, at 90.0251 RUB/USD trailing 6 months before 2024-08-15). **A per-joint cost difference across an entire flat is a large enough sum to explain the advice.**
- He was eventually removed, **but the site stalled meanwhile and the client simply wants to move in.**

**3. «Технадзор может стройку просто не позволить доделать до конца из-за подобных глупостей.»** The role has the power to halt work, and no automatic obligation to weigh that against the client's own interest in finishing.

**4. Corruption is named directly as a reason to vet the supervisor yourself.**

**5. ⚠️ A capacity constraint that makes replacement expensive: they are all busy — two to three months to replace one, and the object stands still meanwhile.** «Это жёсткая реальность.»

## ⚠️ Governance — the fix, and it is a paperwork fix

**Any supervisor's instruction must be formalised**, per the same source:

- **A технадзор journal (журнал), corresponding акты, and a written предписание.**
- **And then a named party — the supervisor, the client, the client's representative or the designer — takes responsibility for that decision in writing.** «Дай нам решение… и человек берёт на себя ответственность. Да ради Бога.»
- **⚠️ Vet the supervisor before he is appointed, and write that right into the contract.** One panellist did exactly this while still a builder: **any технадзор must be agreed with him first**, so that he can meet the person and establish **by what criteria they intend to inspect.** *(The same interview-the-appointee discipline this channel applies to прорабы — see [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Contract_Practice|Contract Practice]].)*

## ⚠️ The criteria problem — "we comply with СНиП" is not a quality claim

**The single most transferable technical point on this page.**

- **СНиП tolerances are loose enough to pass almost anything: «по СНиПу можно сдать любой объект, самые кривые».** The figure he cites is **5 mm per metre** — e.g. across the whole height of a tiled wall — **and he notes developers hand over their objects on exactly that basis.**
- **→ A contractor stating that the work meets СНиП has told you very little.** If acceptance criteria matter, they have to be tighter than СНиП and they have to be written down.
- **The alternatives he names as usable criteria: технические карты** — from the material manufacturer, and versions developed by builders within the market, which he describes as **«такой новый СНиП, обновлённый»** — **and whatever the contract itself specifies.** The chain he wants is: **технадзор refers to the contract, and the contract refers to the criteria.**

## When to change the supervisor — or the crew

The same source is unusually direct: **«не бояться менять бригаду, не бояться менять технадзор, не бояться менять дизайнера»** at some stage if the signals are there. With one exception stated as an absolute:

**⚠️ Changing the DESIGNER mid-project and handing your project to someone else is «безумие»** — «с этим чужим проектом попробовать что-то реализовать это будет безумие». Changing the brigade is hard but he says he does it constantly.

**And the honest limit of supervision as a remedy: «технадзор — это на самом-то деле таблетка».** Where two supervisors disagree — one saying the electrics are fine and the next saying it all has to come out — the client is back to adjudicating between experts.
