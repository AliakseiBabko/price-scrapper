## Bathroom / Materials — Durable Facts & Rules

1. **⚠️ Sharp inside-corner tile cuts on large-format tile create a stress
   concentration point that can crack later, after installation** — when a
   piece is cut to notch around an obstacle (creating an acute/sharp
   internal corner in the cut edge), that sharp point ("острый угол")
   builds up mechanical stress in the slab; the source states this crack
   risk showed up "very rarely" in their own experience but is real enough
   to design around. **Named mechanism**: tension/stress concentrates
   specifically at the sharp point of the cut, not along the straight cut
   edges.
2. **Fix: round the sharp corner with a diamond-core-bit hole, "releasing
   the tension" before the tile is installed**: drill a semicircular hole
   at the sharp corner using a diamond core bit ("алмазная коронка") with
   water cooling, converting the acute-angle cut into a rounded one. This
   is done as a deliberate, named technique — **"снять напряжение"**
   (relieve/release the tension/stress) — not just cosmetic corner
   rounding. Framed as effective ("это действительно работает") based on
   the crew's own repeated use.
3. **Practical execution details for the core-drill technique**: work on a
   rigid, stable surface (a table or stable platform) — the substrate must
   not flex during drilling. A dedicated guide/fixture ("фиксатор") holds
   the core bit in place on the tile surface so it doesn't slide/skate
   during drilling; the crew's own kit covers core-bit diameters from
   **4mm up to 50mm**, sized to the specific corner being relieved.
