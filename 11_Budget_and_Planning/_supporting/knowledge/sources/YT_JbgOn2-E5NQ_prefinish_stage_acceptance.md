## Durable Facts

- **Sequencing**: tile installation should happen BEFORE wallpapering, because tiling is one of the messiest trades — tiling after wallpaper risks ruining the paper with tile-dust spray. If a skilled tiler is unavailable and wallpaper must go up first, protect the paper with plastic film. `confirmed`, speaker's stated practice.
- **Grout timing**: grouting tile joints does not have to happen immediately after laying tile — it can be done any time later. Recommends grouting together with joint sealing (silicone) as one combined step. White grout left ungrouted for a while during ongoing renovation can discolor/darken. `confirmed`.
- **Electrical must be tested under load before the finish stage closes walls**: the breaker panel must be fully assembled and load-tested, and any defective/miswired cables identified and pulled out, BEFORE plastering/tiling — doing this later (at pre-finish) is described as slow, painful, and expensive. `confirmed`.
- **Electric-point coordinates should be verified right when the electrician just finishes rough wiring — not at the pre-finish stage.** If your site supervisor is only checking point coordinates at pre-finish, that's flagged as a sign of a bad supervisor. `confirmed`, presented as a standing rule.
- **Wet-room electric points**: the one electrical item still checked at pre-finish is confirming the tiler cut the tile openings correctly for already-placed electric points (towel warmer cable, mirror-light cable, two outlets at the sink, washer/dryer outlets) — because the tiler has a diamond core bit and the homeowner typically doesn't. `confirmed`.
- **Pre-order and dry-fit towel warmers and mixers on-site before tiling.** Towel warmers have handedness (left/right water connection); mixers have model-specific hole spacing. Without pre-ordering/fitting, installers cut connection points in the wrong spot, forcing a full tile tear-out and re-tiling. `confirmed`, stated as a routine failure mode this practice prevents.
- **A given for large-format/thin-joint tile**: even with quality tile, factory manufacturing tolerance causes visible joint-width variation at 45°-cut corners (joint appears to widen then narrow) — this is a supplier/manufacturing limitation, not evidence of poor installation. `unverified`, speaker's own claim.
- **Wall-flatness inspection standards Q1–Q4**: Q2 finish is sufficient for heavy vinyl or fleece (флизелин) wallpaper; Q3 is generally adequate; Q4 (near-mirror flatness) is needed only for glossy paint, Venetian plaster, or silk-screen ("шелкография") finishes, and costs meaningfully more (business/premium-segment pricing). Demanding Q4 for standard wallpaper is flagged as unnecessary over-spec. `confirmed`, speaker's stated standard.
- **Kitchen tile backsplash prep**: if the backsplash will be tiled, leave that wall area un-primed (plastered only) — tile adhesive won't bond properly to a primed surface. Speaker marks the area "не надо" (don't [prime]) directly on the wall, sometimes twice, to prevent the mistake. `confirmed`.
- **Self-leveling floor (наливной пол) check**: verify flatness with a straightedge across multiple planes before finish flooring goes down. Undetected defects later surface as unmatched seams or a creaking floor, requiring the finish flooring to be pulled up and redone. `confirmed`.
