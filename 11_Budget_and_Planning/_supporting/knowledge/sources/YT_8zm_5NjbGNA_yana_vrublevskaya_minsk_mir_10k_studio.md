## Durable, Broadly-Reusable Facts & Rules (Level 1)

- Buy and install a temporary/used toilet with a flexible connector immediately at the start of rough plumbing work, before any other rough-in — avoids the improvised alternatives (using a neighbor's bathroom, worse) during the weeks the real bathroom is unusable. `confirmed`, concrete and specific.
- Hire an independent new-build handover inspection service rather than self-inspecting when accepting keys on a new-construction apartment — catches defects (the speaker mentions window-related issues specifically) that an untrained buyer would likely miss. `confirmed`, the speaker's own stated practice.
- Riser (stояк) water shutoffs for plumbing modification work go through the building's management company as a paid service, not a free/informal shutoff — budget a small explicit line item for this per riser. `confirmed`.
- White/light-colored wallpaper over plain plastered walls (without a full white-priming pass first) will visibly show through, since the underlying plaster is grey/tan, not white — colored/patterned wallpaper is more forgiving of this and doesn't need the same priming step. `confirmed`, a concrete, checkable finishing-sequence rule not previously in this store.
- A tank-mounted/wall-hung toilet's flush hardware and a large-format textured (rough-surface) tile finish are both cited as regret points post-completion — textured tile is described as difficult to keep visually clean and requires periodic sealing (a specialty порcelain-tile lacquer, ~100 BYN/can, mentioned as the fix under consideration). `single-account`, but a concrete cautionary data point on a material-selection tradeoff (grip/slip-resistance vs. cleanability) not previously recorded in this store.
