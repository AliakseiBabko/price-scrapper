## Doors / Trim (Windows)

- **When direct anchor/dowel fastening is inappropriate: soft base
  materials (aerated concrete/gas-block, foam block)**. Demonstrated live:
  a screwdriver bit drills into aerated concrete with no impact/hammer
  action at all — the material is too soft, and a direct fastener
  (anchor or dowel) simply spins freely under load instead of holding.
  `confirmed`, demonstrated directly.
- **Correct method for soft materials: distributed multi-point mounting
  plates, not a single thicker fastener** — explicitly the inverse of the
  default rule from #007 (there, plates were only for preliminary
  positioning; here, they're the correct permanent fastening method
  specifically because the base material can't hold a single strong
  point). Rationale: load distributed across several fastening points
  holds better in a soft material than one thicker fastener, and is
  harder to defeat by impact (more points to fail simultaneously).
- **Numeric fastening spec for aerated-concrete/foam-block walls**:
  - Vertical frame sections fastened to wood-based structural elements:
    dowel, pre-drilled with an **8mm bit**.
  - Frame sections fastened into foam block: mounting plate on the frame,
    positioned flush against the profile's structural shoulder (better
    wind-load resistance), fastened to the block with **4-7 self-tapping
    screws per plate**.
  - Screw spec into foam block: **90mm length, no dowel/plug** — driven
    directly into the block.
  - **Plate spacing ≤60cm** — a 1.5m frame length typically uses **3
    plates**.
  - Stated comparison: 5× 4mm-diameter screws hold more securely and
    resist impact better than 1× 8mm dowel, in soft material specifically
    (load distribution + more points to defeat under impact).
- **Hardened ("каленый") screw caveat**: hardened screws are normally
  discouraged for window fastening because they're brittle in **hard**
  materials — but this rule doesn't apply in soft materials, since the
  soft material itself absorbs/cushions impact load that would otherwise
  shatter a brittle hardened screw. Demonstrated informally (plate +
  hardened screws into soft material, struck with a hammer/sledgehammer,
  holds).
