## Windows — Durable Facts & Rules (new topic area — no dedicated wiki page exists for this project yet)

- **Zemskov's central claim: PVC window profile brand/model has essentially no effect on window quality within the same market class.** His framing: a profile is just an extruded plastic shape; window quality differences that salespeople attribute to "my profile is better" are, in his view, marketing narrative rather than a real technical differentiator — he states that even he, having seen thousands of profiles, can't visually distinguish one from another once brand stickers are removed.
- **Zemskov's supply-chain explanation for why this myth persists**: profile manufacturer (extrudes the raw profile) → processor/fabricator (buys 6m profile bars, produces finished window units) → dealer (measures, orders from the factory, installs — doesn't manufacture anything) → sometimes a factory's own retail arm sells direct. Per Zemskov, in the market's early days profiles genuinely differed and lacked common standards, which is when brand-differentiation marketing took hold — his claim is that this technical gap has long since closed as standards matured, but the marketing habit persisted past the point where it reflected a real difference.
- **Zemskov's replacement framework: classify by market tier (economy/standard/premium), not by brand.** His claim: real quality differences exist *between* tiers, but not *within* a tier across different brand names.
- **Zemskov's five real quality factors, in his stated order of importance** (all more consequential than profile brand, in his view):
  1. **Sealing gaskets (уплотнительные резинки)** — must stay soft/elastic/springy not just when new, but for decades of service life.
  2. **Hardware (фурнитура)** — the mechanism that lets one handle turn engage 5-6 locking points simultaneously; must withstand a large number of open/close cycles and the full weight of a glazed sash.
  3. **Factory assembly quality** — Zemskov states good windows are assembled at a proper factory on dedicated equipment, not informally ("not in a garage").
  4. **Double-glazing unit (стеклопакет) quality** — even a window with excellent profile, hardware, and gaskets will still frost/condensate if fitted with a poor-quality glazing unit; a good unit is factory-made, hermetically sealed, and clean internally.
  5. **Installation quality — Zemskov's stated single most important factor of all.** His claim: correct installation by skilled hands with good tools is what everything above depends on — without it, even the best window from the best profile will start frosting, sagging out of alignment, or losing its seal within roughly six months of installation, and will start drafting. He states this is why he produces many separate videos/tutorials specifically on installation technique — framed as arming buyers to push back against installers who try to talk over their objections.
