3. **White LDSP for the cabinet carcass/interior** (not matched to the facade color) is an explicit accepted cost-cut — invisible once doors are closed; "don't overthink it" if a plain white interior doesn't bother the client.
4. **⚠️ Minimize built-in appliances for budget**: keep only dishwasher, cooktop, and oven as true built-in units; put the fridge and microwave as external/freestanding units instead — saves on both the appliance itself (freestanding units are typically cheaper) and the cabinetry cost (no matching door panel, hinges, or carcass cutout needed for those two).
