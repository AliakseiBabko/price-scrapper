## Design Concept — The Three Items Worth Recording

- **⚠️ White is not a safe default, because whites carry undertones.** "Белый бывает тёплый, холодный, сероватый и с кремовым подтоном. Если выбрать не тот, комната может выглядеть грязной или мёртвой." Two named failure cases: **a cool white under daylight turns the room office-like; a cool white under warm artificial lighting produces "желтоватый хаос."** His recommendation for a genuinely clean read is **молочный or ivory** rather than a pure white. Corroborates ARCHWOOD's warm-white-for-north-rooms rule (`AKxOtRhIcJY`) and Vittoria Selina's warm-white-ceiling rule (`jJ05zqugmSc`) from a third direction, and adds the specific white-undertone taxonomy.
- **⚠️ Don't paint the ceiling white out of habit.** "Белый потолок не закон. Иногда он делает комнату визуально короче." Two named alternatives: **paint the ceiling the same colour as the walls** (especially with calm tones) so the space reads whole without a cutting contrast, or **go one tone darker than the walls** for depth and an enveloping feel. Independently corroborates the same-colour "коробочка" technique from ARCHWOOD (`AKxOtRhIcJY`) and, in the one-tone-darker variant, points the same way as that source's contrast-scaled lowering rule.
- **⚠️ Colour as a zoning instrument in place of physical partitions**: "не обязательно ставить перегородки, чтобы разделить комнаты на зоны. Цвет справится с этим лучше" — a kitchen distinguished by a different wall shade or tile, a rest zone by a soft rug or contrasting sofa, a work spot by an accent wall. Framed explicitly as "цвет — это инструмент планировки, просто он визуальный." Worth recording because this vault's zoning material (`Functional_Zoning_and_Furniture_Arrangement.md`, the Room-Zoning Partition Methods reference) is built almost entirely around physical mechanisms — slats, curtains, shelving, sliding glass — and does not currently list colour as a zoning method at all.
