## Design Concept / Furniture — Durable Facts & Rules

- **Core mechanism**: two side mounting plates fixed to the vertical
  cabinet side walls, plus a pivoting plate carrying the bed frame,
  actuated by **two gas-spring struts rated 200N each** in this build.
- **⚠️ Gas-strut force-rating selection rule, with a real failure mode**:
  the required strut force depends on bed width, mattress weight, AND
  whether a front facade panel is added to the bed frame. An
  under-rated strut (sized for a "light" bed configuration that turns
  out heavier once assembled) means the bed doesn't fully lower under
  its own weight and hangs partway open until a person's body weight
  pulls it the rest of the way down — a real selection risk, not just a
  theoretical one.
- **⚠️ Real cross-installation reliability finding — daily use correlates
  with a specific failure point**: the cabinet body material at the
  mounting-plate attachment point cracks/splits over time under repeated
  stress. Source specifically flags **16mm particleboard (ДСП) body
  thickness as too thin** at this connection point and recommends either
  a thicker panel or metal reinforcement plates on the back panel at that
  location. **Dose-response pattern observed across 3 real
  installations**: her own bed (used rarely) had not failed at time of
  filming; two neighbors' units, used **daily**, developed this crack
  first — a genuine reliability data point tied to use frequency, not
  just material choice alone. Compare against `YT_Gy68fsIdDLY`'s
  independent claim that a steel-tube frame (vs. this ДСП-panel
  construction) avoids this exact weak point.
- **Hardware kit contents**: folding/concealable legs with a hinge/lug
  that tuck away automatically as the bed lifts closed, so nothing
  obstructs closing; three sets of mattress/bedding retention
  straps/holders distributed along the sleeping-surface length to keep
  the mattress and bedding from sliding or falling forward when the unit
  is raised to closed position.
