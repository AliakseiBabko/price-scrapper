## Mistakes / Warnings — Client Product-Substitution Cost, and Exclusive-Material Lead Time

9. **Real cost consequence of a client substituting a switch/outlet
   brand against the design spec**: the client bought a different
   manufacturer's switches/outlets than the ones specified in the design
   project; that brand turned out to have no vertical-mount option at
   all, making the covers incompatible with the planned orientation —
   result: ~85,000 RUB ≈$1,100 of installed work effectively wasted,
   requiring a search for a replacement/adaptation. **Rule reinforced
   with a real number**: verify a substituted material/fixture's
   physical compatibility against the design spec before buying, not
   just its price or appearance.
10. **Exclusive/custom-order material lead-time risk, independent
    brigade retention caution**: an exclusive-collection quartz-parquet
    color took **6 months** to arrive after ordering. The source's
    explicit planning advice: build known long lead times into the
    project schedule up front, for both clients and builders — and warns
    that an independently-hired private crew (not a company with salaried
    staff) may not return to finish after a gap that long, since crew
    members often pick up other, closer/more convenient jobs during an
    extended wait. A herringbone-pattern flooring install itself was also
    flagged as inherently slower (2-3× a standard install) and less
    error-tolerant — a placement error as small as half a millimeter
    compounds to a ~1.5cm misalignment by the far end of a run.

## Rules / Heuristics — Proactive Raking-Light Prep Check

11. **Raking-light ("под прожектор") technique applied proactively during
    wall-prep, not only as a final QC check**: drywall niche surfaces are
    puttied, primed, re-puttied, and sanded under a work light during
    prep specifically so that once the real accent/directional lighting
    is installed later, no priming/puttying defect is newly revealed by
    it — a proactive variant of this store's already-documented raking-
    light QC check (previously recorded as a post-completion inspection
    technique, not a prep-stage discipline).
