## Mistakes / Warnings — Two Reusable Workshop Lifehacks (the only material extracted)

- **A simple lifehack to keep a hanging tool from resting against a bare
  screw's sharp thread on a tool-storage panel**: cut a short segment
  from a length of medical IV/drip tubing (available cheaply at a
  pharmacy) and thread it over the screw before driving it in — the tool
  then rests on the smooth plastic tube instead of the screw's sharp
  thread, preventing it from scratching or wearing against the metal
  over repeated use. `single-account`, a minor but genuinely reusable
  technique.
- **A tool-silhouette outlining technique using aluminum foil and spray
  paint, as an alternative to tracing with a marker**: wrap the tool
  tightly in food-grade aluminum foil, press it against the mounting
  surface, then spray paint over the wrapped tool from above; once dry
  and the foil is removed, a clean outline remains on the surface with no
  paint transferred onto the tool itself. `single-account`.
