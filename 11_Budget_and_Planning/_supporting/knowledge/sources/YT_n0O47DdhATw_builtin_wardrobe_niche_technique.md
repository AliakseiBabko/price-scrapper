## Furniture / Built-ins — Durable Facts & Rules

- **Built-in wardrobes are cheaper and more practical than free-standing ("corpus") ones**: floor-to-ceiling use is more space-efficient; junction detail is simpler (only a baseboard-to-door and a bottom rail, vs. a full boxed cabinet needing dust-cleaning behind it); and cost is lower because only the sliding door panels are purchased, not side/back/top/bottom panels. **Directly corroborates `VgsPDOcPV7c`'s built-in-vs-corpus framing** with a real project's stated reasoning. `confirmed`.
- **Technique for creating a built-in-wardrobe niche where none exists**: recess the room's entry-zone wall inward (this project's case: 72-78cm) to create a wall niche exactly sized for a built-in wardrobe with sliding doors — converts what would otherwise require a bulky free-standing unit into a flush built-in. `confirmed`, real technique with as-built measurements.
- **When recessing a wall for a wardrobe niche interacts with an adjacent balcony/loggia door opening, the opening may need repositioning** to keep the sliding-door track plane clear of the door swing/reveal — worked example: shifted a balcony-door opening 18cm one direction and widened the other side by 34cm, preserving a functional balcony-door clear width (85cm, stated as "more than enough") while aligning the wardrobe's door-track plane correctly. `confirmed`, real dimensioned example.
- **A wall niche recess converts an exposed exterior corner into a protected one once boxed with a wardrobe door front** — flagged as a design plus beyond the storage itself (an exposed outside corner is vulnerable to impact/wear; once enclosed by cabinetry it no longer takes that wear). `confirmed`, secondary benefit of the same technique.
- **Precompute the wardrobe sliding-door rail width and its wall-junction detail independent of which side the door panel will ultimately occupy** — described as what separates "a real [engineered] project" from "a designer's rendering": every junction, clearance, and possible door-plane variant is modeled and dimensioned in advance so nothing needs improvised correction during construction. `confirmed`, a general planning-discipline principle stated explicitly, not just implied.
