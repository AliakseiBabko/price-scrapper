## Materials / Mistakes-Warnings — Tile-Crack-Avoidance Technique Around Door Openings ("Pseudo-Jamb" Tile Detail)

- **⚠️ Named failure mode and its fix**: the conventional way to tile around a door opening is to wrap a single piece of tile around the door reveal in an L- or U-shaped ("Г-" or "П-образный") cut — the speaker states directly that **any L- or U-shaped cutout in a tile slab carries a real risk of cracking at the internal corner** (a classic stress-concentration failure in a brittle material with an inside-corner cutout). **Fix**: build a "pseudo-jamb" (`псевдоналичник`) instead — the door reveal/jamb area is tiled as **its own separate piece**, mitered at 45° where it meets the field tile, rather than being cut as one continuous L/U-shaped piece with the field tile.
