## Numeric Data — Real Object Cost Breakdown, Rough Stage, 2024

1. **Headline total**: **1,122,000 RUB for the entire rough stage**
   (≈$12,400, trailing 6-month USD/RUB average ending 2024-07-21).
2. **Demolition stage, itemized into three sub-costs, genuinely new
   granularity vs. this store's existing demolition content**: full
   interior demolition (every non-load-bearing wall removed, down to
   the bare shell) labor = **250,000 RUB (≈$2,800)**; debris removal
   = **60,000 RUB (≈$700)**; welding work (a new balcony parapet,
   rebuilt with improved rigidity, replacing the old glazing and
   parapet that were also removed) = **72,000 RUB (≈$800)**. The
   client's own reaction to the demolition labor price alone is used
   as the video's hook ("вы рехнулись, почему так дорого?" — framed
   directly, not edited out).
3. **Wall-building + plastering, combined billing line: ≈250,000 RUB
   (≈$2,800)**. New partition walls built (layout was changed);
   gypsum plaster applied at ≈4cm thickness; reinforcing mesh used
   throughout specifically because the underlying substrate material
   couldn't be confirmed reliably on this object ("неизвестно какой
   материал" — an explicit, practical reason for defaulting to mesh
   reinforcement even where it might not otherwise be strictly
   required); primed with a concentrate-diluted primer before
   plastering.
4. **Electrical rough-in: 140,000 RUB (≈$1,500)**. Ceiling-routed
   wiring; two internet-cable runs pulled per the client's own request;
   an intercom cable run left available at the building entrance;
   panel contents: main breaker, a voltage relay, per-room breakers
   (kitchen, room, corridor), a separate breaker for the AC, and a
   **separate breaker explicitly for the leak-protection system**
   (named directly: **"Нептун"/Neptun**) — reminder given on-camera not
   to skip this. Named appliance circuits: bathroom, cooktop,
   refrigerator, washing machine, water heater, oven, and a
   high-power dishwasher.
5. **Plumbing rough-in: 150,000 RUB (≈$1,700)**. Bathroom scope: an
   access hatch (люк) for a paint-finish ceiling, provisions for
   washer + dryer, shutoff valves, a water-pressure regulator, angled
   filters, water meters, a water-hammer arrestor, a leak-protection
   system connection, and two manifolds (hot and cold). Also within
   this stage: bathroom waterproofing, boxing-in of the toilet
   installation frame, and finishing the access hatch installation.
6. **Screed: 9,000 RUB (≈$100)** — semi-dry screed poured over an
   underfloor soundproofing layer (named: "Шуманет"), following a
   measured layout for the pour.
