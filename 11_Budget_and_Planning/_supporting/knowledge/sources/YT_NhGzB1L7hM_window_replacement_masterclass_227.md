## Doors/Trim (Windows) — Old-Window Removal Sequence

- **Unpack the new window first and place a foam board between it and the wall** to protect both during old-window removal.
- **Remove sashes from the old window first, before extraction**, to lighten it for handling.
- **Tape a cracked/broken glazing unit with reinforced tape before further handling**, to prevent glass falling out.
- **Safety check before pulling the top hinge pin**: always verify the handle is in the "closed" position first — if it's in "open" position, the sash can fall on the installer.
- **Remove all fasteners (screws, anchor plates) from the old frame before cutting the surrounding foam** — cutting foam first means the saw blade repeatedly strikes leftover fasteners, causing extra rework.
- **Unscrew an exterior sill/drip-cap before extracting the frame**, unless it's structurally integrated into a ventilated-facade system (in which case leave it in place).
- **Use a specialized pry tool to break the frame free from its seated position** before removal — without it, extraction is much harder.
- **Clean out all leftover foam/debris from the rough opening** after old-window removal, to properly form the new installation seam.

## Doors/Trim (Windows) — New-Window Installation Masterclass

- **Restore the exterior-side weatherproofing tape/membrane** around the opening before installing the new window — protects the installation foam seam from precipitation, wind, and UV.
- **Add sound-insulation material and light foam fill under a retained exterior drip-cap/sill specifically to dampen rain noise** — flagged as a step junior/inexperienced installers commonly skip or don't know about; demonstrated audible before/after difference.
- **Remove sashes and glazing units from fixed ("dead") window sections before installing the new frame**, to reduce handling weight — factory glazing beads on fixed sections are typically left not-fully-seated specifically to make this pre-install removal easier.
- **Fastener type by wall material**: through-fasteners (pass through the frame directly into the wall, requiring a pre-drilled hole in the frame) vs. anchor plates (mounted to the frame edge, self-tapping/drilling screw) — choice depends on wall material.
- **Stated "Zems standard" policy update**: previously only direct/through fastening was permitted; anchor plates are now allowed specifically for soft substrates (e.g. aerated concrete/gas-block), provided they're spaced closer together than the base GOST standard requires.
- **Anchor-plate spacing spec: maximum 700mm between plates along the frame perimeter; 150-180mm from each internal corner.** `confirmed`, explicit numeric spec.
- **Always remove the factory protective film before setting the frame in the opening** — an easy step to forget.
- **Positioning/leveling sequence**: set the frame on support blocks/shims in the opening, use a laser level on an elevation tripod for a horizontal reference, level the bottom of the frame to horizontal first and temporarily fix it at two points, then plumb the frame vertically on both axes (front-to-back and left-right).
- **Anchor-plate fixing**: one end to the frame, the other end to the wall with a minimum of two fastening points.
- **Exterior trim fitting**: attach the drip cap to the new frame after leveling; cover any gap between a ventilated-facade system and the window's outer plane with bent, facade-color-matched metal trim, then seal the trim-to-window joint with sealant.
- **Foam-sealing technique**: keep support blocks/shims in place during and after foaming (needed for proper cure against a stable frame). Apply foam in **at least two passes** (more/thicker for a thicker frame profile), using a **zigzag gun motion** for denser, more even fill — produces a multi-layer seam.
- **Interior vapor-barrier tape sequencing**: apply an interior-side vapor-barrier tape around the full perimeter (protects the foam seam from interior room humidity) — apply after the first foam pass, do the second foam pass, let it cure, then final-fix the vapor tape.
- **Final assembly order**: reinstall the glazing unit into the central fixed section and sashes, install handles, install decorative hinge covers, then perform final sash adjustment/calibration, and install insect screens with upper retention clips positioned on the sides specifically so wind doesn't blow the screen off.
