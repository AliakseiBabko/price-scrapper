## Plumbing

Konstantin Kruglov / Ontario says (level 1, spoken directly):

- **⚠️ Manifold ("коллекторная гребёнка"/коллектор) two-function framing, stated explicitly, plus a hard buying rule**: (1) pressure isolation — flushing a toilet shouldn't cause a drop that could scald someone showering elsewhere; (2) serviceability — a single fixture (the source's example: a toilet) can be isolated and repaired/replaced without shutting off water to the rest of the apartment. **Explicit buying rule: if a plumbing quote doesn't include a manifold, walk away — no modern plumbing layout is built without one.**
- **⚠️ Automatic ball valve (as used with a leak-protection system) reset workflow, stated precisely**: sensor detects water → signal to the control module → module signals the valve closed. To reopen: the user presses a reset button, the module first checks whether its sensors are still wet; only if they read dry does it send the open signal — the valve does not simply reopen on a timer or a blind button press.
- **⚠️ Water meter: pulse-output type transmits readings automatically (e.g. to the management company)**, distinct from a standard non-pulse meter — an option worth knowing when replacing/choosing a meter. **Replacing a water meter requires inviting the management company (or ЖЭК) to reseal the new one** — meter work is stated as something that must happen under management-company oversight, not purely a private plumber decision.
- **⚠️ Check valve placement rule, generalized beyond the hot/cold-riser case already in this store**: always install a check valve at the inlet (both hot and cold), and additionally at specific downstream devices where a reverse-flow risk exists — named examples: pump equipment (reverse flow can damage a pump), a storage water heater/boiler, and a hygienic shower/bidet spray.
- **⚠️ Pressure reducer mechanism and old-building-noise note**: a reducer only ever reduces pressure — it cannot raise a low input pressure. Framed benefits beyond fixture protection: it mitigates the pipe-noise/gurgling ("гул") sometimes heard in older buildings' risers, and at very high pressure (6-10 atm) it prevents a tap's water stream from being uncomfortably "sliced" by excess force — both framed as comfort/water-conservation benefits distinct from the equipment-protection rationale already recorded in this store.
- **⚠️ Optional solenoid valve for a manual one-button seasonal switchover, with a spoken Moscow example**: a solenoid (electromagnetic) valve, distinct from the leak-protection system's own automatic valve, can be wired to a simple switch (e.g. mounted in the utility/plumbing cabinet) to switch the water path with one button press instead of manually opening/closing multiple valves or connecting a bypass — the source's own stated use case is switching over to a storage water heater during a scheduled summer hot-water outage, citing Moscow as an example city where such scheduled outages occur.
