## Regulatory / Legal — A 2025 Russian Law Allowing Developers to Set Their Own Finish-Quality Standards

- **⚠️ Named legal change, effective 2025-03-01**: a federal bill (transcript renders the number inconsistently — "4985-8" / "49-8", `ASR-uncertain` exact digits) passed its second and third readings allowing developers to apply their own internal quality standards for apartment finish work, rather than being held to a single universal government norm — the bill nominally requires developer standards to meet Minstroy Russia's minimum requirements, but (per this video) the practical effect is that developers can set their own numeric tolerances as long as they're framed as meeting that minimum.
- **⚠️ Warranty period shortened, apartment finish work specifically**: the standard minimum warranty period on apartment finish work was cut from **3 years to 1 year** in the same legislative package; a developer may voluntarily offer a longer warranty, but the video argues (as opinion, not fact) that there's no real incentive for one to do so.
- **⚠️ Damages-claim cap introduced**: a buyer's claimable compensation for finish work that fails to meet the developer's own stated standard is now capped at **3% of the contract price**, unless a higher cap is separately specified in the purchase contract — combined with generously loose tolerance tables (below), the video argues this makes it arithmetically difficult for a buyer to even establish a qualifying defect, let alone recover a meaningful sum.
- **⚠️ Stated industry-scale rationale for the lobbying effort behind this law, cited as the trigger**: Russian developers collectively lost **≈150 billion RUB (≈$1.56 billion)** to buyer lawsuits over substandard finish work in the prior year (2024); PIK alone is stated to have paid out **over 24 billion RUB (≈$249 million)** in 2024 on such claims. `single-account`, not independently verified against a primary source here, but presented as the direct financial motive for the standard being loosened rather than construction practices being improved.

## Regulatory / Legal — PIK's Own Published Finish-Work Tolerance Tables (a Real Developer Standard, Direct Read-Through)

**⚠️ All figures below are this video's own stated reading of PIK's named internal standard document (СТО 4032-777-001-2024), not independently retrieved here.** Presented as concrete numeric tolerance ceilings a major developer's own document permits for handover-condition finish work in a "with finish" apartment:

- **Plaster/wall flatness (Table 2)**: vertical-plane deviation up to **20mm per 1m, capped at 40mm over a wall's full height** — i.e. a wall can legally be out of plumb by up to 4cm top-to-bottom under this standard. Surface defects (cracks, tool marks, gouges) up to **20mm deep** are permitted as long as the finish layer's structural integrity holds.
- **Tile/cladding installation (Table 3)**: verticality deviation up to **10mm per 1m, capped at 30mm over full height**; flatness (2m-straightedge gap) up to **40mm**; joint-line deviation from vertical/horizontal up to 10mm per 1m of row length; joint-width variance ±3mm; **up to 30% of a room's tiled surface area may show cracks/chips/defects**; grout cracking/loss permitted up to **10 linear meters** per room's tiled area. **⚠️ This 40mm-straightedge-gap figure is a direct, quantified contrast with this store's existing 2mm/2m-straightedge acceptance norm from this same channel's own Round 2 source (`AnBRspXdtZc`)** — the channel's own crews are held (by their own stated practice) to a tolerance 20× tighter than what a major developer's own published standard permits for a "with finish" handover.
- **Paint (Table 4)**: surface defects (drips, unpainted patches, streaks) up to **60 sq cm per 4 sq m** of painted surface (a second painted-surface table cited up to 50 sq cm per 4 sq m); tool marks and gouges up to **10mm deep** permitted.
- **Wallpaper (Table 5)**: air bubbles, wrinkles, delamination, and stains up to **60 sq cm per 4 sq m**; seam misalignment, seam gaps, and seams landing in internal/external corners are all explicitly permitted by this standard.
- **Laminate flooring (Table 9)**: floor-slope deviation up to **1.5% of the room's corresponding dimension (≈15mm per 1m), capped at 30mm** across a room; height mismatch between adjacent boards up to **6mm**; gap width between adjacent boards up to **10mm**.
- **Floor tile/porcelain (Table 10)**: surface flatness (2m straightedge) up to **12mm**; step/height mismatch between adjacent tiles up to **4mm**; joint width up to **10mm**; up to **30% of a room's tiled floor area** may show cracks/chips/adhesive residue/grout defects.
- **Baseboard/PVC threshold installation (Table 11)**: gap between baseboard and floor/wall under deformation load up to **10mm**.
- **Interior doors (Table 13)**: gap at horizontal/vertical trim (наличник) junctions up to **3mm** (fillable with sealant, color of sealant not specified/regulated); gap between trim and floor **not regulated at all** (trim may hang short of the floor); the door-leaf-to-frame closing gap ("притвор") **not regulated at all**, as long as the latch still functions; up to **10 scratches, total combined length up to 500mm**, are permitted on a single door leaf; warping of the door leaf/frame is permitted as long as it doesn't impair the locking mechanism's function.
- **Entrance doors**: mechanical damage to hardware (handles, hinges, lock, peephole) is permitted as long as it doesn't impair the door assembly's function — explicitly including, per the video's own reading, a missing peephole with just an open hole, as long as the door still closes and locks.
- **Windows**: out-of-plumb installation up to **4mm per 1m, capped at 8mm** over the unit's full height; defects are to be assessed **visually, unaided, from 0.6-1m away** under natural light (no instrument-based measurement required or expected); scratches/chips up to 1mm are unregulated entirely, up to 6mm permitted (up to 10 per unit), and up to 30mm permitted (up to 2 per unit) — the video's own conclusion: a window that is already borderline-defective, or even a used/secondhand unit, could plausibly be installed and pass this standard.
