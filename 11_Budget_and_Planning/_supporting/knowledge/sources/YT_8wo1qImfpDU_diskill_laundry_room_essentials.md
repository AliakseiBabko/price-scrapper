- **Full-size washing machine is the baseline, and multi-story houses need one per floor**: for a house with 3+ bedrooms, a single washing machine gets overloaded by bedding volume alone; the source explicitly recommends installing a washing machine on **every floor** of a two-story-plus house (200m²+), based on what her own clients who did this report back positively. - **Dryer myth rebuttal — clothes wrinkle only if left in the dryer too long, not from the dryer itself**: the source states that the common objection ("dryers wrinkle clothes") is a timing issue, not a technology flaw — pull items out promptly after the cycle ends. States her clients who were convinced to buy a dryer report daily gratitude for the decision. Also notes a hygiene angle not mentioned in this project's existing dryer content: the dryer's own lint/fur collection tray fills with a substantial amount of lint/pet-hair debris after every cycle — material that would otherwise be airborne/inhaled if line-dried indoors — framed as a health argument for owning a dryer beyond convenience. - **Chest-height mounting for washer/dryer, elderly-accessibility rationale**: a non-obvious layout tip the source attributes to a client's own suggestion — washer and dryer don't have to sit on the floor; raising them to chest height avoids bending over, which she flags as especially useful for older household members. - **Laundry basket subdivision, two distinct rules**: (1) use **several** baskets rather than one large bin, including narrow baskets pre-labeled by the manufacturer by color, specifically to avoid having to dig through a mixed pile hunting for one white sock; (2) **separate adult and children's laundry into different baskets** — framed as both more hygienic and more convenient (not just organizational).
