## Numeric Data / Cost Drivers — Real Rough-Stage Case (45 m², 2022)

1. **Headline total, most reliable figure in this source**: **1,424,000
   RUB spent on the rough stage alone** (≈$19,400, trailing 6-month
   USD/RUB average ending 2022-01-27) for a **45 m² apartment**
   (measured by floor area), rough ceiling height (before screed)
   ~3m. **≈31,644 RUB/m² (≈$430/m², same conversion basis)** —
   Turnkey/Full-Service delivery model, level-2 region. This is a
   genuinely new $/m² benchmark for this store — not corroborating an
   existing figure, since it's this channel's own first case study
   processed.
2. **Project context**: renovation follows a paid design ("проект"),
   originally commissioned together with construction from a different
   company whose execution fell through — client then hired
   Petrishin-Stroi to execute the existing design. Layout: kids room +
   bedroom separated from the kitchen zone by a sliding partition;
   combined bathroom/WC with wall-hung toilet, hygienic shower, washer,
   and dryer. **Client's explicit stated priorities**: perfectly flat
   walls at true 90° corners, best-available material quality, and
   especially reliable engineering systems (electrical, plumbing,
   heating) — driven by the client's own **prior bad experience of
   flooding a neighbor**, a concrete example of a past-incident driving
   an engineering-reliability priority.
3. **Act 1 — demolition, new partitions, plastering: ≈200,000 RUB**
   (≈$2,700, same conversion basis). Work: removed one developer-built
   row of foam/gas block (layout was being changed); covered
   (kept, not replaced) windows with protective film; opened and
   foam-sealed wall-to-ceiling junctions; built ≈40 m² / ≈5 linear
   meters of new gas-block partition wall; plastered ≈120 m² / ≈25
   linear meters of wall with gypsum plaster. **Genuinely new
   billing-mechanism fact**: sections where the plastering layer
   thickness exceeded the standard norm were billed at an **added
   coefficient**, not a flat per-m² rate — a real example of how
   "crooked walls needing thicker plaster" becomes a directly billed
   cost driver, not just a vague warning.
4. **Act 2 — electrical rough-in: ≈170,000 RUB** (≈$2,300, same
   conversion basis). ≈60 socket-box points installed; a 48-module
   power distribution panel; a separate low-voltage panel for LED-strip
   transformer, router, and TV-signal combiner; an instant water
   heater and a leak-protection system both electrically connected;
   wiring routed via the ceiling.
5. **Act 3 — plumbing/heating rough-in, itemized scope** (cost figures
   below are `ASR-uncertain` — digit groups in the auto-generated
   transcript are garbled and could not be reliably reconstructed;
   recorded as approximate, not to be treated as exact): 7 fixture
   points (kitchen: sink + dishwasher; bathroom: sink, tub, washing
   machine, toilet, dryer). Manifold assembly ("гребёнка") built with a
   named Oventrop shutoff valve, a leak-protection valve, a water
   pressure regulator, a check valve, a mandatory fine filter, and a
   water-hammer arrestor — the same arrestor treatment also applied to
   the cold-water riser. Instant water heater installed; hygienic
   shower fed through a thermostatic mixer housed in a utility cabinet
   (hot/cold blend to a set comfort temperature before reaching the
   fixture). Toilet installed on a wall-mount frame with the hygienic
   shower/thermostat. Underfloor soundproofing (named: "Шуманет
   Combi"-type product, ASR-uncertain exact name) laid before heating
   pipes, which were then wrapped in automotive sound-insulating tape
   along with radiator replacement. `ASR-uncertain` cost figures given
   on-screen for this stage's labor, materials+delivery+floor-lift, and
   organizational costs were not reliably parseable from the
   auto-generated transcript and are deliberately not extracted as
   numbers here — the reliable, cross-checkable total remains the
   1,424,000 RUB headline figure in item 1.
