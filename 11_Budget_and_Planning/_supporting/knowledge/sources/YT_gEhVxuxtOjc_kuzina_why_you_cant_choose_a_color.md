## Design Concept — Colour Choice as a Cultural Problem, Not an Aesthetic One

- **⚠️ Core framing — the buyer is solving two problems at once, and only one of them is conscious**: a *rational* task (compatibility, lighting, the rules of working with color — which is why people build mood boards, do выкрасы, and pile up references) and a *cultural* one, which is the actual source of the paralysis and which the person rarely recognizes. The stated symptom is looking at an already-approved palette and still feeling "что-то не так."
- **⚠️ Two physically-related colors can read as different worlds**: an interior in terracotta shades vs. one in cool-leaning reds are "родственники внутри одной цветовой семьи," but the first reads warm/ecological/nostalgic and the second conceptual/active/aggressive. The claim is that the reaction is to the *cultural association*, not to the wavelength.
- **⚠️ The historical argument (attributed to color historian Michel Pastoureau)**: "цвет- явление прежде всего социальное." Worked example given — in the Middle Ages the most common clothing pairing was **red+green**, perceived as almost monochrome rather than contrasting, while **yellow+green** was read as hellish kitsch fit for a jester or for forcibly dressing a criminal. Today the readings are reversed, because the operative model changed from Aristotle's color sequence to Newton's physics and Itten's wheel. Recorded because it is a concrete, checkable historical counterexample to the assumption that harmony rules are physical constants.
- **⚠️ Three named mechanisms by which cultural context enters an interior**, presented as the designer's working model:
  1. **"Образы среды" (environment images)** — a color cites a specific kind of place or period. Named instances: green → an old library; whitewashed lime walls → the Mediterranean; red-with-gold → palace aesthetics or China; white-with-gold → classicism; light wood + white → Scandinavia. Her own worked project: a brief for "простота и нейтральность" delivered almost entirely through oak-tone wood plus white, with almost no other color, which reads unmistakably as сканди/эко purely from the color decision.
  2. **"Социальные коды" (social codes)**, citing Bourdieu — "вкус классифицирует не только вещи, но и того, кто их выбирает." Named market evidence: Armani Casa, Fendi Casa and Gianfranco Ferré Casa all build on the same family of complex neutrals — warm grays and gray-beiges, lime plasters, natural stone in muted tones — which is offered as the actual reason those palettes read as expensive. **The stated mechanism is exposure, not physiology**: "дело не в том, что человеческий глаз физиологически предпочитает серо-бежевый цвет" — those shades simply recur in luxury visual culture, while simple saturated colors recur in advertising, packaging, mass retail and children's goods. **Named trap: a status palette is not automatically a personal one.**
  3. **"Личная память" (personal memory)** — said to be the strongest of the three. Same olive: a trip through Tuscany for one person, a district clinic's walls for another, a grandfather's jacket for a third.
- **⚠️ Direct methodological consequence, and a direct contradiction of the per-color-association genre**: she states she treats universal advice such as "оливковый успокаивает," "оранжевый бодрит," "бежевый делает интерьер дорогим" **with caution**, because the same color produces different emotions in different people for biographical rather than perceptual reasons. This conflicts head-on with the per-hue psychology table already on this project's `Color_Palette_and_Material_Direction.md` (Sergey Kodolov, `T-W5qFAVG9o`) — route as a Perspectives block.
- **⚠️ Her stated intake sequence, a concrete process rule**: she does **not** open with "какой цвет вам нравится." She reads the client's references to infer style and gamut, then interviews in this order — **atmosphere first, then style, and only then the specific color**. The stated reason is that the same blue "рассказывает совершенно разные истории" depending on what surrounds it, so the color is the last decision, not the first.
- **Closing position**: "здесь нет правильного цвета. Есть только цвет, который поддерживает именно вашу историю" — a person stuck on color is choosing between several versions of themselves, not between two samples.
