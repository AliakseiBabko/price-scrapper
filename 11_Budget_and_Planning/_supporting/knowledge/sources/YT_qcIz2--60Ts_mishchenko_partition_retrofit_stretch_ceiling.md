## Design Concept / Furniture — Durable Facts & Rules

- **⚠️ Structural anchor-point warning, generalizable to any partition
  type added under an existing stretch ceiling**: don't anchor a new
  partition's top framing to the stretch-ceiling membrane/track itself —
  it will wobble ("шататься"). The correct anchor point is the actual
  structural (raw/"black") ceiling behind the membrane, reached by
  cutting into the finished stretch ceiling at the partition's location.
- **⚠️ Membrane-cutting-and-re-welting retrofit technique**: cut the
  stretch-ceiling membrane along the new partition's planned path; glue a
  new pre-fabricated "harpoon" welt strip ("гарпун," bought to length
  from a ceiling-material supplier — buy with margin) along the cut edge
  using a cyanoacrylate-type adhesive ("Супер момент" or "Cosmofen");
  start gluing from the center of the run outward, pre-forming corners
  before full gluing.
- **Membrane-handling technique during the cut**: partially pull the
  membrane out of the *existing* perimeter track only near the work zone
  (not the whole room) to have room to maneuver without tearing it; use a
  heat gun to soften the film/harpoon for easier extraction and later
  reinsertion; the membrane can also be pushed out from inside the newly
  cut gap.
- **Sequence**: remove the section of the old ceiling track blocking the
  new partition's path → install the new partition framing/profile →
  run a new track/baguette along the new partition's own face to receive
  the rejoined membrane piece.
- **Two corner-construction methods for the new welt strip**: (a) a more
  durable method — fold and pre-glue the harpoon pieces together at the
  corner before attaching to the membrane; (b) a simpler method — cut the
  harpoon to length at the corner and glue only the rear portion, trimmed
  flush — easier, but a structurally weaker joint. Source demonstrates
  both, states the tradeoff explicitly.
- **⚠️ Real material-age risk and a documented mitigation**: an OLDER
  stretch-ceiling membrane (one that's been installed a long time before
  being cut and reheated) may no longer shrink/stretch back to a fully
  taut finish — expect visible wrinkles near the new seam on old
  material. A newer or less-aged membrane (this source's own test piece,
  ~1 year old) still stretched cleanly. **Mitigation**: cover any
  resulting wrinkle area with ceiling trim/molding ("плинтус") rather
  than relying on a perfectly taut re-stretch.
- **Real labor-time data point**: this whole retrofit (cutting, re-
  welting, reinstalling the partition, re-stretching) was close to a
  full day of work for one person, using a heat gun/space heaters (2×
  2kW) to soften and re-stretch the membrane.
