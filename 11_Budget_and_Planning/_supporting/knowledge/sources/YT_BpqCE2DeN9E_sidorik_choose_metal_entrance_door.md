## Doors / Trim

- **Apartment-door vs. house-door construction, a distinction not yet
  recorded on the wiki page**: a door meant for a standalone house (facing
  the street/exterior directly) needs weather/UV/moisture resistance a
  building-corridor apartment door doesn't — moisture-resistant MDF facing,
  zinc-coated metal (rust prevention), a stainless-steel threshold cap
  (vs. a polymer threshold on an apartment door, available as a paid
  option), a multi-layer insulation stack (foam + foil-faced polyethylene +
  an air gap, with stiffening ribs deliberately kept from touching the MDF
  to avoid cold bridges), a thermal-break frame, and sometimes a glazed
  insert. An apartment door instead prioritizes soundproofing and is filled
  fully with mineral wool. **Selection-criteria priority stated explicitly
  by the speaker**: for an apartment — (1) security, (2) soundproofing,
  (3) appearance; for a house — frost resistance/weather durability is the
  primary criterion. Both share a hermeticity requirement (blocking odors
  and sound from a shared corridor).
- **Series (70/80/100) spec breakdown, this manufacturer's own product
  line** — series number is said to derive from leaf thickness (7cm/8cm/
  10cm respectively). Per-series specs as stated (`ASR-uncertain` on a few
  exact figures — the transcript's auto-generated captions render some
  numbers awkwardly, e.g. "1 и 2 миллиметра" for what is very likely "1.2
  мм"):
  - **70 series**: metal thickness ≈1.2mm (leaf and frame); 50mm mineral-
    wool insulation, insulated around the full frame perimeter except the
    lock zone; 10mm MDF facing (both sides); two Russian-made locks plus a
    night-latch bolt; two sealing contours on the frame.
  - **80 series**: 12mm MDF facing (both sides, thicker than 70-series);
    better-quality weatherstripping; an eccentric ("эксцентрик") adjustment
    mechanism letting the leaf-to-frame press fit be fine-tuned; available
    in custom sizes; hardware/MDF facing swappable within the same product
    line.
  - **100 series**: metal thickness ≈1.5mm; 100mm mineral-wool insulation;
    16mm MDF facing (both sides); 3 sealing contours on the frame plus 4
    additional on the leaf; exterior/interior finish fully customizable to
    a designer project.
  - **All series are rated "class 4" strength/break-in resistance per
    Belarusian standards**, stated by the speaker as *the highest available
    class under Belarusian standards* — **this appears to conflict with
    the class scale already recorded from `YT_TKTKBmTkebg`** (a 13-class
    scale where class 4 was described as a mid-range choice, not the
    ceiling). Flagging as `uncertain`/unreconciled rather than silently
    picking one: possibly two different classification systems (a
    manufacturer's internal "прочность"/strength class vs. a
    burglary-resistance class), or an ASR/speaker imprecision in one of the
    two sources. Needs a third source or an official standard citation to
    resolve.
- **This manufacturer's own showroom pricing, Minsk, stated directly in
  USD (no conversion needed), 2017-08-26**: 70 series **$250-300**; 80
  series **$350-500**; 100 series **$500+**. Higher tiers are explicitly
  tied to greater leaf-metal thickness, better soundproofing, hardware
  quality, and finish-material quality. **Brand-specific catalog pricing,
  not a market-wide benchmark** — same caveat as the existing `Novakey`
  price-tier entry on the wiki page, and now nearly 9 years old (2017) vs.
  that entry's 2026 dating, so not directly comparable without inflation
  adjustment even within the same category.
