## Genuinely New Content

**⚠️ Dust-sourcing statistic**: an unnamed study cited putting **~40% of household dust/dirt as entering from outside** via windows or the entry door — the stated basis for prioritizing window-sealing/entry-defense over indoor-only cleaning habits. New quantified framing for this store's existing (extensive) dust-mechanism content, which documents where dust accumulates but not where most of it originates.

**Anti-dust window mesh**: a distinct mosquito-mesh product line marketed specifically as "anti-dust," with a meaningfully finer weave/smaller cell size than standard insect mesh — still needs periodic cleaning, but reduces street-dust infiltration through an open window. New device category.

**Three-tier entry doormat defense system**: (1) tap/scrape shoes outside the building entrance itself; (2) a stiff mat immediately outside the apartment door; (3) a second mat just inside. Explicitly stated as reducing, not eliminating, tracked-in dirt, and dependent on regularly cleaning at least the two apartment-adjacent mats. New structured framework — existing entry-mat content on this store (Alla Krol) covers planning/sizing a mat at design stage, not this layered structure.

**⚠️ Shoe-podium height figure + wipe-routine, extending an existing robot-vacuum note**: raise a shoe shelf ~**10cm** above the main floor specifically so a robot vacuum clears underneath without shoes needing to be moved; recommends wiping the shelf itself before a cleaning run so swept-off dust lands where the robot can then collect it. Extends this store's existing `qt5mQQ6W6Z4` note (podium concept, no figure) with a concrete number and routine.

**⚠️ Sink-type default, applying to both kitchen and bathroom**: rather than reasoning only from angle/roundness rules, recommends skipping top-mount/rimmed/vessel sinks entirely in favor of an **undermount (glued from below) or fully countertop-integrated** sink — frames the existing angle/roundness rules (already on this store) as damage control for a sink type worth avoiding outright.

**⚠️ Trash-bin door-mounted slide mechanism**: mounting a cabinet-concealed trash bin directly to the door face (or on a purpose-made rail carriage that rides out with the door) fixes a real everyday failure — a stationary bin not moving with the door means thrown items are more likely to miss. Generalizes beyond kitchens to any cabinet-concealed bin (e.g. a bathroom vanity).

**Steam-cleaner material-compatibility caution**: quartz-vinyl and laminate flooring are largely incompatible with steam cleaners (verify the specific product's own care instructions); the presenter states personal opposition to steam cleaners generally for this reason, despite their popularity — framed as a caution, not a universal ban across every flooring type.

**Sharp interior corners as a general robot-vacuum-reach problem**: any sharp, non-rounded/non-chamfered interior corner in a floor plan becomes a permanent manual-touch-up spot, beyond the fixture-specific pinch points (e.g. sink edges) already documented — a layout-level generalization.

**Dryer lint is normal, not itself a damage signal**: fabric sheds lint/fuzz regardless of whether a dryer is used; the dryer's lint trap just concentrates it visibly in one place. Genuine caveat: dryer/cycle quality still affects *how much* extra wear clothing takes, but visible lint after a cycle isn't itself evidence of damage.

**Pet-hair reduction, two named methods**: professional "express shedding" grooming-salon treatments, and home deshedding brush tools (named **deshedder** and **furminator** — same tool category, different manufacturer names). New device category — no dedicated pets page exists on this store.
