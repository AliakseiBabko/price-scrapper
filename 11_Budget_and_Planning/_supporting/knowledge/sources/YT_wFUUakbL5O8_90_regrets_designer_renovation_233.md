- **Countertop must be cut to reach fully to the wall, with a parallel cut** — a real defect left a ~3-4mm non-parallel gap, an unusable/unreachable dead space behind the counter run.
- **Flooring should run as one continuous, single-color surface with no "designer insert" color-break strip or open surface-mounted threshold between adjacent zones of the same open-plan room** — presented as this channel's own practice (continuous quartz-vinyl flooring, no color break, no threshold).
- **AC indoor-unit placement, two-branch rule extending this store's existing AC-placement content**: (a) default — place the indoor unit where the condensate drain and outdoor condenser unit can be routed to the building exterior, letting incoming air mix and reach the room comfortably; (b) **if the building/HOA prohibits any exterior condenser or condensate routing** (real example: an "elite" new-build development with this restriction), place the indoor unit above the entrance door instead — anywhere that avoids blowing directly onto a seating or sleeping occupant. - **Prefer concealed/hidden curtain cornices and concealed (boxed-in) radiators over open/visible ones** — restated aesthetic/functional preference; visible radiators noted as commonly misused for hanging wet items.

- **A sink should not be positioned so close to a toilet that it pinches the seating/use area** — creates an awkward, hard-to-clean corner.
