## Walls / Ceilings / Electrical — New Facts

- **Three smoke-detector housing types mentioned**: battery-powered,
  mains-wired (two-part: mounting platform + detector head, the type
  installed in this video), and water/sprinkler-type (mentioned but not
  covered further).
- **⚠️ Mounting platform (закладная/площадка) must be installed *before*
  the stretch-ceiling film is fitted** — the platform is fixed to the
  structural ceiling first, wiring is run to it, then the PVC/fabric film
  is stretched over the whole ceiling, and only afterward is the film cut
  open at the platform location to bring the detector through. This is
  the core sequencing fact for stretch-ceiling compatibility.
- **Mounting platform spec**: ~135mm diameter, ~20mm center hole. Can be
  secured with 2 suspension points/hangers, but **4 hangers are
  recommended for rigidity** — fewer risks the platform flexing/sagging
  inward over time.
- **⚠️ Placement clearance rules, stated as regulatory ("по регламенту"),
  matching the ceiling-detector clearances in `YT_RwyPR2RFZYM`**: not
  less than 50cm from walls/corners; not less than 50cm from light
  fixtures (including track lighting); **not less than 50cm between
  adjacent detectors themselves** (a distinct rule from the wall/light
  clearances, not stated in the other fire-safety sources processed this
  session). Framed as something confirmed with the client in advance
  (aesthetic tension with light-fixture layout is real and worth flagging
  to a client up front, even though the rule itself isn't optional).
- **Film-penetration technique**: after the film is stretched, a
  reinforcing ring (кольцо) is glued at each point where a wire/fixture
  needs to pass through — **35mm rings used here, could go smaller, and
  even 50mm still works since the platform itself overlaps/covers the
  ring** either way. A cross-shaped slit is then cut through the ring
  opening to pull the wiring through without the film tearing further.
- **Detector mounting-and-wiring mechanics**: the detector head detaches
  from its base plate; the base plate screws into the center of the
  embedded platform (through the platform's window/opening where wires
  exit) using a single screw. Two wires connect to two screw-terminal
  contacts on the base — genuinely simple ("you can't really get this
  wrong, two wires, two contacts"). **⚠️ Practical caution: the terminal
  screws are described as fragile/easily stripped** — if you strip one on
  the first attempt, you won't be able to tighten it a second time, so
  take care not to over-torque.
- **Wire prep**: insulation strips easily by hand/fingernail on this
  detector's wiring, no tool needed; when daisy-chaining, the source
  doesn't cut the wire at each junction — just strips and loops it
  through in sequence.
- **Final verification**: once the detector head snaps onto its base, a
  blinking indicator confirms it's powered and functioning correctly
  before the installer leaves the site.
