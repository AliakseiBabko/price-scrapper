## Rules / Heuristics — Genuinely New Content

- **⚠️ Curved/rounded facade walls or glazing are a purchase-time red flag**: furniture doesn't fit a curved bay; baseboards/curtain rods aren't made for curved runs; an internal curved decorative wall compounds the problem. Curved-wall finishing is billed per linear meter (2-3× the labor of a straight wall, no trowel pre-matched to an arbitrary radius); custom-fit furniture for a curved bay commonly runs 3-10× standard cost.
- **⚠️ Narrow "spread-wing" (распашонка) rooms: avoid room width under ~2.5m** even if the listed square footage looks fine — worked example: a 2m mattress + headboard leaves only 20-30cm clearance in a 2.5m room, and a wall-TV means walking across the bed to reach the far side. A wider spread-wing variant (~3.5m room width, e.g. П-44 3-room) doesn't have this problem.
- **⚠️ Always request the developer's actual dimensioned floor plan (width/length per room), not just total m²**: a furnished rendering can mislead via incorrect furniture scale (a bed drawn to *look* like a 2m mattress but scaled to 1.5m), with no enforceable recourse since the furnished rendering typically isn't part of the binding purchase contract (ДДУ).
- **⚠️ Long, narrow corridors are worth avoiding at purchase time**: some layout families (П-44 variants) allow merging the living room with the corridor (usually easy to approve) to eliminate it, at the cost of the living room becoming a pass-through space — prefer a layout that avoids the problem from the start.
- **⚠️ Natural insolation (solar orientation) as a Moscow-specific selection factor, mechanism explained** (vitamin D/serotonin/melatonin): South = max sun/heat, winter heating assist but summer overheating; Southwest/West = bright and warm, milder than south, but panoramic glazing there can get hot enough to burn skin in summer; North = even soft light, cooler summers/less AC but darker/colder winters; Northeast/East = comfortable morning sun, no harsh afternoon glare. A weighing factor for both whole-apartment choice and per-room placement (office vs. living room vs. bedroom).
- **Load-bearing walls/columns/non-widenable openings restrict layout planning and reduce resale liquidity** — check before buying, not just before renovating.
- **⚠️ Verify sewer riser-stack placement before buying**: documented case of a riser beside a kitchen window (confirmed via developer plans, not an AC-condensate line), forcing a choice between a macerator "sololift" pump or a long sewer reroute.
- **⚠️ A small labeled "kitchen-niche" alcove often can't actually fit a full kitchen + table despite its stated square footage** — verify with planning software before assuming it works; if it doesn't, relocate the dining table to the living room or combine the niche with the living room outright.
