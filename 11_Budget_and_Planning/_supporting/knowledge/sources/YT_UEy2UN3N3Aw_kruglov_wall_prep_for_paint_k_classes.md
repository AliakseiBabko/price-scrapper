## Genuinely New Content

- **Named prep-class framework, with a stated international equivalence**: Russia's K1-K4 substrate-prep classes are stated to correspond to Swiss/German Q1-Q4 standards, with Russia's own requirements described as at least as strict as Germany's and matching Switzerland's — framed as among the world's strictest.
- **Full four-tier price ladder with defect tolerances (RUB/m²)**:
  - **K1 ≈100 RUB (under $10)**: one primer coat after plastering, nothing more. Suitable for tile, other sheet materials, and decorative panels. Scratches/gouges/pits/abrasive marks up to **3mm** deep allowed. Never checked with raking/side light.
  - **K2 ≈1,000 RUB (≈$10)**: suitable for facade paints/plasters with ≥1mm grain, and heavy wallpaper. Defects up to **1mm** deep allowed. Not checked with side light.
  - **K3 ≈2,000 RUB (≈$20)**: suitable for interior paints with <1mm grain, thin wallpaper, and roller-applied matte paint. Defects up to **0.3mm** deep allowed (still faintly visible). Not checked with side light.
  - **K4 ≈4,000 RUB (≈$40)**: the only tier checked with a raking/side-light ("лампа Люси") inspection — zero visible shadow or abrasive mark tolerated. Required for the thinnest finish materials (decorative plasters, Venetian plaster) and is the **only tier that permits spray-gun/airless paint application** — all lower tiers are roller-application only.
- **⚠️ The single decisive trigger for needing K4, not aesthetics generally**: any wall that will have raking/grazing light hitting it — most concretely, a continuous LED strip run in a shadow-gap ("теневой") profile around a room's perimeter — forces K4 prep on that wall, since the light itself reveals K3-level defects invisible under ordinary daylight. Under plain daylight alone, K3 and K4 walls look identical; the difference only appears once a directional/raking light source is added.
- **⚠️ Worked whole-apartment cost example (85m², 3m ceiling)**: using a stated **wall-area-to-floor-area ratio of 3:1**, gross wall area ≈255m²; after subtracting non-painted areas (bathroom, two closets, kitchen units), paintable area ≈200m². **K3 for the whole apartment ≈400,000 RUB (≈$4,200)**; **K4 for the whole apartment ≈800,000 RUB (≈$8,500)** — labor only, not counting the extra putty material and time K4 also requires. **Per-apartment-floor-area cost of upgrading everywhere from K3 to K4: the 400,000 RUB (≈$4,200) difference ÷ 85m² apartment area ≈5,000 RUB/m² (≈$50/m²) of apartment floor area.**
- **Practical recommendation**: don't blanket the whole apartment in K4 — apply it only where raking light will actually hit (the specific walls behind a planned LED cove, etc.), and consider dropping excess directional lighting fixtures from a design to avoid the K4 requirement entirely, unless a client deliberately wants a maximum "luxury" spec everywhere.
- **Timeline consequence of full-apartment K4**: painters alone, on an 85m² apartment fully prepped to K4, take roughly **4-5 months**.
- **Regulatory affordability contrast, a real named data point**: a Swiss client paying ≈100 EUR/m² for K4-equivalent prep is described as normal there; quoting a Russian client 10,000 RUB (≈$110/m², at this video's own trailing-6-month rate) for the same prep is stated to reliably cause the client to walk away from the job entirely — used to frame Russia's price ceiling as much lower even though the technical requirement is comparably strict.
