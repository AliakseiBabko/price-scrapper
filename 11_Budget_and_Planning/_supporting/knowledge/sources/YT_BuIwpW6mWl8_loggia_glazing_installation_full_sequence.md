## Balcony / Loggia — Durable Facts & Rules

- **Pre-installation prep**: remove sashes and glass units from fixed sections before handling the frames, specifically to reduce weight and simplify installation — store the removed glass/sashes on protective foam board (пеноплекс) laid against the floor and wall.
- **Sequencing rule to minimize weather exposure**: assemble the new window units completely first, *then* demolish the old glazing, *then* install the new construction — minimizes how long the opening sits fully exposed.
- **Assembly technique**: attach jamb-extension (добор) profiles and connectors; clean/dress welded seams before fixing for a tighter joint; apply sealing tape and sealant at every добор-to-frame joint. **Corner joints use a dedicated angled connector**, distinct from добор profile, that snaps together at 90° — the joint surface must be cleaned first for a reliably tight click; a separate 6mm connector plate is used at another joint type, with the same clean-first requirement.
- **Добор color rule**: a jamb-extension profile that will be hidden behind the building's facade doesn't need color lamination — plain white is fine, since it stays invisible once installed.
- **Demolition order for old glazing**: remove sashes and fixed-section glass first (lighter, easier). **Glass-bead removal order matters: sides first, then bottom, then top last** — specifically so the glass pane doesn't fall out mid-removal. To preserve an old frame for reuse, carefully drill out any rivets first; if the frame is being discarded, a pry bar suffices instead. **A specific developer defect noted**: some developer-installed rivets punch partway through the frame material itself, which the installers cite as one reason the old frame in this project couldn't be preserved intact regardless.
- **Drip-cap/flashing removal caution**: be extremely careful not to drop any piece — even a small fastener — off the building during removal.
- **Fixing-hardware decision, made only after full demolition**: sides and top get anchor plates (анкерные пластины); the bottom gets through-fastening (сквозной крепёж) instead — a per-side decision, not one method for the whole frame.
- **Zemskov's own stated policy change ("Zem Standards")**: he previously only permitted direct/through anchor fixing, never mounting plates — **now mounting plates are allowed, but must be spaced more frequently than the code minimum**, not just at the bare minimum spacing.
- **Anchor-plate spacing rule, per code as cited**: 150-180mm from an internal corner, then no more than 600mm spacing along any straight run after that.
- **Installation sequence**: assemble the full multi-section window construction one unit at a time (not all sections placed simultaneously), starting from the left side. **Remove the exterior protective film before placing each frame** — left on and exposed to sunlight, it bonds permanently to the profile and becomes very difficult to remove (needs mechanical scraping or a chemical solvent).
- **Jamb-extension width-combination technique**: this profile system's добор widths come in 2/4/6/10cm increments; when a needed width doesn't match one available size (their example: 140mm needed), **combine two добор pieces (100mm + 40mm) rather than sourcing a custom size** — joined the same way as any добор-to-frame joint (sealing tape + sealant). After combining, form the needed 90° angle at that junction and bring the separate window units into one shared flat plane before final fixing.
- **"Temporary" initial fixing rule**: leave the first-pass fixing intentionally loose/temporary specifically so the whole assembled multi-section construction can still be "played" (nudged forward/back) to set correct planes across all sections — don't fully torque fasteners down until the entire assembly's planes are confirmed correct.
- **Fastener embedment depth rule**: rod/stud-type fasteners driven into the substrate must penetrate the base material by **at least 5cm (50mm) minimum**.
- **Drip-cap (отлив) installation**: color-matched to the facade, with additional sound-dampening material added, and a dedicated waterproofing tape applied underneath before final fixing — Zemskov and Konstantin both flag this as genuinely skill-dependent sheet-metal work, not the trivial task it might appear to be. A dedicated support/sill profile under the window is what the drip-cap actually screws into.
- **Final sealing and glazing sequence**: after the drip-cap and exterior trim/cover strips are installed, seal all remaining joints with sealant, then install the glass into fixed sections and hang the sashes. **Glass-bead seating technique**: use a rubber-faced/dead-blow mallet, never a standard hammer, to avoid cracking the glass or damaging the bead profile.
- **Vapor-barrier tape rule**: apply around the full glazing perimeter (sides and top) specifically to protect the foam joint from interior room humidity, **in at least two passes** — to avoid pinholes/voids in the foam joint, the same "seal twice" principle recorded elsewhere in this store for other joint types.
- **Final handover steps**: install handles, decorative cover caps, and insect screens, then perform a final adjustment/calibration pass on every sash before considering the installation complete.
