## Materials/Systems — Шумопласт Spec Sheet and Installation Detail

1. **Manufacturer engineering-album spec sheet** (ASTic Group's own
   published "инженерных решений" reference table): total floor
   soundproofing-construction thickness **≈80mm (±5mm)** = 20mm
   Шумопласт layer + a **minimum 60mm screed** on top. Manufacturer
   dB figures: **-28dB reduced impact-noise index (Ln,w)**; airborne
   additional-insulation index rendered as "79dB" in this transcript —
   flagged `ASR-uncertain`, very likely the same -7-9dB range already
   on this page from Round 6, not treated as a new/different figure.
2. **⚠️ Second product-selection criterion, distinct from Round 6's
   substrate-unevenness rule**: choose Шумопласт over roll-type
   Шуманет-100/Шуманет-Гидро specifically when **many utility
   lines/pipes run under the floor** within the room being treated (this
   room: water-supply pipes plus some corrugated conduit) — a granular
   material conforms around obstructions better than a sheet product
   would. Rooms with few/no utility lines can use the cheaper roll
   products instead.
3. **Perimeter-damper application using Шумопласт itself**: prime the
   room's perimeter (floor-to-wall junction) with the manufacturer's own
   "Шумопласт грунт" primer, applied slightly taller than the total
   floor buildup (~10cm primer height for an ~8cm total construction);
   while the primer is still fresh, press/tamp Шумопласт granulate by
   hand along the primed perimeter strip as the damper layer separating
   the screed from the walls. **Practitioner's own critical reflection**:
   the crew found this specific perimeter-damper method "not very
   convenient" compared to two named roll-based alternatives — cut
   10-12cm strips of Шуманет-100/Шуманет-Гидро roll and glue them around
   the perimeter, or use a "Вибростек"-type roll product adhered with
   "Вибросил" silicone sealant.
4. **Tool list**: the material itself, "Шумопласт грунт" primer, a
   half-trowel/float ("полутёрок"), a brush, a small (~10cm) roller, and
   a utility knife to open the material's boxes.
5. **Packaging detail**: shipped in large boxes/polyethylene bags; the
   manufacturer glues the bag's own seam shut with the same acrylic
   binder used in the product, specifically to keep air out of the sealed
   package before use.
6. **⚠️ Beacon-installation rule, a critical mechanism not in Round 6's
   note**: when setting up plastering/screed beacons over this material,
   **never drill or screw into the soundproofing layer itself** — the
   crew instead built dedicated tracks/paths that avoid penetrating the
   Шумопласт, because the entire mechanism depends on the screed being
   fully decoupled ("floating") from both the walls and the structural
   floor slab; any rigid penetration through the material short-circuits
   that decoupling.
7. **Reinforcement mesh spec**: 5×5cm cell rebar mesh, panels overlapped
   at seams, tied together with binding wire **every 20cm** — a specific
   spacing figure not previously recorded (Round 6 already noted the
   mesh must be mid-pour-lifted so it embeds within the screed body, a
   separate but complementary mechanism, correctly not double-counted).
8. **Thickness-discipline rule, with the cost/time reason stated
   explicitly**: the installer's main task is holding the material at
   exactly ~2cm — not thicker. A thicker layer wastes material without
   meaningfully improving the acoustic result, **and significantly
   increases dry/cure time**, since a thicker Шумопласт layer takes much
   longer to set.
9. **Granular material-only cost breakdown, distinct from Round 6's
   all-in figure**: one package (covering ≈10m²) costs **2,750 RUB**,
   i.e. **≈275-300 RUB/m² for the Шумопласт material alone** (excluding
   screed/mesh/labor); a ~3L bag of the matching primer costs
   **≈450-500 RUB**. Kept separate from Round 6's already-recorded
   **all-in ≈6,000-7,000 RUB/m² (≈$80-90/m²)** figure, which bundles
   material + labor + the 6-7cm screed pour — these are two different,
   non-comparable cost bases (material-only vs. fully-installed), not a
   conflicting price.
