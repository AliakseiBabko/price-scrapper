## Mistakes / Warnings

- **A room that must be walked through to reach two other rooms is not a usable isolated room — check every room's access path before finalizing a layout, not just its floor area.** The defect found: the self-designed layout created two large pass-through spaces (one running the full depth of the apartment from the entry, another an L-shaped area) with no alternate access route to the rooms beyond them — the source's fix was as simple as adding one wall with two door openings to convert the pass-through into two properly isolated rooms, a fix the client's own 3.5-year design process never found. **General rule: for every room in a layout, trace its only access path(s) and confirm no other room's function depends on walking through it.**
