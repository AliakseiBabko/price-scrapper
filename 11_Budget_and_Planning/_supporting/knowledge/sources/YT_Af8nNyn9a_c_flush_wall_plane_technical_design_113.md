## Rules / Heuristics — Wall-Plane Alignment Technique

- **⚠️ A short wall segment that's slightly out of plane with an adjacent load-bearing/structural wall (creating a small "step" or external+internal corner pair) should almost always be rebuilt flush into a single plane with the structural wall, not left as-is** — explicitly **not** a space-saving move despite looking like one: the resulting recessed pocket is too shallow to ever furnish (nothing gets placed there), only collects dust/dirt, and adds real finishing cost/labor (the step needs extra taping/plastering, wallpaper or paint work around two extra corners, extra baseboard routing, and extra stretch-ceiling perimeter routing around both corners) — the cost of fixing it at the rough-in stage is explicitly cheaper than the extra finishing-material and labor cost of leaving the step in place, and far cheaper than the ongoing cleaning burden of a permanently dirt-catching external corner that gets scuffed by furniture use.
- **Two fix options depending on whether the misaligned segment is load-bearing:**
  - If it's a **non-load-bearing partition**: demolish and rebuild it flush with the load-bearing wall's plane.
  - If it's **itself load-bearing** (can't be moved): build out a **furring/frame layer** (каркас) over the offending short segment to bring it flush, or — cheaper still — simply skim/finish that short segment directly with a leveling compound ("газовый лаками"/plastering) rather than framing it out; either approach can shift a nearby door opening by a small offset (documented example: door shifted ~7cm) so the door reads flush and "more refined" (благородно) when closed, hiding the correction entirely.
- **Exception carve-out, quantified**: in roughly **10% of cases**, the correct move is the opposite — deliberately move the wall segment *outward* (away from flush) rather than into plane, when doing so creates a genuinely useful niche (a "нужную заказчикам берегу" — a useful pocket for the client) rather than a dead, unfurnishable gap. The default (flush-align) still applies in the other ~90%; this isn't a blanket "always flush" rule, it's "flush unless a deliberate, purpose-built niche is the actual goal."
