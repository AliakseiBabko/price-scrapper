## Genuinely new content

1. **Installer method choice — tiling directly onto a heated floor mat
   with adhesive alone, no pre-skim coat**: this crew's tile installer
   (Andrei) applies tile adhesive directly onto the exposed heated-floor
   mat/cable ("pink" — visibly uncovered) and sets tile straight into it,
   rather than first covering the heated element with a self-leveling
   compound or a preliminary tile-adhesive skim coat, which the source
   says this company "sometimes" does instead. Presented as this
   installer's own working preference, not a universal rule — a distinct
   installer-methodology data point this vault's existing heated-floor
   cure-time/substrate-flatness content doesn't cover (those address
   curing time and flatness tolerance, not whether a pre-coat is used at
   all).
2. **Paint-swatch-behind-furniture technique, a real client-decision
   practice**: rather than buying all wall paint for the whole apartment
   upfront, apply small paint swatches ("выкрасы") in a low-visibility
   spot (this job: behind furniture placement) so the client can judge
   the actual color in the room's real light before committing to a
   full-apartment purchase — explicitly framed as advice the source gives
   to every client/prospective client, not just this one. Genuinely new
   money/mistake-avoidance heuristic, not previously on `Walls_and_Paint.md`.
3. **⚠️ Textured ("с пупырышками" — raised-bump/pebbled-texture) tile
   grout-cleanup difficulty warning**: if grout gets onto the raised
   texture/bumps of this tile type during grouting, it becomes
   practically impossible to clean off afterward — installers must mask/
   work the perimeter carefully around each textured tile during
   grouting, not just wipe residue off after the fact the way a smooth
   tile allows. New QC-relevant caution for `Tile_Selection_and_Layout.md`,
   distinct from the existing coin-test/finish-check content there.
