## Rooms / Zones — Kids Room
- **Rename "детский дом" (children's home/orphanage) to "детский домик" (kids' house/loft-bed structure) in client-facing language** — explicitly framed as an emotionally jarring word choice to avoid with clients. `confirmed`, single-account.
- **A kids'-house/loft-bed structure (детский домик) must be planned and wired into the finish electrical from the start of the renovation, not added afterward** — described as too tightly integrated with finish electrical (switches at each sleeping spot) to retrofit cleanly once the renovation is otherwise complete. `confirmed`, single-account, but a specific, checkable sequencing rule.
- **Provision two switches near each sleeping spot in a shared kids' room**: one pass-through (проходной) switch controlling the room's main light, and one separate switch for a personal night-light/backlight at that sleeping spot — lets each child control their own light independently without needing to negotiate with a sibling. `confirmed`, demonstrated in the company's own project.
- **Deliberately leave the middle section of a shared two-child wardrobe unused/non-functional as a neutral buffer zone between each child's own storage area** — framed as accepting a real storage-capacity loss (their term: "потерянная секция", lost section) in exchange for reducing recurring sibling territorial conflict ("you're in my space"). Presented as this company's specific tradeoff judgment, not a formula. `confirmed` as stated design rationale, single-account.
- **When building a wardrobe with this kind of intentionally-unused middle section, add a separate dedicated electrical cable for wardrobe interior lighting during the same install** — stated explicitly as something that becomes impossible to add after the wardrobe itself is installed. `confirmed`, single-account, a specific sequencing rule extending the general "wire before you build" theme also seen elsewhere in this channel's content.

## Electrical / Switches, Sockets, Cables
- **Place smart-speaker (умная колонка) outlets on the window sill/reveal in every room, including kids' rooms** — reasoning given: lets a resident play their own audio locally per room rather than relying on sound carrying from the living room, and lets voice commands ("Alisa, close the curtains") be correctly scoped to the room the speaker is physically in rather than ambiguous across the apartment. `confirmed`, single-account, stated as this company's now-standard practice ("in every modern renovation").
- **Add extra outlets in the *upper* portion of a window reveal (откос), in addition to the lower-reveal outlets already recommended elsewhere by this channel** — purpose given: powering holiday garlands/string lights from above so cords hang down neatly rather than needing to be run up from a lower outlet ("hanging strands" look). Framed as a repeated recommendation ("we've said this many times, but repetition is the mother of learning"). `confirmed`, single-account.
