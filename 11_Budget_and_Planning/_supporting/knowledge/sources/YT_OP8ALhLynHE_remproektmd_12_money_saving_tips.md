## Materials / Mistakes-Warnings / Flooring / Walls-Ceilings / Doors-Trim — Durable Facts & Rules

1. **Wallpaper ("обои") over paint for budget work**: wall prep for paint requires plaster-to-guide-rails ("по маякам"), one coat of putty, fiberglass mesh ("паутинка"/стеклоткань, to prevent hairline cracks), a second putty pass, then 2+ paint coats — a long, expensive sequence. Dense wallpaper can be hung on a barely-prepped or even bare concrete wall with far less prep. `confirmed`, `single-account`.
2. **Plain (no-pattern) wallpaper wastes less material than patterned wallpaper** — pattern-matching between strips increases material consumption per room; a plain wallpaper roll yields more usable strips than a patterned roll of the same size. `confirmed`.
3. **Stretch ceiling ("натяжной потолок") vs. drywall-boxed ceiling — a real "X vs Y, and here's why" comparison**:
   - **Practicality/flood protection**: a real incident — a client's apartment (owner frequently away, works as a pilot) was flooded by upstairs neighbors after the stretch ceiling ("ПВХ"-film type) was already installed; the film bulged and held the entire volume of water, with **zero water reaching the floor**, protecting the owner's engineered/parquet flooring. A drywall or plastered ceiling would have let all that water through onto the floor, ruining the flooring and the finish.
   - **Installation speed**: drywall ceiling requires profile install, drywall screwing, seam taping, fiberglass mesh, two putty passes, and paint — a multi-step sequence taking **at minimum 2 weeks**. A stretch ceiling was installed in the speaker's own home in **1 day**.
   - **Visual result**: a matte stretch ceiling is visually indistinguishable from a painted drywall/plaster ceiling from a normal viewing distance.
   - **Cost**: a stretch ceiling is **2-3× cheaper** than a painted drywall-boxed ceiling.
   - **Ecological safety claim**: stretch-ceiling film is certified and doesn't off-gas under normal use (per the speaker; he explicitly declines to comment on combustion byproducts, noting he hasn't tested this himself). He notes the same "what does it off-gas" scrutiny could equally be applied to non-woven wallpaper, PVC window frames, and laminate — treating the ecological objection as somewhat inconsistent when selectively applied only to stretch ceilings. `unverified`/single-account opinion, flagged as such.
   - **Fabric ("тканевый") stretch ceiling variant**: costs more than standard PVC-film stretch ceiling but is still cheaper than a painted drywall ceiling; the speaker states he personally sees little practical difference from PVC film, framing the extra cost as mainly an ecological-preference premium, not a functional upgrade.
4. **Recessed/point-light ("точечные светильники") cost pitfall in a stretch ceiling**: each individual penetration through the ceiling film is priced separately (a backing/mounting plate — "закладная деталь" — is required under every hole, and that hardware plus the extra labor costs money per hole) — recommends 1-2 fixtures per room (a chandelier, or a diffuse "soft-box"-style surface fixture with LED) instead of many point lights, which the speaker says add cost with limited functional benefit.
5. **Foam-insulating a bathtub ("утеплить ванну пеной")**: apply canned expanding foam to the exterior of an installed bathtub (works for both cheap steel tubs and more expensive acrylic tubs) to add thermal and acoustic insulation — water cools more slowly, and the sound of running/draining water is quieter. Cosmetically hidden, since a bathtub is normally clad behind a tile-covered access panel/screen anyway. `single-account`, presented as a real jobsite practice ("наш объект").
6. **Plastic baseboards ("плинтуса") for budget work, over MDF or polyurethane**: plastic baseboards have a rubber edge gasket that presses flush against an imperfectly-flattened wall, visually hiding wall waviness that MDF/polyurethane baseboards (rigid, no gasket) would reveal. **Secondary benefit**: the gap between a plastic baseboard and the wall can route a forgotten/late-added cable (e.g. a missed outlet) without re-cutting into the wall — though the speaker explicitly still recommends planning cable routing in advance rather than relying on this as a first resort.
7. **Kitchen laminate flooring as a budget alternative to tile**: laminate (grade/class **33**, wear-resistance rated for commercial/industrial use) held up with no issues after **4 years** in the speaker's own kitchen. Class 33 is his stated minimum recommendation for laminate anywhere in an apartment. **Beveled-edge ("с фаской по периметру") laminate is specifically recommended**: at the seam between planks, a beveled edge conceals any gap that appears from thermal expansion/contraction — a square-edge plank would show a visible seam gap under the same movement.
8. **Skip tile trim/border strips ("фризы," "бордюры") in bathrooms**: these decorative border elements are priced per-piece or per-linear-meter (not per m² like field tile), and their installation labor is also priced per linear meter/piece — both add real cost. Also noted as a declining stylistic trend (more solid-color, full-field tile without borders). `confirmed`, `single-account`/opinion on the trend claim specifically.
9. **Plastic window returns/slopes ("откосы") over plastered returns, for budget work**: a plastered-and-painted window return can look slightly better but costs meaningfully more (plaster, insulation with foam board, putty, mesh tape, corner beading, paint) and is prone to a specific defect — a crack at the joint between the window frame and the plastered return, caused by seasonal thermal/humidity cycling, that must be periodically resealed. Plastic returns (ordered with the window itself) don't develop this joint-crack defect. `confirmed`, `single-account`.
10. **Unheated balcony/loggia — don't over-invest in finishes that will fail from cold/humidity cycling**: drywall ceiling will crack over time from the temperature/humidity swings; wood paneling ("вагонка") also performs poorly under the same conditions, especially with a window periodically open. Laminate flooring on an unheated balcony will similarly fail over time. **Recommended budget approach instead**: floor — tile or (cheapest) linoleum with plastic baseboards; walls — cement-based (not gypsum-based) plaster finished with facade-grade exterior paint; ceiling — same cement-based plaster/putty + facade paint. A built-in bench/storage seat is suggested as a good-value addition. Explicitly advises against drywall ceilings, recessed lighting niches, or other "super renovation" treatments on an unheated balcony as not worth the investment given the failure modes above. `confirmed`, `single-account`.
11. **DIY wallpaper-removal technique for well-adhered non-woven-backed wallpaper**: use a spiked/perforating roller ("игольчатый валик") to puncture the wallpaper surface, then wet it with water so the moisture penetrates through the perforations — this loosens well-adhered wallpaper (especially non-woven-backed) that would otherwise resist simple peeling. Framed as a real cost-saving DIY task a non-specialist homeowner can do themselves to reduce contractor labor cost.
12. **Technical design ("технический дизайн интерьера") deliverables list — corroborates/extends existing content**: описывается как a set of ~6-7 working drawings: (a) measurement plan ("план обмеров"), (b) furniture layout plan with actual furniture dimensions tied to each room's real dimensions, (c) outlet/socket plan ("план розеток"), derived from the agreed furniture plan so each outlet is positioned for the furniture it will actually serve, (d) switch and lighting plan ("план выключателей и освещения") — specifying which switch controls which fixture, lighting-group breakdown, and pass-through switch ("проходные выключатели") locations relative to door-swing direction, (e) ceiling plan (per-room ceiling configuration, niches, lighting), (f) flooring plan — critical for coordinating transitions between different floor coverings so seams land under door leaves. Framed as preventing after-the-fact regret ("забыл", "неправильно сделали") from skipping this step. **This closely previews/likely reuses this channel's own script for Video 5 of this trial batch** (`Oxv_w9zejsA`, "Technical design of an apartment") — flagged here explicitly so Video 5's extraction can check for script/content overlap rather than treat it as fully independent confirmation.
