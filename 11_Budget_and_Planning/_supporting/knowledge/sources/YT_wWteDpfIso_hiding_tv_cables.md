## Durable Facts

- **Simple case (small TV, no extra AV devices)**: needs only one power outlet (220V), one TV/coax outlet, and one network (Ethernet) outlet — mount the TV bracket offset from the wall-mount area's center (left or right half), and place the electrical points in the other half so cables can be gathered/folded behind the bracket and secured with a rubber band or cable tie, kept taut against the mount with the same tie.
- **Complex case (TV + additional AV devices in a TV stand/console below)**: requires planning both the point behind the TV and the points behind the console at the **design stage**, before rough-in:
  - Behind the TV: a 4-module outlet plate with (left to right) power outlet, TV/coax cable, network cable, and the **4th (rightmost) module left empty**.
  - Behind the future console: two 4-module outlet blocks, positioned so the right block sits directly under the TV's block and the left block sits further left; block height should be set **~10 cm below the underside of the console's top surface** — going lower (e.g. 20–30 cm below) risks landing the outlets behind the console's internal shelves, where they'd be inaccessible.
  - Each console-level block, left to right: 5 power outlets, TV/coax cable, network cable, with the **rightmost (8th) module also left empty** — positioned directly under the empty module on the block above.
- **Rough-in stage**: connect the two empty (rightmost) modules — one on the TV-height block, one on the console-height block — with a **vertical chase (shtroba)**, then install a plastic cable channel/raceway inside it.
- **Final/furnishing stage** (after rough-in, after furniture is placed): route cables from the console-level devices up through the cable channel to the TV, and cover the channel opening with decorative end caps/plugs.
- **Common mistake called out explicitly**: undersizing the cable channel. A channel sized only for the base 4 thin wires (power, coax, ethernet, + spare) is too tight once you later need to pull an HDMI cable with bulky molded connectors on each end through the same channel alongside the existing wires. **Minimum recommended cable channel/conduit size: a 40 mm diameter tube, or a 40×40 mm square channel.**
- **Process/responsibility note**: cable-channel routing, TV mounting height, and outlet coordinates should be specified by the designer/project planner, not left to the customer or installer to improvise on-site — if a project doesn't document this, the speaker states the customer has grounds to demand it and to hold the designer accountable for it.
