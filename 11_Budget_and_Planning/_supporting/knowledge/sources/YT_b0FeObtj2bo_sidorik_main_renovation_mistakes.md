## Genuinely New Content

- **Ceiling-based soundproofing is described as ineffective, and the
  practitioner's recommended alternative is negotiating with the
  upstairs neighbor instead**: soundproofing installed on the underside
  of *your own* ceiling doesn't meaningfully block vibration transmission
  (as opposed to airborne sound alone); the more effective fix is to
  arrange for the **upstairs neighbor** to include sound-insulating
  material in **their own floor screed** — a negotiation-based mitigation
  this store hasn't previously recorded (existing soundproofing content
  is all about your own unit's own assembly, not a neighbor-coordination
  angle). `single-account`, no product/cost detail given beyond naming
  mineral/stone wool ("каменная вата") as the simplest available
  soundproofing material category.
- **Budget overrun range cited as typical: 20% up to 100% (double) over
  the initial figure**, attributed mainly to genuinely unforeseeable
