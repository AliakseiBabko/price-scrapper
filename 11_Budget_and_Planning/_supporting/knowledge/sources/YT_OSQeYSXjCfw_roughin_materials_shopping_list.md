## Durable Facts — Purchasing Strategy

- **Professional crews typically buy materials with a margin/surplus** and settle leftover costs with the customer afterward, reusing spare stock on the next job. **For a one-off private/DIY buyer, the speaker recommends splitting the purchase in two**: buy ~80% upfront, then buy the remainder once actual on-site needs are clearer — avoiding being stuck reselling or storing large unused leftovers.
- **Small apartments (under ~50 m²) are frequently declined or minimum-priced by professional crews** — the reasoning given: a crew must bring the same full toolkit (power tools, ladders, temporary panel, consumables) to a small job as to a large one, so crews prefer fewer, larger jobs. Practical implication for a buyer: expect to pay a "minimum job" rate on a small apartment regardless of actual square footage. `unverified`, single-account.
