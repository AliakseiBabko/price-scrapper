## Design Concept — Six Named Colour Failure Modes, Each With the Corrected Version

- **⚠️ Mistake 1 — repeating one accent colour across many large blocks kills the accent.** Sofa, curtains, accent wall and chair all in one shade: "акцентный цвет перестаёт быть акцентным… он переходит в разряд дополнительных цветов" — it now dominates rather than accents, and the interior additionally reads flat and monotonous. **Corrected version given: blue sofa + light curtains matching the walls + a wine-coloured armchair contrasting with the blue.** Note this is a *specific and different* claim from the vault's existing Kruglov rule that an accent must repeat at least three times — Kruglov is about repetition across **small** objects, this is about repetition across **large planes**; both should be recorded, with the distinction made explicit.
- **⚠️ Mistake 1a — adjacent large colour blocks merge.** "Если ваши крупные цветовые пятна находятся близко друг к другу, вот как этот диван и шторы, они начинают друг с другом сливаться. Это ещё хуже." Proximity, not just quantity, is the aggravating factor.
- **⚠️ Mistake 2 — mixing warm and cool variants inside what looks like "the same colour."** Worked example: a dark blue sofa (cool) with a light "blue" armchair that is actually turquoise (warm) — **"бирюзовый цвет — это синий, в который добавили небольшую каплю жёлтого, от чего он потеплел."** Two separate gradients exist, blue-dark→light-blue and turquoise-dark→light-turquoise, and the fix is to stay on one of them: dark blue + light blue, *or* deep sea-green + light turquoise. **Fallback rule for anyone who can't see the difference: "тёплые оттенки хорошо сочетаются с тёплыми, а холодные с холодными."** She explicitly offers this as a memorisable rule for readers whose colour sense doesn't yet register the shift — a useful framing.
- **⚠️ Mistake 3 — mixing "чистые" and "припылённые" shades.** Definition given: a **чистый** colour is bright, taken straight from the tin with nothing added; a **припылённый** colour carries an admixture of grey and is the more complex one. **Rule: dusty/pastel shades pair with other dusty/pastel shades, saturated with saturated.** Worked failure: a child's room in soft dusty warm pinks wrecked by heavy deep violet on the rug and bedspread — "они делают эту комнату очень грубой и совсем не женственной." Corrections walked through in order: lighten the rug and bedspread; then note they are still too cool for the warm curtains; swap the gloss stretch ceiling for plain matte white; replace the violet with a light calm grey (stated to always work with pink) or a warm green. Second worked failure: bright orange curtains in a bedroom whose every other shade is complex and dusty — fixed by matching them to the more complex terracotta-brown already present in the cushions.
- **⚠️ Attention-direction as a deliberate lever, not just harmony**: saturated contrasting curtains pull a lot of attention to the window. **If the intended focal point is elsewhere** (in her example the printed headboard wall), the fix is to take the curtains down to roughly the wall colour so the accent moves to the bed. Colour choice is framed as choosing where the eye goes, which is a distinct idea from "does this match."
- **⚠️ Accent-wall rule 1 — an accent wall should be at least somewhat *darker* than the room's other walls.** A lighter accent wall "как будто бы проваливается, вместо того чтобы выглядеть каким-то главным цветовым пятном." Whether it is achieved with patterned wallpaper or plain paint makes no difference. Easiest colour choice is a darker shade of the same hue; a contrasting colour already present elsewhere in the room (her example: the floor cushions) also works.
- **⚠️ Accent-wall rule 2 — one accent wall per room.** With two (a patterned blue wall and a wood wall, separated by a stripe of the base colour) "комната как бы разваливается на две равнозначные части" and neither reads as the accent. A third example with three wallpapers at once is called simply "мешанина" with no discernible logic. **This restates the identical rule already on `Decor_and_Finish_Selection_Technique.md` — but that entry is from this *same* channel (Anuta Vlady, Alaska decor case, 2026-08-25), so it is a same-practitioner restatement, `single-account`, and must not be recorded as independent corroboration.**
- **⚠️ Accent-wall rule 3 — placement must be justified by an object.** "Акцентную стену нужно размещать именно там, где есть какой-то объект, который мы хотим дополнительно выделить" — a fireplace, a console, an entrance zone, a furniture composition, artwork. **Named as a большая ошибка: putting an accent wall on an arbitrary wall just to make the room livelier.** The accent wall is defined as a tool for reinforcing something, not a decoration in itself.
