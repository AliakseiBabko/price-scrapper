## Design Concept / Furniture — Durable Facts & Rules

- **Three lift-mechanism types compared, general taxonomy**: (1)
  hinge-only — cheapest, least convenient, rarely used by premium
  manufacturers; (2) spring mechanism — affordable, easy to service, but
  springs stretch and wear out over time, eventually failing; (3) gas-lift
  — most popular, near-silent/smooth damped motion, usable even by
  children or people with limited mobility, more durable with quality
  materials, but costs more than a spring mechanism.
- **⚠️ Gas-lift quality-selection checklist, generalizable to a
  Murphy-bed strut too**: (a) strut material/certification, typical rated
  service life **5-10 years**; (b) load rating must match mattress +
  bedding weight — corroborates `YT_9jDv0Iibbfw`'s independent finding
  that an under-rated strut fails to fully actuate; (c) **audible
  creaking or jerking during motion is a low-quality signal**, a real,
  checkable red flag at point of purchase or demo; (d) wider lift angle
  = easier access to whatever the mechanism reveals; (e) the frame the
  mechanism is bolted to must be rigid and well-secured — a weak frame
  deforms over time even with a good strut; (f) check strut
  replaceability/spare-parts availability with the seller before buying;
  (g) **a reliable gas-lift mechanism typically carries a minimum 2-3
  year manufacturer warranty** — a real benchmark to check a specific
  product against.
- **Common buyer mistakes flagged**: choosing a mechanism without
  accounting for mattress weight; buying the cheapest available
  mechanism (false economy, leads to frequent failures); ignoring
  manufacturer reviews/recommendations.
