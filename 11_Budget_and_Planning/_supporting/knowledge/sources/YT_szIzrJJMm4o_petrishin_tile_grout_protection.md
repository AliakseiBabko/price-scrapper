## Bathroom / Tiling — Grout-Joint Water/Mold Protection Technique

1. **⚠️ Problem mechanism**: tile grout joints darken over time because
   moisture and dirt penetrate the porous grout surface, which can also
   grow mold/mildew inside the joint itself — once this happens the
   joint becomes genuinely difficult to clean back to its original
   fresh-renovation appearance (not just cosmetically dulled, but
   structurally harder to restore).
2. **Prevention technique, timed immediately post-renovation**: while
   grout joints are still fresh (right after tiling, before any real-world
   moisture/dirt exposure), apply a water-repellent antibacterial grout
   sealant, purchased at the same specialty store as the grout itself, with
   an ordinary artist's paintbrush, painted carefully along each joint.
   Drying time: 2-3 hours; a second coat can be applied with no downside
   ("от этого хуже не будет"). Described as tedious/labor-intensive but
   worthwhile — once applied, the joints become effectively mold-resistant
   and much easier to keep clean going forward.
