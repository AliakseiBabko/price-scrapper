## Planning Rules

- **Absolute rule: wet rooms must be squared/rectangular regardless of shell geometry**; irregularity (trapezoidal, curved) may be absorbed into circulation space (corridor) instead — see Bathroom/WC section above for the full statement. This is the single most emphasized rule in the video.
- **All walls should be built straight/plumb, including compensating for structural column/wall protrusions** — speaker explicitly criticizes a common beginner mistake of leaving a small structural protrusion (e.g. a few centimeters of a load-bearing wall edge) as-is and just wallpapering over it; instead, build a false wall sized to the full protruding element's thickness (e.g. matching a column's thickness) so no wall surface has an uneven jog.
