## Rules / Heuristics — New Mechanisms

1. **Corrugated-conduit ("гофра") hidden-leak-travel mechanism**:
   developer-installed pipe is typically routed inside a corrugated
   sleeve/conduit. If a joint or fitting fails inside that conduit, water
   travels *inside the conduit itself* before finding an exit point at
   the joint between two conduit sections — meaning the visible leak
   location (e.g., a stain in the kitchen or kids' room) can be
   physically far from the actual failed fitting (e.g., in a corridor).
   **Practical consequence**: diagnosing this kind of leak can require
   opening not just the screed but the finish flooring too, and a large
   amount of built-in furniture over the affected area can make this
   very costly. A genuinely new hidden-leak diagnostic caution, distinct
   from this project's existing tee-joint/radial-vs-tee content.
2. **AC-condensate elimination heuristic**: when unexplained water stains
   appear, check whether the timing rules out an air-conditioner
   condensate-drain leak as the cause — condensate only forms while the
   AC unit is actively running, so a leak appearing in December (AC off
   season) can be confidently ruled out as condensate-related and pointed
   toward a plumbing/heating source instead. A concrete, checkable first
   diagnostic step.
3. **Radiator side-connection to protect the baseboard-drilling path**:
   this company connects radiator supply pipes from the side rather than
   routing them across the floor toward the middle, specifically so the
   pipe run doesn't cross the path where the baseboard will later be
   drilled/screwed to the wall — avoiding drilling into (and risking
   puncturing) the finish flooring or a hidden pipe when the baseboard
   goes in afterward.
4. **Heating-system pressure-test water-temperature wait, a specific
   number**: this source fills the heating system with water and waits a
   full 24 hours ("сутки") for it to reach ambient/room temperature
   before running the hydraulic pressure test — extends this project's
   existing (Sidorik-sourced) "let hot water cool to ambient before the
   timed hold" caution with a concrete wait-time figure, since a still-
   warming or still-cooling system's pressure reading is not stable
   enough to trust.
