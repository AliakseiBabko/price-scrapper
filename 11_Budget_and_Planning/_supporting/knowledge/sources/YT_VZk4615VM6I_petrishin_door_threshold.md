## Doors/Trim, Flooring — Removable T-Molding Transition-Threshold Technique

1. **Purpose (two functions)**: a transition threshold at a
   material-junction seam (tile-to-laminate in this demo) conceals an
   otherwise-visible seam unevenness (up to ~1-1.5mm even from a skilled
   installer) and physically holds down a floating laminate edge that can
   lift over time without it.
2. **Three-part construction**: (1) a static base channel fixed
   under/against the tile; (2) a decorative metal T-profile ("тэшка")
   cut to length; (3) a pre-cut rubber gasket that slides over the
   T-profile's stem, providing the friction fit that holds the whole
   assembly snug against both surfaces with no rattling.
3. **⚠️ No visible fasteners**: contrasted explicitly against threshold
   types screwed directly into the floor — this rubber-friction design
   needs no visible screws, a stated aesthetic advantage.
4. **Serviceability**: because it's held only by friction (not glued or
   screwed), a scratched/worn threshold can be pulled out and replaced
   later without special tools.
