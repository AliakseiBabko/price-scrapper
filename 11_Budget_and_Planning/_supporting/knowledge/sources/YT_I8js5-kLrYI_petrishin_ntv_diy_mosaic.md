## Genuinely new content

1. **⚠️ White tile adhesive required for glass mosaic**: glass mosaic is
   translucent enough that a dark-colored tile adhesive shows through
   and visibly dulls the tile's own shine/sparkle — must use white
   adhesive specifically for glass mosaic (not previously stated on this
   page, which has adhesive-classification content but not this
   color-specific glass-mosaic rule).
2. **Notched-trowel tooth-depth rule scaled to mosaic thickness**: the
   notched trowel's tooth depth should be roughly **half the mosaic
   tile's own thickness** — sized specifically to avoid excess adhesive
   squeezing up between the (very narrow) mosaic joints during
   installation. A mosaic-specific refinement of this page's general
   adhesive-application guidance, which doesn't currently scale notch
   depth to tile thickness this way.
