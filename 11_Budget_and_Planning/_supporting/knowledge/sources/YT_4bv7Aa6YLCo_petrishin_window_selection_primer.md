## Rules / Heuristics — New Detail

1. **Window anatomy named explicitly as the comparison framework**:
   reinforced frame profile, glazing unit (стеклопакет), hardware
   (фурнитура), windowsill (подоконник), drip cap/sill flashing (отлив),
   and gaskets (уплотнители) — each has its own function and its own
   comparison parameters, used as this video's own organizing structure.
2. **⚠️ Perspectives — brand does matter, contradicting an existing
   claim on this page's own topic**: this source names Rehau and Veka
   as the best-known, most-recommended profile brands, and recommends
   European manufacturers generally for hardware "reliability, quality
   metal, no rust/no wear" — in direct tension with the existing
   `Windows_Quality_and_Buying.md` claim (Zemstandart/Alexey Zemskov)
   that profile brand has essentially no effect on quality within a
   market tier. Recorded as an open disagreement, not resolved either
   way — added as a Perspectives note on that page.
3. **Profile thickness ladder with a stated tradeoff**: standard sizes
   are 58mm (thinnest, not of interest for residential use — used in
   non-residential contexts), 70mm, 70-82mm, and 90mm — thicker profile
   means better thermal/sound insulation but more premium market
   positioning (higher price).
4. **Chamber-count range and market-segment default**: profile chamber
   count typically runs 3-7; the mid/average market segment typically
   uses 5-6 chambers. More chambers within the frame profile track
   directly with thermal-resistance-coefficient and sound-insulation
   comparison figures — framed as the actually-useful comparison metric
   over chasing individual spec-sheet details (reinforcement type, chamber
   shape, PVC composition).
5. **Tilt-turn (поворотно-откидной) hardware mandated even without
   using the tilt-ventilation function**: recommended as the default
   choice over a simple turn-only mechanism specifically because it has
   more pressure/locking points around the sash perimeter, not because
   of the ventilation feature itself.
6. **Glazing-unit chamber count and add-on features, cost-linked**:
   glazing units typically come 2- or 3-chambered — more chambers means
   better energy-saving and sound-insulation, requiring a wider window
   profile to accommodate. Additional coatings, inert-gas fill between
   panes, or a deliberate offset in how panes of glass are spaced from
   each other can add anti-resonance properties, better sound/thermal
   insulation, and impact resistance — each raises final cost, framed as
   "better to pay a bit more than install a window that doesn't do its
   job."
7. **Gasket contour count and cross-section shape both matter, not just
   material**: two-contour sealing is generally sufficient, but a
   three-contour profile gives higher airtightness; more gaskets/contours
   raise both airtightness and draft protection. **Longevity depends on
   both material and cross-section shape — a gasket with an internal
   hollow chamber lasts longer.** Complements (doesn't duplicate) the
   existing single/double-lobe seal *replacement* decision rule on
   `Windows_Hardware_Selection.md`, which is about diagnosing an
   installed seal's wear, not selecting a gasket profile at purchase
   time. **Maintenance rule**: treat gaskets with automotive silicone
   grease before each cold season to extend service life.
8. **Windowsill material selection by function, not just aesthetics**:
   purely decorative sill → plastic (named brands: Danke, Möller);
   sill meant to visually match/blend with the room's interior → a
   costlier color/texture-matched option; sill meant for functional use
   as a reading nook or desk surface → wood or stone; porcelain tile
   also seen used as a sill material.
9. **Drip cap (отлив) fastening/length rule and a rattle fix**: must be
   securely fastened with every joint/gap sealed; length matters in both
   directions — too short leaves gaps, an unnecessarily long overhang
   rattles loudly in rain. **Fix for an already-rattling drip cap**:
   apply self-adhesive automotive vibration-damping sheet ("шумка"/
   sound-deadening mat) to its underside.
