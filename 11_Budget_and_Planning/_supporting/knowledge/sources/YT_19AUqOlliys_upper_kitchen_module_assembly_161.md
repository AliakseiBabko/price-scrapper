## Furniture / Built-ins — Upper Kitchen Module Assembly Technique

- **Pre-assembly checklist, general**: confirm all renovation work in the install room is complete before starting; unpack every module and inspect for shipping damage (corners specifically, as the most impact-prone point); cross-check each module against the kitchen's own design-project specification (size, position); verify all parts arrived by checking each panel's own factory labeling sticker against the specification; sort parts by module and lay them out along walls, clear of the assembly work area, before starting.
- **Confirmat-screw joining technique**: set the driver to a medium torque setting; align pre-drilled holes between mating panels before driving any screw fully.
- **Back-panel (оргалит) sizing rule**: measure the module's rear opening width, add 4mm, cut the back panel to that dimension — the extra 4mm is a fit allowance, not a measurement error.
- **Eurorail (евронавес) hanging-rail installation**: mount into the module so the rail sits flush against the side panel with its retaining hook engaged in a pre-cut notch; secure with screws; finish with a decorative cap over the fastener.
- **False-panel (фальш-панель) installation using eccentric fasteners**: insert expanding dowel sleeves into the panel's pre-drilled holes, place eccentric mechanisms into the module's side-panel holes, engage the panel's dowel sleeves into those mechanisms, then rotate each eccentric clockwise with a screwdriver to lock.
- **Range-hood duct cutout technique, genuinely new for this store**: mark the module's top-panel front-face center, extend a perpendicular line toward the long edges with a square, then mark a point 45mm back from the rear plane on that line — this is the duct-hole center. Center-punch (awl) the mark, then drill: match a wood-boring hole saw to the duct's own measured diameter, make a shallow guide cut from the front face until the pilot bit just breaks through the back, flip the panel and drill from the back roughly halfway, then flip again and complete the cut from the front — this three-pass, alternating-direction sequence is specifically to avoid chip-out/splintering on a laminated panel face. Repeat the same marking-and-drilling sequence for the module's bottom panel, referencing the top panel's own now-cut hole for alignment. Back-panel duct clearance: align the back panel against the top panel, mark two points spaced at the duct hole's own diameter, square perpendicular lines through them, and cut with a jigsaw into three pieces — discard the center piece, reattach the two remaining side pieces with 16mm screws and the manufacturer-supplied board-specific corner brackets.
- **Motion-sensor module assembly**: uses the same expanding-dowel-plus-eccentric hardware system as the false-panel technique above, applied to join the module's full carcass (bottom, both sides, back panel, mounting strip, top panel, eurorail hangers) rather than just one panel.
- **Standard eccentric-hardware assembly sequence, reusable across most remaining modules**: install dowel sleeves and expanding dowels in the bottom panel's pre-drilled holes first, then mount eccentric mechanisms on each side panel before joining side panels to the bottom, then complete with back panel, mounting strip, top panel, and eurorail hangers in that order.
- **Finish step**: cap every confirmat screw head with its decorative cover once assembly is complete.
- **Explicit stakes stated up front**: an assembly error in the best case forces full disassembly and rework; in the worst case damages parts beyond repair, requiring paid replacement parts (stated as "several thousand RUB per module," `unverified`, no specific figure given) plus remanufacturing lead time — framing for why the step-by-step precision in this video matters.
