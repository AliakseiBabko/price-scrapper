## Durable Facts — Bedside Electrical Point Placement Formula

The core content is a worked geometric formula for positioning bedside outlets/switches so they land symmetrically behind nightstands after furniture placement, rather than being hidden or off-center. Presented as the speaker's own design-office method — `unverified`, `single-account`.

- **Mattress-width starting points**: 160 cm and 180 cm cited as the two standard/most comfortable widths; 180 cm flagged as oversized, used only for large occupants or unusually large rooms.
- **Bed-frame allowance**: add 10 cm to mattress width because most bed frames are ~10 cm wider than the mattress itself (→ 170 cm / 190 cm). Halving this gives "half-bed" reference values of **85 cm / 95 cm** used throughout the rest of the calculation.
- **Formula for the bed's center axis, measured from the wall you're aligning to**:
  - From the exterior/window wall: offset = 15 cm (radiator false-wall allowance) + 15 cm (curtain zone) + 50 cm (nightstand depth) + half-bed (85 or 95 cm) → **165 cm for a 160 cm bed, 175 cm for a 180 cm bed**.
  - From the opposite (wardrobe) wall: offset = 60 cm (wardrobe clearance) + 50 cm (approach space) + 50 cm (nightstand) + half-bed → **245 cm for a 160 cm bed, 255 cm for a 180 cm bed**.
  - General reusable formula: **false-wall/radiator allowance + curtain zone + nightstand + half-bed-width**, adaptable to non-standard beds if you get the actual bed spec sheet from the furniture seller and substitute the real half-width.
- **Bedside light fixtures**: from the bed's center axis, offset 60 cm left and 60 cm right, mount at **170 cm height** — but only if the fixtures are meant to sit directly above the headboard (recommended default; an asymmetric-shaped fixture centered over a nightstand instead tends to look visually off).
- **Switch placement, option A (over the headboard)**: mount three switches on the vertical centerline of the bed at **120 cm height** — center switch is a two-way (проходной) switch for the main room light, left/right switches control the bedside lighting on their respective sides.
- **Switch/outlet placement, option B (over the nightstands, for a high-headboard bed)**: from the bed's center axis, offset **110 cm** (for a 160 cm bed) or **120 cm** (for a 180 cm bed) left/right, then mount at **70 cm height** — left-to-right: bedside-light switch, two-way main-light switch, one or two power outlets. Result (if measured correctly) is that these land centered over the nightstand at a comfortable height after furniture is placed.
- **Process note**: all of this must be finalized at the design stage, before rough-in electrical work begins — once wiring is installed it should be treated as locked, not subject to on-site changes.
