## Genuinely New Content

- **AC pre-install planning, even when deferring the actual purchase —
  new to this store's AC pages**: don't defer AC planning entirely just
  to save money now. Three concrete pre-install steps recommended even
  if the unit itself isn't bought yet: (1) the outdoor unit location is
  constrained to a window-facing wall unless the refrigerant lines are
  pre-routed — routing them later means an ugly large surface-mounted
  plastic conduit box, which the source assumes nobody actually wants;
  (2) route the condensate drain to the sewer system now (rather than
  the older practice of just letting it drip outside); (3) even without
  installing the unit, embed the refrigerant-line conduit/sleeve in the
  wall and cap it — years later, hang the indoor unit on the pre-routed
  conduit and the outdoor unit outside, with a clean, planned-looking
  result rather than retrofitted surface conduit.
- **Wall-squareness (90° corner) work is only needed selectively, not
  across the whole apartment — a minor scoping nuance not previously
  stated this explicitly**: precise 90° corners matter specifically
  where built-in or free-standing furniture will sit flush against two
  walls (kitchen, bathroom, toilet, any built-in-furniture zone) — not
  as a blanket whole-apartment requirement.
