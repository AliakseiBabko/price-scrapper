## Advertising / Promotional Content Notes

Per explicit user guidance: renovation-content channels are very often
run by the company doing the work, a sponsored partner, or a
real-estate/supplier affiliate — expected to be the **majority** of
sources processed for this topic (the user estimates 70-80% of sources
provided are from turnkey/full-service companies). This section flags
each source's own promotional format and which specific content within
it should be read as a commercial mention rather than a neutral,
universal recommendation. **Nothing below is deleted from the sections
that follow** — brand/property mentions stay in the record with their
sourcing, but should not be treated as validated general advice, and
none of them were carried into `Budgeting_Guide.md` (which stays brand-
and property-name-free by design — confirmed clean on review).

**A finer distinction, per follow-up user guidance**: within a
turnkey-company source, separate (a) **general market-rate/technique
information** the company reports (usually reasonably fair, since it
describes a real market the company also operates in) from (b) **the
specific tier/product/service the company steers the viewer toward**
(carries a structural incentive toward the company's own higher margin,
even when phrased as neutral advice — a turnkey company's revenue scales
with the tier a client picks). When a source's brand/tier mentions below
are tagged "commercial mention," this is the distinction being applied:
the surrounding market data is treated as normal evidence, but the
specific recommended choice is not adopted as a default without noting
that a cheaper in-category alternative likely exists in the same market
for a self-managed buyer.
