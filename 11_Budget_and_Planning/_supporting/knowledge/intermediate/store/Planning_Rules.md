# Planning Rules routing guide

The legacy store has no standalone top-level Planning Rules section. Its Planning Rules subsections remain verbatim in Durable_Facts.md (and their numeric entries in Numeric_Data.md) to keep this migration mechanical and lossless. A future taxonomy rewrite can move those subsections after a separately reviewed migration.
