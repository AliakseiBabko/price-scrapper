## 1. Architectural Concept & Layout Overview

This 60 m² apartment exemplifies modern comfort-class interior design with premium minimalist execution. Key architectural elements include:
- **Zoned Open Plan**: Combined kitchen, dining, and living room space separated from the private bedroom by a sliding glass/metal partition (`$1,350 USD` [dfc91747]).
- **Visual Expansion**: Full-length floor mirror in the main corridor placed directly opposite the living area entrance to mimic a large exterior window.
- **Concealed Storage & Radiators**: Radiators in the living room and bedroom are fully enclosed inside custom millwork cabinets with ventilation grilles.
- **Dedicated Concealed Laundry**: A hidden laundry and utility storage room accessible via a concealed flush door.

## 3. Uncosted Technical & Design Implementation Details (Sourced from `26bc9c25`)

> [!NOTE]
> The following technical solutions were detailed in companion video [[90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_minsk_world_60m2_design_tour_26bc9c25|26bc9c25]]. They expand on the implementation methods used in the apartment.

1. **Entrance Master Cut-Off Switch**:
   - Single master switch installed at the entrance door cuts power to all lighting fixtures across the apartment upon exit.
2. **Hidden AC Unit Inside Wall Cabinet**:
   - The split-system air conditioner is mounted inside an upper kitchen wall cabinet with ventilated doors, hiding the unit visually for 300+ days a year while remaining functional during hot summer months.
3. **Window Reveal Framing with Furniture Boards**:
   - Window sills and reveals are framed with Egger furniture boards protruding slightly past the wall edge, protecting corners from impact damage and simplifying cleaning.
4. **Window Reveal Power Outlets**:
   - Power sockets installed directly into window sills/reveals facilitate working with laptops or powering seasonal holiday decorations (e.g., Christmas trees).
5. **Dishwasher with Floating Sliding Door Hinge**:
   - Dishwasher appliance utilizes a floating sliding door hinge, avoiding the need to notch or cut lower kitchen baseboard plinths during door opening.
6. **Inverted Flat Aluminum Baseboard Hack**:
   - Recessed baseboard effect achieved using standard flat aluminum corner profiles inverted and set into pre-milled wall plaster slots.
7. **Waterproof Epoxy Topcoat over Bathroom Plaster**:
   - Decorative wall plaster on walls and MDF cabinet facades in the bathroom is sealed with an epoxy varnish topcoat (identical to microcement topcoats), rendering surfaces fully scrubbable and impervious to water.
