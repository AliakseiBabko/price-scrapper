## 1. The 7-Stage Framework

Applies to a "typical" 60–120 m² apartment. Not one specific real project — a generic pricing framework the studio uses in its own sales pitch, but internally coherent and arithmetic-consistent.

| Stage | Stated Range (USD/m²) | Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1. Design project | $39/m² (single figure) | 3D visualization (market-available materials), floor plan, construction drawings, full specification of what to buy and where |
| 2. Finishing works (labor) | $220–450/m² | All rough + finish construction labor, demolition through painting, including foreman/project-management coordinating subcontractors |
| 3. Rough materials | $100–200/m² | Adhesives, mixes, blocks, wiring, pipes |
| 4. Finish materials | $250–350/m² | Flooring, wall paint, etc. (parquet/tile specifically cited at $200–300/m² on its own) |
| 5. Appliances | $120–200/m² (of livable area) | All household appliances |
| 6. Interior fixtures | $70–110/m² | Lighting, plumbing fixtures, doors — if avoiding a named premium lighting retailer |
| 7. Custom furniture | $300–400/m² (see note) | All built-to-order cabinetry (studio's own base spec: Egger particleboard, Blum hardware) plus custom soft furniture |

**ASR note (Stage 7 upper bound)**: transcript renders this as "300-4 долларов," clearly truncated. Treated as $400 by consistency with the overall-total arithmetic check below, not asserted as directly legible.

## 2. Overall Total — Arithmetic Cross-Check

- **Original statement**: "качественный продуманный ремонт обойдётся вам в сумму от 1.100 до 18800 долларов за метр квадратный" — from $1,100 to $18,800/m².
- **Cross-check**: summing the 7 stages' own stated minimums: 39 + 220 + 100 + 250 + 120 + 70 + 300 = **$1,099/m²** — matches the stated $1,100 almost exactly. `confirmed`.
- Summing the stated maximums: 39 + 450 + 200 + 350 + 200 + 110 + 400 = **$1,749/m²** — does **not** match the stated $18,800 at all.
- **Conclusion**: the working overall range is **$1,100–$1,749/m²** by arithmetic reconstruction. The literal "$18,800" is an ASR/speech error (a spoken "1.749" or similar almost certainly mis-transcribed) and is **not used** anywhere in this project's synthesis.
