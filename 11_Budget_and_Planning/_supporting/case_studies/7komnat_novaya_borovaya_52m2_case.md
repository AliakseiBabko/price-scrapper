## 1. Project Overview

Two-room, 52 m² apartment in Novaya Borovaya, Minsk. Developer handover February 2025; full cycle from design project through move-in took approximately one year, completing around February 2026. The renovation company's own director (Aleksey) is the client, and the video is a walkthrough/interview with the company's founder (Vladimir Ermakov) — i.e. an internal case study used as company marketing, not an independent third-party project.

Notable non-standard/premium choices called out by the speaker himself as driving cost above the general market rate: floor-to-ceiling concealed interior doors, concealed (hidden) baseboards with shadow-gap rails throughout, engineered wood herringbone flooring, a vertical vertical radiator requiring re-plumbing approval, custom-order furniture and mirrors, and multiple lighting scenarios in the bathroom.

### 2a. Arithmetic Cross-Check (Level-4 Validation)

Performed by summing the source's own itemized figures against its own stated group totals — this is an internal consistency check, not an independent outside source, but it's a genuine validation step, not a guess:

- **Group 2**: $1,800 + $2,400 + $1,900 (upper bound of stated $1,800–1,900 range) + $2,700 + $2,900 + $5,800 = **$17,500 exactly**, matching the stated total precisely.
- **Group 1**: $50 + $900 + [design project] = $2,700 requires the design project to be **$1,750**. The speaker says "$1,700" earlier in the same passage, but the video description independently states $1,750, and the group-total arithmetic only closes with $1,750. **Two independent pieces of evidence agree on $1,750**; the $1,700 mention is recorded as a single, likely speech-slip or ASR artifact, not deleted from the record.
- **Group 4**: $14,000 + $6,800 + $12,500 + $6,800 + $2,800 = $42,900 vs. stated $42,800 — **$100 off**, plausibly absorbed by the Group 2 plumbing range rounding. Treated as corroborated within rounding, not a real contradiction.

## 3. Market-Level Rule Stated by the Speaker (Not Project-Specific)

The speaker explicitly distinguishes this project's own $1,346/m² from his stated *general* 2026 Minsk market guidance:

- **Typical 2026 Minsk renovation cost**: **≈$1,000/m²**, "often more."
- **Budget/simplified approach** (technical/simpler design project, budget materials): **$700–800/m²**, described as difficult to hit "in today's reality."
- This specific project is explicitly called *above* that typical range by the speaker himself, due to its concentration of non-standard, higher-cost choices (concealed doors, concealed baseboards, engineered wood flooring, custom furniture) — **do not treat $1,346/m² as the general Minsk benchmark**; treat $1,000/m² (typical) and $700–800/m² (budget floor) as the speaker's general-market claims instead, both `unverified` beyond this single source's own say-so (no independent/level-4 corroboration of the *market-wide* claim, as opposed to this *project's own* figures, which were arithmetically cross-checked in §2a).
