## 2. Two Arithmetic Cross-Checks (Level 4)

- **Plaster rate resolved**: transcript renders Stage 3's per-m² rate as "от полу тысяч руб" (nonsensical). Dividing the stage's own stated total (450,000 RUB) by its own stated area (~300 m²) gives exactly **1,500 RUB/m²** — used instead of the garbled fragment.
- **Flooring subtotal recovered**: Stage 10's flooring portion (85 m² floor area, rate 1,500 RUB/m²) was never multiplied aloud in the source. Summing every other explicitly-stated stage gives 4,710,000 RUB — **127,500 RUB short** of the stated grand total (4,837,500). That exact gap equals 85 × 1,500 = 127,500 — a clean, non-guessed recovery, not an assumption.

## 3. Scope Caveat

This smeta **excludes** the design-project fee and furniture/appliances entirely — neither is mentioned anywhere in the source. **Do not compare its ≈48,375 RUB/m² total directly against this store's broader "full realization" turnkey figures** (e.g. Бородатый Прораб's 150,000 RUB/m², which explicitly includes design + furniture + appliances) without adjusting for scope — they are not measuring the same thing.

## 4. Smeta-Literacy Rules Contributed (Durable, Delivery-Model-Agnostic)

- Price water/heating rough-in by fixture "points" (a sink needs 2: hot + cold), not by pipe length — avoids a common "turns out we needed more pipe" upsell.
- Price electrical rough-in by floor area, not cable length, for the same reason.
- A proper putty/skim-coat job has up to ~9-10 sub-steps (primer → base coat → sand → primer → fiberglass mesh → primer → finish coat → sand → primer/paint); a smeta line reading just "wall putty" hides scope and makes verification impossible.
- Verify flooring installation pattern (straight/diagonal/herringbone) is specified before work starts — pattern materially affects cost.
- Watch for scope commonly added mid-project rather than itemized upfront (partition reinforcement, plaster corner-bead/window-reveal work, ancillary tile-cutting) and common-area/lobby covering requirements some residential complexes impose.
- This company's own contingency buffer (~26% of subtotal) runs notably higher than this project's guide's stated 10–15% figure — single-source, recorded as a comparison point, not adopted as a replacement.
