### ⚠️ The verdict on the real question: can a manufacturer's own channel be trusted on materials it sells?

**Yes, with named caveats — and the evidence is behavioural rather than a matter of tone.** The trial was designed to test tier-steering, and the channel repeatedly steers *away* from spend:

- **Argues down массив**, the material buyers revere, as a glued-lamella panel at ~3× a veneered facade for the same look, with the ecological argument undercut.
- **Explains that the imitation looks more perfect than the real thing** — and diagnoses his own client's complaint about genuine veneer as an expectation set by an imitation German kitchen in the same flat.
- **Says outright that selling the dearer kitchen is more profitable for him, then insists cost-optimising clients take simpler ЛДСП facades plus a stone countertop.**
- **Demolishes two of his industry's selling claims, one favouring companies of his own size** ("special supplier access"; "a big manufacturer is cheaper").
- **Publishes an entire delivered object in the cheapest board, with the full price.**
- **Refuses PVC film with three specific reasons** rather than praising everything he could sell.

**The caveats that must stay attached:**

1. **Brand preferences (Egger over Cleaf/Kaindl and over Шексна/Невский/Lamarty; Blum; Grass) are a manufacturer's stated practice, not an independent comparison.** The only falsifiable quality claim is chipping at the cut edge on cheap board.
2. **The hiring/contract video is inherently self-serving in subject** — "how to choose a furniture maker," from one — and its closing "come to trusted companies" is not recorded as neutral advice. Its extractable content is the two demolished myths and the ~10% spread rule.
3. **"Come to us early" is simultaneously good sequencing advice and the pitch**, and is recorded with that noted. It survives because he draws an honest scope boundary that excludes most purchases.
4. **His self-reported durability data point** — his own laminate kitchen at five years with no problems — is the flattering case, and is the only longitudinal evidence offered.
5. **No absolute prices outside the one 2022 case.** Everything else is a ratio, and the vault should not infer levels from ratios.
