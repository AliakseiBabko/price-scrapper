### ⚠️ Why video 21 is the pick, with evidence from the spot-check

It is the **only manually-captioned video in the playlist**, and it fills a real gap. This vault holds Кузина's `H43m5bn7W8k`, which gives the *stages* of a design project (measurements → layout → styling → render → drawings). **This gives the actual deliverables, sheet by sheet** — which is what a client commissioning a project needs in order to know whether they are being short-changed.

**Three project tiers she distinguishes**: эскизный (visualisation only, no reference to real materials — for a client unsure of you or of the style), стандартный пакет (the minimum viable drawing set, with real materials and specifications), and полный (maximum detail, extra drawings, 3D views of contentious junctions).

**And the sheet list**: title → contents table → survey plan → survey plan after replanning → demolition/construction plan → furniture layout → sanitaryware plan → electrical and heating appliances → sockets and switches → ceiling with light fittings → switch-to-fitting binding plan → ceiling dimensions and niches (combined with ventilation) → floor plan → underfloor heating → elevation index → elevations → specifications.

**Individual items already visible in the spot-check that are directly useful to this project:**

- **⚠️ On the floor plan you must mark the tile starting point and its setting-out dimensions — *"это входит в обязанности дизайнера показать, как эту плитку разложить."*** A concrete scope item to demand.
- **⚠️ Underfloor heating: not under furniture, not under appliances, with offsets from walls, areas stated per zone, and agreed with the client.**
- **⚠️ She draws SEPARATE black-and-white electrical elevations**, and says she has not seen others do it — **because many sockets end up behind furniture and a ceiling outlet is easy to miss on a coloured drawing.**
- **⚠️ Label every electrical outlet even when it is not connected to a switch** (e.g. cabinet lighting switched by the door) — **her stated reason is self-checking: "чтобы вы могли сами себя перепроверить."**
- **And a detail that lands squarely on Round 5's finding**: her worked project goes from **2,800 mm ceiling height to 2,650** after floor levelling — **the finished-floor-level arithmetic that Round 5 established makes fixtures unusable when it is got wrong.**

### What the bathroom pair carries, from the spot-check

Real planning method — **start from the sewer riser, because WC position is constrained by it** — worked across three actual layouts, one of which requires replanning. Then selection constraints with genuine buying consequences:

- **⚠️ A bath-rim mixer fits an acrylic bath without difficulty; other bath materials may not — so choosing a rim mixer effectively forces the bath material.**
- **⚠️ Check the mixer's widest dimension against the bath rim before ordering — the manufacturer's drawings often omit it, so telephone the supplier.**
- **⚠️ Floor-standing mixers need clearance around them for cleaning, are essentially decorative, and are wrong for perhaps 90% of homes** — water runs off them and they are inconvenient in use.

### ⚠️ The finding that justified processing it

**It contradicts a claim this vault already holds, head-on, on a physical fact.**

- **Михайловская**: wallpaper means *"не видно трещин"* — cracks do not show through it. **This is the FIRST of her three justifications for specifying wallpaper at all.**
- **Шеврина, already on the wallpaper page**: **"wallpaper does not hide cracks — it accentuates them,"** with a mechanism, and with reinforcing mesh dismissed as *"пластырь на дыру в асфальте."*

**Recorded as a Perspectives block rather than folded into either side.** Two reasons it matters more than a typical disagreement here: **it is checkable rather than a matter of judgment**, and **the argument for wallpaper over paint in a new build rests substantially on it.** Шеврина's side is currently better supported — she gives a mechanism and backs the adjacent restorability claim with a worked case — **but it should be settled by a third source, not by weighing two assertions.**

**⚠️ And a process point worth keeping: I only found this because I grepped the destination page for existing coverage before writing the note.** Had I routed on the strength of the transcript alone, a contested claim would have been filed as a plain recommendation. **Check the destination before writing, not only after.**

### The rest of what it gave

- **The organising idea: wallpaper must not imitate what it cannot imitate, and the mechanism is TACTILE** — hard relief materials are known by touch, wallpaper is smooth, and the dissonance is what reads as fake. **Plus the render trap that explains why it keeps being specified: the failure is invisible in exactly the medium the decision is made in.**
- **Her test — ask the wallpaper what it claims to imitate, and take it only if the answer matches what you see and feel** — worked across five samples.
- **Three non-visual justifications**, including **the children's-room case where the material's weakness is the argument for it** (easily damaged, easily replaced), **with the caveat that the ecological claim applies to PAPER and not to non-woven.**
- **A checkable pattern test**: a repeat appearing twice within one roll width reads as a template; and **narrow rolls compound it**, since wider is better for seams but demands a larger repeat.
