### ⚠️⚠️ The owner's steer was right, and the numbers back it

**Countertops were the correct priority.** The five-part series delivered **~74 facts**, and three of the five covered ground the vault did not have — **HPL scored zero mentions on the 283-line worktops page before this round**, stainless two, керамогранит two.

### The findings that changed something

1. **⚠️⚠️ A HEALTH REFUSAL: liquid acrylic releases large amounts of STYRENE as the resin cures, and he will not use it for worktops.** Nor 6 mm sheet acrylic, which cracks. **New to the vault, from the person who would otherwise sell it.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ TWO FABRICATOR DISSENTS from what this vault records as desirable** — the same-material integrated quartz sink (*dirt collects in the corners*) and the flush-mounted hob (*the thermal sealant in the expansion gap degrades and needs replacing*). **Routed as Perspectives, not corrections.**
3. **⚠️⚠️ THE ETCHED-MATT-GLASS MECHANISM, which resolves a standing disagreement.** Etching creates micropores that grease enters and cannot be cleaned. **Polished tempered glass is durable, etched matt glass is a grease trap — the variable is the finish, not the material.**
4. **⚠️⚠️ THREE TOLERANCES AT THREE SCALES, from two unrelated sources in one batch** — the building at ±50 mm, finishing at ~10 mm, fitted furniture at **3 mm per wall just to install**. **They do not reconcile, which is why both sources insist on a post-handover measurement before anything is ordered.**
5. **⚠️⚠️ A HANDLE CHOICE PROPAGATES INTO WORKTOP HEIGHT**: gola profile + dishwasher forces **≥920 mm**, which then competes with the **15 cm minimum plinth** a Blum SPACE STEP needs. **Plinth height, worktop height and opening method resolve together or not at all.**
6. **⚠️ Two numbers that bear on quantities**: quartz at **52 kg/m²** needing a steel frame beyond **600 mm** of bar overhang, and porcelain stoneware's **mandatory 50 mm perimeter trim** — a waste figure that is neither a percentage nor a cutting allowance.
