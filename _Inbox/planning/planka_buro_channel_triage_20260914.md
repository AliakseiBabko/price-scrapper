## ⚠️⚠️ 3. The spot-check — chosen for EVIDENCE TYPE, and both failed it

**Since every topic was saturated, the two videos fetched were the two whose *evidence type* might be scarce** — a retrospective across 120 projects, and post-occupancy regret. **That is the fairest possible test of the channel, and it is the test the Сосновская channel passed.** Both of these failed it.

**`k8lB0jtuogE` — «Я отремонтировал более 120 квартир»** (7:57, 2026-08-25, 305 views)

- **⚠️⚠️ NOT A TECHNICAL RETROSPECTIVE. It is a bureau values-and-branding piece**, addressed as much to other contractors as to clients: *«не работайте с чудаками»*, *«не бойтесь расставаться с теми, кто тянет вас вниз»*, *«отказ от сомнительного проекта — это не потеря»*.
