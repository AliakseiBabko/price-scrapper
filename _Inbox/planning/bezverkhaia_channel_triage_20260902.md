### ⚠️ The triage signal for this channel: sponsorship, not topic

**The videos that look most technical are the ones most likely to be sponsored, and the unsponsored ones yielded the most.** Four of the six had a plausible sponsor slot and three were sponsored. **The two unsponsored technical videos took the top and third places; the three sponsored ones took second (only after heavy filtering), fourth and sixth.** The sponsored ones still contain real, checkable material-property content — that is why they were processed partially rather than skipped — but every brand-level verdict in them had to be discarded, and in one case a claim had to be quarantined outright as sponsor-shaped (the "dimmable neon implies low quality" assertion, see `12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Lighting_Design.md`).

**Practical rule for selecting from the remaining 157 videos: prefer her solo explainers over anything that reads as a product, brand, store or showroom feature — and expect a sponsor whenever a video's subject is a purchasable product category (fittings, paint, furniture, appliances) rather than a decision or a room.**

### Verdict — this is a Group A-style channel presented by a designer, but it is not yet vetted for the full pipeline

- **11.8 facts/video is well above the 1.0 floor** and above the Group B trial results that led Шеврина/SMBUREAU and Кузина to be reclassified. **Like those two, this channel breaks the Group B premise**: it routed to ten pages across five folders, including `12_Engineering_and_Systems` and `13_Surfaces_and_Finishes`, and produced structural, regulatory and material-mechanism content, not just design opinion.
- **Two things temper that, and both should be said plainly.** First, **the sample was deliberately chosen from Tier 1** — the six most concrete, most vault-mappable titles on the channel — so 11.8 is a best-case figure, not a channel average; a random draw would include the trend, tour and personal-brand formats this triage put in Tier 3. Second, **three of six required the advertising filter**, which is a materially higher sponsorship rate than any Group A channel processed so far.
- **The flooring page in particular is now near-saturated** — it already held five multi-material comparisons before this round, and hers was mostly corroborating apart from the three disagreements. **Do not expect another flooring-adjacent video from this channel to yield similarly.**
