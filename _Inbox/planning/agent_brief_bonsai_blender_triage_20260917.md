## 2. What this project is doing, so you can judge relevance

**The architecture is: canonical authored data → deterministic geometry compiler → IFC / DXF / quantities / renders.** IFC is the *issued* representation, **not** the authoring store. The model is **generated by `ifcopenshell` from CSV and JSON**, not hand-modelled in Bonsai.

> ⚠️ **That inverts the usual relevance test.** A video about *clicking through Bonsai's UI to draw a wall* is worth little here. A video about **IFC structure, scripted authoring, what Bonsai does to a file, phases, or drawing production from a model** is worth a lot. Say so explicitly when you judge a video.

The immediate goal the owner has stated: **produce 2D discipline plans (electrical, plumbing, ventilation) AND a 3D model AND renders, all from one source of truth** — with electrical wiring modelled **in 3D from the start**, not drawn as lines on a raster.
