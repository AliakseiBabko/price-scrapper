# Digital Toolchain — Change Log

Editorial history for [[18_Digital_Toolchain/Digital_Toolchain_Guide|Digital Toolchain]] and its `analysis/` pages. Not reader content.

- **2026-09-13 — Folder created, and `Drawing_Conventions_From_Practice.md` moved into it from `00_Master/`.** Per an explicit owner decision, after the owner asked whether this source class needed its own knowledge-base folder.

  **What this revisits.** The [2026-09-08 Group C scoping pass](../../_Inbox/planning/design_toolchain_sources_triage_20260908.md) §5 asked exactly this question and answered `00_Master/`, on the sound reasoning that *"the taxonomy is a taxonomy of the renovation, not of the project's paperwork"* and that `00_Master/` was already where the project's own method lived. **That decision was made when the class had zero processed sources.** It had eleven by 2026-09-11, with a second batch arriving.

  **Three things changed, and two of them are mechanical rather than editorial:**
  1. **⚠️⚠️ `00_Master/` IS EXPLICITLY EXCLUDED FROM THE PAGE-SIZE GATE.** `tools/check_page_sizes.py`'s `NUMBERED_FOLDER_RE` is `^(?!00_)\d{2}_` — it skips `00_Master` by design, as project docs rather than wiki pages. **So `Drawing_Conventions_From_Practice.md` had been growing ungated**, reaching 243 lines past the ~300 soft target with no fragmentation check ever run against it, while every comparable page in the vault was gated. Moving it into a numbered folder puts it inside the backstop and the FRAGMENTED check for the first time. **This was the decisive argument, and it was not visible in 2026-09-08.**
  2. **`00_Master/` had accumulated four distinct kinds of document** across 33 `.md` files — the project's own decisions (`Design_Concept`, `Family_Requirements`, `project_decisions`), model and geometry status (`V0_*`, `Model_and_Views`, `Wall_Model_Closure_Check`), working discipline (`Evidence_Reading_Discipline`, `Validator_Design_Discipline`, `wiki_page_format`), and general toolchain knowledge — with nothing in the folder distinguishing them.
  3. **The vault already had the precedent for the split.** `17_Design_and_Ergonomics/` holds general practitioner design knowledge while `00_Master/Design_Concept.md` holds this apartment's own palette. The same line applies here, and drawing it makes the folder's scope self-policing.

  **The split as adopted.** `18_Digital_Toolchain/` = **general, source-derived** knowledge: AI workflows over construction data, 2D plan production and drawing conventions, 3D modelling and visualisation, cost and quantity take-off, tool interchange. **`00_Master/` keeps this project's own toolchain decisions and status** — `Model_and_Views.md`, `Planning_Project_Deliverable_Set.md`, `Sheet_Production_Roadmap.md`, `Revit_AutoCAD_Integration_Strategy.md`, `How_To_View_Outputs.md`, the `V0_*` pages, and the `_Inbox/planning/` gap analyses. **Nothing but `Drawing_Conventions_From_Practice.md` moved.**

  **Numbered (`18_`) rather than underscore-prefixed**, because `AGENTS.md` reserves the underscore prefix for cross-cutting *infrastructure* that is "not a topic" (`_Sources/`, `_Archive/`, `_Drawings/`, `_Knowledge/`). This behaves exactly like `17_` — practitioner attributions, extraction notes in `_Sources/`, a source-notes page, content gathered from external sources. It is a topic.

  **Link handling follows the `_supporting` dissolution precedent** (2026-08-30): **live cross-references were rewritten; historical records were left as written**, because they record what was true when they were written. Rewritten: `00_Master/Evidence_Reading_Discipline.md`, `00_Master/Planning_Project_Deliverable_Set.md`, `data/layout_rules/rules.jsonl`, `_Inbox/planning/youtube_channel_queue.md`. **Deliberately left pointing at the old path**: `_Knowledge/store/Change_Log.md`, `00_Master/processed_sources.csv`, the two triage documents, `deep_research_review_geometry_20260911.md`, and the two `_Sources/` extraction notes — all of which are dated records of a past state. The moved page's own relative links were re-depthed from `../` to `../../`.

  **⚠️ One thing a folder does not fix, and it needs a separate change**: the intake wrapper's 34-bucket taxonomy is entirely renovation-domain, so toolchain content has no bucket and falls to `Other / Unclassified`. Buckets added to `.agents/skills/renovation-knowledge-intake/SKILL.md` in the same session — see that file's own history.

- **2026-09-13 — AI toolchain batch, Round 1: 11 videos, 56 new facts, yield 5.1. Three new `analysis/` pages.** Triage: [`ai_toolchain_sources_triage_20260913.md`](../../_Inbox/planning/ai_toolchain_sources_triage_20260913.md). Pages created: `AI_Reading_Construction_Drawings.md`, `AI_For_Concept_And_Visualisation.md`, `Agent_Connected_CAD.md`. **FIVE THINGS WORTH CARRYING:**
  1. **⚠️⚠️ NINE OF ELEVEN SOURCES WERE ENGLISH, AND THE LANGUAGE RULE BIT IN THE OPPOSITE DIRECTION TO USUAL.** This vault's standing rule 1 exists to stop an auto-translated ENGLISH track being taken for a Russian source. Here the same rule required `--languages en` per video, because the default `ru,en` would have pulled auto-translated RUSSIAN captions for nine English-spoken videos. **Every transcript was fetched in its original spoken language and the `language` field was verified per file after fetching.** → **The rule is "original language", not "Russian" — and a batch can invert which way it points.**
  2. **⚠️⚠️ A SOURCE CLOSED A GAP THIS VAULT HAD EXPLICITLY RECORDED AND LEFT OPEN.** `YT_3tAYEJTyUFY` was routed on 2026-09-11 with the complaint, in its own CSV row, that it had *"NO ACCURACY MEASUREMENT of any kind."* `ItW-ielFvGg` is the same practitioner's same workflow two months later **with a 44-question benchmark: 86% / 98% / 100% accuracy at 104,000 / 66,000 / 1,400 tokens.** → **Recording precisely what a source FAILED to provide is what made the follow-up recognisable as the answer.**
  3. **⚠️⚠️ THE MEASURED RESULT SPLITS IN A WAY THE HEADLINE HIDES.** The vendor sells "50x less tokens, 20% more accurate" as one number. **The vector extraction buys nearly all the accuracy (86 → 98%); the database buys nearly all the cost reduction (66,000 → 1,400) and only two points of accuracy.** → They are separable purchases, and which one matters depends on whether your problem is being wrong or being expensive.
  4. **⚠️⚠️ AN OUTSIDE PRACTITIONER INDEPENDENTLY RESTATED THIS PROJECT'S OWN STANDING RULE 9 — AS A WAY OF CATCHING A MACHINE'S MISREADING.** The order-of-magnitude cross-check (a cable tray measured at 7 m, where another drawing gives the building as 35 m and the run spans it, is *clearly wrong*) is chain closure, reached with no connection to this project. **The strongest external validation `Evidence_Reading_Discipline.md` has.** Two more arrived the same way: an agent-connected Revit failing at spatial logic while succeeding at transcription corroborates the silently-supplied-values rule, and a practitioner reverse-engineering IFC by hand argues for reading the standard rather than inventing fields.
  5. **⚠️ A TRIAGE CORRECTION WORTH KEEPING: A ONE-MINUTE VIDEO PREDICTS LOW VOLUME, NOT LOW VALUE.** `sujS9Mgveo4` was flagged a likely skip on the title-skim (60 seconds, "perfect 3D" in the title). It was fetched anyway and **turned out to hold the only measured dimensional-fidelity check in the batch** — 610 drawn against 616 modelled — answering the open question the 35-minute masterclass left hanging. **The masterclass measured nothing.**
  **Also: one source was routed as a DOCUMENTED ANTI-PATTERN rather than as technique.** `EibFZPrtAp0` uploads a plan image to a chat model, asks for "all the dimensions... as copy-paste format for AutoCAD", and pastes the result straight into the drawing **with no verification step of any kind** — precisely the failure every other source in the batch measures or designs around. Recorded as what not to do, the same way this vault records a self-flagged live-wiring demonstration. **And every model name, version, ranking and free-tier claim across all eleven sources was deliberately discarded**, per the standing rule that capability verdicts date within months.
