# What the flat actually permits — a structural reading before any layout

**2026-09-18.** Written against the compiled model (`v0-existing/spec.json`, 25 walls, schema v2), not against any drawing. Every coordinate below is the compiler's.

The owner's question: *"It doesn't sound like our possibilities are without any limits — we're limited a lot."* **He is right, and the limits are sharper than they look.** This page says what they are, and what genuinely remains free.

---

## 1. All daylight arrives from ONE side

Three windows and the лоджия glazing, and every one of them is on the **south** façade, which steps northward as it runs west:

| opening | host | x extent | y (south face) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | ---: |
| `O3` living-room window | `MC` | 9381 … 12946 | 8231 |
| `O2` middle-room window | `MB` | 6131 … 9131 | **7561** — the most southerly |
| `O4a`/`O4b` window + лоджия door | `MA` | 2931 … 5881 | 9051 |
| `O9` лоджия glazing, full height and length | `M2 → M6b` | — | the mitred south-west corner |

The entrance `O8` is at **y 15990**, the far north. So the flat is **~10 m deep on one axis, lit from one end of it.**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **⚠️⚠️ THIS IS WHY `DI-008` IS NOT A PREFERENCE.** The owner proposed entrance → storage → bed → window as *"kind of a universal rule as I see it now."* The building has already imposed it: every habitable room is entered from the north and lit from the south, so **every room has the same depth axis and the same light gradient.** A rule that would be arbitrary in a flat with windows on two sides is here simply a description of what the daylight does.
>
> **The consequence is that the rule is testable, not just recordable** — a placement can be checked against the ordering, because there is only one axis to order along.

## 2. The flat is a comb: one service spine, three bays hanging south

**The north strip, y 12600 … 16240**, holds everything that cannot move: the entrance `O8`, the прихожая, the туалет and ванная, both ventilation shafts, and both plumbing anchors.

**Three habitable bays hang south off it**, separated by the two 75 mm dividers `G8` (x 6056…6131) and `G7` (x 9131…9206):

| bay | x extent | width | daylight | concrete return |
| :--- | :--- | ---: | :--- | :--- |
| **west** | 2931 … 6056 | ~3125 | `O4a`/`O4b` + the лоджия | **`R6`, 1750 clear** |
| **middle** | 6131 … 9131 | **3000** | `O2` | `R8` / `R9`, 1490 each |
| **east** | 9206 … 12946 | ~3740 | `O3` | **`R7`, 1750 clear** |

⚠️ **The bay widths are set by `G7` and `G8`, which are 75 mm block and genuinely movable** — and they are close to the only real freedom in the plan. But moving either **re-proportions two bays at once**: they are the shared boundaries, so width taken from the middle room is width given to a neighbour. There is no slack to distribute.

## 3. ⚠️⚠️ The two wall-bed positions are structurally symmetric, and that is not a coincidence

`R6` and `R7` are the **same element on opposite sides of the flat** — each is the north continuation of a façade return, one per *outer* bay:

- `R6` — x 2981…3231, y 9350…11100, directly north of `MA` → the **west bay**, the 9,36 room
- `R7` — x 12696…12946, y 8531…10280, directly north of `MC` → the **east bay**, the living room

Both are 250 mm concrete with **1750 mm clear**. The middle bay has no equivalent: it gets `R8`/`R9` at **1490** instead, and those flank the window wall, which `DI-007` rules out for sleeping.

> **So the flat contains exactly two places a wall bed can be fixed to concrete, they are one per outer bay, and they correspond one-to-one with the two Phase-2 scenarios.** `SC-A` puts adults on `R7` and the boy in the `R6` bay; `SC-B` swaps them. The symmetry is why both scenarios are feasible on the same shell — and why neither needs the other's wall.

**And both are 1750, so both take 1600 and neither takes 1800.** The preference *"place 1800 if possible"* is not satisfiable on concrete anywhere in this flat.

## 4. The wet zone is the hardest constraint in the plan

- **`P1`** — туалет, beside `V1`. Hot and cold water — **the flat's ONLY water source** — plus the main sewer stack and the capped towel-rail tails. Immovable.
- **`P2`** — between `R3` and `V2`, 400 × 200, **sewer only, no water**. Serves the kitchen and a potential laundry. Immovable.
- **Kitchen water arrives from `P1` under the floor.** So the kitchen is tied to a run that starts in the туалет.
- **`T1`** — the towel-rail tails cross `G4b`, a known penetration, and *their emergence point fixes where the rail can go.*

⚠️ **`DI-001`'s laundry is constrained by `P2` and by nothing else nearly as much.** A laundry wants drainage; `P2` is the only second drain and it does not float — the record is explicit that *"the clearance from R3 to V2 IS 200, so P2 does not float in that gap, it defines it."*

## 5. What is actually free

| fixed — cannot change | free — but see the cost |
| :--- | :--- |
| `R1`–`R9` concrete frame | `G7`, `G8` bay widths — **but they re-proportion two bays at once** |
| the three windows and the лоджия glazing | `G6`, `G4a`–`G4d`, `G4C` — wet-zone and corridor partitions |
| entrance position `O8` | the kitchen/living divider line |
| `V1`, `V2` shafts; `P1`, `P2` anchors | the retractable glass partition line and its parked position |
| the 2500 mm clear height | furniture, fittings, and all first-fix routing |

**That is the whole of it.** The owner's instinct — *"we're not going to change this a lot"* — matches what the geometry says: the frame, the light and the water are all fixed, and they between them decide most of the plan.

## 6. ⚠️ What I would add to the owner's own rules

**Three that follow from the above and are not yet stated anywhere:**

1. **The middle bay cannot host a wall bed at all.** Its only concrete is `R8`/`R9` at 1490 — under even a 1400 bed once a cabinet is counted — and both are at the window end, which `DI-007` reserves for study. **So the shared children's room needs ordinary beds, and that is a constraint rather than a choice.** It also means the middle bay is the one room whose furniture can be rearranged freely later, because nothing in it is fixed to structure.

2. **`G7` and `G8` are a single decision, not two.** They set all three bay widths between them, and the middle bay at 3000 mm is the tightest. Any width question — *"can two desks fit at `O2`?"*, *"does the two-sided storage block fit?"* — is really a question about where `G7` and `G8` sit.

3. **The first-fix must serve both scenarios in the two OUTER bays only.** The middle bay's role is the same in `SC-A` and `SC-B` — the girl's room — so it needs one wiring answer. The west and east bays swap occupants, so each needs wiring that works as an adult bedroom *and* as a child's bedroom. **That halves the problem, and nobody had noticed it was halved.**

## 7. What is genuinely undecided

- **The living-zone window band** — desk or not. `DI-008` contradicts itself here and the answer differs between `SC-A` and `SC-B`. See `room_depth_bands.csv`, `BAND-KL-3`.
- **Bed position within the middle bay.** The owner said *"next to `R4` or `R5`, or adjacent to `G8` or `G7`"* — but `R4`/`R5` are at **y 12600…13650**, the room's *entrance* end, which `DI-008` assigns to storage. Either the bed is in the storage band or the rule bends here; it is not yet clear which.
- **Cabinet external width** for a 1600 wall bed. Until it is known, `1600 on R6/R7` is plausible and not demonstrated.
- **`V1`'s footprint**, which is unestablished and blocks any question about clearance around it.
