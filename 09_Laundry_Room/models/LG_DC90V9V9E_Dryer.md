## 🎯 Why It Was Selected

1. **Self-Cleaning Condenser**:
   - **Reasoning**: The absolute number one convenience feature. The dryer flushes its own condenser radiator with condensation water multiple times during each cycle. This prevents lint build-up, maintains maximum energy efficiency, and spares you from manually vacuuming or scraping the radiator fins.
2. **Dual Inverter Heat Pump**:
   - **Reasoning**: Two cylinders in the compressor adjust speed dynamically, achieving high energy efficiency (A+++ energy rating) and drying fabrics gently at lower temperatures, preventing shrinkage.
3. **Eco Hybrid**:
   - **Reasoning**: Allows you to choose between saving energy (using heat pump mode) or saving time (hybrid mode which uses a heating element to dry clothes faster).

## ⚠️ Concerns and Technical Risks

* **Stacking Configuration**:
   - **Detail**: To stack this dryer securely on top of the F4V9LA2W 13 kg washing machine, you must use the official **LG KSTK1** stacking connection kit. Ensure the installation is level to prevent the dryer from sliding due to drum spin vibrations.
* **Drain Connection**:
   - **Detail**: While it has a built-in water collection drawer, it is highly recommended to connect the dryer's drain hose directly to the sewage system in the laundry room to avoid having to empty the drawer manually after every dry.
