## 🎯 Why It Was Selected

1. **i-DOS Intelligent Auto-Dosing**:
   - **Reasoning**: The machine automatically measures the exact amount of liquid detergent and fabric softener needed based on the weight, fabric type, and soil level of the load. This prevents detergent overdosing (which leaves soapy residue on children's clothes) and underdosing (which leaves dirt/stains), saving water and detergent.
2. **European Assembly**:
   - **Reasoning**: This premium model is manufactured and assembled in Europe, ensuring strict quality control and long-term mechanical reliability.
3. **Home Connect (Wi-Fi)**:
   - **Reasoning**: Allows monitoring of wash progress and notifications (e.g. when a cycle finishes) on your phone, making laundry management easier.

## ⚠️ Concerns and Technical Risks

* **Premium Price Tag**:
   - **Detail**: The i-DOS system and European assembly increase the price of the machine significantly.
* **9 kg Load Restriction**:
   - **Detail**: The load capacity is still capped at 9 kg, which requires multiple washes for large bedding sets.
