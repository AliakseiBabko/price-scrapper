## 🎯 Why It Was Selected

1. **SelfCleaning Condenser**:
   - **Reasoning**: Automatically flushes the condenser radiator up to four times during the drying process. This ensures the dryer always runs at peak energy efficiency and prevents you from having to manually clean the delicate metal cooling fins.
2. **SensitiveDrying System**:
   - **Reasoning**: The drum structure and curved paddles slide clothes gently on a cushion of warm air rather than dropping them flat, resulting in softer, crease-free laundry.
3. **High Energy Efficiency**:
   - **Reasoning**: Utilizing heat pump compressor technology, this dryer uses a fraction of the electricity consumed by traditional heating-element condensation dryers.

---

## ⚠️ Concerns and Technical Risks

* **Extended Drying Cycles**:
   - **Detail**: Heat pump dryers dry clothes at lower temperatures (to protect fabrics). Consequently, a full dry cycle takes longer than standard hot-element dryers (usually ~2 to 2.5 hours).
