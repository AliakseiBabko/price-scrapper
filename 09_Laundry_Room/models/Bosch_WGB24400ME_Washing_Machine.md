## ⚠️ Concerns and Technical Risks

* **Smaller Load Capacity (9 kg)**:
   - **Detail**: In a family of 4 with children, a 9 kg drum is significantly smaller than LG's 13 kg drum. Washing large bedding sets will have to be split into separate loads.
* **No Auto-Dosing (in this model)**:
   - **Detail**: Does not feature the i-DOS auto-dosing system. You must measure and add detergent manually.
