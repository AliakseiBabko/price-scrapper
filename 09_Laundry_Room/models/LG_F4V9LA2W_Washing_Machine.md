## 🎯 Why It Was Selected

1. **Unmatched 13 kg Loading**:
   - **Reasoning**: In a family of 4 with children, the volume of laundry (bedding, towels, sports clothes) is huge. The 13 kg drum handles large bulk loads (such as two full sets of double bedding plus bath towels) in one go, cutting down the weekly laundry cycles in half compared to standard 9 kg machines.
2. **TurboWash 360 (Speed)**:
   - **Reasoning**: This cycle uses 4 multi-directional spray nozzles to thoroughly wet and rinse clothes quickly, completing a full load wash in only 39 minutes without compromising washing performance.
3. **AI DD Fabric Protection**:
   - **Reasoning**: The machine detects the weight and fabric softness of the load, then automatically adjusts the wash motions. This protects children's cotton clothes from wearing out, thinning, or shrinking.
4. **Direct Drive Inverter**:
   - **Reasoning**: Direct Drive eliminates the belt, connecting the motor directly to the drum. This reduces vibrations and noise, making it highly reliable for heavy daily use.

## ⚠️ Concerns and Technical Risks

* **Appliance Depth (Vibrations)**:
   - **Detail**: The machine is deep (~61.5 cm or ~66 cm including the door/hoses). It requires a solid, leveled concrete floor to prevent wobbling during high-speed spins (1400 RPM).
* **No Auto-Dosing**:
   - **Detail**: This specific model does not have an automatic detergent dispenser (unlike Bosch's i-DOS). You must dose liquid gel or powder manually for each wash.
