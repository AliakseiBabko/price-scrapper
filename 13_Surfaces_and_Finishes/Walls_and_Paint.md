## Do's

| Rule | Applies To | Reason | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Use decorative slats/panels for visual zoning instead of moving walls | Living Room, Bedrooms, Hallway | Significant budget savings over full demolition/rebuilding | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_budget_video_4b421350.txt` |
| Allow wet gypsum plaster to dry completely (2-3 weeks) before applying putty | All Rooms | Prevents plaster detachment, cracking, and mold growth | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_mistakes_video_21ade3f6.txt` |
| Apply paint using airless sprayers for large wall areas | All Rooms | Achieves near-perfect finish uniformity across extensive wall surfaces | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_apartment_renovation_guide_360f4c7c.txt` |
| Choose quality wallpaper over smooth paint-grade plaster finish on tight budgets | Bedrooms, Living Room, Hallway | Saves high wall preparation labor costs while achieving clean, durable wall finishes | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_a8e90887.txt` |
| Install aluminum concealed door frames before wall plastering and puttying | Interior Door Openings | Enables seamless flush plaster/putty alignment; door leaf is hung after final wall finishes | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_2_61e3a372.txt` |
| Dismount developer radiators from walls prior to wall plastering | Rooms with Radiators | Ensures smooth, complete plastering behind radiators rather than uneven patching around mounted units | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_7_b385361e.txt` |
| Apply betokontakt or a scrubbed adhesive tile-glue layer over smooth monolithic concrete before plastering | Monolithic Concrete Walls | Prevents heavy plaster coats from debonding and cracking off smooth concrete substrate | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_7_b385361e.txt` |
| Document per-wall labeled areas, not a lump total, per Zemskov/Zemstandart | Gas-concrete-block walls | A lump total only supports a plastering calc; per-wall areas support block/adhesive quantity by thickness | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260810_gas_block_wall_material_calculation_2e558d93.txt` |
| Use specialized mineral or polyurethane block adhesive, not tile adhesive or cement-sand mix, per Zemskov/Zemstandart | Gas-concrete-block walls (beyond the first course) | Tile adhesive is a real ~50% overpay; cement-sand mix uses ~5x the material and is slow/weak | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260810_block_adhesive_destructive_comparison_18922877.txt` |
| Choose plain (no-pattern) wallpaper over patterned for lower material waste, per RemProektMD | All Rooms | Pattern-matching between strips uses more material per room than a plain roll of the same size | [[_Sources/YT_OP8ALhLynHE_remproektmd_12_money_saving_tips\|OP8ALhLynHE]] |

## Don'ts

| Rule | Applies To | Risk | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Do not demolish walls unnecessarily on tight budgets | All Rooms | Demolition costs accumulate 4x (demolish, haul, rebuild, plaster) | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_budget_video_4b421350.txt` |
| Do not use painted smooth plaster for dark-colored window reveals | Window Openings | Dark paint rapidly fades under direct sunlight and plaster joints crack from frame thermal expansion (use color-matched dark composite or PVC reveals) | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_3_a0e895b1.txt` |
| Do not install window sills or window reveal finishes before wall putty sanding is complete | Window Openings | Heavy airborne plaster and putty sanding dust irreversibly ruins new window sills and reveal surfaces | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_4_21a6e3c1.txt` |
| Do not glue gas-concrete blocks with ordinary construction/mounting foam, per Zemskov/Zemstandart | Gas-concrete-block walls | Real secondary expansion pushes blocks out of alignment, despite looking similar to the correct specialized foam-glue | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260810_block_adhesive_destructive_comparison_18922877.txt` |
| Do not buy the full calculated block quantity in one purchase, per Zemskov/Zemstandart | Gas-concrete-block walls | Exact final quantity is never predictable in advance (openings, cutting) — a second purchase is unavoidable anyway | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260810_gas_block_wall_material_calculation_2e558d93.txt` |
| Join drywall framing elements with a professional crimping tool, not screws, per Zemskov/Zemstandart | Drywall false walls | Screws locally deform the drywall face at every head; a professional crimp is essentially unbreakable | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260810_drywall_false_wall_masterclass_6d77f6f5.txt` |
| Cut drywall sheets in place rather than by tape-measure-and-pencil, per Zemskov/Zemstandart | Drywall false walls | Same eventual result with fewer wasted steps | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260810_drywall_false_wall_masterclass_6d77f6f5.txt` |
