# Ceilings Guide - Do's and Don'ts

## Do's

| Rule | Applies To | Reason | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Coordinate ceiling heights with door openings and cabinet heights | All Rooms | Prevent collision with tall furniture or door trims | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_vid1_transcript_d04723c5.txt` |
| Install full-height cabinetry with top false panel before stretch ceiling | Wardrobe, Kitchen | Enables mounting stretch ceiling track to cabinet false panel for seamless built-in look | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_tips_video_f23c504a.txt` |
| Prime and paint bare concrete ceiling slabs before installing stretch ceilings | All Rooms | Seals concrete surface and prevents mortar dust or debris from flaking onto top of stretch membrane | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_4_21a6e3c1.txt` |
| Construct a rigid drywall framing box above full-height kitchen cabinetry lines | Kitchen | Accepts stretch ceiling perimeter track on its vertical face so cabinet doors open smoothly underneath | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_4_21a6e3c1.txt` |

## Don'ts

| Rule | Applies To | Risk | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Do not lower ceilings excessively in low-height apartments | Living Room, Bedrooms | Reduces living comfort and space feel | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_vid1_transcript_d04723c5.txt` |
