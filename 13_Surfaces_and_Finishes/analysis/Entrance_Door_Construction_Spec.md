# Doors & Trim — Entrance Door Construction & Spec Evaluation

Detail page for [[13_Surfaces_and_Finishes/Doors_and_Trim|Doors & Trim]]. Companion to [[13_Surfaces_and_Finishes/analysis/Entrance_Doors|Entrance Doors]], which holds sizing, installation, pricing tiers and the security dispute.

**How to evaluate an entrance door's construction — the checkable thresholds, the shop-floor tests, and the marketing numbers that mean nothing.**

> **Why this page exists (2026-09-14): the vault's entrance-door material was almost entirely about INSTALLATION and PRICE. It had no procedure for judging a door before buying it. Two manufacturers arriving in one batch supply one, and where they converge they do so independently.**
>
> ⚠️⚠️ **READ THE INCENTIVES FIRST. Every source on this page sells doors**, and the two that supply most of it sell expensive ones. **What earns their place is the FORM of their claims — mechanisms and numbers you can test — not their neutrality.** Where they merely assert, this page says so.

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. The causal chain the vault was missing

**Steel thickness is not only a burglary question, and this is the single most useful idea here:**

> ***«Толщина стали… это не только взломостойкость. Она ещё отвечает и за геометрию всей металлоконструкции, то есть обеспечение прилегания дверного полотна к дверной коробке. И именно поэтому правильное прилегание будет обеспечивать ещё и шумоизоляцию.»*** — Бастион-С

**thickness → the metalwork holds its geometry → the leaf seats against the frame → it seals → it soundproofs**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ This is why "buy thin steel and pay for soundproofing instead" does not work as a strategy: they are the same variable.** **Novakey reaches the identical chain independently** — thin metal deforms from a slammed door or something caught under the threshold, loses geometry, **and then fails specifically in the lock zone.**
>
> **→ It also explains the most common complaint about entrance doors — a key that will not turn, a door needing a shoulder or a lift on the handle. That is usually a geometry failure, not a lock failure.** See [[13_Surfaces_and_Finishes/analysis/Entrance_Doors|Entrance Doors]] for the two competing root causes of that symptom.

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Steel thickness — four figures, and they sort by the seller's price position

| Source | Stated minimum | Date | What they sell |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gusev** | **1.2 mm is adequate** | 2025-11-28 | the cheapest of the four |
| **Gusev** | **"at least 1.5 mm"** | **2024-10-22** | ⚠️⚠️ *the same seller, 13 months earlier* |
| **Novakey** | **not less than 2 mm** | 2026-01-04 | a full ladder incl. premium |
| **Бастион-С** | **under 3 mm "doesn't qualify"** | 2024-03-10 | premium |

> **⚠️⚠️ THE RECOMMENDED MINIMUM TRACKS WHAT THE SOURCE STOCKS, across four data points, monotonically.**
>
> **This does not decide which figure is right, and it should not be read as "they are all just talking their book" — the two higher figures come with mechanisms and tests, the lowest comes with an assertion.** **What it does establish is that no single vendor's minimum can be taken as the market's answer.**

**⚠️ Бастион's thresholds are stated as things you can do to a sample, which is what makes them usable:**

| Attack | Threshold |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cutting with an ordinary utility knife** | **under 3 mm cuts; over 3 mm needs power tools** |
| **Prising with a crowbar** | 1–1.5 mm prises easily; 2 mm harder but achievable; 3 mm *«крайне проблематично»* |

⚠️ **He states he prised a door himself on camera at the first attempt. His own demonstration on his own channel, not independently reviewed.**

**⚠️ Leaf thickness by application (Gusev, corroborated by the Стальная линия series data on the companion page):** utility rooms **50 mm**; **apartment doors 80–100 mm**; country house **100 mm+**. **A Russian retailer and a Belarusian manufacturer agreeing on the apartment band.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. The marketing numbers that mean nothing on their own

**Three specs are quoted at buyers as quality signals and are not:**

| Number quoted | Why it is empty | What to ask instead |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rigidity rib COUNT** ("we have 5, we have 7") | *«Как они расположены, для чего они нужны — эту информацию мне, как правило, никто не раскрывает»* | **What are they made of; are they vertical AND horizontal; then how many** |
| **HINGE COUNT** | A 30 kg leaf in 1.2 mm steel fitted with three hinges is *«тот самый маркетинг»* — three hinges are a necessity only at 100–150 kg | **What does the leaf weigh** |
| **SEAL CONTOUR COUNT** | *«Можно поставить три дешёвых уплотнителя на слабую конструкцию, и это не даст никакой реальной пользы»* | **Does the leaf seat — see §1** |

**⚠️⚠️ On ribs specifically, the defect to look for is VERTICAL-ONLY ribs** — the leaf then resists bending one way and folds *«гармошечкой»* the other. **Both orientations are required, and Novakey independently lists the same requirement.** **Also reject a bent outer sheet with MDF or chipboard packed into the leaf edge.**

**⚠️ A counter-intuitive material trade, from a real customer case:** a customer asked for profile tube instead of angle, having read that a closed section is stiffer. **The maker's answer: profile tube wall runs to about 2 mm, while their angle has a 4 mm flange welded flange-inward and tied into one frame — and cutting the lock pocket through a tube edge removes the full thickness, leaving roughly 5 mm of steel around the lock, where an angle only gets holes drilled for the bolts and is not weakened.** ⚠️ **Single-account, and it defends his own construction — but the lock-pocket argument is specific and checkable by asking how the pocket is cut.**

> ⚠️⚠️ **THE SEAL-CONTOUR ROW OVERTURNS ADVICE THIS VAULT PREVIOUSLY CARRIED.** Gusev's "at least two contours, and more contours means fewer stairwell smells" is now contradicted by both manufacturers. **The correction is not that contours are useless but that they are DOWNSTREAM of geometry: a contour seals only if the leaf presses on it.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Locks, the lock zone, and the one cheap component with outsized effect

**The logical point that governs the rest:**

> ***«Если защитить только замковую зону, а везде оставить ту же полторашку — можно вырезать всю замковую зону и так же добраться до замков.»***

> **→ A door's resistance is set by its weakest area, so a spec sheet that is strong on one line and silent elsewhere has told you nothing. Applies well beyond doors.**

**⚠️⚠️ THE BRONENAKLADKA (armoured cylinder escutcheon) — the highest-leverage item in this batch, and the vault had nothing on it.**

> ***«Брать себе дверь без броненакладки недопустимо — вы сразу сокращаете время взлома вашей двери… до 30–40 секунд.»***

It forces an attacker to defeat the armour before reaching the cylinder at all. Named: **Mottura Top Defender** (a skirt that cannot be torn out of the door body) and **DISEC**, claimed to resist an angle grinder for around half an hour before the cylinder is even reachable.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ One comparatively cheap component claimed to move break-in time from ~30 seconds to ~30 minutes. If one thing from this page is acted on, it should be this.** ⚠️ **Both figures are the manufacturer's, uncorroborated, and he fits these — but the component is cheap enough to specify regardless of whether the exact numbers hold.**

**⚠️ Lock service life — the vault's first quantity on lock quality** (Novakey): cheap locks **10,000–50,000 cycles**; quality locks **from 250,000–300,000**. **Roughly a 6–20× spread, and it is a datasheet figure you can demand in writing.** He adds that about **80%** of doors ship with the cheapest locks and cylinders.

**⚠️⚠️ A lifecycle trap, new to the vault:** cheap doors often fit locks by **mortising (врезным способом)**, which both weakens the structure and makes later replacement harder — *«подобрать аналог тут может быть просто невозможным»*. **→ A lock is a wear part. A door that cannot accept a replacement lock has a service life set by its lock.**

### ⚠️ Cylinder vs. lever (сувальдный) — and a myth the vault was carrying

**On consumer grounds Бастион says the cylinder wins outright**: silent geared versions, durable, no bulky keys, **and on key loss you replace only the cylinder core rather than the whole lock.**

- **⚠️⚠️ A lever lock ALWAYS has a through-hole.** *«Какими бы шторками вы его ни закрывали, сквозняки всегда будут проходить, он не будет герметичен.»* **→ A lock choice with a sealing and soundproofing consequence, which the vault had not connected.**
- **⚠️⚠️ MYTH: that real security requires combining one cylinder with one lever lock.** He says the belief arose because burglars need different tools for each type **and because budget doors expose an unprotected protruding cylinder** — in that case mixing types genuinely does help. **With properly armoured cylinders you can instead combine different cylinder systems (e.g. a standard pin and a slider).** ⚠️ **The vault records Gusev's product as "сувальдный + cylinder"; on this account that is a workaround for an unprotected cylinder, not a security principle.**

**Cylinders named**: abus (Germany, "iTec" secret system) and EVVA (Austria, from 3KS adding a slider system and cut verification) as leaders, Mottura also rated, with protection claimed against **bumping, picking and self-impressioning**. Budget cylinders differ by pin count and anti-drill protection. **⚠️ And the memorable low end: cylinders exist where pressing the inner plug with a long object lets the key turn.** For a lever lock, **Mottura 525J** is named as the price/security balance, awkward in use because of the ball in the key. ⚠️ **Brand names recorded as this manufacturer's stated preferences, not as an independent comparison.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Soundproofing — a density number, and a marketing-name debunk

- **⚠️⚠️ The filling slab must exceed 40 kg/m³**, above which Бастион claims the door seals reliably regardless of wall construction (panel, monolithic, thick brick).
- **⚠️⚠️ THE MATERIAL NAME IS NOISE, THE DENSITY IS THE VARIABLE**: *«минвата, каменная вата, базальтовая плита — называют это всё по-разному… по сути это всё очень однообразные слова. Именно плотность отвечает за шумоизоляцию.»*
- **⚠️ LAYER COUNT matters alongside density** — *«чем больше различных слоёв материалов с разной плотностью, тем лучше»*. **Novakey independently specifies the same: multilayer, medium-to-high density basalt, acoustic materials, and vibration-damping layers.**
- **⚠️ A decorative MDF panel outperforms powder coating acoustically**, all else equal — so the finish choice is not purely cosmetic.
- **⚠️⚠️ Thickness alone is NOT the signal, and he says so against his own industry**: a bent door can be made any thickness and stuffed with anything, and *«если у такой двери будет геометрия страдать… три контура резины с палец, всё как в шлюзе — а по факту дверь всё равно пропускает шумы»*.

> **⚠️⚠️ FOAM FILLING IS THE CHEAP-DOOR TELL, and Gusev is the outlier.** He names пенополистирол as probably the best material for soundproofing; **both manufacturers name пенопласт/foam filling as the marker of a cheap door.** **Two builders against one retailer, and foam is the cheapest filling for the seller with the cheapest doors. Treated as contradicted rather than open.**

> ⚠️⚠️ **ONE ARGUMENT DELIBERATELY NOT ADOPTED.** Answering commenters who say installation foam does not hold soundproofing, Бастион argues that windows are always foamed and a closed window blocks street noise, citing **a Russian СНиП requiring a wall-to-window gap of no more than 55 mm.** **Not carried as a finding: it is a Russian regulatory citation (standing rule 4 — not routed to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`), the analogy is weak because a window's acoustic seal is its glazing unit and frame gaskets rather than its perimeter foam, and he disclaims his own expertise on it in the same breath.** **Recorded because the candour is a credibility signal, not because the argument works.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 6. Two shop-floor tests that need no tools

- **⚠️⚠️ *«Надёжная дверь, она тяжёлая, жёсткая и плотно закрывается.»*** Weight and rigidity are the proxy you can feel.
- **⚠️⚠️ The failure tell: half-open a weak door and *«конструкция болтается как селёдка»*** — which Novakey says reveals the total absence of internal reinforcement.
- **Ask for the cutaway display (стенд-разрез)** so the internal structure is visible rather than described. ⚠️ **Also a sales device — but asking costs nothing, and a seller with no section to show is itself information.**

**Together with §2's knife-cut threshold and §5's density figure, this is the vault's first actual evaluation procedure for an entrance door.**

## ⚠️ 7. Finishes — a vocabulary correction and a repairability trade

**⚠️ MDF is the SUBSTRATE, not a finish.** Buyers ask *«у вас ПВХ или МДФ?»*; the answer is that MDF is the base and PVC film, veneer, enamel or anti-vandal plastic are coatings on it.

| Finish | Notes |
| :--- | :--- |
| **PVC film** | Mechanically tough — a key drawn across it does not scratch; washable with detergent; convincing stone, tile, concrete, marble and wood imitations. **⚠️⚠️ BUT it cannot be repaired in place: damage means re-covering or replacing the whole panel.** |
| **Enamel** | MDF milled, sanded, hand-finished, sprayed to RAL or NCS. **Budget work shows inclusions and rough patches.** Gloss is the demanding variant. **⚠️ Restorable in place by a visiting specialist.** |
| **Veneer** | Any species. **⚠️ Also restorable in place.** |
| **Alcantara** | Rated from the presenter's own use in his car; breathable, easy care. `single-account`. |
| **Powder coating** | Ranked lowest by Бастион — acoustically worse than a decorative panel and plain-looking, though some buyers deliberately want an inconspicuous strong door. |

> **→ ⚠️ The repairability split is the part worth carrying: film wins on day-one toughness and loses on year-five repair.** **Given the vault's existing finding that the entry zone is a high-mechanical-wear area (Круглов), and Gusev's point that the exterior panel takes abuse from neighbours and furniture, repairability is the more relevant axis here than scratch resistance.**
