## 💡 Lighting & Electrical
* **Lighting**: IP44 waterproof-rated recessed spotlights for general illumination, **plus a dedicated front-facing mirror light** (not just ambient/decorative light washing the tile) — see [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Lighting_and_Electrical|Lighting & Electrical]]. Avoid toe-kick/floor lighting unless committing to daily cleaning.
* **Sockets**: IP44 sockets for hairdryer/shaver, placed safely away from direct water contact. Also plan for: washer, dryer, water heater, UPS, leak-protection system, mirror backlight/electric towel warmer, and a separately-switched extractor fan — see [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Lighting_and_Electrical|Lighting & Electrical]]. A bathroom also needs its own КУП (potential-equalization/bonding box) for metal fixtures, distinct from ordinary socket grounding.
