# Bathroom - Do's and Don'ts

## Do's

| Rule | Applies To | Reason | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Apply continuous waterproofing up walls in shower zone | Shower Area | Prevents moisture damage to structural walls | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_vid1_transcript_d04723c5.txt` |
| Use artificial stone bathtubs where budget and design class justify it | Bathtub Area | Matches premium bathroom concept and was used in the cited designer renovation | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_tips_video_f23c504a.txt` |
| Select a bathtub over a shower stall if an apartment has only one bathroom | Single Bathroom Layout | Bathtubs accommodate both showering and bathing, offering superior versatility (except for elderly/low-mobility occupants where low-threshold walk-in showers are safer) | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_mistakes_budget_371bd212.txt` |
| Maintain at least 80 cm (90 cm for occupants >180 cm tall) clear width when embedding a vanity sink into a 3-wall tile niche | Vanity Sink Niche | Prevents occupants from repeatedly hitting their elbows against side tile walls while washing | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_mistakes_budget_371bd212.txt` |
| Orient the bathroom doorway so the vanity mirror is the immediate focal point upon entering | Bathroom Entrance Layout | Improves visual aesthetics and room ergonomics compared to facing a toilet bowl or tub edge | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_mistakes_budget_371bd212.txt` |
| Install top structural metal stabilizer bars on stationary glass shower panels and magnetic vinyl door gaskets | Shower Glass Enclosure | Prevents glass deflection under body weight and eliminates self-opening glass doors that leak water onto dry floors | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_3_a0e895b1.txt` |
| Position tropical rain shower head height at a maximum 45-degree spray angle relative to shower enclosure edge | Shower Area | Prevents clogged shower nozzles from spraying water sideways past glass partitions onto bathroom floor | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_3_a0e895b1.txt` |
| Seal decorative plaster on bathroom walls with transparent epoxy varnish | Bathroom Walls | Provides a seamless, fully washable, and water-resistant wall finish while saving tile installation labor | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_5_dfc91747.txt` |

## Don'ts

| Rule | Applies To | Risk | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Do not place sockets near shower spray zones without IP-rated protection | Wet Zones | Electrical safety hazard | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_vid1_transcript_d04723c5.txt` |
| Do not install a 3-wall recessed vanity sink in a niche narrower than 80 cm | Vanity Niche | Causes constant elbow impact against wall tiles during daily hygiene routines | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_mistakes_budget_371bd212.txt` |
| Do not install vessel bowl sinks on countertops situated over washing machines | Vanity Countertop | Countertops over washing machines sit at ~90 cm height; adding a vessel bowl raises rim excessively, causing water to run down user elbows | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_3_a0e895b1.txt` |
| Do not build doorless open-entry walk-in showers if household has shedding pets | Shower Area | Shedding pets sleep on heated shower floor, causing frequent pet hair drain clogs (use enclosed glass doors) | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_3_a0e895b1.txt` |
