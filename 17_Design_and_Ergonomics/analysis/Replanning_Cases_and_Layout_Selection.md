# Replanning — a Worked Case, Legality, and Which Layouts to Avoid

Working with a layout you did not draw: a 77.1 m² Moscow euro-3-room replan case study, the ten RUSSIAN replanning prohibitions with the ground-floor exception and the resale mechanism, and eight layout types to avoid when choosing a flat. **Split out on 2026-09-02 under the vault's 300-line page ceiling, verbatim.**

> [!WARNING]
> **The replanning prohibitions here are RUSSIAN, not Belarusian.** They are recorded as a technical and resale argument, not as law that applies to this project. See rule 4 in `AGENTS.md`.

## Whole-Apartment Replan Case Study: 77.1m² Euro-3-Room, Moscow (Karen Karapetyan, added 2026-08-25)

A full worked before/after replan (developer layout → constraint mapping
→ redesign), complementary to the Zlobin philosophy list above — this one
shows the mechanics of applying a similar "fewer wasted micro-spaces,
more genuinely usable room" philosophy to one real floor plan. See
`11_Budget_and_Planning`'s Durable Facts store for the wet-zone/
partition-movement mechanism and plumbing-coordination facts from the
same source; this page's share is the furniture/storage/zoning-specific
findings:

- **⚠️ Walk-through-wardrobe false economy**: the developer's original
  plan included a walk-through ("проходная") closet room fitting only
  ~2.8 running meters of storage — no better than an ordinary hallway
  closet, while the extra partition wasted floor area on corridor space
  for zero storage gain. Either remove the partition (open corridor
  closet, same storage) or ensure a walk-through design earns genuinely
  more capacity than the plain alternative.
- **⚠️ Quantified storage-yield payoff from replanning alone**: same
  77.1m² footprint, no added area — developer layout topped out around
  ~8.5-9 running meters of total closet/wardrobe storage; the redesigned
  plan reached ~13.8 running meters (~+50-60%) purely by eliminating dead
  corridor/"appendix" space and correcting room geometry.
- **Two full bathrooms over one bath + one WC-only as a default even in a
  modest apartment**: stated rationale is that bathing/showering is the
  single most time-consuming daily routine, so a second (even smaller)
  full shower prevents household members queuing for the only wash
  point.
- **L-shaped-room correction technique**: straighten an L-shaped bedroom
  with a wasted "appendix" nook by aligning its far wall to an adjacent
  room's partition line and absorbing a shallow strip from the neighbor
  — recovers a clean rectangle that can fit furniture (here: a 3.9m
  wardrobe run + a bed) the original irregular footprint physically
  couldn't.
- Entry hall should never double as a walkthrough/thoroughfare between
  other rooms — keep it a genuinely separate zone with its own
  shoe/outerwear storage so residents aren't crossing it in dirty
  footwear en route elsewhere.
- Door placement as a family-lifecycle decision: a home-office door was
  deliberately placed off the kitchen-living room (rather than the entry
  hall) specifically to ease future parental supervision once the room
  becomes a nursery.

`single-account`, Moscow level 1 (named residential complex + city in
transcript). [source: [[_Sources/YT_frt8tQOrjiM_karapetyan_planning_for_life_euro3room|frt8tQOrjiM]]]

## ⚠️ RUSSIAN Replanning Legality — Ten Prohibitions, the Ground-Floor Exception, and the Resale Mechanism (Татьяна Безверхая, added 2026-09-02)

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **These are Russian norms, recorded on this planning page with the jurisdiction flagged — NOT in `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`, which is Belarus-only.** Check the Belarusian equivalent before relying on any of them. **The source cites no article, decree or code number for any item: all `single-account`, `unverified`.** This section extends the existing *Constraints to establish before any of the above* block above, which already carries the shorter Кузина version of several of these rules. [source: [[_Sources/YT_JgDzAoWlyJI_bezverkhaia_ten_illegal_solutions|JgDzAoWlyJI]]]

**Where this corroborates what the page already holds**: wet zones essentially immovable except on the ground/top floor; a gas kitchen unmovable and required to be an enclosed room; load-bearing openings sometimes possible and removal never. **What follows is the part that is new or sharper.**

- **⚠️ The kitchen/bathroom boundary is absolute in both directions, "даже на 10 см."** Not kitchen onto bathroom, not bathroom onto kitchen. **Her stated rationale, which the page did not hold: even though both are wet zones, the law treats the swap as worsening your own and your neighbours' housing conditions.** Nor bathroom onto a living room (rationale: a flood would reach the neighbour below's bed), nor kitchen onto a living room.
- **⚠️ A kitchen niche in a corridor is legal, and she gives the exact conditions — the permissive counterpart to this page's existing riser-distance caution.** A kitchen may be expanded onto or relocated onto non-living space including a corridor, subject to only: **(a) area not less than 5 m²**, **(b) the extract connected to the *kitchen* ventilation system, not the bathroom's vent duct even if that duct is closer**, and **(c) no appliance — sink, refrigerator or hob — extending beyond the corridor's own boundary as drawn on the БТИ plan.** She reports fully approved projects of this kind. **Read this together with Кузина's caution above**: the niche can be *approved* on these three conditions, and will still suffer the ~0.5 m → ~3 m drain-run problem she describes. **Legality and buildability are separate tests.**
- **⚠️ The gas workaround, which this page's "a gas kitchen cannot be moved at all" entry did not include: you may formally renounce gas.** Apply, the pipe is cut and welded shut, **and you receive a certificate that the apartment is not gasified** — after which, **if the electrical capacity allows**, an electric hob and an open-plan studio become possible. She notes the gas-door norms are Soviet-era and not worth arguing with, only working around legally. **With gas, the kitchen must be separated by full interior doors with a tight closure (плотный притвор)** — sliding or hinged, single or double-leaf, but real doors that close properly, on the reasoning that in a leak the doors are deemed to hold gas out of the living rooms.
- **⚠️ Ventilation and plumbing shafts may not be touched at all** — not reduced, not notched for niches or shelves, no WC installation placed inside them. **The reasoning is ownership, and it is the transferable part: by the documents the shaft may be common building property and not part of your apartment**, so no amount of apparent dead space inside it creates a right to encroach.
- **⚠️ Hydronic (water) underfloor heating is not permitted in an apartment — electric only; unlimited in a detached house.** **Second independent account of this claim in the vault** (see `13_Surfaces_and_Finishes/analysis/Flooring_Material_Selection.md`, Kruglov/Ontario), and **a third and fourth from her own bathroom source this round**. Still no code cited by anyone.
- **⚠️ Load-bearing openings — the full procedural sequence, which the page states only as "sometimes possible"**: commission a reinforcement project from a **licensed** organisation → have that project approved by the territorial authority → cut and reinforce strictly per the project → **retain all hidden-works certificates (акты скрытых работ) for the acceptance inspection.** **Her own cost-benefit verdict argues against her own service line and is worth keeping: her studio does this routinely, but on large premium projects — "если вы считаете, что в двухкомнатной панельке игра с проёмом в несущей стене стоит свеч, вы не правы. Лучше придумать какую-то другую планировку."**
- **⚠️ The ground-floor exception, with a definition trap the page should carry: "first floor" means the first *residential* floor.** If there is a shop on the ground floor, **you are effectively the second floor and the exception does not apply.** Where it does: a kitchen or bathroom may be moved onto living space, and a bathroom may be expanded onto your own kitchen — subject to the remaining rules (not a load-bearing partition, no gas). **She notes buyers actively hunt these units for the replanning freedom.**
- **⚠️ A constraint that depends on your neighbours' history, and it is easy to miss entirely: if the flat below has already replanned — say expanded a bedroom onto a piece of corridor — you cannot site your wet zone over that same piece.** Her term for the overlap is «криминальные пересечения». **So a wet-zone move must be checked against what the flat below actually did, not only against your own БТИ plan.**
- **⚠️ One recent liberalisation: an entrance to the apartment's sole bathroom may now be made from a bedroom** (and, per her bathroom source the same round, **from the kitchen**) — previously forbidden, and highly relevant to small flats and studios. Undated and uncited; **the two accounts are the same channel, so not independent corroboration.**

### ⚠️ The enforcement consequence that actually bites is resale, not the fine

- **The mechanism is concrete**: most flats, including resale, are sold to mortgage buyers through banks. **The bank sends a valuer who compares the БТИ plan against the actual apartment and records every discrepancy as «красные линии» on the plan.** The bank then will not take the property, leaving only a cash buyer — of whom there are now very few. Her framing: an unapproved replanning is you "наступаете на горло своей же будущей финансовой выгоде."
- **State-side escalation, in order**: an initial administrative fine on the individual → a repeat fine → bailiff enforcement → **ultimately a court order to restore the original boundaries, i.e. to undo a brand-new renovation.**
- **⚠️ No fine amount is recorded here on purpose** — the auto-captions render it ambiguously, and she characterises it in the same breath as "смешной копеечный штраф," which does not fit the larger reading. **Do not quote a figure from this source.**

## Layout Selection — Eight Layout Types to Avoid, and the Positive Criteria (Татьяна Безверхая, added 2026-09-02)

Moscow designer, 2025, `promotional_ratio: medium` (no sponsor; heavy own-studio pitch). **Corroborates and quantifies the existing *Irregular apartment layout as a real-estate red flag* entry above** (Краснов), which makes the same pre-purchase due-diligence argument qualitatively. [source: [[_Sources/YT_v4pHJDpGTSM_bezverkhaia_eight_worst_layouts|v4pHJDpGTSM]]]

- **⚠️ Pass-through rooms — and her numeric ceiling converges with this page's existing pro-walk-through-room argument rather than contradicting it.** Her rule: **a living room should open into at most one other room besides the corridor.** **Permitted: a living room opening into a master block** (master bedroom plus its ensuite) in a three-room flat. **The failure case: a living room opening into the master bedroom *and* the first *and* second children's rooms** — it becomes "сплошной перекрёсток," and worse if a sofa bed there is someone's bed. **Read against Кузина's *case for walk-through rooms* above: she advocates accepting one to reclaim ~15 m² of corridor; Безверхая's ceiling is also one. The two agree on the number and differ only in emphasis.**
- **⚠️ Large corridors — a deliberate dissent from the designer consensus, and it is conditional in a useful way.** She notes most designers and almost all laypeople treat a 25 m² corridor in a 70 m² flat as waste. **Her position: where the walls are not load-bearing, a large corridor is a major replanning asset, because RU norms permit expanding bathrooms and kitchens and relocating wet points onto non-living space.** Uses she names: separate dressing rooms, a laundry room, a WC-plus-basin for washing a dog's paws, a proper utility block. **⚠️ The condition is intent: an asset if you are buying a whitebox and commissioning a design project; wasted area if you want to move into a finished flat as-is.** **This complements rather than contradicts Кузина's anti-corridor position above — both treat unusable corridor area as waste and differ on the remedy (convert it to wet/storage zones, versus absorb it by accepting a walk-through room).**
- **Almost all walls load-bearing (typical panel houses)**: room and corridor boundaries are fixed. **The one common exception she names: an opening between kitchen and living room in П-series houses (П44 and similar) — and even that needs separate approval.**
- **⚠️ Her positive criteria for a good layout, as a compact pre-purchase checklist**: overall form close to square; **if rectangular, all corners 90° or at minimum all but one**; **windows on as many sides as possible — ideally three, at least two**; not many load-bearing walls; an adequate original layout needing only minor correction. **Her resale framing: such housing stays liveable, worth an investment-grade renovation, and resellable at a margin.**

## ⚠️⚠️ Recovered Corridor Area — an Estate Agent Quantifies the Gain, and a Designer Names Where It Destroys a Room

**Two sources from one intake round, arguing opposite sides of the same move. Neither is wrong, and together they define the boundary — which is more useful than either alone.**

### The gain, from the party who sells the flats

**Игорь Михайлов, head of a St Petersburg agency (~400 flats sold last year), on what he rates as the designer's most quantifiable contribution:** [source: [[_Sources/YT_vTgY6vioNik_nsdsgn_realtor_vs_designers_podcast|YT_vTgY6vioNik]]]

- **«Дизайнеры очень круто влияют на то, СКОЛЬКО МЕТРОВ в квартире. Можно купить квартиру 100 м², где будет ПОЛЕЗНОЙ ПЛОЩАДИ дай Бог 50-60 — потому что у ЗАСТРОЙЩИКА СВОИ ЦЕЛИ, они в каждой планировке НЕ КОПАЮТСЯ. Это [решается], когда КОНЕЧНЫЙ ПОТРЕБИТЕЛЬ покупает [и делает] КАЧЕСТВЕННУЮ ПЕРЕПЛАНИРОВКУ, которую может посоветовать ДИЗАЙНЕР.»**
- **⚠️ «Заплатив ОДИН РАЗ дизайнеру… на выходе получишь из стометровой квартиры НЕ 60 квадратных метров полезной площади, А 70, 72 В ИДЕАЛЕ.»** The host confirms he offers **five or six replanning variants** per flat.
- **→ A claimed ~10-12 percentage-point gain in usable area, i.e. ~10-12 m² recovered on a 100 m² flat, asserted by someone who sells the flats rather than the replanning. `single-account`, `unverified`, and the figures are round.** ⚠️ The transcript garbles the arithmetic at this point and emits a stray "180"; **that is transcription noise, not part of the claim.**
- **⚠️ Note what makes this worth recording despite being round numbers: it is a COMMERCIALLY ADVERSE witness. An agent has no stake in a buyer spending money on a designer, and if anything benefits from flats being seen as adequate as built.**

### ⚠️ And the specific way it goes wrong, from the designer

**Александр Синчуков, 2022-01 — and he is unusually blunt about it:** [source: [[_Sources/YT_CN-Ab_g4CAI_nsdsgn_thirtyfive_beautiful_but_impractical|YT_CN-Ab_g4CAI]]]

- **He grants the premise: the corridor is «САМОЕ БЕСПОЛЕЗНОЕ в плане расходования пространства помещение — там мы проводим МЕНЬШЕ ВСЕГО ВРЕМЕНИ», so «очень часто возникает ЖЕЛАНИЕ ИЗБАВИТЬСЯ от него и ПРИСОЕДИНИТЬ к жилой комнате, таким образом РАСШИРИВ её».**
- **⚠️⚠️ THEN THE FAILURE: «очень часто в ходе перепланировки и присоединения коридора комната ПРЕВРАЩАЕТСЯ В ПРОХОДНУЮ, А ЖИТЬ В ТАКОЙ КОМНАТЕ ПРОСТО УЖАСНО. КТО НЕ ЖИЛ — ПРОСТО ПОВЕРЬТЕ МНЕ, ТАМ НЕВОЗМОЖНО НОЧЕВАТЬ: ВСЕГДА КТО-ТО МИМО ВАС БУДЕТ ХОДИТЬ В ТУАЛЕТ ИЛИ НА КУХНЮ.»**
- **⚠️ THE MITIGATION IF YOU DO IT ANYWAY: «если вы объединяете коридор с КУХНЕЙ, то ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬНО сделайте ЗОНИРОВАНИЕ с помощью РЕЕК, каких-то СТЕЛЛАЖЕЙ и ШКАФОВ, чтобы пространство коридора хоть как-то ЗОНАЛЬНО ОТДЕЛЯЛОСЬ. При этом перегородка может быть СВЕТОПРОХОДЯЩЕЙ, и пространство комнаты ВСЁ РАВНО БУДЕТ БОЛЕЕ ОБЪЁМНЫМ, чем с ГЛУХОЙ СТЕНОЙ.»** See [[17_Design_and_Ergonomics/analysis/Room_Zoning_Partition_Methods|Room Zoning and Partition Methods]].
- **⚠️ AND THE SCOPE LIMIT, which is the operative sentence: «объединять коридор С КОМНАТОЙ я бы рекомендовал ТОЛЬКО В ОДНОМ СЛУЧАЕ — ЕСЛИ ЭТА КОМНАТА ЯВЛЯЕТСЯ ГОСТИНОЙ.»**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **⚠️⚠️ THE BOUNDARY THE TWO SOURCES DEFINE TOGETHER: "USABLE AREA" IS NOT A SCALAR.**
>
> **Recovered circulation area is a genuine gain in a LIVING ROOM — which is already a room people walk through — and a functional LOSS in a BEDROOM, because it converts the room into a thoroughfare and destroys the one thing a bedroom is for.**
>
> **→ So the agent's ~10-12 m² figure is achievable, but only some of it is achievable WITHOUT COST. Before accepting a usable-area gain as a benefit, ask which room absorbed the corridor and whether anybody sleeps in it.** A replanning that recovers 12 m² by routing traffic through a bedroom has not made the flat bigger; it has made it worse in a way that shows up in occupancy rather than on the plan.
>
> **⚠️ This also qualifies the "developers don't optimise layouts" claim: a developer's stock plan spends area on circulation partly BECAUSE circulation has to go somewhere. The recoverable share is real but smaller than a plan comparison suggests.**

- → **⚠️ For this project the same trade is arithmetically available — a 9.79 m² прихожая against a 9.36 m² kids' room — and this is the vault's only worked precedent for it at this exact scale and household.**

The transferable part is the SHAPE of the conclusion — find the one or two moves that buy real area, and do not assume a good designer will re-cut the whole flat, because a good designer with a free hand did not.**

## ⚠️ Moving a Bathroom or Kitchen — the Constraint That Is Forgotten, and Its Technical Consequence

**Legitimate reasons to replan are named** — a better view from the bedroom than the living room, wall configuration that suits the appliances better, wanting the bathroom nearer the bedroom «чтобы не ходить через всю квартиру мыться». **Then:**

- **⚠️ «ОДНА ОЧЕНЬ ВАЖНАЯ ОШИБКА, о которой ПОСТОЯННО ПОЧЕМУ-ТО ЗАБЫВАЮТ — это ИНЖЕНЕРИЯ. Если вы хотите ПЕРЕНЕСТИ ванную либо кухню в другую часть квартиры, то ОБЯЗАТЕЛЬНО нужно ОБЕСПЕЧИТЬ К НЕЙ ДОСТУП ВОДЫ — и это очень часто ЗАКАНЧИВАЕТСЯ ПОДЪЁМОМ ПОЛОВ, организацией НАСОСОВ и другими моментами, которые просто ПОРТЯТ ВАМ ПЛАНИРОВКУ И ДИЗАЙН.»**
- **→ The point is the recursion: the engineering needed to realise a better plan degrades that plan. A raised floor eats the ceiling height the replan was partly meant to showcase, and a pump introduces the noise and reliability problems recorded against macerators on [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Planning_and_Layout|Bathroom: Planning and Layout]]. So the drainage route should be checked BEFORE a layout variant is presented, not after it is chosen.**

>
> **«Вы НАРУШИЛИ СТРОИТЕЛЬНЫЕ НОРМЫ, и если СОСЕДИ УЗНАЮТ, что над их СПАЛЬНЕЙ появилась ВАННА, МОКРАЯ ЗОНА, то это может кончиться ОЧЕНЬ БОЛЬШИМ ШТРАФОМ и ПРЕДПИСАНИЕМ ВОССТАНОВИТЬ ВСЁ в первоначальное состояние.»** And an exception: **«НОРМЫ ПЕРЕПЛАНИРОВКИ НЕ РАСПРОСТРАНЯЮТСЯ НА АПАРТАМЕНТЫ, и единственное ограничение для апартаментов — это конечно же ИНЖЕНЕРИЯ.»**
>
> **No code or article is cited. What transfers regardless of jurisdiction is the TECHNICAL trigger and the ENFORCEMENT ROUTE: the specific breach is a wet zone over a habitable room below, and the thing that surfaces it is A NEIGHBOUR NOTICING — which matches this page's existing finding that the consequence that actually bites is resale rather than the fine, since both depend on the breach becoming known rather than being detected.**
