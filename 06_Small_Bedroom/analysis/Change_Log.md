# Small Bedroom — Change Log

- **2026-08-25 — Page created.** Crossed the 3+-source page-creation
  threshold during a dedicated 8-video Anuta Vlady batch (targeting
  `06_Small_Bedroom`, previously placeholder-only in the wiki, with 2
  sources already accumulated store-only). Sources contributing: the 2
  pre-existing (`TJBcEpXymYk`, `GfixqWjsD58`) plus 5 new from this batch
  (`ohOpaJczckE`, `DvQTBiItLpY`, `Bb4Hh-GDRdA`, `uwGxjv9O1kk`,
  `ZlkBfwlhYFU` Case 1) plus one cross-referenced living-room case
  (`SUN8UiXAjf0`) whose proportion-correction technique generalizes
  directly to a narrow bedroom. Page created as a layered guide +
  3 `analysis/` pages (Space Recovery & Proportion Technique, Furniture
  & Storage for Tight Clearances, Decor/Color/Lighting Technique) +
  Source Notes + this Change Log, per this vault's "layered from the
  first source" default for a topic that already decomposes into
  several distinct sub-decisions.
  **Routing decision recorded**: kept this content on a *dedicated*
  `06_Small_Bedroom` guide rather than folding it into
  [[00_Master/Bedroom_Design_Principles|Bedroom Design Principles]]
  (the existing phase-agnostic "whichever room is the adult bedroom"
  reference page) — the two pages serve genuinely different questions.
  `Bedroom_Design_Principles.md` answers "how do I lay out *a* bedroom"
  (door/wardrobe/bed/nightstand sequence, universal mistakes, applies
  whether the room is 9m² or 20m²). This page answers "how do I make a
  *small or awkwardly-shaped* bedroom work" (space recovery, proportion
  correction for narrow/irregular rooms, tight-clearance furniture
  sizing) — a room-size/shape-driven problem set that six real cases in
  this batch alone (14m², 43m² khrushchevka, pentagonal, long/narrow)
  showed to be a coherent, recurring topic in its own right, not a
  handful of one-off exceptions to the general sequence. Both pages
  cross-link each other; a fact that's genuinely general (not
  size-driven) stays on `Bedroom_Design_Principles.md`, and this
  project's own `06_Small_Bedroom` (2.78m×3.35m, 9.29m²) is squarely the
  kind of room this new page is for.
  Cross-linked from `06_Small_Bedroom/Small_Bedroom_Index.md`.
