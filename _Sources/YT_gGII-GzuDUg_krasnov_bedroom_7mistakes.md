- **Lighting — restates the three-layer principle already covered in this round's own dedicated lighting video (`GpkgVoPesDI`)**: avoid a single bright ceiling-only light source in a bedroom; layer with table lamps, night-lights, string lights, warm-toned bulbs instead. - **Decluttering — restates a generic "unused >1 year → discard" rule** already close to the existing household-decluttering-method content in this vault (`14_Furniture/analysis/Household_Storage_and_Decluttering_Method.md`) — no new mechanism.
- **Wall colour**: avoid acid-bright/saturated colours (cited as stimulating/energising, wrong register for a sleep space) and avoid flat dull grey/beige ("hotel room" flatness with no warmth) — prefer soft/muted pastel or deep calm tones, optionally with one accent wall. - **Textile balance**: some textile is necessary (curtains, at least one throw, pillows) — neither zero textile ("anatomical museum") nor an overloaded pile. **Specific figure: max 2–3 pillows on the bed**, not a large decorative pile.
- **Storage-zone planning inside the bedroom**: under-bed drawers/containers, wardrobe organisers — to prevent visible clutter (floor books, under-bed shoes, chair-draped clothes). Generic, overlaps existing storage-method content.
- **Personal/decorative touches in moderation**: a few personal objects (a book, a framed photo, a plant, a small art piece) recommended over either zero personalisation ("hotel room") or an overloaded themed collection.
