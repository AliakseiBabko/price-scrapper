# Source Note - Progress Design: ⚠️⚠️ two children, one room — the play floor is real, and the children say so (YouTube XL2Dsw7FonM +2)

## ⚠️⚠️ Why these three were fetched, and what changed to make them worth it

**The [[_Inbox/planning/progress_design_channel_triage_20260914|triage's Tier 2A]] identified children sharing one room as a gap. It became URGENT on 2026-09-16, when the owner ruled out bunk beds** — leaving [[05_Kids_Room/Kids_Room_Guide|an open question]]: **two static beds or two 90 cm wall beds for two children (3 and 6) sharing 15.28 m² through Phase 1.**

⚠️⚠️ **The tenth, eleventh and twelfth Progress Design videos in this vault. NONE of this is independent corroboration of the other nine.** **Taken because the question is now live and because one of them is a post-occupancy review in which the children speak for themselves — which no amount of maker commentary substitutes for.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE PLAY FLOOR IS REAL, AND IT IS THE CHILDREN WHO SAY SO

**This is the finding the open question needed.** The vault holds a designer's warning that **two children without bunk beds "reduces the room to a bedroom with a workspace, and nothing else will fit"** — the cost of ruling out bunks. **Here is the same arithmetic from a parent, and then the consequence from the children.**

**The father, on why a transformer rather than two beds:**

> ***«Если две кровати — это МИНУС ПОЛОВИНА КОМНАТЫ СРАЗУ, плюс добавляется ещё диван, где сидеть, и место кончилось. Поэтому трансформер в данном случае необходим, если нужно это уместить всё в одну комнату.»***

**And the children, unprompted, on what the recovered floor is for:**

> ***«Заниматься спортом очень хорошо позволяет. МНОГО МЕСТА здесь. Если бы, конечно, не трансформер, вряд ли бы мы смогли также хорошо на нём заниматься.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS AN INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL AT THE DESIGNER'S FINDING, FROM A HOUSEHOLD LIVING IT — and it carries it one step further.** **The designer said what two static beds COST. This says the wall bed BUYS that cost back, and names what the floor is used for.**
>
> **→ For this project's open question, it is the strongest evidence on the wall-bed side that this vault holds.** ⚠️⚠️ **But read the ages before transferring it: these children are described as *«двое юных спортсменов»* and are plainly school-age, and in the companion project the children are 17 and 11.** **This project's younger child is 3.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Operating effort — the children say it is easy, and that is still not the answer for a 3-year-old

> **A child, on camera:** ***«Нет вообще никакой сложности в поднятии её. Мне легко её поднимать, ОЧЕНЬ легко. И в принципе мне нравится её механизм.»***
> **And the maker, of a girl's room:** *«поднять такую кровать можно ОДНОЙ РУКОЙ, девочка должна справиться»*, and elsewhere *«с этим без проблем справится ребёнок»*.

> **→ ⚠️ This corroborates [[_Sources/YT_KgBcJ4zAHi8_progressdesign_seven_year_owner_account|the seven-year household's]] 7-year-old working a 90 cm bed — but it is the SAME VENDOR, so it is a restatement, not a second voice.** **And the maker's two claims are sales claims; only the first is a child speaking.**
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE GAP THAT MATTERS FOR THIS PROJECT REMAINS EXACTLY WHERE IT WAS: nothing in this vault, from any source, reports a THREE-YEAR-OLD operating a wall bed.** **The evidence keeps arriving for children aged roughly 7 and up. That is not an argument against wall beds here — it is a precise statement of what is unevidenced, and it means the 3-year-old's bed is one an adult folds.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. A DEFECT REPORT, in the vendor's own promotional video

> **Of a bed opened by button or by voice assistant:** ***«Мне за кадром пожаловались, что АЛИСА ТЕРЯЕТ СВЯЗЬ и почему-то через некоторое время перестаёт отвечать.»*** The presenter's response is to recommend contacting their managers and to praise their own after-sales service.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A REAL FAULT REPORT SURVIVING INTO A SALES VIDEO, which is worth more than a hundred satisfied lines.** **Voice control of a wall bed is reported failing in service — the assistant drops the connection and stops responding after a while.**
>
> **→ This bears on [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Smart_Home_Systems|the smart-home material]] and on the Progress Design automation claims already recorded: an automatic bed's voice layer is the least reliable part of it.** **A wall bed that can ONLY be opened by voice would be a bad specification; button and manual fallback matter.** ⚠️⚠️ **And it connects to a question this vault could not answer because the video was silent — how an automatic bed opens with no power (`o6_HwxwZau4`, 29 bytes, no speech). Still open, and still worth putting to a supplier.**

## ⚠️ 4. Details worth carrying into a shared children's room

- **⚠️⚠️ SOFT BOLSTERS AT THE BED EDGE ARE A SHIN GUARD, not a styling choice** — *«нужны, чтобы, проходя мимо кровати, не удариться ногой и не оставить на голене синяк»*, and in a nursery *«не бывает никаких ограничений скорости»* because children run. **Nylon-covered, on both beds, and called a must-have in a children's room.**
- **⚠️⚠️ A SHELVING UNIT USED AS A ROOM DIVIDER** so two children in one room *«обрести свои границы и не мешать друг другу жить»*. **→ Directly applicable: the boundary problem is the real problem in a shared room, and a стеллаж-перегородка solves it without a partition.**
- **⚠️⚠️ DELIBERATE PARITY BETWEEN SIBLINGS, stated as a design principle**: soft options and lighting *«точно такие же, как в комнате брата — хорошо придумано, никто не будет жаловаться, что у одного что-то есть, а у другого нет»*. **→ Relevant twice over here: in the shared Phase 1 room, and again at Phase 2 when the children split into two rooms of unequal size (15.28 m² and ~9.3 m²).**
- **A night light plus a socket at each bed**, for reading and charging — **which is exactly the per-child, locally-switched provision [[00_Master/Layout_Option_Review|the layout review]] lists as a first-fix item whose heights follow the bed positions.**
- **Adjustable headboard on both beds**; ceiling lighting on a remote or touch button; flexible reading lights each side.
- **Vertical niches beside the bed REPLACING bedside tables** — *«такое расположение обеспечивает больше полезного пространства»*; a through-passage so shelves stay reachable when the bed is down.
- **Storage sized to the object**: upper drawers meant to take *«самокат или даже что-то побольше»*; one shelf per category; books two rows deep.
- **⚠️ Bedding stays inside when the bed folds, so no bedspread is needed** — *«постельное бельё не пылится»*. ⚠️ **Set this beside [[_Sources/YT_qMGSfO-glvc_freedom_wallbed_drawbacks_and_struts|FREE_DOM's "large vacuum cleaner"]]: dust is drawn into the CABINET off the floor, while the bedding on the bed stays covered. Both can hold; they are about different surfaces.**
- **⚠️ At measurement they record wall curvature, cornice offset AND SOCKET POSITIONS** — *«только так мебель встанет идеально»*. **→ Socket positions are an input to the joinery, which is another reason the electrics and the bed decision are coupled at first fix.**

## ⚠️ 5. Two honest limits, from the makers themselves

- **Deployed, one project has no circulation at all**: *«это занимает всю комнату… когда это раскладывается, здесь ходить негде»* — **and he uses that to argue that non-transformer furniture was never an option in that room.**
- **But in the larger project a passage survives**: *«даже когда кровати разложены, есть место для прохода»*. **→ Whether you keep a walking route with both beds down is a function of room size and of the depth trick [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Installation_and_Daily_Use|recorded from FREE_DOM]] — not a property of the device.**
- **⚠️ A FIFTH arrival at the sofa-bed rejection**, from the father: *«это был бы какой-нибудь диван… но даже диван с ортопедическим матрасом — это не полноценный матрас на кровать. Это НЕ КРОВАТЬ, это никогда таким не будет.»*
