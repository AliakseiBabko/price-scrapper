## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE WARRANTY SPLIT — this narrows a worry this vault recorded three weeks-worth of reasoning on

| Component | Stated |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Frame** | **One-piece WELDED steel, rated 600 kg, ⚠️⚠️ 10-YEAR WARRANTY.** *«Здесь нет болтов, поэтому ничего со временем скрипеть не будет»* |
| **Gas struts** | **Spanish, ⚠️ 20,000 cycles ≈ «примерно 27 лет ежедневного использования»** (his own arithmetic, and it checks out at twice daily) |
| **Mechanism warranty** | **⚠️ 3 years** — from [[_Sources/YT_u_gIgDqRdYw_progressdesign_wallbed_buyer_walkthrough|the same firm's buyer walkthrough]] |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE FRAME AND THE MECHANISM ARE WARRANTED SEPARATELY, AND BY A FACTOR OF MORE THAN THREE.** **[[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|The mechanisms page]] currently records the 3-year figure as bearing on this project's Phase-1-install / Phase-2-activate plan — the warranty expiring before the bed's first night of use.** **That worry is real but NARROWER than recorded: it applies to the WEAR PART, not to the structure.**
>
> **→ The question to put to any supplier is therefore not "what is the warranty" but "what is warranted for how long, per component" — and the follow-up already recorded stands and gets sharper: struts are the cheapest component and the one that ages, so their separate availability matters more than the headline term.**
>
> ⚠️⚠️ **Note the frame here is WELDED STEEL, which is the construction [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|the page's frame-material section]] records as the structural answer to the ДСП-cracking failure mode. A 10-year warranty on a welded steel frame is consistent with that; a 10-year warranty would be a surprising thing to offer on 16 mm particleboard.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. "LOAD-BEARING WALL" WAS TOO LOOSE — the criterion is CONCRETE, and the fix is priced in

> ***«Надёжное крепление кровати возможно ТОЛЬКО К БЕТОННОЙ СТЕНЕ. Для других стен существует ОПОРНАЯ РАМА. Она распределяет нагрузку НА ПОЛ.»*** **And, decisively: *«в моделях с автоматикой эта опция уже ВКЛЮЧЕНА ПО УМОЛЧАНИЮ»*.** The price walkthrough repeats the rule in the same words — *«опорная рама нужна, если стена не из бетона»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS CORRECTS A LOOSE PARAPHRASE IN THIS VAULT'S OWN PAGES.** The mechanisms page records the choice as *anti-tip frame vs fixing to a **load-bearing** wall*, taken from the same firm's buyer walkthrough. **The criterion the maker actually applies is the MATERIAL — concrete — not the structural role.** **A load-bearing wall of brick or block is still not concrete, and a non-load-bearing concrete wall would still take the fixing.** ⚠️ **The two are correlated in most flats, which is exactly why the paraphrase slipped through.**
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ AND THE SUPPORT FRAME IS NOT A TIPPING FIX — IT IS A LOAD PATH.** *«Распределяет нагрузку на пол»*: it transfers the load to the floor instead of the wall. **That reframes the ~40,000 RUB decision recorded from the buyer walkthrough: you are not paying to avoid tipping, you are paying to avoid depending on the wall.**
>
> **→ ⚠️ FOR THIS PROJECT, whose Phase-2 wall bed sits in the combined living space: the wall it fixes to must be identified as concrete or not AT ROUGH STAGE, and if the automatic mechanism is wanted the support frame comes anyway — so the wall question partly dissolves for automatic models and bites hardest for manual ones.** **Consistent with [[_Sources/YT_qMGSfO-glvc_freedom_wallbed_drawbacks_and_struts|FREE_DOM's]] chemical anchors for aerated block — a different maker solving the same non-concrete problem a different way.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Two specifications this vault did not have, both purchasing constraints

1. **⚠️⚠️ THE MATTRESS MUST BE RATED FOR VERTICAL STORAGE.** *«Для наших кроватей нужен СПЕЦИАЛЬНЫЙ матрас, предназначенный для ВЕРТИКАЛЬНОГО ХРАНЕНИЯ»*, and they sell a range in several firmnesses.
   > **→ [[14_Furniture/analysis/Mattress_Construction_And_Selection|The mattress page]] does not mention vertical storage anywhere — a grep across the whole vault returned nothing on it.** **This project intends to buy a wall bed, so it is a live constraint on a purchase the vault otherwise treats as free: not every mattress that wins on construction can be specified into a wall bed.** ⚠️ **He states the requirement and not the reason; a maker has an obvious interest in selling the mattress too, so the CONSTRAINT is recorded and the necessity is flagged as unverified.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ CEILING HEIGHT LIMITS BED LENGTH.** Widths **90–180 cm**; standard length **200 cm**, *«но можем сделать и длиннее, ЕСЛИ ПОЗВОЛЯЮТ ВАШИ ПОТОЛКИ»*.
   > **→ Obvious once said and absent from this vault: a vertical wall bed stands on end, so the ceiling is the constraint on the SLEEPING LENGTH, not just on the cabinet.** **Combined with the 5 cm erection gap already recorded, a room's clear height sets the longest bed that can ever go in it.**

## ⚠️ 4. Construction details worth carrying

- **Base: birch lamellae fixed onto the steel frame** — support plus mattress ventilation. ⚠️ **Same direction as [[_Sources/YT_qMGSfO-glvc_freedom_wallbed_drawbacks_and_struts|FREE_DOM's]] "light base, not metal" argument, reached by a different route: here the lamellae mount to the frame as one system rather than sitting in it as a separate loose base.**
- **⚠️ Front legs of 8 mm solid steel, free-swinging: they rotate into the support position automatically as the bed opens**, with fluoroplastic pads underneath to protect the floor, and optional suede or nylon covers. **→ A checkable detail — the legs deploy themselves, so nothing is left to the user.**
- **⚠️⚠️ On MANUAL models the sofa is NOT fixed to the façade** and can be pulled away and used as separate furniture. **This corroborates the Качанова households from a maker's side — they reported an unattached sofa as deliberate, with the trade-off that you must hold it while folding.** **Here it is confirmed as a property of the manual model specifically.**
- **On AUTOMATIC models the sofa cushions and seat stow themselves**: *«сверху подушки и сиденья дивана прячутся под кровать сами»*, one button, unlocking an electromechanical latch; remote or voice assistant optional.
  > **→ ⚠️⚠️ A FIFTH POSITION ON THE OPEN RETENTION QUESTION, and the first that removes the manual step rather than restraining it.** The vault's four-way space (straps / no straps / elastics on the mattress / elastics on the bedding) all assume a person handles the bedding. ⚠️ **This handles the SOFA CUSHIONS, not the bedding — so it narrows the problem rather than solving it, and the bedding question stays open.**
- Configurations named: with sofa (day living room / night bedroom), without sofa, twin sleeping places for a nursery, corner-sofa version as a separate section, and a **horizontal model as the answer for narrow rooms**.
- Add-on modules: side pencil cases reachable from outside and inside, antresols, hinged wardrobes with shelves and rails, dressing corners with mirrors and poufs, **and a стол-трансформер** — see [[_Sources/YT_oT4828iDsSk_progressdesign_transformer_tables_and_joinery|the tables note]].

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. The cost STRUCTURE — more useful than the one figure

**His build-up, in his own order — which is itself the finding, because it says what drives the number:**

1. **Size** — *«первый этап, и он же САМЫЙ ВАЖНЫЙ»*; standard or bespoke. **⚠️ He tells you to measure the room, or have them measure, BEFORE anything else.**
2. **Shelves at the sides, or none.**
3. **Mechanism — manual or automatic.** ⚠️ With manual *«самому придётся убирать подушки и основания дивана»*; automatic deploys in about **10 seconds**, remote optional.
4. **Options**, which is where he says comfort actually lives: soft bolster (two profiles, square and rounded — their own design), antresol for bedding, **support frame if the wall is not concrete**, soft headboard, **soft covers over the gas struts**, sockets at the bed, reading lights, armrests — which can swallow a folding coffee table or a bottle holder.
5. **Additional modules** — *«мебель-трансформер, она как ТЕТРИС»*: pencil cases, wardrobes, corner units, chests, sliding wardrobes, a long list of table types, **and a concealed ironing board.**
6. **Material** — see the percentage rule below.
7. **Sofa upholstery**, including a fabric *«которому не страшны царапки от ваших домашних питомцев»* — ⚠️ the same pet-durability point the Качанова households reported from the owner side.
8. **Mattress.**

**⚠️⚠️ TWO PERCENTAGE RULES, which are the transferable part because they survive currency and border:**

- **МДФ costs about 30% more than ЛДСП** — *«естественно, во втором случае мебель выйдет дороже процентов на 30, поэтому сразу рассчитывайте»*.
- **⚠️⚠️ ASSEMBLY IS CHARGED AT 10% OF THE TOTAL ORDER VALUE**, with **delivery in Moscow at 3,000 RUB flat.** Outside Moscow he explicitly refuses to quote — it depends on city, dimensions and project — and points to self-assembly from the supplied instructions.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE 10% ASSEMBLY RULE IS THE MOST USEFUL NUMBER IN EITHER VIDEO for this project.** **It is a ratio, not a price: it transfers across currency and jurisdiction in a way none of this vault's Moscow RUB figures do, and it converts any bespoke-furniture quote into a checkable expectation.** ⚠️ **One firm's commercial policy, not a market rate — but it is the first fitted-furniture assembly ratio this vault holds at all.**

**⚠️ THE ONE ABSOLUTE FIGURE, handled under rule 2**: *«одну кровать-трансформер вместе с диваном можно купить всего за 115.000 руб»*.

It is an entry-level headline price from a seller, framed with *«всего за»*, and it is NOT transferred to this Belarusian project.** **No USD is attached: it is a single promotional anchor rather than a market observation, and normalising it would give it a precision it does not have.**
