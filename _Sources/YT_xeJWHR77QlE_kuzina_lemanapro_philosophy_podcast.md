## Advertising / Promotional Filter — the mechanism behind style "philosophies"

- **⚠️ Кузина's structural claim, and it is a usable lens for this vault's own advertising filter**: "философия... насаждается теми, кто должен что-нибудь продать." She is explicit that talk of worldview in interiors triggers her suspicion — "мне всё время кажется, что мне хотят что-то продать, и у меня на это аллергия."
- **⚠️ The mechanism she gives is specific and explains why style-philosophies proliferate now.** Before social media, a brand had a brief window to reach an audience, so it used compressed emotional slogans («живи на яркой стороне») that carried no product characteristics at all — pure emotional sale. **Those no longer work, because banner-style messaging is ignored.** Social media is instead **a long, daily, sequential conversation**, which requires a *worldview* to talk about — so brands now promote a philosophy and try to correlate it with the client's, to build loyalty and stop them leaving. **A named style-philosophy is therefore a marketing artefact first, and only sometimes a design idea.**
- **She notes interior designers themselves have no slogans** — she looked and could not find one — whereas developers' billboards have regressed to bare facts ("дом сдан", "квартира за 8 млн"), competing on price with no emotional content at all.
- **A counter-position is recorded, not resolved.** Жанна Бадальян argues the causation runs the other way: lifestyle and philosophy come first, marketing observes what already resonates and then exploits it until it causes revulsion and the pendulum swings. **Both agree the pendulum is real; they disagree on which end drives it.**
- **⚠️ Pantone's colour of the year is dismissed as pure marketing by both designers** — "маркетинговый ход, но не некий вектор, которым стоит" следовать. Кузина calls the whole idea of a colour of the year "голубая идея… очень странная." Relevant because this vault carries colour-trend content from other sources; **treat annual colour announcements as market signal, not guidance.**

## Design Concept — characterisations worth keeping

- **⚠️ The fashion pendulum, as a reason to discount any current consensus**: at any given moment it is conventional to criticise some particular style. Classical is currently the one being criticised; **"потом все наедятся этим минимализмом"** and it will invert. Her historical example: Northern Art Nouveau in Petersburg was once dismissed as bourgeois and is now admired — enough time has to pass before the value is visible.
- **⚠️ Her actual client-demand observation, which is a data point rather than an opinion: "все хотят минимализм."** Most requests she receives are for minimalism, **understood differently by each client** — she flags the definitional spread as the real problem.
- **⚠️ Scandi and Japandi furniture is mid-century 1950s–60s furniture** with different upholstery — "это та же самая мебель, просто визуальный ряд другой." Useful because it collapses three apparently distinct style briefs onto one procurement category.
- **⚠️ Wabi-sabi and Japandi are about фактура (tactile texture), not colour and not ornament — and they depend heavily on correct lighting design.** "Орнаментов там нету никаких, про фактуры, тактильное ощущение. И очень сильно это завязано со световым дизайном... цвет — это лишнее в данном уравнении." **The lighting dependency is the actionable part**: these schemes fail without a resolved lighting design, because texture is what they rely on and texture only reads under directional light.
- **Overproduction argument (Бадальян)**: furniture has been produced "на 200 лет вперёд," so reuse and restoration are not a passing trend — new catalogue furniture will keep standing next to a restored family armchair. Offered as a durable direction rather than a fashion.

## Planning Rules — client intake, and the best item in the source

- **⚠️ Asked what advice to give someone trying to make an interior match their inner world, Кузина declines the premise**: **"я бы советовала не копать слишком глубоко, потому что если начнёшь задумываться о том, что конкретно соответствует моему сложному внутреннему миру, то можно остаться без ремонта."**
