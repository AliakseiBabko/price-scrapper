## ⚠️⚠️ 2. The storage principle worth keeping

> **The task of balcony storage is to free area for the other uses and make the storage as invisible as possible — *«лучше отдать часть площади под один большой закрытый шкаф, где всё скроется от глаз, чем делать много маленьких полочек и комодов»*.**

> **→ One large closed cupboard beats several small open ones.** **Complements [[_Sources/YT_HeUN7nE3fNA_elitbalkon_balcony_fitout_planning|ЭлитБалкон's]] material on WHAT that cupboard must be made of on a cold balcony (aluminium carcass, not ЛДСП) and on deciding its position before the glazing is measured.** **Together they answer "one cupboard, here, in these materials."**

**⚠️ And the qualification, which keeps it honest: in loft or Scandinavian schemes OPEN storage is acceptable and can be a feature — a bicycle on a brick wall needs no hiding.** Wall and ceiling bike systems and compact pull-out or fold-down drying racks are named as the space-savers.

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. A workspace on a NARROW loggia — the only usable dimensional content

- **⚠️ At 85–100 cm depth the only option is a desk or worktop against the END wall, so the pier screens the workplace as much as possible** — or a light-transmitting partition. **That depth suffices for a small desk, with document storage on closed shelves above.**
- **⚠️ Leave free space above your head.**
- **⚠️⚠️ At ≥1.5 m the desk can be turned, or you can sit with your back to the wall** — and she advises keeping the arrangement changeable so the view or the setting can be altered later.
- **⚠️ Blackout curtains to shield the monitor from daytime sun**, plus real artificial light — a desk lamp or linear lighting under wall cabinets.
- **⚠️⚠️ THE FIRST-FIX DETAIL: bring the sockets out into the BACK WALL of the document-and-printer cabinet** — cables hidden, the printer stays free of dust, and the cabinet reads as part of the interior rather than as office equipment.
  **→ A socket position decided by a cabinet that does not exist yet. Same shape as this batch's other "decide the furniture before the services" findings, and it is cheap only at first fix.**

⚠️ **She repeats the vault's existing point that a study on a loggia needs insulation and re-glazing first, since winters are cold and you sit there for hours.**

## ⚠️ 4. The winter-garden tidiness principle

> **Plants otherwise end up scattered across floor, shelves and stools in mismatched pots, which *«выглядит часто неопрятно»*. Her fix: gather them onto dedicated stands, or dedicate one or two whole walls using IDENTICAL pots.** Ready "agro-farm" units with their own irrigation and lighting are named as a tidy alternative.

> **→ Recorded because it is a composition rule with a functional payoff — consolidating the plants frees floor for a second use, which is the same logic as §2.** ⚠️ **Her terrace-styling suggestions (clinker, decking, facade light fittings, outdoor furniture) are aesthetic and are not routed.**
