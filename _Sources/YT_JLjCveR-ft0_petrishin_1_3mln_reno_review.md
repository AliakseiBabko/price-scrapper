## Design Concept — Window-Portal Conversion, Ceiling, and Balcony Technique

6. **Window-portal-to-desk conversion for ergonomic continuity**: an
   interior window block (between two rooms) was removed and a desk was
   built flush/level with the room's existing countertop across the
   opening, so the work surface reads as one continuous level rather
   than two mismatched surfaces at different heights.
7. **Felt-backed decorative sound-dampening panel used at both a sleep-
   nook accent wall and a window-portal surround**: framed by the source
   as serving two purposes simultaneously — decorative finish and extra
   sound isolation from neighbors — a second application context for
   this project's already-documented decorative acoustic panel material,
   here also used to finish a converted window-portal opening.
8. **Concealed curtain track built into a one-piece stretch ceiling**: the
   stretch-ceiling install includes a hidden cornice/curtain-track recess
   as part of the single continuous ceiling membrane, avoiding a visible
   surface-mounted curtain rod.
9. **Balcony insulation-and-glazing effectiveness verified in practice, not
   just claimed**: this project's balcony (пеноплекс insulation on floor/
   walls/ceiling, heated floor, tension ceiling, upgraded glazing) stayed
   comfortable during finishing work even before the radiators/heating
   system were connected — a real-world confirmation that the
   insulation+glazing combination alone (without active heating) can hold
   heat, provided it's done "по технологии" (per correct technique).
   **Glazing spec given as the concrete lever**: a 70mm-profile REHAU
   frame with a double-chamber glass unit is recommended; the source
   explicitly warns that a thinner single-chamber glass unit risks
   freezing/heat loss at the glazing itself, regardless of the frame.
