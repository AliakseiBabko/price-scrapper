## Furniture / Built-ins — ⚠️ two adjustment arguments that generalise beyond these products

**These are the strongest items in the source and neither is specific to Chinese hardware.**

- **⚠️ Facades bonded directly to a stone carcass have no adjustment.** "На петлях есть регулировка — ты петли можешь двигать влево-вправо, вниз, вверх, по чуть-чуть. Эти ты приклеил. Всё." **If a fitter bonds them slightly off, prising them off a expensive stone carcass and re-bonding true is misery — "ты устанешь это всё монтировать."** **Adjustability is a property to check before choosing a construction, not an afterthought.** (Note this is a different construction from the *facing* technique in [[_Sources/YT_Ss-JlEvrR2w_flat_facade_materials_part2|Ss-JlEvrR2w]], where stone is bonded to an MDF substrate and the hardware fixes to the MDF — that one keeps the adjustment.)
- **⚠️ Pull-out and lift mechanisms often lack sufficient adjustment range to set gaps properly — and the failure is progressive and visible.** "Зачастую регулировок недостаточно и выставить зазоры нормально просто невозможно." **His worked scenario: load a tall pull-out column (~150–200 cm) with heavy kitchen items and it sags slightly; a large facade then rubs against the adjacent one. "Ты заплатил кучу, идеального результата по итогу не добился. И ты смотришь постоянно на кухню, и у тебя там щели вот такие."**
- **⚠️ And the client-relations consequence, recorded because it is honest about his own trade: they do warn clients of these nuances, the client waits three or four months, sees the result, and does not remember being warned — "он смотрит в итог, и здесь диссонанс возникает."** **The practical lesson: get the nuances in writing, which is exactly what his own specification rule in [[_Sources/YT_TNMzWzhAqqI_flat_choosing_furniture_maker|TNMzWzhAqqI]] is for.**

## Furniture / Built-ins — the other mechanisms, briefly

- **A tall pull-out column versus plain shelves behind a hinged door: "по функционалу у тебя просто полки."** Access may be marginally better; **the question is whether it is worth the adjustment risk above.**
- **"Trolleybus-door" parallel/folding hinge systems**: not seen on the Russian market. Plausibly good, needs testing; **they experimented with something similar on very large doors and have "вопросики." Certainly costs multiples of ordinary hinges — "скорее всего, кратно дороже петель."** Justified only if you specifically want the effect and accept the price.
- **A steel/metal countertop**: he likes the look and accepts water will not harm it or swell it — **but scratch resistance is unproven and the clip's fork test is not a real crash test. "У меня нет личного опыта эксплуатации такой столешницы вдолгую."**
- **⚠️ A pop-up/extending bar counter above an island — rejected on ergonomics, and the reasoning is reusable: it sits higher than the island, so it needs tall stools. "Рабочая поверхность у тебя 900, стол у тебя там 790" — and the choice of ordinary chairs is far wider than semi-bar stools, and sitting at them is more comfortable in a classical posture.** Also: if you keep it permanently extended it has no purpose. **Better to plan a proper low table sized to the household.** (The 900 mm worktop / ~790 mm table pair is a useful dimensional anchor.)
- **A pull-out hidden table**: more interesting than the bar counter *if* the mechanism is reliable — **but the sped-up demo hides the reality: "тебе же просто кофе попить, тебе надо стол вытащить, стул приставить."** Plan the right table up front.
- **Track sockets (movable along a rail)**: sensible where the run covers a whole wall panel; **pointless on a small island — "у тебя любой провод дотянется. Поставь её чуть-чуть сбоку асимметрично, чтобы симпатично было."** He would not fit them.
- **A retracting hob** he dismisses outright — "уезжает, чтобы что?"

## Market Data — ⚠️ procurement reality for one-off exotic mechanisms

- **The genuinely useful market fact: to obtain a mechanism like these you must approach the Chinese supplier directly, "и они скажут: берите фуру." Ask for one unit and the answer is effectively a prohibitive one-off price.**
- **So a viral mechanism seen online is frequently not procurable as a single item at a sane cost** — a constraint to check before designing around one.
