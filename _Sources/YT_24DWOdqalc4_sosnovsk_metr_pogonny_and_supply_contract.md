## ⚠️⚠️ Why this source matters: the vault had zero coverage of the unit kitchens are sold in

**A grep before this round found `метр погонный` in NO file** across `03_Kitchen/`, `14_Furniture/` and `15_Appliances/`. **It is the unit every kitchen in this market is advertised in, and it is where the comparison between two quotes goes wrong.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. The definition, and why the number is nearly meaningless on its own

> **A погонный метр is the indicative cost of a 1-metre length of kitchen with a MINIMAL set of elements** — standard cabinet depth and height, the simplest finish, the minimum equipment.

**Method: sum every element, then divide by the run length.** *«Если стоимость гарнитура стандартной комплектации равна 50 000 руб за 2 метра, то стоимость одного метра погонного будет 25 000 руб.»*

> ***«Финальную стоимость этого кухонного гарнитура без детального просчёта всех её элементов вам не сможет сказать НИ ОДИН производитель. Цена погонного метра позволяет предварительно оценить стоимость, но С БОЛЬШИМИ ОГОВОРКАМИ. Корректнее будет говорить о МИНИМАЛЬНОЙ цене метра погонного — то есть "от".»***

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. What a standard погонный метр actually contains — and what his own kitchen's metre contains

**This is the comparison that makes the point, and he runs it on his own kitchen:**

| A quoted «стандартный» metre | The same metre of his own kitchen |
| :--- | :--- |
| Hinged doors top and bottom | A **Vibo pull-out basket**, a **Hettich TandemBox**, two more drawer boxes and one hidden box |
| **No drawer boxes, no hidden drawers** | — |
| **One bottom drawer on ball-bearing runners** | — |
| Standard oven position; no extra side panels | — |
| — | ⚠️ *«Тут фактически наполнения тысяч на 15–20»* **on that one metre** |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE ADVERTISED FIGURE PRICES AN EMPTY BOX. The fitting-out is a separate cost of the same order as the metre itself**, which is why a real kitchen lands at a multiple of the headline number. **→ Compare quotes on the SPECIFICATION, never on the metre price.**

**⚠️⚠️ And a distortion nobody corrects for — the corner.** *«Если берётся для расчёта УГЛОВАЯ кухня, то расчёт получается изначально НЕКОРРЕКТНЫМ: по длине столешницы получается одна длина, но в этом углу НЕТ ФАСАДА, поэтому цена должна быть меньше. Но никто это не принимает в расчёты — рассчитывается по стандарту.»*

> **→ A corner kitchen quoted per linear metre is systematically over-quoted, because the corner contributes length but no front.** ⚠️ **A structural bias in the industry's own unit, named by someone selling in it.**

## ⚠️ 3. The base specification a погонный метр assumes — a checklist of what to upgrade

Plain slab fronts, no milling, no glass · **plastic countertop 38 mm**, budget, no overlay or rounded edge · a simple rail or bow handle with galvanic coating · **Chinese hinges** (soft-close *«разница небольшая»*) · the wall bead supplied with the countertop · **PVC plinth** · **carcass of ЛДСП 16 mm** · upper modules on a metal rail with adjustable hangers.

**Standard dimensions he states:** upper cabinets **720 mm** high (his own are 920); lower **860 mm** including front, plinth and countertop; upper depth **300 mm**; lower side panels **560 mm** deep, with a technological gap left behind for services.

- **⚠️⚠️ AND A FASTENER SUBSTITUTION TO CONFIRM IN WRITING:** *«Крепёж может быть заменён: евровинты, сделанные ради экономии, — на эксцентриковые стяжки, Rafix и так далее. Это тоже надо уточнять обязательно при заказе.»* ⚠️ **Note this cuts the OPPOSITE way to [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Cabinet_Assembly_Technique|CAPPUCCINO's rule]] that visible faces should use minifix rather than confirmat** — there the cam fitting is the upgrade; here the euro screw is. **→ Both are true of different locations, which is exactly why the CONTRACT has to name which fastener goes where rather than which is "better".**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. The contract: the three documents, and the one that proves what you ordered

> **Предмет договора**: *«Продавец обязуется изготовить и передать заказчику кухонный гарнитур СОГЛАСНО СПЕЦИФИКАЦИИ И ЭСКИЗУ, приложенным к этому договору.»*

- **СПЕЦИФИКАЦИЯ** — the list of materials and hardware.
- **⚠️⚠️ ЭСКИЗ — the drawing of your kitchen WITH DIMENSIONS.** *«Может получиться так, что вы заказали УГЛОВОЙ кухонный гарнитур, а вам привезут ПРЯМОЙ — и вот именно эта картинка с размерами является ДОКАЗАТЕЛЬСТВОМ того, что вы заказали.»*

**Why it matters, in his framing:** several people act for the maker — **the sales designer who takes the order, the measurer, and the fitter** — *«каждый из этих лиц может накосячить»*. You end up with the wrong size, the wrong front colour, **Chinese hardware instead of Italian** — *«и вроде бы вы помните, что была итальянская фурнитура, но ДОКАЗАТЬ ЭТО НЕ МОЖЕТЕ»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A DIMENSIONED DRAWING IS THE EVIDENCE ARTEFACT, and this project already produces dimensioned drawings.** **The album is the эскиз.** ⚠️ **Pair this with [[_Sources/YT_Lfvs1XUz53s_sosnovsk_carcass_construction_and_edge_technology|the ДЕТАЛИРОВКА]] from the same channel — that proves the carcass is genuinely custom, this proves what you agreed. Two different documents, both requestable before payment.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Six contract terms he says to fix before signing

1. **⚠️⚠️ ENUMERATE THE ADDITIONAL SERVICES.** Assembly, appliance installation, appliance connection, carrying to the flat. **The failure he names: if the LIGHTING connection is not listed, the fitters can decline to connect it and be entirely within their rights.** *«Поставьте паузу и [перечитайте] эти услуги.»*
2. **⚠️ SPECIFIC DELIVERY DATES, NOT RANGES.** A range is acceptable only for the installation window (1–5 days) or interregional transport. **⚠️⚠️ Check for any clause letting the maker extend the deadline UNILATERALLY** — *«иначе вы свою кухню никогда не дождётесь»*. **Also check for unilateral rights to change the facade colour or shade.**
3. **⚠️⚠️ PAY PARTIALLY, NEVER 100% UP FRONT** — he suggests 50–70%. *«Эти оставшиеся проценты будут вашей СТРАХОВКОЙ»* against delay, and against the firm going bankrupt.
4. **⚠️⚠️ DO NOT SIGN THE АКТ ПРИЁМКИ-ПЕРЕДАЧИ IN A HURRY.** *«Монтажники могут подсунуть акт — "подпишите, потом разберёмся". Я вас уверяю, что потом разбираться с вами НЕ БУДУТ, и будут полностью правы, потому что вы подписали акт.»* **→ The inspection checklist to run before signing is [[_Sources/YT_A9Kef6CvNZ4_sosnovsk_kitchen_after_nine_years|the same presenter's nine-year walkthrough]].**
5. **A reasonable rectification period — 14 working days, not 35.**
6. **On cancellation the maker should retain only ACTUALLY INCURRED costs, and must be able to prove them** — not an arbitrary percentage of the price.

## ⚠️⚠️ 6. Russian consumer law as he states it — recorded, flagged, NOT routed

> **⚠️⚠️ EVERYTHING IN THIS SECTION IS RUSSIAN LAW, ASSERTED BY A FURNITURE SELLER AND NOT CHECKED AGAINST THE STATUTE. Standing rule 4 makes `16_Legal_and_Regulations/` Belarus-only, so NOTHING here is routed there. Belarusian equivalents must be confirmed separately and none of these numbers should be assumed to carry.**

- **⚠️⚠️ THE PENALTY RATE DEPENDS ON THE CONTRACT TYPE — and this is the sharpest item.** A **договор купли-продажи** carries **0.5% per day** of delay; a **works-and-services contract** carries **3% per day**. *«Многие крупные компании делают этот договор СМЕШАННЫМ: продают вам кухню [по купле-продаже], а монтаж делают отдельным договором — чтобы за кухню платить 0,5% просрочки, а не 3%. Практически 90% крупных компаний именно так и делают.»*
  **→ ⚠️⚠️ A DELIBERATE STRUCTURING CHOICE THAT MOVES THE BUYER'S REMEDY BY 6×, disclosed by an insider. Whatever the Belarusian equivalent, the QUESTION transfers: which contract type covers which part of the job?**
- **If delivery is late** you may: send a written claim; set a new deadline; cancel and recover the prepayment; **claim a per-day penalty — and he says it is owed even if the contract omits it.**
- **Warranty: minimum two years if the contract is silent.** The contract should state that furniture is made to the showroom samples and that materials conform to GOST with hygiene certificates.
- **On defects, four remedies**: free rectification; a price reduction; free replacement (returning the original); or rectification by a third party at the maker's cost.
- **⚠️⚠️ GETTING YOUR MONEY BACK NEEDS A "SUBSTANTIAL" DEFECT, and his calibration is the useful part:** either the repair cost approaches the cost of making a new set, or the defect makes the set unusable for its purpose. ***«Царапины, сколы, отошедшая кромка — ДАЖЕ ЕСЛИ ЦВЕТ ФАСАДА НЕ СООТВЕТСТВУЕТ ЗАКАЗАННОМУ — это всё НЕсущественный брак.»*** **→ A wrong facade colour does not entitle you to a refund. That is a sobering calibration and it is an argument for catching everything at the эскиз stage.**
- **⚠️ A kitchen set cannot be returned for "it did not suit by form, colour or style"** — unlike a chair, table or sofa.
- **Escalation**: a written претензия in two copies (one retained, signed and stamped, or sent by registered post) → **no reply in 10 days** → Rospotrebnadzor plus a court claim.
