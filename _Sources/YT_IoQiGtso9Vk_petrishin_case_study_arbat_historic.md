## Flooring — Rules / Heuristics

3. **⚠️ Adjustable/leveling floor system used instead of screed, for a
   structural-weight reason specific to an old timber-beam building**:
   most rooms on this object got a "regulated floor" (регулируемые
   полы — an adjustable-pedestal floor system) rather than a poured
   screed; screed was poured **only in the corridor**, over the
   existing structural timber beams, specifically because that
   location's load capacity allowed the extra screed weight ("там балки
   были, и по весу это всё проходило"). A concrete real-object example
   of choosing screed vs. a lighter dry floor system room-by-room based
   on the old structure's actual load capacity, not a blanket choice for
   the whole apartment.

## Ceilings — Rules / Heuristics
