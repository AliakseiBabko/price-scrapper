## ⚠️⚠️ 1. The fork, and he refuses to crown a winner

> ***«Пружинный или беспружинный, какой лучше? Ответ: они ОБА хороши, но КАЖДЫЙ ДЛЯ СВОЕГО ДИАПАЗОНА ЗАДАЧ.»***

| | **Spring (independent block)** | **Springless (foam / coir / combinations)** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Strength** | High anatomicity and point elasticity; **can be built across a wide range for different builds and requirements** | **⚠️⚠️ "The king of THIN mattresses"** |
| **⚠️⚠️ Ventilation** | **Space between springs AND free space inside each pocket → ventilates well; excess moisture does not accumulate, even if liquid gets in** | ⚠️⚠️ **The weak point — see below** |
| **Transport** | — | **⚠️ Resists critical deformation, compression and bending → ships and stores ROLLED UP**; you can carry one to a dacha yourself |
| **Best for** | Adults, high loads, comfort and anatomicity requirements | **Children's mattresses; sofa mattresses; toppers; thin mattresses that bridge a fold-out sofa's gaps; cheap dacha or rental sleeping places; anything that must be moved** |

- **⚠️⚠️ A MONOLITHIC FOAM BLOCK USUALLY FAILS ON ONE AXIS OR THE OTHER** — *«не обладает либо нужной анатомичностью, либо нужной нагрузочной способностью»* — **so a good springless mattress has to COMBINE different foam types.** **→ "Springless" is not one product; a single-slab one is the cheap case.**
- **⚠️⚠️ AND THE MOISTURE WARNING, with the best analogy in the round:** foam and latexed-foam mattresses are **very sensitive to moisture ingress.** *«Представьте себе губку, которой мы моем посуду. Если помыть посуду с утра и не отжать губку, вечером вы придёте — губка всё равно мокрая. Может появиться запах, могут размножаться бактерии.»* **→ A springless mattress has no internal air path to dry itself out.**

**⚠️⚠️ AND THE PRICE ANSWER, WHICH IS THE OPPOSITE OF WHAT A BUYER EXPECTS:** *«Получится ли сэкономить, выбирая тот или иной вариант? Скорее всего, НЕТ.»* **Same height, same load capacity, same cover → the two types land at about the same price.** **→ The spring/springless choice is not a budget lever. Choose it on task.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Choosing a springless mattress — three numbers

1. **⚠️⚠️ DEFORMATION: ideally a mattress should compress no more than 30%, better 20%.** This is what guarantees it does not press on protruding body parts — **he names the shoulder when side-sleeping** — and that it lasts.
2. **⚠️⚠️ HEIGHT FOR A SIDE SLEEPER: take your shoulder dimension and multiply by THREE** — *«скорее всего получится 17–18 сантиметров и больше»*. **Stated again as a floor: minimum 17–18 cm for a full adult springless mattress.**
3. **Standard build and weight → standard foams at a load capacity of 110–120 kg**, chosen with a margin.

> **⚠️⚠️ AND TWO DISCOMFORTS FROM A MATTRESS THAT IS TOO THIN, both of which are about the EDGE rather than the middle:**
> - **When you SIT on it, it compresses too far and your legs press against the bed frame's edge** — *«это некомфортно, может быть даже больно»*.
> - **It becomes hard to get up from, especially in older age.**
>
> **→ Mattress height is an ergonomic dimension, not only a comfort one — and it interacts with the bed height rule (knee bend ± a palm).**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Springless materials — and the one outright warning

- **⚠️⚠️ COCONUT COIR IS A REINFORCING LAYER, NOT A COMFORT ONE.** Placed **between foam layers** it spreads load onto the main foam block, **raising load capacity — and simultaneously raising perceived firmness and support.** **→ If you need higher load capacity or long service, look for reinforcing isolator layers of coir or similar.**
- **Memory foam is normally a TOP layer of a few centimetres**: better anatomical conformity, **less back-pressure, less soft-tissue numbness, less tossing.** *«Это напрямую влияет на качество сна. За это можно доплатить, если у вас со сном проблемы.»*
- **Modern foams combine LARGE cells (comfort) with SMALL cells (support)** — expensive, complex to make, and **not a place to economise.** ⚠️ Foams with a cell-structured surface are *claimed* to give a light micro-massage effect and better top-layer ventilation — **a manufacturer's claim, recorded as such.**
- **⚠️⚠️ THE LATEX WARNING, which is the sharpest item here:** latexed foams generally **lack air permeability**, which is why they are perforated. **In an all-springless mattress there is no internal air for ventilation at all.** ***«Если помещение влажное, если у вас повышенное потоотделение — избегайте, скорее всего вариант с латексом вам не подойдёт.»***
  **→ ⚠️ Read with §1's sponge analogy: the moisture problem and the latex problem are the same problem.**
- **Cover tiers, all three legitimate**: a quilted cover that is itself a mini-mattress; **plain synthetic jacquard** (cheaper — rental, dacha, temporary); or **a dense unquilted KNIT**, which *«не скрадывает ощущение от матраса»* and gives high point elasticity. **→ A quilted cover masks what the mattress underneath is doing; an unquilted knit reveals it.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Economy vs premium, compared side by side

**Both are the same maker's, which removes the brand variable and is the reason this comparison is worth anything.**

| | **Economy** | **Premium ("DELUXE")** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cover** | Synthetic jacquard — dense but **does not stretch** — on a thin volumetric layer | **Effectively a thin mattress**: foam, a thick volumetric layer, and **elastic KNIT** giving point elasticity |
| **Comfort layer** | Standard inexpensive foam | **A combination of modern foams, large + small cells, high-density** |
| **Spring protection** | Non-woven thermofelt against premature abrasion | A more durable abrasion layer that **also raises perceived firmness and permissible load** |
| **Spring block** | Standard height | **⚠️⚠️ Substantially GREATER HEIGHT** |
| **Rating** | 110 kg, medium firmness | Higher load, more universal across sleeping positions |

> **⚠️⚠️ THE MECHANISM BEHIND THE PRICE, AND IT IS GEOMETRIC RATHER THAN MARKETING:** a taller spring block has a **greater deformation range**, so **the same absolute deformation that is critical for the economy block is non-critical for the tall one** — and under the same load a taller block deforms **less**. *«Кратно увеличивается срок службы.»*
>
> **→ You are buying DEFORMATION HEADROOM. That is a checkable idea, it explains why height tracks price, and it is the most transferable thing in the three videos.**

- **⚠️ One construction detail worth an ask**: on the premium model **the filling is assembled as a SEPARATE PACKAGE, structurally divided from the spring block**, which he says increases the mattress's elasticity.
- ⚠️ **His claim that the economy model has "no hidden economy on wire thickness, pocket material or stakany" is unverifiable and is about his own product.**
