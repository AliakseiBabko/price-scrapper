> **⚠️ On cycles this is a SECOND and LOWER figure than the vault already holds.** [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|The mechanisms page]] records **30,000 cycles** from an earlier source, ~41 years at twice-daily use. **20,000 at twice daily is ~27 years. Either way cycle life is not the binding constraint for a household — so the range 20k–30k can be recorded without needing to resolve it.**
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ BUT THE 3-YEAR WARRANTY IS THE NUMBER THAT BITES HERE, and it is new.** **This project's plan is to INSTALL the unit during the current Phase 1 build and ACTIVATE it in Phase 2, ~3–4 years later** (see [[00_Master/Bedroom_Design_Principles|Bedroom Design Principles]]). **On this maker's terms the mechanism warranty would expire at or before the moment the bed enters daily use — the unit would go through its entire warranted life unused.**
>
> **→ That does not argue against installing now; the cavity, fixings and wiring genuinely must be built now. It argues for settling, before ordering: whether the warranty runs from delivery or from commissioning, and whether the gas struts specifically can be bought or replaced separately later.** **Struts are a wear part and the cheapest component in the assembly.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Two model types — and this one IS a rough-stage decision

> **One model carries SUPPORT FRAMES at the base that prevent tipping, so it *«может стоять у любой стены»*. The other has no frame and REQUIRES fixing to a capital / load-bearing wall.** **The difference between them is about 40,000 RUB.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS A PHASE-1 BUILD CONSTRAINT, not a furniture choice made later.** **If the frameless type is chosen, a load-bearing wall must be identified and the unit's position fixed to it before partitions and finishes go in. If the anti-tip-frame type is chosen, the position is free but it costs more and the base frames consume floor depth.**
>
> **⚠️ Consistent with [[_Sources/YT_qMGSfO-glvc_freedom_wallbed_drawbacks_and_struts|FREE_DOM]], who fixes at six points and uses CHEMICAL anchors where the wall is aerated block — i.e. a non-capital wall is workable, but only with the right fixing specified in advance.**
>
> ⚠️⚠️ **PRICE HANDLING, rule 2: the ~40,000 RUB figure is a DELTA between two of this maker's models, not a product price. Region is Russia (Moscow showroom named at the close); no year is stated, so the publication date bounds it — `price_date_basis: upper_bound_from_publication`, 2024-05-25. ## ⚠️ 3. Fittings, and the strap position

- **Side shelves reachable from inside the made-up bed, acting as bedside tables; lighting and sockets can be built in.**
- **⚠️⚠️ ON STRAPS THEY ARE FIRMLY FOR — and this IS a new independent maker voice on that side.** **Two straps hold the mattress, pillows and duvet together; you make the bed, strap it, fold it away.** *«Нет необходимости предусматривать отдельное место, какие-то ящики, какие-то шкафы.»* **So no separate bedding storage is needed at all.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|The mechanisms page]] records straps as an unresolved manufacturer disagreement. This adds a third independent maker to the FOR side, with the strongest version of the argument: the straps REMOVE a storage requirement.** **Against it stands FREE_DOM's documented failure — a strap coming undone and jamming bedding between mattress and wall — and [[_Sources/YT_ztubNCfTyhg_kachanova_seven_transformer_households|two households using ELASTICS instead]], which is a middle path neither maker proposes.** **Still open, and now better mapped.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Installation constraints — all of them design-stage, from the companion video

> **His own framing: *«Обсуждение этих деталей крайне важно на этапе проектировки»*.**

1. **⚠️⚠️ LEAVE A 5 cm TECHNICAL GAP TO THE CEILING.** **The unit is assembled flat on the floor — standard practice — and then raised. A wall bed or antresol built exactly to ceiling height cannot be tilted up without a high probability of damaging the ceiling.** **A filler panel can close the gap afterwards, floor-to-ceiling, for a seamless look.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ SKIRTING MUST COME OFF BEHIND THE BED.** **The bed fixes to the wall and its bearing point must be hard against it; a standard skirting leaves a gap behind the bed and obstructs installation.** It can be carefully refitted to the visible sections afterwards.
3. **⚠️⚠️ FOR THE WARDROBE the rule differs: you can notch the PLINTH around the skirting instead — but the plinth must be TALLER than the skirting. A typical plinth is 7 cm; against a 12 cm skirting the plinth must be at least 12 cm.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ ALL THREE ARE DECIDED BEFORE THE UNIT IS DRAWN, and two of them constrain choices this project makes at rough stage anyway.** **The skirting rule in particular now has TWO independent constraints pulling on it in this vault: [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Cable_Circuits_and_Panel_Design|REMCRAFT]] notes that shadow and hidden skirtings need a deep recess and interact with wiring depth; this says skirting HEIGHT decides whether a transformer unit can sit tight to the wall and how tall its plinth must be.** **Skirting is not a finishing detail here — it is an input to both the electrics and the joinery.**
