## ⚠️⚠️ 1. The organising principle: height follows sleeping position

**Stated across every pillow type he shows, which is what makes it the finding rather than the product notes:**

> **Back sleepers → LOWER pillow. Side sleepers → HIGHER pillow.**

**The mechanism is implicit but consistent: on your side the gap between head and mattress is a shoulder-width deep; on your back it is not.** ⚠️ **He never states it that plainly — this is the vault's reading of a rule he applies four times.**

## ⚠️ 2. The types, and the one he warns against

| Type | His position |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Memory foam** | ***«Практически эталонный выбор»*** — universal for back AND side sleepers, **provided the height is chosen for the position.** Conforms to head and neck |
| **Latex** | **Natural, hypoallergenic** — good where there are allergic reactions to dust or odours. The sample shown is **low → back sleepers**; side sleepers need a taller latex pillow |
| **⚠️⚠️ Rigid orthopaedic foam** | **Strong neck fixation. Near-ideal for SIDE sleeping IF you have essentially no cervical problems.** ⚠️⚠️ **But if you sleep on your BACK and have issues with cervical lordosis, that rigid curve *«будет лишь вредить вашему сну»* — and you risk waking with headache and neck pain** |
| **Лебяжий пух (synthetic)** | **⚠️ NOT an orthopaedic pillow** — different shape, and he says so. Hypoallergenic despite the name |
| **Three-layer adjustable** | Memory / latex / memory under a cooling ventilated cover — **layers can be removed and reordered** |

**⚠️⚠️ THE SYNTHETIC-DOWN TECHNIQUE IS THE MOST ACTIONABLE THING IN THE VIDEO, because it costs nothing and most people do it wrong:**

> **On your back, your HEAD AND SHOULDERS must BOTH be on the pillow.** *«Если вы подбиваете подушку и пытаетесь лечь на спину, а плечи ваши лежат ВНЕ подушки, то ваша шея будет ПЕРЕГРУЖАТЬСЯ.»*
>
> **On your side you can simply crush it to the height you need.**

**⚠️ The three-layer pillow's adjustability is the interesting product idea**: latex layer on top = firmer; memory on top = softer; latex moved to the bottom = softer still; a single layer if preferred. **→ It converts pillow height and firmness from a purchase decision into an adjustment**, which is the honest answer to §3.

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Two rules that generalise past pillows

1. **⚠️⚠️ *«Каждому человеку надо подбирать подушку ИНДИВИДУАЛЬНО, и даже с одинаковым типом сложения два разных человека могут испытывать разные ощущения от одной подушки.»*** **→ Build does not predict the answer, so a recommendation — including his — cannot substitute for trying it.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ THE TEST-CONDITION RULE, and it is genuinely clever:** *«Когда вы идёте в магазин за подушкой, ЗАПОМНИТЕ ЖЁСТКОСТЬ ВАШЕЙ КРОВАТИ и протестируйте подушку на точно такой же кровати с точно такой же жёсткостью. В противном случае вы рискуете прийти домой и испытать другие ощущения.»*
   **→ A pillow is not tested in isolation — how far you sink into the mattress changes the pillow height you need.** **This is the pillow analogue of the mattress source's ten-minute lie-down, and the two together mean: test both, on each other, before buying either.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Toppers — and the first thing is what a topper is NOT

> ***«Топперы, или тонкие матрасы — НЕ ПУТАТЬ с наматрасниками или защитными чехлами.»***

**Purpose: rescue a sagging mattress or an uncomfortable sofa when a proper bed and mattress are not possible.** *«Логично, что это должна быть достаточно бюджетная покупка.»*

**⚠️⚠️ TWO OPPOSITE GROUPS, AND BUYING FROM THE WRONG ONE IS THE FAILURE MODE:**

| Group | Material and thickness | What it does |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **① RAISE FIRMNESS and LEVEL** | **Coconut coir, 1 cm or 3 cm**; **6 cm coir ≈ a thin or child's mattress, very firm** | **Levels ANY sagging mattress.** ⚠️⚠️ **Cannot be folded — folding breaks the structure and leaves a permanent crease**, so storage is awkward for sofa use |
| **② RAISE COMFORT** | Foam 3 cm or 6 cm; **memory foam**; latex foam (denser, dearer) at 2–3 cm | Levels less; changes how the surface feels. **Memory foam distributes pressure, reduces soft-tissue numbness, less tossing** |
| **Combination** | **2 cm coir + 2 cm foam**; or struttofaiber + coir at 6 cm, reversible | **Levels and firms while the foam preserves comfort** |

> **⚠️⚠️ THE SOFA RECOMMENDATION IS THE MOST SPECIFIC AND PRACTICAL ITEM: a 3 cm FOAM topper on a fold-out sofa.** It **closes the gaps at the joints of the opened sofa**, raises comfort, and means *«вы спите не на том, на чём сидите»*. **Cheap, foldable any way, and hides inside or behind the sofa.**
>
> **→ ⚠️ And note the pairing with the coir group: a coir topper would solve the same sagging but CANNOT be folded, so it is the wrong tool for a sofa specifically.** **That is the whole reason the two-group split matters.**

**⚠️ His memory-foam topper case**: worth it *«если есть проблемы с качеством сна, особенно на диване»* — **and specifically where the sofa's sleeping surface is already FLAT** (he names an accordion mechanism), i.e. where levelling is not the problem and comfort is.
