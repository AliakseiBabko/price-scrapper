## Kids Room — Sports Corner Minimum Configuration

- **A Swedish wall (шведская стенка) plus a pull-up bar alone is stated to be an inadequate default that most clients mistakenly assume is "enough"** — real repeated client feedback across "hundreds of projects": a child engages with a Swedish-wall-only setup for at most ~2 weeks, then loses interest; even adding a hanging rope to it doesn't change the outcome. - **Space requirement caveat, explicit**: this full configuration needs meaningfully more floor/wall space than a single Swedish wall — only viable in a sufficiently large apartment (worked example: a 91m² unit with two separate kids' bedrooms).
