## Walls / Ceilings — Paint Pros / Cons (durable facts)

1. **Paint durability**: 15-25 years when properly applied per
   technology, the longest-lived of the three options compared.
2. **Maintenance/cleanability**: a stain wipes off easily with a damp
   melamine sponge; higher-gloss paint cleans easier than matte, but
   over-glossing risks a "hospital" look — balance cleanability against
   aesthetic.
3. **Full repaint/redecoration freedom**: color changes or adding
   molding/plaster trim later is easy — "you never become a hostage to
   painted walls."
4. **Unlimited color/shade customization** — any color, any shade, any
   custom tint mix, described as ideal for precise designer color-matching.
5. **⚠️ Cost driver: most of paint's expense is wall-prep, not the paint
   itself** — walls must be leveled near-perfectly before painting,
   since a thin paint layer highlights rather than hides surface
   imperfections. Corroborates (independently, general-technique level)
   this store's existing RemProektMD wallpaper-vs-paint prep-cost
   mechanism finding.
6. **⚠️ New-build settlement-crack risk, explicit timing rule**: painting
   walls in a building less than **3 years** past handover carries a real
   risk of the paint cracking as the building settles — framed as "at
   your own risk" if done anyway.
7. No-texture/"office" coldness cited as a taste-based downside some
   people feel about painted walls (explicitly flagged as subjective).

## Walls / Ceilings — Wallpaper Pros / Cons (durable facts)

8. **Affordability, both material and installation** — wide price range
   in the material itself, and installation is cheaper because the wall
   only needs plastering + a putty pass, not the near-perfect leveling
   paint requires.
9. **Imperfection-masking**: wallpaper hides minor wall imperfections
   that would be fatal for a paint finish.
10. **⚠️ Building-settlement resilience mechanism, explained**: wallpaper
    doesn't sit in full rigid contact with the ceiling, so it isn't under
    downward structural pressure the way a paint film effectively is;
    even if the plaster underneath cracks from settlement, the crack
    doesn't show visually through the wallpaper surface.
11. **Durability: 5-7 years typical**, up to ~20 years with good care —
    genuinely shorter-lived than paint's 15-25-year figure.
12. **Non-repairable damage**: a scratch (e.g. from a cat) or marker
    damage (e.g. from a child) can't be spot-patched invisibly — the
    entire damaged section/panel must be replaced.
13. **⚠️ Removal-difficulty risk after ~10 years**: old wallpaper can bond
    tightly enough to the wall that careless removal pulls a layer of
    plaster off with it, requiring localized wall-releveling before
    rehanging new paper.
14. Visible seams are unavoidable regardless of installation quality —
    a hard limit for anyone who wants a perfectly seamless wall.
15. **Shade-precision limitation**: wallpaper offers huge choice in
    color/pattern/texture but not the fine-grained custom shade-tuning
    paint offers — a specific desired shade may simply not exist as a
    wallpaper SKU.

## Walls / Ceilings — Paintable Wallpaper Hybrid (durable facts, new topic)

16. **⚠️ Paintable wallpaper ("обои под покраску") as an explicit hybrid,
    with a stated repaint-cycle durability figure**: same base material
    as ordinary wallpaper but colorless/white, then painted any color —
    combines wallpaper's imperfection-masking and building-settlement
    resilience with paint's shade freedom. Positioned as a mid-price
    "golden middle" (costs more than plain wallpaper since both
    wallpaper and paint must be bought, but doesn't require paint-grade
    wall leveling). **Restorable**: a damaged section can be touched
    up/repainted rather than replaced outright; a quality product can
    reportedly withstand roughly **5-15 repaint cycles** over its life
    (ASR renders the number ambiguously — likely "пятнадцати," 15 — flag
    `ASR-uncertain` on the exact upper figure, though the general "5 to
    ~15" range is clearly stated).
    - **Cons carried over from base wallpaper**: same ~5-7-year fresh
      lifespan and the same ~10-year removal-difficulty risk as ordinary
      wallpaper.
    - **⚠️ Cheap-tier quality risk specific to this hybrid**: budget
      paintable-wallpaper texture can look poor once painted — overly
      raised relief specifically reads as cheap, and a more pronounced
      texture is more prone to pet-scratch and child-picking damage than
      a flatter surface.

## Summary Framing (as stated, not this project's own recommendation)

The source's own closing framing: choose **paint** for maximum durability,
a perfectly smooth/uniform surface, and unlimited shade choice, accepting
higher cost and the new-build crack caution; choose **classic wallpaper**
for budget/simplicity, accepting shorter lifespan and shade limitations;
choose **paintable wallpaper** as a middle ground offering color-change
flexibility, imperfection-masking, and the coziness of texture.
