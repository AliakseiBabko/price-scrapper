# Source Note - Бастион-С: ⚠️⚠️ the entrance door has a THREE-WEEK lead time, and the renovation schedule is what actually decides which door you get (YouTube VKrvugwjnVI)

## Evidence levels

⚠️ **Same channel and presenter as the 26-minute [[_Sources/YT_nPSB41PtdMA_bastion_ten_criteria_how_to_check_a_door|construction walkthrough]], 25 months later. Mostly restates that video's spec argument in compressed form; kept for ONE finding it does not contain, plus a notably fair passage on risk.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE FINDING — lead time, and how a good door gets lost to the schedule

> **The failure he describes: the tilers reach the threshold and announce they need the entrance door. The owner goes shopping and discovers that a proper door takes *«недель от трёх, а то и больше»*, but he needs one in about three days. So he takes what is in stock — *«какие-то штампованные вещи стандартных размеров, которые стоят у кого-то на складе»* — while being told it is burglar-resistant and quiet.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS A SEQUENCING CONSTRAINT, NOT A SHOPPING TIP, and it is the most actionable item in this batch for this project.** **Every spec argument in the other three sources is moot if the door is ordered three days before it is needed, because at that point the choice set is whatever is on a warehouse shelf in a standard size.**
>
> **→ The consequence for the plan: the entrance door must be ordered roughly THREE TO FOUR WEEKS before the floor and threshold work reaches it — which is far earlier than it feels necessary, since the door is one of the last things installed.** ⚠️ **The "three weeks or more" figure is one manufacturer's, for their own custom production; a stock-size door is obviously faster and a different premium maker may differ. The structure of the trap is the transferable part, not the number.**
>
> ⚠️ **Self-interested in an obvious way — a custom manufacturer benefits from you not buying off the shelf. The mechanism is nonetheless real and checkable simply by asking two suppliers for their lead times.**

## ⚠️ 2. Buying the door and installing it yourself splits the warranty

> **If you buy the door and use your own fitter, a later problem becomes a dispute: the fitter blames the door, the seller blames the fitting, *«и на чьи плечи, как вы думаете, всё это ложится?»*** He says it sometimes ends with the owner buying a second door because the first cannot be used.

> **→ His recommendation is to take door, installation and servicing from one supplier so a single party owns the outcome.** ⚠️⚠️ **Directly self-interested — he sells installation. But the failure mode is a real property of split responsibility, and it is worth weighing against the vault's existing [[_Sources/YT_TKTKBmTkebg_sidorik_entrance_door_ep10|Sidorik]] material, which documents a competent independent installation in detail.** **The two are not in conflict: Sidorik shows that independent fitting can be done well; this says that when it goes wrong, nobody is obliged to fix it.**

## ⚠️ 3. The same look across a 10× price span — and the same causal chain as the long video

> ***«Дверь может выглядеть одинаково, но стоить при этом 30.000, 60.000, 100.000, 300.000.»***

**What differs beneath an identical appearance, per him:**

- **Metal thickness — and he repeats the triad verbatim from the long video: *«это взломостойкость раз, это геометрия конструкции два, это шумоизоляция три»*.** ⚠️ **Consistent with himself across 25 months, which is the same consistency check that [[_Sources/YT_YIJ332ibiyM_gusev_entrance_door_four_criteria|Gusev]] fails.**
- **Locks from ~3,000 to ~30,000 RUB**, differing in service life, how they are defeated, and how pleasant they are to use.
- **Paint: *«одна скалывается и желтеет, а другая нет»*, and up close a budget finish shows debris and orange-peel texture.** ⚠️ **Corroborates Novakey's error 3 — that finish does not indicate quality — from the opposite direction: here the finish DOES differ, but only on inspection at close range.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. A seller of expensive doors conceding the probability is small

**On whether people are actually at risk, he is more even-handed than either side of the vault's existing dispute:**

> ***«Понятно, что сейчас мы живём не в девяносто третьем году, когда таких краж квартирных было просто на каждом шагу. Сейчас есть система "умный город", то есть это всё уменьшилось, но тем не менее она есть. Вот вопрос: маленькая вероятность — насколько цена этой вероятности для вас дорога?»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ WORTH RECORDING PRECISELY BECAUSE OF WHO SAYS IT.** [[_Sources/YT_Mfzce0Qm4HM_prodveri_entrance_door_myth|Gusev]] cites falling burglary statistics to conclude *therefore do not spend*. **Бастион accepts the same premise — the risk has fallen and is small — and concludes the buyer must price it themselves.** **He does not dispute the statistic in order to sell against it.**
>
> **→ For the Entrance Doors page, this reframes the open question usefully: the two retailers do not actually disagree about the burglary rate. They disagree about what a small probability of a severe outcome is worth, which is the owner's judgement and not a factual dispute at all.**
>
> He also notes **developer-fitted doors exist only to close the building envelope and match the render**, and that people typically act after a neighbour is burgled.

## 5. Street doors — context only, not applicable to this flat

- **⚠️ A street door sees roughly a 100 °C annual swing** — −30 °C in winter against up to +60 °C on a sunny face — **and he says it deforms after a single season**, producing the familiar shoulder-shove and lift-the-handle symptoms.
- **A genuine thermal break (терморазрыв) is therefore essential**: the outer and inner metal must be separated by a durable poor conductor, in their case **a polyamide insert, "something between plastic and rubber"**, which also stops the door condensing.

> ⚠️ **This project is an apartment, so none of §5 applies directly.** **Recorded because the one-season deformation figure is the clearest illustration in the batch of the geometry argument that underlies everything else — and because it corroborates the Termo thermal-break tier already on the Entrance Doors page from Novakey.**
