## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE FINDING NOBODY ELSE HAS — mattress-depth brackets trade against storage volume

His bed has height-adjustment brackets that set how deep the mattress sits inside the frame.

> **Paraphrase (the captions garble this passage): the lower you drop the mattress into the frame, the LESS free volume remains in the storage box beneath — but the closer the mattress top sits to the level of the bed's own sides.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ This is a genuine, specific, non-obvious coupling, and it is the first time the vault has it.** Two things people choose independently — *how much can I store under the bed* and *do I want the mattress flush with the frame or proud of it* — turn out to be **the same adjustment**. He notes mattresses come in different heights and tastes differ (*«кто-то любит, чтобы матрас был прямо в уровень с кроватью, кто-то любит повыше»*), and that his own choice took getting used to.
>
> **Practical consequence: this is a decision to make with the actual mattress in hand, not at order time**, because it depends on the mattress height you end up buying — which the vault's own mattress pages show is the *last* thing settled.

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. The lift mechanism — his argument, and what to inspect

**The argument:** *«вы получаете ещё один шкаф»* — he compares the opened volume directly against the wardrobe standing in the same room, on camera. **Without the mechanism that volume is simply dead: *«просто здесь у вас это будет место, которое впустую никак не будет использоваться»*.**

**⚠️⚠️ What to inspect, and this is the part a catalogue will not tell you:**

- **The gauge of the metal in the lifting frame.** His test is stated as a floor, not an ideal: *«чтобы металл был хотя бы как здесь — не была фольга, не был какой-то алюминий»*. **Because the load concentrates there**, and thin stock *«довольно всё быстро это разболтает и начнёт разваливаться»*.
- **The hinges and fittings likewise** — anything *«на соплях»* and *«ваша кровать очень быстро начнёт скрипеть и разваливаться»*.

> **→ Corroborates the vault's existing ARMOS inspection rule (ask to see the internal construction; bolts and metal fittings) and extends it with the specific place to look on a lift bed.** ⚠️ **It also matches ARMOS's flag that soft damping elements matter *especially* on a lift-mechanism bed — same component, two sources, two different failure modes named (knocking vs. squeaking loose).**

## 4. Sizing — and the cost rider the other bed sources omit

- **160–180 wide × 200 long** given as the adult standard; his own is **160 × 200**, and he gives the bodies it serves (*«рост метр 74»*, partner ~164–165 cm) — **a data point with its occupants attached, which is rare.**
- *«Чем больше, тем лучше»*, if the room allows.
- **⚠️ The rider: a bigger bed raises the price TWICE — the frame and then the mattress.** *«Матрас тоже у вас будет дороже, чем он будет больше.»* **→ Same double-payment structure [[_Sources/YT_f7AXve8AyJo_svitmatraciv_bed_types|Світ Матраців]] identifies for NON-STANDARD sizes, here applied to merely LARGE ones. Two sources, two routes, one conclusion: the mattress is a hidden multiplier on every bed dimension decision.**
