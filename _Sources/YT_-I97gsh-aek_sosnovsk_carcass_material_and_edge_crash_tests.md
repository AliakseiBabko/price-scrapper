# Source Note - Сосновская фабрика: ⚠️⚠️ WHAT ACTUALLY SURVIVES 24 HOURS UNDER WATER — Two Destructive Test Series (YouTube -I97gsh-aek + iKWdPE1fRY8)

(1) transcript text - (2) YouTube metadata - (3) contextual inference - (4) **on-camera physical testing by the source** — the highest evidence level available in this source class, and still single-specimen, uncontrolled and self-scored.

## Why this source matters

**This vault's standing complaint about kitchen sources is that claims are asserted, not tested.** The CAPPUCCINO round was singled out for running four physical tests. **This channel runs destructive testing as a format**, and these two videos test the exact question the carcass source argues about: **what actually holds up, and is the board or the edge the binding constraint?**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE HEADLINE RESULT: ЛДСП FAILS ON WATER WITH EVERY EDGE TECHNOLOGY

**Six specimens, 24 hours in warm water** (`-I97gsh-aek`, 2022-06-14):

| Specimen | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **ЛДСП, laser/Airtec edge** | ⚠️ **Destroyed within ~30 minutes of immersion** |
| **ЛДСП, classic EVA edge** (Felder F600) | ⚠️ **Destroyed within ~30 minutes** |
| **ЛДСП, PUR edge** (Homag 500) | ⚠️⚠️ **Also failed by 24 h** |
| **МДФ with facade-grade decorative coating, PUR** (Homag 500 Profline) | ✅ *«Идеально выдерживает влагу. 24 часа под водой — вся кромочка на месте, и сзади и спереди»* |
| **МДФ, PUR, different machine** | ✅ Perfect, no swelling |
| **Фанера 18 mm birch, HPL both faces, lacquered edge** | ✅ *«Конечно, ничего не случилось»* — some edge absorption, dried out perfectly |

> ***«ЛДСП — PUR, ЭВА, лазер — не держит влагу однозначно. Разбухает ЛЮБАЯ технология. Пропускается влага через саму ЛАМИНАЦИЮ по торцу.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE WATER GETS IN THROUGH THE LAMINATION AT THE EDGE, NOT PAST THE EDGE BANDING. That is a different failure path from the one every other source in this vault describes**, and it means **the edge technology cannot fix ЛДСП's water behaviour** — which is precisely what the same presenter claims four years later (see §4).

### ⚠️⚠️ And the result that inverts the obvious ranking

**Laser/Airtec produces the most beautiful edge — a monolithic part with no glue seam at all.** In the water test:

> ***«Лазер разбух даже БОЛЬШЕ, чем классическая ЭВА технология. То есть эстетики здесь на высоте, но эксплуатационные характеристики практически вообще отсутствуют.»***

**Confirmed independently in the second video's 3-hour immersion** (`iKWdPE1fRY8`): EVA lifted ~0.5 mm; **laser swelled MORE than EVA**; **PUR showed no swelling at all.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE SEAMLESS EDGE IS AN AESTHETIC PRODUCT, NOT A PROTECTIVE ONE. Zero visible seam does not mean zero water path** — and it is the option a showroom will push, because it is the one that looks most expensive.

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. FASTENER RETENTION — the result that should change a specification

He drives a cross-plate into each material and tears it out on camera:

| Material | Result | His score |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| **ЛДСП** | *«Очень нестабильный материал, очень сильно крошится»* — pulls out easily | **0 / 5** |
| **МДФ** | *«Однозначно более плотный материал, но такая же история — тоже крошится, легко уходит крепёж»* | **1 / 5** |
| **Фанера** | *«Я его выдрал и даже чуть погнул крестообразную планку, но материал не крошится»* | **5 / 5** |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS THE MECHANISM BEHIND A FAILURE THE VAULT ALREADY RECORDS.** [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Cabinet_Assembly_Technique|CAPPUCCINO's argument against 16 mm board]] was that **a hinge tears out** in a room whose doors move every day. **Here that failure is reproduced on camera — and it happens to 18 mm ЛДСП too.** **→ The thickness argument was directionally right and materially incomplete: fastener retention is a property of the BOARD TYPE, and ЛДСП scores zero on it whatever its thickness.**
>
> **→ AND IT EXPLAINS WHY THE CARCASS SOURCE'S THIRD-HINGE RULE EXISTS** — *«когда петля вывалится из ЛДСП, оно там всё разрушится»*. **Same presenter, same material, and the test shows why re-fitting is hopeless rather than merely hard.**

## ⚠️ 3. Impact and heat — where ЛДСП is fine, and where it is not

- **Impact** (steel balls of increasing diameter dropped from 1 m): **all five materials passed on the face with no dents.** On the **edge**: plywood took it easily; MDF and ЛДСП showed *«микро-вмятинки на ABS кромке, незначительное повреждение»*. ⚠️ **A 1 mm edge protects the corner well** — *«кромочка-единичка отлично защищает»*.
- **Heat** (heat gun at 300 °C+, *«очень актуально на кухне, особенно у кого газовая панель»*): **ЛДСП 0 — the glue between edge and panel melts immediately**; EVA 0; **PUR 4** (*«поплавилась кромка, но PUR клей лучше выдерживает температуру»*); MDF 4; **plywood 5**, which has no banded edge at all, only HPL, and *«температуры практически не боится»*.
- **Heat, measured in the edge series**: **EVA is rated 70 °C and detached at 250 °C; PUR is rated 150 °C and held at 250 °C**; the laser specimen held at 250 °C too — **the acrylic surface began melting before the edge failed.** PUR is also given **frost resistance to −40 °C**.

## ⚠️ 4. THE THREE EDGE TECHNOLOGIES, and the honest reality check

| | **ЭВА / PVA** | **PUR** | **Laser Line / Airtec** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **How** | Granules melted, roller-applied | One-component polyurethane, heated, roller or nozzle | **No glue.** A two-layer edge — functional lower, decorative upper — fused by **laser** or **hot air** |
| **Seam** | Visible «строчка» | *«Практически незаметный»*, called «нулевой шов» | **None — a monolithic part** |
| **Cure** | Seconds | ⚠️ **Five days**, then turns to plastic | n/a |
| **Heat** | 70 °C | **150 °C** | held to 250 °C |
| **Water (tested)** | lifts ~0.5 mm at 3 h | ✅ **no swelling** | ⚠️ **worse than EVA** |
| **Peel adhesion (tested)** | comes off easily | ✅ **much the strongest** | ⚠️ *«кромка отломалась, несложно сдирается»* |

**Why PUR gives no visible seam, in his terms**: viscosity at 120 °C is far lower than EVA's — *«как шоколад и мёд»*. ⚠️ **The two numbers he gives are garbled in the ASR and the unit is spoken wrongly ("мегапаскалей" for a viscosity); the RATIO is the recoverable part and the absolute figures were dropped.**

> **⚠️⚠️ AND THE CAVEAT HE VOLUNTEERS AGAINST HIS OWN PITCH, WHICH IS THE MOST USEFUL LINE IN EITHER VIDEO:**
>
> *«Если у вас в любом случае зальют соседи, и у вас полностью корпус на PUR клею, и фасады тоже — **всё равно [плохо] на кухне, потому что СТЫКИ не обрабатываются.** Исключением является полностью клеёный корпус, да и боковины тоже клеёные.»*
>
> **→ A FLOOD DEFEATS EVERY EDGE TECHNOLOGY, BECAUSE THE PANEL-TO-PANEL JOINTS ARE NOT SEALED BY ANY OF THEM.** **That is a limit on the whole argument, stated by the person selling the upgrade.**

**⚠️ And the market reality he states plainly:** *«90% кухонь в данный момент всё равно будут кромиться на ЭВА клей, потому что основные станки не оснащены этими PUR узлами… это очень затратно.»* **→ PUR is a question to ask, not an assumption to make.**

**His stated gold standard: carcass edged with PUR, fronts laser or PUR.**
