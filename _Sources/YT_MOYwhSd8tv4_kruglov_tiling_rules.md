## Rules / Heuristics — Genuinely New Content

- **Substrate flatness tolerance**: laser + 2m straightedge check, max 2mm deviation over 2m; primer dry time 4-8 hours before tiling.
- **Tile-adhesive classification (C1/C2)**: C1 = cheapest/basic, tile <60cm long side, dry rooms only, not for mosaic, this source's company doesn't use it. C2 = practical minimum for any real project, rated for porcelain/heavier tile. C2-reinforced/armored for large-format tile. Separate certifications layered on top: heated-floor-rated, mosaic-rated (more elastic), frost-/water-resistant (mandatory outdoors/unheated loggia). Most common tile-detachment cause: cheapest adhesive under heavy large-format tile.
- **Adhesive application technique + size thresholds**: wall tile >30×30cm and floor tile >60×60cm both need adhesive combed onto the substrate AND buttered onto the tile back (double-sided). Two named mistakes: old dot/dab application (obsolete), and too-thick adhesive layer (shrinks on cure, raises detachment risk).
- **Tile joint sizing with mechanism**: inter-tile joint 1.5-2mm (accommodates differential substrate/tile movement, not just installation convenience); perimeter expansion gap 5-10mm, larger than the inter-tile joint — skipping it risks the whole tiled plane buckling.
- **Temperature rules**: indoor tiling fine above ~10°C; outdoor/unheated-loggia tiling needs >5°C plus frost-/water-resistant adhesive.
- **⚠️ Heated-floor cure rule**: never activate a heated floor until tile adhesive has cured a full 28 calendar days — early activation to "speed up drying" is named as the most common mistake in this category and deforms the adhesive, detaching the tile.
- **Grout material pricing (2025-08-08 publish date, trailing-6-month USD/RUB 82.56)**: cement grout 350 RUB/2kg (under $10 — RUB figure kept as-is rather than forced into a $10-rounding bucket that would zero it out); epoxy (two-component) grout ≈4,500 RUB/2.5kg (≈$50).
- **Grout labor pricing, same date**: cement 200 RUB/running meter (under $10); epoxy from 600 RUB/running meter (under $10) and up, no stated ceiling.
- **Epoxy grout repairability — a stated change from older practice**: previously considered effectively non-repairable; a heat gun can now soften cured epoxy enough for minor repair work. Real caveat: some epoxy grout is a neutral base plus added colorant, so a repair batch may not color-match the original exactly — unlike cement grout, which can simply be dug out and re-applied with no such risk.
- **Restated, more concrete epoxy-vs-cement placement rule**: epoxy in every constant-water-contact zone (tiled shower pan/podium) and at every external tile corner (cement bonds poorly there); cement elsewhere if budget-constrained — independently corroborates this page's existing external-corner-epoxy rule with a stated mechanism (poor cement bond area at a mitered corner).
