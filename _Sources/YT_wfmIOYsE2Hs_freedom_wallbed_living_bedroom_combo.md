## Design Concept / Furniture — Durable Facts & Rules

- **⚠️ Real placement-decision reasoning, directly relevant to variable
  daily schedules**: the maker considered placing the wall-bed opposite
  the TV mid-room with storage cabinets on the far wall, but rejected it
  because the client works shift work and needs the rest of the room
  usable by someone else even while the bed is deployed — the bed was
  placed in the far corner away from the door instead, specifically so
  the room stays accessible/usable independent of the bed's open/closed
  state. A left-in walkway clearance beside the window lets the bed be
  approached/exited from both sides.
- **⚠️ No-strap mattress-retention design — a real disagreement with
  `YT_9jDv0Iibbfw`'s mechanism**: this maker's own design holds the
  mattress via a support/bracket integrated into the frame itself, with
  no retention straps at all for either the mattress or the bedding.
  States they specifically advise AGAINST straps: a strap can come
  undone over time, letting pillows/blankets fall down and jam between
  the mattress and the side panel — a real documented failure mode of
  the strap approach. Notes their design *could* still accommodate straps
  if a client insisted, but doesn't recommend it. **This directly
  contradicts** `YT_9jDv0Iibbfw`'s hardware kit, which includes three
  sets of retention straps as a standard, positive-framed feature — a
  genuine cross-source manufacturer disagreement on retention-strap use,
  unresolved either way.
- **Closed-position clearance and incidental surface**: 10cm of space
  remains between the wall and the mattress when folded closed — usable
  as a small shelf-equivalent surface (phone, book) while the bed is
  closed.
- **Handle-less door design**: doors open via routed hand-recesses built
  into the panel edge rather than visible handles, specifically to keep
  the furniture visually unobtrusive.
- **Color-matching for visual disappearance**: furniture color
  deliberately matched to the wallpaper or laminate flooring so the
  whole wall-bed/wardrobe unit visually "dissolves" into the room rather
  than drawing attention — this project's build is color-matched to the
  wallpaper.
- **Full custom, floor-to-ceiling, wall-to-wall build**: all this maker's
  projects are individually custom-designed and built to the ceiling and
  walls for a seamless fit, rather than a modular/off-the-shelf unit.
- **⚠️ Adjustable/leveling legs, a practical floor-unevenness
  accommodation**: the bed unit sits on adjustable legs specifically to
  compensate for floor unevenness — flagged as common even after a
  recent renovation, and observed in this specific job.
- **Overhead mezzanine storage**: two large storage niches
  ("антресоли") built above the wall-bed cabinet, exploiting otherwise-
  wasted vertical space for bedding or general storage.
- **⚠️ Concrete durability figure — gas-lift cycle rating**: imported
  gas-lift struts rated for up to **30,000 open/close cycles**. At
  roughly twice-daily use (one open + one close cycle per day), 30,000

  figure for this household's own multi-year activation timeline
