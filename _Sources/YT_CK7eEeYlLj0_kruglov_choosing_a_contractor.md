## Planning Rules — A Named Cross-Tier Sampling Methodology

- **⚠️ A concrete, previously-undocumented benchmarking technique**:
  deliberately solicit quotes from **one company in the high-price
  tier, two in the mid-price tier, two in the low-price tier, and one
  from either the friend-referral or "pig in a poke" category** — six
  total data points — before committing to any one contractor. The
  stated payoff is a genuinely accurate composite picture of: real
  labor cost range, average rough-materials/consumables cost, and a
  realistic project timeline.
- **A real secondary-benefit framing for running multiple quotes in
  parallel**, not previously recorded this explicitly in this store: in
  the process of collecting six competing quotes, a client incidentally
  learns the specific engineering-routing approach each company takes
  for electrical/heating/water-supply, sees several different smeta
  *formats* side by side (letting them judge which format they
  personally find clearest), picks up professional terminology fluency,
  and — as a stated side effect — frequently ends up with referred
  designer contacts as well, even without actively seeking one out.
