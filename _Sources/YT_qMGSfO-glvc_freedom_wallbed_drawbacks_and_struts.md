⚠️ **Same channel as [[_Sources/YT_wfmIOYsE2Hs_freedom_wallbed_living_bedroom_combo|wfmIOYsE2Hs]], already in this vault from the 2026-08-25 wall-bed batch.** **A maker whose video is organised around his own product's drawbacks, and who ends by asking viewers to name any he missed — the direction that lends the drawbacks weight.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE GAS-STRUT FINDING — a named acceptance criterion the vault did not have

> **⚠️⚠️ Even the mechanism manufacturer recommends buying SEVERAL force ratings, because only by trial can you establish which one a given bed needs.**

**His worked case:** they fitted the maximum strut, **2200 N**, and the bed — when lowered — **began to lift by itself.** They swapped to **2000 N**, and the bed now has a proper neutral point:

> **⚠️⚠️ THE TEST: a correctly matched wall bed has a "zero point". Below it, pushing from underneath and the bed continues opening on its own; at the zero point it descends gently and you simply steady it. It must NOT rise by itself when down.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|The mechanisms page]] already records gas-strut force-rating selection as a risk "corroborated twice". This is a THIRD arrival — and it is the first to give a CHECKABLE PASS/FAIL you can apply at handover rather than a warning to be careful.**
>
> **⚠️⚠️ AND A FOURTH ARRIVAL, from the DIY side, in [[_Sources/YT_ztubNCfTyhg_kachanova_seven_transformer_households|Качанова's]] batch-mate: a self-built wall bed whose owners over-specified the struts and had to WEIGH THE BED DOWN WITH DUMBBELLS, because otherwise it would not stay down even with a person on it.** **Same failure, opposite budget, and together they establish that the rating must be MATCHED, never maximised.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. The drawbacks he names — and the first is genuinely new

1. **⚠️⚠️ A WALL BED WORKS LIKE A LARGE VACUUM CLEANER.** *«Когда мы открываем кровать, снизу большим потоком воздуха в шкаф затягивается пыль и мусор, который находится на полу.»* **The fold action pulls floor dust and debris up into the cabinet.**
2. **⚠️ Consequently, cleaning inside needs TWO PEOPLE** — one holding the bed up, one clearing the debris off the bottom shelf.
3. **The safety fear — that it may open or fall by itself.** **His position: correctly specified struts, correct fixing, correct construction, and it cannot; never once in his practice, though he has heard of cases.** **⚠️ He then names the first cause of such cases himself: wrong gas-strut selection (§1).**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ POINT 2 IS INDEPENDENTLY CONFIRMED BY AN OWNER, and reframed.** [[_Sources/YT_ztubNCfTyhg_kachanova_seven_transformer_households|Вести с полей]], a year in, describes exactly this — her son or husband holds the bed while she wipes underneath — **but treats it as a net GAIN over a conventional wardrobe: *«ни один шкаф не отодвинете, чтобы протереть пыль — просто сил не хватит; а тут всё пожалуйста»*.** **Both are right: the two-person requirement is real, and the alternative is a cupboard nobody ever cleans behind.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Fixing — six points, and what to do when the wall is not concrete

- **The cabinet is fixed at SIX points: four angle brackets at the top, two concealed at the bottom, on long anchors.** *«Огромный запас прочности, кровать не расшатать.»*
- **⚠️⚠️ If the wall is gypsolite, warmite, sibit or similar aerated block and anchors will not hold, they use CHEMICAL ANCHORS — *«с ними проблем никогда не было, они держат в любой поверхности, даже в полом кирпиче»*.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ DIRECTLY A PHASE-1 BUILD CONSTRAINT FOR THIS PROJECT, whose plan installs the unit and its supporting cavity during the current renovation.** **The wall it fixes to has to be decided, and specified to take six anchor points, at rough stage — see [[00_Master/Bedroom_Design_Principles|Bedroom Design Principles]]' Household Plan.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. The base is PLYWOOD, not metal — and the reason is the mechanism

> **They use a glued-plywood orthopaedic base and explicitly NOT a metal one, *«потому что они тяжёлые, и при тяжёлых [основаниях] идёт большая нагрузка на газовые лифты, и срок службы их уменьшается очень быстро»*.**

**He adds a second reason: the sprung base evens out the mattress's firmness and takes load off it — if someone drops onto the bed, the base absorbs alongside the mattress, extending the mattress's life.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A CONSTRAINT THIS VAULT'S BED-BASE MATERIAL DOES NOT HAVE.** [[14_Furniture/analysis/Bed_Frames_And_Bases|Bed Frames and Bases]] discusses lamellae, the 7–8 cm gap rule and curved lamellae for a STATIC bed. **On a wall bed the base's WEIGHT becomes a design driver, because it is permanently loading a gas strut. A heavy "better" base shortens the mechanism's life.**

## ⚠️ 5. Mattress and bedding retention — the SAME maker restating its own position

⚠️⚠️ **CORRECTION ON WEIGHT: [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|the mechanisms page]] already carries the "against straps" position — including the identical jamming failure mode — sourced to [[_Sources/YT_wfmIOYsE2Hs_freedom_wallbed_living_bedroom_combo|wfmIOYsE2Hs]], which is THIS SAME CHANNEL.** **So this video corroborates FREE_DOM with itself and does NOT add an independent voice to that disagreement. Recorded as a restatement, not as a third source.**

- **They use NO straps at all.** The support that holds the mattress upright becomes the bed's LEG when lowered, so the mattress simply cannot slide out. **You make the bed normally and tuck the sheet between the side and the mattress.**
- **⚠️⚠️ THE FAILURE CASE: strapped pillows and duvets have come loose, fallen, and JAMMED between the mattress and the wall — and freeing them then needs a second person to hold the bed.** **So they recommend a separate drawer for pillows and duvet instead.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ WHAT THIS BATCH DOES GENUINELY ADD to the open question is two things, neither of them this video.** **First, [[_Sources/YT_u_gIgDqRdYw_progressdesign_wallbed_buyer_walkthrough|Progress Design]] is a NEW independent maker on the FOR side — two straps holding mattress, pillows and duvet together, so no separate drawer or wardrobe space is needed at all.** **Second, a MIDDLE PATH neither manufacturer mentions: ELASTIC retention.** **Качанова's households use elastics on the mattress, and one DIY household copied the elastic bands from TRAIN-COMPARTMENT bunks to hold the bedding, so nothing is stowed at all.** **The question is still open, but the option space is now four-way — straps / no straps / elastics on the mattress / elastics on the bedding — and the elastic variants address exactly the jamming failure he names.**

## ⚠️ 6. Layout — why he puts it on the SHORT wall

**Usual practice is the LONG wall with a sofa beside it. He dislikes that: the sofa ends up too close to the TV, and it makes the room narrower.** **On the SHORT side the room reads more square, and on entering you notice the sofa rather than the bed** — with the ЛДСП colour matched to the wallpaper so the unit reads as one continuous wall.

**⚠️ Joinery notes:** handleless, with cut-outs — **plus an extra cut-out in the side panel for anyone who cannot reach the antresol.** Full-extension concealed soft-close runners. **⚠️ Pull-out hanging rails he dislikes but was forced into here, because the wall-bed depth could not change and the door frame had already been shifted to fit it.** **⚠️ In the middle section, rails and no shelves, so long garments do not bulge and stop the door closing.**

**⚠️ Ease of operation:** *«С этим даже справится хрупкая женщина»*, and he says elderly clients manage easily. ⚠️⚠️ **An owner in this batch qualifies that — see [[_Sources/YT_ztubNCfTyhg_kachanova_seven_transformer_households|Вести с полей]], who manages but uses a squat and normally leaves it to her husband. The difference is consistent with §1: perceived effort is a function of how well the strut is matched.**

**⚠️ Mattress care, corroborating the vault:** rotate every 3 months, alternating head-to-foot and top-to-bottom, so the mattress completes a full cycle of turns over a year.
