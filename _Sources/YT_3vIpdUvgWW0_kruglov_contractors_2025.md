## Rules and heuristics

- A full-service company may take longer than a private generalist crew because work proceeds through staged inspection, defect correction, acceptance, and payment before the next specialist stage begins.
- A company with contractual responsibility is less likely to accept a requested violation of building rules, technical cards, or electrical/plumbing requirements; this is a delivery-model claim from the source, not independent legal advice.
- A complete design project makes trade sequencing, material logistics, and stage timing more predictable; the source frames it as useful to the contractor as well as an additional client expense.
- Avoid an opaque “whole renovation for X RUB/m²” quote when it does not expose stage scope, acceptance triggers, and payment milestones. Require a detailed estimate of hidden and visible work instead.
- Contractor-supplied or contractor-recommended materials create a warranty/accountability relationship in the company's stated model; this is commercially framed and should not be generalized to every contract.
