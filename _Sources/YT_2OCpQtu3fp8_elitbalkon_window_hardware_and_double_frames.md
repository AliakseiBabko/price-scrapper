## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE REFRAME — people ask the wrong question about hardware

**People write to him naming two brands and asking which is better. He declines to answer that and substitutes three criteria.**

### ⚠️⚠️ Criterion 1 — is the brand still SUPPORTED where you live

> **The better-known the brand the better, and better still if it is present in your city or country — because then it can be repaired. A lesser-known brand may vanish from the market: no updates, no stock, and then the hardware cannot be repaired at all.**

**What happens instead, in his trade's own words, is that parts get "married" (женить) to each other** — a broken corner switch or hinge replaced with something from another system. He reports this being asked constantly in professional chats, **and the professionals' own answer being to scrap the old hardware entirely and fit something current that is actually available.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE FINDING: window hardware is a SPARE-PARTS-AVAILABILITY decision, not a performance decision.** **That is a genuinely different frame from the vault's existing "buy reputable hardware" and from [[_Sources/YT_M8ho5TC86eQ_okna20_choosing_pvc_windows|Окна 2.0's]] "reputable beats cheap Turkish analogues" — both of which argue on quality. This argues on a 15-year horizon: the hardware WILL need service, and the question is whether parts will exist.**

### ⚠️⚠️ Criterion 2 — go and operate it, and treat inability to show it as information

> **If a window company has no office and does not have the hardware to show you, *«это уже звоночек»* — they may never have handled it themselves and are simply reselling it.**

**What to feel for:** poor hardware is *«твёрдая, чёрствая»* and works mechanically and stiffly; good hardware is *«помягче»*, closes easily and adjusts easily. **Ask what can break, and whether there is a representative in the country.**

> **→ ⚠️ Same shape as the Novakey "ask for the cutaway stand" test already in the vault's entrance-door material: a seller who cannot show you the component is itself the answer.** **Two unrelated trades, same test.**

### ⚠️⚠️ Criterion 3 — the opening ORDER, which is a specification and not a preference

**He is for tilt-and-turn, and then adds a detail the vault did not have: when the handle is turned to 90°, does the sash TILT first or SWING first? He specifies tilt first.** His reasons:

- **Ventilation is the frequent action, so make it the first one.**
- **⚠️ It prevents condensate** — which ties directly to [[_Sources/YT_Nr5_Pma2Tvg_elitbalkon_ventilation_and_condensation|his own ice-on-windows material]], where opening the sash is remedy number one.
- **⚠️⚠️ The sash does not sag** *(«не провисает»)*, because tilting does not hang its weight on the side hinges.
- **⚠️ Child safety**: a child who has tilted a sash will not work out that it must first be pushed back before the handle can be raised.
- Plus a safety lock (at the bottom, in the handle, or in the hardware) gives *«окно три в одном — проветривается, безопасно, и оно ещё долго будет служить»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE ANTI-SAG POINT INDEPENDENTLY CORROBORATES the vault's existing Zemskov finding** that a heavy sash operated by full swing bears its own weight on the side hinges and deforms — Zemskov's worked example being a ~2×2 m sash deforming within about a week. **Zemskov's remedy is to RESTRICT a large sash to tilt-only; this generalises it to the default handle order on any sash.** **Second independent source, same mechanism, and this one is free to specify at order time.**

**His summary: *«бренд, плавность хода, возможность обслуживания и удобство открывания»*.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. "Двойные стеклопакеты" almost always means двойные РАМЫ — a second terminology trap

> **People ask him to quote "double glazing units". On discussion it turns out they mean TWO SEPARATE FRAMES with a gap between them — you open one sash, then the other — not a standard sealed unit (two panes bonded with a spacer at 32 / 40 / 50 mm).**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ Pairs with [[_Sources/YT_M8ho5TC86eQ_okna20_choosing_pvc_windows|Окна 2.0's камера/стекло trap]] in this same batch. Two separate, very common glazing terms that customers reliably use to mean something other than what they say — and in both cases the misunderstanding changes what gets ordered.**

**When double frames are actually used:** very cold northern regions, or a genuinely noisy street. **⚠️ He calls them *«единственное решение, как помещение сделать максимально тихим»*.**

> ⚠️ **Pair that against Окна 2.0, who answers the same noise problem with a three-chamber (4-pane) unit plus acoustic glass or film.** **Not a contradiction so much as two tiers: she is specifying within one frame, he is saying that past a point only a second frame will do. Recorded together so the ladder is visible.**

**⚠️⚠️ Construction details, which are the non-obvious part:**

- **The two frames are NOT identical and NOT flush.** The **outer frame must be SMALLER and the inner LARGER**, because the outer sash has to pass through the inner frame to be opened for cleaning or ventilation. **Special extension elements reduce the outer frame.**
- From outside the arrangement is inconspicuous; from inside the inner frame reads slightly larger and is set inward.
- **⚠️⚠️ TEMPERED (закалённое) glass is required in such a unit, especially on a sunny elevation, to prevent THERMAL SHOCK (термошок) cracking the pane.** **→ The vault has nothing on thermal shock in glazing, and the mechanism — a trapped cavity between two frames heating unevenly — is specific to this construction.**
- Can be built from any window system or brand.
