# Source Note — NSDSGN, nine suppliers on the April 2022 price and supply shock

**26 minutes, a round-table assembled from separate interviews with NINE suppliers across St Petersburg, plus the host's own summary. Manual Russian caption track.**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **⚠️ MY SELECTION HYPOTHESIS FOR THIS SOURCE WAS WRONG, AND I AM RECORDING IT RATHER THAN QUIETLY REFRAMING.**
>
> I picked this expecting **absolute price figures** to sit behind the "~2×" ratio recorded in Round 5. **There are almost no absolute figures in 26 minutes.** It is a **percentages-and-supply** source: rates of change by category of origin, lead times, substitutability, and procurement method. That turns out to be *more* useful for the vault's purposes than a 2022 price list would have been — a 2022 Russian price list would be unusable today — but the prediction was still wrong, and the pattern to note is that I inferred content from a title containing «Цены».

## ⚠️ Currency handling — why no USD equivalents appear in this note

- **Almost every figure here is a PERCENTAGE CHANGE, which needs no conversion.**
- **⚠️ For the few roubles-denominated items, a USD equivalent is DELIBERATELY OMITTED, and the reason must be recorded: a trailing six-month USD/RUB mean before 2022-04-06 STRADDLES the February–March 2022 currency shock, so it averages two incompatible regimes and produces a number that describes neither.** The suppliers themselves refer repeatedly to the rate having spiked and then partly returned. **Converting these figures would manufacture false comparability, which is exactly what the vault's price-comparability rule exists to prevent.**
- → **Route every figure in this source as a rouble percentage with the date attached, and treat it as HISTORICAL MARKET EVIDENCE rather than as pricing input.**

| Category / supplier | Reported rise since February 2022 |
| :--- | :--- |

| Goods made in **Russia or Belarus** | **~+20%** |

| **Kitchen furniture overall** (≈half Russian content) | **+20–25%**, then stalled |

- **⚠️⚠️ THE FINDING IS NOT ANY ONE NUMBER — IT IS THAT THE RANGE RUNS FROM +20% TO +150% ACROSS THE SAME MARKET IN THE SAME WEEK.** A single headline "materials went up X%" is meaningless. **The rise tracked IMPORT SHARE AND CURRENCY EXPOSURE, category by category** — which is a durable analytical result even though the 2022 numbers are not.
- **⚠️ AND THE EXTREME CASE HAS A NAMED CAUSE: hardware rose most (+100–150%) BECAUSE IT IS THE ONE CATEGORY WITH NO DOMESTIC SUBSTITUTE.** Several suppliers say this independently. **Blum cut supplies by 60–70%.**
- **⚠️ Meanwhile Russian-made goods rose ~20% and then HELD STEADY, while imports «капитально варьировались и прыгали» with the rate.** → **Domestic content buys price *stability*, not just price level** — which is a different and more useful proposition than "buy local, it's cheaper".
- **⚠️ A Belarus data point, flagged for jurisdiction: Belarusian-made goods are grouped WITH Russian-made in the ~+20% band by the supplier who mentions them.** ⚠️ **This is a market/price observation, not a regulatory one, so it routes to the budget and procurement pages with the jurisdiction noted — NOT to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`.**

## ⚠️ Import share by category — a useful structural map

- **Kitchens: ~50–60% imported content, and it is overwhelmingly the HARDWARE** (Blum named; Austria, Czechia, Germany).
- **Facades and carcasses: ~30–40% already produced in Russia** — «их заменять не нужно».
- **Doors and parquet: always majority Russian**, and more so now; but the **pockets (пеналы) and hardware were always European or Chinese.**
- **Tile: roughly 50/50**; one tile supplier is **~90% import** («отечественных товаров… процентов 10»).
- **⚠️ DECORATIVE LIGHTING: «в нашей стране ПРАКТИЧЕСКИ НЕ РАЗВИТ»** — the starkest gap named, with China expected to fill it.
- **Textiles: «вряд ли, это сложное производство».** **Wallpaper: hard, with only ONE Russian producer** — though that producer's print and material quality is said to improve yearly.
- **⚠️ AND THE QUALITATIVE LIMIT ON SUBSTITUTION, stated by a supplier who is otherwise optimistic: «ЕВРОПА ОТЛИЧАЕТСЯ БОЛЕЕ ИНТЕРЕСНЫМИ ОТДЕЛКАМИ, КАКИМИ-ТО ЭФФЕКТАМИ, И В РОССИИ ПОКА НИКТО ПОВТОРИТЬ ИХ НЕ МОЖЕТ.»**
  - → **⚠️ The substitution story holds for the STRUCTURAL product and fails for the FINISH. Carcasses, doors, basic tile — replaceable. Surface effects, decorative finishes, decorative lighting, textiles — not.** That distinction is the single most transferable idea in the source and it is not time-limited.
- **⚠️ A substitution route with a quality caveat worth keeping: Russian mechanisms MADE TO ITALIAN DRAWINGS AND CERTIFICATES were being tested, and «некоторые механизмы показали себя с очень хорошей стороны».** → Licensed-drawing manufacture as a middle path between original and generic.

## ⚠️⚠️ Logistics, not production, is the binding constraint — the host's own summary

- **His conclusion: «ГЛАВНАЯ ПРОБЛЕМА для большинства поставщиков — это конечно же ЛОГИСТИКА… БОЛЬШИНСТВО ЕВРОПЕЙСКИХ ФАБРИК ПРОДОЛЖАЮТ ПРОИЗВОДИТЬ свою продукцию и готовы нам её предоставлять, но главный вопрос — это ТО, КАК ЕЁ ПРИВЕЗТИ.»**
- Supporting detail from the suppliers:
  - **Sea freight closed to Russia; road haulage possible but expensive** — one supplier took a deliberate pause to see how it settled.
  - **Belgium lead time 3–4 weeks → ~6 weeks.**
  - **A Portuguese underlay container simply not arriving**, while **Italy continued shipping.**
  - **10–15 factories on a stop-list out of ~1,500 partners** — «в масштабе… это вообще ничто».
  - **Some routing via Finland, in limited quantities.**
  - Several suppliers had **bought heavily in February and early March** and had 2–3 months of stock, particularly of European hardware.
- → **⚠️ The distinction between a PRODUCTION shock and a LOGISTICS shock is what makes the rest predictable: a logistics shock lengthens lead times and raises landed cost but does not remove the product, so the correct response is schedule slack, not substitution.** ⚠️ That inference is **mine**; the suppliers state the facts and the host states the diagnosis.

## ⚠️⚠️ The panic-buying lesson — with evidence, which is rare

- **The sequence one supplier describes: hardware prices jumped; «КТО-ТО БЫСТРО ПОБЕЖАЛ В ПАНИКЕ [и] ЗАКУПАТЬ УЖЕ ПО НОВЫМ ЦЕНАМ»; then prices came back down; «И ЛЮДИ УЖЕ НЕМНОЖКО РАССТРАИВАЮТСЯ, ЧТО КУПИЛИ ДОРОЖЕ.»**
- → **⚠️⚠️ BUYING IN THE PANIC LOCKED IN THE WORST PRICE OF THE WHOLE PERIOD. That is a documented outcome, not a caution, and it is the most valuable line in the source.**
- **Near-unanimity across the nine on what to do: «БЕЗ ПАНИКИ», keep to the planned schedule, «спешить покупать уже смысла нету».** One puts it as: **«в любую такую ШОКОВУЮ СИТУАЦИЮ всегда оставаться ТРЕЗВОЙ ГОЛОВОЙ и с ХОЛОДНЫМ РАСЧЁТОМ… потому что во время СПЕШКЕ можно допустить кучу ошибок в выборе — не того оборудования, не низкого качества, или просто НЕ ПОДХОДЯЩЕЕ ПОД ПРОЕКТ.»**
- **⚠️ AND THE SECOND-ORDER COST IS NAMED: panic buying produces the WRONG MATERIALS, not just expensive ones — «у вас потом будут какие-то материалы, которые вам потом будет НЕКУДА ДЕВАТЬ, они будут вам НЕ НУЖНЫ».** → The loss is specification error plus dead stock, on top of the price.
- ⚠️ **Read the unanimity with the promotional flag in view: nine suppliers with warehouses full of pre-shock stock all saying "no need to rush" is advice that also happens to suit them.** It is still the position that the subsequent price fall vindicated.

## ⚠️⚠️ The procurement techniques — the durable half of this source

### 1. The move-in-horizon test

- **«Если вы планируете ЗАСЕЛЯТЬСЯ ЧЕРЕЗ ТРИ-ЧЕТЫРЕ МЕСЯЦА, то СЕЙЧАС САМОЕ ВРЕМЯ приобретать. Если вы планируете заселяться ГОДА ЧЕРЕЗ ДВА — ТОРОПИТЬСЯ АБСОЛЮТНО НЕ ИМЕЕТ СМЫСЛА, лучше подождать, аккуратно всё просчитать, просмотреть, подобрать.»**
- → **⚠️ A clean decision rule that survives the 2022 context entirely: buy when your INSTALLATION DATE is inside the lead time, not when the price news is bad.** This is the most directly reusable thing in the source and the vault has nothing equivalent.

### 2. Price-fixing by early purchase with free storage

- **«Если есть бюджет — ЗАКУПИТЬ МАТЕРИАЛ и ОСТАВИТЬ ЕГО НА ХРАНЕНИЕ НА ФАБРИКЕ. Наши многие фабрики ХРАНЯТ МАТЕРИАЛ БЕСПЛАТНО, в этом нет никакой проблемы — то есть ЗАФИКСИРОВАТЬ ЦЕНУ и закрыть этот момент.»**
- → **⚠️ Free factory storage turns an early purchase into a price hedge without creating a site-storage problem — and site storage is normally the reason not to buy early.** A concrete, askable question at order time: *do you store it, and for how long, and at what cost?*
- ⚠️ **Pair this with the Round 6 rescue-contractor finding on custody: fixing the price by early purchase is sound, but the supply contract must be in the CLIENT'S name, or the stored material becomes leverage in a dispute.** The two findings together give the complete rule, and neither source states both halves.

### 3. ⚠️ Collapsing procurement into a single window — his studio's own policy change

- **«Мы в нашей студии решили, что сейчас есть ТОЛЬКО ОДИН СПОСОБ комплектовать проекты — это комплектовать их В ДАННЫЙ МОМЕНТ, НА ДАННУЮ СЕКУНДУ, потому что очень сложно что-то предположить на будущее… У вас есть НЕСКОЛЬКО ДНЕЙ, чтобы приобрести материал… РЕАЛЬНУЮ ЦЕНУ МОЖНО ИМЕТЬ ТОЛЬКО В МОМЕНТЕ, НА НЕСКОЛЬКО ДНЕЙ.»**
- → **⚠️⚠️ Under currency instability a specification's prices are valid for DAYS, so forward procurement becomes impossible and the whole purchase must collapse into one window at the start of the build. That is a strong methodological claim and it inverts the normal advice to procure progressively.**
- ⚠️ **And it has a consequence he does not draw: a single-window procurement requires the ENTIRE specification to be complete and the ENTIRE budget to be liquid on the same day. That is a much harder precondition than staged buying, and it is the reason the technique is a crisis measure rather than a default.** (My inference, flagged.)

## ⚠️ The design-project argument — real structure, but flag the self-interest

- **«ЦЕНЫ НА МАТЕРИАЛ ВЫРОСЛИ, [а] ЦЕНЫ НА ДИЗАЙН-ПРОЕКТ ПО-ПРЕЖНЕМУ ОСТАЮТСЯ ЕЩЁ ЯНВАРСКИМИ, и сейчас есть возможность сделать дизайн-проект ПО СТАРЫМ ЦЕНАМ — дальше будет дороже.»**
- **⚠️ THIS IS A SALES PITCH FOR HIS OWN SERVICE AND MUST BE ROUTED WITH THAT FLAG ATTACHED.**
- **⚠️ But there is a real structural point inside it: DESIGN FEES REPRICE MORE SLOWLY THAN MATERIALS.** Fees are a labour price set per project and renegotiated periodically; materials are repriced on the currency. → **This is adjacent to, and independent of, the Round 5 finding that the fee is segment-independent while the build cost scales. Together: a design fee is STICKY BOTH IN SEGMENT AND IN TIME, so its share of a project budget falls whenever material costs move.** That is worth recording, with both halves attributed and the promotional context stated.
- **⚠️ The second argument is better and less self-serving: a design project FIXES THE IDEA while procurement waits — «сейчас вы можете ЗАФИКСИРОВАТЬ СВОЮ ИДЕЮ и ПО МЕРЕ ПОСТУПЛЕНИЯ ФИНАНСОВ, по мере поступления материалов РЕАЛИЗОВЫВАТЬ ЕЁ В БУДУЩЕМ.»**
  - → **Design and procurement can be DECOUPLED in time, and under uncertainty that is an advantage: the cheap, durable part of the project (the decisions) can be completed while the expensive, volatile part (the goods) waits.** ⚠️ Connects directly to the Round 4 finding that a design project functions as an evidentiary artefact — same document, third distinct function.

## ⚠️ On whether to start a renovation at all

- **His honest refusal to answer: «НАЧИНАТЬ ИЛИ [НЕ НАЧИНАТЬ] РЕМОНТ СЕЙЧАС — здесь НЕЛЬЗЯ, просто НЕВОЗМОЖНО дать какой-то конкретный совет. Здесь КАЖДЫЙ ДОЛЖЕН РЕШАТЬ ДЛЯ СЕБЯ, выстраивать какую-то ЦЕПОЧКУ ПРИОРИТЕТОВ… поэтому здесь я СОВЕТ ВАМ НЕ МОГУ ДАТЬ.»**
- **He then gives a conditional one anyway: if the money is available now, spending it on materials preserves its value, «потому что ДЕШЕВЛЕ УЖЕ ВРЯД ЛИ ЧТО-ТО БУДЕТ», and there may still be old stock at pre-shock prices.**
- → ⚠️ **Record the refusal alongside the advice. A source that declines to give a universal answer and then gives a conditional one is behaving correctly, and the vault should preserve the shape of that rather than flattening it into a recommendation.**
