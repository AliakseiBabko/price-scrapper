# Source Note — Постановление Совмина РБ № 164 от 03.04.2026 (переустройство и перепланировка)

**The primary-source pass `16_Legal_and_Regulations/` was deliberately held open for.**
Read in full from the PDF, all 15 pages. **Level 1 by construction: this is Belarusian
law, not a practitioner's account of it.**

## ⚠️ What this document IS, because the name matters

It is a **постановление Совета Министров** — a Council-of-Ministers resolution, secondary
legislation adopted on the basis of **абзацы девятый и тринадцатый статьи 5 Жилищного
кодекса**. **It is not a presidential decree.** Secondary summaries circulating in English
call it "Decree No. 164"; that is wrong in kind, and it matters because it sits *under* the
Housing Code and can be superseded by another постановление.

**It approves two separate Положения:**

1. **«Об условиях и порядке переустройства и (или) перепланировки»** — the one that governs this project.
2. **«Об условиях и порядке установки на крышах и фасадах многоквартирных жилых домов индивидуальных антенн и иных конструкций»** — which turns out to govern **air-conditioning outdoor units** (see below).

## ⚠️⚠️ It REPEALS постановление № 384, it does not amend it

`Приложение`, item 1: **постановление Совета Министров от 16 мая 2013 г. № 384** — the
document every older Belarusian renovation guide cites — is **признано утратившим силу**,
in full. Eleven further partial repeals follow (in № 764, 180, 377, 506, 391, 537, 175,
770, 155, 619 and 266).

> **→ Any guide, article or video that cites № 384 as live law is now out of date. Treat every "по постановлению 384" claim as needing re-verification against № 164.**

## Entry into force

**п.5**: пункты 1–3 come into force **three months after official publication**; the rest
on publication. The portal stamp is **07.04.2026**.

> ⚠️ **So the two Положения and the repeals take effect in early July 2026 — 7 or 8 July depending on how the three months are counted.** Secondary sources state both dates. **The exact day is a counting question this note does not resolve**; nothing in this project turns on the difference.

## Положение 1 — переустройство и перепланировка

### ⚠️⚠️ §3 — a CLOSED list of what counts, and a closing sentence that does real work

**Works that ARE переустройство and/or перепланировка:**

- замена или перенос систем **газоснабжения, центрального отопления**;
- устройство **гидро-, паро-, звукоизоляции**;
- изменение площади, количества жилых комнат и подсобных помещений **в квартире** за счёт разборки существующих и (или) устройства новых **перегородок**;
- изменение площади и количества помещений **в изолированных нежилых помещениях** за счёт разборки/устройства перегородок;
- **устройство, увеличение проёмов в ненесущих стенах и перегородках**.

> **«Иные работы не являются работами по переустройству и (или) перепланировке.»**

**→ That single sentence is the most useful line in the document.** Anything not on this
list — finishes, tiling, screed over an existing screed, replacing sanitaryware in place,
sockets, lighting, furniture — **is not перепланировка and needs no согласование under
this Положение.** ⚠️ It does not exempt it from building, fire or sanitary requirements
(§4 keeps those live), and it does not exempt window/facade work, which п.2 handles
separately.

**⚠️ Note what is on the list and is easy to miss: «устройство гидро-, паро-,
звукоизоляции».** Laying acoustic underlay or applying waterproofing is, on the face of
the text, a work requiring согласование.

### §4 — the prohibitions

Переустройство/перепланировка is **запрещено** where it:

- violates **строительные, противопожарные, санитарно-эпидемиологические** requirements, or culture / architectural-and-construction-activity legislation;
- **reduces the bearing capacity** of foundation soils, foundations, columns, walls, beams, floor slabs, supports or roofs, **or impairs the hydro-, vapour-, thermal- and sound-insulation, or the bio- and fire-resistance, of load-bearing and enclosing structures**;
- **disrupts the operating regime of engineering systems**, or impairs the working parameters of the building's or individual premises' engineering equipment;
- affects **вентиляционные и дымовые шахты и каналы**;
- converts **балконы и лоджии в отапливаемые помещения**;
- creates living rooms **without natural light**, or puts a partition **into a window opening**;
- installs **additional central-heating, hot-water or electrical equipment if that would exceed the design calculated engineering loads for the premises**;
- changes the **architectural or colour solution of the facade**;
- reduces the operational suitability of the building's structures;
- applies to a house declared unfit for habitation and not restorable.

**⚠️ Two of these bear directly on this project.** The **balcony/loggia heating
prohibition** rules out treating the лоджия as a heated room. And the **design-load
prohibition** is the real constraint on adding electric underfloor heating or a powerful
appliance — **not a requirement to obtain технические условия, which §6 imposes only for gas
and central heating.** A widely repeated claim that you must request ТУ from the power
utility for high-wattage appliances **is not supported by this document.**

### §5–7 — TWO approval tracks, and which one this project is on

| Works | What is required |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Замена или перенос систем газоснабжения, центрального отопления** (§6) | согласование (разрешение) **+ технические условия + a разработанный проект** |
| **Insulation; partition changes in a flat; openings in non-load-bearing walls** (§7) | **согласование (разрешение) WITHOUT a проект** |

**Approving body: the district / city исполком, or the local administration of a district
within a city**, by location of the premises (§5).

> **→ For this flat, whose external walls are non-load-bearing and whose partitions are already built: partition changes and new openings in non-load-bearing walls need исполком согласование but NOT a designed project — provided nothing touches gas or central heating.** ⚠️ Moving a radiator is «перенос систем центрального отопления» and would flip the project into the проект track.

### §8–12 — procedure

- **Citizens**: application + the documents listed in **подпункт 1.1.21 пункта 1.1 перечня административных процедур, утверждённого Указом Президента от 26 апреля 2010 г. № 200.** ⚠️ **The citizen's document list is by reference and is NOT in this document** — see Open Questions.
- **Legal entities / IP** (listed in full here, and a useful proxy): технический паспорт (for an owner) **only if it was issued before 1 January 2023**; **план-схема или перечень (описание) работ**; owner's written consent if the premises are leased or in free use; **written consent of all co-owners** where the premises are in shared ownership; consent of the организация застройщиков where applicable.
- **§9 — an inspection with access obligation.** The initiator must let in representatives of the housing-operating organisation, the **energy and gas supply organisations**, and the исполком. An **акт технического осмотра** is drawn up in **free form**, recording whether the existing layout matches the technical passport and the condition of structures and engineering equipment; signed by the initiator and the authorised official, **approved by the исполком representative**.
- **§10 — decision within one month** of application. **And the согласование itself must state**: the initiator; the types of works; whether a проект is needed; **the dates of completion AND of acceptance**; **the conditions for storing building materials and the procedure for removing their waste**; and any other requirements from technical regulations.
  > **⚠️ So the waste procedure is set by your own permit.** General waste legislation is the backdrop; the operative rule for a given renovation is the line written into its разрешение.
- **§11 — refusal grounds**: false information in the submitted documents; the works are prohibited under §4; **risk of collapse of the premises' structures.**
- **§12 — appeal** to the superior body, then to court.

### §13–14 — designers and who may do the work

- Where a проект is required: after obtaining согласование, the initiator contracts a designer **entitled to produce project documentation**; **the composition, form and content of the проект are determined by Минжилкомхоз**; the проект is then agreed with the **территориальное подразделение архитектуры и градостроительства** (a different body from the исполком).
- **⚠️ §14 — «Работы… могут выполняться самим инициатором либо с привлечением лиц, основным видом деятельности которых является строительная деятельность».**
  > **→ Self-performance is expressly permitted.** Directly relevant to a self-managed renovation: the law does not require a licensed contractor for these works. Where a contractor *is* engaged, construction must be its **main** activity.

### Chapter 4 — самовольная перепланировка, and the way back

**§22 — prohibited.** **§23** — whoever did it **must obtain согласование retroactively**,
and it is granted only if the work **does not affect the safety of the building in service
and was done in accordance with technical regulations, as confirmed by a техническое
заключение.** That заключение rests on the **ведомость технических характеристик** and on a
general or, if needed, detailed survey of structures and engineering systems by a legal
entity or IP **entitled to that activity** — **at the offender's own order and expense**.

**§28 — if retro-approval is refused, the premises must be restored to their previous
state** by, or at the expense of, whoever did the work, **within a deadline set by the
исполком** — and **the restoration itself must follow the same production-and-acceptance
procedure** as a permitted перепланировка. **§29** — liability under legislative acts (not
specified here).

## Положение 2 — roofs and facades

### ⚠️⚠️ This ANSWERS the vault's open question about air-conditioning units

**§2 defines «индивидуальные антенны и иные конструкции»** as: any kind of individual
antenna equipment, including for terrestrial and satellite television reception,
**«оборудование, применяемое для кондиционирования воздуха»**, and other constructions.

> **→ An air-conditioning outdoor unit on the roof or facade of a multi-apartment building requires согласование from the ТЕРРИТОРИАЛЬНОЕ ПОДРАЗДЕЛЕНИЕ АРХИТЕКТУРЫ И ГРАДОСТРОИТЕЛЬСТВА — not from the исполком, and not from the building management.** Level 1, from the definition itself.

- **§3 — laundry-drying constructions are exempt** from согласование, **and may be installed only within the internal space of the balcony or loggia.**
- **§6 — refusal grounds**: documents not compliant with legislation, including forged or invalid ones; the installation reducing the building's fitness for use or creating a collapse risk; **or the исполком having prohibited such installations on the facades of houses on the State List of Historico-Cultural Values, or on facades giving onto main streets and squares whose list the исполком determines.**
- **§10 — a continuing duty**: keep the installation in technically sound condition and repair it in time; **a faulty unit must be dismantled**; and if a breach of technical regulations is discovered during use, remedy it or dismantle.
- **§11 — dismantle for the duration of roof or facade repairs** carried out by the housing-operating organisation, if required.
- **§12–14 — самовольная установка is prohibited** and follows the same retro-approval route.

### п.2.1–2.3 of the постановление itself — glazing and window/door infills

- **⚠️ Glazing of balconies and loggias is NOT переустройство/перепланировка** (п.2.1) — but it must follow **variants approved by the территориальное подразделение архитектуры и градостроительства** (colour, materials, configuration), which are **published publicly and on исполком websites.**
- **⚠️ And a continuing obligation most people will not expect**: where the housing-operating organisation carries out repairs to the facade, balconies or loggias, **the person who initiated the glazing must, if necessary, dismantle the glazing elements for the duration of those works** (п.2.1).
- **Replacing window and door infills, and glazing balconies/loggias facing main streets and squares** (list determined by the исполком) is **not перепланировка either, but must preserve the configuration and colour of the existing infills/glazing** (п.2.2).
- For houses on the **State List of Historico-Cultural Values**, window/door infill replacement follows variants approved by the architecture subdivision **in agreement with the Ministry of Culture** (п.2.3).
