## Mistakes / Warnings — the opening failure scenario

## Planning Rules — the five selection mistakes

1. **⚠️ Entrusting a large, complex project to a private craftsman (частный мастер).** He may lack the resources to deliver it, **and material liability is effectively unrecoverable from an individual — "взыскать потом что-либо с физического лица будет невозможно."** If you go ahead anyway, the minimum diligence he specifies is concrete:
   - **⚠️ Check the person on the FSSP site (Russian bailiff service) for debts.**
   - **Verify he has actually done comparable projects — where, and how.**
   - **⚠️ Control is not optional: "нельзя просто так отдать ему кучу денег и ждать 6 месяцев." Require a photo report of the materials actually purchased.**
2. **⚠️ Working without a contract and without a specification.** "Без договора доказать факт сделки будет невозможно — договор ваша страховка."
   - **⚠️ Read it, not just the deadline.** Check it is not one-sided — that obligations do not all fall on the buyer while the maker owes nothing. **His real example is worth keeping as a pattern to look for: a B2B contract clause providing that if the buyer failed to prepare the premises for measurement by a given date, the prepayment for drawings was forfeited and had to be paid again.** His advice: **test the contract for reasonableness, and ask questions about anything you dislike.**
   - **⚠️ The specification is a separate document and is the one that protects you: a mandatory annex to the contract stating exactly what you are buying — not "кухня за миллион" but a sketch with dimensions, materials, hardware and everything else.** **Without it the failure mode is "а мы с вами не договаривались… доплатите ещё 20%."** "Договариваться об этом нужно на берегу."
3. **⚠️ Relying on a recommendation alone.** His case: a foreman you met only on your own site says "есть нормальные ребята," and you transfer your own responsibility to him without doing the elementary checks. **He is explicit that recommendations are good — "этим наша отрасль живёт" — but verify anyway, because "это много времени не занимает."**
   - **The checks he lists**: social media presence (a company working retail maintains it one way or another), some kind of website, and reviews.
   - **⚠️ And if being a manufacturer rather than a reseller matters to you, go and physically look at the production — "не доверяйте просто словам 'вы производители'… доедьте и посмотрите."** What you are looking for is whether people are actually working there. **He reports cases of people showing production facilities that were not theirs, taking the money and disappearing.**
4. **⚠️ Choosing the openly cheapest offer — and this point carries the most usable number in the source.**
   - **⚠️ For an identical specification the honest market spread is small: "адекватный разброс в пределах 10%, причём даже 10 иногда это много."**
   - **⚠️ A large price imbalance therefore means one of two things, both of which are your problem: someone counted carelessly, or a premium project has been mis-scoped as economy** — "они сами его идентифицировали неправильно."
   - **Being promised for 100 what costs 300 and expecting no consequences is "маловероятно."**
   - **⚠️ Myth demolished #1 — the "special supplier access" claim: "нету ни у кого никаких выходов на поставщиков." Furniture-hardware and panel-material suppliers are well known and few in the country, and nobody can buy those materials substantially cheaper. "Это миф — забудьте."** Only a very large company can obtain a real advantage.
   - **⚠️ Myth demolished #2, and this one runs against his own scale: "если ты крупный производитель, у тебя дешевле — тоже отчасти миф. Нет: содержать большое производство стоит много денег." Scale buys quality, lead time and a certain level of service — not a lower price.** **A manufacturer telling you his size does not make him cheaper is the opposite of a sales argument, and is the reason this source is worth trusting on the rest.**
5. **⚠️ Working on 100% prepayment.** Acceptable **only** where you genuinely know the company and have worked with it before. **Otherwise: "отдали 100% — вот можно забыть про них. Не всегда, но…"**
