## Materials — ЛДСП tiering, as his stated practice

- **Expensive laminate: Cleaf (Italian), Kaindl (Austrian).** "Очень хороший ламинат," **but the price approaches natural materials and veneer, so "целесообразность его использования на наш взгляд такая под вопросом."**
- **Their working choice: Egger** — a German brand **manufactured in Russia at two large plants**, significantly cheaper than Cleaf and Kaindl.
- **⚠️ Cheap laminate they refuse: Шексна, Невский ламинат, Lamarty — "нам меньше нравится качество: он более пористый, более рыхлый, при распиле сколы на нём образуются."** **A checkable quality tell: chipping at the cut edge.**
- **⚠️ Two decor categories, and the rule that follows is the useful part: plain (однотонные) versus wood-effect (древесные). They rarely use wood-effect laminate on facades "потому что натуральному дереву он сильно проигрывает." Plain laminate is fine — and their recommended combination is plain laminate plus natural veneer, which "ты получаешь такой оптимальный бюджет и очень классную картинку."**

### ⚠️ The colour-range constraint, with numbers — the real limitation of choosing laminate

- **Egger's range: ~46 colours, of which ~26 are greys (warm and cold) and ~20 are colours.**
- **⚠️ The consequence he states directly: "тебе нужна синяя кухня — там один всего синий цвет. Если в RAL 1,960 разных цветов и оттенков синего может быть 10, то здесь синий только один."** **Greys are well covered; a specific non-grey colour may simply not exist.**
- **⚠️ So the decision rule is: laminate ties you to an existing decor, enamel lets you specify any colour. If the available laminate colour genuinely suits, "страшного нет."**
- **⚠️ And the sequencing move he praises on this object, which is the smart part: the laminate colour was chosen FIRST, and the wall colours and everything else were then selected around it — "достаточно грамотный ход, чтобы получилась красивая картинка в целом по квартире."** **The material with the least colour freedom sets the palette; everything with more freedom follows.**

### The other laminate constraints

- **⚠️ All laminate on the Russian market is 16 mm.** Closed, you see the same thing as any other facade.
- **Tactile: enamel is smoother; ЛДСП has slight orange-peel (шагрень).**
- **⚠️ The one real visual difference is the edge banding (кромка)** — laminate is always banded around the perimeter, whereas enamel is painted in the mass (**and he notes to check whether painting is one- or two-sided**). **His own view: "если бы я об этом не сказал, я думаю, что ты бы сам его и не заметил."**
- **⚠️ Edge-banding quality depends on the equipment: done well, the glue line is visible but uniform everywhere, and it will not peel.** **⚠️ For wet rooms — and even above a hob where wall units sit over steam — polyurethane adhesive is the specified glue**, and "никаких проблем с фасадами как правило не возникает."
- **⚠️ A damaged single laminate facade is easy and cheap to replace** — the inverse of the discontinued-plastic-decor risk this channel raises elsewhere.
- **⚠️ Two cases where laminate cannot be used at all:**
  1. **Any milling/routing in the project — "ламинат мы не фрезеруем, его фрезеровать невозможно, потому что у него основа древесно-стружечная плита, не МДФ."**
  2. **If the required colour is not in the plain-decor range.**
- **Visually, enamel items look dearer — "как раз вот на 30% и выглядят."** He is candid that the money buys the appearance.

## Furniture / Built-ins — ⚠️ the hard rule: cheap board, never cheap hardware

- **"Нельзя при этом использовать дешёвую фурнитуру. Наш подход: мы используем ламинат для оптимизации бюджета там, где это нужно, но на фурнитуре мы никогда не экономим."**
- **On this object specifically: Grass drawers — "такие, какие мы ставим в самые дорогие проекты" — veneer trays they make themselves, and Blum hinges throughout.** Recorded as his stated practice.
- **⚠️ His four reasons for those hinges, and the fourth is the non-obvious one**: years of use with no complaints; **adjustment in all planes with a large adjustment range**; simple mounting; and **the option of fitting a limiter so a facade set tight against a wall does not strike it when opened.**
- **⚠️ A compatibility trap with a number, and this is the kind of detail only a fabricator states: most hardware, especially European, is designed for 18 mm board. With 16 mm laminate the profile protrudes by a couple of millimetres — "за этим надо внимательно смотреть, это касается всей фурнитуры."**
- **Aluminium Gola profiles are used identically whether the facades are laminate, veneer or enamel** — so handle-free detailing is not a tier-dependent feature. **"Получить классную картинку можно и в ламинате."**
- Internal fit-out on this object is anthracite throughout; he notes newer bottle-unit inserts with glass and an anti-slip mat.

## Cost Drivers — their allocation philosophy on larger projects

- **⚠️ On a large project (his example ~200 m²) they mix tiers deliberately: kitchen in natural veneer or enamel, children's rooms budget-optimised.** "Наша философия заключается в том, чтобы бюджет распределять."
- **⚠️ His reasoning on children's rooms is a genuine planning insight and it interacts with existing vault content: "иногда ты делаешь ремонт 2 года. Ты запланировал под ребёнка четырёхлетнего — а ему уже кроватка нафиг не упала. Иди продай это всё."** **Renovation duration itself invalidates age-specific furniture, so age-specific furniture is the wrong thing to spend on.**
- **⚠️ This does not conflict with Безверхая's kids-room method — it completes it.** She argues for specifying the *unchanging infrastructure* (sockets, lighting positions, a fixed wardrobe footprint) for the oldest future occupant while treating furniture as cheap and swappable ([[_Sources/YT_u8-y7QPc48I_bezverkhaia_kids_room_growing_base|u8-y7QPc48I]]). **He supplies the cost half of the same argument from the maker's side: do not put veneer, enamel and milling into a child's room.** Two trades, same conclusion.
- **⚠️ And the whole object corroborates the allocation rule stated in this channel's kitchen-mistakes video** — simpler facades plus a good countertop and good hardware, rather than dear facades with cheap everything else ([[_Sources/YT_CEKciia5vh0_flat_ten_kitchen_design_mistakes|CEKciia5vh0]]).
