## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE CROSS-CUTTING PATTERN — three instances in one batch

**Both these videos, plus the extension-profile finding from [[_Sources/YT_gRQaOBGDBa4_elitbalkon_balcony_thermal_envelope|his insulation video]] and [[_Sources/YT_w2x6LPIvZw0_okna20_balcony_glazing_five_mistakes|Окна 2.0's]] sash-width case, are the same structural finding arriving four times:**

> **⚠️⚠️ THE GLAZING ORDER IS WHERE THE BALCONY'S ENTIRE FIT-OUT GETS LOCKED IN. Decisions that feel like they belong to a later, cheaper stage — where the wardrobe goes, whether you dry laundry there, whether you will insulate someday, which way the sashes swing — all have to be made BEFORE the glazing is measured, or they become impossible.**

| Decision | What it forecloses if deferred |
| :--- | :--- |
| Where the wardrobe stands | That section should have been **blind (глухая), not glazed** |
| Whether laundry dries there | Sash positions collide with the rack; **nothing can be done afterwards** |
| Whether you will ever insulate | **Extension profiles** omitted → a cracked pane cannot be replaced without demolishing the finish |
| Sash width vs. balcony depth | Opened sashes strike the back wall |

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. The drying rack — a real unfixable case

> **His account: an owner rings, the ceiling rack fouls the sashes, the fixings slip, he cannot use it. Photos show a wardrobe one side, opening sashes the other, a narrow balcony, and a large ceiling rack.** ***«Я понимаю, что там ничего сделать нельзя.»*** **His advice to that owner is to put up with it and adapt.**

**⚠️ THE RULE: if laundry will be dried on the balcony, the glazing configuration must be chosen around it — raise it with the surveyor at measurement time.** **And wardrobe doors are the second collision: a wardrobe leaf can block the rack just as a sash can.**

**Rack types and their constraints:**

| Type | Note |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Wall-to-wall lines** (on one or two reels, removable or fixed) | **⚠️ Physically impossible where the balcony has open side sections — there is no end wall to fix to** |
| **Wall-mounted concertina**, fixed to a side or back wall, extending forward | Several rails, about 1 m or more |
| **Ceiling-mounted** | **From ~2,000–3,000 RUB to 50,000–100,000 RUB** with a mechanism and a fan to move air |

- **⚠️ Electrical coordination too:** ceiling rack mechanisms fix to the ceiling, where a light fitting can overheat a line or cord, or wiring can interfere. **Must be resolved with the same drawing.**
- **⚠️ Every ceiling rack is a *«точка внимания»*** — it must be extended, stretched or lowered, and a powered one depends on electricity and a remote.

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Balcony furniture — and the material rule that matters for THIS project

**⚠️ Plan the wardrobe's position BEFORE glazing** so that section is made blind: *«Зачем там стекло, если там будет глухо и темно?»* Tell the window company explicitly, and an experienced surveyor will then dimension the recess for you.

**⚠️ Order of work: the wardrobe is made AFTER the interior finish** — floors laid, walls made good.

**Two routes:** a ready-made cabinet, **which must fit through the balcony door as well as into the recess** (depth, width and height all checked); or a bespoke fitted unit with one to four sides, shelves at chosen levels, fronts, optionally a hanging rail, or one taller bay for a stepladder or ironing board.

**⚠️⚠️ AND THE FAILURE MODE, which is the finding:**

> ***«Если у вас балкон или лоджия холодные и там нет обогрева круглогодичного, то в любой момент на этой мебели может проявиться влажность, и далее может быть ещё плесень — и в итоге это ЛДСП наберёт воду и просто разбухнет.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ DIRECTLY APPLICABLE: this project's balcony is planned unheated and three-season.** **So ordinary ЛДСП carcass furniture — which is what a joinery firm will quote by default — is the wrong material for it.** **The vault's balcony pages had nothing on furniture materials at all.**

**⚠️⚠️ HIS ALTERNATIVE, and it is specific and buildable:**

- **Carcass from ALUMINIUM uprights** — no ЛДСП in the structure, so nothing absorbs water.
- **Fronts from aluminium window profile** (thin, ~40 mm, hinged or sliding), **or PVC leaves built like a window** with blind PVC infill, or frosted or clear glass if you want to see in. **Can be laminated or painted to any colour.**
- **Result: a compact wardrobe that is both water-resistant and frost-resistant.**
- **⚠️ Its one requirement: it must be ventilated.** He notes this is true of all wardrobes — air stagnates, the more so if damp — **so provide deliberate gaps at the bottom under the door and at the top of it.**

> **→ ⚠️ The ventilation requirement corroborates two other things in this vault from unrelated directions: [[_Sources/YT__uYgVG6qnHU_kulekina_wardrobe_vs_walkin|Кулекина's]] finding that closed storage is a dust-and-ventilation question, and [[_Sources/YT_Nr5_Pma2Tvg_elitbalkon_ventilation_and_condensation|his own ventilation material]] on stagnant air producing mould.**
