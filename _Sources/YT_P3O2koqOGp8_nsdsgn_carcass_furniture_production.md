## ⚠️⚠️ The edge band (кромка) is the quality benchmark — and there is a test a client can run

- **His flat claim: «КРОМКА ЯВЛЯЕТСЯ ПРЯМО ВОТ САМОЙ ВАЖНОЙ ВЕЩЬЮ ИМЕННО В КОРПУСНОЙ МЕБЕЛИ — ЭТАЛОН КАЧЕСТВА.»**
- **⚠️ Corroborated by market behaviour: in a top-tier German kitchen showroom, «первое, что они скажут, — это "смотри, какая у нас крутая кромка"».** → The industry itself leads with it, which is evidence about what matters rather than an opinion about it.
- **⚠️⚠️ AND THE TEST, WHICH IS THE MOST DIRECTLY USABLE ITEM IN THE SOURCE: INSPECT THE EDGE ON A WHITE PANEL, BECAUSE THE ADHESIVE IS NOT WHITE.** «Этот эталон качества можно хорошо просмотреть НА ФОНЕ БЕЛОГО МАТЕРИАЛА… наносится КЛЕЙ, и КЛЕЙ ИМЕЕТ НЕ БЕЛЫЙ ЦВЕТ, и когда кромка нанесена плохо… КЛЕЕВОЙ ШОВ ОН ХОРОШО ЗАМЕТЕН.»
  - → **A visible glue line on a white sample means poor edge banding. On a dark panel the same defect is invisible — so ask to see the white sample specifically, and do it in the showroom before ordering.** No tools, no expertise.
- **⚠️ WHY THE GOOD MACHINE PRODUCES NO GLUE LINE — ФУГОВКА (pre-milling): the machine mills 1 MM OFF THE PANEL EDGE IMMEDIATELY BEFORE APPLYING THE BAND**, so the sawn edge and any chipping are removed in the same pass. «По итогу СКОЛОВ после такого станка КАК ТАКОВЫХ НЕ МОЖЕТ БЫТЬ на любом материале.» High-temperature adhesive plus firm pressure closes the joint.
- **⚠️ He can date the difference, because they replaced a machine: their previous bander had NO pre-milling and «КЛЕЕВОЙ ШОВ КОНЕЧНО БЫЛ». After the change, «это конечно НЕБО И ЗЕМЛЯ — в плане качества, в плане эстетики, А САМОЕ ГЛАВНОЕ — И ПРАКТИЧНОСТИ».**
- **⚠️⚠️ AND THE PRACTICAL CONSEQUENCE IS THE FINDING THAT MATTERS, BECAUSE IT CONNECTS THE COSMETIC TEST TO A FAILURE MODE THE VAULT ALREADY HOLDS: «ХОРОШО ПРИКЛЕЕННАЯ КРОМКА на любой материал ПРИ ПОПАДАНИИ ВОДЫ намного ДОЛЬШЕ И БОЛЬШЕ ВЫДЕРЖИВАЕТ… ОТ ВСПУХАНИЯ, ЕСЛИ ВОДУ НЕ УБРАТЬ.»**
  - → **⚠️ The visible glue line is a PROXY FOR WATER RESISTANCE. The vault records ДСП/chipboard swelling as the characteristic failure of carcass furniture and records ДСП frames as single-use; this supplies the mechanism at the edge and turns it into a purchasing test.** A well-banded edge does not make chipboard waterproof — he is careful to say it buys *time*, not immunity, and only if the water is eventually wiped up.

## ⚠️ Why precision costs money — and the argument is structural, not cosmetic

- **The production chain, in order:** design → **constructive/shop drawings** («конструктивный проект мебели») → **CNC beam saw** → **CNC drilling and routing** → **edge bander** → assembly tables → **control assembly** → site.
- **⚠️ THE MECHANICAL ARGUMENT FOR CNC ACCURACY, which is better than the usual "it looks neater": hole-position error ACCUMULATES. «Если сверление отверстия сделано вручную или [на] недорогом станке, ТОЧНОСТЬ ПРИСАДКИ ИМЕЕТ ПОГРЕШНОСТЬ, и эта погрешность потом [встречается] НА СБОРКЕ КОРПУСА… когда сборка корпуса произведена и СТЯНУТА НЕ ОЧЕНЬ ПЛОТНО, детали под углом 90 градусов, горизонт и вертикаль — ЭТО ВСЁ ВЛИЯЕТ НА ПРОЧНОСТЬ ВСЕЙ КОНСТРУКЦИИ шкафа [или] кухни.»**
  - → **⚠️ A carcass that does not pull up tight is not merely untidy — it is weaker, because the joint relies on face-to-face contact and squareness. Drilling accuracy is therefore a STRUCTURAL specification, and that is the honest answer to "why is this twice the price".**
- **⚠️ THE PRECISION CLAIM, AND IT SHOULD BE FLAGGED RATHER THAN REPEATED: he states the beam saw's tolerance as «ПОГРЕШНОСТЬ ЗДЕСЬ ОДНА СОТАЯ МИЛЛИМЕТРА» — 0.01 mm.**
  - → **⚠️⚠️ RECORD AS CLAIMED AND FLAG AS ALMOST CERTAINLY OVERSTATED. Panel sawing of chipboard at 0.01 mm is not physically meaningful — the material itself moves more than that with humidity, and industrial panel saws are specified in tenths of a millimetre at best.** The *direction* of his argument (a better saw is more accurate, and accuracy compounds) is sound; the number is marketing. **This is exactly the kind of figure the vault should hold with the flag attached rather than silently drop, because it is the sort of number that gets quoted back.**
- **⚠️ Human factor deliberately engineered out: «процентов на 95 все детали СВЕРЛЯТСЯ, ПРИСАЖИВАЮТСЯ, ФРЕЗЕРУЮТСЯ — всё автоматизировано», with only 1–2% of holes and cuts done by hand, and those at installation.**
- **⚠️ EVERY PART IS BARCODE-LABELLED, and the reason is site error: the drilling machine reads the barcode and knows every hole for that part; and when «привозят… БОЛЬШУЮ ГРУДУ ДЕТАЛЕЙ», the assembler «ТОЧНО ЗНАЕТ, КУДА КАКАЯ [деталь]».** → Traceability as an error-reduction measure, not a logistics one.
- **⚠️ Routing for INTEGRATED LIGHTING and for the GOLA PROFILE is done at this stage** — «здесь делаются все отверстия, все фрезеровки под подсветку, под профиль гола».
  - → **⚠️⚠️ THIS IS A SEQUENCING CONSEQUENCE THE VAULT NEEDS: recessed cabinet lighting and handleless Gola fronts are FACTORY OPERATIONS, cut before assembly. They cannot be retrofitted, which means the lighting layout and the handle strategy must be final BEFORE furniture production starts.** It is the direct explanation for why the host's own worktop light is invisible from the edge (`AEJlxbTmQJU`) — and why the touch-switch decision had to be made at the same moment.
- Non-rectangular parts are cut from rectangular blanks — triangles, trapezoids, corner cut-outs.
- ⚠️ The host compares the plant favourably with **Antonio Lupi in Italy** and notes cleanliness as a quality signal: «производства всегда ассоциируется [с] какой-то грязью, пылью, с каким-то бардаком — здесь просто всё идеально вычищено». → A soft observation, but a factory visit is something a client can actually do.

## ⚠️⚠️ The curved cabinet — a quantified buildability trap, and the best finding here

- **His warning about designers: «НАЧИНАЮЩИЕ ДИЗАЙНЕРЫ РИСУЮТ НЕРЕАЛИЗУЕМЫЕ ВЕЩИ — не то, что их невозможно построить, а то, что они будут стоить НЕПОДЪЁМНЫХ ДЕНЕГ. Сейчас в моде ТЁПЛЫЙ МИНИМАЛИЗМ, СКРУГЛЁННЫЕ, такие ПРИРОДНЫЕ ФОРМЫ, и я ЧАСТО ВИЖУ 3D, в которых ШКАФ ЗАКРУГЛЁН, ещё и с ФРЕЗЕРОВКОЙ.»**
- **⚠️⚠️ AND THE COST: «когда этот шкаф начинают считать, то СТОИМОСТЬ ЭТОГО УГЛА МОЖЕТ БЫТЬ СОПОСТАВИМА СО СТОИМОСТЬЮ САМОГО ШКАФА.»** One curved corner ≈ the price of the whole cabinet.
- **⚠️ THE MANUFACTURING REASON, which is why it costs that and why there is no cheaper way:**
  - You **cannot** take solid timber and simply rout a curve — **«потом погнёт»**, it will warp.
  - MDF will not take the trick either as a single piece.
  - **Instead: THIN ROUTED MDF STRIPS are cut, stacked and threaded together, CLAMPED WITH SPECIAL METAL PARTS and GLUED UP — and those laminated sub-assemblies are then combined into the full corner.** He shows one small glued-up piece and points out how many are needed.
  - → **A curved cabinet corner is a LAMINATED BUILT-UP ASSEMBLY, not a machined one. «И конечно же это ДОРОГОЕ ИЗДЕЛИЕ. И НЕ НАДО ЭТОМУ УДИВЛЯТЬСЯ.»**
- → **⚠️⚠️ THIS CONNECTS DIRECTLY TO THE ROUND 5 RENDER-VETTING HEURISTIC and upgrades it from a caution to a specific, priced test: a curved or radiused element in a render is a cost question to ask BEFORE approving the drawing, and the order of magnitude is "the corner can cost what the cabinet costs".** The vault's material and joinery pages have nothing on curved-element buildability.

## ⚠️⚠️ The white-balance trick for judging a near-white colour

- He walked into the assembly shop, saw his own fronts and **«просто ПЕРЕКОСИЛСЯ ОТ УЖАСА — МНЕ ПОКАЗАЛОСЬ, ЧТО ОНИ БЕЛЫЕ»**, which was not the colour he had specified.
- **⚠️ THE RESOLUTION: the unpainted BACK of the panel is plain white, and they held a SHEET OF WHITE A4 PAPER against the painted face — «СРАЗУ ЖЕ ПРОИЗОШЁЛ БАЛАНС БЕЛОГО, и сразу же стало видно, что они ТЁПЛОГО БЕЛОГО ЦВЕТА. Я УСПОКОИЛСЯ.»**
- → **⚠️ Judge a near-white against a sheet of plain white paper. Without a true-white reference the eye has no anchor and reads any off-white as white — and this happened to the DESIGNER, about HIS OWN specified colour, in good light.**
- **⚠️ This is a free, repeatable acceptance test for every off-white in the project — paint, fronts, tile, stone — and the vault's colour-selection content has nothing equivalent.** It also explains a whole class of "the colour came out wrong" complaints: the sample was judged without a reference.

## ⚠️ Solid timber fronts — the gaps are deliberate

- The solid-timber shop is largely hand work: small parts, glue-ups, **filling of the joints (шпатлевание)**, sanding, then enamel finish. **«По сути ОЧЕНЬ ДОРОГАЯ, ПОТОМУ ЧТО РУЧНАЯ РАБОТА.»**
- **⚠️⚠️ AND THE DESIGN RESPONSE TO WOOD MOVEMENT, which is a genuinely good principle: on the assembled solid-timber fronts «видно все эти ФИЛЁНКИ, красивые стыковки — вот эти ЩЕЛИ, ОНИ ВСЁ РАВНО ПОТОМ БУДУТ. ОНИ ПО СУТИ ЯВЛЯЮТСЯ ЭЛЕМЕНТОМ ДЕКОРА, ПОТОМУ ЧТО МАССИВ ГУЛЯЕТ В ЛЮБОМ СЛУЧАЕ… ПОЭТОМУ ИХ НЕ МАСКИРУЮТ — ИХ ДЕЛАЮТ КАК БЫ ЭЛЕМЕНТОМ ДЕКОРА.»**
  - → **Solid timber moves, so panel joints WILL open. Good practice EXPRESSES the joint rather than concealing it, because a concealed joint that opens looks like a defect while an expressed one looks like a detail.**
  - **⚠️ This is the same principle as the vault's «благородное старение» content and as expansion-gap logic generally — design for the movement you cannot prevent — and it is the clearest statement of it the vault has.** It is also the honest counter to anyone specifying a seamless painted solid-timber front.

## ⚠️ The assembly stage — two checkable supplier practices

- **⚠️ CONTROL ASSEMBLY IN THE WORKSHOP for kitchens: modules are assembled, fronts hung, checked by QC (УТК), packed, and only then delivered.** → **A question a client can ask any kitchen supplier: is the kitchen trial-assembled at the works before delivery?**
- **⚠️ Delivered in LARGE PRE-ASSEMBLED BLOCKS, not as flat-packed parts — «вот эти КУБИКИ LEGO… собираем предварительно… ТАКИМИ БОЛЬШИМИ БЛОКАМИ их уже повезут на квартиру, поставят на место и уже будут ПРИКРУЧИВАТЬ ФАСАДЫ».** His own kitchen's upper section, with five upper drawers, is visible pre-assembled.
  - → Reduces on-site work to positioning, pulling together and hanging fronts — which is where the 1–2% of hand operations occur. ⚠️ **The consequence for the flat: access route and lift dimensions matter, because the units arrive as large rigid blocks rather than as boards.** (My inference, flagged — he does not raise it.)
- Large-format parts are packed and shipped directly without pre-assembly.
- A tilting/sliding saw in the assembly shop handles local adjustments — «этот диск он крутится в любой плоскости, до миллиметра наклоняется, выдвигается».
