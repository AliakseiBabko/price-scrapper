## Bathroom — Durable Facts & Rules

- **Wall-to-wall vanity/countertop must be decided at the design stage, not adjusted during construction** — it affects plumbing rough-in height/position and multiple wall-junction details. **Real cited regret**: a client requested a design change to skip the custom matching countertop over the washing machine (to save 15,000 RUB); at time of filming, the same client was willing to pay 50,000 RUB to retrofit it properly after seeing the finished result elsewhere. **Explicit rule**: never deviate from an already-designed solution to save a small amount during construction. `confirmed`, real named cost figures on both sides.
- **Never confuse a wall-to-wall vanity/countertop's installation method with a built-in bathtub's**: a bathtub is installed such that tile overhangs/integrates over its rim (tile goes in after the tub is positioned); a vanity/countertop is installed strictly *after* tiling is complete, and gets a baseboard/trim strip around its own perimeter instead of a tile overhang. `confirmed`, a specific sequencing/integration distinction, corroborates and clarifies this store's existing tub-vs-tile-overhang fact from a different source.
- **An integrated sink-in-countertop (undermount/seamless basin) is the easiest configuration to clean** — a single continuous surface can be wiped clean of hair, toothpaste, soap, or other mess in one motion, versus a separate sink sitting on/in a countertop (already less practical than integrated) or a fully separate freestanding sink (least practical of the three for cleaning). Recommended despite being one of the pricier options in this tier. `confirmed`.
- **Mixer/faucet placement should match the client's dominant hand**: when washing your face with soap in your eyes, you find the tap by feel using your dominant hand — the mixer should be positioned on that side. Handedness must be determined before finalizing mixer position. `confirmed`, corroborates this store's existing handedness-based-fixture-placement theme (hygienic shower, wardrobe-zone overlap direction) with a third specific application.
- **Washing machine positioned under a vanity countertop has a real ergonomic flaw, often overlooked**: for a sink to be usable without dripping water onto the floor, the countertop height must let the user lean over with elbows positioned higher than wrists, so water runs back down into the basin rather than off the elbows. **Even the shortest washing machine on the smallest available legs raises the countertop too high for this to work** — regardless of how far the user leans forward, water will drip off the elbows onto the floor. `confirmed`, a specific, non-obvious ergonomic mechanism — genuinely new content for this store.
- **A low-mounted "bottom tap" (floor-level faucet) is a rarely-used but genuinely valuable feature** for washing feet (human or pet), filling buckets, or mopping — particularly valuable for households with pets, a habit of rinsing feet separately, or elderly occupants who have difficulty bending down (they can simply place a foot under a low stream rather than bending to a normal-height fixture). **Explicitly flagged as something that must be proactively suggested to clients**, since most don't know the option exists and wouldn't think to ask for it. `confirmed`, genuinely new fixture-option content for this store.
- **Level/flush bathroom flooring (no threshold, matching the rest of the apartment's floor level) carries real risk beyond its premium appearance**: described as often non-compliant with applicable regulations ("противозаконно" — the specific regulation isn't named or sourced in this transcript, so this claim is `unverified`/not independently checked), and as a real leak-exposure risk — even with a leak-protection sensor at every fixture, some water still reaches the flooring surface before a sensor can trip and shut off the supply. **Only pair this flooring choice with genuinely water-immune flooring** — the source names a specific material (transcribed as "скворцинил," almost certainly a mis-transcription of "кварцвинил" i.e. quartz-vinyl/SPC flooring — `ASR-uncertain` on the exact material name, though the underlying requirement — genuinely waterproof flooring only — is clear). `confirmed` mechanism; `unverified` legal claim; material name `ASR-uncertain`.
