- **A curtain-track-concealment technique using a stretch-ceiling

## Mistakes / Warnings — A Genuine Same-Channel Disagreement on Baseboard Tiering

- **⚠️ Flagged, not silently merged**: this vault's existing
  `Budget_Tiers_Cheap_Optimal_Premium.md` (from this same channel's
  `Tyl0yPQkO5g`) places **PVC baseboard at the cheap tier**, explicitly
  endorsed there as fine so long as the hardware/fittings are decent
  quality. **This video takes a stricter position**: it states PVC
  baseboard should be avoided entirely, since it signals "this is a
  cheap renovation" — this source's own "budget-but-not-cheap"
  recommendation instead starts at polyurethane or durapolymer

  **MDF paint-ready baseboard specifically protected by a moisture-
  proof film/coating** (MDF's core weakness being moisture sensitivity)
  as a cheaper-than-polyurethane alternative once that protection is in
  place. **A further named alternative, not on the existing tiers
  page at all**: a plain metal corner/angle trim run along the room
  perimeter, described as a good-looking, inexpensive option distinct
  from any baseboard product. **Recorded as an open, two-sided
  disagreement** between two same-channel sources on where the
  PVC-baseboard line sits — not resolved in favor of either.
