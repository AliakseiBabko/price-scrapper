## Perimeter Tape and Frame-Depth Budgeting

1. **Perimeter damper tape ("Vibrostek M") installation**: glued to both
   wall and floor with Вибросил silicone, applied in **two layers**
   around the entire wall perimeter.
2. **⚠️ Frame-depth budgeting rule, worked example**: total finished
   assembly depth (frame + insulation + triplex + Акулайн board) is
   ≈10cm; the frame alone (without board) is ≈6cm. Before installing the
   tape, find the wall's single most protruding point and measure back
   from it: tape sits ~2cm off that point (≈10cm final depth budget with
   ~1cm reveal left to trim at the end), and the guide track sits 6cm off
   the same reference point, transferred via plumb-bob or laser level so
   top and bottom tracks stay in one vertical plane.

## Frame Assembly and QC

3. **Track fastening spacing** uses the manufacturer's pre-punched
   profile hole spacing (~57-60cm on this product).
4. **Vibro-hanger spacing**: manufacturer recommends 80-90cm apart; this
   crew deliberately tightened to **70cm** on this specific wall — a real
   installer discretion to exceed the minimum spec, not a violation of it.
5. **Vertical profile spacing**: 60cm between profiles.
6. **⚠️ Straightedge QC check, three points per profile**: hold a
   straightedge against each profile at top, middle, and bottom — any
   gap anywhere along the length (even if top/bottom touch perfectly)
   means the profile isn't in-plane and must be corrected before fixing.
7. **⚠️ Two-screw-per-connection rule**: every profile-to-hanger
   connection point must use **two** screws, not one — called out
   explicitly as a common builder shortcut/mistake that looks minor but
   isn't.
8. **Material-quality visual tell**: a good track/profile has a
   textured/dimpled ("пупырышки," ribbed) surface — check for this when
   buying or verifying delivered material.

## Insulation and Boarding

9. **Eco-wool handling caution**: despite manufacturer claims of being
   fully eco-safe, installers report it still causes skin irritation
   during handling (less than standard mineral wool, but still
   unpleasant) — a protective coverall suit is worn during installation.
10. **⚠️ Insulation-density myth correction**: don't pack/overstuff the
    Шуманет Эко insulation tightly into the frame cavity — a common
    builder mistake assuming more material packed in means more
    soundproofing. The material must sit **loose/relaxed** in the cavity
    to perform its actual acoustic function; the vibro-hangers' free tabs
    are bent back over the loose insulation afterward to help hold it in
    place before boarding.
11. **Triplex (GVL sandwich) board sizing**: 1.2m × 2.0m sheets.
12. **Board orientation rule**: each triplex sheet has a printed arrow
    indicating its required installation orientation — not
    freely rotatable.
13. **⚠️ Staggered-seam rule, two layers deep**: triplex sheet seams must
    be staggered relative to each other, *and* the final Акулайн drywall
    layer's seams must be staggered relative to the triplex seams beneath
    it — explicitly to prevent cracking at a coincident seam line.
14. **Finishing technique**: seams between triplex sheets are filled with
    Вибросил silicone (not taped); after boarding, the excess perimeter
    Vibrostek M tape (the ~1.5-2cm reveal left by the earlier depth
    calculation) is trimmed with the blade held at 45° specifically so
    the resulting angled gap can be filled with Вибросил sealant — vacuum
    the floor/corner before applying sealant.
