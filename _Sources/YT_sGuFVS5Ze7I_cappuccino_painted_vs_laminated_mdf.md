## ⚠️⚠️ 1. How to tell them apart — the checkable core

**MDF** = *"спрессованный картон и мелкая древесная пыль"*, available with one-sided or two-sided lamination, **so the back of a front is very often just white.**

| Tell | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Same colour on both sides** | **painted MDF** |
| **Wood structure one side, white the other** | **laminated MDF** |
| **Comparing two gloss fronts** | ⚠️ **lamination clearly loses on the gloss layer — duller, and on close inspection you can see ADHESIVE LUMPS giving an orange-peel (шагрень) effect** |
| **A visible film seam on close inspection** | laminated |
| **A hole on the BACK of a milled front** | laminated — ⚠️ it exists *"чтобы под плёнкой не образовывались пузырьки во время вакуумной поклейки"* |

> **⚠️ This is the same shape as the FLAT series' existing "how to tell natural veneer, fine-line and ЛДСП apart" section** — and it is the right shape for a buyer checking what they were actually sold.

**Production differs accordingly**: film fronts are made by applying a thin adhesive layer and bonding under **vacuum**; painted fronts take **four stages — isolator → primer → paint → lacquer.**

| Test | Method | Result | His score |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

> **⚠️⚠️ THE TEST RESULTS MATTER MORE THAN THE SCORES, AND TWO ARE WORTH CARRYING:**
> - **⚠️ Зелёнка is the stain neither front survives.** A household antiseptic found in most Russian-speaking homes, and it permanently marks both. **Not a material choice — a household-risk fact.**
> - **⚠️⚠️ The water result is about the SUBSTRATE, not the finish.** Both fronts fail the same way because **both are MDF underneath**; the coating only changes how long it takes. **→ Any MDF front is a moisture-limited product, and the finish is not the variable that fixes it.** ⚠️ **Relevant wherever a front meets standing water — under a sink, beside a dishwasher, in a bathroom.**

## 3. The remaining criteria, scored

- **Visual**: painted 5, laminated 4. **Tactile**: painted 5, laminated 4 — ⚠️ *"у ламинации есть ощущение пластика… даже древесные декоры воспринимаются как искусственное покрытие."*
- **Coating types**: painted has **gloss, matt, soft-touch**; laminated adds a **structural** finish that reproduces wood and silk well. Both 4.
- **Colour range**: painted is **limited only by the RAL/NCS catalogue**, and **can be painted in two or three colours, mixing matt and gloss on one front, to any pattern**; film has soft-touch effects but **a very limited colour choice.** Both 4.
- **⚠️ UV**: **painted fronts fade even with UV-protective lacquer**, and **film also dislikes direct sun.** Both 3.
  > **⚠️⚠️ AND THE CONSEQUENCE HE NAMES IS A SERVICEABILITY ONE: *"если вы повредили фасад спустя год эксплуатации, то подобрать цвет будет практически невозможно."*** **→ A damaged front after a year cannot be colour-matched. Order a spare at the time of manufacture, or accept that a replacement will be visible.** **New to this vault, and it applies to every painted surface in the flat, not only kitchens.**
- **Heat**: **a hot pot up to 100 °C affected neither**; **above that, film can deform.** Painted 5, laminated 4.
- **Care**: **painted must not meet abrasive sponges, solvents or harsh chemistry**; ⚠️ **and *"от частого протирания на одном и том же месте может со временем вытереться слой лака, что создаст пятно на фасаде."*** Laminated is slightly more forgiving but the same restrictions apply. Both 4.
  > **⚠️⚠️ That lacquer-wear mechanism is the hidden cost of PUSH-TO-OPEN** — see [[_Sources/YT_e7CPmsT_P8Q_cappuccino_handleless_opening_and_blum_hardware|the hardware note]] §1, where TIP-ON is criticised precisely because you press the face of the front every time. **Two videos from the same fabricator, and the finding only appears when they are read together.**
- **⚠️ Cost: painted MDF is roughly TWICE the price of laminated.** ⚠️ **Recorded as a RATIO, which is structural; no absolute figure is routed (Ukraine 2020, rule 2).** Laminated 5, painted 3.

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Design constraints — two of them are fabrication limits with numbers

- **⚠️⚠️ From painted MDF you can make an ASSEMBLED front whose internal angles are exactly 90°. Milling the face cannot do this, *"потому что самая минимальная фреза имеет радиус 3 миллиметра."*** **→ A 3 mm minimum internal radius is a hard limit of milled fronts. Sharp internal corners require an assembled painted front.**
- **⚠️⚠️ An internal (J-) handle CANNOT be made in laminated MDF.** **→ Directly couples to the opening-method decision: choosing a J-handle eliminates film fronts entirely.** See [[_Sources/YT_e7CPmsT_P8Q_cappuccino_handleless_opening_and_blum_hardware|the hardware note]] §1.
- **Radiused fronts are possible in either; complex geometric detail is better in painted MDF.**
