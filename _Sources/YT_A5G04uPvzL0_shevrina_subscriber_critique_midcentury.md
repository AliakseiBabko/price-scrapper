## Design Concept — Her Corrections to the Submitted Interior

- **⚠️ Unify wood tones.** The TV-zone veneer sits outside the general gamut; she would tint it to match the dining zone and choose all future wood decor in that same tone. **Consistent with the one-wood-tone rule from `Bnuim5NjgCU`** — good internal consistency across the round.
- **⚠️ Unify the metal finish.** A black-metal side table among chrome pieces should be chrome — or wood.
- **The floor reads too grey and too "loft"**; she would warm it, while acknowledging that changing a floor is not a small change.
- **⚠️ Remove mouldings where there are no other classical elements.** She adds a detail: when mouldings *are* used, they are normally **inset from the edges of a construction**, not run along them.
- **⚠️ Skirting can be omitted entirely** where the corners are neatly formed — easier cleaning, furniture sits closer to the wall. On the "children will scuff the wall" objection: a child scuffs above skirting height too.
- **Swap the bench and chairs**: bench against the wall, chairs to the living-room side.

## Design Concept — Textile, Warmth and Which Fixes Are Still Available

- **⚠️ Textile softens corners and adds warmth, and is worth hanging even where you don't need light control.** Her reasoning: textile smooths a room's corners so you need no other decor there, and removes the sense of emptiness and coldness. **She underlines this specifically because the floor here is microcement rather than wood** — a hard, cool floor has to be compensated with textile and wood elsewhere.
- **⚠️ A practical framing worth keeping: curtains and wall panels are "clean-stage" changes.** They can be added after the renovation is finished, with no messy wet trades — so they remain available as fixes long after most decisions are locked. Useful for anyone deciding what to defer.
- **⚠️ A dark or black wall recedes and becomes background** — "она как бы отдаляется от нас, отходит на задний план, является фоном для всего остального интерьера, не привлекая к себе внимание." She approves of it in the submitted flat. A clean mechanism statement for using dark colour to *reduce* prominence rather than create an accent.
- **Composition suggestion**: a commode with a picture above it, partly open and partly closed storage so it doesn't read bulky.
