## Mistakes / Warnings & Planning Rules — Genuinely New Content

- **Window trickle-vent hardware (щелевое проветривание / micro-ventilation
  mode)** recommended as a bedroom-specific window upgrade — lets air
  exchange without fully opening the window, specifically framed as
  helping offset winter radiator-driven dryness. **Cost: ~500 RUB added to
  the window's own price** — a specific, low-cost figure not previously on
  this store's bedroom or windows content.
- **A private/master en-suite bathroom off the bedroom is recommended
  whenever the floor plan allows it** (free-plan apartment or a unit where
  a wall can be reconfigured) — reasoning: avoids sharing a bathroom with
  guests/other household members sleeping elsewhere, and creates a fully
  private zone no one else (including children) needs to access. Framed as
  worth doing "without question" (однозначно) when feasible, even if only
  a small shower-only en-suite is possible.
- **Reading-light fixture type, more specific than a generic "bedside
  lamp"**: recommends a small, precisely-aimed, individually-controllable
  spot light — explicitly analogized to a business-class airline-seat
  reading light — over a hanging glass-globe fixture (flagged in this
  channel's companion video as accident-prone), so the light illuminates
  only the phone/book, not the room or the other occupant.
- **⚠️ Direct contradiction against this channel's own companion video on
  bedside outlets**: this video states the "correct" (правильно) choice is
  **no charging outlets directly at the bed** — reasoning given is reduced
  EMF/radiation exposure while sleeping — while explicitly admitting his
  own bedroom doesn't follow this (he keeps a phone charging bedside) and
  that most viewers will disagree in the comments. **The companion video
  (`a_i8pGVa7-w`) instead recommends keeping bedside charging outlets**,
