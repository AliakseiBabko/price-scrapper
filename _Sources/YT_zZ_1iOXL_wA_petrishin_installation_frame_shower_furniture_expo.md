## Toilet Installation Frame ("инсталляция") — Load, Repair, and Corner-Mount Facts

1. **Load-bearing distribution**: the frame's **lower fixation points bear
   the main structural load**, not the rear/upper anchors — those upper
   points exist mainly for leveling/plumb adjustment, not load-bearing.
   The rep states the frame is tested to hold a person weighing ≈400kg —
   **independently corroborates** the existing 400kg reinforced-frame
   capacity figure already on [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Wall_Hung_Toilet_Installation|Wall-Hung Toilet Installation]]
   (Kruglov/Ontario), from a second, unrelated brand/source.
2. **Repairability, genuinely new detail on this page**: only two
   mechanisms exist inside the frame (inlet valve, outlet/flush valve);
   both are reached through the frame's maintenance hatch/window, and the
   flush-valve fittings use hand-tightened connectors (no wrench needed)
   to keep servicing simple. **Warranty terms**: 15 years on the frame and
   tank body; 2 years on the fittings/valve mechanism from the
   manufacturer, extendable with a certified installer. Parts continue to
   be produced even after a specific model is discontinued.
3. **Named common failure point**: the outlet-valve flap gasket
   ("резинка") degrades over time from water minerals/limescale, losing
   elasticity and eventually causing a slow leak — sold as a separate
   replacement part; framed as routine wear, not a design defect.
4. **Tile-to-metal-frame cold-crack risk**: where ceramic tile meets the
   frame's exposed metal maintenance-window edge directly (no grout
   joint), the different thermal-expansion behavior of metal vs. tile can
   crack the tile — keep a proper grout gap at that junction rather than
   a tight fit.
5. **Corner installations are handled via a separate universal bracket
   kit, not a dedicated corner-frame product**: any standard frame can be
   converted to corner mounting by swapping the standard included bracket
   for a separately-sold corner-mount bracket kit — cheaper/simpler than
   stocking a distinct corner-specific frame SKU.

## Toilet-Bowl Forced Ventilation Fan — a Distinct Mechanism from Room Ventilation

A ventilation fan built into the toilet bowl rim itself (not yet priced
at the time of filming, upcoming product) captures odor **at the source
inside the bowl** before it disperses into the room air, rather than
relying on a room-level exhaust fan to remove already-dispersed odor
after the fact (the rep explicitly contrasts this against ordinary
room-air diffusion). Wired to share a switch with the room's light
fixture for simultaneous activation. Can vent to either the building's
existing ventilation duct or a dedicated outside vent.

## Linear Shower Drain Channel — a Three-Tier Product Ladder

A named mid-tier product line was introduced specifically in response to
installer/tiler complaints about all-plastic drain channels being
difficult to get a clean tile fit against: **budget tier** — plastic
body/grate (cheapest, e.g. the popular "apz 8910" reference model);
**new mid-tier** — plastic channel body with a combined trap plus a
stainless-steel grate (priced between the two other tiers); **premium
tier** — full stainless-steel channel body and grate (most expensive, e.g.
the "Z1" line). 15-year warranty on the channel body across the range.
`single-account` naming/pricing, brand-specific — recorded as a
three-tier product-category taxonomy pattern (plastic → hybrid →
stainless), not this specific brand's SKUs.
