## Bedroom / Furniture — Durable Facts & Rules

- **Bed and TV placement should be decided before wardrobe placement, not after** — in this case the bed could only go against one specific wall (any other wall either blocked balcony access or was too short), and the TV had to go opposite the bed (any other wall meant facing curtains instead of the TV before sleep) — once those two are fixed, the wardrobe has to fit whatever remains, sometimes requiring the room's window/balcony opening itself to be reconfigured to create space. `confirmed`, the core planning-sequence lesson of this source.
- **Technique: widen/reposition a window or balcony-block opening to free wall space for a wardrobe** — worked dimensioned sequence (this project): offset 700mm from the right wall, then another 800mm to set the new opening width, offset 500mm from the left wall to set window width, raise the balcony-block threshold to exactly 100mm above the screed, then form a radiator niche 850mm down from the ceiling with the niche sized 5mm larger than the radiator grille's actual back-mounting dimensions (not its visible decorative face) — worked example: a 1000×600mm grille needs a 1005×605mm niche. `confirmed`, precise dimensioned sequence, directly actionable.
- **A wardrobe adjacent to a reconfigured window opening needs its clearance pre-verified against the window/balcony reveal trim** — worked example: a 700mm-wide space fits a 600mm-deep wardrobe with clearance to spare against the opening's own trim/reveal, explicitly checked so the wardrobe door doesn't conflict with or overlap the window reveal. `confirmed`.
- **Bed-and-electrical layout formula**: center the bed on the room's horizontal midline; place symmetric independently-switched bedside lights on each side of the headboard; at each nightstand, group an electrical point containing a light switch, a through-switch (проходной выключатель) for the main room light, and 1-2 sockets; project the bed's centerline onto the opposite wall to center the TV on the same axis. `confirmed`, a repeatable bedroom-layout checklist.
- **Always add at least two outlets inside a balcony/window reveal at the final design step** — described as "a signature move of any good designer," a small, easily-forgotten detail. **Corroborates this store's existing window-reveal-outlet fact** from a different Zemstandart source. `confirmed`.
