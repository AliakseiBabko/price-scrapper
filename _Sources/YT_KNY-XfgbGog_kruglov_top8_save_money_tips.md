- **⚠️ Correction on cross-check: this is largely a restatement of this
  channel's own existing ceiling cost ladder, not new** — this video's
  100 m² worked totals (stretch ≈250,000 RUB ≈$2,800; drywall

  `13_Surfaces_and_Finishes/Ceilings_Guide.md`'s existing Kruglov/Ontario
  five-way cost ladder (`YT_lhNC30_adGc`, Round 5: stretch ≈250,000 RUB,
  multi-level drywall ≈1,350,000 RUB) — same channel, same order of
  magnitude, not independently re-extracted as a new cost data point.
  **The one genuinely new addition is a material-science detail behind
  the stretch ceiling's hypoallergenic/non-yellowing claim**:
  the stretch-ceiling membrane is made from **primary (virgin) plastic
  pellets, melted directly** — this is why it doesn't darken/yellow over
  time, unlike **recycled plastic** (the source's own comparison: PVC
  window-frame profiles themselves don't yellow, being primary plastic,
  but the plastic *insert* strips sometimes fitted to windowsills do
  yellow, being made from recycled plastic).
- **⚠️ Concealed-mount interior door, full real-cost breakdown —
  extends video 1's brief 10,000-15,000 RUB touch-up figure into a
  complete model**: a naive comparison (regular door ≈20,000 RUB +
  ≈5,000 RUB install = ≈25,000 RUB ≈$280 vs. concealed door minimum
  ≈30,000 RUB + ≈10,000 RUB install = ≈40,000 RUB ≈$440) understates
  the real gap. A concealed door is typically paint-finish on both
  faces (+≈5,000-10,000 RUB ≈$60-110), needs the flush wall-side
  junction professionally finished (+≈5,000-6,000 RUB ≈$60-70), and the
  opposite-side reveal needs its own plaster-prep-and-paint pass
  (+≈3,000 RUB/linear m ≈$30/linear m) — **real total ≈60,000 RUB
  (≈$670) vs. ≈25,000 RUB (≈$280) for a regular door, more than double
  the naive estimate**. **Corroborates (does not extend) this vault's
  existing concealed/shadow-baseboard price-ratio content**: a
  concealed door structurally requires a matching concealed/shadow-gap
  baseboard, not a standard one — regular paintless MDF baseboard
  ≈850 RUB/linear m (≈$10/linear m) vs. concealed/shadow baseboard
  ≈2,000+ RUB/linear m (≈$20+/linear m), a >3× difference.
- **Minimize the count of pass-through ("проходные") three-way
  switches to only where they genuinely simplify life** (named examples:
  bedroom entry point + bedside) — wiring every switch in an apartment
  as pass-through, rather than scoping it deliberately, adds substantial
  wiring/chasing complexity and cost without proportional convenience
  gain.
- **⚠️ Epoxy (two-component) grout scoped to constant/prolonged
  water-contact zones only, with concrete price figures**: a shower
  drain/podium zone genuinely needs epoxy grout (water sits there
  meaningfully longer than elsewhere) — the source's explicit advice is
  **not to epoxy-grout an entire bathroom**, since cement grout at
  **≈250-300 RUB/m²** (well under $10 at this project's rounding-bucket
  convention) versus epoxy at **≈1,000-2,000 RUB/m² (≈$10-$20/m²)** —
  up to roughly 7× more expensive — for the same visual/functional
  result outside true wet zones.
- **Built-in/concealed mixer valve vs. external/surface mixer — a real
  cost driver named directly**: a concealed mixer requires embedding a
  mixing unit inside the wall before tiling, meaningfully more
  expensive than a surface-mounted mixer; a thermostat function can be
  added to either type, so choosing external-only (skipping concealed)
  is a real cost lever independent of the thermostat decision.
- **⚠️ Large-format tile's real cost drivers, beyond a higher per-m²
  material price — a named mechanism list not previously this
  detailed in this store**: delivery cost for large-format slabs can
  run nearly as much as the material itself; lifting/hoisting cost is
  similarly disproportionate; breakage risk during delivery is
  meaningfully higher, which delivery pricing itself accounts for;
  installation requires an unusually flat wall substrate; and **every
  hole cut into a large slab for plumbing, mirror mounts, or a faucet
  carries a real risk of cracking the entire slab** (a small mistake
  destroys an expensive unit, unlike a small individual tile).
  **Practical caution**: large-format tile is frequently specified
  across an entire wall in a design project, then ends up mostly hidden
  behind towels, mirrors, and furniture once the room is finished —
  wasted spend on invisible area.
- **⚠️ Tiled-niche shower shelving vs. glass shelving, a concrete cost
  comparison**: recessed shelf niches lined in tile (cutting/finishing
  tile edges for each individual shelf opening) cost roughly **≈10,000
  RUB (≈$110) per shelf** — 5 separate shelves this way total ≈50,000
  RUB (≈$560) — versus a single larger tiled niche fitted with 4 (or
  more) built-in tempered-glass shelves (functionally equivalent
  storage) at a meaningfully lower total cost. **Glass-safety detail**:
  the glass used is a tempered/safety type, compared directly to
  automotive windshield glass — it won't cut on breakage the way
  ordinary glass would. *(The video's own stated total for the
  glass-shelf alternative is ASR-garbled in the fetched transcript — a
  music-tag placeholder replaces the actual number — so only the
  qualitative "meaningfully cheaper" comparison is extracted, not a
  precise total.)*
