## Ceiling-Specific Datum and Layout

1. **⚠️ Ceiling low-point datum procedure**: use a laser level to measure
   slab-to-floor deviation at every corner/edge of the room (worked
   example: corners measured 0.5cm, 1cm, 1cm, and 2-2.3cm out of level),
   identify the single lowest point, then **drop 9-10cm below that
   lowest point** and snap a level horizontal chalk line around the
   entire room at that height — this sets the frame's actual height
   budget for the whole room, not an assumed uniform slab height.
2. **Profile/hanger layout rule**: first row of vibro-hangers/profiles
   at **15cm** from the wall, then subsequent rows spaced **60cm** apart;
   vertical spacing between hangers along a row: **80-90cm** (matching
   the manufacturer spec video 7's crew chose to tighten on that job).

## The Decoupling Mechanism — Temporary, Removable Perimeter Anchoring

3. **⚠️ The single most important mechanism-level fact in this pair of
   videos, not previously documented on this vault's `Soundproofing.md`**:
   the perimeter guide track is fastened with only **3 anchors per
   length** (roughly one every 0.5m, per ACUSTI Group's own
   recommendation) — and, deliberately, **not driven fully home**,
   leaving the anchor slightly proud so it can be **physically removed
   later**. Once the vibro-hanger system is fully installed and the
   frame is self-supporting from the hangers alone, **every one of these
   temporary anchors is pulled back out**, along with any track section
   that no longer serves a structural purpose — eliminating any direct
   rigid connection between the new ceiling frame and the structural
   walls/slab. This is the concrete construction step that actually
   achieves the "no direct coupling to the structure" acoustic
   independence this page's existing taxonomy describes only abstractly
   — the perimeter track's real function is positioning the frame during
   assembly, not permanent structural support.
4. **Fastener substitution for soft exterior-wall material**: this
   building's exterior walls are a soft masonry material (cinder/foam
   block); the crew used coarse-thread wood screws instead of standard
   anchor/dowel nails for the *temporary* track fixation specifically
   because they hold adequately in soft material and are easier to pull
   back out later.

## Two-Tier Grid Construction and a Real Fit Tradeoff

5. **Two-tier connector system**: a first layer of profiles is crossed
   by a second perpendicular layer, joined with dedicated "two-level
   connectors" ("двухуровневый соединитель") — building a genuine
   two-layer perpendicular grid, not a single-layer frame.
6. **⚠️ A real, candidly documented fit/tradeoff problem**: on this job,
   the second-layer connectors fit poorly at the crossing points,
   requiring extra screws as a fix. The crew explains the better original
   layout choice would have offset the seam to the middle of a grid cell
   instead — but that would have required shifting/cutting the whole
   layout and increased material waste, so they accepted the poorer fit
   and remediated it with extra fasteners instead. A genuine cost-vs-fit
   tradeoff shown in practice, not glossed over as a clean process.
7. **Leveling technique for a profile row**: fix only the row's middle
   profile at both ends to the already-leveled track, stretch a string
   line between those two fixed points, then adjust every other profile
   in the row until it sits within ~1mm of the string along its full
   length before final-fixing to the hangers.

## Hardware, Material, and Logistics Detail

8. **Vibro-hanger type differentiation**: ceiling hangers are physically
   larger and thicker-gauge metal than wall hangers, since a ceiling
   frame's hangers must bear the entire suspended structure's load
   (frame + insulation + board), a materially higher load than a wall
   hanger carries.
9. **Акулайн drywall weight spec, quantifying video 7's qualitative
   "denser/heavier" claim**: one full sheet weighs **≈36kg** — a
   concrete number for a board variant noticeably heavier than standard
   drywall.
10. **⚠️ Logistics caution**: full-size triplex/GVL sandwich sheets did
    not fit in this building's elevator and had to be cut down before
    they could be brought up — a real practical constraint worth
    planning for before ordering full-size acoustic board sheets for a
    mid-rise building.
11. **Frame load-test demonstration**: a ~70kg crew member stood directly
    on the completed (unboarded) ceiling frame to demonstrate it holds a
    person's weight with minimal deflection — the slight give observed
    was attributed to the vibro-hanger's own soft cushioning pad
    flexing, not the frame structure itself.
12. **Insulation technical datasheet, read directly from the product's
    own included spec sheet** (not just a marketing claim): Шуманет Эко
    composition = staple fiberglass with an acrylic synthetic binder (no
    formaldehyde/phenol-based binder); manufacturer states acoustic,
    hygienic, and fire-safety certification, non-combustible rating.
