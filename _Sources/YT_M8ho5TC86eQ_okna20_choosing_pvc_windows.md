## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE TERMINOLOGY TRAP, AND IT CAUGHT THIS VAULT

**Customers ask for *«самые простые однокамерные стеклопакеты»* without knowing what a камера is. Her correction:**

> ***«Стеклопакеты — это именно стёкла, сколько камер между стёклами. В данном случае у нас двухкамерный стеклопакет, потому что вот они две камеры здесь. Когда два стекла — это получается одна камера.»***

| Term | Panes | Cavities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **однокамерный** | **2** | 1 |
| **двухкамерный** | **⚠️⚠️ 3** | 2 |
| **трёхкамерный** | **4** | 3 |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A камера is the GAP between panes, not a pane. So `двухкамерный` is a THREE-pane unit, not a double-glazed one.**
>
> **⚠️⚠️ THIS VAULT HAD IT WRONG IN ONE PLACE.** [[10_Balcony/analysis/Insulation_and_Heating|Insulation & Heating]] read **«Double-pane ("двухкамерный") glazing … is non-negotiable»** — which understates the spec by an entire pane, and it is exactly the line you would read out to a supplier. **Corrected this round.** Every other occurrence in the vault correctly says "double-chamber", so this was a single slip rather than a systematic misunderstanding — but it was in the one sentence phrased as a purchasing instruction.
>
> **⚠️ She adds the second half of the trap: камеры exist in BOTH the profile and the glass unit, and they are different things sharing a word.** **→ "70 mm five-chamber profile with a 40 mm two-chamber unit" contains two unrelated chamber counts, which is precisely the spec floor the vault carries.**

## ⚠️ 2. Profile depth, and where single-chamber units are actually allowed

- **PVC profile mounting depths: 60 / 70 / 80 mm**, differing in internal chamber count; deeper = warmer. **⚠️ *«Разница между профилями в стоимости не такая уж и большая, поэтому, выбирая профиль более толстый, вы не так уж много переплатите, но получите намного больше плюсов.»*** **→ Corroborates the vault's existing 70 mm spec floor and adds the ladder it sits on.**
- **⚠️⚠️ Single-chamber (2-pane) units are acceptable in a flat ONLY on the balcony block — the door/window between room and loggia — or on the loggia itself. On a window opening directly to outside she says it is *«крайне не рекомендуется и вообще запрещено ГОСТом»*.**
- **Busy road or highway → three-chamber (4-pane) unit**, optionally with acoustic glass or films.

> ⚠️⚠️ **REGULATORY CLAIM FROM A RUSSIAN SOURCE — flagged, and deliberately NOT routed to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`, which is Belarus-only.** **She cites no GOST number, so this is `unverified` even as a Russian claim.** **Recorded as the shape of the constraint — that single-chamber units on external windows are treated as substandard in the RU market — not as a statement about Belarusian requirements.**

## ⚠️ 3. Two component details the vault did not have

- **⚠️⚠️ SPACER BARS (дистанционные рамки) between the panes come in aluminium or plastic. Aluminium is colder; plastic conducts less, so the same unit is warmer.** **→ A "warm edge" spec item that is invisible once installed and costs little at order time. New to the vault.**
- **⚠️ GASKETS (уплотнитель): rubber, silicone, or EPDM.** Her company uses **EPDM only**, as the most durable — *«при использовании других видов уплотнителей впоследствии начинаются проблемы с продуванием»*. Colours black, white, grey. ⚠️ **Her own practice, self-interested, single-account — but a named, askable spec.**

## ⚠️ 4. Hardware as "the heart of the window"

> ***«Фурнитуру бы я назвала вообще сердцем окна, потому что это самое важное в надёжности окна.»***

Hardware = hinges, the whole perimeter locking mechanism, covers and handles. **She recommends reputable makers over cheaper Turkish analogues, with the cost argument being avoided service call-outs for repeated adjustment.**

> **→ ⚠️ [[_Sources/YT_2OCpQtu3fp8_elitbalkon_window_hardware_and_double_frames|ЭлитБалкон in this same batch reframes this more usefully]] — the criterion is not brand prestige but whether the brand is still represented locally, because unsupported hardware cannot be repaired and gets "married" to mismatched parts.** **Two sources agreeing that hardware matters; the second gives the better test.**

## 5. Opening types and cosmetic options

Opening types named: **глухая** (fixed), **поворотная** (turn), **поворотно-откидная** (tilt-and-turn), **откидно-сдвижная** (tilt-slide, which she associates with houses or large loggia exits), and plain **раздвижные**.

Cosmetics: lamination inside, outside or both, including wood-effect and RAL-type colours; gasket colour; hinge-cover and handle colours; and **⚠️ sash and glazing-bead (штапик) profiles can be rectangular, semicircular or shaped** — she notes few people know a sash can be made semicircular. ⚠️ **Recorded as options, not findings.**
