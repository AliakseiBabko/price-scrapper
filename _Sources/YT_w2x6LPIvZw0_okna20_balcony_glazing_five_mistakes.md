## ⚠️ 3. Five glazing installation defects

1. **⚠️ Canopy (козырёк) pitched the wrong way.** It must slope OUTWARD so rain and snow run to the street — **if water reaches the mounting seam (монтажный шов) you get leaks and mould.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ Heavy warm PVC glazing on a standard outrigger slab** — see §1.
3. **⚠️ New glazing on an OLD parapet.** If the existing parapet is loose or decayed, the frame will skew *(«раму может повести, перекосит»)* and the glazing will not last. **Inspect the parapet's condition before ordering.**
4. **⚠️⚠️ Exposed foam.** All mounting seams must be closed from outside with **нащельники** (cover strips) that seal the foam against UV and moisture. **Otherwise the foam turns orange, breaks down, and then leaks and draughts** — *«готова поспорить, вы, идя по улице, по-любому видели такие балконы, на которых торчит отвратительная рыжая пена»*. **→ A visible, checkable defect you can spot on other people's balconies before it is yours.**
5. **⚠️ П-shaped and bay (эркерные) balconies: corner junctions between frames must use purpose-made 90° or bay CONNECTORS, not foam plus a cover strip.** Some firms simply foam the joint. Proper connectors butt and seal against both frames and give rigidity against draughts and leaks.

## ⚠️ 4. Sash choices — two geometric traps

- **Sliding sashes are cheaper than tilt-and-turn**, so use them where you do not need the function. **⚠️ BUT arrange the opening sashes so you can actually REACH THE GLASS TO WASH IT** — too few openers makes cleaning very awkward.
- **⚠️⚠️ Sash WIDTH must be checked against balcony DEPTH.** Her real case: a customer wanted 1 m sashes; opened inward, they struck the back wall, **damaging both the wall finish and the sash itself.** **→ A geometric constraint that is invisible on an elevation drawing and obvious only in plan.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Specify extension profiles NOW even if insulating LATER

> ***«Если вы сейчас не планируете сразу после остекления утепление либо отделку — задумайтесь об этом наперёд и предусмотрите заранее специальные доборные профиля. Об этом у вас должен обязательно уточнить замерщик при замере.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ INDEPENDENTLY CORROBORATED IN THIS SAME BATCH, and [[_Sources/YT_gRQaOBGDBa4_elitbalkon_balcony_thermal_envelope|ЭлитБалкон supplies the mechanism she omits]]: without extension profiles the later finish overlaps the glazing bead or the glass unit, and a cracked unit then cannot be replaced without demolishing the finish.** **Two unrelated companies, same component, and together they make a complete argument.**
>
> **→ This is the cheapest decision in the whole batch and it is made at measurement time or not at all.**
