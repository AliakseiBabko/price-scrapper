## Ostendorf (Максим, technical specialist) — soil and waste pipe

**⚠️ The best answer in the whole video, and it corrects a common belief:**

> **Q: casting a sewer socket joint into a wall or screed — is extra sealing needed, or is the factory seal enough?**
> **A: for tightness, nothing extra at all.** The reason to tape the joint is different: **to stop cement laitance («цементное молочко») getting into the socket, where it damages the sealing ring or gasket.** Any tape will do. **And it applies only to cement-based mixes — «если гипсом, то всё нормально».** — Максим, Ostendorf

That reframes a step most people perform for the wrong reason, and it tells you when
to skip it.

**⚠️ Why low-noise ("white") soil pipe is actually quieter — no additive mystery:**

- «Никаких нанотехнологий» — the mechanism is **wall thickness plus material density**.
- **Wall thickness comparable to SML cast iron: 5.3 mm on DN110**, and **the fittings carry the same thickness as the pipe**, not just the barrel.
- **Mineralised, higher-density polypropylene** — mineral additives raise stiffness and density, so the same nominal wall is heavier and stiffer than a lookalike. Stated stiffness class **SN16**, which he says is stiffer than even the KG2000 external system, and it is specified for indoor use precisely to guarantee the acoustic result.

**⚠️ Transition from jointless (безраструбный) SML cast iron to plastic** — he calls the SN system «одна-единственная альтернатива»: it **matches the SML outside diameter, so no adapters are needed** — cut, butt the pipe against the cast iron and clamp with the wide **CV clamp already sold in the cast-iron range**.

**⚠️ Socket restorer («насадная муфта»), and this is the tool behind the 5 cm gained in his own П-44Т job:** restores a socket on a plain cut length of pipe — for a broken socket, or **when the stack has to be dropped and there is no access to the neighbour above**. Against the more familiar **insert coupling («вставная муфта»)**:

| | Socket restorer (насадная) | Insert coupling (вставная) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Internal bore | **not reduced** | reduced |
| Diameters | **DN50 – DN200** | **DN110 only** |
| Installation height | **twice as tall** | lower |

Rated to **2 atm**, «учитывая, что система проточная безнапорная, этого достаточно». Works on horizontal runs too, and can be set lower by breaking out screed. **⚠️ Honest hedge recorded**: Василий suggests bore reduction causes noise; the rep declines the framing and says the real issue is **throughput** — a DN110 main riser «по-хорошему таким и оставить», because there are norms and tolerances on it — **and that these are repair components, used «когда нет другого выбора».** See the height-gain arithmetic in [[_Sources/YT_z0hcMY5PYoc_sanuzel_pik_cabin_prep_works|the ПИК prep-works video]].

**Flash/seam («облой») along a pipe or fitting — is it a defect?** Answer: it occurs on **injection-moulded fittings**, and **yes, it is a defect the manufacturer must control**, because a professional installer should not be spending time trimming it. `vendor claim` for the rest: German thermoplastic machines, continuous QC, flash present on «3–5%, а может и меньше», nothing prepared or cleaned up before being sent to exhibitions. **The transferable part is the acceptance criterion — visible flash is a defect, not normal** — not the percentage.

`vendor claim`: sealing rings are a proprietary compound, **SBR fitted as standard, oil-resistant NBR available separately**; **stated service life 50–100 years** — «от 50 лет» for the base HT/KG systems, higher for premium SN/KG2000. Василий adds that he has visited the factory and seen the QC, which is a disclosure, not a verification.

## Elson (Сергей Васильев) — pipe, fittings, manifolds

- **⚠️ Axial PEX: the coil memory that bends the pipe off the press axis does not matter.** The conditions given are specific — **the cut must be square (90°) and clean, no burrs or nicks, and the sleeve drawn correctly**. If those hold, the residual bend «не влияет на соединение никак» and «если вы сделали опрессовку, это навсегда». Answers a real installer worry with a checkable condition rather than reassurance.
- **⚠️ Euroconus across manufacturers — and note what the reason turns out to be.** He says mixing «теоретически… в умелых руках, скорее всего, будет работать», then recommends single-manufacturer anyway. **The reason he actually gives is the warranty: they warrant the joint for 5 years, and only on their own complete assembly.** `tier-steering, but disclosed` — recording the mono-brand rule as a **liability boundary rather than an incompatibility** is what makes it usable.
- **PP versus PEX**: PP comes in sticks up to 4 m, so **every turn is another welded fitting**; PEX runs manifold-to-fixture with **two connection points**, and the cross-linking gives it memory and better tolerance of temperature and pressure loading. Both need fixing; **PP needs more of it**. Василий draws the conclusion out loud — more joints, more chances to get one wrong — and the rep agrees. **Corroborates the existing PEX case in this vault from a manufacturer's mouth.**
- **⚠️ Why pipes burst now, and it is not corrosion**: either **operating parameters exceeded** — PEX takes **18–20 bar** short-term, essentially never reached domestically, and «если системы собраны правильно, это должно быть исключено» by the safety fittings — or, far more commonly, **mechanical damage by another trade after the pipes are laid**: «заходят бригады, там плиточники или ещё кто-то, и кто-то нечаянно уронил молоток, просверлил или порез сделал». Василий's own framing, offered and not contradicted: **a burst pipe is «мнение из прошлого»**, from steel corroding at the cut thread where the wall is thinnest.
- **⚠️ What a manifold actually prevents, as a specific scenario**: the dishwasher starts, takes cold water, **cold flow to the shower head drops and the shower goes hot**. Corroborates the manifold's pressure-isolation rationale already held in this vault with a concrete failure the user would recognise.
- **Brass versus bronze — and a third option**: **bronze carries a hygiene certificate** and is fully suitable for potable water, «но у бронзовых фитингов очень высокая цена». Their answer is **tin-plated fittings and modular manifolds** — protecting against internal and external corrosion and holding the hygiene certificate at lower cost. `vendor claim` on equivalence («не уступает бронзе»); **the decision axis — potable-water hygiene certification, not just corrosion — is the reusable part.** Note the same practitioner independently bought **bronze** union halves in [[_Sources/YT_M8EyOCrm0tw_sanuzel_water_inlet_unit_assembly|his own inlet node]].
- The banya question («installation frame in an unheated banya in winter — how to keep it from freezing?») got the only genuinely null answer: **no ready solution exists; either heat the room or winterise it completely** — glycol for pipework, but **equipment that holds water must be in a heated space.** Василий says so himself: «в целом-то ответ очевидный, но я должен был его задать, потому что это ваш вопрос».

## Русteplopol (Алексей Гордеев) — structural profile

**This is the profile system Василий used for the false wall in [[_Sources/YT_z0hcMY5PYoc_sanuzel_pik_cabin_prep_works|the ПИК job]]**, so the two sources connect.

- **⚠️ The cost question got a weak answer and it should be recorded as weak.** Asked what a metre of profile costs to make and what would make it three or four times cheaper, the answer was **«покупайте больше»** — volume, logistics and warehousing. No cost figure was given at any point.
- **Any manufacturer's installation frame can be mounted to it** — and the brackets attach **to any plane, not only to a frame**: «не столько на инсталляцию, сколько просто на любую плоскость».
- **⚠️ Against Knauf profile, the claim is load**: «на Кнауф не повесишь… этому профилю можно доверить то, чего нормальный человек в здравом смысле ни в коем случае не доверит Кнауф». With the system's **inserts («закладные») corner-jointed into the profile rib and backed by good glued plywood**, he claims a partition can carry **a bath grab rail, or even a pull-up bar**. `vendor claim`, but a specific and testable one, and it matches the independent capability gap this vault already records for Knauf-based boxing.
- **DIY take-off**: a downloadable **«альбом технических решений»** on every product page. `vendor claim` that rigid fixing makes the angle square regardless of error.

## Fastener manufacturer (Алексей) — mounting systems

- **⚠️ The load question they actually ask is about the person, not the fixture.** For a wall-hung WC frame: **who will use it?** «Большой крупный мужчина 150 кг веса» → **two fixing points, upper and lower**; an ordinary household → the classic solution, since **dynamic load on sitting stays under about 200 kg**. A user-based sizing heuristic, and it engages the frame load ratings this vault already holds.
- **⚠️ Chemical anchor in aerated concrete**: their position is that anything can be fixed to anything short of air. **In good, dry D500 aerated concrete, up to 200 kg** — a boiler or a storage heater. `vendor claim`, but a specific figure with the substrate quality stated as a condition. Their own framing: what matters to them is **not what you are fixing but what it weighs**.
- **⚠️ Replacing a cast-iron stack with polymer without disturbing the existing fixings**: use a **clamp on a doubled profile that allows lateral shift**, or set a console **beside** the pipe — «точки фиксации к самой стене у вас не будут нарушены… не насквозь этой трубы, а чуть-чуть левее». The reusable idea: **fix alongside the old line rather than through it**, so the old cast iron's weight stops bearing on the new plastic.
- **Range consolidation as error insurance**: roughly six interchangeable element types — profile, console, quick-mount bolt, clamps — «по сути, в руках находится Lego». The stated benefit is not price but **procurement error minimisation**: fewer distinct SKUs to get wrong, and a wrongly-ordered console or profile is still usable.
- Reference anecdote: Лахта Center, St Petersburg — a **300 m** vertical pipe run with about **10 t** at the bottom fixing point. No transferable content.
