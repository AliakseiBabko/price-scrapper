**[[03_Kitchen/analysis/Cabinet_Assembly_Technique|the CAPPUCCINO acceptance checklist]] closes by stating its own limit**: every front material has a delayed failure mode — film delaminates, paint sinks, glass unsticks, wood cracks — **and none of them is visible at handover.** *«На первый взгляд, после установки кухни всё может быть хорошо, но со временем…»*

> **⚠️⚠️ THIS SOURCE IS THE OTHER END OF THAT GAP.** The maker walks a kitchen **his own shop built nine years earlier** and reports what actually happened to it. **Nothing else in this vault observes a kitchen after years of use.**

⚠️ **And it is not shaped the way the title implies.** **The acceptance-checklist half is the LARGER half** — he uses the nine-year-old kitchen as the specimen for *«на что бы я обратил внимание, когда принимал кухонный гарнитур после установки»*. **That complements the CAPPUCCINO list rather than repeating it: that one is what arrives on a lorry, this one is what to check after the fitters have gone.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. What nine years actually did — and the failure point is not where you would look

| Location | Condition after 9 years |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Under the sink** | ⚠️ **Dry, and the carcass had not delaminated** — *«корпус сделан PUR клеем, поэтому здесь ничего у нас не расклеилось… здесь вообще сухо»* |
| **⚠️⚠️ The евро запил — the countertop mitre joint** | ***«Основное место — не место под мойкой, а ЕВРОЗАПИЛ, это стыковка столешницы»*** — slight **вспучивание** (swelling), which he calls negligible |
| **The postforming plastic countertop over the dishwasher** | *«9 лет — всё идеально, пластиковая столешница в идеальном состоянии»* |
| **Blum lift mechanisms (3), soft-close** | *«9 лет эксплуатации — доводка»* — still closing softly |
| **Drawer boxes, hidden box inside** | *«Всё чётко работает 9 лет, никаких проблем»* |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE SINGLE MOST USEFUL SENTENCE IN THE SOURCE: THE WET-RISK LOCATION IS THE COUNTERTOP JOINT, NOT THE SINK CABINET.** **Everyone protects under the sink. The joint is where water actually gets into the substrate**, and it is a location with a name you can put in an order — *евро запил*.
>
> ⚠️ **A sample of ONE kitchen, his own, that he says was carefully used** (*«аккуратно очень кухню эксплуатировали»*). **Nine-year survival here is evidence that this construction CAN last nine years, not that it typically does.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. «Крест на фасаде» — a one-glance geometric gate

Check **horizontals** across the whole run (both above and below the cabinets), then **verticals** — and where four fronts meet, the joint must read as a **true cross**: *«идеальный крест на фасаде. Вот этот крест раз, крест два.»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS THE MISSING TEST FOR A DEFECT THE VAULT ALREADY RECORDS.** **CAPPUCCINO diagnosed uneven gaps as carcass diagonals that do not match, from an uncalibrated saw — a cause with no field test attached.** **This is the field test**: sight the crosses. **A whole-run property with a defined pass state, checkable in seconds, by eye.**
>
> ⚠️ **It is the same shape as the geometry gates this project builds for itself** — a visual invariant with an unambiguous pass condition, which is exactly what `18_Digital_Toolchain/` records as the form a good check takes.

**He also names what produces the pass state:** the horizontals are *«идеально выровнены после монтажа»* — **alignment is an INSTALLATION adjustment**, not only a manufacturing property.

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Hardware authentication — where to actually look

**The vault already records from CAPPUCCINO that genuine Blum carries a factory engraving. This says where the marking is on each component:**

- **Lift mechanisms**: branded caps/заглушки — *«вот здесь написано Blum»*.
- **⚠️⚠️ HINGES ARE MARKED INSIDE THE CUP** — *«петли маркируются в чашечке»*. **On this kitchen: Hettich.**
- **Drawer boxes**: also marked — Hettich here.

> **→ A materially more useful instruction than "check the brand", because the cup is hidden until the door is open and nobody looks there.**

**⚠️ And the specification-compliance check that goes with it:** *«если в спецификации в договоре указаны ящики с рейлингами, ящики с разделителями — они все должны быть укомплектованы… потому что такие ящики дороже, чем обычные боксы без рейлингов»*. **→ Open every drawer against the contract line by line. The upgrade you paid for is the one quietly omitted.**

## ⚠️ 4. Six more checks, each doable in minutes

| Check | What he says |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Inside the cabinets, at the exposed edges** | *«Никаких сколов не должно быть… кромочка идеально приклеена — значит, работал хороший автоматический кромочник и распиловочный центр»*. **⚠️ The interior edge is the tell for the MACHINE, and therefore for the shop** |
| **⚠️⚠️ The plinth (цоколь)** | Aluminium or plastic — no issue. **ЛДСП plinth — a silicone seal underneath is MANDATORY**, *«потому что основа ДСП»* and a spill destroys it |
| **Countertop-to-wall junction** | Here the countertop was run **under the tile** with a dedicated aluminium trim — *«и здесь силикона никакого нету»*. **A detail that has to be decided before the tiling** |
| **⚠️ Over the dishwasher** | A **термошов** on silicone where the postforming plastic meets the chipboard, **plus a metal protective strip — mandatory above a dishwasher**. *«Посмотрите, 9 лет — всё идеально»* |
| **⚠️ Countertop provenance** | *«Снизу любая столешница фабричного качества, промышленное производство, маркируется»* — brand and producer printed on the underside. **→ A provenance check anyone can do with a torch, and the answer to "is this really the countertop I ordered"** |
| **Corner planks + every front** | Corner planks keep drawers from fouling at 90°; then open **every** front and check nothing catches, scrapes or binds |

## ⚠️ 5. The facade tell: «строчка»

Fronts here were **acrylic plastic on an Austrian substrate** (⚠️ **the brand is garbled in the ASR as «seen as an» — most plausibly Senosan, and NOT recorded as confirmed**).

> *«Самый главный [показатель] — не сам пластик, наклеен обычно всё идеально… а как облицована КРОМКА, то есть торцы. Не должно быть большого шва, или как это называется в мебельном бизнесе — СТРОЧКА на фасаде.»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIRD INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL, ACROSS THREE SOURCES, AT THE SAME PLACE: THE EDGE IS WHERE A FRONT IS JUDGED, NOT THE FACE.** He names two consequences — **moisture resistance** and appearance. **CAPPUCCINO made the edge a health item; this makes it the quality tell on the visible face too.**
