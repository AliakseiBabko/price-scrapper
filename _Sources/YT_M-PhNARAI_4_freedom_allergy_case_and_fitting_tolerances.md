## ⚠️⚠️ 1. A DUST AND ALLERGY CASE — and it appears to contradict this same channel

> **A client's report, relayed:** ***«От стационарной кровати мусора намного больше, чем от шкафа-кровати.»***
>
> **And the maker's own household, which is the concrete part:** his son has a pollen allergy, and they were told to **damp-dust under the bed daily and put the pillows and duvet away** so airborne pollen does not settle on the bedding. *«Тоже это было бы проблемно, если бы у меня не стояла шкаф-кровать… у меня у детей шкаф-кровати стоят.»* **Under a wall bed you simply lift it — *«под ней очень просто сделать влажную уборочку»* — and the bedding is not left out on the bed at all.**

> **⚠️⚠️ SET THIS AGAINST [[_Sources/YT_qMGSfO-glvc_freedom_wallbed_drawbacks_and_struts|THE SAME CHANNEL'S]] BEST-KNOWN FINDING**, that a wall bed *«работает как большой пылесос»*, drawing floor dust up into the cabinet and needing two people to clean inside. **On its face the two pull opposite ways, and it is the same maker.**
>
> **→ THEY RECONCILE, AND THE RECONCILIATION IS THE USEFUL PART.** **The dust a wall bed collects is ACCESSIBLE — lift the bed, wipe the floor. The dust under a static bed is PERMANENT, because nobody moves a bed daily.** **Same argument [[_Sources/YT_ztubNCfTyhg_kachanova_seven_transformer_households|the household a year in]] made about a fitted wardrobe: the comparison is never against a clean floor, it is against a floor nobody can reach.**
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ WHAT IS GENUINELY NEW IS THE ALLERGY USE CASE, and it is a health-adjacent reason this vault did not hold**: bedding stored INSIDE a closed cabinet by day is not exposed to airborne pollen or settling dust. **For a household with an allergic child that is a functional argument, not a tidiness one.** ⚠️ **It is a maker's account of his own family and of one client, with no clinical source behind it — tagged `unverified`, and recorded as a consideration to raise rather than a medical claim.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. THREE FITTING TOLERANCES, WITH NUMBERS — the most transferable part

**He names the defects this project had and how each was absorbed:**

| Defect found | How it was taken up |
| :--- | :--- |
| **⚠️⚠️ Floor fall of 1 cm across the bed's width** | **One side of the bed was built taller and the other 1 cm shorter.** *«Визуально это не видно — кровать стоит ровно.»* |
| **⚠️ Wall not flat** (a beam-built house, силикатная/солитовая wall only partly levelled) | **Side panels SCRIBED to the wall on both sides** — *«подогнали к стене, кровать идеально»* |
| **Ceiling** | **A 1 cm shadow gap to a stretch ceiling** — *«минимум расстояние до потолка, там как теневой зазор у нас получился сантиметровый»* |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE 1 cm FLOOR FALL IS THE NUMBER WORTH KEEPING.** [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Installation_and_Daily_Use|The installation page]] records that every unit goes on adjustable feet *«чтобы нивелировать все неровности пола»*. **This says what magnitude gets absorbed in practice — about 10 mm across a bed — and that it is taken up in the CARCASS, not just the feet.** **→ Against this project's ±50 mm nominal build tolerance, a 10 mm floor fall under a fitted unit is ordinary and expected, not a defect to chase.**
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ AND THE 1 cm CEILING GAP CLOSES A LOOSE END.** The vault records Progress Design's **5 cm technical gap** (the unit is assembled flat and tilted up) against FREE_DOM's *«всегда до потолка»*. **Here is the finished result from the second maker: a 1 cm SHADOW GAP under a stretch ceiling.** **So the 5 cm is confirmed as a FITTING allowance, and the delivered reveal can be ~1 cm — read as a deliberate shadow line rather than a leftover.** ⚠️ Note it was achieved under a **stretch** ceiling, which is itself installed after and can be tensioned to the unit.

## ⚠️ 3. Retention, restated with the mechanism named

> **No straps at all, and the reason is a detail rather than a principle**: *«мы не используем никаких ремней. Можно использовать ОБЫЧНУЮ ПРОСТЫНЬ, потому что у нас есть боковая — сюда заправляется простынь, и всё, она держится.»*

> **→ ⚠️ Same maker, same position as already recorded, so NOT a new voice on the open four-way retention question — but this is the first time the mechanism is stated plainly: a side rail deep enough that a normal tucked sheet holds the bedding.** ⚠️ **Note the tension with [[_Sources/YT_cZNP6TaUyCU_freedom_wallbed_installation_and_two_layouts|his own strapped project]], where he fitted straps because there was nowhere for a bedding drawer. His objection to straps is practical, and he abandons it under constraint.**

## ⚠️ 4. On the "daily exercise" objection, and who he says manages it

> **Responding to comments that raising and lowering a bed daily is *«достаточно трудоёмкое занятие»*:** his beds are **lighter than metal-framed ones**, so it is easy, and *«у нас есть клиенты — и пенсионерки заказывают, вот они с удовольствием пользуются»*.

> **→ ⚠️ A sales answer to a real objection, and the mechanism behind it is already recorded here: [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|a lighter base loads the struts less]], which is the same reason he uses glued plywood rather than metal.** **The pensioner claim is unverified and uncounted; the construction reason is the part that transfers.**
