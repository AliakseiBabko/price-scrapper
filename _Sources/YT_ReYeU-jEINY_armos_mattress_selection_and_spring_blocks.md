## ⚠️⚠️ 1. The three-part model — and why the spring block cannot do the fine work

**A mattress is three things, and they determine its cost, character and comfort level:**

| Layer | What it actually does |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Пружинный блок** (spring unit) | **COARSE adjustment to the main anatomical contours only** |
| **Настилочные / комфортные слои** (comfort layers) | **The FINE adjustment — точечная эластичность** — conform to the body and distribute load down into the springs |
| **Чехол** (cover) | On a good mattress **a thin mattress in its own right** |

> ***«Сколько бы ни было пружин на квадратный метр, всё равно каждая из них ДИСКРЕТНА — они не обеспечивают плавной подстройки, высокой точечной эластичности.»***

- **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS THE WHOLE ARGUMENT AGAINST BUYING ON SPRING COUNT, and it is a physical one rather than a preference.** **A spring is small-diameter wire; it will press on soft tissue whatever its neighbours do.** **Settling layers, isolators and a cover are therefore NOT optional extras — they are what makes the spring block usable.**
- **⚠️⚠️ THE COVER IS NOT TRIM.** On a good mattress it is synthetic volumetric material quilted onto soft foam, and its stated job is that **soft tissue is not compressed and microcirculation is not disturbed — with the consequence that you turn over less during the night.** ⚠️ **He flags this specifically for online buyers comparing models on spring height and layer composition, because the cover is invisible in that comparison.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Spring blocks, with three samples on camera

| Block | Density | Height | Note |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Зависимый / боннель** | — | — | *«Не отвечает современным требованиям»*. ⚠️⚠️ **And the real objection is asymmetric wear: the metal interconnections accelerate abrasion of the settling layers, so the MATTRESS wears out while the block survives** — *«пружинному блоку ничего не будет, но сам матрас будет изношен»* |
| **Independent, 256/m²** | **256 springs/m²** | **14 cm** | ***«Золотой стандарт отрасли»*** — the sales leader |
| **Independent, 512/m²** | **512 springs/m²** | **14 cm** | **Double the count, but SMALLER WIRE DIAMETER** |
| **Independent, honeycomb** | **290 springs/m²** | — | Same wire diameter as the 256, packed in a denser «сотовая» structure |

- **⚠️⚠️ MORE SPRINGS IS NOT BETTER, AND THE TRADE IS NAMED: a higher count comes with thinner wire.** **→ 512/m² is not "twice as good as" 256/m²; it is a different compromise.**
- **⚠️⚠️ THE PARADOX WORTH CARRYING: *«чем лучше матрас, чем дольше он служит, тем ТОЛЩЕ и массивнее будет слой-изолятор»*.** Isolator layers (felt, thermally-bonded felt, synthetic бекатон/фарплит types) protect against abrasion **and spread a load across several springs instead of one.** **→ A thick isolator is a quality signal, not padding.**
- **⚠️ What is hidden from the buyer**: the wire's own characteristics, the pocket (stakan) material and how fast it wears, whether the springs are joined **by hand or by machine**. ⚠️ **His answer — buy from a maker who makes its own blocks — is where the argument becomes a pitch.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Load capacity, and how it silently changes the firmness you feel

**Нагрузочная способность is quoted per sleeping place** and depends on construction, spring type and **wire thickness**. *«Всегда нужно выбирать с запасом.»*

> **⚠️⚠️ AND THE MECHANISM THAT MAKES IT MORE THAN A SAFETY NUMBER:** load a mattress at or above its rated capacity and **it will not feel like its stated firmness.** *«Для пользователя 100 кг матрас, заявленный с нагрузочной способностью 90 кг и как жёсткий, скорее всего не будет казаться жёстким.»* **And it will wear out much faster — *«возможно даже раньше, чем минимальный гарантийный срок»*.**
>
> **→ A heavier person on an under-rated mattress gets BOTH the wrong feel AND early failure, and will most likely blame the firmness label rather than the rating.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. The five numbers

1. **⚠️⚠️ SIZE: measure the bed's INTERNAL opening and round DOWN.** Measure 162 cm → buy **160 cm**. Measure 202 → buy **200**. **The gap is needed to tuck bedding, fit a protector, and — on a lift-mechanism bed — to stop the mattress catching the sides.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ HEIGHT (bed + mattress together): the bend of your knee, ± a palm's width.** *«Тогда вам будет удобно ложиться и удобно вставать.»* **A low bed with a low mattress is specifically named as hard for older people.**
3. **⚠️⚠️ LAMELLA SPACING: not more than 8 cm** (pt3 of the base source gives 7–8), on a base that is firm, flat and free of protruding or sharp elements.
4. **A decorative headboard element caps mattress height** — measure from the base to its lower edge before choosing.
5. **Mattress height tracks the amount of filling**, and more filling means more universal, more durable, higher load capacity and dearer.

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Firmness — the most useful passage, and it undercuts every label

> ***«Эта величина СУБЪЕКТИВНО ВОСПРИНИМАЕМАЯ. Также НЕТ НИКАКИХ ЕДИНЫХ НОРМ, ПРАВИЛ И СТАНДАРТОВ. Поэтому производители могут обозначать свои матрасы по-разному: матрас средней жёсткости одного производителя будет вам казаться мягким, а жёсткий другого — средней жёсткости.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ FIRMNESS LABELS ARE NOT COMPARABLE BETWEEN MANUFACTURERS. That is a cross-vendor warning issued by a vendor, and it is the single most valuable sentence on the channel.** **It means an online comparison on "medium firm" is comparing nothing.**

**Perceived firmness depends on the cover, the cover's filling, the comfort layers, their characteristics, their combination and even their ORDER, plus the spring block.**

**His heuristics** — ⚠️ recorded as heuristics, with no evidence offered:

| :--- | :--- |
| Middle-aged; sleeps on back | Medium |

| Heavy build | Firm |

## ⚠️⚠️ 6. The in-store test — a procedure with a duration and a pass condition

> **Lie in YOUR usual sleeping position for TEN MINUTES.** *«Если действительно глазки стали закрываться, если не появилось желания перевернуться, не появилось болевых ощущений — это ваш матрас.»*
>
> **And the converse is the operative half:** *«Если эти ощущения появились, значит, дома они будут такими же самыми.»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A FALSIFIABLE IN-SHOP TEST WITH A STATED DURATION, WHICH IS RARE.** **It also quietly defeats the firmness-label problem in §5: you are testing the object, not its description.** ⚠️ **And it is the one recommendation that costs the seller floor time rather than earning a margin.**
