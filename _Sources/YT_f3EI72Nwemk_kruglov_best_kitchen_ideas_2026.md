## Genuinely New Content

1. **Food-waste disposer/grinder (диспоузер/"dispor"), a device not previously documented on this store's kitchen pages**: installs directly under the sink drain and grinds food waste that would otherwise go into the trash. **Benefits**: eliminates the odor buildup from trash sitting for a day or two before disposal; reduces sewage-clog risk (waste is ground fine rather than going in whole); genuinely useful during vegetable prep (peelings go straight down the drain); modern units run quietly. **Activation**: a water-flow sensor triggers grinding automatically, with a manual override button to force it off.
2. **⚠️ Lift-up (vs. hinged) upper-cabinet facade mechanism, an ergonomic case specifically for the sink/cooktop zone**: hinged upper facades in the sink or cooktop zone are a real head-collision hazard when left open during active use — the source cites his own 183cm height as a concrete reason this affects him personally; a lift-up mechanism instead raises the facade up and out of the way, letting the cook use the sink/cooktop and immediately put dishes to dry without ducking around an open hinged door. **Named premium-tier brand: Blum**, described as offering adjustable, low-effort lift mechanisms.
3. **Undermount sink compatibility caution**: undermount (under-countertop) sink installation is not universally compatible with every countertop material/type — confirm compatibility before committing to the sink choice, not after. **Benefit restated**: crumbs/debris can be swept directly off the counter edge into the sink with no rim to catch and need separate cleaning — an aesthetically "always clean" result, at a real cost premium.
4. **Toe-kick (plinth) pull-out drawer under the oven, for oversized bakeware**: holds baking trays, oven racks, and other oversized cookware that would otherwise clutter the oven cavity itself before each use — frees the oven interior from needing everything removed and re-stacked each time it's used.
5. **⚠️ Pull-out trash bin mounted on drawer rails, with named failure mode it solves**: a fixed/stationary bin under the sink causes trash to miss the bin and fall behind/beside it, especially in a household with children — a rail-mounted pull-out bin (extended, used, then pushed back) eliminates this specific miss-and-mess failure mode; the lid can be set to auto-open tied to the cabinet door's own opening motion; multi-compartment versions exist for households that sort waste by type.
6. **Pull-out spice/bottle rack ("bottle module"), sizing detail**: typical width **15-20cm**, sized for spices/oils/condiments needed within reach during cooking — conventionally placed adjacent to the cooktop.
7. **Drawer-organizer/insert system, a material-consistency recommendation**: many types exist for cutlery, cups, plates, pots/pans, in plastic/metal/wood — the source's own recommendation is to keep all organizer inserts in one consistent material/style throughout the kitchen rather than mixing types drawer-to-drawer.
