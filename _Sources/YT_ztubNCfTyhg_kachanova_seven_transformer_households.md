# Source Note - Four households living with a wall bed: ⚠️⚠️ the daily-folding question answered, and the sofa-bed seam named again (YouTube ztubNCfTyhg +3)

## Evidence levels
(1) transcript text - (2) YouTube metadata - (3) contextual inference - (4) external validation (none)

## ⚠️⚠️ Why this group matters to THIS project specifically

**[[00_Master/Bedroom_Design_Principles|Bedroom Design Principles]] records a wall-bed transformer as this household's own Phase 2 sleeping arrangement, to be INSTALLED during the current build and ACTIVATED in ~3–4 years.** **Everything the vault holds on wall beds so far comes from makers and mechanism suppliers. These are people who live with one — including one household a full year in, and one reporting several years.** **The question they answer is the one a maker cannot: what is it actually like every day.**

⚠️ **All four are region-unresolved (no city named anywhere), so no figure here clears standing rule 2 as a price benchmark.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE DAILY-FOLDING QUESTION — answered by two independent households

**Asked directly how tolerable daily folding is, a household with a young child:**

> ***«Каждый вечер мы раскладываем, мы привыкли это делать, нам не доставляет никакого труда.»*** **Their evening: wash, bathe the child, unfold, sometimes all lie on it together to read or watch something, put the child down, lights out.** **And in the morning — *«мы просыпаемся, сразу складываем кровать, и у нас приличный вид дома»*.**

**A second household, several years in, independently: the same — folded nightly and morning, no burden, *«но мы спим на хорошем ортопедическом матрасе, это было для нас важно»*.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ TWO INDEPENDENT HOUSEHOLDS, DIFFERENT VIDEOS, SAME ANSWER: the twice-daily cycle is not experienced as a chore, and both volunteer the SAME reason for accepting it — a proper mattress.** **That is the single most decision-relevant finding in the batch for a Phase 2 plan built on daily folding.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. THE SOFA-BED SEAM — a third arrival, this time from a user

**This vault already holds two practitioner positions against sofa beds:** Шеврина (*most are uncomfortable; a rolled 10 cm mattress is the better answer*) and Краснов (*the fold-seam junction is a classic source of lower-back strain*) — both on [[14_Furniture/analysis/Seating_Selection|Seating & Bed Selection]].

**Here is an owner naming the identical defect from the other side:**

> ***«До этого был диван. Ребёнок спит с родителями. В диване всегда есть какая-то граница на две части — посередине, либо поперёк, либо вдоль — то есть туда кто-то ПРОВАЛИВАЕТСЯ, это очень неудобно. Если поперёк, то спине неудобно. Здесь полноценный матрас.»***

**And two further households give the same reason unprompted: *«на диване мы себе как-то не представляли свою жизнь»*, and *«спать на диване я очень не хотела»*.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIRD INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL AT THE FOLD-SEAM PROBLEM, and the first from someone who slept on it.** **It also supplies what the vault's two practitioners did not: the ALTERNATIVE they were implicitly reaching for. Шеврина's answer was a rolled mattress for GUESTS; these households needed a nightly bed for ADULTS, and the wall bed is what takes a full mattress without a seam.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Cleaning — the maker's drawback confirmed, then reframed

**A household one year in confirms [[_Sources/YT_qMGSfO-glvc_freedom_wallbed_drawbacks_and_struts|FREE_DOM's]] two-person cleaning requirement exactly — her son or husband holds the bed while she wipes underneath.** **But she reaches the opposite conclusion about it:**

> ***«Ни один шкаф не отодвинете, чтобы протереть пыль — просто сил не хватит. А тут всё пожалуйста.»***

**She also slides the sofa out to mop, and stands on the hard armrest to wipe the top of the unit.** *«Нет таких мест, которые бы не доставала моя рука.»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ BOTH ARE TRUE AND THE RECONCILIATION IS THE FINDING: the two-person requirement is real, and the thing it is being compared against is a fitted wardrobe nobody ever cleans behind at all.** **A wall bed makes the space under it accessible on demand; a conventional unit makes it permanently inaccessible.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Effort to operate — and it is NOT uniform

| Source | Account |
| :--- | :--- |
| **FREE_DOM (maker)** | *«С этим даже справится хрупкая женщина»*; elderly clients manage easily |
| **⚠️ Вести с полей (owner, 1 yr)** | **Usually her husband raises it. Alone, she uses a SQUAT** — *«я приседаю… я же не Геракл, хрупкая девушка, но я справляюсь»* — **and notes *«можно, конечно, механизм другой заказать»*** |
| **Качанова's daughter's room** | A 20-year-old manages it: *«одно нажатие, небольшое усилие»* |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ NOT A CONTRADICTION BUT A DEPENDENCY, and it points straight at the gas-strut finding:** **perceived effort is a function of how well the strut rating is matched to that specific bed.** **See [[_Sources/YT_qMGSfO-glvc_freedom_wallbed_drawbacks_and_struts|FREE_DOM's "zero point" test]] — and note the owner herself identifies the fix as ordering a different mechanism.** **→ For this project: operating effort is a SPECIFIABLE property, not a fixed property of wall beds, and it should be tested at handover.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. A DIY household reaches the strut failure from the opposite direction

**A household that converted a kitchen into a second bedroom and BUILT their own wall bed:**

> ***«Мы немножко не рассчитали — поставили даже более мощные [газлифты], чем надо, и нам пришлось кровать УТЯЖЕЛЯТЬ ГАНТЕЛЯМИ»*** — otherwise it would not stay down, even with a person lying on it.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE SAME FAILURE FREE_DOM DESCRIBES (a 2200 N strut that lifted the bed, swapped to 2000 N), arrived at independently and fixed by adding dead weight instead.** **Together they establish the rule the vault's existing "corroborated twice" entry only gestured at: the rating must be MATCHED BY TRIAL, never maximised. Over-specifying is a failure mode in its own right.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 6. Bedding retention — two households use ELASTICS, which neither maker proposes

- **One household holds the MATTRESS on elastics (*«матрас держится на резинках»*) and does not bother strapping the duvet at all — *«нам лень каждое утро расстилать, поэтому просто поднимаем»*, with pillows stowed under the sofa and in a chest.**
- **⚠️⚠️ A DIY household copied the elastic bands from TRAIN-COMPARTMENT bunks to hold the BEDDING to the bed: *«человек встал, закрепил резинками бельё, и точно так же кровать обратно поднял»* — so nothing is stowed anywhere.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|The mechanisms page]] records straps as an unresolved two-sided manufacturer disagreement. These are a MIDDLE PATH neither side proposes, and they address exactly FREE_DOM's stated failure mode — a strap coming undone and jamming bedding between mattress and wall.** **The option space is straps / no straps / elastics on the mattress / elastics on the bedding.**

## ⚠️ 7. Details worth carrying

- **⚠️⚠️ A PET SAFETY FEATURE: the mechanism deliberately does not close fully — *«это сделано для того, чтобы не прижать лапки вашим питомцам»*.** One household also uses the sofa cushions as STEPS so a small dog can reach the bed height.
- **⚠️⚠️ AN AIRING DETENT (*«положение проветривания»*): the bed can be left part-open so the mattress ventilates, and it will not fall.** **→ Named only by the vendor testimonial, but it addresses a real concern this vault raises elsewhere — a mattress shut inside a cabinet.** ⚠️ **Vendor-produced; worth ASKING about rather than assuming.**
- **⚠️ TWO cat-owning households independently report the upholstery copes** — hair wipes off with a damp cloth, no snags in the weave; one had a plant pot spill soil on it and cleaned it dry. **⚠️ And one adds: order HARD armrests with a pet — hair does not show, they wipe clean, and you can set a laptop or a cup on them** (fabric armrests would let a phone slide off).
- **⚠️ Handleless push-open fronts stay clean**: a year in, never wiped, *«в отличие от кухонных фасадов, там постоянно залепки»* — because no handles are touched.
- **⚠️⚠️ THE SEAM ON THE FRONT: every maker runs a seam down the middle of such a bed's face, vertical or horizontal. One household forced their maker to omit it — the company resisted, claiming it broke their construction — and used edging instead. *«Очень сильно эстетически разгружает.»*** **→ A negotiable constructional default, and worth knowing it IS negotiable.**
- **⚠️ Integrated fittings recur across households**: dimmable bedside lights on both sides, wireless charging, USB ports. **⚠️ One owner's complaint: a light that does not rotate the way he wants.**
- **⚠️ The gap left between module and wall can take an IRONING BOARD** — solving where to put a flat awkward item.
- **⚠️ A free-standing sofa NOT fixed to the unit is deliberate in two households** — it can be slid aside, which matters in a studio. **⚠️ The trade-off: when unfixed you must HOLD it while folding — *«это единственное неудобство»*.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 8. And a household plan that mirrors this project's own

**Asked whether she would rather have a separate bedroom, one owner answers she would rather simply have 50 m² more — and then states her position:**

> ***«У ребёнка должна быть своя комната. Ты сам можешь как-то подстроиться под какие-то вещи, а у ребёнка должен быть свой угол, где она живёт своей жизнью. Это комфортнее в итоге и ей, и вам.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THAT IS EXACTLY THIS PROJECT'S PHASE 2 LOGIC — the adults take the folding bed in the shared space so the children get the rooms — reached independently by a household that has lived it.** **Recorded as corroboration of the reasoning, not as advice about this project's own decision.**

## ⚠️ 9. Prices — recorded, and NOT usable

**One owner who states he paid his own money and that the company does not know he is a blogger:** the full wall + sofa + bed system cost **210,000 RUB at purchase, around 2021, without the mattress**; checking the same maker's site at filming (2022-11-09) he found **340,000** for the same thing.

> ⚠️⚠️ **RULE 2: LOCATION IS UNRESOLVED — no city is named in any of these four videos — so the absolute figures are NOT carried as benchmarks and are recorded for provenance only.** ⚠️ **He also hedges the purchase year (*«по-моему, 21 год»*).** **What survives the location problem is the RELATIVE movement: roughly +60% on one identical product from one maker over about eighteen months, as one owner's observation.**

⚠️ **A separate household bought a sofa module for ~5,000 RUB in a closed clearance sale with ~1,000 delivery — explicitly a one-off deal on an odd size nobody else wanted, and worthless as a benchmark.**
