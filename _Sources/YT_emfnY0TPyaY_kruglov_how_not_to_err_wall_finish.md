## Genuinely New Content

- **⚠️ Three-way painting-substrate taxonomy with a cost-inversion insight, not previously distinguished on this page**: painting (a) a plain prepared bare wall, (b) paintable wallpaper ("обои под покраску"), or (c) paintable non-woven lining paper ("малярный флизелин") are three distinct approaches. The presenter's explicit recommendation is (a): **painting flizelin/paintable wallpaper actually costs *more* than painting a plain prepared wall**, since the substrate prep required is identical either way and the flizelin/wallpaper material cost is simply added on top — with the added downside that achieving invisible seams when painting over flizelin is very difficult (a micro-seam remains visible even when done well).
- **Micro-crack-visibility myth debunked for flizelin/paintable wallpaper**: contrary to a common belief that these materials hide new-build settlement micro-cracks, a crack forming behind either material doesn't tear it, but the crack still becomes visible through it — flizelin/wallpaper doesn't provide real crack camouflage, only crack-tolerance (no tearing).
- **Small-format tile (<10cm) flagged as a third genuinely on-trend porcelain-tile size option**, alongside the already-known 60-120cm "sweet spot" — distinct from old standard 20-40cm tile (explicitly out of trend) and oversized >80cm-tall slabs (expensive material, installation, and delivery, with installers charging a risk premium to handle them).
- **⚠️ Porcelain-tile-flooring pairing rule**: whenever porcelain tile is used as flooring, pair it with electric underfloor heating — described as an inexpensive addition relative to the comfort gain it provides, framed as close to mandatory rather than optional for a tile floor.
- **Kitchen application detail**: porcelain tile can be used as a full accent wall in a kitchen specifically *without* upper cabinets in front of it (rather than only as a splashback strip), for a fuller visual effect.
- **Decorative plaster's inherent uniqueness, a new characteristic claim**: because it is hand-applied by an individual craftsman using their own technique/style, no two decorative-plaster walls — even using the identical product — will look truly identical; framed as a genuine selling point (exclusivity), not a quality-control risk.
- **⚠️ Named company cost-hack: ordinary Rotband gypsum plaster used directly as a decorative-plaster substitute**, hand-applied to achieve a decorative finish look at dramatically lower material cost than a specialized decorative-plaster product — used on multiple real company projects with reportedly satisfied clients. Distinct from this round's own video 4 (`6FbZY6YHrxQ`) DIY "concrete look" technique, which used a temporary guide profile to build a raised relief texture — this is plain Rotband hand-troweled directly as the finish layer, no profile involved.
- **Panel taxonomy extension**: soft/upholstered panels used specifically as a bedroom headboard substitute, adding both coziness and acoustic damping; veneered panels, formerly seen mainly in commercial spaces, are now increasingly used in residential apartments.
- **Baguettes/moldings flagged as a declining trend with a resale-value caution**: the presenter states he would not install them if planning to sell the apartment within 10 years, given their falling popularity.
