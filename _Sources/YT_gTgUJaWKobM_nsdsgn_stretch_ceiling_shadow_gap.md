## Теневое примыкание — what it replaces and why

- The bad reputation of натяжные потолки traces to a specific component: the
  **маскировочная лента** (masking strip) around the perimeter. It is thick and
  visually heavy, **yellows over time, and sometimes falls out** — «весь ваш дорогой
  ремонт сразу же превращается непонятно во что».
- **Теневое примыкание** is a family of profiles producing a deliberate shadow gap
  between ceiling and wall, eliminating the strip. Developed **about two to three
  years before January 2023**, i.e. ~2020 — so it is a recent detail, not a long-
  established one.
- Main profile line at the time: **Слот 140 / 160 / 180**, differing in height.
- The host's own caution, worth keeping: for many designers «современный дизайн —
  это теневое примыкание потолка и теневые плинтуса, и на этом дизайн
  заканчивается». He treats it as baseline minimalist attribute, not a design.

## ⚠️ The 27 mm drop — the claim and its own correction

**This is the single most useful passage, because the host pushes back and the
qualification survives.**

- Claim: a dedicated profile line (**Slim 27**) minimises the drop to **27 mm** «в
  идеальных условиях, когда соблюдены все факторы».
- Host's objection: slabs are never ideal, and **a распределительная коробка is
  typically ~60 mm high**. How can it be 27 mm?
- **Correction accepted: the drop is counted from the lowest existing obstruction,
  not from the slab.** From that lowest point, **standard systems drop 40–60 mm**;
  the Slim 27 line drops **20–27 mm maximum**.
- **So 27 mm is a drop below the lowest obstruction.** Whether the ceiling ends up
  27 mm below the slab depends entirely on the builders — «это уже вопрос к вашим
  строителям, чтобы они максимально всё прижали».
- Comparison basis given for plasterboard: the build-up is wire, then profile, then
  **two sheets** of board — «складывается очень серьёзный пирог».

## Materials, sag and durability

- Two families: **PVC**, and **textile/тканевые**, which are **polyester** — not PVC.
  Impregnated with **polyurethane or acrylic**; he claims acrylic-impregnated is the
  most ecological, and that these are made in Europe under tight control.
  **⚠️ His ecology argument is weak and self-serving** («ванны из акрила, детскую
  одежду делают из акрила») — recorded as his claim, tagged `unverified`.
- **Natural sag (естественный провис) ≈1% of the room's diagonal** for PVC. Worked
  example given: 3 m diagonal → 20–30 mm of sag, visually undetectable.
- **⚠️ They do not sag further over time — and increased sag is a diagnostic.**
  «Со временем они не провисают… этот провис останется таким, как сделал
  изначально.» If it grows, the cause is normally a **non-airtight over-ceiling
  space**: air gets behind the fabric and inflates it. A profile named **Aircraft**
  leaves a deliberate gap at the junction so that air escapes instead.
- **Textile has higher tension and density, so sag is a few millimetres** and always
  less than PVC.
- **Yellowing: confirmed, not denied** — from smoke over time, «как любой потолок».
  They have not tried cleaning; replacing the fabric is easier.
- **Odour absorption**: PVC is a solid material and physically cannot absorb; textile
  has structure and can retain odours, but he redirects this to being a ventilation
  question about the room where you cook.
- **Repair rule**: damage **within 50–100 mm of a wall** can usually be re-cut and
  repaired in place; damage nearer the centre generally means replacing the whole
  fabric — which is tolerable precisely because of the 10–20% figure above.
- **Champagne cork does not pierce a stretch ceiling — they tested it.**

## Dimensional and clearance constraints

- **⚠️ Wardrobe door clearance, directly usable: a hinged door needs a minimum 30 mm
  between the door top and the ceiling fabric**, or the door touches the fabric as it
  swings. Stated for a 600 mm hinged door.
- **If a smaller gap is needed (10 mm, even 5 mm), run a linear system along the
  wardrobe** — a light line or track — because it supports the fabric locally and
  minimises sag there.
- **Width limits: PVC maximum 5.5 m** (some stretch to 6 m; «мы не сторонники такой
  практики»). **Textile web is 5 m wide with a usable limit of 4.90 m** because of
  installation specifics.
- **Beyond those widths there is a seam.** The recommended handling is to design
  around it rather than weld or glue: a **теневой разделитель**, a light line, a track
  system, or a **level change**. If a splice is unavoidable, ask the installers where
  it will be invisible.
- **Column problem**: fabric cannot be welded on site — it is prefabricated — so a
  free-standing column needs a dedicated profile that closes the fabric from two
  sides, leaving a visible dark seam. He met this for the first time on a **~60 m²
  living room with a single column** and notes he had never considered it before.
- Shower-curtain track detail: they **stop the system ~10 mm short of the wall** and
  cut a **ПАЗ** through which the hooks are threaded, closed with a trim.

## Integration, lead times and sequencing

- **Claim: stretch ceilings now integrate more than plasterboard does**, specifically
  low-voltage track systems, projectors, roman-blind mountings, multi-level forms.
  Profile line includes backlit profiles, track profiles, backlit lines, and
  single- and double-row curtain-track mounts.
- **Evidence offered for that claim, and it is checkable**: they built a
  **1.5 × 1.5 m inspection hatch (ревизионный люк)** into a stretch ceiling for access
  to concealed services; the plasterboard crew said a hatch that size was not
  possible in board — it would be too heavy to lift.
- **Combined slot: a ventilation diffuser and a track system in one gap**, with
  **aerodynamic and acoustic testing claimed** (his claim, `unverified`). The host's
  standing complaint is the ventilation trade's default «огромные страшные решётки»
  and his ask is a single- or double-slot grille.
- **⚠️ Lead time for the made-to-order integration items: 10–15 working days.**
- **⚠️ Sequencing rule, stated twice: «настройка начинается всегда с вентиляции».**
  Ventilation design first, then the ceiling integration is consulted against it.
- **Call the ceiling installers as early as possible — ideally at design stage.** If
  construction has begun, at the very start: walk the flat and check where power
  supplies sit, how electrics and ventilation are routed, and whether systems will
  conflict.
- **The generalised warning, which applies well beyond this product**: minimalist
  profile solutions look trivial — «просто там тёмная линия» — and are not. Discuss
  them with builders and installers **before they arrive on site**; afterwards it is
  rework. **This corroborates the builder's independent statement in
  [[YT_M0Wv4SOSUhs_nsdsgn_builder_qa_costs_and_estimates]] that теневой профиль is
  where crews are least competent.**
- **Luminaires**: buy them anywhere, but **agree the specific model with the fitters
  first**. A frameless modern fixture is installable but costs more in labour; a
  simple budget fixture does not change the labour price.

## Choosing an installer

- The market splits into **mass-market** (discount offers, free third ceiling, bundled
  fixtures, leaflets in new-build lifts) and teams **specialising in executing design
  projects** — level changes, expensive track systems.
- His selection route: ask the profile manufacturer for regional recommendations;
  failing that, check social media for whether they show their own work and how
  qualified the fitters look. **⚠️ The first step is "phone our supplier", which is
  self-serving — recorded as such.**
