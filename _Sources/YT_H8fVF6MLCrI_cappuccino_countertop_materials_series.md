## ⚠️⚠️ 1. QUARTZ AGGLOMERATE (кварцевый агломерат)

**Structure**: 94% natural quartz crumb + 5% synthetic resin (the binder) + 1% pigment. Slabs ~**3000 × 1400 mm**, thickness usually **12 or 20 mm**.

> **⚠️⚠️ THE STRUCTURAL FACT WORTH MOST: all quartz in the world is produced by ONE technology, developed and patented by the Italian company Breton.** *"Основные различия производителей сводятся лишь к цветовым решениям… и стоимости человеческих ресурсов."* **→ Brand differences in quartz are largely colour range and labour cost, not material performance.** ⚠️ **That materially changes how a quartz quote should be read.** ⚠️ He adds that counterfeits not meeting the standard are sold at tempting prices — *"всегда требуйте сертификат"*.
>
> ⚠️ **Price within quartz is driven by PATTERN**: simpler and more uniform is cheaper, complex and textured dearer. **And by yield** — *"чем больше вероятность, что столешница по площади займёт сляб или его половину, тем ниже цена в расчёте за квадратный метр."* **Nesting efficiency sets the effective unit price** — the same principle as the `rectpack` note in [`toolchain_gap_analysis_20260908.md`](../_Inbox/planning/toolchain_gap_analysis_20260908.md).

**Strengths**: hardness and abrasion resistance close to granite; **non-porous → no bacteria or mould**, used in medical labs, food plants and children's facilities; **absorbs no dyes** — coffee, tea, brightly coloured juice leave no stain; resistant to aggressive chemistry including acids and solvents; tolerates short exposure to high and low temperature without deforming or losing colour; **needs no waxing or mastics, unlike granite and marble** — sponge and non-abrasive detergent; very wide colour range including marble imitation and semi-precious-stone textures.

> **⚠️ Colour stability at production is the advantage over natural stone, and he names it as such**: *"выбрав цвет кварцевого агломерата, можно не беспокоиться, что листы будут отличаться по оттенку или рисунку"* — **something you will not get from natural stone.**

**⚠️⚠️ Weaknesses, and three of them are structural:**

- **52 kg/m² at 20 mm.** *"Требует усиленных опор на кухонном гарнитуре и специально изготовленного металлического каркаса на барной стойке длиной более 60 сантиметров."*
  > **⚠️⚠️ A LOAD RULE WITH A THRESHOLD — 600 mm of unsupported bar and you need a fabricated steel frame.** **This corroborates and sharpens a finding [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]] already holds** — *«если у вас есть пролёт… и под столешницей нет закладной… она может легко сломаться»* — **which stated the principle without a number. Now it has one.**
- **Cannot be restored**, unlike acrylic — damage means full replacement.
- **A red-hot pan left in place can CRACK it by thermal shock** — hence trivets.
- **Slabs cannot be joined seamlessly** — the seam is faint but visible; some techniques make it invisible beyond ~1 m.
- **Cannot be bent** — no curved worktops (explicitly contrasted with acrylic).
- **Tactilely cold. Few wood-look decors and a small matt range** (contrasted with porcelain stoneware).
- **Cutting directly on it leaves fine scratches that show over time.**
- **Prolonged UV fades it** → not for outdoors or direct sun.
- **⚠️ Measurement requires the cabinets ALREADY INSTALLED**, because the slightest error and it will not fit, and trimming in place is very problematic. **→ A hard sequencing constraint.**

### ⚠️⚠️ Two things he actively talks clients OUT of — and both contradict what this vault records as desirable

1. **A same-material integrated sink**: *"я обычно отговариваю от такого варианта, потому что в углах этой мойки скапливается грязь и достаточно проблематично оттуда убрать."*
   > **⚠️⚠️ [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]] records the same-material quartz undermount sink as a newer premium capability.** **A fabricator who makes them advises against it on a cleaning mechanism.** **→ Route as a genuine Perspectives disagreement, not a correction** — the capability is real, the maintenance objection is also real.
2. **A flush-mounted hob** (заподлицо), which quartz mills well enough to allow: *"в результате нагрева стеклокерамика расширяется, ей необходим зазор… этот зазор заливается термогерметиком, который со временем теряет свои первоначальные свойства и требует замены."*
   > **⚠️⚠️ A MECHANISM WITH A MAINTENANCE CONSEQUENCE: the glass-ceramic hob expands when hot, the expansion gap must be filled with thermal sealant, and that sealant degrades and must be replaced.** **Entirely new to this vault.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. PORCELAIN STONEWARE (керамогранит)

**Structure**: quartz sand + clay + crushed granite + feldspar + ceramic pigments, pressed under high pressure and **fired above 1200 °C**. **Thickness 3–20 mm: 3–5 mm for facing furniture FRONTS, 10–20 mm for worktops.**

> ⚠️ **He rebuts a common claim directly**: *"многие мебельные эксперты в кавычках говорят, что керамогранит — это обычная плитка, но это не так"* — worktop-grade differs in size, thickness, composition and properties, and **processing needs more serious tooling than a glass cutter and an angle grinder.**
>
> ⚠️ **The 3–5 mm figure corroborates a note this vault already holds from Шеврина** — thin large-format ceramic light enough to be fixed to MDF fronts, so worktop, splashback and fronts can carry one continuous pattern. **Now with the thickness band that makes it possible.**

**Strengths that distinguish it from quartz:**

| Property | Porcelain stoneware | vs Quartz |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weight** | *"не очень тяжёлый… позволяет снизить нагрузку на несущие конструкции"* | ⚠️ **quartz is 52 kg/m² and needs reinforced supports** |
| **UV** | **does not fade** | ⚠️ **quartz fades under prolonged UV** |
| **Bending strength** | *"очень прочен на изгиб… прочнее камня, практически невозможно образовать трещину"* | ⚠️ **quartz can crack from thermal shock** |
| **Decors** | **includes WOOD texture, rusty metal, full stone replication** | ⚠️ **quartz has few wood-look decors** |

**Also**: coloured through the full body via metal oxides, so **wear barely changes the colour**; near-zero water absorption → grease and dyes wipe off; chemically resistant to the most aggressive cleaners; fire-resistant, emits no smoke or toxins; frost-resistant; **low electrical conductivity, dissipates static**; hygienic, food-safe, recyclable.

> **⚠️⚠️ And it hosts things the others cannot**: *"можно установить скрытую индукционную варочную поверхность… платформу для беспроводной зарядки и всевозможные пульты дистанционного управления"* — **a hidden induction hob UNDER the surface, wireless charging, embedded controls.**
>
> **⚠️ A fabrication trick worth keeping**: on thin slabs you can run a straight edge **or a 45° mitre, which lets you visually create a worktop of ANY apparent thickness.** **The same trick appears on HPL (§3).**

**⚠️⚠️ Weaknesses, and one is a hard yield rule:**

- **⚠️⚠️ *"Чтобы снять напряжение с камня и избежать трещин, нужно по периметру снять минимум по 5 сантиметров."*** **→ A MANDATORY 50 mm PERIMETER TRIM to relieve stress.** ⚠️⚠️ **This is a waste figure that is not a percentage — it is a geometric constant, and it bears directly on the waste-is-not-one-number finding in [[18_Digital_Toolchain/analysis/Quantity_Takeoff_and_Cost_Join|Quantity Take-Off and the Cost Join]] §2.**
- **Fragile at the edges and around an undermount sink.**
- **Seams visible**; on textured stone finishes the seam is made as a small groove **which collects dirt over time**.
- **Cannot take a shaped/profiled edge** in the classic sense.
- **Tactilely cold; cannot be restored.**
- **Very high cost** — not only the material but the consumables and equipment wear in fabrication.

⚠️ He promotes **Dekton** by name as an improved variant (slabs **3200 × 1440 mm**, thicknesses **8 / 12 / 20 mm**). **Brand content, not routed as a verdict.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. ACRYLIC (искусственный камень) and HPL — the pairing this vault had nothing on

### Acrylic — and a HEALTH finding

**Structure**: fine mineral crumb + pigments + acrylic resin. **Sheet acrylic 3600 × 760 mm, thickness 6 or 12 mm.**

> **⚠️⚠️ TWO REFUSALS, STATED AS RULES:**
> 1. **6 mm acrylic must not be used for worktops** — it is prone to cracking.
> 2. **⚠️⚠️ LIQUID acrylic (4–6 mm) he refuses outright**: *"при химическом отвердевании смолы выделяется огромное количество СТИРОЛА, который является токсичным и опасным для здоровья человека."* **→ A health objection to a whole product category, from the person who would otherwise sell it.** **Entirely new to this vault.**

**Strengths**: a **single seamless plane** — joints sanded until completely invisible; **sink and worktop from one piece**; some decors **transmit light → back-lit worktops**; homogeneous and impermeable to bacteria; **best of all these materials for complex, round and curved forms**; **warmer to the touch than stone**; **can be sanded repeatedly** to refresh.

**Weaknesses**: shows cuts, dents and scratches; susceptible to acids and dyes, **wipe spills immediately**; **low heat resistance — a hot pan can leave a black mark and a temperature change can crack it outright**; fades under UV; joints visible on black.

> **⚠️⚠️ Two consequences this vault did not have:**
> - **Restoration means a professional sanding on site, and *"ваша квартира запомнится тонким слоем пыли, и вам понадобится вызывать заново cleaning."*** **The repairability that makes acrylic attractive has a dust cost attached.** ⚠️ **Read against Шеврина's existing position in [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]] — *"чаще люди ленятся… лучше предусмотреть материал понадёжнее"* — which now has a second reason behind it.**
> - **⚠️⚠️ *"Без дополнительной подложки из более твёрдого материала акриловый камень со временем проседает, особенно… в районе варочной поверхности."*** **→ Acrylic SAGS without a substrate. A structural requirement, with the hob named as the worst location.**

### HPL (пластик высокого давления)

**Invented in the USA in 1913.** Thickness **0.8–20 mm**; **furniture uses 12 mm** with double-sided lamination; sheets up to roughly **4200 × 1850 mm**.

> **⚠️⚠️ The property that matters most, and it is the exact inverse of ЛДСП's failure mode**: *"стопроцентная влагостойкость — даже если вы повредили верхний слой ламината, столешница не разбухнет и не вздуется."* **ЛДСП swells when water reaches the substrate; HPL does not.**
>
> **⚠️ And the edge needs no treatment at all**: *"торец HPL-панели не требует дополнительной обработки, является полностью функциональным сразу же после распила."* **No edge banding, no PVC, no glue line.** ⚠️ **The core is white or black, and he says to factor that into the design** — the core line is visible at every edge.

**Also**: same 45° mitre-and-glue trick for any apparent thickness; many wood and stone decors; undermount sink possible; weather-resistant enough for outdoor furniture; **does not glare in sun**; solvent-resistant; **not prone to cracking, does not bend over large spans**; frost- and fire-resistant; **non-toxic, no halogens, sulphur compounds, heavy metals or asbestos**.

**⚠️ Weaknesses, and he is honest about the first**: **scratch resistance is *"такая же, как и у столешницы из ДСП"*** — no better than chipboard; **impact resistance poor — a falling pot can chip a piece out**; **weak at the edges and around an undermount sink** (the same weak points as porcelain stoneware); high temperature can ruin the laminate; **cannot be bent**; **cutting hob and sink openings is impossible without special equipment**; **no on-site adjustment — any hand work risks chipping and ruining the piece**; joints visible even with anchor connections.

> ⚠️ **A practical note that lands on an existing vault finding**: HPL *"легко перфорируют, но только на специальном оборудовании — особенно актуально, если кухня стоит под окном и нужно сделать отверстие для вентиляции батареи."* **→ [[03_Kitchen/Kitchen_General|Kitchen General]] already records a real case where kitchen cabinetry blocked the radiator and the window began streaming condensation. This is the fabricator-side answer: the vent hole is drillable, but only in the shop.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. GLASS and STAINLESS STEEL — and he restricts BOTH to non-work zones

### Glass

**Requires tempered glass thicker than 8 mm, or triplex (laminated).** Assembly **5–20 mm**, several laminated layers with the top one tempered. **Two clear glasses: ordinary (slightly GREENISH) and super-clear/optiwhite (slightly BLUISH).** Can be left clear or painted any colour; tinted bronze or graphite; **sizes over 3 m**.

> **⚠️⚠️ THE WARNING WORTH THE MOST, AND IT IS A MECHANISM: *"ни в коем случае не используйте матовое покрытие лицом наружу. Матовый слой технологически создаётся в результате химического травления, что создаёт МИКРОПОРЫ, в которые очень хорошо проникают жир и пыль, и оттереть его практически невозможно."*** **He extends the same warning to matt glass on a skinali (splashback).**
>
> **⚠️⚠️ This supplies the MECHANISM behind a claim [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]] already carries** — that tempered glass *"shows fingerprints/grease heavily"* — **and it sharpens it into an actionable rule: the problem is specifically the ETCHED MATT finish, not glass as such.** ⚠️ **It also qualifies Синчуков's position on the same page** (*"на кухне на фартук подойдёт калёное стекло, с которым тоже ничего не будет"*) — **both can be true: polished tempered glass is durable, etched matt glass is a grease trap.**

**Strengths**: does not abrade or deform in constant use; unafraid of moisture; no bacteria or mould; **thermostable — hot pans straight down**; ecological; **the best gloss of any of these materials.**

**Weaknesses**: **fragile — even tempered glass will eventually crack** under significant load; **tactilely cold**; **⚠️ metal cookware produces an unpleasant squeak that causes real discomfort while working**; **shows everything** — dish marks, water streaks, greasy fingerprints; **high price**; afraid of edge impacts; scratched by metal.

> **⚠️⚠️ His verdict is a placement rule, not a rejection: *"я советую использовать столешницы из стекла на барных стойках или участках кухни, на которых не будет происходить рабочие процессы"*** — and specifically for a **second-tier bar counter, to create an effect of weightlessness.** **→ Glass is a display surface, not a work surface.**

### Stainless steel

**Strengths**: practical, needs no special care, not subject to wear; suits loft / hi-tech / modern minimalism; **absorbs neither odours nor moisture; not stained by food**; cleans with any detergent; **unafraid of high temperature — a red-hot pan does no damage**; matt or gloss, flat or lightly textured.

**Weaknesses**: **scratches appear** (not very noticeable, but factor them in); **a falling metal object leaves a dent as well as a loud noise**; **⚠️ the SEAMS oxidise and need additional polishing**; costs somewhat more than ordinary ЛДСП.

> **⚠️ His verdict: stainless is perfectly usable on the MAIN work zone** — the opposite of his glass verdict. **The pair is therefore a work-surface/display-surface split, not a ranking.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. WOOD and ЛДСП — the budget end, with two rules worth extracting

### ЛДСП (laminated chipboard)

> **⚠️⚠️ THICKNESS RULE WITH A FUNCTIONAL REASON — and this one is directly checkable against our model: *"толщина может быть 28 и 38 миллиметров. Я рекомендую 38 — это связано с габаритами техники, а именно с толщиной варочной поверхности. Если вы располагаете выдвижные ящики в районе варочной поверхности, то, чтобы содержимое ящика не соприкасалось с техникой, нужен минимальный зазор, который обеспечивает столешница 38 миллиметров."***
>
> **→ A 38 mm worktop exists to create the clearance a drawer needs under a hob.** ⚠️ **Entirely new to this vault, and it is the kind of dimension that silently breaks a drawer layout.**

**Strengths**: huge decor range, wood and stone; **modern printing and 3D embossing are good enough that beyond ~1 m it is hard to tell ЛДСП from real wood**; **large formats over 4 m with no joint** — but only for linear kitchens; **easy to clean** — laminate is non-porous; **matching carcass ЛДСП available**, which matters for "portal" kitchens where the worktop colour should continue into tall units.

> **⚠️⚠️ A LOGISTICS CONSTRAINT THAT BITES LATE, and nothing else in this vault records it: *"обязательно убедитесь, что такой размер возможно провести… между пролётов этажей, потому что такая столешница в лифт точно не поместится."*** **→ A >4 m jointless worktop will not fit in a lift and must go up the stairwell. Check the stair flight before specifying it.**

**Weaknesses**: colour can wear where frequently wiped, especially with abrasives; **weak heat resistance**; **⚠️ an undermount sink CANNOT be fitted — do not even consider that sink type** (⚠️ **corroborates the existing sink-mounting note exactly**); joints via an **aluminium strip**, and **⚠️ a mitred "еврозапил" joint must not be used next to the sink, because that is the first node to swell when water gets in**; **swells** if water reaches unprotected substrate, from a badly fitted sink, or damaged laminate; **a wall upstand is mandatory and usually cannot be colour-matched**, adding a visible break line; texture direction on corner kitchens *"оставляет желать лучшего"*.

### Wood

**Three production types**: **евробрус** (small bars glued and sanded — ⚠️ shade may be non-uniform and it will be visibly made of pieces); **евробрус со шпоном** (the same, veneered for a whole-looking surface); and **⚠️ a single solid piece — which he says is unfortunately NOT usable for a kitchen worktop at all.**

**Strengths**: natural and very pleasant tactilely; individual sizes; adds warmth; **with the right lacquer can be moisture resistant**; **can be sanded repeatedly to refresh.**

**Weaknesses**: **very weak heat and fire resistance**; **dries out or sags with humidity differentials**; swells where moisture reaches unprotected joints or cracks; weak against acids and dyes; needs regular thorough care; deforms with temperature or humidity change; **subject to fungi and micro-organisms with frequent wetting**; **shade varies along the length**; and, drily, *"как ни странно, есть только один декор — деревянный."*

> **⚠️⚠️ THE MAINTENANCE FINDING, with intervals, and it is new to this vault: protection is either LACQUER or OIL-WAX, and both have a cost. *"На лаке будут оставаться белые пятна после контакта с водой. Масло-воск нужно обновлять каждые полгода, потому что он стирается."*** **→ Lacquer: white water marks. Oil-wax: re-apply every six months, for the life of the worktop.**
>
> **⚠️ His verdict, again a placement rule: wood on a bar counter, a windowsill, and surfaces that do not meet water; ЛДСП for everything else, because the decor choice is colossal.**
