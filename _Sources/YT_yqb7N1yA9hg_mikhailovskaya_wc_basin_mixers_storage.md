## Fixtures — ⚠️ the WC

- **⚠️ Beyond "keep it near the riser": "унитаз не терпит прямых углов" — right angles in the WC's drain run risk poor operation of the drainage system.** A constraint on *how* the connection runs, not just how far.
- **Three installation types**: **floor-standing with a visible cistern** (no installation frame needed; cheaper in simple forms — the budget option); **floor-standing with a concealed cistern** (an installation frame but not wall-hung — *"для современного интерьера это тоже очень красиво"*, and she notes a wall-hung WC is not obligatory); and **wall-hung concealed.**
- **⚠️ Floor-standing has two practical costs: cleaning around it, and the installation detail — a coloured pan sealed with white silicone "может испортить внешний вид."**
- **⚠️ Choose rimless («без ободковый») — it is more hygienic.**
- **⚠️ Square forms photograph well and are often impractical in use. Unless the brief specifically demands hard-cornered square, choose ergonomic rounded forms — square with rounded corners is fine — "которые в эксплуатации будут более надёжные."**
- **White is the cheapest; colour costs more.**

### ⚠️ The flush plate, and a constraint most people meet too late

**Two kinds: surface-mounted («накладная») and tile-insert («под плитку»).**

- **⚠️ Chromed buttons finger-mark badly and need constant polishing.**
- **⚠️ A tile-insert plate is much easier to keep clean — provided the tile or porcelain chosen is non-marking — but it "требует хорошего мастерства плиточника" to cut neatly.**
- **⚠️ AND THE CONSTRAINT: a tile-insert button requires the installation to be FACED IN TILE. If the installation is clad in MDF, HPL, chipboard or other furniture panel, you must use a surface-mounted button.** **The button choice and the installation cladding are one decision.**

### ⚠️ The installation frame

- **⚠️ The installation is chosen TO SUIT the WC — "технические характеристики инсталляции должны соответствовать техническим характеристикам унитаза."** Not any frame under any pan.
- **Depths vary — 15, 17, 18. ⚠️ 18 cm is standard, and "если вы в проекте закладываете 18 плюс плитка или плюс панель, вы точно в принципе угадаете"** without having yet chosen a model.
- **Typical envelope: ~180 mm deep × ~1100 high × 500–550 wide**, varying slightly by model.
- **⚠️ Whether a given pan will hang on a given frame is determined by the fixing studs — check that the mounting-centre spacing matches.**
- **⚠️ THE SEQUENCING FACT: the installation is mounted on the CONCRETE floor, not on tile. It is among the very first things installed — before the floor is poured and before tiling. So the WC type and the frame must be decided at PLANNING stage, not at fit-out.**

### Bidet, and the hygienic shower

- **A bidet is rarely used in flats because of limited area** — more likely in a house or a large flat with several bathrooms. **It comes as a set with the WC, so match manufacturer and form, and it needs a dedicated bidet mixer.**
- **⚠️ General mixer rule: buy mixers as a set — "хотя бы производителя, хотя бы одного цвета."** Not always possible, but try.
- **The hygienic shower replaces the bidet and is far more common in contemporary flats and houses.**

### ⚠️ Hygienic shower — a planning decision, not a fitting decision

- **Two configurations: ONE-POINT (holder, water supply and mixer at a single point) and TWO-POINT (mixer and holder in separate places).**
- **⚠️ You must decide which at planning stage, because a two-point unit needs materially more wall space.** **Her worked case: 850 mm available beside the WC where 900 would be preferable — with a hygienic shower needed to the right, "целесообразнее выбрать гигиенический душ на одну точку, так как для его монтажа требуется меньше места."**
- The mixer can be offset up or down from the holder, **but consider whether that will actually be convenient** — normally they sit in one line, and offsetting is *"чревато неудобными моментами и неправильным монтажом."*
- **⚠️ A specification item almost nobody asks about: the hose. Some are corrugated metal tube, some rubber cord. Neither is better — "они просто разные, разная их мягкость и разная эксплуатация." Ask the client which they prefer when taking the brief.**

## Fixtures / Ergonomics — ⚠️ the basin, and the coupling that decides the layout

> **⚠️ THE STANDARD: 850 mm from finished floor to the TOP EDGE of the basin.**

**Three types: vessel/countertop («накладная»), inset or undermount («врезная»), and integral solid-surface cast with the worktop.**

### ⚠️ The chain: a vessel basin and a washing machine are mutually exclusive

**This is the best single item in the source, because it is a constraint people discover after ordering.**

- A **vessel basin sits ON the worktop**, so the worktop must be correspondingly lower.
- **Working back from the 850 standard: if the bowl is ~100 mm high, the worktop must be at ~750.**
- **⚠️ A washing machine is 860–870 mm high. It will not go under a 750 worktop.**
- **⚠️ So: "если в планировке у вас заложено расположение стиральной машины под столешницей, тогда раковина-чаша вам не подходит" — because putting a vessel basin over a machine-height worktop puts the rim at 950–960, which is simply wrong.**

> **Where a washing machine goes under the worktop, the basin must be inset, undermount, or an integral solid-surface top. The basin type is dictated by the plan, not by taste.**

**Her reason for insisting on it: "самое главное у нас в интерьере — это эргономика… если ваша раковина на расстоянии метр, а у вас ребёнок — он не помоется."**

### ⚠️ Basin type also dictates the worktop material

- **An undermount basin is GLUED to the underside, so the worktop must be genuinely moisture-resistant — not chipboard, and better not timber.**
- **A vessel basin permits a chipboard postforming top in very budget cases** — she calls it *"нежелательно крайне"* but acceptable as a temporary budget answer.

### Two further basin rules

- **⚠️ Check basin width against the plan.** Her worked case: **860 mm between washing machine and cupboard means a basin no wider than ~800**, because an inset basin has a rim beyond the bowl — a 1 m basin *"просто не влезет туда."*
- **Sharp corners on a basin accumulate dirt.**

## Fixtures — ⚠️ basin mixers

**Types: on the basin, on the wall (surface or concealed), on the worktop — and a basin mixer WITH an integrated hygienic shower, which is a combined, lighter, budget option.**

- **⚠️ Spout length again, and worked concretely: with a vessel basin and a concealed wall mixer you must know the worktop depth. If the basin is shallow (350) and stands 150 off the wall while the spout projects only 100, the water pours BEHIND the basin.**
- **⚠️ From the basin's outer edge to the worktop's front edge: 20 mm maximum, and ideally flush.** Otherwise it is awkward to wash, awkward to lean over, and water runs off.
- **⚠️ A tall vessel basin needs a tall mixer.** **Some spouts turn at an angle so the water arcs inward — those are forgiving. Where the water falls straight down, spout length and mixer position become critical.**
- **⚠️ Some basins come with a tap hole already — those cannot then be used with a wall mixer, because the hole remains.**
- **⚠️ Therefore choose basin and mixer TOGETHER: "выбрали раковину, а потом может быть когда-то выберу смеситель — нет, так не работает."** And **you will be dimensioning the mixer position on the drawings, so it must be a specific model.**

## Fixtures — ⚠️ washing machine and dryer

- **Placements: under the worktop; in a pencil unit; in a separate cupboard; stacked with a dryer.**
- **⚠️ Built-in needs a cabinet of roughly 600 × 600. Free-standing needs ventilation gaps and some play — the cabinet must be larger, around 700.**
- **⚠️ A washer-plus-dryer stack is only available as free-standing units** (carried over from part 1).
- **⚠️ THE DETAIL THAT IS USUALLY DRAWN WRONG: a dryer stacks on a washer via a SPECIAL MOUNTING KIT, not on a chipboard shelf or worktop between them.** Going through chipboard needs specific routing and fixing. **"В проектировании и визуализации мы понимаем, что вот так вот мы не делаем."**
- **⚠️ "Красиво но неправильно": a washing machine set high in a tall cabinet — "клиент либо так не сделает, либо проклянёт своего дизайнера, потому что ему высоко."** Worse with small children.
- **⚠️ A washer/dryer cupboard is deep, and in a small bathroom it must either be recessed into a niche or moved out of the room entirely.** Her current project puts it **in the kitchen beside the fridge**, which the vent channels permitted and the client agreed.

## Design Concept — ⚠️ storage, on which she is emphatic

> **"Можно супер круто расположить сантехнику, офигенно круто выбрать смесители, но не предусмотреть место для хранения — и это всегда будет провально."**

- **Use the space under the basin to the maximum, especially in a flat.**
- **⚠️ AND THE DETAIL ALMOST EVERYONE MISSES: the basin type dictates the drawer layout. With an inset basin, the bowl's inset portion and the trap occupy the cabinet — so a full-width drawer drawn under it cannot actually exist. Plan the drawer positions around the chosen basin. A vessel basin leaves only the trap inside, so there is materially more usable storage.**
  - **⚠️ She demonstrates this by reading a real rendering and showing the drawer shown is impossible, and that an exposed trap should be visible and is not — "дизайнеры, которые делали эту визуализацию, не понимали каких-то технических моментов."**
- **⚠️ Storage behind the mirror is mandatory where storage is short.** She concedes a decorative backlit mirror is prettier — *"но если нет других вариантов, обязательно делаем место хранения за зеркалом."* `[partial restatement]`
- **⚠️ Above the installation: by default always a cupboard, or the boiler — push the installation out to 400 mm to create it.** For washing powder, toilet paper, jars.
- **In showers: built-in niches where the layout permits, otherwise surface-mounted shelves — and the choice is made at planning stage.**
- **⚠️ A reach check she applies out loud: a cupboard running from 800 up to 1600–1700 is beyond a short person's reach** — she can reach only something standing at the very edge.
- **⚠️ Her ranking, stated plainly: "пусть мы жертвуем красотой визуализации в Инстаграме, но человек будет доволен, и этот дизайн будет сделан правильно."**

## Labor / Delivery Model — ⚠️ access for servicing, which is a whole section

> **"Обязательно думаем, как же мы будем технически обслуживать нашу ванную."**

- **⚠️ Provide access to the TRAP.** She pre-empts the objection: modern traps are cleaned from outside — *"да, я знаю, что есть сифоны, они сейчас практически все такие… но как показывает опыт, случается разное, и лучше предусмотреть лючок скрытого монтажа, или съёмную плиточку, или доступ через шкаф."*
- **⚠️ Where a bath abuts a vanity, there are two routes: a hatch in the tiling, or drop the vanity and access through it.**
- **⚠️ DRAW THE HATCH ON THE VISUALISATION. "Лучше сделать этот лючок на визуализации, для того чтобы этот момент был учтён."** Her reason is expectation management: **a client sees an unbroken tile face and does not realise a hatch means a seam and a visible square.** Show it, or the argument happens on site.
- **⚠️ A free-standing bath cannot have a hatch at all — access to the trap is from behind or from outside. Know that before choosing one.**
- **⚠️ Meters and water supply points must be located at survey and hidden deliberately — in a cupboard or behind the installation. "Если мы сделаем всё красиво, а счётчики должны быть в ванной, а места нет — это будут какие-то изменения, и не очень в хорошую сторону."**

## Mistakes / Warnings — ⚠️ her "красиво но нереализуемо" gallery, as a checklist

**She reads roughly a dozen real renderings and names the failure in each. This is the most directly usable section in the source.**

- **A free-standing bath wedged between walls** — *"я в судорожных конвульсиях представляю, если зальётся сюда вода, как человек будет вымывать вот здесь."*
- **A free-standing bath with a visible gap behind it and a wall mixer** — *"как помыть и вытереть за ней?"* **⚠️ Her fix: there are baths that read as free-standing but have a flat back that abuts the wall — use those where it must sit against a wall.**
- **⚠️ A podium with too little tread beyond the bath edge: you step out onto a narrow ledge, cannot put down a non-slip mat, and "можно просто убиться."**
- **A floor-standing mixer in a corner** — nowhere to clean around it. **Her positive counter-example is deliberately an ugly picture: a ribbed non-slip podium material, the floor mixer standing clear with access on all sides, and access to the bath — "не такая красивая картинка, но с технической точки зрения гораздо правильнее."**
- **A mirror hung too high to clean.**
- **⚠️ The vessel-basin failure worked end to end: a thick worktop, a vessel basin on top, and a washing machine below — the basin ends up at neck height, and the mirror goes "куда-то под небеса" as a consequence.** *"Каким же параметрам должен обладать этот человек, чтобы пользоваться этой ванной?"*
- **⚠️ Timber or chipboard vanity in a bathroom — "всё будет приходить в негодность."**
- **⚠️ A bath screen with no room to open it: "мы себя прищемим — мы должны залезть в ванную и закрыть эту дверку."**
- **Excessive decor — huge pictures and pendant fittings in a small wet room — as unrealisable staging.**

### ⚠️ And the client-management rule she draws from the gallery

> **"Когда заказчик приносит картинку с такой раковиной — вы обязательно предупредите его про высоту раковины. Если его это устроит, он подпишется на это, тогда вы можете смело снимать с себя всю ответственность. Если нет — тогда даже и не думайте."**

**Recorded because it reads as a client-side entitlement, not only a designer's protection: being warned about a foreseeable ergonomic failure, and signing off on it knowingly, is part of what the fee buys.** *(The same structure as her shower-versus-bath warning in part 1.)*
