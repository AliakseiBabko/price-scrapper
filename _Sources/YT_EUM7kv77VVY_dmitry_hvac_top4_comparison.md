- **⚠️ Option 4 (worst) — window airing**: modern sealed double-glazed windows pass essentially zero air when closed (unlike old Soviet-era wooden windows, which leaked passively) — airflow only happens with the window physically open, driven by convection (a radiator under the window heats air, which rises, escapes through the open gap, and is drawn toward exhaust shafts, typically in kitchen/bathroom). **Hidden cost claim**: window hardware wear/replacement can reportedly reach up to ~50% of a window's total cost over its life (per an unnamed "window expert" the source consulted) — `single-account`, `unverified`, but a genuinely concrete hidden-cost claim worth flagging.
