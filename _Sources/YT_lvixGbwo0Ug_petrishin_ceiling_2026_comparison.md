## Ceilings — Painted Ceiling ("Потолок под покраску")

1. **⚠️ Common misconception corrected**: a painted ceiling is widely
   assumed to be the simplest and cheapest option — actually one of the
   hardest and priciest, since it requires plastering the raw concrete
   slab dead flat (by beacons) before a multi-coat paint job. **Chasing
   ("штробить") into the structural slab is categorically prohibited** —
   wiring can only run in the seam between two slab panels, enough for
   a single ceiling light fixture, with **no possibility of a complex
   multi-scenario lighting layout with concealed wiring**. A building-
   settlement crack later needs visible touch-up repair, and a flood
   from upstairs is very hard to patch invisibly — usually needs a full
   redo. **Genuine advantage**: doesn't consume ceiling height, making
   it a common choice in low-ceiling secondary-market ("вторичное
   жильё") housing specifically.
2. **Historical-terminology correction from the company expert**:
   "побелка" (traditional lime-wash whitewash, applied by roller) is an
   outdated term many clients still use by habit — this company hasn't
   whitewashed a ceiling in years; they paint instead, which requires
   a genuinely flawless substrate. **Explicit ceiling-specific flatness
   standard**: no gaps against a 3m straightedge ("на 3м плоскости...
   зазоров не должно быть"). **⚠️ Ceiling-specific raking-light failure
   mode**: poor prep can look fine under artificial evening light but
   reveal visible waviness under natural morning/midday/sunset light
   hitting the ceiling at a specific angle — a ceiling-specific version
   of this project's existing raking-light QC theme (previously
   documented for walls in `Walls_and_Paint.md`).
3. **Full ceiling-paint-prep sequence, with a slab-panel-specific
   detail**: plaster by beacons flawlessly → putty in 2-3 coats → glue
   fiberglass mesh to prevent cracking → for a panel-seam ceiling
   specifically, open/rout the seam between slabs, foam-fill it, then
   apply a stronger filler (Пуфас or Унифлот) over the cured foam,
   reinforce with mesh plus a paper bandage tape. Extends this page's
   existing panel-ceiling-joint treatment (previously documented for
   older Stalin/Khrushchev-era buildings) with a ceiling-paint-specific
   sequencing detail.

## Ceilings — Drywall Ceiling

4. **Design flexibility and constraints**: drywall enables any curved
   line, wave, or recessed lighting niche, and hides any service
   (wiring, ventilation ducting) — but only reads well in a
   high-ceilinged room; in a low-ceilinged room the mass visually
   "presses down" and looks poor. Point/recessed lighting is the
   typical fixture choice, with dedicated niches/ledges built in for
   fixtures and wiring concealed behind the board.
5. **Real timeline/cost data point, worked example on a 70m² apartment,
   two workers**: framing + board installation alone takes roughly **2
   weeks**; taping/mesh/plastering/finishing before paint adds a further
   **minimum 2 weeks** — a full sequence that can run to a month or
   more depending on complexity and crew size. Materials (profile,
   hangers, board, fasteners, finish materials) are expensive, and
   **installation labor typically costs more than the materials
   themselves**. Poor installation or building settlement can crack it
   (touch-up possible, imperfect); **⚠️ a flood from upstairs neighbors
   is described as "catastrophic" for this ceiling type — typically
   requires full demolition and rebuild**, distinct from stretch
   ceiling's flood-recoverability (see below). Outcome quality depends
   entirely on the installer's hand-skill/craftsmanship.
6. **⚠️ Company expert's own explicit ranking, distinct from the general
   comparison**: he would deprioritize drywall ceilings in most cases in
   favor of stretch ceiling, reasoning that anything drywall can hide
   (services, ventilation, humidification lines) can equally be hidden
   behind a stretch ceiling instead, at much lower time/cost. **Stated
   exception where he'd still choose drywall**: when soundproofing
   characteristics are a genuine priority for the client — though he
   notes the extra time this costs is itself a real expense.

## Ceilings — Stretch Ceiling and Named Design Techniques

7. **⚠️ Flood-recoverability detail, extending this page's existing
   RemProektMD flood-containment finding with a concrete remedy**: if
   flooded from above, the membrane holds the water, which can simply
   be carefully drained and the membrane re-stretched — and even a full
   membrane replacement after a flood is described as easy and
   inexpensive relative to redoing a painted or drywall ceiling.
8. **Two named stretch-ceiling design techniques, new to this page**:
   a **"floating ceiling"** effect — a shadow gap between wall and
   ceiling with an LED strip mounted inside it; and a **"seamless wall
   junction"** — the membrane is tucked into a specific trim profile at
   the perimeter, producing a clean, gap-free line at the wall-ceiling
   junction.
9. **⚠️ Company expert's own top personal recommendation, stated
   directly**: stretch ceiling, citing speed/cost/cleanliness over
   aesthetic-purity objections — he explicitly notes many designers
   dislike stretch ceilings, but frames his own recommendation as
   coming from a practicality-first perspective rather than a design
   one. (A candid personal aside dismissing "but it's not eco-friendly"
   objections by referencing general Moscow/Moscow-region air quality —
   flagged explicitly as the speaker's own opinion, not adopted as
   fact.)

## Ceilings — ⚠️ "Practically Free" Loft-Style Ceiling

10. **⚠️ New ceiling-type content for this page: a deliberately exposed,
    unfinished structural slab as a loft-style design choice** — no
    need to hide wiring/ductwork or achieve a flat surface, since
    visible roughness is the whole point of the aesthetic (works only
    if the rest of the apartment commits to the same style). Any
    lighting type is compatible. Installation cost is near-zero, there
    is effectively no timeline, and it's immune to flood/settlement
    damage since there's no finish layer to damage in the first place.
