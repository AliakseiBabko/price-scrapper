## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE THREE-PART SYSTEM — and the middle part is the one people omit

**His organising claim: ventilation is three things in series, and if any one fails the whole thing fails.**

### ПРИТОК — supply

New builds now fit supply valves (клапана): on warm loggias, on PVC, aluminium or even wooden windows, or cut through the wall.

- **⚠️⚠️ PLACEMENT RULE: high, at the top under the reveal (откос)** — so incoming cold air does not drop straight down and *«дуть в поясницу»*, but mixes near the ceiling and leaves via the extract. **A specific, checkable installation detail.**
- **His historical framing, which is the clearest explanation of why this is a modern problem:** Soviet wooden windows had a gap in the lower frame and leaked around the perimeter, and **that was the supply air.** Sealed modern windows removed it: *«человек себя закупоривает и по факту он себе делает хуже»*.

### ⚠️⚠️ ПЕРЕТОК — transfer between rooms, the part nobody thinks about

> ***«Если двери установлены максимально герметичные, то может быть проблема — просто воздух из этого помещения он не сможет выйти в вентиляцию.»***

**Fix: an undercut gap beneath the door, a transfer grille (переточная решётка) in the door leaf, or as a last resort transfer valves.** He notes people dislike undercuts — dust, dirt, appearance — **which is exactly why transfer grilles exist.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ NEW TO THE VAULT AS A NAMED REQUIREMENT, and it couples a DOOR decision to a VENTILATION outcome.** **A well-sealed interior door — which the vault's own door material treats as a quality signal — defeats the extract in that room unless transfer is designed in.** **Relevant to bathroom and WC doors in particular.**

### ⚠️ ВЫТЯЖКА — extract, which may simply be dead

- **⚠️ Neighbours above may have rebuilt or blocked the shaft.** He names **mass-built housing from the 2000s in dormitory districts**, where residents altered ventilation stacks freely, so the duct can be physically blocked higher up and *«вентиляция может просто физически не работать»*.
- **⚠️⚠️ THE KITCHEN TRAP: the cooker hood is NOT the room's ventilation.** *«Нужно, чтобы на кухне была независимая вентиляция. То, что касается вытяжки над плитой — это одно, а ещё должна быть вентиляция.»* **People see a grille, assume it works, and never notice it is dead.**
- **⚠️ Fans can seal the duct when off.** Fans fitted to speed extraction from a bathroom or kitchen may block natural flow; there are fans with a shutter that opens only when running.
- **Maintenance: the ventilation needs cleaning periodically.**

> **⚠️ He cites *«в госте, в документах написано»* that there are required humidity, temperature and CO₂ figures.** ⚠️⚠️ **RUSSIAN REGULATORY REFERENCE with no number cited — flagged, `unverified`, and deliberately NOT routed to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`, which is Belarus-only.**

## ⚠️ 2. The three symptoms of failed ventilation, named as a set

1. **Stale, stuffy air you do not want to be in** — he describes measuring CO₂ and finding you are breathing *«воздухом отработанным… своими испарениями»*.
2. **⚠️ Windows that sweat or "weep".**
3. **⚠️⚠️ Black corners and mould** — *«она как раз появляется, потому что нет конвекции внутри помещения: воздух не обменивается, он там застаивается»*.

> **→ The set is the useful part: these three are usually treated as three separate complaints with three separate fixes, and he ties all of them to one cause.** **It also independently corroborates [[_Sources/YT_gRQaOBGDBa4_elitbalkon_balcony_thermal_envelope|his own insulation video's]] mould mechanism from the other direction — that one says thin insulation makes cold spots, this says stagnant humid air is what condenses on them.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. ICE ON WINDOWS — the diagnostic reframe, and the best single idea in the batch

> ***«Окно — это же не греющий элемент помещения, это не радиатор, это не толстая стена. Окно — это светопрозрачная ограждающая конструкция.»***

**Thermal imaging, as he describes it:** wall at +22, window surface at +17 or +15, some places +5, and somewhere around **0 °C**. **At −20 to −25 outside, that zero-degree zone is where ice appears.**

**⚠️⚠️ AND THE QUESTION THAT REFRAMES THE WHOLE PROBLEM:**

> ***«Окно — это же не источник воды. Там нету ни родника, там нету подведённого трубопровода.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ SO THE MOISTURE IS COMING FROM INSIDE THE ROOM, AND ICE ON A WINDOW IS A HUMIDITY PROBLEM, NOT A WINDOW PROBLEM.** **The window is merely where the coldest surface happens to be. Replacing the window does not address the cause — which is why the vault's existing "condensation fix" material is a retrofit rather than a cure.**

**His remedies, in his order — and note they are all about the AIR, not the glass:**

1. **Open the window slightly.** Winter air is cold and dry; it dilutes the moisture-laden indoor air, which then leaves via the extract.
2. **If you will not open it: a wall valve or supply equipment**, so air enters through the wall instead — he notes this also spares houseplants a cold draught.
3. **A dehumidifier** — mains-powered, collects moisture into a reservoir.
4. **⚠️⚠️ REMOVE THE MOISTURE SOURCES, named concretely: an aquarium, watering plants, and raising the room temperature.** Move the aquarium to another room; water plants less, or move them out. **→ The vault had nothing naming indoor humidity sources at all.**
5. **⚠️⚠️ THE RADIATOR'S REAL FUNCTION: it is a screen (ширма).** It cuts off the cold air falling off the glazing **and keeps humid room air from reaching the glass and profile in the first place.**
6. **⚠️⚠️ THE WINDOWSILL TRAP: a windowsill that overhangs and covers the radiator stops it working as intended — the heat goes forward into the room instead of up across the glass. *«Чем шире подоконник, чем он больше перекрывает радиатор, тем хуже.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ §5 AND §6 TOGETHER ARE THE MOST ACTIONABLE THING HERE, because they turn a cosmetic choice into a performance one.** **Windowsill depth is normally decided on looks and on whether you want to put things on it. This says an over-deep sill over a radiator causes the condensation that the vault's existing [[13_Surfaces_and_Finishes/analysis/Windows_Hardware_Selection|windowsill-drilled directional vent]] exists to retrofit.** **The vault held the cure; this is the cause.**
