## Genuinely New Content

- **Bathtub wall-opening (niche) precise sizing rule, new specific figure**: for a 170cm tub set into a three-wall alcove with no tile yet installed, the rough opening should be either exactly 170cm (if the walls will be chased/recessed to fit the tub with no tile buildup inside the niche) or 172cm (if tile + adhesive will line the niche's interior faces, since that buildup is roughly 0.5cm per side). Routed to [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Structure_and_Framing]].
- **Washing-machine niche clearance restated as a tolerance, not a fixed number**: don't build a niche to the machine's exact nominal width (e.g. exactly 60cm) — leave roughly 1-2cm clearance per side so the machine actually slides in. Consistent with, not exceeding, this page's existing "64cm niche for a 60cm machine" figure (2cm/side) — recorded as a corroborating restatement, not a new number.
- **Toilet installation-frame finished height and a wall-reinforcement mechanism, new for this store**: the frame should be set so the finished toilet seat sits 40-44cm above the finished floor. **Mechanism, genuinely new**: once boxed in, the wall covering the frame must be at least two sheets of standard drywall or one sheet of moisture-resistant GVL — thinner cladding risks the toilet's own lower rear edge pressing through the wall face under a sitting person's weight over time, since the seated user's weight bears on the bowl against the wall behind it via the frame's own two mounting studs. Routed to [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Structure_and_Framing]] as an addition to the existing frame-load-rating content.
- **Tropical/rain-shower riser upsized relative to standard distribution — new specific figure**: if the rest of the bathroom's supply is run in ~16mm pipe, the branch feeding a tropical/rain shower head specifically should step up to ~20mm, since a rain head needs meaningfully higher flow volume than a standard shower/mixer outlet. Routed to [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Fixtures_Mixers_and_Sinks]].
- **Vanity-cabinet stub-out coordination rule, new for this store**: before finalizing water-supply and drain stub-out positions for a sink, confirm where the vanity cabinet's own mounting hardware (anchors/brackets/legs) will land, so a stub-out or embedded wire doesn't end up directly behind a drilling/anchoring point used to hang the cabinet. Routed to [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Fixtures_Mixers_and_Sinks]].
- **Heated-floor cable damage mechanism specific to pre-grout joint cleanup, new for this store**: after a tile-setter lays tile over an electric heating mat and tests it (confirming no breaks), a second failure point exists between tiling and grouting — cleaning excess tile adhesive out of the joints (to prepare them for grout) can physically nick/cut the heating cable, which sits just beneath the tile. **Recommended mitigation**: protect the mat with a cement-based self-leveling screed poured over it (between mat and tile) rather than embedding it directly under tile adhesive alone — and re-test continuity a second time after tiling, before grouting. Routed to [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Heated_Floor_and_Thermostat]].
