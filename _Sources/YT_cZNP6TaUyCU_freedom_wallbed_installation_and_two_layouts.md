## Why these three, and how to weight them

**From the [[_Inbox/planning/freedom_channel_triage_20260915|FREE_DOM triage]], which argued that this maker matters because he is NOT Progress Design**: the wall-bed pages rest on 13 sources of which Progress Design is 8, so an independent maker's account is worth more than another video from the saturated one. ⚠️ **He is the same channel as `wfmIOYsE2Hs` and `qMGSfO-glvc`, so nothing here corroborates THOSE two.**

**⚠️⚠️ THE INSTALLATION VIDEO IS THE MOST VALUABLE THING IN EITHER CHANNEL, and the reason is its form: it is a working record of fitters doing the job, with no pitch anywhere in it.** **A seller describing installation says what should happen; this shows what does.**

⚠️ **Region resolves to OMSK**, which the vault did not previously know for this maker — relevant because his two earlier videos were filed `region_unresolved`.

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE CONTRADICTION — this unit is built to be assembled ONCE

> ***«Боковые с низом кровати собираются и монтируются ОДИН РАЗ, их многоразовая разборка-сборка не нужна, поэтому сборка на КОНФИРМАТ.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS CUTS DIRECTLY AGAINST THE FINDING THIS VAULT RECORDED TWO DAYS AGO.** [[_Sources/YT_KgBcJ4zAHi8_progressdesign_seven_year_owner_account|Progress Design's seven-year household]] had their units **dismantled by the maker's fitters for a renovation and refitted undamaged**, and that finding was used on [[00_Master/Bedroom_Design_Principles|Bedroom Design Principles]] to **split this project's Phase-1 decision in two** — wall and wiring are rough-stage commitments, joinery and mechanism are not.
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE SPLIT STILL HOLDS, BUT IT IS NOW CONDITIONAL ON THE FASTENING, WHICH IS A SPECIFIABLE THING RATHER THAN A PROPERTY OF WALL BEDS.** **Confirmat screws are a one-time fixing — they cut their own thread in chipboard and lose grip when backed out and re-driven.** A carcass built on confirmats is not designed to come apart twice; one built on demountable fittings is. **Neither maker is wrong: they build differently.**
>
> **→ SO THE QUESTION TO ASK A SUPPLIER IS NOW SHARP AND CHECKABLE: is the carcass assembled on confirmats or on demountable fittings, and will you dismantle and refit it later?** **Recorded as an open, vendor-dependent question rather than as a settled capability — and the Phase-1 reasoning that leaned on it needs that caveat attached.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. THE STRAPS ARE A TOOL, NOT PACKAGING — and they imply a strut life nobody advertises

> **After the gas struts are fitted the transport straps come off, and then:**
>
> ***«Ремни никуда не выбрасываем, ОСТАВЛЯЕМ их. Если надо будет заменить газлифты впоследствии, ЛЕТ ЧЕРЕЗ ПЯТЬ, нужны будут ремни — поэтому их надо куда-то спрятать надёжно.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ TWO FINDINGS IN ONE SENTENCE, AND BOTH ARE NEW TO THIS VAULT.**
>
> **First, a practical one worth acting on**: the straps that hold the bed shut against the struts are **the tool you need to change the struts safely later**, because with the struts off and no restraint the leaf is dangerous. **They must be kept and their location recorded.** **Nobody who has not been told this would keep them.**
>
> **⚠️⚠️ Second, and much bigger: A FITTER'S WORKING ASSUMPTION THAT THE STRUTS GET REPLACED AT ABOUT FIVE YEARS.** **Set that against what the makers' own catalogues claim** — [[_Sources/YT_jZAAwUZVb0I_progressdesign_spec_and_cost_structure|Progress Design]] rates its Spanish struts at **20,000 cycles ≈ «примерно 27 лет ежедневного использования»**, and another maker claims 30,000. **He is not disputing the cycle count; he is assuming replacement an order of magnitude sooner.**
>
> **→ These can both be true and the reconciliation is the useful part: a cycle rating is not a service life.** **A gas strut loses charge with time and temperature whether or not it is cycled, so the binding limit is likely AGE, not use.** ⚠️ **Neither source says this outright — it is inference, flagged as such.** **But it makes the catalogue figure the wrong number to plan around, and it strengthens the existing conclusion that STRUT REPLACEABILITY matters more than the headline warranty.**
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ AND IT LANDS ON THIS PROJECT'S PLAN.** Installing now and activating in Phase 2 (~3–4 years) means **the struts may be near replacement at the moment the bed enters daily use.** Combined with the 3-year mechanism warranty, that makes *"can I buy these struts separately, and what do they cost"* a question to settle **before ordering**, not later.

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. The acceptance test needs a condition attached — it is only valid WITH the mattress

**During the fit, repeatedly:** *«Без матраса она будет подниматься»*, and they weight it with a suitcase to hold it down; at the end, *«матраса нет — без матраса кровать поднимается сама»*. **Then, with one fitted:** *«С матрасом НУЛЕВАЯ ТОЧКА где-то будет здесь, дальше она будет закрываться сама.»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS QUALIFIES THE PASS/FAIL TEST THIS VAULT RECORDS on [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|the mechanisms page]]** — *"a correctly matched bed must NOT rise by itself when down"*, taken from this same maker's `qMGSfO-glvc`. **An unmattressed bed self-lifting is NORMAL and expected, because the strut is sized for the mattress.**
>
> **→ So the test as written could fail a perfectly good bed at handover, if handover happens before the mattress arrives.** **The condition belongs in the test: judge the zero point with the actual mattress in place, and expect self-lift without it.** **Corrected on the page.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Independent corroboration of the skirting rule — different maker, same requirement

> ***«В квартире установлены плинтуса, поэтому придётся их ДЕМОНТИРОВАТЬ. После монтажа мы их поставим на место.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ [[_Sources/YT_u_gIgDqRdYw_progressdesign_wallbed_buyer_walkthrough|Progress Design]] states the same rule, and until now it rested on that one company.** **This is a different maker, in a different city, doing it on camera. The skirting rule is now genuinely cross-vendor** — which is exactly the kind of confirmation the triage predicted this channel could supply and more Progress Design videos could not.

## ⚠️ 5. The installation sequence, as actually performed

1. **⚠️ Walls checked with a LASER LEVEL on arrival, and a control re-measure taken** even though *«замеры мы проводили до монтажа»*. **Measure twice is not a slogan here; the second measure happens with the furniture in the room.**
2. Skirting removed (§4). Vertical marked, bracket set to the mark, hole centres marked, drilled, fixed.
3. **⚠️ The carcass is aligned with the BOTTOM DELIBERATELY UNFIXED** — *«внизу уголков нету, поэтому можно ещё низ подавать влево либо вправо, если это необходимо»* — and only then screwed home; 16 mm screws first, replaced by 30 mm to draw two panels together.
4. Mechanism fitted, then the supports, then the headboard flange.
5. **⚠️⚠️ THE HEADBOARD FLANGE DOES TWO JOBS**: it stops the mattress (sitting slightly proud of the side panel) **and acts as a spreader — *«как распорная планка, не даёт газлифтам сдавить боковые вовнутрь»*.** **→ The gas struts push the side panels INWARD; something has to resist that. A structural detail this vault did not hold.**
6. The bed is offered up and the mechanism holes caught top-first, then the lower ones by sliding it back and forth. **Without struts fitted it is heavy — two people throughout, *«удобнее собирать, конечно же, вдвоём»*.**
7. **⚠️ Levelling is done LYING DOWN** — *«места очень мало между кроватью и регулировочными отверстиями, поэтому приходится всё делать лёжа»* — with a hex key on a clamping plate.
8. **⚠️ Struts fitted last, nuts *«аккуратно подтягиваем, сильно не тянем»*.** ⚠️ **On one side the PAINT fouled the strut seat and had to be cleaned off** — a finish-tolerance clash worth knowing about when the wall is freshly decorated.
9. Straps removed and kept (§2); edges filed; side shield and the two pencil cases fitted; end caps last.

## ⚠️⚠️ 6. The depth trick — a deliberate non-flush front, to keep a walking route

**In two of the three projects the wall bed is made SHALLOWER than the units beside it, and the pencil cases are brought forward.**

> ***«Глубина самой шкаф-кровати МЕНЬШЕ, чем глубина пенала и шкафа-купе… если бы мы сделали всё вровень, здесь бы не было прохода.»*** **The alternative he rejected — running the headboard flush — *«мы бы потеряли расстояние транзитное между стеной и кроватью»*.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A FLUSH, MONOLITHIC FRONT IS THE DEFAULT AESTHETIC INSTINCT AND IT COSTS CIRCULATION.** **Deliberately stepping the bed back buys a transit route past it when it is down** — which is precisely the clearance problem [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Installation_and_Daily_Use|the installation page]] records from the household that chose a projector over a wall TV. **Same problem, a joinery answer rather than an equipment answer.**
>
> ⚠️ **Dimensions given**: deep wardrobe **550 mm**, pencil-case shelves **350 mm** *«удобно хранить вещи, они располагаются в один ряд»*, antresols **420 mm** deep and low — *«невысокие, но глубокие, поэтому вещи туда войдут, те же подушки, одеяло дополнительные»*. **→ That last one answers the bedding-storage question the retention debate keeps circling: a 420 mm antresol over the bed takes the pillows and spare duvet.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 7. Two build rules stated as always-rules

- **⚠️⚠️ BUILD TO THE CEILING, AND WALL TO WALL.** *«Мы ВСЕГДА рекомендуем делать всю мебель до потолка… чтобы она не была обрезанной, тогда она будет смотреться монолитно, от пола до потолка и от стены до стены.»*
  > **→ ⚠️ Not in conflict with [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Installation_and_Daily_Use|the 5 cm erection gap]] recorded from Progress Design — that gap is a FITTING allowance closed afterwards with a filler panel. Both makers end floor-to-ceiling; they differ on how. Worth stating together so the 5 cm is not mistaken for a finished reveal.**
- **⚠️⚠️ ALL UNITS ON ADJUSTABLE FEET** — *«вся мебель у нас на регулируемых ножках, поэтому мы нивелируем все неровности пола»*.
  > **→ Ties to this vault's screed and floor-flatness material: the joinery absorbs floor deviation, so the tolerance that matters for fitted furniture is what the FEET can take up, not perfect flatness.** ⚠️ Worth asking a supplier for the adjustment range.

## ⚠️ 8. Layout findings from the two project videos

- **⚠️⚠️ A BUNK BED WAS CONSIDERED FIRST AND REJECTED ON FOOTPRINT** — *«хотели поставить сначала двухъярусную кровать, но она заняла [место]… единственное правильное и экономное по квадратуре решение — это шкафы-кровати»*. **→ A SECOND, INDEPENDENT arrival at the bunk-versus-wall-bed rejection**, alongside the Progress Design household that rejected one because it is permanently visible. **Two makers, two reasons — footprint and visibility.**
- **⚠️⚠️ WHY SLIDING DOORS AND NOT HINGED, stated as a clearance calculation**: a small sofa is planned at the window, and *«распашные двери… будут упираться в диван и мешать транзиту»* — hinged doors could not open fully. **→ A checkable rule for this project's own wardrobes: a hinged door needs its swing clear of whatever else is planned, and a sofa placed later can retrospectively break a wardrobe specified earlier.**
- **⚠️ Four sleeping places in a one-room flat**: two wall beds plus a fold-out sofa, with **a wardrobe with a rail placed BETWEEN the two beds**, plus two workstations and shelving with a TV.
- **⚠️⚠️ AND HE STATES THE LIMIT HONESTLY, which a seller rarely does**: deployed, *«в комнате место остаётся только для транзита вокруг кровати и дивана — места больше нет»*, and in the other project *«при опущенной кровати в комнате места не остаётся совсем»*. **→ The space saving is real and it is a DAYTIME saving. At night the room is full. That is the honest framing of the whole device category.**
- **A linen drawer doubles as a bedside table in the evening**, holding the TV remotes; the freed floor takes **an ironing board or a drying rack** — ⚠️ independently corroborating the ironing-board point from [[_Sources/YT_ztubNCfTyhg_kachanova_seven_transformer_households|Качанова's households]].

## ⚠️ 9. Straps again — the same maker, compromising his own position

**He fits strap retention here while restating his dislike of it:** *«Два спальных места с РЕМНЯМИ. Я хоть не очень люблю ремни, так как они иногда вот бывает что расстёгиваются, но здесь негде было… место и для хранения одежды»*.

> **→ ⚠️ NOT A NEW VOICE ON THE OPEN RETENTION QUESTION — it is the against-straps maker already in this vault. But it is informative anyway: his objection is practical, not principled, and he abandons it when there is nowhere to put a bedding drawer.** **→ The real variable is whether the room affords separate bedding storage; where it does not, even the strap sceptic uses straps.** **The 420 mm antresol in §6 is the same answer from the other direction.**
