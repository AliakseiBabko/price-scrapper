## Bathroom — Non-Standard Design Features and Their Practical Fixes

1. **Large bathroom windows, and the practical privacy fix**: the
   apartment has genuinely large windows in the bathroom (unusual enough
   that the practitioner says he was surprised himself on first seeing
   the object). Stated solution set: blinds, Roman-style curtains, or —
   as a glazing-level alternative — frosted/sandblasted film or tinting
   applied directly to the glass, which blocks a neighbor's direct view
   into the room without blocking light entirely.
2. **Curved ("radius") wall clad in mosaic, and why it needed
   plastering despite accurate masonry**: the developer's original
   foam-block masonry for this curved wall was built reasonably
   accurately, but had to be plastered anyway because mosaic tile is
   unusually sensitive to substrate precision — a masonry wall "accurate
   enough" for ordinary tile isn't necessarily accurate enough for
   mosaic.

## Walls / Ceilings — A Novel Curved-Wall Beacon Fabrication Technique

3. **⚠️ Curved/radius-wall beacon-making method, genuinely new to this
   store**: find the wall's circle center, then mark the intended radius
   as a line on the floor (offset ~15mm out from the existing foam-block
   face). Mark two horizontal reference lines on the wall (one ~15-20cm
   above the floor, one higher up); drive self-tapping screws along both
   lines so the lower row's screws align with the floor's radius line,
   then set the upper row's screws level with the lower row using a
   level. Tie two lengths of metal-plastic pipe to these screws with thin
   wire — the pipe's own flexibility lets it follow the curve — then
   pack the pipe with plastering compound and let it set. Once cured,
   the wire is removed, leaving two curved beacon strips that define the
   radius wall's finished plane. The same technique was mirrored to
   create matching curved beacons at the top of the same wall (left and
   right sides). A genuinely distinct beacon-fabrication method from
   every straight-wall beacon technique already on `Waterproofing_and_
   Plastering.md` (laser-level rigid strip, string-beacon).
4. **Mosaic-layout precision, real coordination example**: the tiler
   pre-calculated the mosaic course so a whole, uncut tile pattern runs
   continuously corner to corner around the curved wall, and separately
   coordinated the bathtub podium's height/edge so the transition point
   where tile ends and mosaic begins lines up exactly with where the
   podium itself begins — described as genuinely painstaking
   calculation work.
5. **Named materials**: wall/floor tile brand Atlas Concorde; the mosaic
   and tile joints were both grouted with a two-component grout (named,
   ASR-uncertain exact brand rendering: "Star Elite"/possibly a
   Starlike-family product) that the tiler specifically praised as easy
   to work with.

## Plumbing — Real Internal Dispute, Resolved by Calling the Manufacturer's Dealer

6. **⚠️ A genuinely candid QC/self-correction case, invited for viewer
   feedback rather than presented as settled**: standard utility-cabinet
   plumbing (Rehau PEX-A piping, manifolds, pressure regulators, a
   backwash filter system, leak-protection system, meters, fine filters)
   with a **water-hammer compensator ("гидрокомпенсатор") mounted on the
   fine filter** — this specific placement caused a real internal
   disagreement within the company (the practitioner explicitly says it
   became an argument). They called the product's official dealer to
   ask, were told this placement was incorrect, and **redid the work to
   match the dealer's own reference photo**. The practitioner explicitly
   invites more specialized viewers to weigh in with their own opinion
   in the comments — a rare instance of a source presenting a real
   technical dispute as still open rather than resolved with full
   confidence.
7. **Towel warmer — electric-over-hydronic recommendation, corroborating
   this project's already-established stance**: states plainly that
   water-based towel warmers are increasingly something the company
   avoids recommending, in favor of electric units. Matches this
   project's existing, well-sourced electric-over-hydronic case on
   `Hygienic_Shower_and_Towel_Warmer.md` — not re-counted as a new fact,
   recorded as a same-conclusion corroboration from a new practitioner.

## Bathtub — Podium Rationale and a Site-Protection Practice

8. **Podium (raised platform) rationale, a real distance-driven design
   reason**: the bathtub sits on a raised podium specifically because
   the tub's own drain sits an unusually long distance from the sewage
   stack ("фановая труба") — the podium's slope ensures water actually
   drains cleanly across that distance rather than pooling. The podium
   is finished in the same mosaic as the curved wall.
9. **Site-protection practice**: the finished mosaic-clad podium was
   covered with hardboard ("оргалит") to prevent it being scuffed or
   damaged during the remainder of the ongoing work.

## Other Real Layout Detail

10. **Washer/dryer drain prep**: two separate sewage stub-outs prepared
    for a washing machine and a separate drying cabinet.
11. **Ceiling design — a curtain-pocket detail**: electrical wiring
    routed in black corrugated conduit above a drywall ceiling section;
    the remaining ceiling area gets a stretch ceiling set slightly lower
    than the drywall section, joined via an L-shaped/right-angle
    transition that deliberately forms a recessed niche — curtains will
    hang down from inside this niche rather than from a surface-mounted
    track.
