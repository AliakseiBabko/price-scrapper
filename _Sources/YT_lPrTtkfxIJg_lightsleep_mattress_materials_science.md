## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE MEASUREMENT THAT RESOLVES AN OPEN DISAGREEMENT IN THIS VAULT

**[[14_Furniture/analysis/Mattress_Construction_And_Selection|The mattress page]] carries an unresolved conflict: ARMOS and Круглов say "don't chase spring count", Мебель & Текстиль says smaller coil diameter and higher density is simply better — and the vault's own note says the two "may be arguing about different variables… and the buyer can see neither at the point of sale."**

**He measures both levels.**

**A SINGLE spring, from each block:**

| Block | Spring Ø | Wire Ø | Coils | **Load** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **256 / m²** | 6 cm | 1.9 mm | 6 | **2.040 kg** |
| **Multipacket ~512–570 / m²** | 4.3–4.5 cm | 1.3 mm | — | **1.473 kg** |
| **1000 / m²** | 2.9–3 cm | 1.1 mm | 10 | **1.107 kg** |

**The SAME AREA of block, so different spring counts:**

| Block | Springs on the test area | **Load** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **256 / m²** | 9 | **18.360 kg** |
| **Multipacket** | 20 | **29.460 kg** |
| **1000 / m²** | 36 | **39.852 kg** |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ BOTH CAMPS WERE RIGHT ABOUT DIFFERENT LEVELS. A spring from a 1000-block is the WEAKEST individually — about half the 256-block spring — yet the 1000-BLOCK is roughly TWICE as strong as the 256-block over the same area.**
>
> **→ So a high count does not mean a firmer mattress at the spring level and does mean a stronger, finer-following block. "Don't chase the count" is right as a warning against treating the number as a quality score; "denser is better" is right about block behaviour. The vault can now state that instead of recording a standoff.**
>
> ⚠️ **His own demonstration, his own rig, not independently reproduced — but the method is simple enough that it could be.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Two density manipulations — one corroborates Гуру Сна, one is new

- **⚠️ The formula is springs per SQUARE METRE.** If a shop quotes "density 2000", ask what it is per — *«может, они имеют в виду на спальное место? А может, на матрас? А может, на 10 матрасов?»* **→ Independently corroborates the vault's existing Гуру Сна finding that the count is quoted in a gamed unit.**
- **⚠️⚠️ NEW: STACKING BLOCKS DOES NOT CHANGE DENSITY.** Put a second 256 block under the first and it is still 256/m². Density is AREAL, not volumetric — *«вот оно может идти, идти, идти — плотность не меняется»*.
- **⚠️⚠️ The same error for springless: the unit is kg/m³, so making a BIGGER mattress out of density-18 foam does not raise its density.** *«Вы можете сделать матрас 10 м на 20 м при плотности 18 — она так и будет 18.»* He says grades **18, 20, 22 have effectively no load-bearing** — *«даже заводы-производители прочерк ставят»*. ⚠️ **Consistent in direction with the vault's existing Мебель & Текстиль figures (plain foam 10–15, furniture-grade 35–40).**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. FOAM MARKING DECODED — and the middle number is the one that matters

**A foam designation has three parts: MARK + DENSITY + a comfort number.**

| Mark | Meaning |
| :--- | :--- |
| **HS** гипер-софт | super soft |
| **S** | soft |
| **ST** | standard firmness |
| **EL** | raised load-bearing — firm |
| **HL / «Ашель»** | high load-bearing — super firm |
| **HR** | high resilience / elasticity |
| **L** | **latex-LIKE foam** |

> **⚠️⚠️ THE KEY: the MIDDLE number is DENSITY, and density is what determines SERVICE LIFE** — how long the piece holds its initial state with minimal loss of firmness and thickness. **The LAST number governs only comfort — the recipe.**
>
> **→ So "Ашель 4065" in his own build reads: mark Ашель, density 40, comfort 65. This decodes the recipe notation that mattress sellers quote and the vault could not previously read.**
>
> **⚠️ AND A NAMING TRAP: L and HR are latex-LIKE foams — *«никак не искусственный латекс»*.** ⚠️ **Plus: a material at 15–20% natural content cannot honestly be called natural — *«был бы он хотя бы 50 на 50»*.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. COIL COUNT — a variable the vault had nothing on, with a failure mechanism

> ***«Жёсткость пружины обратно пропорциональна числу рабочих витков.»*** **More coils → softer spring, smoother travel. Fewer coils → stiffer and cruder.**

**⚠️⚠️ THE SCAM HE FOUND at a furniture-fittings wholesaler, sold as an "anti-crisis option": a 14 cm spring with 3.5–4 coils instead of the standard 6.** *«Из пружины половина материала забрали.»*

**The mechanism, stated properly:** fewer coils → greater pitch between coils → greater coil twist angle → load carried by fewer coils and more load per coil → **higher stress in the wire** → more plastic deformation → **faster accumulation of fatigue damage and microcracks** → loss of stiffness, softening, loss of load-bearing, eventually fracture.

**⚠️ His lifespan prediction, all else equal (14 cm, high-carbon steel, same wire and spring diameter, 100 kg): 4 coils fails first, then 6; 10 coils lasts longest.**

### ⚠️⚠️ And the autopsy — a mattress fails by losing STIFFNESS long before it loses THICKNESS

> **They dissected a used spring mattress. Thickness loss was small — about a centimetre. But measuring LOAD-BEARING by resting two 10 kg plates 3.5 cm thick: where there had been no load the plate sat on top; where there had been local load it sank the full 3.5 cm.**
>
> ***«Потеря толщины небольшая, а вот потеря жёсткости — этого хватило, чтобы человек уже сказал: всё, я не могу на этом спать.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE ACTIONABLE CONSEQUENCE: you cannot judge a worn mattress by looking at it.** "It isn't sagging" is the wrong test; the failure is in stiffness, which is invisible. **The vault's mattress page has service-life discussion but no test for when the life has ended. This supplies one — load it locally and compare against an unloaded area.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Two marketing claims he calls outright fake

- **⚠️⚠️ "Double / triple tempering" (двойная, тройная закалка) is FAKE.** Modern tempering happens **by impulse as the spring is wound** — a discharge during coiling. There is no repeated furnace firing. He compares it to *«двойная, тройная мерсеризация»* in textiles, also fake.
- **⚠️⚠️ Proprietary steel-grade claims are FAKE.** Factories use high-carbon steel; "our special HD carbon steel" is marketing.

> **→ These join a pattern the vault has now collected across five mattress sources: certificates prove nothing (Мебель & Текстиль), filler names without thicknesses are meaningless (Гуру Сна), branded foams are just polyurethane (Гуру Сна), and now tempering and steel-grade claims.** **The common shape: the spec sheet is decorated on exactly the axes the buyer cannot check.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 6. Textile science — the part no other source in this vault has

**Fibre taxonomy, stated precisely:** natural exists ready-made; **artificial (искусственное)** is made by man from NATURAL polymers needing no synthesis — raw material natural, fibre man-made; **synthetic** is built from scratch from monomers, i.e. petrochemical products.

> **⚠️ Viscose is ARTIFICIAL — neither natural nor synthetic. Polyester is SYNTHETIC, *«не является натуральным, не является искусственным, как заявляют некоторые»*.**

**⚠️⚠️ Why it matters for the top of a mattress — and the number that drives everything:**

> **A sleeper loses 100–300+ ml of water a night.** The textile against the body must absorb it and release it, for thermoregulation and microclimate.

| | **Hygroscopicity** |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cotton | **7–8%** |
| **Viscose** | **⚠️⚠️ 12–14% — nearly double** |
| **Polyester** | **⚠️⚠️ 0.5%** |

- **Viscose**: low pilling, low shrinkage, silk-like lustre when well finished (**⚠️ so it gets passed off as silk**), pleasant hand, **low static precisely because it is hygroscopic**, good air permeability, hypoallergenic, biodegradable, **and high thermal conductivity — it COOLS, which is what you want under a body.**
- **⚠️⚠️ Viscose's main weakness: it loses up to 50% of its strength WET.** Plus low form stability, high creasing, poor wash and light resistance. **→ The care rule that follows: clean a mattress top DRY, or damp but delicately — no scrubbing, no wringing, no drying in sun.**
- **Polyester**: excellent strength, form stability, abrasion and heat resistance, **strong wet as well as dry** — but **0.5% hygroscopicity and poor air permeability**, so the night's moisture stays under you and you overheat and sweat more, compounding. **Plus high pilling and high static.**
- **⚠️⚠️ AND A CROSS-BATCH CORROBORATION: he says they tried anti-pilling and antistatic treatments and *«всё это ненадолго»* — you must reapply them continually.** **Same structural claim as [[_Sources/YT_SwOVFr5YRP8_yakobi_upholstery_fabric_choice|Якоби's anti-vandal debunk]] already in this vault, in a different product category: an applied treatment is not a material property and expires.**

### ⚠️⚠️ 7. The best spec-sheet debunk in the batch — density does not describe a nonwoven

**Two bulk nonwovens, BOTH 300 g/m², visibly different in thickness.** The differences in the better one (Halcon):

1. **Hollow fibre** rather than solid — lighter for the same mass.
2. **Highly crimped, spiral** fibre rather than straight.
3. **Vertical, partly chaotic laydown** rather than horizontal.

**Result: a far more developed network of air channels and pockets → better air permeability → moisture enters and evaporates → better hygiene; and better form stability, where pressing the horizontal-laydown sample leaves a mark.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ SO g/m² ALONE DOES NOT DESCRIBE A NONWOVEN. Two materials identical on the spec line perform differently according to fibre hollowness, crimp and orientation.** **This goes one step further than the vault's existing "density is the variable, the name is noise" finding from Бастион on mineral wool: here even the density figure is insufficient.**
>
> **⚠️⚠️ AND A HAND TEST TO TELL THEM APART: pull the material. HORIZONTAL laydown separates into layers — *«она расползается… идёт пластами»*. CHAOTIC or VERTICAL laydown does not come apart — *«оно как одно целое»*.** **A free, physical test for a property no label states.**

## ⚠️ 8. His own build, and a construction position worth recording

**Recipe:** Ашель 4065, 4 cm → **14 cm spring block, 1000/m² (EVA Spring)** → calendered geotextile isolator 500 g/m² both sides → **latex Artilat (Belgium) 6527, 4 cm** → Memory 5014 Energy, 4 cm.

- **⚠️⚠️ NO FOAM PERIMETER AT ALL.** He attributes this to American premium practice — a full 1000-spring block edge to edge — and contrasts it with the common construction of low-density springs in the centre with a 1000-spring or foam perimeter at the edges. ⚠️ **His claim about the American market is uncorroborated.**
- ⚠️ **The vault has no prior material on edge/perimeter construction, so this is recorded as one manufacturer's position rather than a settled question.**

## ⚠️ 9. The "assess a mattress in a minute" checklist, and two new items

- Size: either measure first, or buy the mattress and build the bed to it.
- **Knee angle 90° on sitting and standing → sets bed height → sets mattress height.** ⚠️ **FIFTH arrival at the 90° rule in this vault.**
- **⚠️⚠️ NEW — the ЦАРГА CHECK: make sure the bed's side rail does not catch your legs as you stand up.**
- **⚠️⚠️ NEW — clearances measured FROM THE MATTRESS: to the socket or switch, to the bedside table, to the wardrobe, to the window.** **→ Treats the mattress top as the datum for bedroom ergonomics, which no other source in this vault does, and it connects the mattress decision to [[14_Furniture/analysis/Bed_Frames_And_Bases|the clearance disagreement]] recorded there.**

**⚠️ And his closing heuristic: *«гарантия — это зеркало товара»*.** Materials, the finished article and the guarantee are one whole; if any one is evasive, there is a defect. ⚠️ **A reasonable screening rule, though he offers no warranty lengths to compare against.**
