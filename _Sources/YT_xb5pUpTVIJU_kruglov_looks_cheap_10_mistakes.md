plain white perimeter insert trim is now considered dated; **switching

genuinely premium options: the existing shadow-gap ("теневой") profile

profile type not previously named on this page, producing a stretch

present but effectively invisible), distinct from the shadow-gap
profile's visible recessed channel.

**Excessive glossy surfaces — a general critique, new topic for this
store**: heavy use of glossy facades, cabinets, mixers, and countertops
together causes glare/reflection throughout a room, described as
eye-fatiguing and reading as artificial/uncozy regardless of the
underlying material cost — framed as a "high price, cheap result"
combination distinct from this store's existing narrower gloss-vs-matte
warmth and glossy-ceiling-in-a-dark-room content.

**Fake/replica luxury furniture — a new ethical/practical distinction,
no prior coverage on this store**: distinguishes (1) knowingly buying a
replica of an expensive/exclusive piece specifically to appear wealthier
or more successful — explicitly discouraged — from (2) buying a piece
because it genuinely suits your taste/budget/style, only learning
afterward (or never learning) that it happens to be a replica of
something else — explicitly fine. For anyone drawn to scenario (1), the
source's concrete recommendation is to instead find a genuine original
product within your actual budget rather than a fake of something more
expensive — avoids compromised paint quality/hardware versus a true
replica, at the real price the product is actually worth.
