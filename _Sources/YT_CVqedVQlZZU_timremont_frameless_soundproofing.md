## Why frameless was chosen here (Decision Framework corroboration)

- **Chosen specifically because the room was too small to give up the
  depth a frame-based system requires** — direct confirmation of this
  store's existing Decision-Framework factor #2 (depth/space budget) on
  [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Soundproofing|Soundproofing]].
- **⚠️ Prerequisite not previously stated on this page: the wall must be
  plastered/skim-coated dead flat before a frameless system can be
  installed at all** — unlike a frame system (whose hangers can compensate
  for an uneven wall), a frameless panel is glued/fastened directly to the
  wall surface, so any waviness in the substrate telegraphs straight
  through into the panel. Checked with a straightedge ("правило") before
  starting; the practitioner notes he'd deliberately saved this wall for
  last in his own plastering work, after "practicing" on the apartment's
  other walls first.
- **Electrical routing must be fully chased/hidden in the wall before this
  system goes up** — a frameless build has no cavity to route cable
  through afterward the way a frame system's air gap allows.

## Full Installation Technique (Sonoplat "Slim" Frameless System)

- **Material handling**: let delivered sheet panels rest flat on an even
  surface for 2-3 days before installing, so the material "takes" a flat
  shape — never store this panel type standing on edge.
- **Named parts list**: perimeter damper tape (branded "Сонаплат" tape),
  a separate vibration-damping tape, polymer hammer-in dowels, 5×30mm
  soundproofing-rated self-tapping screws, acoustic sealant, a
  soundproofing-rated electrical outlet backing box ("звукоизоляционный
  подрозетник"), the Sonoplat sand-filled panel itself, and 12.5mm
  acoustic-rated drywall ("акустик ГКЛ") as the finish layer.
- **Perimeter prep**: damper tape glued along every wall/ceiling/floor
  junction, sealant applied on top of the tape before the first panel row
  goes up.
- **Tongue-and-groove edge trimming**: where a panel meets a wall/corner,
  cut off the panel's own tongue-and-groove edge so it sits flush against
  the perimeter tape with no gap — acoustic sealant applied at every
  panel-to-panel joint specifically to prevent "sound bridges" ("мостики")
  at the seams.
- **⚠️ Cut-edge taping rule, corroborating and extending this store's
  existing brief note** (previously recorded from `yt_fnTjaXPEDDE`: "any
  cut edge of a sand-filled panel must be taped or the sand filler
  spills"): this source confirms the same rule for the same product type
  and adds that the manufacturer supplies a dedicated tape for exactly
  this purpose, applied immediately after any cut.
- **Cutting technique lesson**: a utility knife works but is slow, and its
  blade dulls quickly cutting this panel directly on a hard screed
  surface — cutting on a softer foam-board (пеноплекс) surface underneath
  preserves the blade edge much longer. For outlet-box cutouts
  specifically, an oscillating multi-tool ("реноватор") was faster and
  cleaner than the utility knife.
- **Fastening**: polymer dowels hammered (not screwed) into 6mm
  pre-drilled holes — described as holding very securely despite the
  simple hammer-set method. First row set to a laser level; every
  subsequent row referenced off the first rather than independently
  re-leveled. Panels installed in a staggered/brick pattern ("в шахматном
  порядке"), 3 vertical fastening columns per full sheet, dowel spacing no
  more than 30cm apart.
- **Electrical box integration**: existing outlet boxes were re-chased
  deeper into the wall specifically to leave room for the soundproofing-
  rated outlet backing box within the new wall build-up, positioned so the
  final box face ends up flush with the finished drywall plane (not
  proud/sunken) — sealant applied around the box opening too.
- **Second-layer (acoustic drywall) fastening**: screws driven into the
  Sonoplat panel (not into the original wall/plaster) with a 10mm
  counterbore drilled first so the screw head sits recessed and doesn't
  telegraph through the finish coat.
- **⚠️ Named mistake**: cut a full-length tongue/groove trim on one panel
  when only a partial-length trim was needed — acknowledged directly on
  camera as a rushed-work error.
- **⚠️ Fastener-hits-rebar workaround**: when a dowel hole hits embedded
  rebar and won't seat, relocate the fastener point rather than forcing it,
  and fill the abandoned hole with acoustic sealant.
- **Perimeter-tape trim residue, a flagged manufacturer feedback item**:
  trimming the excess perimeter tape after installation reliably leaves
  behind sealant residue that's hard to fully clean — the practitioner
  reports having raised this directly with the TechnoSonus/Sonoplat
  manufacturer as a product/process gap, not yet resolved as of this
  video.
- **⚠️ Real total assembly thickness, a directly comparable figure to this
  store's existing frame-system depth data**: the finished two-layer
  build-up (Sonoplat panel + acoustic drywall) measured **34mm total** —
  substantially thinner than the ~10cm total assembly this store's
  existing frame-system source records, confirming the frame-vs-frameless
  depth tradeoff in concrete numbers rather than just qualitatively.
