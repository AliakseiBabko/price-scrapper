## New facts

1. **⚠️ Work-triangle (fridge-sink-stove) distance range stated as 1-2.5m** — a real
   numeric discrepancy with this store's existing Zlobin-sourced work-triangle rule on
   `Kitchen_General.md` ("each side ≤1.5m"). Recorded as an open numeric variance between
   two independent sources rather than merged/resolved — see routing note below.
2. **Backsplash outlet-count recommendation: 4-6 outlets, distributed at different points
   along the backsplash**, not clustered at one or two locations — explicit rationale:
   modern households routinely need several small appliances (toaster, blender, juicer,
   waffle iron, grill) powered simultaneously; 1-2 outlets forces choosing which
   appliance gets unplugged.
3. **⚠️ Avoid a classic rimmed/bordered inset sink (raised lip where it meets the
   countertop)** — called an outdated design that traps debris at the rim/seam, making
   cleaning harder over the sink's lifetime. **Prefer an undermount sink with no visible
   lip, or a fully integrated sink made from the same solid-surface material as the
   countertop itself** — no seam/rim means debris sweeps directly into the sink with
   nothing to trap it.
4. **Sliding/extending dining table clearance planning**: when a kitchen includes an
   extending table, plan the fully-extended footprint and its resulting walkway clearance
   in advance, not just the table's closed/default size.
5. **Named narrow pull-out pantry type: "карго" (cargo) pull-out cabinets** for bottles,
   baking trays, or cutting boards — a specific named storage-hardware category not
   previously recorded on this store's kitchen pages.
6. **Task lighting positioned near the upper-cabinet edge (not at the backsplash/wall
   junction) gives more uniform illumination across the full work surface** — the
   backsplash-junction mounting (an older convention, per this source) only lights the
   counter's rear corner nearest the wall. **This corroborates and reinforces the
   facade-edge-mounting side of this page's existing open lighting-placement disagreement**
   (`YT_dJMsXYUyh7A` vs `YT_f3EI72Nwemk`, Round 1 vs Round 8) — recorded as additional
   support for the facade-edge position, not a resolution of that disagreement.
7. **Smart-home voice-control integration for task lighting** (e.g. a "Alice"-type hub) —
   hands-free light on/off while actively cooking with dirty/wet hands, avoiding a
   physical switch.
8. **Dedicated downlight fixture aimed at the dining table** — a specific lighting-zone
   recommendation distinct from general cooking-zone task lighting, framed as making food
   look more appetizing and letting the table double as a secondary work surface.
9. **Named anti-fingerprint matte facade coating: "Феникс" (Phoenix)** — a specific
   super-matte coating brand recommended for reducing visible fingerprints/smudges,
   complementing this page's existing dark/light/glossy avoid-list.
10. **⚠️ Avoid bright/saturated facade colors (pink, red, green)** — distinct from this
    round's own `e3bHUlP0PMY` dark/light/glossy caution (a different axis: saturation, not
    lightness/gloss). Real-world client-regret claim: owners of highly saturated-color
    kitchens reportedly tire of the color within about a year. Recommend calm/neutral tones
    instead.
11. **Integrated dish-soap dispenser, top-refill type** — a built-in dispenser refillable
    from above the countertop, described as a genuine convenience upgrade.
12. **Door-mounted trash-bin attachment kit** — a specific bin-mounting method (the bin
    attaches directly to the inside face of the cabinet door itself) as an alternative to
    a drawer-rail pull-out bin (already on this store's `Kitchen_Furniture.md` from Round 8).
13. **Dedicated vertical or horizontal niche for cutting-board/baking-tray storage**
    (example placement: under the oven) — a specific storage-niche type not previously
    recorded on this store's kitchen pages.
14. **Built-in vacuum-sealer (вакуматор) integration into cabinetry** — recommended
    specifically for households that already frequently vacuum-seal food for storage.
15. **Countertop-to-windowsill merge technique**: where ceiling/window height allows,
    extend the countertop surface to physically merge with the windowsill into one
    continuous surface — **requires a planned convection gap (cut into the countertop
    itself) so warm air rises past the window and cool air is drawn in below**, to avoid
    condensation/heat-trapping at the glass. Framed as especially valuable in a compact
    kitchen needing maximum usable surface.

  "never actually seen installed" and impractical outside an extreme space-constrained

  rather than a formal two-sided callout, since this source doesn't affirmatively rebut
  the self-opening failure mode — it simply doesn't mention it while describing the trend.
