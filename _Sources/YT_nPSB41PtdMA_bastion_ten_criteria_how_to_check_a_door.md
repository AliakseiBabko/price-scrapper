## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE MECHANISM THE VAULT WAS MISSING — steel thickness is not only about burglary

> ***«Толщина стали — один из главных показателей… это не только взломостойкость. Она ещё отвечает и за геометрию всей металлоконструкции, то есть обеспечение прилегания дверного полотна к дверной коробке. И именно поэтому правильное прилегание будет обеспечивать ещё и шумоизоляцию.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS THE DIRECT ANSWER TO GUSEV'S POSITION, and it is an argument rather than a counter-assertion.** [[_Sources/YT_Mfzce0Qm4HM_prodveri_entrance_door_myth|Gusev]] says: buy thin steel, because what actually matters is soundproofing and odour sealing. **Бастион says those are the SAME VARIABLE — thin steel loses geometry, a leaf that does not seat cannot seal, and then no amount of sealing rubber helps.** **The two are not trading opinions; one of them has a causal chain and the other does not.**

**⚠️ His falsifiable thresholds for steel, all stated as things you can do to a sample:**

| Attack | Threshold |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Cutting with a utility knife** | **⚠️⚠️ anything under 3 mm cuts with an ordinary канцелярский нож; over 3 mm needs power tools** |
| **Prising with a crowbar (отжим)** | 1–1.5 mm prises easily; **2 mm harder but still achievable**; 3 mm *«крайне проблематично»* |
| His own summary | *«всё, что меньше 3 мм, под этот критерий [взломостойкости] не совсем попадает»* |

⚠️ **He states he prised a door open himself on camera, first attempt, successfully.** **Demonstration rather than assertion — but it is his own demonstration on his own channel and was not independently reviewed.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Building settlement — a root cause for a symptom already in the vault

> **A new-build settles for anything from one to ten years. If the door's structure is weak, the concrete wall loads the frame, the frame deforms, the diagonal goes out, and the deadbolts then bind against the frame — the key will not turn, or the door needs a shoulder or a lift on the handle to open.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ The vault already documents this exact SYMPTOM under "Diagnosing and Fixing a Binding Deadbolt" (Амельченко), whose root cause is a strike hole slightly off-centre and whose fix is reaming that hole.** **Бастион supplies a SECOND, structural root cause for the same symptom — and the two have different fixes.** **Reaming the strike plate on a door whose frame is being crushed by a settling building treats the symptom and will recur.**
>
> **⚠️⚠️ DIRECTLY RELEVANT TO THIS PROJECT, which is a new-build.** **The discriminator worth recording: Амельченко's cause is present from handover and static; settlement deformation appears or worsens over months to years and is usually accompanied by a visibly uneven gap.**
>
> ⚠️ **He attaches a 10-year structural warranty pitch to this. The warranty is promotional; the settlement mechanism is not, and matches [[_Sources/YT_6r8IVBsXjnE_novakey_six_door_selection_mistakes|Novakey's]] independent opening scenario — a door that starts jamming about six months after a finished renovation.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Rigidity ribs — where the vault had nothing, and the buyer's question is the wrong one

> ***«Многие продавцы употребляют: "у нас там два, пять, семь рёбер жёсткости"… Как они расположены, для чего они нужны — эту информацию мне, как правило, никто не раскрывает.»***

- **⚠️⚠️ Vertical-only ribs are the specific defect**: the leaf resists bending one way and folds *«гармошечкой»* the other. **BOTH vertical and horizontal are required.**
- **⚠️⚠️ And a counter-intuitive engineering trade, from a real customer case.** A customer asked for profile tube instead of angle, having read that a closed section is stiffer. **His answer: profile tube wall is up to ~2 mm, whereas their angle has a 4 mm flange welded flange-inward and tied into one frame.** **And the decisive part — cutting the lock pocket through a tube edge removes the full thickness and leaves about 5 mm of steel around the lock, while an angle only gets holes drilled for the bolts and is not weakened.**
- **What to avoid: vertical-only ribs, no ribs at all, or a bent outer sheet with MDF or chipboard packed into the leaf edge** — the last he calls *«просто недопустимо»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE TRANSFERABLE FINDING IS THE QUESTION, NOT THE ANSWER: rib COUNT is a marketing number. The three things that determine rigidity are what the ribs are made of, how they are oriented, and only then how many.** **A seller who answers the count and not the first two has told you nothing.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Hinges, the lock pocket, and the armoured escutcheon

**Hinges — a marketing tell you can spot on the shop floor:**

> **He describes seeing a 30 kg door in 1.2 mm steel fitted with THREE hinges** — *«это как раз тот самый маркетинг, чтобы впечатлить покупателя… это бесполезная история»*. **On their own leaves, which weigh 100–150 kg, three hinges are a necessity.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ HINGE COUNT IS A FUNCTION OF LEAF WEIGHT, NOT A QUALITY SIGNAL — and three hinges on a light door is evidence of marketing, not of strength.** Closed-type bearing hinges with a dust seal preferred. He rates axle-free (безосевые) Barq hinges well but says they do not suit his own heavy leaves.

**The lock zone — and the logical point that undercuts "thin steel is enough":**

Attacks named: cutting the lock out, drilling it, and **knocking the lock bodily inward** (he cites having seen a police battering ram drive a lock into a flat). Protection is a layered pocket, in his doors from ~5 mm to 18 mm or more: **стекломагниевый лист** against burn-through, a **ferro-manganese plate** against drilling, extra steel, and **a stainless sheet chosen as a viscous, poorly heat-conducting metal so a drill bit overheats and binds.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ *«Если защитить только замковую зону, а везде оставить ту же полторашку — то есть можно вырезать всю замковую зону и так же добраться до замков.»*** **A door's resistance is set by its weakest area, so reinforcing the lock pocket on an otherwise thin leaf buys nothing. This is the cleanest argument in the source and it applies to any spec sheet that is strong in one line and quiet elsewhere.**

**⚠️⚠️ THE BRONENAKLADKA — the highest-leverage single item in the whole video, and the vault has nothing on it:**

> ***«Брать себе дверь без броненакладки недопустимо — вы сразу просто сокращаете время взлома вашей двери… до 30–40 секунд.»***

An armoured escutcheon protects the cylinder so an attacker must defeat it first. Named: **Mottura Top Defender** (a skirt that cannot be torn out of the door body) and **DISEC**, which he says resists an angle grinder for roughly half an hour before the cylinder is even reachable.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A single, comparatively cheap component that he claims moves break-in time from ~30 seconds to ~30 minutes. If one item from this source is worth acting on, it is this one.** ⚠️ **Both figures are his, uncorroborated, and he sells doors fitted with these — but the component is cheap enough to specify regardless of whether the exact numbers hold.**

## ⚠️ 5. Locks — a myth debunked, and a sealing consequence

**Cylinder vs. lever (сувальдный), on consumer grounds he says cylinder wins outright:** silent geared versions, durable, no bulky keys, **and on key loss you replace only the cylinder core rather than the whole lock.**

- **⚠️⚠️ A LEVER LOCK ALWAYS HAS A THROUGH-HOLE.** *«Какими бы шторками вы его ни закрывали, сквозняки всегда будут проходить, он не будет герметичен.»* **→ A soundproofing and draught consequence of a lock choice, which the vault did not connect.**
- **⚠️⚠️ MYTH DEBUNKED: that real security requires COMBINING one cylinder and one lever lock.** He says the belief arose because burglars need different tools for each type, **and because budget doors expose a protruding cylinder with no protection at all** — in that case, yes, mixing types helps. **With properly protected cylinders you can instead combine different cylinder systems, e.g. a standard pin and a slider.** ⚠️ **The vault currently carries Gusev's product spec as "сувальдный + cylinder" without comment; this reframes that as a workaround for unprotected cylinders rather than a security principle.**

**Cylinder brands and attack modes** — abus (Germany, "iTec" secret system) and EVVA (Austria, from 3KS adding a slider system and cut-verification) named as leaders, Mottura also rated; protections cited against **bumping, picking and self-impressioning**. Budget cylinders differ by pin count and anti-drill protection. **⚠️ And the memorable low end: there exist cylinders where pressing the inner plug with a long object lets the key turn.** For a lever lock he names **Mottura 525J** as the price/security balance, inconvenient because of the ball in the key.

## ⚠️⚠️ 6. Soundproofing — the density number, and a marketing-name debunk

- **⚠️⚠️ *«Плита должна быть плотностью больше чем 40 кг на кубометр»* — and above that he claims the door seals reliably regardless of wall type** (panel, monolithic, thick brick).
- **⚠️⚠️ THE NAME IS NOISE, THE DENSITY IS THE VARIABLE**: *«минвата, каменная вата, базальтовая плита — называют это всё по-разному… по сути это всё очень однообразные слова. Именно плотность отвечает за шумоизоляцию.»*
- **⚠️ Layer COUNT matters as much as density**: *«чем больше различных слоёв материалов с разной плотностью, тем лучше будет шумоизоляция»* — and a decorative MDF panel measurably helps, so **the same door with a decorative panel outperforms the same door with powder coating.**
- **⚠️⚠️ Thickness alone is NOT the signal, and this cuts against his own industry**: in bent (гнутые) doors the leaf can be made any thickness and stuffed with anything — *«если у такой двери будет ещё геометрия страдать, какой бы толстой она ни была, и сколько бы контуров уплотнителя мы ни делали — три контура резины с палец, всё как в шлюзе — а по факту дверь всё равно пропускает шумы»*.
- Noise split into **airborne and structure-borne**, with a lift shaft producing both.
- The lever lock's through-hole again, as a sealing leak.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ TWO INDEPENDENT MANUFACTURERS IN THIS BATCH SAY SEAL-CONTOUR COUNT IS NOT A SELECTION CRITERION.** [[_Sources/YT_6r8IVBsXjnE_novakey_six_door_selection_mistakes|Novakey]] lists it as one of six buyer errors outright. **The vault currently carries the opposite advice from Gusev. See [[13_Surfaces_and_Finishes/analysis/Entrance_Door_Construction_Spec|the spec page]] §5.**

**⚠️⚠️ ONE ARGUMENT DELIBERATELY NOT ADOPTED — the foam analogy.** Answering commenters who say installation foam does not hold soundproofing, he argues windows are always foamed and a properly closed window blocks street noise, and cites **a СНиП requiring the wall-to-window-block gap to be no more than 55 mm.**

> ⚠️⚠️ **NOT CARRIED AS A FINDING, for three reasons.** (1) **It is a RUSSIAN regulatory citation, so under standing rule 4 it may not go to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/` and is recorded here with the jurisdiction flagged.** (2) **The analogy is weak** — a window's acoustic seal is the glazing unit and the frame's own gaskets, not the perimeter foam, so "windows are foamed and windows are quiet" does not establish what foam contributes. (3) **He disclaims his own expertise on it in the same breath**: *«я в этом не эксперт и на это даже не претендую… может быть, действительно я там ничего не понимаю»*. **Recorded because his candour is itself a credibility signal, not because the argument works.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 7. Burglary class 4, defined physically — the anchor the vault's unresolved dispute needed

**And the retrospective test he offers:** subscribers write that they bought a "burglar-resistant" door, lost their keys, and a locksmith opened it in 20 seconds. *«О чём это говорит? Вас обманули, продали не взломостойкую.»*

## 8. Finishes, and a vocabulary correction worth carrying

- **⚠️ MDF is the SUBSTRATE, not a finish.** Customers ask *«у вас ПВХ или МДФ?»*, which he says is the wrong question: MDF is the base, and PVC film, veneer, enamel or anti-vandal plastic are coatings applied to it.
- **PVC film**: mechanically tough (a key drawn across it does not scratch), washable with detergent, and now imitating stone, tile, concrete, marble and wood convincingly enough that *«неопытному сложно отличить»*. **⚠️⚠️ Its one real drawback is a LIFECYCLE one the vault did not have: enamel and veneer can be restored in place by a visiting specialist, but damaged film cannot be repaired — the whole panel must be re-covered or replaced.**
- **Enamel**: MDF milled, sanded, hand-finished and sprayed to RAL or NCS; budget doors show inclusions and rough patches. Matte or gloss, gloss being the demanding one.
- **Veneer** in any species; **alcantara panels** he rates from personal use in his car.
- **Powder coating he ranks lowest** — worse soundproofing than a decorative panel, and plain-looking, though he notes some customers deliberately want an inconspicuous but strong door.

## 9. Start from your own threat and comfort profile

**Ground-floor and top-floor flats are the most burglary-prone per МВД statistics he cites, and ground floor is also the noisiest.** His complaint is that a salesperson should elicit this before quoting anything. ⚠️ **The МВД statistic is uncorroborated and Russian; recorded as attributed, not adopted.**
