## ⚠️⚠️ 3. COIR HAS TWO TYPES, AND THE SELLER OFTEN DOES NOT KNOW WHICH

**The most actionable new item in the source.**

| Type | Behaviour |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Латексированная** (impregnated and stabilised with latex) | More plastic, **more resistant to loads, lasts longer, holds shape and firmness.** ⚠️ **Dearer** |
| **⚠️⚠️ Иглопробивная** (needle-punched, no stabilisation) | ***«В течение ГОДА может начать расползаться.»*** |

> ***«Продавцы ЧАСТО САМИ НЕ ЗНАЮТ, какая койра в их матрасе. Выясняйте до покупки этот момент — пусть покажет вам ОБРАЗЕЦ. Иначе вам впарят обычную койру как латексированную.»***

- **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE VAULT ALREADY SAYS "ASK HOW MANY CENTIMETRES OF COIR". THIS ADDS: ASK WHICH COIR, AND ASK TO SEE A SAMPLE.** **Two mattresses can list "кокос 2 см" and one of them starts disintegrating within a year.**
- ⚠️ **It also qualifies the vault's existing "don't chase coir" finding**, which came from ARMOS and Круглов: **the point stands that coir presence is not a quality marker — but coir TYPE is a durability marker, and that is a different question.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Firmness — a thickness-to-firmness mapping, and a number for PU density

**Firmness is achieved through additional layers, and he gives the mapping:**

| Construction | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Coir layer 2 cm** | **Firm** |
| **Latex + coir up to 1 cm** | **Medium** |
| **Foam alone, no additional layers** | **Soft** |

**⚠️⚠️ AND THE MEASURABLE PROXY THE VAULT HAS BEEN MISSING.** Круглов said *density, not height*, and never quantified it:

> ***«Простой поролон имеет плотность от 10 до 15 единиц, а МЕБЕЛЬНЫЙ — 35–40. Нужно выбирать матрас с более высоким значением плотности.»*** And *«чем выше плотность поролона, тем жёстче и дороже матрас, но и тем ДОЛЬШЕ ОН ПРОСЛУЖИТ»*.

- **→ ⚠️⚠️ A NUMBER TO ASK FOR: PU foam density, where 10–15 is plain foam and 35–40 is furniture grade.** ⚠️ **He does not state the unit; it is almost certainly kg/m³, and that inference is this vault's, not his.** **He also refers to a table of required PU density per furniture component, shown on screen and not readable from the transcript.**
- **⚠️ A safety point nobody else raised: PU foam's main drawback is *«сильная ГОРЮЧЕСТЬ»*** — *«представьте, что вы случайно закурили на поролоновой кровати»*.

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Sizing — the most complete set of rules in the file, and a NUMBER for bed height

- **Measure the INTERNAL part of the sleeping place; the mattress must enter freely, with no gap greater than 1 cm.** ⚠️ **Note this is TIGHTER than ARMOS, who wanted a deliberate gap (measure 162 → buy 160) for bedding, a protector and lift-mechanism clearance. → Both agree a gap exists; they disagree on whether ~1 cm or ~2 cm per side.**
- **⚠️⚠️ THE MATTRESS HEIGHT MUST BE EQUAL TO OR GREATER THAN THE HEIGHT OF THE BED'S SIDE (борт).** **New — nobody else stated it, and it is a hard geometric constraint rather than a comfort preference.**
- **⚠️⚠️ AND THE FIGURE THE VAULT'S BED-HEIGHT ARGUMENT HAS BEEN MISSING:** *«Старайтесь выбирать высоту так, чтобы вы могли свободно сидеть на кровати с согнутыми коленями — при среднем росте эта высота составляет 50–60 см.»*
- **If there is no bed yet**: **length = your height + 15–20 cm**; **width = double your shoulder width**.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ 50–60 cm IS THE NUMBER THAT ARMOS'S "KNEE BEND ± A PALM" AND КРУГЛОВ'S "KNEE ANGLE ≥ 90°" BOTH IMPLY BUT NEITHER STATED.** **Three independent ergonomic sources now converge on roughly 45–60 cm for bed plus mattress — decisively above the ≤40 cm compositional rule the vault holds from Шеврина.**

## ⚠️ 7. Spring packages — a taxonomy, and a second contradiction of ARMOS

**Three independent-spring package grades:** **стандартный** (largest springs, lowest density per m²) → **мультипакет** (medium springs, medium density) → **микропакет** (smallest, highest density; the word is garbled in the transcript).

> **His rule: *«Чем МЕНЬШЕ диаметр пружины и чем ВЫШЕ плотность пружин, тем ЛУЧШЕ матрас»*** — better wear resistance, durability, comfort, and the best orthopaedic effect. **And *«чем их больше на квадратный метр, тем лучше»*.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS CONTRADICTS ARMOS AND КРУГЛОВ, WHO BOTH SAID "DO NOT CHASE THE NUMBER OF SPRINGS".**
>
> ⚠️ **But the variables are not the same, and that matters: ARMOS's objection was that a higher COUNT is bought with THINNER WIRE, and that every spring is discrete regardless. This source is talking about COIL DIAMETER and packing density.** **Those can move independently — a smaller coil at higher density does not necessarily mean thinner wire.**
> **→ RECORDED AS A GENUINE DISAGREEMENT ON THE PRACTICAL ADVICE, with the note that the two may be arguing about different variables.** ⚠️ **The safe synthesis: spring count alone is not a ranking, and neither source gives a way to see the wire gauge, so the buyer cannot resolve it at the point of sale.**

**⚠️ Useful concrete detail:** the pocket material is **спанбонд** — *«кстати, им укрывают растения на зиму от холодов»* — and glued pockets form *«конструкцию, похожую на закрытые пчелиные соты»*.
