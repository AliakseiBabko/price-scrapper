## Cost Drivers / Materials — ⚠️ the three legitimate justifications

**Her framing is that wallpaper is defensible when the reason is NOT visual.** *"Когда это обосновано техническими какими-то моментами или бюджетом."*

### 1. Technical / budget

- **Wallpaper is easy to hang and it is cheap.**
- **⚠️ Her worked budget case, and it is a real allocation argument: a new build on a modest budget where you want the money to go into interesting FURNITURE and to save on wall finishes — then wallpaper in the living room or bedrooms may be the right call.** The saving is being deliberately moved somewhere it shows more.
- The crack argument — **see the contradiction above; do not rely on it.**

**⚠️ On paintable wallpaper («обои под покраску»), which she calls "супер коварный вид обоев":**

- **She uses it very rarely.**
- **⚠️ If you must, the imitation has to be maximally like PAINT — not plaster, not wood, not Venetian — meaning the finest possible texture fraction.**
- **Prefer a wide roll.**
- **⚠️ And it must be seen in person.** `[partial restatement — the wallpaper page already carries a paint / wallpaper / paintable-wallpaper three-way comparison from Петришин-Строй; the "must imitate paint specifically, not plaster or wood" criterion is the new half]`

### 2. Style

**Some styles themselves presume wallpaper**, and there the choice creates no dissonance: **Art Deco, classic, contemporary classic, Provence.** Stripes are fine.

**⚠️ Her criterion: where silk or fabric wall coverings are characteristic elements OF the style, wallpaper is the right material rather than a substitute for one.** And in that case it need not be cheap — **it can be expensive, good wallpaper hung by a good craftsman, in a non-budget interior.**

### 3. ⚠️ Function — the children's-room case

**She describes projects where everything is painted — kitchen, living room, corridor, bedroom — and the child's room alone is wallpapered, justified by function:**

- **⚠️ PAPER wallpaper is a very ecological, safe material**, which matters in a child's room.
- **It carries atmosphere in a way paint cannot** — small hares, stars, flowers.
- **⚠️ And its weakness is the feature: paper wallpaper is easily damaged and easily replaced.** In a child's room that is an argument *for* it.
- **⚠️ The caveat that makes this usable: this applies to PAPER wallpaper. Non-woven/fleece («флизелиновые») is "не совсем про экологию."** The ecological argument does not transfer to the more durable type.

## Materials — ⚠️ the central idea: wallpaper must not imitate what it cannot imitate

**This is the source's real contribution, and it is a diagnosis rather than a preference.**

> **⚠️ Her explanation of why wallpaper has a reputation as dated and cheap: "обои претендуют на имитацию тех материалов, которые они имитировать в принципе не могут."**

**The mechanism is tactile, not visual:**

> **Brick, stone, tile, board and cracked concrete are HARD, RELIEF materials, and you know that by touch. Wallpaper is smooth. "Идёт диссонанс восприятия того, что мы видим, и того, что мы чувствуем, и мы понимаем, что это подделка."**

- **⚠️ So wallpaper CANNOT imitate hard materials — brick, stone, tile, board, concrete with cracks and bumps. "Не претендуйте обоями на то, что вы сможете сымитировать кирпичную кладку."**
- **⚠️ And the render trap: "на визуализации может смотреться хорошо, в интерьере никогда — это будет сразу подделкой пахнуть."** The failure mode is invisible in exactly the medium the decision gets made in.
- **⚠️ Wallpaper CAN handle: light plaster, fabric and silk textures, and fine grit or crumb textures** — anything that is itself smooth or only slightly relieved.
- **⚠️ Gloss compounds the lie.** A stone that would be matte and slightly rough in reality, printed glossy and smooth, is *"точно нет, никогда нет."*

### ⚠️ Her test, which is the transferable part

> **Ask the wallpaper: "что вы хотите мне сказать? что вы имитируете?" If the answer matches what you actually see and feel, take it. If it does not, do not.**

**Her worked applications, which show the test being run:**

| The wallpaper | Its claim | Verdict |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Stamped gold on a ground | *"I am paint, stamped with something"* | **⚠️ Take it — she checks whether she could reproduce it by hand with a stamp; the stamp is not too large, the repeat looks ~1 m, and if the gold sheen is good in person the claim is honest** |
| Board / plank imitation | *"I am assembled from boards"* | **Reject — "досточку я вижу, но это будет гладко. Я не верю этим обоям"** |
| A painted fish panel | *"I am something smooth painted on the wall"* | **Take it — the claim is true.** She likes photo-panels generally |
| Hard woven / plait imitation | *"I am a hard weave"* | **Reject — "они не жёсткие, они не плетёные, они гладкие. Я им не поверю"** |
| Stone, especially glossy | *"I am rough quarried stone"* | **"Сто процентов нет, сразу нет"** |

**⚠️ And her framing of why it matters, which is stronger than a taste claim: a dishonest imitation "будет обманывать моего зрителя и будет вносить в мой интерьер нечестность, неправдивость."**

## Materials — ⚠️ pattern rules

- **Pattern types that work: floral, geometric, and imitation of a material** — the last subject to the honesty test above.
- **⚠️ THE CENTRAL PATTERN RULE: the repeat («рапорт») must be as large as possible, so the repetition is as infrequent as possible.** *"Я предпочитаю, чтобы шаблон был либо не виден, либо максимально незаметный."*
- **⚠️ The checkable version: if you can see the pattern repeat twice within a single roll width, the paper reads as a template and will be hard to place.** She rejects several samples on exactly that basis.
- **Her worked acceptance: a peach motif she judges from the image to repeat at roughly 80 cm, which she calls fine.**
- **⚠️ Named as dated: Soviet-photomural-style florals, and tropical palm-leaf papers with a visibly frequent repeat.**
- **The pattern must match the style, the colour scheme, the mood and the rhythm of the interior.**

### ⚠️ Roll width, and the trade against pattern

> **"Обои лучше брать чем шире, тем лучше" — a wider roll means fewer seams. If only 50 cm is available, take 50 — but then the pattern repeat must be large.**

**A concrete trade: narrow rolls and busy patterns compound, because a small repeat on a narrow roll puts the template in front of you twice per drop.**

## Design Concept — ⚠️ a composition caution worth keeping

**On hanging art against patterned wallpaper**, her worked example is unusually precise: a picture works on that wall **only because it has a white mount («паспарту»).** **⚠️ Remove the mount in Photoshop and the picture's own image merges into the wallpaper pattern, and the wall reads badly.**

**The general rule: against an active-patterned wall, something must separate the artwork from the ground.**

**What she does like as accents**: **wallpaper with animals — flying swans, fish, monkeys, birds; photo-panels («фотопанно»), which she rates highly; and photo-wallpaper combined with plain wallpaper in a child's room.**

## Rules / Heuristics — her conclusion

**Wallpaper can be used in contemporary interiors, and can be "очень круто, стильно и бюджетно в то же время" — but only when chosen against the right criteria**: the pattern must suit the style, colour, mood and rhythm; the function must justify it; **and if the imitation is not truthful, do not buy it.**

## Mistakes / Warnings

- Choosing wallpaper for a visual reason rather than a technical, budget, stylistic or functional one.
- Wallpaper imitating brick, stone, tile, board or textured concrete — and worse, doing so in gloss.
- Judging an imitation wallpaper from a render, where the tactile dissonance cannot appear.
- A pattern that repeats more than once per roll width.
- A narrow roll combined with a small repeat.
- Paintable wallpaper whose texture imitates plaster, wood or Venetian rather than paint.
- Relying on the ecological argument for non-woven wallpaper — it applies to paper.
- Hanging unmounted art against an active-patterned wall.
- **⚠️ And relying on wallpaper to conceal cracks — see the contradiction at the top of this note.**
