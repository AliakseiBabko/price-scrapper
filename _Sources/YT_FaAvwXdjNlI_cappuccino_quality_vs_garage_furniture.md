## Why this source, and how to read its bias

**The owner will be buying a kitchen from someone.** This vault holds a great deal about *what to specify* and almost nothing about *how to tell, on the doorstep, whether what arrived is what was specified*. **This is that checklist**, and most of its items are **checkable in minutes with no tools**.

> ⚠️ **The bias is structural and it points one way: a fabricator listing the failure modes of cheaper competitors ("гаражное производство") is describing the market in terms that make its own price look necessary.** `promotional_ratio: medium` — the pitch is the frame, but the **specific, falsifiable, mechanism-carrying** items inside it are what was extracted. **Two items (9, and partly 3) are self-serving enough to flag inline.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. It should arrive as ASSEMBLED MODULES, not a pile of boards

> *«Если вам домой привезли **груду дощечек** вместо кухни — знайте, это беда.»*

**Quality production includes a пред­варительная сборка — a trial assembly at the factory** — *«которая помогает выявить недочёты и максимально быстро их устранить ещё на производстве»*. Delivery is **собранными модулями**; **only tall units (пеналы) may be broken down, and only so they fit in the lift.**

⚠️ **The named consequence of on-site assembly:** *«при сборке на адресе **путаются детали, делаются лишние отверстия**»* — and installation stretches out while defects surface in your flat instead of in the workshop.

> **→ A CHECKABLE ACCEPTANCE CRITERION, and it also decides a LOGISTICS question this project already has open.** The vault already records from this channel that **a jointless worktop over 4 m will not fit in a lift**; **this is the same constraint applied to carcasses.** ⚠️ **→ Ask the fabricator, before ordering, what arrives assembled and what does not — and confirm the lift dimensions, because the answer changes the module breakdown.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. MISSING EDGE BANDING IS A HEALTH ISSUE, NOT A COSMETIC ONE — the strongest finding here

Check the **кромка** (edge banding) at **the joints between modules, at the back panel, and where the worktop lands** — the places a corner-cutter assumes nobody looks.

> *«Даже если вся кухня сделана из ДСП Egger, который имеет **класс эмиссии E1**, то нормы, при которых она безвредна для человека, указаны **в закромкованном состоянии**. **Открытый торец ДСП увеличивает показатели испарения в 10 раз.**»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE E1 EMISSION CLASS IS STATED FOR THE EDGE-BANDED CONDITION. An unbanded ЛДСП edge is claimed to raise formaldehyde emission TENFOLD.** **That reframes edge banding from a finish-quality item into an indoor-air item**, and it makes every unbanded edge — including ones you cannot see behind a plinth or against a wall — a live concern rather than a blemish.
>
> ⚠️ **The 10× figure is unsourced and this is a fabricator, not a laboratory.** **Recorded as a claim with a named mechanism, not as a measurement.** ⚠️ **But the DIRECTION is not in doubt and the check costs nothing: look at every edge.**

## ⚠️ 3. 16 mm substituted for the promised 18 mm

> *«Вместо обещанного 18 привезли ДСП толщиной **16 миллиметров**.»* *«**Даже два миллиметра решают.**»*

His argument against 16 mm is **not** about the 2 mm as such — it is that *«производители такого ДСП очень редко выпускают качественный продукт, способный выдержать активное использование»*, and the failure he names is **a hinge tearing out of the board** (*«вывалилась петля»*), in a room where you open and close doors every day.

> ⚠️ **PARTLY SELF-SERVING — 18 mm board costs more and he sells it.** ⚠️ **But the substitution itself is the real finding: board thickness is a spec you can verify with a ruler on delivery day, and it is exactly the kind of thing that is quietly downgraded between quote and delivery.** **→ Put the board thickness in the written order, then measure it.**

## ⚠️ 4. Confirmat screws on VISIBLE uprights

**Конфирмат** (the euro screw) is *«самый распространённый способ крепежа»* and he concedes *«он является самым надёжным, поэтому без конфирмата большие производства тоже не могут обойтись»*.

> ⚠️ **The defect is not the fastener — it is the fastener ON A VISIBLE FACE**, plugged with plastic caps *«которые не всегда попадают в ДСП»* (that do not always match the board). **On visible uprights the correct hardware is minifix / эксцентриковая стяжка**, which leaves no hole on the show face.

> **→ A SPECIFICATION SENTENCE, not just an inspection: "visible faces use minifix; confirmat is acceptable only where it cannot be seen."** ⚠️ **And a caveat this vault should keep: the cam fitting is weaker than the confirmat, which is why it belongs on visible faces and not everywhere.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Genuine Blum carries a factory engraving

> *«Обещали поставить фурнитуру **Blum** — поставили Китай. … На фурнитуре Blum есть **фирменная гравировка**. Обязательно проверьте это при получении кухни.»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A DIRECTLY ACTIONABLE ACCEPTANCE CHECK, and it lands on hardware this project has already been steered toward.** The vault's cappuccino round already records **Blum mechanisms by name** (SPACE STEP and the plinth height it forces, the handleless opening systems). **Specifying Blum and not checking the engraving is specifying nothing** — hardware is the single easiest line to substitute, because it is buried inside a cabinet and nobody looks after installation day.

## ⚠️⚠️ 6. The dish-drainer cabinet must be VENTILATED

> *«Если вам изготовили шкаф под сушку **без проветривания** … уже через пару месяцев ваша сушка проржавеет.»*

**A second, independent cause:** *«любая сушка, которая изготовлена из некачественной стали, будет ржаветь»* — he names **Vibo** and **Inoxa** (Italian) as brands his shop has not had problems with.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ TWO CAUSES, AND BOTH MUST BE ADDRESSED — the CABINET needs ventilation and the DRAINER needs decent stainless. Either alone still rusts.** ⚠️ **A failure with a stated timescale — "пара месяцев" — which is rare and useful: it is a defect that shows up well inside any warranty, so it is worth raising at the project stage rather than discovering it.**
>
> ⚠️ **Brand names recorded as this fabricator's experience, not as a recommendation.** **No price, no year attached, so nothing comparable.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 7. Uneven front gaps trace back to the SAW, not only to the room

Crooked fronts and uneven gaps: *«я, конечно, не исключаю того факта, что это возможно из-за **кривизны помещения**, но чаще всего это возникает из-за дешёвого распиловочного цеха, в котором **не отрегулированы пилы**. Поэтому при сборке такого гарнитура **в тумбах не сходятся диагонали**, и сделать фасады с идеальными зазорами уже невозможно.»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE DIAGNOSTIC CHAIN IS THE VALUABLE PART: uncalibrated saw → carcass that is not square → diagonals that do not match → gaps that CANNOT be adjusted out, because the error is in the box, not the hanging.**
>
> ⚠️⚠️ **AND IT PARTLY REVISES WHAT THIS VAULT ALREADY RECORDED FROM THE SAME CHANNEL.** The cappuccino round notes that **the fabricator names out-of-square room geometry as the dominant error term** — this project's own premise, reached from the furniture side. **Here the same presenter says the room is the LESS frequent cause and a badly set-up cutting shop is the MORE frequent one.** **→ Both are real; they are different populations.** ⚠️ **→ The operational consequence: a bad gap is NOT automatically the flat's fault, and accepting that explanation is how a fabricator's own defect gets charged to your walls.** **Check the carcass diagonals.**
>
> ⚠️ **This also sits beside the three tolerances the vault already holds — the building at ±50 mm, finishing at ~10 mm, fitted furniture at 3 mm per wall just to install. A carcass out of square consumes that 3 mm before installation even starts.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 8. Cut-outs must be on the DRAWING, not made on site

Holes for the **riser, the hood duct, the gas meter, water stub-outs** *«делаются по месту, при этом остаются открытые торцы ДСП»*.

> *«Если вы обращаетесь к профессионалам, то ваша кухня продумывается **ещё на стадии проекта**, и чётко указывается, где будут вырезы. Эти вырезы **должны быть обязательно закромкованы**.»*

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS ITEM 2 WITH A CAUSE ATTACHED: on-site cut-outs are the single largest source of unbanded edges**, and they are made at exactly the points where services enter — behind the sink, beside the hob, at the riser. **The fix is upstream and costs nothing at the drawing stage.**
>
> ⚠️⚠️ **AND THE WORST CASE HE NAMES IS A DIRECT HIT ON THIS PROJECT'S OPEN WORK:** *«самая смешная ситуация — когда **розетки попадают на стык модулей**. Приходится кромсать всю кухню.»* **A socket landing on a joint between two carcasses cannot be cut cleanly in either one.** **→ The kitchen module grid and the socket layout must be resolved TOGETHER**, and this project has both live — the electrical layout in `12_Engineering_and_Systems/` and the kitchen run in `03_Kitchen/`. ⚠️ **Recorded as an explicit coordination check before either is frozen.**

## ⚠️ 9. The "Вася" argument — one person doing every stage

> *«Заказ принимал Вася, замер делал Вася, распил делал Вася, и собирать приехал Вася. А завтра Вася заболел.»*

The stated principle: *«каждый должен быть специалистом в своём деле»*, and the cost argument — *«стоимость мебели складывается не только из материалов, но из **трудозатрат узкоспециализированных специалистов**»*.

> ⚠️⚠️ **THE MOST SELF-SERVING ITEM IN THE SOURCE, and it should be read as a Perspective rather than a finding.** It is a **direct argument for buying from a firm rather than an individual**, delivered by a firm. **The bus-factor point is genuine — a one-person supplier is a single point of failure on a dated delivery** — but *«каждый должен быть специалистом»* is also **precisely how a larger shop justifies its overhead**, and this vault already holds the opposite view from a different source, that specialist division is where coordination errors are born.
>
> ⚠️ **The honest extract is narrower than his: ask who does each stage and what happens if that person is unavailable. That question is worth asking of a firm too.**

## ⚠️ 10. Delayed failure modes, by front type

> *«На первый взгляд, после установки кухни всё может быть хорошо, но со временем…»*

| Front type | Failure he names |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Плёночный (film-wrapped) МДФ** | *плёнка может отклеиться* — the film delaminates |
| **Крашеный МДФ** | *окраска просесть, появится шагрень* — the paint sinks and orange-peel appears |
| **Стекло** | *стекло может отклеиться от фасада* — the glass unsticks from the frame |
| **Дерево** | *«вообще беда — могут пойти трещинами»* — solid wood cracks |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ EVERY FRONT MATERIAL HAS A DELAYED FAILURE MODE, AND NONE OF THEM IS VISIBLE AT HANDOVER.** *«Если вы сэкономили — то на чём-то сэкономили: на качестве материалов и технологии производства. Касается абсолютно всех фасадов.»*
>
> ⚠️ **This is the acceptance checklist's own limit, stated by its author: the ten checks above catch what is wrong ON DELIVERY DAY, and this item catches nothing.** **→ Which makes the WARRANTY TERM, and who is still trading in three years, a real selection criterion and not a formality.**
>
> ⚠️ **It also corroborates two things the vault already records from this channel's MDF head-to-head**: that **painted fronts fade, so a replacement cannot be colour-matched** (order a spare at manufacture), and that **the moisture limit is in the MDF SUBSTRATE, not the coating.**
