## Planning Rules — the ordered ten-step plan

**⚠️ Her framing thesis, and it is the point of the whole source: nine of the ten steps come *before* the builders arrive.** The common failure is builders walking on site and asking the owner "а делать-то что?" — she treats that as the error itself. If the nine steps are done, "вы выдаёте ему список решений."

1. **⚠️ Key handover — and do not economise on an acceptance inspector (приёмщик).** **Her reasoning is that this is a one-time window: it is the only moment when defects can be forced back onto the developer.** What to check with the inspector: **with a thermal imager, that the windows are not losing heat**; that frames are properly sealed; that there are no cracks or chips in the windows; that they do not leak. **She flags developer windows as a recurring problem on her own projects specifically** — "для меня это такой вечный головняк." **For a whitebox, additionally**: wall flatness, wall geometry, filler quality, and screed flatness — everything the developer is handing over. **Force any remediation before starting work.** Her candid note: you have probably waited a long time and want to start, but skimping on acceptance costs far more time, money and nerves later.
2. **⚠️ Measure the apartment and establish its technical constraints — and the constraints half is the substantive one.** Measurement can be done yourself with a tape, better by specialist measurers (either inside a design bureau or standalone) who deliver a dimensioned plan with heights. **The constraints to establish before anything else:**
   - **Ventilation.** In business/premium buildings there is typically a **common-building supply ventilation system** — establish where the outdoor units sit, **how ventilation enters the apartment, and what its capacity is.** In more budget buildings instead: **where are the AC baskets, how may air conditioners be positioned, may breathers (бризеры) be installed** — i.e. how fresh air will actually be supplied.
   - **Obtain the БТИ plan and identify where the wet zones are on it** — that is what makes a later replanning possible or not.
   - **⚠️ In the old fund especially: the gas question, and the number of kilowatts allocated to the apartment.** "Возможно, вам банально нужно докупать мощности, потому что текущих не хватит для современной техники." **This is a purchasable constraint, and it has to be discovered before appliances are chosen.**
   - **⚠️ In the very old fund: whether the floor slabs below need reinforcement, if the building has timber slabs.**
3. **The layout solution.** **⚠️ Her gate: do not move on until you have a layout you are actually in love with** — "пока у вас нет планировки, в которую вы буквально влюблены, дальше двигаться не нужно." **She advises against typical/stock layouts except on a genuinely budget renovation**, on the grounds that habits differ and the layout should reflect them.
4. **⚠️ Storage systems, deliberately separated out as its own step — with a number.** She reports storage is among the sharpest issues across all her clients: own clothes, children's clothes, household items, suitcases. **The method: audit your actual possessions, understand how you are comfortable storing them, and build in storage with a 20–25% margin above that.** **⚠️ Two preferences: avoid free-standing wardrobes** (the exception she allows is a decorative painted vitrine functioning as decor), **and prefer built-in storage, ideally consolidated into a separate dressing room.**
5. **⚠️ Engineering — ventilation, plumbing, electrics, heating, all of it — and it comes before aesthetics.** **Commission a dedicated engineering project** from one of the companies that specialise in them, and **hire a crew with the matching qualifications and permits (допуски)**. Her framing: engineering is comfort, safety, warmth, the absence of leaks. Items to integrate at this stage in the higher segments: ventilation, humidification, air ionisation, smart-home and remote climate control. **She notes her bureau allocates dedicated project time to this stage.**
6. **Only now, design and aesthetics** — colours, textures, curtains. **⚠️ Her rationale, stated as the reason for the whole ordering: "красота без планировки, красота без инженерки, красота без технологий — она бесполезна."** She acknowledges the pull to start here (Pinterest boards, collages) and explicitly defers it to sixth place.
7. **⚠️ Working documentation — drawings in some form, and she gives a genuinely low-budget floor for it.** If the budget is very tight, **use a homeowner-grade program — she names RemPlanner — to at least lay out tile and place sockets crudely; or literally print the layout and draw on it by hand in pen.** With budget, a bureau does it. **⚠️ The consequence of skipping it: "без планов, без развёрток, без точного технического задания для строителей ваш дальнейший ремонт превратится в аттракцион невиданной щедрости с вашей стороны, потому что вы будете бесконечно платить за переделки."**
8. **⚠️ Budgeting — an itemised estimate, and she describes the DIY method concretely.** Take Excel; **list everything in the apartment — porcelain, paint, curtains, radiators, fittings, sockets, even towel hooks — broken down by room, with quantity or area and price for each**, and autosum. **The stated purpose is not the total but the sequencing: knowing what you can pay for now and what can be deferred.**
   - **⚠️ The failure mode she describes is specific and worth keeping**: people run out of money at the very end, exhaust credit cards and credit limits, **and move into an unfinished flat — sometimes not the mild version (missing a free-standing piece of furniture) but a radically unfinished one** — or start cutting important things and buy "кухню, сделанную из дров," **purely because the tile they bought at the start was far above what the budget allowed.**
9. **Procurement and contractor selection** — tilers, builders, ventilation specialists, replanning-approval agents — and begin planning and executing purchases.
10. **Bring the construction crew on site.**
