- **Contemporary style vocabulary**: clean lines, no ornate/complex forms; neutral base (white/grey/black/beige/cream) with restrained bold accents; mixed materials (smooth + soft textile, wood + metal, glass + stone) for texture contrast even within a neutral palette; heavy reliance on layered technology-integrated lighting (track, pendant, LED strip) as a primary decorative element.
