## Design Concept — Choosing a Main Colour, and Living With It

- **⚠️ Ready-made palettes as the deliberate non-specialist answer, restated three years after `AKxOtRhIcJY`**: "если нужно быстро и однократно, берите готовую палитру, приятную глазу, и всё." The laws of colouristics are worth learning for their own sake, but for a one-off renovation a harmonious ready-made palette is sufficient — and an interior photo can be loaded into a program that decomposes it into specific colors. This is now a **twice-stated position from the same practitioner across a three-year gap**, not an offhand remark.
- **⚠️ The second and harder task, stated as the core of the video**: "подобрать цвета — огромное дело, но это далеко не всё и не гарантирует гармоничный интерьер. Вторая задача — определить, какой из этих цветов сделать основным, один или несколько, и в каких пропорциях распределить по помещениям." **The same palette produces completely different spaces**: if the palette contains terracotta, whether that becomes a side table, a vase, a whole wall, or a wall *and* ceiling is "разница огромная." Directly corroborates the blue+terracotta worked example in `AKxOtRhIcJY`.
- **⚠️ The volume question is independent of design training** — an explicitly stated, unusually candid claim: "этот выбор вообще никак не связан с опытом и образованием в дизайне или архитектуре. Даже если архитектор или дизайнер делает интерьер себе, он задаётся ровно теми же вопросами: сделать смелее или так, чтобы не надоело." The named exception is someone with a long-settled "картина мечты" they have mentally lived in for years.
- **⚠️ Her own worked counter-example, given against her own taste**: she likes active colors but tires of them, and rejected a dusty burgundy ceiling she genuinely liked for her own home specifically because she knew she would tire of it — "он бы очень прикольно смотрелся здесь в обзоре интерьера, но я знаю, что он быстро надоест." Generalised into a professional duty: an architect should warn the client about the role of color and materials before a serious investment, analogised to a good plastic surgeon declining further work at some point.

## Design Concept — Family Disagreement as the Actual Driver of Neutral Palettes

- **⚠️ A pattern claim from observing dozens of the bureau's own projects**: **clients living alone more often choose bold or unusual combinations**; it is much rarer for a couple's taste to coincide on something bold, or for one partner to have convinced the other. Sometimes the second spouse simply doesn't care.
- **⚠️ The consequence, stated plainly**: "чаще необходимость найти компромисс в семье — это основная причина выбора нейтрального оттенка в качестве основного." Larger families, children of different ages, or several generations in one space converge on **a base that at minimum irritates nobody, with accents added on top**. A neutral natural-toned gamut is convenient because almost everything combines with it, **and because each family member can add their own preferred colour on their own personal territory**.
- **Reversibility as a design criterion**: a bright throw or pouffe that palls is far easier to swap than finishes. "Чем больше возможностей легко изменить интерьер, тем дольше он проживёт, если правильно собран с точки зрения композиции."
- **⚠️ Stated process order when opinions differ**: find a shared style or an intersection of styles **and** a shared gamut first; only then decide whether the interior is built on light tones, dark tones, bold color or small bursts. "Нужно общее техническое задание, прежде чем идти в детали" — the bureau routinely does this step for clients, because if one person disagrees the work cannot proceed.

## Design Concept — Why One Flat Base Colour Fails, and How to Fix It

- **⚠️ Direct answer to "won't one base colour on all walls be boring?": "скорее всего получится."** Decoration can fix almost anything afterwards, but the interior will still read flat and uniform.
- **⚠️ Named mechanism — every colour and material has a weight**: "у каждого оттенка, цвета, материала есть свой вес. Чем темнее или ярче цвет, тем он больше" — so a restrained interior is always **a composition of planes**, and if you strip out all decor and leave only the large built-in furniture, it should *still* be balanced. A concrete, testable acceptance criterion for a palette.
- **⚠️ The prescribed fix — complicate the base rather than add colours**: decompose the chosen base into **half-tones and textures** — smooth surface, микробетон, wood grain; use dusty neighbouring shades, drift toward grey or white. Justified by natural precedent: "любой камень, кора дерева, горы имеют сложные переходы. Представьте, если бы они были в одном цвете." **The more complex the colour transitions, the more alive the interior reads** — and this matters *most* for calm interiors without accents, "иначе есть все шансы получить продвинутый ремонт от застройщика." An unusually concrete failure image.

## Design Concept — One Palette Per Apartment, and Where to Be Bold

- **⚠️ For non-professionals: do not run several distinct palettes in one apartment.** If they are fundamentally different, joining them causes trouble. Named exception: a room deliberately excluded from the overall concept — "комната бабушки, или подростка, студия в квартире" — where the occupant chooses their own colors and style. If one palette isn't enough, **widen it** — keep all the neutrals and vary only the accents (her example: two palettes alike in tone and mood, one with blue accents and one with green, merged into one).
- **⚠️ The framing question to answer for yourself**: do you want a single gamut flowing from room to room? This does **not** mean identical rooms with the same quantity of base and accent color — it means rooms that are whole and alike, each with its own accents and quirks, so that moving through the interior it is visibly one project. "Помещения связаны между собой не только стилем, но и цветами. Например, тем же базовым цветом." Corroborates and extends the cross-room coherence methodology already on `Whole_Apartment_Coherence.md` from Anuta Vlady and Iolanta Fedotova.
- **⚠️ A distinctive named method for reconciling boldness with longevity — the bureau's own recommended compromise**: put the neutral base plus decor in the rooms where **most time is spent** (living room, bedroom) and **flood the transit and short-dwell rooms with colour** — коридоры, прихожая, гостевой санузел, гардеробные. Stated mechanism: "мы видим большие объёмы цвета короткое время, он не успевает надоесть." Offered specifically for the case where someone wants boldness that lasts, or where one family member objects to strong color in the main rooms but not everywhere. **This is a genuinely new technique for this vault** and is the strongest single item in this source.
