## Materials — Partition-Material Cost-Tier Comparison (durable facts)

7. **⚠️ Three-way partition-material relative cost-tier ranking, stated
   directly**: pазогребень (tongue-and-groove gypsum block) is the
   cheapest, both material and labor; aerated-concrete block ("газобетон
   /газоблок") costs somewhat more and specifically requires a skilled
   mason ("каменщик") to lay accurately; drywall partition framing costs
   meaningfully more than either, both material and labor. All three are
   framed as time-tested, standards-compliant options — the choice is
   presented as budget-driven, not quality-driven.

## Technique — Plastering Method Comparison (durable facts)

8. **Mechanized vs. hand-pulled plaster application, explicit tradeoff**:
   mechanized (sprayed via a plaster gun) is significantly faster but
   produces a less-concentrated mix; hand-pulled application allows a
   thicker, more concentrated mix, which the source states positively
   affects strength. Both methods are stated to meet quality standards;
   this object's foreman had a personal principled preference for
   hand-pulled application specifically.
