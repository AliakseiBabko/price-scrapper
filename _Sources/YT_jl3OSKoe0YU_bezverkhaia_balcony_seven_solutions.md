## Balcony / Loggia — the structural distinction, and why it decides the glazing

The definitional part she herself calls "немножечко душного контента," but it is the load-bearing fact for everything after:

- **Балкон (balcony)** — a **cantilevered structure**, fixed to the wall on one side only, with a railing on the other. Typically small — from a miniature French balcony up to the modest balconies of Stalin-era buildings.
- **Лоджия (loggia)** — **part of the floor slab itself, enclosed by three bearing walls**, with its front face necessarily flush with the rest of the facade.
- **⚠️ The consequence, and the most important structural fact in the source: a cantilever cannot carry much weight.** You **cannot** simply install a heavy double-chamber glazing unit on a Stalinka balcony without reinforcement — **"это небезопасно."** She reports her studio has done **separate reinforcement projects for old-fund balconies more than once** ("мы уже несколько раз делали… усиление балкона в старом фонде"), which makes this a repeated-practice claim rather than a one-off caution.
- **A loggia, being a slab between three bearing walls, "может вынести хоть слона"** — the load question effectively does not arise.

**Practical routing of that fact: on a balcony, the glazing spec and any structural reinforcement are one coupled decision, and reinforcement is a real line item that has to be priced before the glazing is chosen.**

## Planning Rules — apartment-selection advice that depends on the balcony

- **⚠️ A daylight mechanism worth keeping, because it is stated as a two-layer loss**: light reaches the room through the balcony's glazing, then through the balcony, and **only a small part of it continues into the room**. So a living room or bedroom that faces **north** *and* has a balcony *and* has a **solid (глухой) parapet up to waist height** gives "вечный мрак" in the room.
- **The same configuration on the south side can be an advantage** — less overheating in summer.
- **⚠️ All else equal, when choosing an apartment prefer units where balconies/loggias are glazed floor-to-ceiling by default with no solid elements.**

## Planning Rules — decide the balcony's function from the rest of the plan, never in isolation

- **⚠️ Her sequencing rule: "никогда не смотрите на балкон или лоджию в отрыве от всей остальной планировки."** Do the layout — partitions, furniture, zoning — first; **the balcony's purpose then falls out of whatever the apartment's own perimeter could not fit.**
- The candidate functions she enumerates, useful as a checklist: **open summer sitting area** (or left unglazed deliberately, so it does not block light into the flat); **enclosed, fully insulated sitting area**; **home office** — the case she calls "очень часто," where a family member works from home and there is no room for a real study; **extra play area** where the kids' room is small; **winter garden**; **home gym** (exercise bike, pull-up bar); **bar**; and **storage**, which she names last and calls the least preferable use.

## HVAC — the AC outdoor unit on the balcony

- **⚠️ The problem is developer-created and she says it spans econ, comfort and even business class**: developers put the outdoor unit on the balcony instead of providing facade baskets or dedicated plant space, "и греет его летом."
- **The fix, with the two constraints that make it work**: box it in (короб), but **size the box so the unit actually ventilates and does not heat itself**, and **provide an access hatch (люк)** for servicing.
- **Then make the box do work**: extend the windowsill across it, build it into shelving, stand plants on it, or top it with an acrylic worktop as a bar counter. The point she insists on is that this is decided **in advance** — otherwise the unit is "грустным железным гробиком."

## Regulations / Permits / Approvals — Russia (Moscow), NOT for `16_`

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Russian jurisdiction — Moscow municipal. Recorded here as a Russian data point only.**

- **⚠️ Joining a balcony/loggia to the heated apartment is illegal.** She is unambiguous: "К сожалению, ответ всего один. Это незаконно."
- **The history matters for reading the second-hand market**: until roughly ten years before the video, combining the warm apartment with the cold balcony **was legal in Russia and was easily approved** — which is why "на вторичном рынке огромное количество квартир с присоединёнными балконами." A pre-2017 joined balcony is not evidence the practice is currently permitted.
- **⚠️ The change she cites: the updated version of Moscow city-government decree No. 508 (постановление правительства Москвы № 508), 2017**, which she says prohibits in plain text the removal of the glazing between a balcony/loggia and the apartment. `single-account`, `unverified` — the decree number and year are recorded as she stated them and have not been checked against the text.
- **⚠️ The one legal route to visual joining: a "French window"** — a tall, large window with **sliding leaves** — and **by law it must be a double-chamber glazing unit** so heat does not escape.
- **The stated rationale, which is the transferable part**: if everyone up and down a heating riser removed the glazing, heat would flow outdoors and the heat-supply capacity would not cover it.
- **Her honest note on enforcement, recorded as observation not advice**: this is among the most common perpetration alongside a missing shower threshold; she explicitly does not recommend breaking the law, while noting clients ask for it directly and it is usually overlooked — an unauthorised opening in a bearing wall makes an apartment hard to sell, a missing balcony door generally does not.

## Heating — the balcony's own hard rule

- **⚠️ Never extend heating appliances fed from the building's common riser (общедомовой стояк) onto the balcony.** This is stated as the single most important don't in the section: "самое главное."
- **A balcony may be heated by electricity only** — **underfloor heating, convectors, heated baseboard — but not radiators.**
- **⚠️ Heated baseboard (тёплый плинтус) is her "крутой и неочевидный" option**: a baseboard with a heating element inside it, creating a **thermal curtain in front of the balcony's glazing.** Recorded as the mechanism, which is what makes it suited to a fully-glazed balcony specifically.

## Balcony / Loggia — glazing upgrade paths by building age

- **Old buildings**: simplest route is replacing the cold glazing units with **warm double-chamber (двухкамерные)** units.
- **New buildings with unified facade glazing**: either **add a second pane into the existing unit**, or **install your own warm glazing as a second contour inside the building's overall glazing.**
- **Either way, the glazing has to change** — "в любом случае остекление нужно менять."
- **⚠️ Windowsill blocks (подоконные блоки): whether they may be removed is individual to the building's construction and series — verify for your specific case.** She declines to generalise, which is itself the useful instruction.

## Surfaces / Finishes — what to finish a balcony with

Her framing: "это вроде бы и не улица, но и не квартира."

- **Floor: tile or porcelain, unambiguously, with underfloor heating beneath it.** "Керамогранит — лучший материал на пол на балконе." Any neutral pattern that matches the interior — loft, concrete-look, eastern ornament, modern classic.
- **Walls: paint — and she recommends using facade paint** ("краску я на всякий случай рекомендую фасадную использовать"). Also viable: porcelain, clinker.
- **A real project she cites**: walls finished in **вагонка (tongue-and-groove boarding), painted**, with woven cushions on the floor — "смотрелось очень уютно," and matched the owners' brief.
- **Windowsill**: decide deliberately whether it is thin and narrow or takes on a function — bar counter, plant stand.

## Balcony / Loggia — making it genuinely usable year-round

Her five points for turning it into "плюс одна комната," as distinct from merely insulated-on-paper:

1. **⚠️ Do not economise on insulation and heating — the failure mode is stated concretely**: save **50,000 RUB** (2024-09, Russia) ≈ **$550** (USD equivalent, 12-month trailing mean to 2024-09-05, 91.2317 RUB/USD; comparability aid, not a quote) and you either sit in the study under a blanket all winter and get bronchitis, or abandon it and end up working at the kitchen table. **She deliberately does not cover insulation build-ups** ("про это есть тысяча отдельных роликов"), only the decision not to underspend.
2. **⚠️ Plan the electrics, and the outlet list is the useful part**: general light **plus** local light — cosy over a lounge spot, or bright over a work position. Then outlets beyond the obvious spares: **an infrared plant lamp, a Christmas-light garland in winter, gadget charging, outlets at the work position, an outlet for charging a tablet while reading.**
3. **Choose a style that "дружит" with the adjoining room** — but because a balcony is small and not used constantly, **it can carry more intensity than the rest of the flat**: ethnic, eastern, boho, loft.
4. **⚠️ Plan ventilation explicitly.** A south-facing balcony is "просто пекло" in summer; an open window may be unusable because of traffic noise, in which case **a breather (бризер)** is the route. And **if the balcony is a full room, it will probably need an air conditioner of its own.**
5. **Privacy from neighbours' sightlines**: roller or Roman blinds, venetian blinds, or simply reflective film.

## Furniture / Built-ins — storage, treated as the Russian default use

- **⚠️ If storage is genuinely needed, split the balcony into two parts** — storage at one end, something useful at the other — "так хоть не жалко будет."
- **Decide what is being stored before choosing the system** — jars behave differently from bicycles.
- **Named hardware routes**: ceiling mounts and cable hoists for **bicycles** ("велик можно подвесить к потолку и за тросы подтянуть"); a **snowboard** can become decoration; for laundry, **electric ceiling-lift dryers**, **"лиана"-type ceiling-lift racks**, and **retractable dryers** — explicitly instead of the floor-standing frame that occupies the whole balcony.
- **If a cabinet is needed: one large cabinet with opaque fronts**, not several.

## Cost Drivers — the "is it worth converting at all" check

- **⚠️ The strongest budget argument in the source, and a genuine counterweight to the whole video**: those 3 m² "могут стать просто золотыми в масштабе вашего ремонта" on a limited budget. Insulating, replacing glazing, finishing, running heating, laying underfloor heating and furnishing it is **"не копейки" and "не одна сотня тысяч рублей"** (i.e. several hundred thousand RUB, 2024) in this climate.
- **Her stated legitimate outcome: decide not to do it** — and use the balcony twice a year on a summer evening. Recorded because a designer conceding the null option is unusual and is directly relevant to this project's own budget prioritisation.
