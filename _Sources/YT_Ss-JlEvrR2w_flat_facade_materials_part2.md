## ⚠️ A correction to part 1 — laminate CAN be machined, just not decoratively

- **⚠️ What it cannot take is decorative milling on the visible face** — a pattern, a texture, grooves, recesses. **"Сделать ты это можешь только из МДФ."**

## Materials — three routes to a wood texture

1. **Imitation** — plastic or ЛДСП carrying a wood decor. Available in many colours and textures, **thermostructured/deep or smooth.**
2. **Natural veneer** — a 1.2 mm slice, prepared and bonded to MDF, then either tinted to the required colour or simply lacquered.
3. **⚠️ Fine-line** — cheap timber brought to the plant, made into a large block, sliced into thin strips, **and printed.** **"У тебя как бы настоящее дерево, но на него всё равно нанесён принт." In mass it reads homogeneous, unlike natural veneer.**
   - **⚠️ His fairest framing of it, and it is more precise than part 1's: "нам продали дуб, либо нам продали условную осину под дуб — но это тоже натуральное дерево." The species is substituted, not the material.** What matters is being told.

## Materials — ⚠️ stone facades, and the mounting engineering (entirely new)

- **Stone imitation on plastic: they use it very rarely — "ещё дерево как-то похоже на натуральное; камень совсем не то."** **The one exception he grants: Egger's concrete-look is "неплохо сделан."**
- **⚠️ Natural marble on a facade must be thinned down, because standard slabs are 20 or 30 mm and far too heavy — "если ты вот это повесишь на фасад, у тебя ни одна фурнитура его не выдержит."** Natural stone also absorbs, so under normal use a countertop risks spoiling, and facades are delicate. **Cost "в космос," to the point he calls it effectively unnecessary.**
- **⚠️ The practical alternative: quartz agglomerate — "на 90% камень, на 10% пластик."** Usable on facades; an expensive technology but "вид имеет."
- **⚠️ The thickness rule: use 5 mm on facades, never 20 mm.** Matte or glossy available; **matte is the better choice for contemporary interiors.**
- **⚠️ And the mounting mechanism, which is the genuinely new engineering: you cannot bond a drawer box or a hinge to stone, and drilling it will crack it — "это будет почти нереальной задачей." So a substrate is required.** Two constructions he shows:
  1. **A painted MDF facade with the 5 mm stone bonded to its face; the drawer box is then fixed to the MDF.** Only the stone is visible.
  2. **MDF painted on the reverse (any colour, tactilely pleasant) plus an aluminium frame, with the stone bonded on top.**
- **⚠️ The same aluminium-frame substrate also accepts thin veneer, or glass** — so it is a general carrier for heavy or fragile facings, not a stone-only technique. Complements part 1's finding that glass goes on an aluminium frame because of weight.

## Materials — plastics, deeper than part 1

- **⚠️ HPL is a distinct product, not a synonym: "он более толстый, слоистый — у него есть слои, как в фанере."** AGT and the rest are "просто пластик — как ты его не назови."
- **⚠️ Why they do not work with the expensive plastics — four objections**: much dearer than laminate, with a good plastic's price "стремится уже к эмали, к шпонам, к натуральным материалам" and sometimes exceeding them; **far fewer colours than enamel — his example is Fenix, "вот у тебя все цвета, всё, больше нет"**; **fingerprints remain**; and **any plastic still has edge banding, where enamel has none.**
- **⚠️ The economising trap, and it is specific: cheap plastic application leaves the reverse a different colour (white) — so opening a door adds a colour to the kitchen. Cladding both faces in expensive plastic is very costly — "готовь бабки."**
- **⚠️ The one case where AGT plastic IS the rational choice: white gloss.** AGT is relatively cheap against Fenix — **"может, в два раза даже дешевле"** — and **gloss in enamel costs substantially more than gloss in plastic.** Caveat he attaches: gloss is "для продвинутых пользователей," to be used carefully, and appears less often in contemporary interiors.
- **⚠️ Matte grades, useful vocabulary: lacquers come at 10% and 20% matte, and the mattest is "пятёрка" (5%).** Fenix has a deeper structure and reads slightly more matte, **but enamels can achieve a similar effect.**

## Materials — metal and metal-effect facades

- **Metal-effect is achievable in enamel** — brass, copper, oxidised and non-oxidised copper — via polyurethane enamels plus additives, giving rubberised, metallic, matte or gloss surfaces. **⚠️ This is his stated reason for building the business around enamel: "эмаль универсальна… любой каприз она может выполнить" — but "с ней нужно уметь работать."**
- **⚠️ Actual metal facades he scopes out of residential use: "больше такая тема коммерции — ресторанных кухонь, больших зон для готовки, загородных домов со специальными техническими кухнями."**
- **⚠️ The durability objection is the reason: "поцарапал ты этот фасад — что ты с ним сделаешь? Ничего, будешь менять. Дорого ли это стоит? Да, дорого."** And copper will oxidise. Do not overdo it — a kitchen entirely in metal is a taste question, not a default.

## Design Concept — colour combination

- **⚠️ "Не нужно сочетать более трёх цветов на кухне"** — note this is stated as **three** here, where his ten-mistakes video says **a maximum of two plus the countertop.** **Effectively the same envelope from two directions; the ten-mistakes formulation is the more precise one because it names what the third slot is for.**
- **⚠️ "Не нужно сочетать разные по теплоте оттенки"** — same rule as the ten-mistakes video, and he demonstrates a better and a worse pairing on camera: **wood that is warm against a cool plain tone is usable, but the pairing whose warmth matches "более уместно и более стильно выглядит."**

## ⚠️ Cost Drivers — his own current renovation as a worked allocation

**The most valuable block in the source, because it is the maker spending his own money:**

- **Flooring: engineered board.**
- **Kitchen furniture: natural veneer combined with Fenix.**
- **⚠️ All other furniture: ЛДСП facades — "я не олигарх, делать всё вокруг в шпоне, в эмали смысла нет. Поэтому рационально использовать ЛДСП. И у меня 70% мебели будет сделано в ЛДСП."**
- **No milling anywhere** — his design does not call for it.
- **⚠️ The demonstration that carries the argument: he holds an ЛДСП sample, a veneer sample and a Fenix sample together. "Он имеет практически такой же оттенок — один в один. Оба этих цвета сочетаются со шпоном. Разницы визуально практически нет никакой." And the conclusion: "зачем тебе в два раза больше денег отдавать, например, за гардеробный шкаф?"**
- **⚠️ So the operative rule is two-part: choose the colours correctly, then use the materials rationally.** Their standing advice to clients.
- **⚠️ Carcasses: they mostly use contrast — dark carcasses with light facades, or the reverse — "но не белый. Белый смотрится некрасиво."** A specific rejection this vault's existing carcass-colour content (economy white/light-grey; premium matched to facade) did not include.
- **Countertop: quartz agglomerate. Bathrooms: an inexpensive porcelain in an interesting colour that coordinates with everything.** **⚠️ And his reasoning is the coherence point: "по факту ты эту плитку в интерьере не видишь, она в санузлах за закрытыми дверьми — но со всем остальным она всё равно сочетается."**

## ⚠️ Design Concept — whole-apartment coherence, and the closing thesis

- **"Иногда в квартире ходишь — ты в зале находишься, зашёл в другую комнату, у тебя там другая картина, как в другой мир попал. Заходишь в третью — третья картина. В этом дизайна нету. Дизайн есть тогда, когда у тебя всё между собой концептуально совпадает. И материал в этом случае не так важен."** **Concept coherence over material grade — from a materials supplier.**
- **⚠️ The closing thesis, and it is the channel's philosophy in one line: "Нету такого понятия — плохой фасад, хороший фасад. Есть фасад, сделанный плохо."**
  - **The one genuinely bad case he names: cheap chipboard with melamine paper, where holding it to the light shows the sawdust through it** — fit only for very economy furniture.
  - **Metal is not bad where it is appropriate and fits the budget.**
  - **His analogy for "the best facade": it is like "the best car." "Audi RS5 классная машина — а если ты в деревне живёшь, не очень."** Rationality, appropriateness, budget.
