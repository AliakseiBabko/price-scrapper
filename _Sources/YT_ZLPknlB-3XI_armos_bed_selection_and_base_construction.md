## ⚠️⚠️ 1. A DIRECT CONTRADICTION with a prescriptive rule already in this vault

**[[14_Furniture/Seating_and_Tables|Seating & Tables]] states, from the Шеврина/SMBUREAU sources:** *"**A bed — and any soft furniture meant for rest — should not exceed 40 cm from the floor.**"* **And [[_Sources/YT_Rcd9gkPC6CI_kuzina_apartment_ergonomics_bedroom_kids_hallway|Кузина]] separately records that standard Russian bed height IS up to 40 cm, explicitly contrasted with American beds.**

**This source says the opposite, twice, from an ergonomic rather than a compositional argument:**

> **The flagship: the optimal combined bed + mattress height is *«сгиб нашего колена, плюс-минус ширина ладони»*** — the bend of your knee, ± a palm's width.
> **This video: *«если совместная высота кровать плюс матрас будет значительно НИЖЕ, чем ваше колено, то скорее всего вам такую кровать будет использовать не очень удобно»*.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ FOR MOST ADULTS THE KNEE BEND IS ROUGHLY 45–50 cm, SO THE TWO RULES GENUINELY DISAGREE.** **Шеврина's is a COMPOSITIONAL rule — low rest furniture reads better in a room. ARMOS's is an ERGONOMIC one — you have to get out of it.** **Both are honestly held and they pull in opposite directions.**
>
> **⚠️⚠️ AND КУЗИНА'S OBSERVATION RESOLVES WHY: if the Russian market norm really is ≤40 cm, then the market norm and the design rule agree with each other AND SIT BELOW THE ERGONOMIC OPTIMUM.** **The cost of that is paid by exactly the people both mattress videos name — older users, and anyone who finds a low bed hard to rise from.**
>
> **→ RECORDED AS A PERSPECTIVE, NOT RESOLVED. The decision for this flat depends on who will sleep in it and for how many years, and it is a question the owner should answer deliberately rather than inherit from whichever source was read last.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Sleeping-place widths, and the clearance rules around them

| Case | Width |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Child, single** | **80 or 90 cm** (standard) |
| **Adult, single** | **better 120 cm** |
| **Couple** | **from 140 cm — but that is the MINIMUM**; better **160–180**, or even 200 |

- **⚠️⚠️ THE RULE UNDERNEATH THE WIDTHS: from each person to the edge of the bed, or to their partner, there should ideally be 20 cm or more.** **→ That converts "how wide a bed" from taste into arithmetic.**
- **⚠️⚠️ PASSAGES: 50 cm or more** around the bed. **→ *«Замерьте место, где будет стоять кровать — это будут максимально возможные габариты вашей кровати.»*** **The room sets the ceiling, then the 20 cm rule tells you whether what fits is enough.**
- **⚠️ And his honest consequence, which is why the next item exists:** *«большинство из нас живёт не во дворцах»*, so passages stay tight and **TRAUMA SAFETY comes to the fore**.

## ⚠️ 3. What to check on the bed itself

- **⚠️⚠️ Trauma safety, and it is specific:** corners **soft and rounded**; **legs must not protrude — ideally set slightly INWARD from the bed's outline**, which is what stops you kicking them. ⚠️ **Directly relevant to a tight bedroom, which is what `06_Small_Bedroom/` is about.**
- **⚠️⚠️ ASK TO SEE THE INTERNAL CONSTRUCTION** — in a shop ask the seller; **online, ask for photographs.** **Everything should be assembled on BOLTS with reliable metal fittings.** **Ideal: a metal-frame bed — strongest and most durable.**
- **The base is an ортопедическая решётка / lamellae base**, usually a metal structure with springy wooden elements. **Ideal: solid-welded, non-dismountable** — the guarantee against squeaking and against it coming apart.
- **Lift mechanism on gas struts** plus a large linen box — practical, saves space in a flat.
- **⚠️ Ground clearance is a cleaning decision**: **no legs** (sitting on the floor) means no dust accumulates underneath, **but the plinth can be damaged by a vacuum cleaner**; **high legs** let a **robot vacuum pass underneath** and are easier to clean around.
- **⚠️⚠️ UPHOLSTERY, AND THIS ONE CORROBORATES THE VAULT INDEPENDENTLY:** **экокожа (faux leather) is unsuitable if you want 10–15 years** — it seems easy to wipe but does not last. **And with pets, рогожка is unsuitable however good and expensive, because the interlacing fibres catch claws; velour or flock are better, since a claw has nothing to grip.**
  **→ ⚠️⚠️ SECOND INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL. [[14_Furniture/analysis/Loose_Furniture_Selection_Principles|Loose Furniture Selection Principles]] already records the same mechanism from Шеврина about bouclé and ribbed velvet snagging on pet claws. Two unrelated sources, same physical reason, different fabrics named.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. The base — the number, and why it matters

**Lamellae are springy wooden elements that *«работают вместе с упругими элементами матраса, усиливая его анатомические свойства»*** — the base is part of the suspension, not a shelf.

> ***«Самое главное, чтобы расстояние между ними не было больше чем 7–8 сантиметров, чтобы ПРУЖИНЫ МАТРАСА НЕ ПРОВАЛИВАЛИСЬ в эти промежутки — тогда срок службы матраса существенно сократится, он будет изнашиваться гораздо быстрее.»***

- **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE MECHANISM IS NAMED: individual springs drop into an over-wide gap and are worked outside their design range.** **The victim is the MATTRESS, not the base** — the same asymmetry as the bonnell block wearing out the settling layers. ⚠️ **The flagship says "not more than 8 cm"; this one says "7–8". Recorded as 7–8 cm, with 8 as the outer limit.**
- **⚠️ Frame**: solid welded and non-dismountable is strongest; **a dismountable frame gives no guarantee against squeaks later.**
- **⚠️ If the lamellae sit on a separate metal frame, check for SOFT DAMPING ELEMENTS where that frame rests on the bed** — they prevent knocking, **and he flags this as especially important on a lift-mechanism bed.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Building a base yourself — rare, specific, and it covers the pallet trap

**Two routes:**
1. **Buy a ready metal frame and supports**, then make the suspension, sides and headboard.
2. **Make the base from ЛДСП, sanded plywood or similar sheet material.** ***«Главное, чтобы у конструкции было достаточно большое количество рёбер жёсткости и дополнительной опоры. Заложите в неё ИЗБЫТОЧНУЮ ПРОЧНОСТЬ — это гарантирует вас от поломок, скрипов и проблем в эксплуатации.»***

> **⚠️⚠️ AND THE PALLET WARNING, which follows directly from §4 and is worth having written down:** pallet beds are a known design idea, **but the gaps between pallet boards are far larger than 7–8 cm**, so the surface *«не подходит для современных матрасов»*. **→ It must be levelled and covered with sheet material — ЛДСП or sanded plywood — before a modern mattress goes on it.**
>
> ⚠️ **A solid sheet base is acceptable here, which is worth noting because it contradicts the folk belief that a mattress must always breathe through slats — though it sits against the SAME source's emphasis elsewhere on ventilation, and that tension is not resolved in the video.**
