## Kitchen, Furniture/Built-ins

- **⚠️ Backsplash-mounted rail/rack systems ("рейлинги," quoted inline) for hanging utensils** — critiqued as a 2000s-era relic; collects grease/dust/grime on every hanging utensil within about a month of use; breaks a clean minimalist sightline; challenges the reader to admit how rarely the hanging utensils are actually used vs. just displayed. Recommends hidden drawer or wall-cabinet storage instead.
- **Pull-out wire/metal baskets for pots and pans** — critiqued for constant rattling noise when opened/closed, contents shifting/falling especially with small items, "feels like working in a metal warehouse." Recommends standard soft-close drawers with proper organization.
- **⚠️ Drawer dividers/organizer inserts (general) — with an explicit named exception**: critiqued as inflexible — consume space, force rearranging the whole layout whenever new dishes/cookware don't fit the pre-set slots. Recommends properly-sized labeled containers for bulk dry goods, or stacking dishware. **Explicit stated exception: cutlery dividers specifically ARE worth using** — forks/spoons/knives are small and genuinely benefit from sorted compartments.
- **Electronic touch-sensor / motion-activated cabinet openers** (hands-free, foot- or hand-wave-activated) — critiqued as finicky: requires precise hand/foot placement in the exact sensor zone, misses the trigger or worse triggers accidentally when someone brushes past.
- **Electronic/button-actuated cabinet openers** (open by hand, close via a button) — critiqued as an unnecessary extra step, plus a real mechanical/electronics failure risk (the button stops responding, leaving a half-open cabinet).
- **⚠️ "Tip-on" push-to-open mechanisms — independently corroborates `7bhTD8FnV5Y`'s (this same round) push-to-open critique**: requires precise/firm pressure to trigger reliably, fingerprints accumulate FAST especially on glossy fronts, mechanism degrades over time and starts sticking/requiring extra force.
- **Countertop-integrated trash chute/opening** (a hole cut directly into the countertop leading to a bin below) — critiqued for breaking the clean look of the work surface, always visually reading as "an open trash can," odor buildup if not emptied promptly, food debris/grease collecting around the opening.
- **Pull-out/extendable auxiliary tables** (a slide-out worktop/table from within a cabinet or island) — critiqued for wobbling/tipping under load (explicitly: don't put a heavy pot of soup on one); the slide mechanism wears out over time. Recommends a proper fixed additional work surface instead.
- **⚠️ Built-in bottle racks/wine-bottle cabinet inserts ("бутылочница," quoted inline)** — critiqued as one of the least reliable kitchen mechanisms: bottles roll, shift, and fall when the rack is closed/opened; a broken/leaking oil bottle inside creates a smell that's extremely hard to fully remove from the cabinet interior afterward. Recommends skipping this feature entirely unless you have enough bottles to keep the rack genuinely full/stable at all times.
- **⚠️ Hidden drawer nested behind a single cabinet door (double-open storage) — independently corroborates `7bhTD8FnV5Y`'s (this same round) identical critique**: opening the visible front THEN pulling the inner drawer wastes motion/time; items in the hidden drawer are easy to forget entirely, causing you to re-buy/search elsewhere for something you already own. Recommends standard single-action drawers instead.
