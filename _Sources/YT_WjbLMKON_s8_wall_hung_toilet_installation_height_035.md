## Bathroom / WC

- **Wall-hung toilet target installation height: 40cm** (measured to the
  seat/rim, matching the long-standardized height of an ordinary
  floor-mounted toilet) — the source's core rule, stated as the *only*
  correct answer, explicitly **rejecting per-client height customization**
  as a wrong approach even though hidden-frame ("инсталляция") systems
  physically support a wide adjustable range. **Explicit reasoning against
  customization**: a client uses toilets outside their home constantly
  (work, restaurants, other people's homes) — nearly all of which are
  ordinary floor-mounted units at the standard height — so a home
  installation tuned to one household member's individual comfort (or an
  average across a multi-generational household with very different
  heights, e.g. the anecdote's 1.88m and 1.59m household members plus
  young children) actually produces a less comfortable, less familiar
  fixture than simply matching the universal standard everyone is already
  used to. Toilets also physically arrive on-site near the very end of
  the renovation (the hidden frame installs early, encased in
  moisture-resistant drywall + tile with only the drain stub left open,
  and a disposable temporary toilet used by the crew in the meantime) —
  so fitting the real fixture to a specific person mid-project isn't
  practically possible anyway, reinforcing the standard-height default.
- **Two reasons the installed height commonly ends up above the 40cm
  target, and two remedies to try before resorting to a podium**: (1)
  toilet distance from the stack — the farther the toilet sits from the
  soil stack, the higher its outlet must sit to maintain the drain
  pipe's required gravity slope; (2) the stack's own connection point
  being set too high to begin with. **Remedy 1: position the toilet as
  close to the stack as the layout allows.** **Remedy 2: lower the
  stack's own drain connection point** (physically rework/lower the
  stack tie-in) so the outlet can sit closer to the floor. Both are
  described as usually sufficient to hit a comfortable height without a
  podium.
- **Podium (raised platform) threshold, when the two remedies above
  aren't enough**: if the toilet still ends up installed too high,
  recommended podium height is **~47cm**, with **~45cm as the practical
  minimum** (45cm is workable but "a bit uncomfortable"; 47cm is
  comfortable). Stated rationale for a podium at all: an elevated,
  ergonomically-awkward toilet height can physically prevent proper
  relaxation/full evacuation, a real functional (not just cosmetic)
  problem — but a podium itself is flagged as "still a bit
  inconvenient" (a daily habituation cost, stepping up), so it's
  presented as the fallback once the two remedies above are exhausted,
  not a first-choice fix.
