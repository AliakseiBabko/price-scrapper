  theme-specific)**: once you've picked what looks like the perfect shade

  card before committing — a small sample reads lighter than the same
  paint will look once applied across a full wall/room, so the as-built
  result consistently reads darker than expected from the swatch. Stated
  as a rule for anyone choosing paint, not specific to this room; flagged

  style**: when a household has a distinctive existing furniture finish

- **Color-repetition dilution rule**: don't repeat a favorite color
  throughout a room in multiple places — pairing it with a contrasting or
  complementary color instead makes the favorite color read more vividly
