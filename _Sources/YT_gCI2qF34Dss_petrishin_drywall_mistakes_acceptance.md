## Rules / Heuristics — Drywall Frame & Board Technique

1. **Bevel GKL board edges at 45° before installation** so boards fit
   tightly together without needing corner/edge trim (`малярные
   уголки`) at the seams — a board-prep step, not just a fastening rule.
2. **Metal profile frame cells must be ≤400mm and uniform in size** — a
   wider or irregular cell spacing lets drywall sag under its own
   weight.
3. **Guide rails (`направляющие`) must be installed strictly level** —
   the whole frame's accuracy depends on this reference line being true.
4. **Use anchor wedges (`анкер-клинья`), not nails or plastic dowels,**
   for both wall fastening and ceiling-hanger attachment — metal anchors
   don't expand/contract with temperature swings the way plastic dowels
   do, reducing pull-out risk under sustained load. **Reinforced by a
   real failure case this video opens with** (a collapsed ceiling
   attributed to poor fastening).
5. **Profile-to-profile cross joints need a proper connector, not a
   simple two-piece butt join** — an improperly joined cross-member
   develops bulges/waviness ("бугры и неровности") over time. Correct
   method: a named crab-style connector/lock (Knauf) plus self-tapping
   screws.
6. **Perimeter damper/compression tape under the guide rails** prevents
   drywall cracking at the wall junction and gives a tighter
   wall-to-frame connection — a brand (Knauf) is named for this tape,
   tagged as a commercial mention.
7. **Two-layer drywall should be installed in a staggered (checkerboard)
   pattern relative to the first layer's seams**, not seam-aligned, for
   structural strength — reinforces this project's general double-layer
   drywall practice with the specific staggering mechanism named.
