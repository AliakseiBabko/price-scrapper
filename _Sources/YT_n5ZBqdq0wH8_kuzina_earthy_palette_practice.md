## Design Concept — The Earthy Palette as a Practical Working Problem

- **⚠️ The counter-intuitive premise, stated as the whole point of the video**: "логика, чем спокойнее цвета, тем проще их сочетать, не работает." Muted natural palettes (бежевый, оливковый, коричневый, терракота) are where boring/dirty beige interiors most often come from, **precisely because** all the shades sit close together — the gap between песочный, льняной, кремовый and бежевый is minimal, each carries a **подтон** that is hard to read by eye and that shifts with lighting and time of day, and **there is no brightness to hide a подтон error behind**. An error in undertone therefore reads immediately as flat and dirty.
- **⚠️ The palette's actual membership, in five named groups** — offered explicitly against the common assumption that it is "five shades of sand":
  1. **Earth and clay** — коричневые, терракота, глина, охра, сиена, умбра, красная земля. Called the hot core: warm, dense, grounded.
  2. **Sand** — бежевый, песочный, plus слоновая кость and лён. Lighter, softer, nearly neutral but warm inside; the group whose job is to dilute the rest and lighten the overall impression.
  3. **Plants** — оливковый, шалфей, мох, эвкалипт, пыльно-розовый. Caution attached: take a natural green that isn't too bright, and a pink that is "почти не розовый."
  4. **Stone and minerals** — мел, галька, сланец. **Explicit exclusion: not мрамор or оникс** — the reference is what you'd find on a streambed or underfoot, not a quarried noble stone; and the shades must not go too cold.
  5. **Wood and its derivatives** — старый дуб, орех, каштан, plus мокрая кора, янтарь/смола, уголь. Dark, viscous, tactile.
  - **Stated correction to a common error**: розоватые, дымчато-голубые and приглушённые сливовые are legitimately inside this palette, so reducing it to beige-and-brown is "большая ошибка."
- **⚠️ The membership test — by image, not by theory**: picture сухоцвет, лён, a clay pot, old wood and stone lying together, then mentally place the candidate color next to them. Does it look like it was always part of that company, or like "случайный гость"? If the latter, it's out. The stated basis is that the palette "строится не на названиях, а на ощущении, что все эти вещи принадлежат к одной среде." She recommends applying the same test to a room you already own.
- **⚠️ Two-sided spacing rule between shades**: too-similar shades give "не глубина, а грязь" — the eye can't separate them and everything merges into one patch. Tone-on-tone can work but demands very precise handling of materials and undertones, so **beginners should start with more contrast *inside* the palette** — named workable pairs: оливковый + кремовый (the light parts then read fresh), or каштановый + тёплый камень + уголь (so the eye has somewhere to stop).
- **⚠️ Her own stated control variable**: "я слежу за растяжкой по светлоте больше, чем за конкретным цветом, и ограничиваю их количество." Concrete prescription: **take two or three colors from the palette and generate variety through lightness spread and texture rather than by adding more hues.**
- **Dark/dramatic variant guidance**: the earthy palette suits it well — **глубокий коричневый works softer and warmer than тёмно-серый**, giving saturation without coldness. If the space starts "проваливаться," add a light shade to lift the warm tones and return air.
- **Materials are load-bearing, not decoration**: earth colors on their own are "плоские пятна"; volume and character come from лён, джут, raw wood, шерсть, керамика, камень. **Named alternation principle: чередовать гладкое и грубое, матовое и чуть отражающее**, so the eye has somewhere to travel.
- **Trend-trajectory note (dated, treat as time-bound)**: the palette started as classic терракота and охра, added оливковый and сливовый, and is now moving toward **"Earthy Vibrancy"** — a more saturated version with complex blues, deep ochres and faded yellows. She notes the palette is expanding faster than its mental image is settling.
- **Selection-criterion meta-rule**: "выбор может строиться на любом критерии. Главное, чтобы он был, и чтобы вы сами его понимали и не потеряли в процессе" — the criterion's existence and consistency matters more than which criterion it is.
