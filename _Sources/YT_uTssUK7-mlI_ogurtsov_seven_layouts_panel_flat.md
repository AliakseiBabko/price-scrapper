# Seven layout variants for one flat, walked through in 3D — the workflow itself

**The owner named this as the flow he wants.** One panel two-room flat, seven layout variants, each walked from inside the model.

## ⚠️⚠️ The variants CONVERGE, and convergence is the stop signal

> *«Эти два варианта, они уже не меняются. То есть это финальный вариант… Можно даже эти два варианта и считать за один.»*

Variants 5, 6 and 7 differ only in trivia — an antresol removed, a bath rotated back. **Seven nominal variants are about three or four real ones.** He is explicit that several were micro-revisions, and that at least one difference was **his own mistake**: *«Я, скорее всего, тут забыл переключиться просто.»*

## ⚠️⚠️ He predicted the client's choice wrong on every count

> *«Вариант, который выбрал бы я, точно не этот… Я прямо не угадал, похоже, ничего.»*

He then lists his own preference room by room and it differs from the client's in the living room, the children's room and the bathroom. **An experienced designer's preference did not predict his own client's choice** — which is a strong argument for presenting feasible options rather than a recommendation, and it matches the rule this project already adopted: the model says what is feasible, the owner chooses.

## ⚠️ Electrical points appear ONLY in the final variant

> *«Вот он финальный, седьмой вариант уже с электроточками.»*

**Sockets and switches are added AFTER the layout converges**, not during.

⚠️⚠️ **This is in genuine tension with this project's sequencing**, which puts first-fix early precisely because it is irreversible. The reconciliation is in the brief, not the method: **his client picks one layout and builds it**, while this flat must serve **two future occupancy scenarios** whose branch point is years away. Waiting for convergence here would mean waiting for Phase 2. **The tension is real and is recorded rather than smoothed over.**

## Method points worth copying

- **Start from the survey walkthrough**, then name the immovables first — two transverse load-bearing panels and the vent block. ⚠️ **The constraint is economic, not absolute**: *«может быть и реалистично, но слишком-слишком дорого»*.
- **Always start the layout at the прихожая**, because the entrance door is effectively immovable.
- **Door position is decided on SIGHTLINES from the entrance**, not circulation alone: mirroring one doorway would have meant *«Добро пожаловать, заходите в детскую, а то и в спальню»*.
- **Plan the children's room for two children from the start**, because the brief says another child is possible — *«Возможно, ещё пополнение. Это всегда учитываем.»*
- **Variant diffs are walked along the same route each time**, room by room: *«можно теперь сверять часы, что у нас изменилось в прихожей»*.
- ⚠️ **He cannot remember why some changes were made** — *«Честно, я не припоминаю, по какой причине это было сделано»*. An argument for recording rationale per variant, which `design_intents.csv` already does.
- **The electrical panel rarely moves in a panel house**, so it is hidden inside a cupboard rather than relocated.

## Transferable design findings

- **Electric towel rail instead of water**: *«Водяной… часто подтекают… не так уж много жрёт электрический, чтобы рисковать, что там всё затопит.»* ⚠️ Directly relevant — `T1` in this flat is a **water** towel-rail tail, capped and awaiting a rail.
- **A bath can shrink to gain storage**: 180 → 170 or even 160, *«некоторым даже этот размер больше нравится»*.
- **A long room is the prime candidate for splitting in the middle** — *«можно и свет сохранить, и сделать две небольшие полновесные комнаты»* — with the honest limit that *«прорубить окно тут никто не даст»*, so the inner half never gets a window. ⚠️ **That is exactly `DI-004`'s situation**, and it is the first outside confirmation that the move is normal practice rather than a compromise peculiar to this flat.
- **Bunk beds cause fights**: *«дети любят вторые этажи, и будет драка вполне возможно»*. `DI-005` already excludes them; here is a second, non-clinical reason.
- **Sink between fridge and hob** reads better than the alternatives.
- **Don't put a mirror where you meet yourself on entering.**
