## Furniture / Built-ins — Durable Facts & Rules

- **A walk-in closet attached to a child's bedroom should be entered from a shared hallway/vestibule, not from inside the child's own room** — entering from the child's room consumes wall space inside that room that could otherwise hold a proper wardrobe (with mirrored doors). Entering from a shared hallway instead keeps the full room wall available for furniture. `confirmed`, the source's stated "biggest mistake."

- **Side benefit of the hallway-access version**: additionally allows placing a full-length mirror on the wardrobe door face in the child's room (impossible if that wall is occupied by the closet doorway instead). `confirmed`, corroborates the entrance-mirror rule already in this store from a different Zemstandart source (`b2rxvzj55Q0`).
