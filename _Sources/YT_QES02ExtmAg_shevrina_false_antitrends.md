## Design Concept — The Framing Thesis

- **⚠️ Her category distinction, which is the reusable idea here**: the items she defends "также не являются и трендами" — they are neither trend nor anti-trend but **permanently current base solutions**, so they can be adopted without timing risk. Her recurring argument for the natural materials in this class (marble, chrome, concrete/microcement, wood slats): **a natural material cannot be a trend or an anti-trend**, because it predates and will outlast the discourse.
- **⚠️ The most quotable diagnostic in the source**: "чаще всего людей тошнит не от мрамора, давайте будем честны, а от плохого дизайна вокруг этого мрамора." Applied identically to slats — "тут плохие не рейки, тут плохой дизайн." **The generalisable claim: a material blamed for a bad result is usually a scapegoat for the composition around it.** Worth recording because it is the direct counterweight to anti-trend listicles as a genre, including her own.
- **⚠️ A stated universal rule for durability**: "любое упрощение чего-либо можно сразу записывать в новую базу" — any simplification, and any concealment of technical elements, produces an interior that stays current. This is the reasoning she applies to track lights, concealed doors and shadow-gap plinths, and it is a compact statement of the design philosophy running through the whole channel.

## Materials / Design Concept — Items With Real Content

- **⚠️ Marble and marble-look format rule.** With marble specifically "не существует золотой середины." **Choose either very large formats — 120×60 and larger — or genuinely small ones; avoid 20×40 and 30×60**, which do not flatter a marble decor because the repeat becomes obtrusive. (Restated in `XGI6FS2ZdCc`; same channel, so not independent corroboration.)
- **⚠️ Microcement and decorative plaster: the selection criterion is texture, not the material.** Smooth, calm, non-mottled, low-contrast, visually light textures stay current; **visually heavy, mottled, obtrusive textures will start to irritate over time.** A usable selection rule rather than a verdict on the material.
- **⚠️ Co-planar sliding systems ("компланарные системы открывания") as the modern alternative to a шкаф-купе.** Mechanism as she describes it: **both fronts sit flush in the closed position; to open, one front moves slightly forward and then travels parallel to the other; closing returns it flush.** Her caveat: **not suitable for tall floor-to-ceiling wardrobes** — better on suspended TV units and similar.
- **⚠️ For floor-to-ceiling sliding storage, order it as a partition, not as a wardrobe — a genuinely actionable procurement instruction.** "Вы обращаетесь не в столярную мастерскую и не заказываете шкаф-купе, а обращаетесь к перегородочникам." Modern partition systems **conceal the floor and ceiling rails**, so you get a full-height sliding front with no visible track and no applied structure, with the storage (shelving, hanging) sitting behind it. **This directly extends [[17_Design_and_Ergonomics/analysis/Sliding_Partition_Mechanisms|Sliding Partition Mechanisms]]**, which covers ceiling-hung partitions as room dividers but not this use of the same hardware as concealed storage frontage.
- **⚠️ Shadow-gap plinth ("теневой плинтус") — coverage and benefits.** Now available for **all floor-covering types and all wall finishes including panels** — laminate, engineered board, paint, wallpaper. Stated benefits: simplifies cleaning and visually lightens the interior. **No dedicated page exists anywhere in this vault for shadow-gap plinths**, despite three sources in this trial discussing them. (The practicality argument is made far more concretely in `0WXiKNXPD_0`, with the 10–15 mm mop / 20 mm profile numbers.)
- **Chrome** defended as a durable specification choice for taps, on the reasoning that it is a natural metal finish widely available and therefore low-risk — offered as a counterweight to the current preference for black, dark steel and warm nickel.
- **Blinds and roller blinds** defended as fully current, particularly for minimalist interiors, against the perception that they read as office-like.
- **Beige interiors** defended on a demand argument: clients ask for them, and the reason is the pace of life and the volume of visual noise people absorb, so they want calm at home rather than visual load. Consistent with the beige defence from Школа дизайна Маши Черной in the 2026-08-30 colour batch, from an unrelated channel.
