## Genuinely New Content — Repairability and Durability

- **A concrete flood-repair cost and mechanism for stretch ceiling, first specific figure for this store**: a technician removes the membrane (**~10,000 RUB, ≈$110**), then reinstalls it after drying (**~10,000 RUB, ≈$110**) — total **~15,000-20,000 RUB (≈$160-$220)** for a full flood-repair cycle. **Drywall's flood outcome is qualitatively worse, not just costlier**: water sits and pools inside the metal channel profiles themselves, where it rusts and rots over time — described as essentially unsalvageable rather than a clean repair. **Plastered ceiling usually survives a flood intact**, with one real failure mode: if water penetrates between the plaster layer and the concrete slab, the plaster can delaminate and fall — described as rare (the source's own company has seen it happen, but calls it "very rare," not never).

- **The presenter's own stated ranking (not a client recommendation, explicitly his own preference)**: stretch ceiling is his personal favorite outright; if budget allows, drywall is his second choice (with shadow-gap-profile stretch as the value pick when budget is more constrained); a plastered ceiling is explicitly ranked last with, in his words, "not a single upside."
