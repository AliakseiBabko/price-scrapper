## ⚠️⚠️ The brief, and a no-deviation clause that did not hold

**The brief, stated crisply: «в шумном городе создать ТИХОЕ, УЮТНОЕ ГНЕЗДО для молодой семьи: СПАЛЬНЮ, ДЕТСКУЮ КОМНАТУ ДЛЯ ДВУХ ПАЦАНОВ, ДВА САНУЗЛА и КУХНЮ-ГОСТИНУЮ.»** Style was carte blanche — **«по стилистике меня особо не грузили»** — with one hard condition:

**⚠️⚠️ «То, что НАРИСОВАНО В ПРОЕКТЕ, должно быть ОДИН В ОДИН выполнено в реальности, то есть НИКАКИХ ОТКЛОНЕНИЙ, просто как нарисовали, так и делаем.»**

**⚠️⚠️⚠️ AND IT DID NOT HOLD. Four departures occurred in this one flat, and the pattern in how they resolved is the finding:**

| # | What happened | Who caught it | Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | **Balcony tile laid with straight 50/50 joints instead of the specified ~20% offset** | **the designer, on a site visit** | **fully stripped and relaid** |
| 2 | **A socket back-box set slightly off-line beside the door** | nobody in time | **⚠️ PERMANENT — «ВЫВОДЫ УЖЕ СДЕЛАНЫ»** |
| 3 | **Skirtings fitted before the wall battens, killing a detail** | too late | **⚠️ design abandoned — removing them meant tearing off the wallpaper too** |
| 4 | **A bathroom wall boxed in solid, omitting the specified niches** | **⚠️ THE CLIENT** | **redone to the drawings** |

- → **⚠️⚠️⚠️ THE CLAUSE PREVENTED NOTHING. DETECTION DID — and detection came from whoever happened to look. Two were caught (one by the designer, one by the client) and fixed; two were missed and are permanent.**
- → **This is the strongest evidence in the vault for a thread it has been building since Round 5: specifying a detail correctly does not get it built.** It sits directly alongside the bath-rim silicone chain (published 2021, violated by his own builders in 2023, leaked), the shadow-skirting content (four instances of crews defeating the detail), and Round 6's contractor-warranty-horizon mechanism.
- → **⚠️ AND IT SHARPENS ROUND 6's "VICTIM PROFILE", whose operative attribute was ABSENT: here the client was in ANOTHER CITY for the whole build, and the one error the client personally caught is the one that got fixed properly.**
- **⚠️ The two he classes as «НЕЗНАЧИТЕЛЬНЫЕ ОТКЛОНЕНИЯ» are of a different kind entirely — a CLIENT-INITIATED functional change: a vanity/makeup desk was designed into the bedroom and then dropped because «заказчик сказал, в принципе, мне это НЕ НУЖНО», replaced with plain storage.**
  - → **⚠️ A no-deviation clause cannot survive the client's own change of mind, and should not — this one caught a never-used item BEFORE it was built, which is the good outcome of the mechanism the vault records as the never-used vanity table.**

## ⚠️ A fully remote project, and material approval as the hard part

## ⚠️⚠️ The same tile reads differently on the floor and on the wall

Octagon tile runs across the kitchen floor and up onto the splashback: **«обратите внимание, насколько ОТЛИЧАЕТСЯ СВЕТ на полу и свет на ВЕРТИКАЛЬНОЙ поверхности. На самом деле ЭТО ОДНА И ТА ЖЕ ПЛИТКА, и смотрятся они СОВЕРШЕННО ПО-РАЗНОМУ. Часто заказчики про это ЗАБЫВАЮТ, и надо понимать, что НА РАЗНЫХ ПЛОСКОСТЯХ ЦВЕТ У МАТЕРИАЛА ТОЖЕ ОЧЕНЬ СИЛЬНО МЕНЯЕТСЯ.»**

- → **⚠️⚠️ A THIRD reference problem for judging a finish, and the vault did not have it: ORIENTATION. A sample approved flat on a table will not look the same on a wall, because the light reaching it differs. Together with the other two — the light you judge under, and the white you judge against — that is a complete set, and all three are free to apply.**
- **⚠️ Also, a mixed decorative tile needs a QUANTITY RATIO decided at design stage and a drawn setting-out: two batches arrived (field octagons and decorative ones) and «нам нужно было РАССЧИТАТЬ ИХ КОЛИЧЕСТВО, чтобы их было НЕ МНОГО И НЕ МАЛО. Я рисовал прям РАСКЛАДКУ вот этой плитки.»** Adjacent to the vault's existing finding that counting tile as equal to wall/floor area is fundamentally wrong.

## ⚠️⚠️ Elongated wood-look tile: lay it with ~20% offset, not 50/50 — with the mechanism

**«Вытянутые плитки в печах немножко ИЗГИБАЮТ, и она получается такой вот ВОЛНОЙ. И если положить плитки со сдвигом 50 НА 50, то у нас вот в этой части получается ПЕРЕПАД, и получается очень НЕРОВНЫЙ НЕКРАСИВЫЙ ПОЛ. Поэтому плитку под дерево, узкую, вытянутую, нужно класть с НЕБОЛЬШИМ СДВИГОМ, порядка 20%.»**

- → **⚠️⚠️ A first-class technical finding: plank-format tile is WARPED BY FIRING along its length, so a half-bond puts the crown of one plank against the end of its neighbour and produces lippage down the whole run. The offset must be small — about 20%. The vault holds large-format lippage content and stress-relief drilling; this is the plank version and it names a number.**
- **⚠️ He says he has a separate video on this.** *(Named candidate for a future round.)*
- **⚠️ This is departure #1 above: the builders laid it 50/50 anyway, and the whole floor was stripped and relaid.**
