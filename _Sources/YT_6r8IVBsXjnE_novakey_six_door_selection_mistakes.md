## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE DEBUNK THAT OVERTURNS ADVICE THE VAULT CURRENTLY CARRIES — seal-contour count

**His sixth error, and he flags it as the rarest but real:**

> ***«Некоторые покупатели уверены: если у двери три контура, значит, она лучше. Но количество контуров уплотнения не определяет качество двери. Можно поставить три дешёвых уплотнителя на слабую конструкцию, и это не даст никакой реальной пользы. Контуры важны, но они лишь часть общей системы, а не какой-то важный критерий выбора.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ The vault's Entrance Doors page currently carries the OPPOSITE from Gusev — "at least two contours, and the more contours the fewer smells from the stairwell" — as buying advice.** **Two independent manufacturers now contradict it: Novakey calls contour-counting a buyer error outright, and [[_Sources/YT_nPSB41PtdMA_bastion_ten_criteria_how_to_check_a_door|Бастион]] says three finger-thick contours still leak if the leaf geometry is wrong.**
>
> **→ The correction is not "contours don't matter" but "contours are downstream of geometry": a contour only seals if the leaf seats against it, so counting them tells you nothing about the door that carries them.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Steel thickness — the fourth figure, and they line up by price position

> ***«Оптимальная толщина стали надёжной, хорошей двери должна быть не менее 2 мм.»***

**Set against the vault's other sources, the pattern is the finding:**

| Source | Stated minimum | What they sell |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Gusev (2025-11-28) | **1.2 mm adequate** | the cheapest doors of the four |
| Gusev (2024-10-22) | **"at least 1.5 mm"** | *same seller, earlier* |
| **Novakey (this)** | **not less than 2 mm** | a full ladder incl. premium tiers |
| Бастион (2024-03-10) | **under 3 mm "doesn't qualify"** | premium doors |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE RECOMMENDED MINIMUM TRACKS WHAT THE SOURCE STOCKS, monotonically, across four data points.** **That does not tell you which figure is right, and it must not be read as "they are all just talking their book" — Бастион's 3 mm comes with a knife-cut test and Novakey's 2 mm with a deformation mechanism.** **But it does mean no single vendor's minimum can be taken as the market's answer, and it is the most honest thing the vault can say about this dispute.**

**His mechanism for the 2 mm figure:** thin metal deforms from a slammed door, bends if something is caught under the threshold, **loses geometry and then stops working correctly specifically in the lock zone.** ⚠️ **Same causal chain as Бастион — thickness → geometry → lock function — reached independently.**

**Required structure:** vertical AND horizontal rigidity ribs, a reinforced lock pocket, and lock-zone armouring. ⚠️ **Independently corroborates Бастион §3 on vertical-only ribs being the defect.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Two in-shop tests, and they need no tools

- **⚠️⚠️ *«Надёжная дверь, она тяжёлая, жёсткая и плотно закрывается»*** — weight and rigidity are the proxy you can feel.
- **⚠️⚠️ The failure tell: half-open a weak door and *«конструкция болтается как селёдка»*, which he says reveals the total absence of internal reinforcement.**
- **Ask for the cutaway display (стенд-разрез)** — he notes they show real door sections in their showrooms, so the internal structure is visible rather than described.

> **→ ⚠️ The vault's existing entrance-door material is almost entirely about installation and price; it had NO shop-floor evaluation procedure at all. These two tests plus Бастион's density and knife-cut thresholds now give one.** ⚠️ **The cutaway-stand point is also a sales device — but asking for one is free, and a seller who has no section to show is itself information.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Locks — a service-life number the vault did not have

> ***«Около 80% дверей комплектуются самыми дешёвыми замками и цилиндрами, которые вскрываются за секунды и имеют минимальный ресурс.»***

| | Cycles |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cheap locks | **10,000–50,000** |
| Quality locks | **⚠️⚠️ minimum 250,000–300,000** |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A spec you can demand in writing and check against a datasheet — roughly a 6–20× spread.** **The vault had lock QUALITY discussed only through brand names and class labels; this is the first quantity attached to it.**

- **Locks should be class 4 minimum.** ⚠️ **Third independent source in this batch using a 4-class scale with 4 at the top — see the class-scale conflict noted on the Entrance Doors page.**
- Cheap-hardware failure modes named: no protection against drilling, snapping or knocking through; low material hardness; **and jamming at the worst moment.**
- **⚠️⚠️ A LIFECYCLE TRAP that is new to the vault: cheap doors often fit locks by MORTISING (врезным способом), which worsens the structure's rigidity AND makes later replacement harder — he says finding an equivalent may simply be impossible.** **→ A lock is a wear part; a door that cannot accept a replacement lock has a service life set by its lock.**

## ⚠️ 5. Filling — and Gusev is the outlier

> **The named error: a door filled with пенопласт or loose mineral wool, where *«если приоткрывать такую дверь, её вес сразу всё выдаёт»*.**

**Correct filling per this source: MULTILAYER — medium-to-high density basalt slabs, acoustic materials, and vibration-damping layers that cut sound transmission through the metal itself.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ This is the second manufacturer to name foam filling as the tell of a cheap door, against [[_Sources/YT_YIJ332ibiyM_gusev_entrance_door_four_criteria|Gusev's]] claim that пенополистирол is probably the best material for soundproofing.** **Two builders against one retailer, and the retailer is the one selling the cheapest doors, where foam is the cheapest filling. Treated as contradicted rather than open.** ⚠️ **Corroborates Бастион's "layers of differing density" point independently, though only Бастион gives the >40 kg/m³ number.**

## ⚠️ 6. Two errors about what price and appearance tell you

- **Error 1 — cheapest is not cheapest:** low price means thin steel, cheap hardware and token filling; such a door *«выполняет только одну функцию: открыть и закрыть»*, and **repairing or drilling out a jammed lock costs more than the gap between a budget and a good door.** ⚠️ **A total-cost-of-ownership argument from someone selling the expensive option — flagged accordingly, but the jammed-lock scenario matches Бастион's settlement mechanism and his own opening scenario.**
- **⚠️ Error 3 — finish does not indicate quality**: film, veneer and enamel can be identical on a cheap and an expensive door, and the coating is a minimal share of the price. **His illustrative pair: two doors that look alike at 200,000 and 70,000 RUB.** ⚠️⚠️ **Region Russia, date bounded by publication 2026-01-04 (`price_date_basis: upper_bound_from_publication`). These are an illustration of spread, not quotes for a product, and per the Entrance Doors page's existing treatment of this manufacturer's RUB figures they are NOT transferred as a market benchmark for this Belarusian project.**
