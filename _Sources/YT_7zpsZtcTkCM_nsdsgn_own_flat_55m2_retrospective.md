# Source Note — NSDSGN, the designer's own 55 m² flat, start to finish

**57 minutes, manual Russian caption track.** A full-cycle account of his own renovation, filmed across the build.

**⚠️ Self-selection bias applies as it does to any practitioner's own object — but it is weaker here than usual, because the source's headline content is a disclosed failure that reflects badly on him.** Prices: **June 2023**, at **75.4770 RUB/USD** (trailing 6 months to 2023-06-14).

## ⚠️ THE HEADLINE DISCLOSED FAILURE — he regrets not hiring a прораб on his own 55 m² flat

**The single most consequential item in Round 3, and it lands directly on this project's own delivery model.**

> **«Главной проблемой моей стройки было то, что у меня работали самые крутые специалисты в своих сферах, но не было человека, который бы их координировал. По сути координацию на себя взял я, а это очень неправильно. Я очень пожалел о том, что не нанял прораба. Одно дело — обсудить примыкание плитки к инженерной доске, а другое дело — скоординировать работу нескольких специалистов.»**

**Why this matters more than an ordinary opinion:**

- **He had the best individual specialist in each trade — and still concludes the COORDINATION role is a separate job he should have bought.**
- **He is an interior designer with 15+ years of practice, i.e. the person best placed to self-coordinate, and he reports that it failed him.**
- **⚠️ And it recalibrates this channel's own threshold. This channel's contractor puts the генподряд threshold at ~300 m² or a two-storey flat. This is 55 m². → The coordination requirement is not primarily AREA-driven; it is driven by the NUMBER OF INDEPENDENT SPECIALISTS who must be sequenced.** A small flat built by many separate best-in-trade specialists can need coordination more than a large flat built by one crew.
- **This vault's own project is self-managed. This is a practitioner reporting, against his own interest, that self-coordination on a small flat was his main problem.** Recorded on [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Cost_Categories_and_Delivery_Models|Cost Categories and Delivery Models]] as evidence, not as an argument to change the plan.

## ⚠️ Concealed-mount doors — a scheduling trap, a two-stage install, and a 200-rouble save

- **⚠️ Terminology flagged first: «двери скрытого монтажа — не путайте со скрытыми дверями, это немного другое, хотя принцип очень похож».**
- **⚠️ THE SCHEDULING TRAP: «коробки от ваших дверей нужны нам на черновой стадии, когда ещё только возводятся стены, и очень часто из-за этого зависает стройка».** **→ Door procurement GATES the rough stage.** He is candid: «мы очень сильно с ними намучились».
- **The two-stage installation, precisely: (1) the builders prepare the openings to the door supplier's ТЗ — height accounting for how much the FINISHED FLOOR will rise, and width; (2) the door company's own fitter installs the concealed frames. The frame goes in TOGETHER WITH the leaf, then the leaf is REMOVED so the builders do not destroy it while carrying materials through, and it waits packed in its box. Hinges and handles are cut in at that time.**
- **Finishing the junction: the outer face is closed with a piece of plasterboard which the painters then fill. On the reverse the plasterboard enters the frame's ПАЗ, producing a clean internal corner that is simply painted. In the bathroom the same ПАЗ is entered with TILE, giving a porcelain-stoneware corner.**
- **⚠️ AND THE CHEAPEST USEFUL TIP IN THE ROUND: buy a length of pipe insulation (~200 RUB, ≈$3), split it along its seam, and slip it over the door frame. «200 рублей — вы покупаете чехол на вашу коробку, и дверь переживёт всю стройку.» He says he saw it on a very expensive site with good builders.**

## ⚠️ Where he lost his own money — the shadow skirting

**Recorded under this source because it is his own object, and it is the third independent instance on this channel of the same detail defeating a crew.**

He was told the crew had done such skirtings a hundred times — «это вообще полная ерунда, мы тебе за копейки сделаем за пять дней» — **and it ended in «потерянное колоссальное время, финансовые затраты». To their credit they returned all the money; he found other contractors who did it properly.** He also says of the technique generally: **«нигде ни у кого нет точной информации, как подойти к теневым плинтусам»** — so the shortage is documentary as well as practical.

**Cross-reference: this channel's contractor names теневой профиль as the detail crews claim to know and have never built, and its own regrets episode names shadow skirtings as the case where the designer himself was the victim. Three instances, one of them costing the designer money.**

## Construction detail

- **Waterproof plasterboard in the wet room, deliberately THINNER — 12.5 mm rather than 15 mm — chosen водостойкий «чтобы он не набирал воду и сохранял свои свойства». Total wall build-up came to 150 mm.**
- **Water inlet reworked: previously two separate inlets, relocated so the box can be fully closed with no visible valves; shutoff by two levers.**
- **⚠️ Countertop levelling is material-dependent: artificial stone can be propped and adjusted («можно как-то подпереть»), whereas with QUARTZ AGGLOMERATE that is impossible because it is very hard — so it must be set perfectly from the start.** A real tolerance consequence of a material choice.
- **The countertop returns inward so a coffee machine and clutter hide behind a door.** Upper facades are **an analogue of Fenix — deeply matt.**
- **⚠️ A material constraint that connects two prior findings: the skirtings are MDF, not plastic, and «перфорация для холодильника, чтобы шла конвекция, сделана вот такая аккуратненькая, эстетичная — в обычном ДСП такого не сделать, это можно сделать только в крашеном МДФ».**
  **→ The fridge convection perforation this channel establishes as mandatory can only be executed neatly in painted MDF. A functional requirement dictating a skirting material.**
- **Three holes in the countertop: mixer, dispenser, and the waste-disposal button.**
- **The fabricators praised his plasterboard builders for perfect verticality — otherwise «здесь появилась бы какая-то морковка, какая-то тень». «Выбирайте хороших строителей.»** *(The same «морковка» failure word this channel uses for a flush windowsill against a wavy wall.)*
- **Marble strips: they took 1 cm marble tile and cut it into thin strips.**
- Doors 2.4 m high; **⚠️ a dated trend statement: «в этой квартире всё сделано из шлифованного металла, здесь ничего не блестит, здесь почти нет латунных оттенков, потому что это уже совсем не тренд».** **Recorded as dated (June 2023) rather than as a rule, and noted because this channel's 2025 site review uses natural brass profiles and brass inlays extensively — two years apart, so a trend claim rather than a conflict.**

## ⚠️ Two more disclosed errors

- **⚠️ The kitchen hood set flush to the wall — a fabricator error with a clean, generalisable rule.** «Мебельщики накосячили: они вытяжку сделали вплотную к стене.» **When he came to fit the backsplash he realised a groove would have to be cut. Resolution: they pulled the hood forward so the backsplash could pass behind it, and it now protrudes slightly — «небольшой косячок, она немножко торчит вперёд».**
  **THE RULE HE DRAWS: «когда делаете вытяжку встроенную, помните: учитывайте, что на стене у вас не плоскость стены, а именно ФАРТУК.» → Dimension a built-in hood to the FINISHED backsplash face, not to the bare wall.**
- **⚠️ He over-ordered the bathroom tile: «дизайнеры тоже ошибаются — я, к сожалению, посчитал плитки в ванную больше, чем нужно было, причём больше так нормально. Осталась плитка, не знаю куда её девать.»** It was salvaged as the kitchen backsplash, which happened to suit the countertop and tie the flat together — **but he presents it as a genuine over-order rather than a plan.**

## ⚠️ The bath raised by the drain run — with a serviceability answer

- **The bath's waste is CENTRAL while the soil stack is in the opposite corner of the bathroom.** The water must run across, then along the wall, then bend, then into the stack — **a long run, and to achieve the correct fall they had to raise the bath considerably. «К сожалению, здесь по-другому было сделать никак.»**
- **⚠️ The mitigation they designed in: a concealed hatch that lifts out ENTIRELY, so «даже человек небольшого роста может легко пролезть под ванну и всё там починить».**
- **→ Usable rule: a centrally-draining bath far from the stack forces bath height, and the correct response is to design a full-size access hatch rather than to fight the fall.**

## Sequencing and logistics

- **Final measurements were taken by the built-in furniture company AFTER the rough stage, and «нам пришлось немножко подкорректировать розетки, чтобы они идеально подошли под уже проработанную [мебель]».** *(Corroborates this channel's appliances-and-furniture-first rule and its measure-after-the-stage rule from two other sources.)*
- **⚠️ A logistics anecdote that belongs with this channel's other two: a round table's pedestal weighing ~80 kg, carried up to the fifth floor with no lift, single-handed — «я чуть спину не сорвал».** Third instance on this channel of a large item's delivery being the unplanned problem, after the crane-and-removed-window glass panel and the washing machine that would not pass a 60 cm door leaf.
- **A small oven chosen deliberately: «все, кто видит, начинают смеяться, но я больше чем одним подносом в готовке не пользуюсь» — and being small it also carries a steamer function.** A worked example of specifying appliances to actual use rather than to convention.
