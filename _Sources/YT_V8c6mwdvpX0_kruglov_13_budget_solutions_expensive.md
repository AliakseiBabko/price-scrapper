## Genuinely New Content

**⚠️ Material-imitation as a deliberate, endorsed cost-saving choice —
new topic, distinct from this store's existing decorative-plaster/
faux-marble content**: the source explicitly endorses choosing
porcelain tile with a marble-look print instead of real marble, and
quality laminate or quartz-vinyl instead of engineered wood board —
framed as a legitimate design choice most designers can't visually
distinguish from the "real" material, not a compromise to be ashamed
of (tactile difference acknowledged, visual difference described as
negligible).

**⚠️ Floating-install herringbone pattern in laminate or quartz-vinyl —
new budget technique, extending this page's existing herringbone
content (previously glued engineered-wood only)**: many laminate and
quartz-vinyl product lines are now sold specifically for herringbone
("ёлочка") patterns and can be installed by the ordinary floating
method (on underlayment, no adhesive) rather than requiring the
glued-down installation a real engineered-wood herringbone floor needs
— a real budget path to the herringbone look without the material/
labor premium this page's existing content documents for engineered
wood.

**⚠️ Quartz-vinyl as a window-reveal material, with a specific crack-
avoidance mechanism — extends this round's own windowsill-only quartz-
vinyl finding (`xb5pUpTVIJU`) to reveals**: applying quartz-vinyl
flooring material to the reveals themselves (not just the windowsill)
is described as looking notably more expensive than a plastered/painted
reveal, fully moisture-resistant, and — the specific new mechanism —
**the joint between the plastic window-frame profile and the quartz-
vinyl reveal is sealed with sealant rather than a rigid material
junction**, so it never develops the crack that can form at a painted-
reveal-to-frame joint (already documented on this page as a real,
unpredictable risk for painted reveals).

**⚠️ Painting a plastic window frame — new concrete price point**:
repainting an inexpensive white plastic window frame (e.g. black, for
style match) costs roughly **2,000 RUB per running meter (≈$30/m,
trailing-6-month USD/RUB mean before the 2026-03-08 upload date,
79.50 RUB/USD)**
— named as meaningfully cheaper than replacing the window units
entirely, and stated to transform the room's visual impression well
beyond that modest cost, especially at a distance where the plastic
frame material itself stops reading as "plastic."

**⚠️ Budget DIY decorative plaster from ordinary starter putty — a
concrete technique extending this store's existing decorative-plaster-
as-marble-substitute concept with an actual how-to and durability
tradeoff**: rather than a professional decorative-plaster compound,
apply the same decorative technique (sponge, trowel, brush, or circular
motion, applied and then smoothed once it begins to set) using **plain
starter putty** over a properly primed and plastered wall. **Two
variants**: (1) applying decorative texture directly over existing
plaster (recommended only for low-traffic zones — balconies, storage,
walk-in closets); (2) the source's actual recommendation — plaster,
prime, apply 1-2 coats of starter putty with inter-coat sanding, prime
again, then apply the same putty in a decorative texture. **Explicit
durability tradeoff, stated honestly**: a genuine professional
decorative-plaster compound is meaningfully more wear-resistant, more
repairable, and harder to chip than this putty technique — but this
budget method costs a small fraction of the professional compound while
still being visually indistinguishable to anyone not specifically
trained to spot the difference.

**⚠️ Vintage vs. antique furniture, a concrete age-based distinction, plus
vintage rugs as an accent category**: defines **vintage** as roughly
20-50 years old (a workable, accessibly-sourced accent category, e.g.
via Avito) versus **antique** (50+ years) — recommends vintage over
antique specifically because antiques carry more restoration/
authentication risk the source advises against experimenting with.
Vintage mirrors, dressers, and nightstands (restored or as-is) plus
**vintage rugs** are named as accessible ways to add character — but
explicitly only in moderation, since overdone vintage decor is called
just as easy to ruin a room with as underdone.
