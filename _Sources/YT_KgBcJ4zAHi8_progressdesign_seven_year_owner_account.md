## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE FINDING THIS PROJECT NEEDS — the unit was DISMANTLED for a renovation and REFITTED

**Room built 2018 for two boys (then 7 and 3). In 2021 the household renovated: new floors, wallpaper, doors.**

> ***«Мы позвонили в Прогресс-Дизайн, попросили ребят, они приехали, нашу мебель РАЗОБРАЛИ, мы сделали ремонт, и ребята приехали, нам её обратно вернули. Стены поменялись, пол поменялся, а МЕБЕЛЬ ОСТАЛАСЬ. Та же — прекрасно перенесла сборку, разборку, помимо ежедневной эксплуатации. Никаких поломок, дефектов нет абсолютно.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS BEARS DIRECTLY ON THIS PROJECT'S PHASE-1/PHASE-2 DECISION, AND IT CUTS BOTH WAYS — which is why it is worth having.**
>
> [[00_Master/Bedroom_Design_Principles|Bedroom Design Principles]] records the directive to **install the wall bed during the current build and activate it in ~3–4 years**, on the future-flexible-planning principle. **The strongest argument for that is that the cavity, fixings and wiring must be built before finishes close over them.** **This household demonstrates the other half of the picture: the UNIT ITSELF is removable and refittable by the maker, around a full renovation, without damage.**
>
> **→ So the two halves of the decision separate cleanly, and should be recorded separately**: the **wall preparation, anchor points and wiring** genuinely are a rough-stage commitment; **the joinery and mechanism are not** — they can come out and go back. ⚠️ **That does not overturn the install-now directive** (which the owner has taken, and which still buys a fitted result and avoids re-opening finishes). **It does mean the WARRANTY problem recorded from [[_Sources/YT_u_gIgDqRdYw_progressdesign_wallbed_buyer_walkthrough|u_gIgDqRdYw]] — a 3-year mechanism warranty expiring before Phase 2 activation — has an option attached to it that was not previously visible: the unit does not have to be BUILT at the same time as the cavity it sits in.**
>
> ⚠️ **One maker, one household, and the dismantle was done by the maker's own fitters. Not a general claim that any wall bed survives this.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Seven years of genuinely daily cycling, and the beds were NOT spared

**The point is made explicitly against the obvious objection:**

> ***«Кровати складываются и раскладываются в ЕЖЕДНЕВНОМ порядке. То есть это не то, что вот стоит там годами, пыль собирает. Нет, всё очень функционально.»*** **Seven years, no breakdowns, no defects.**

> **→ ⚠️ Set beside the cycle ratings: at twice daily, seven years is ~5,000 cycles — well inside the 20,000–30,000 the makers claim, so this CONFIRMS nothing about the rated life.** **What it does evidence is the absence of the failure the vault actually documents — [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|the ДСП cracking at the mounting plate]], which ФурнитураДВ observed correlating with daily use across three installations.** **This is a seven-year daily-use case that did NOT crack. Recorded on the "held up" side of that open question, with the vendor-selection caveat attached.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. A 7-year-old operated it from day one — and the reason is the WIDTH

> ***«У меня ребёнок с 7 лет, старший, он с самого начала её подымал спокойно, потому что КРОВАТЬ РАЗМЕРОМ 90 — ОНА ЛЁГКАЯ, она очень легко закрывается.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS SHARPENS THE OPERATING-EFFORT FINDING INTO SOMETHING SPECIFIABLE.** [[00_Master/Bedroom_Wall_Bed_Mechanisms|The mechanisms page]] records effort as varying (a maker saying a frail woman manages; an owner who squats to do it alone) and resolves it as *a function of how well the strut is matched*. **This adds the other variable, and it is the one you choose on a drawing: BED WIDTH.** A 90 cm single is light enough for a child; the owner who needed a squat was working a double. **→ For Phase 2, the adults' bed is a double, so this case does NOT transfer to it — but it does mean a child's 90 cm wall bed is a different proposition from an adult's, and the two should not be reasoned about together.**

## ⚠️ 4. Two straps, and a user's reason for them

> **The bed is closed with bedding inside: *«есть два ремешка, в которых подушечка, одеяло, ну и любимые подушки-игрушки»*, and the stated benefit is that the boys need not make the bed neatly — *«нам не надо красиво застилать пледом»*.**

> **→ ⚠️ ON THE OPEN FOUR-WAY RETENTION QUESTION this is a USER on the FOR-straps side, seven years in, with no reported jamming.** ⚠️⚠️ **But it is the same vendor as the FOR-straps maker already recorded, so it is one system evidenced twice, not two systems agreeing.** **Its real contribution is the reason: straps are what let a made-up bed be closed by a child without tidiness — a usability argument, where the against-straps camp's argument is a failure-mode argument. They are not actually answering the same question.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. A FOURTH arrival at the sofa-bed rejection, and a new clearance constraint

**On why the adults' room got a wall bed rather than a sofa bed:**

> ***«Хочется спать на комфортном матрасе, НЕ НА ДИВАНЕ, какими бы ортопедическими они там ни назывались.»***

> **→ ⚠️ A fourth arrival at what [[14_Furniture/analysis/Seating_Selection|Seating & Bed Selection]] holds from Шеврина, Краснов and Качанова's households — and note the scare-quoting of *«ортопедический»*, which is the same suspicion of the label that the mattress material records.**

**⚠️⚠️ AND A GENUINELY NEW CONSTRAINT, which is the most transferable thing in the video.** Their adults' bed is **2 m × 1.6 m**, and when it is down the room is so tight that:

> ***«Очень мало места между кроватью и стеной… нами было принято решение, что мы сделаем на этой стене телевизор ТОЛЬКО С ПОМОЩЬЮ ПРОЕКТОРА»*** — so that getting out of bed, *«проходя, не задевала бы спиной телевизор»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A WALL-MOUNTED TV ON THE FACING WALL AND A DOWN WALL-BED COMPETE FOR THE SAME 50–70 cm.** **This vault already records an unresolved bed-clearance disagreement (50 vs 70 cm); this adds that in a wall-bed room the clearance is consumed by the bed only at night, so a PERMANENT projection into it — a TV, a radiator shelf, a projecting unit — is what actually fails.** **Their answer was a projector, which projects nothing into the room.**
>
> **⚠️⚠️ DIRECTLY LIVE FOR THIS PROJECT: Phase 2 puts a wall bed in the combined living/dining space, which is also where a television would naturally go.** **Flagged to [[04_Living_and_Dining_Room/analysis/Living_Bedroom_Zoning|Living-Bedroom Zoning]] as a conflict to resolve on the drawing, not on site.**

## ⚠️ 6. Why they rejected a bunk bed — a reason about the ROOM, not the bed

> **The room is the flat's *«лицо квартиры»* — a living room that must also be a nursery and a workspace. *«Двухъярусная кровать никак не могла стать посреди гостиной»*, and the supporting observation: *«дети не могут красиво содержать свои мебели в порядке»*.**

> **→ The argument is not that a bunk is worse furniture but that a bunk is PERMANENTLY VISIBLE, and a room doing double duty cannot afford a permanently visible children's object. That is the same logic this project applies in Phase 2 in the other direction — the adults' bed folds away so the shared room reads as a living room.**

## ⚠️ 7. Smaller details worth carrying

- **⚠️ The desk is against a WALL, not the window, deliberately** — *«мне очень хотелось закрывать зону окна»* — accepting artificial light for the work surface in order to keep the window zone free. ⚠️ **Against the ergonomic default this vault holds elsewhere (daylight from the side for a work surface); recorded as one household's trade-off, not as advice.**
- Each child has his own drawer at the desk for stationery and books, *«используется каждодневно»*; armrests double as toy storage.
- **A morning rule, stated as household discipline rather than product feature**: beds up and bedding stowed every morning, because the boys bring friends in after school — *«чтобы мебель всегда была так, как задумано изначально»*. ⚠️ **This is the same twice-daily discipline two independent households described in the Качанова group, now at a seven-year horizon.**
