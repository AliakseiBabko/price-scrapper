## Electrical — Rules / Heuristics

6. **⚠️ Low-ceiling wiring-layout rule: keep only lighting circuits on
   the ceiling, run switches/sockets along the floor/walls instead**,
   specifically on a secondary-market apartment with limited ceiling
   height, to minimize how much the finished ceiling needs to drop.
   Stated mechanism: if all electrical (not just lighting) ran on the
