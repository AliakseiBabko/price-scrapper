# Source Note - Сосновская фабрика: ⚠️⚠️ FIVE DESTRUCTIVE TESTS ON FOUR COUNTERTOPS — and Only One Can Be Repaired (YouTube npaV7FELR9k)

## Why this source matters

**[[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktop_Materials_Fabricator_Account|the CAPPUCCINO worktop account]] gave this vault a fabricator's material inventory — quartz, porcelain stoneware, HPL, and a health refusal on liquid acrylic.** **What it did not give is a head-to-head with the same insult applied to every material.** This does that, across the four materials a buyer in this segment actually chooses between.

**The four specimens, with the compositions he states:**

| Material | Composition as stated |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DEKTON** | ultra-dense surface — quartz stone, glass and porcelain |
| **Quartz agglomerate** (Caesarstone, Israel) | **94–97% natural quartz** + pigments + binders |
| **Compact laminate** (Smart, Italian) | homogeneous, non-porous — **65% paper, 30–35% cured resins** |
| **Acrylic stone** (Korean) | acrylic resin + **aluminium hydroxide** + mineral fillers + pigments |

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. YOU CANNOT CUT ON ANY OF THEM — and he says the showroom will tell you otherwise

Cutting beetroot and scoring with scissors on each:

> ***«Ни в коем случае не режем продукты питания без разделочной доски, ЧТО БЫ ВАМ НИ ГОВОРИЛИ ПРОДАВЦЫ В САЛОНЕ. На топовых столешницах в любом случае остаются микроповреждения.»***

- **Most resistant: DEKTON, then quartz.** Even so — *«микроповреждения остаются даже на DEKTON и на глянцевом»*.
- **⚠️ Quartz takes DEEP grooves from a knife** — *«от ножа остаются очень глубокие бороздки»*, visible on the Caesarstone specimen.
- **Compact laminate and acrylic scratch badly.** Acrylic is the softest of the four.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A DIRECT, TESTED CONTRADICTION OF A SALES CLAIM, FROM A SELLER.** **The vault can now record "scratch-proof" as false for every material in this price class**, including the two most expensive.

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. THE FINDING THAT SHOULD DECIDE A MATERIAL CHOICE: only acrylic can be repaired

He scratches all four, then attacks them with a polishing machine and assorted compounds:

> **Acrylic**: *«Царапин нету, я в шоке — всё убрал… на акриле идеально убираются царапины… смотрите, как новый стал.»* **It also polished the зелёнка stain out.**
> **Quartz**: *«Не убираю царапины.»* **DEKTON**: no. **Compact laminate**: light marks remain.
>
> ***«Реставрируется только акриловая столешница. Остальные материалы — если вы поцарапали, вам придётся жить с этими царапинами ДО КОНЦА ЭКСПЛУАТАЦИИ этого кухонного гарнитура.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS REFRAMES THE WHOLE COMPARISON. Acrylic loses every resistance test and wins the only one that is recoverable.** A quartz worktop is harder *and* permanent in its damage; an acrylic one marks easily *and* can be made new again. **Over a 10–20 year life that is a different trade from the one a hardness table implies.**
>
> ⚠️ **Read it against what the vault already holds**: **CAPPUCCINO refused LIQUID acrylic outright on a styrene-emission basis during cure, and refused 6 mm sheet acrylic as crack-prone.** **→ These are not in conflict: that was a health and thickness objection to particular acrylic products; this is a repairability property of the cured material.** **Both belong on the page, and the buyer's question becomes which acrylic, not whether acrylic.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Heat: none of the four was damaged

A frying pan heated **red-hot** on the hob, placed directly on each:

> *«Температуры наши столешницы не боятся… на акриле — ничего не сплавилось, кипящая сковородка, пожалуйста.»* **All four passed, acrylic included.**

> **→ ⚠️ RECORDED AS A SINGLE-TRIAL RESULT, AND WORTH TREATING CAUTIOUSLY DESPITE BEING A PASS.** One brief contact is not the same as a hot pan left standing, and the vault already records from CAPPUCCINO that **a flush-mounted hob needs a thermal expansion gap and a sealant that degrades** — so heat at the hob is a JOINT problem even where the slab itself is indifferent.

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Staining: natural dyes are harmless, зелёнка and iodine are terminal

Eight to nine hours of beetroot, tea, coffee, blackcurrant, cherry, soy sauce, **iodine and зелёнка** (brilliant green):

- **Every natural dye washed off all four without trouble** — *«всё, что в быту мы используем, вообще без проблем отмывается»*.
- **⚠️⚠️ Зелёнка and iodine: *«это смерть абсолютная для столешницы»*.** They penetrate the structure. He scrubbed five to ten minutes **including with imported acetone** and failed.
  - **Compact laminate** — went green, would not come off. **Caesarstone quartz** — went green. **DEKTON** — almost cleaned, a slight speck remained on the matt finish. **Acrylic** — came out, but **only by polishing** (§2).

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIRD INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL IN THIS VAULT AT THE SAME HOUSEHOLD HAZARD.** [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Facade_Materials_Fabricator_Account|The CAPPUCCINO MDF head-to-head]] found **зелёнка was the one stain neither FRONT survived**. **This confirms it on four premium COUNTERTOPS.** **→ It is not a material-selection criterion, because nothing resists it. It is a household-practice finding: keep brilliant green and iodine off the worktop, and wipe any spill at once.**

## ⚠️ 5. Impact: all survive normal life; one splits under abuse

Meat tenderised with a mallet directly on each surface **and on the exposed edge** — all fine. **Quartz and DEKTON edges excellent; compact laminate dents but does not chip; acrylic unaffected.**

Then a **sledgehammer (he says ~6–7 kg) dropped from 1 m** — all survived. **From 2 m, the DEKTON split.**

- **⚠️ His own caveat, which is the honest part: the DEKTON specimen was thin** — *«он был тоненький»*. **→ The split is a thickness result, not a material ranking**, and it is recorded that way.
- **His verdict: quartz has the highest impact strength of the four.**

## ⚠️ 6. Prices — ratios only

⚠️ **RUB, Russia, ~2020. Rule 2 blocks every absolute figure and none is converted.** **The structural relationships are the transferable part:**

- **DEKTON is by far the most expensive — roughly 2.5–3× a quartz sheet** of comparable size, at 20 mm.
- **Quartz sits in the middle**, at 22 mm.
- **Compact laminate and acrylic are the cheapest and roughly equal to each other**, both quoted at **12 mm** — ⚠️ **half the thickness of the quartz and DEKTON slabs, which is why the thin-worktop look and the budget option coincide.**

> ⚠️ **That last point connects to something the vault already holds from this channel's neighbours: the ultra-thin worktop is fashionable AND cheap in material terms**, so a thin slab is not by itself evidence of a premium specification.
