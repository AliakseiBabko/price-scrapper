- **⚠️ False-wall-continuity technique for reveal preservation**: when two separate false walls (each concealing different services — here, heating risers and a breather-unit niche) don't share a common plane, add a connecting filler segment between them **specifically so a nearby window/door reveal doesn't have to be over-narrowed later** — extending one false wall's plane to meet the other avoids a light-blocking, awkwardly-narrowed reveal that would otherwise be needed to reconcile the mismatch.
