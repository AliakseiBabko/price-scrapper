## Doors / Trim — Durable Facts & Rules

- **Interior door material/quality tiers, cheapest to most expensive (speaker's own retail framework)**:
  1. **Cheapest MDF-film doors** (~10,000 RUB): cheap MDF/particleboard frame ("LВ брус"), cardboard-filled core, wrapped in cheap Chinese film (sometimes even paper, not film). Lowest durability, not repairable if scratched/damaged — must be replaced entirely.
  2. **"Eco-veneer" (экошпон) doors** (~15,000–20,000 RUB): better pine-massif frame, MDF panel inserts, wrapped in polypropylene film branded as "eco-veneer" — speaker explicitly notes this branding has **no actual connection to real wood veneer or "eco" materials**, a naming/marketing distinction worth knowing when comparison-shopping. Not repairable if damaged.
  3. **Solid-wood ("массив") doors** (from ~30,000 RUB): genuine solid pine frame and panel inserts, no synthetic cladding materials, individually painted/finished. **Repairable** — scratches/damage can be professionally restored for ~5,000 RUB, vs. the lower tiers which must be replaced outright if damaged. Speaker's own stock (pine/alder/oak solid-wood doors) averages ~25,000 RUB/set.
  4. **Concealed/hidden ("скрытые") doors with aluminum edge trim** — two sub-tiers: (a) cheap version, same cheap-pine-frame + cardboard core as tier 1, with a thin/cheap Chinese aluminum edge trim, from ~12,000 RUB — **explicitly warned against** ("никогда не покупайте такие двери, пожалеете"); (b) full-aluminum-frame version, won't warp, expanded-polystyrene infill for high sound/thermal insulation, from ~50,000 RUB — described as durable and field-repairable (scratches on the aluminum edge can be touch-up-painted/polished, similar result to new).
- **Repairability is the key quality differentiator across all these tiers**, not just up-front price: cheap MDF/film doors and cheap-aluminum-edge concealed doors cannot be meaningfully repaired if scratched/damaged (must replace the whole leaf); solid-wood and quality-aluminum-edge concealed doors can both be restored for a modest fee (~5,000 RUB cited for solid wood). `confirmed`, a genuinely useful cross-cutting purchasing heuristic, complementary to (not duplicating) this store's existing enamel-vs-film repairability fact (see `yt_WMc_AjUqQ-4`, same batch) — this source is about the door's structural material, the other is about its surface coating.
- **"Door set" (комплект) composition, this retailer's own definition**: leaf (полотно) + casing (наличники) + frame (коробка) as the base set; hinges/handle/lock ("фурнитура") priced/sold separately as an add-on. **A quality hardware set (hinges, handle, lock) costs ~5,000–7,000 RUB** and is stated to last "10 years" — speaker's explicit warning not to cheap out on hardware specifically, since low-priced hardware is described as unreliable by default. `confirmed`, speaker's stated claim; new numeric anchor for hardware-specifically (this store's existing door-cost figures were mostly whole-set totals).
