## Design Concept / Room-Zoning — Durable Facts & Rules

- **Two-sided mirrored static partition**: a static partition covering
  ~2/3 of a room's width (leaving a walk-through gap) with a mirror
  mounted on *both* faces — one mirror visually enlarges the kitchen-side
  zone, the other visually enlarges the room-side zone, compounding to
  make the whole apartment read as roughly double its actual size. A
  variant/extension of the "mirror on a partition" idea already recorded
  elsewhere in this guide, specifically calling out the two-sided
  application as a distinct technique.
- **Arched double swing-leaf partition with curtain layering**: fixed
  side sections plus a center pair of hinged leaves opening like a formal
  double-door ("парадный ход"); adding a curtain rod above the opening
  lets the opening itself be curtained off for full closure/light-
  blocking on top of the leaves. Distinct from the plain swing-partition
  entry already implied in this guide's existing content — the specific
  combination (arched double-leaf + curtain layer) is new.
- **"Book"/rotary-fold partition mechanism**: instead of a single door
  leaf that swings open and consumes a large floor-space radius (blocked
  if furniture — a wardrobe, a sofa — sits within that radius), a
  book-fold ("книжка"/"рото") door folds in half into the wall opening,
  needing far less swing clearance; can be built from up to 3 hinged
  leaves per side (like a small accordion) for a wider opening while
  still folding flat. Framed explicitly as a small-apartment space-saving
  alternative to a standard hinged partition door — a genuinely distinct
  mechanism type from this guide's existing sliding/static/swing/curtain/
  shelving/rope/MDF-panel/screen/furniture-divider list.
