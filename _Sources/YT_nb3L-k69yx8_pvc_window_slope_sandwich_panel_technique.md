## Walls / Ceilings (PVC Window Slopes)

- **Sandwich-panel slope system, full install sequence**: level/true-up
  the rough opening perimeter with guide strips (thin wood batten, or —
  as shown — the same metal profile used for drywall framing); mount a
  connector profile onto the guide strips only if profile-style seams are
  planned (vs. simple adhesive corner trim for a non-profile finish);
  fix the sandwich panel (plastic-foam-plastic, **~8mm thick** standard)
  to the guide strips.
- **Corner/trim framing technique**: cut trim profile to the opening size
  plus a 30–40mm working margin; cut a matching plain-edge plastic strip
  from angle stock. Fit the top piece first — temporarily tack it, mark
  the cut line by transferring a straightedge held against the panel face
  onto the profile, remove it, then notch it with a plastic hacksaw: both
  the marked cut *and* part of the corner-lock tab are removed, cut at a
  slight inward angle specifically so the adjoining profile's own lock tab
  doesn't collide with it at the corner.
- **Thermal-expansion gap rule**: leave a deliberate **~3mm gap** at each
  slope corner/joint to accommodate the plastic's thermal linear
  expansion — achieved practically by cutting ~1.5mm short of the marked
  line on each side, so the two sides sum to the target 3mm gap, evened
  left/right after fitting. Applies to vertical trim elements too, not
  just the top.
- **Side-profile placement rule**: mount the plastic corner strip against
  the corner trim itself (not directly against the sandwich panel), since
  that's the actual surface the corner piece must seat flush to.
- **Wallpaper-protection technique while scoring/cutting trim in place**:
  slide the same plain plastic strip used for corner marking underneath
  the corner trim as a backing/protective layer before scoring it with a
  hacksaw, specifically to avoid damaging the adjacent wallpaper — cut a
  ~50cm working section at a time, snap it, then move to the next section.
- **Finish step**: seal all fitted trim edges/joints with sealant before
  handover.
