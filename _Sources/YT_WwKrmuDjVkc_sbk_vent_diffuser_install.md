## Mistakes / Warnings / Engineering — Vent Diffuser-to-Duct-Elbow Connection, a Real Named Fitting Mismatch

- **⚠️ Named root cause of a recurring installer failure**: a round diffuser grille ("диффузор") and the duct elbow ("колено") it needs to connect to are manufactured at the **same diameter**, so the diffuser physically cannot be inserted directly into the elbow — installers who don't realize this simply give up ("chief, it's rattling, can't be fixed") and leave the ceiling installer to hand the unresolved problem to the client, who then typically can't solve it either and ends up calling a different company (this speaker's own) to fix a job that "would take five minutes" for a competent installer.
- **⚠️ Fix: a short connecting duct-pipe segment, cut to length, joins the two same-diameter parts**: since the diffuser and elbow can't connect directly, a short piece of duct pipe (voздуховод) is used as a coupling — one end onto the elbow, the other onto the diffuser. Standard duct pipe is sold in a minimum length of 500mm, too long to use as-is without the diffuser protruding below the ceiling plane.
- **⚠️ Precise trimming required on two parts, not just the pipe, driven by the diffuser's flange depth**: the diffuser has a flange that must sit at its full depth inside the ceiling cavity for a flush fit — this requires cutting down **both** the elbow (trim part of its own flange/collar) **and** the duct-pipe coupling segment to the correct length, not just shortening the pipe alone. Minor trimming imprecision at this stage is acceptable since final adjustment happens once the diffuser is fitted at the ceiling.
- **Height adjustment and sealing at final fit**: once assembled, the diffuser's height is fine-tuned (pulled slightly up or pushed slightly down) until it sits flush in the ceiling plane, then fixed permanently in that position with sealant (герметик) — a simple, low-cost finishing step that closes out the installation with no visible gap.
