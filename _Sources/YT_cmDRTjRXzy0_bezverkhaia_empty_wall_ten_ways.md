## Design Concept — her ten solutions, deliberately ordered large-gesture first

**⚠️ The ordering is itself the argument: "я начинаю с крупных решений. Они обычно намного более выигрышные."** She contrasts them against small additions — "а давайте мы на стену наклеим…, а давайте родовое дерево повесим" — which she dismisses.

1. **⚠️ Colour-block, and her definition is stricter than "paint an accent wall."** Not merely painting one wall a saturated colour, **but treating a whole zone as one block of a shade** — the wall, part of the adjacent wall, part of the ceiling, **plus the sofa and chest of drawers standing there**, all in tones of the same colour, with internal variation (different olive tones on rug, sofa, wall and picture). **The effect she is after: the eye reads it as one large, voluminous, internally interesting accent rather than a patchwork.** **⚠️ It need not be bright — dark grey or dark chocolate qualifies; what matters is that it is a large block of a different shade.**
2. **A contrasting material on the wall.** Bedroom: wallpaper or another decorative material behind the headboard. Living room: an accent material on the most-seen wall, behind or opposite the sofa, **"который бы выделялся, но дружил со всеми остальными."** Options named: patterned, floral or geometric wallpaper; **wallpaper with texture only**; decorative plaster (sand or clay texture for Japandi); **large-format porcelain, e.g. travertine-effect**; and **a DIY version — relief plaster painted over — offered as a legitimate budget route.**
3. **⚠️ Texture alone, at the same colour — the most restrained option and a useful one.** All walls in one shade, but one accent area **the same colour and textured**, achieved with relief paint or decorative plaster then painted in the same colour as the rest. **"Игра фактур" that works in Japandi, Scandi and any contemporary non-classical interior — understated and not showy.**
4. **⚠️ Wall painting (роспись) — and she works to rehabilitate it.** The association is kindergarten murals or a cheap Italian restaurant's crooked Milan. **The real options: contemporary fresco, geometric painting, restrained floral painting, narrative scenes.** Non-trivial and unique.
5. **Mouldings on one wall only.** Works in modern classic and eclectic. **Variations worth keeping: mouldings on one wall rather than all; mirrors set inside the mouldings; the mouldings themselves picked out lighter or darker; or mostly plain mouldings with one area given more ornate moulded elements.** She cites her Kutuzovsky project.
6. **Plants.** Wall-fixed pots and trailing plants, live or artificial; a green wall (фитостена); decorative arrangements of wall planters. **⚠️ She defends good artificial plants explicitly** — "у меня ничего дома не выживает" — as looking fine if well made. Her rationale for plants generally is biophilic: more nature indoors, better mood.
7. **⚠️ "Gallery art" — one large piece, and the constraint attached is the important half.** Not original Picassos, and **explicitly not marketplace triptychs printed on canvas**; instead **one work — an affordable contemporary painting, or a poster printed on paper in a decent frame, geometric, large, functioning as the centre of the wall** and supporting the rest of the interior through its texture, colour, subject or geometry. **⚠️ The constraint: the wall itself must be a background. "Если у вас там обоечки с вензелёчками, а сверху ещё и картина, то это уже будет смотреться как трэш."** Her framing of the advantage: minimal cost, no change to the finishes.
8. **⚠️ Many small objects hung as one composition — with a distribution rule.** Inspired by Scandinavian interiors: large wall panels assembled from photographs, pressed plants, small artworks, vintage posters, framed vinyl records. **⚠️ The rule: do not scatter fifteen small frames across the whole apartment one per wall; make one strong grouping in one place.** She applies the same rule to children's drawings.
9. **Three-dimensional walls — gypsum relief panels.** She notes the term sounds nineteenth-century palace, and argues otherwise: a relief gypsum wall (leaves, birds) in her "Реноме" project. **⚠️ Why it works: the colour matches the other walls, so it stays delicate, and it is a play of light and shadow that reads differently in daylight, artificial light and table-lamp light.** Suits both contemporary and classical schemes depending on subject. **Not cheap.**
10. **⚠️ Folding screens (ширмы) — and this is her recommendation for rented homes specifically.** Stands against the wall, **the wall is never touched**, and it works as a large decorative object that draws attention off the empty wall. **Can be packed up and taken with you.** She notes she collects them (a contemporary one by a young Uzbek artist; a 1980s French vintage one, tropical motifs on gold leaf) and considers them "незаслуженно забытый и недооценённый предмет интерьера."

## Design Concept — what she rejects as dated or cheap-looking

Recorded because it is a compact anti-pattern list, all stated as her own aesthetic judgement (`single-account`):

- **⚠️ Any imitation material, with one explicit exception**: flexible "stone" that is not stone, poor-quality faux marble, imitations generally — **except large-format porcelain imitating natural stone**, which she accepts. **This is consistent with her Round 1 flooring position and with existing vault content on faux-marble substitution.**
- **Rough/"dикий" stone — corners and inserts of it.** Her dating: "грузинский ресторан в подвале в 2004."
- **⚠️ Slat/rail panels (рейки) — "были когда-то очень популярные, сейчас устарели."** **Note: this contradicts nothing in this vault directly but sits against existing slat-panel content on `13_Surfaces_and_Finishes/analysis/Decorative_Wall_Panels.md` (Kruglov/Ontario, Round 15) which treats them as a live option — worth flagging as a dated-trend opinion rather than a technical objection.**
- **Photo-wallpaper murals** of Paris, London and similar.
- **Vinyl wall stickers of any kind**, including family-photo "trees". **⚠️ Her single tolerated case: a small child, a minimalist bathroom adjoining the child's bedroom, some fish stickers removed two years later — "вот только дизайнеру не показывайте."**
- **Many small motley pictures hung unsystematically** — same rule as item 8.
- **Over-decoration generally.**

## Design Concept — ⚠️ the four cases for leaving a wall empty

**The most valuable part of the source, because it is the option the format usually omits.** "Не каждую стену нужно чем-то украшать, отделывать, завешивать."

1. **⚠️ When the style is minimalism or modern classic and the airy laconic quality is part of the design intent.** **Her general rule attached to this: if you have signed off a design project, do what the project says — "любые нововведения мимо дизайнера играют всегда в худшую сторону, а не в лучшую."**
2. **⚠️ When the space is small and much of the surface is already occupied.** If one or two walls already carry something, **do not decorate the third and fourth. "Пусть у вас будет хоть немножечко воздуха."**
3. **When you simply prefer airy, light interiors** — achieved partly through laconic furnishing, and helped by light, grey, grey-blue, white and milky walls.
4. **⚠️ When the property is for rent or for sale**: minimum extraneous decor. **One contemporary artwork on the wall for a premium flip; "128 милых картиночек из чего-то детства — строго нет."**
