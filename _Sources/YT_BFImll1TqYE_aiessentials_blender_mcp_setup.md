## He is unusually explicit about the security exposure, and that is the durable part

> «You are taking an AI coding tool and you are connecting it to Blender and you are allowing it to execute code on your computer… Depending on your settings and permissions, it may be able to access and modify other files on your computer.»

His concrete recommendations:

- **Turn OFF the ChatGPT desktop app's default "full access"**, which he says is on by default: *«by default ChatGPT runs with full access to your entire computer… I usually toggle that off»*.
- **Prefer "allow once" over "allow for this conversation"** where the action touches files.
- **Consider a virtual machine** to sandbox it from the rest of the computer.
- *«Only install tools from sources that you trust»* — he picks his MCP by GitHub stars, which he calls social proof rather than assurance.
