## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE FINDING — the vault's balcony decision was binary and this adds the middle option

**The vault currently holds two positions: Петришин-Строй's principle that insulation conserves heat and does not generate it, so it is *«pointless without a real heat source»*; and Безверхая's null option, that not converting at all is a legitimate budget choice. This source is asked directly whether insulating a COLD-glazed balcony is worth it, and answers in a way neither covers.**

> ***«Без обогревателя толку вообще не будет»*** — **but also** ***«Я конечно же всем отвечаю, что да, смысл есть»***.

**The resolution he gives is by duration of use, not by whether to convert:**

| Intended use | His verdict |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Warm YEAR-ROUND, as if heated** | ***«Нет, конечно, это работать не будет… вы выбросите деньги»*** |
| **Warm ON DEMAND for a few hours** — go out with tea or coffee, sit with friends, heater on | **⚠️⚠️ *«Вполне комфортно вы там себя будете чувствовать»*** |
| **Insulated but with NO heater running** — expecting warmth, or storing things | ***«Это ошибка»*** |

**His analogy is the clearest statement of the principle in the vault:** *«Это как в машину сесть зимой в холодную, завести двигатель, включить печку — и там через какое-то время будет тепло, потому что есть энергия тепловая.»* **Cold walls absorb whatever heat appears instantly; insulation stops that; the heater supplies the energy; neither works without the other.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ DIRECTLY RELEVANT TO THIS PROJECT, whose balcony is planned unheated and three-season** (see [[10_Balcony/Balcony_Index|Balcony_Index]]'s Family Needs). **It says the three-season plan can be extended into winter use for a few hours at a time by insulation plus a portable electric heater, WITHOUT the full warm-conversion spend — and equally that insulating alone, without a heater, buys nothing.**
>
> ⚠️ **Independent second arrival at the vault's existing "insulation conserves, does not generate" principle, from an unrelated company.** ⚠️ **He also concedes a sunny aspect with high sun can give a comfortable temperature on its own, then immediately discounts it: *«если посмотреть трезво, конечно же, без обогревателя толку вообще не будет»*.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. THE SEQUENCING TRAP — extension profiles, and the mechanism the vault lacked

> ***«Обязательно при проектировании нужно учесть расширители, они же доборные профили, которые нужны для компенсации толщины утеплителя и самой отделки — чтобы отделка не находила на штапик или не находила на стеклопакет.»***

**Why it matters, and this is the part [[_Sources/YT_w2x6LPIvZw0_okna20_balcony_glazing_five_mistakes|Окна 2.0 asserts without explaining]]:**

> **If the glass unit ever cracks it must be replaced — and if the interior finish overlaps the glazing bead or the unit, replacing it means dismantling the finish.** *«Всё, что сделали, потом придётся сломать.»* He says these requests arrive periodically and he has to tell people it cannot be done.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ TWO UNRELATED COMPANIES IN ONE BATCH NAME THE SAME COMPONENT, and together they make a complete argument: Окна 2.0 says specify them at measurement, ЭлитБалкон says why.** **Decided at glazing-design time or not at all, and the cost of omitting it is discovered years later by a cracked pane.**

## ⚠️ 3. His spec floors — one corroborates the vault, one exceeds it

| Item | His figure | Vault currently |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Glass unit | **from 40 mm, двухкамерный** | **≥40 mm** ✅ third independent arrival |
| Profile | **70 mm, пятикамерный or thicker** | **70 mm floor** ✅ |
| **Insulation thickness** | **⚠️⚠️ from 80 mm**, dense polystyrene-family | **⚠️ пеноплекс minimum 50 mm single-layer** (Петришин-Строй) |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A GENUINE DISAGREEMENT ON INSULATION THICKNESS — 80 mm against 50 mm, recorded and NOT averaged.** **Note they may be answering different questions: the vault's 50 mm is stated as a minimum for a converted loggia, his 80 mm as the target for a "maximally warm" one, and he adds *«чем толще, тем лучше»* and *«никаких экономий нигде не должно быть»*.** **He also separately calls 100 mm *«уже для тёплого помещения»*.**

**⚠️ Which surfaces, and a nuance the vault's "full-contour" rule does not have:** ceiling, parapet, floor and side walls get maximum thickness; **the BACK wall — the one adjoining the heated flat — generally does not need insulating**, though where the junction is awkward he would insulate it thinly, **3 cm being enough there.** **→ Compatible with the vault's full-contour rule once you see that the back wall is not part of the thermal envelope; worth stating so nobody insulates it at full thickness.**

**⚠️ Add a reflective (foil-faced) layer over the polystyrene** — *«полистирол плюс отражающий утеплитель… получается как термос»*. **Corroborates the vault's existing пенофол usage.** He notes the glass itself carries a low-e coating that reflects energy back (shine a torch and the beam returns) and that the profile's chambers trap heat, so a thermal imager on a correct window shows a positive temperature at −20/−30 outside.

**⚠️⚠️ AND THE FAILURE MODE THAT JUSTIFIES NOT ECONOMISING:** *«при повышенной температуре и при повышенной влажности в самых тонких местах — в углах, например, или где будет меньше толщина утеплителя — может проявиться влажность, и там будет плесень.»* **→ Ties directly to [[_Sources/YT_Nr5_Pma2Tvg_elitbalkon_ventilation_and_condensation|his own ventilation and condensation material]]: thin spots are cold spots, cold spots are where indoor moisture condenses.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Heaters — a sizing rule and a defect-specific recommendation

**⚠️⚠️ THE SIZING RULE, and it is concrete:** buy a heater rated for MORE area than you have — *«не на 3 м², а на 6 или 9; не на 10 м², а на 15 или 20»*. **Surplus capacity can be turned up; running short means buying a second heater.** He frames the failure case as a study or play area on the loggia where you simply end up cold.

| Type | For | Against |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Oil-filled radiator** | Cheap; radiates in **all directions**, good for heating from the middle of a space | **⚠️ If it stands in the walkway you must walk around it** |
| **⚠️⚠️ Fan heater** | **Directs warm air where needed — he names it specifically for existing damp, freezing or mouldy corners, which it blows warm air onto and dries**; also promotes air exchange so air does not stagnate, **and dries the air** | (he names none) |
| **Wall convector** | Draws in cold air, emits warm; hung in a far corner directing heat to the centre | **⚠️ Heats only from the FRONT — it does not warm the wall behind it.** For a ~6 m balcony he would fit **two, or even three** |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ The fan-heater recommendation is the useful one and the vault has nothing like it: a heater type matched to a specific DEFECT (damp corners) rather than to an area.** **It also pairs with §3's mould mechanism — the same corners that are thinnest are the ones a fan heater is used to dry.**
>
> ⚠️ **He claims to have tested radiators with a thermal imager on television expert programmes. Unverifiable credential claim, recorded as stated.**
