## ⚠️ Market Data — a trend-timing anecdote with a real commercial mechanism

- **In 2017 they were among the first to put a particular upholstered bed on their showroom floor** — a client had been asking for it for a year, and he judged it would become current. It sat for a while, then sold well.
- **⚠️ Then the supplier displayed the same beds across his entire retail network, and FLAT's sales of it fell: "у нас в определённый момент просто упали продажи." And "в каждом не знаю третьем современном дизайн-проекте нам такие решения попадались."**
- **⚠️ The point he draws, and it is a reasonable division of labour: "ты должен понимать, что будет актуально — ещё не ты, а специалисты, к которым ты пришёл, либо дизайнеры."** **Anticipating what will still read as current is the specialist's job, not the client's** — which is a different and better argument for hiring one than "it will look nice."
