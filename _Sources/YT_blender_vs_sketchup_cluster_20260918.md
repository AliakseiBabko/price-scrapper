The owner supplied five tool-comparison videos as a sanity check on the Blender choice. **Three were filtered out, and the reason is the finding.**

**All three predate Blender 4.x entirely, let alone the 5.2 LTS installed here.** A 2021 verdict on Blender's precision modelling, its add-on ecosystem or its real-time engine is not evidence about the 2026 product — EEVEE Next alone did not exist. **Extracting their conclusions as current would have been the vault's own date-discipline failure (standing rule 2), applied to software instead of prices.**

They are recorded here so nobody re-triages them, not discarded silently.

## `6dfSakWnqcY` — Can Blender Compete in ArchViz (inspirationTuts CAD, 2026-01-03)

- ⚠️ **A 2024 survey it cites puts 3ds Max at ~59% of ArchViz artists against Blender's 9–10%.** Blender is the challenger, not the standard — worth knowing when hiring or handing over.
- **Cycles for polished stills, EEVEE for interactive walkthroughs**, and it names **EEVEE Next in Blender 5** as the realtime step change.
- It names **Archipack** (parametric walls, doors, windows, stairs, roofs) and **BlenderBIM** — now Bonsai — as what closes the architectural gap.

> **⚠️ THIS IS THE ONE DIRECTLY USEFUL FINDING FOR THIS PROJECT.** The owner wants to *walk through* the model from inside. **EEVEE Next, in the Blender 5.2 already installed here, is the named tool for exactly that** — so the walkthrough requirement needs no new software and no change of plan.

## `CVa-dNIkel8` — Where is the Tape Measure tool? (Expose Academy, 2025-09-28)

Aimed at SketchUp users arriving in Blender, and its answer is the useful part:

> **You do not need guides to model precisely in Blender.** Select an edge, `Shift+D` to duplicate, then the axis and the distance — `X 20`, `Enter` — and the edge lands exactly 20 cm away. Auto-merge on, then a manifold extrude.

He also warns about the habit it replaces: SketchUp users *«create guides for everything»* until the scene is full of guides nobody can attribute. **Two fallback techniques for real guides are given if wanted, but framed as unnecessary.**

⚠️ **Relevance here is limited but real**: this project does not model by hand in either tool — geometry is compiled — so the tape-measure question does not arise. What transfers is the confirmation that **Blender's precise input is numeric and axis-locked**, which is the same discipline the compiler already enforces.
