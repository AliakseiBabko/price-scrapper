# Source Note - Никита Якоби: ⚠️⚠️ the "антивандальная ткань" debunk, from someone who used to perform the demonstration himself (YouTube SwOVFr5YRP8)

## Evidence levels
(1) transcript text - (2) YouTube metadata - (3) contextual inference - (4) external validation (none)

## ⚠️⚠️ Why this source matters: he sees furniture AFTER it fails

**Every other upholstery voice in the vault — Шеврина, ARMOS, Краснов, Пинскдрев — meets fabric at the point of sale. A re-upholsterer meets it at the end of its life, and his examples are the jobs he was called in to fix.** That is a different and harder-to-fake evidence position. ⚠️ **He does sell furniture, and the video ends with a pouffe sale — but the main argument costs him a standard upsell, which is the direction that lends it weight.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE CONTRADICTION — "антивандальность" is a coating with a shelf life, not a fabric property

**The vault currently carries, from Краснов, the claim that anti-vandal fabrics are what hotels and rental flats use and are the answer for pets with big claws. Якоби attacks the category itself.**

**His account:** the stain- and claw-resistance being sold is **an applied treatment, not a property of the weave**, and it **degrades within roughly a year** of normal use and cleaning. **He states he used to perform the standard showroom demonstration himself — drawing on flock with a pen and wiping it off** — as a sales trick, and he names the case that ended it for him: **a customer bought a white flock sofa on that promise, spilled jam on it, and the seat had to be reupholstered.** Her complaint was the honest one: *«вы же мне обещали, что это антивандальный материал, с ним ничего не будет»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ This does NOT flatly refute Краснов — it reframes what he is describing.** A hotel replaces or re-covers on a cycle; a household expects a decade. **The reconciliation the vault should carry: "антивандальный" describes a NEW fabric's behaviour under a showroom demonstration, and the honest planning horizon for that behaviour is about a year.** ⚠️ **No independent confirmation of the one-year figure — it is his trade experience, not a test result.**
>
> **His own generalisation is the more durable claim:** *«нет плохих инструментов… все материалы хороши, но только на своём месте»* — **the defect is the industry selling one fabric as universal, not any fabric being bad.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Pets and claws — the THIRD independent arrival in this vault

**Флок and велюр are the ones he names as holding up against claws** — short, dense pile that gives a claw nothing to hook.

**⚠️⚠️ Рогожка is his clearest negative, and he gives TWO separate failure modes:**

1. **It catches and pulls into snags immediately** — the loose interlacing is the whole problem.
2. **⚠️ A water stain SPREADS into a halo (разводы) and needs dry-cleaning to remove** — not a wipe. **This second one is new to the vault.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIRD ARRIVAL on the claw mechanism.** Already held: Шеврина (bouclé, ribbed velvet) and ARMOS (рогожка explicitly). Якоби makes three, [[_Sources/YT_1UGUjGmZPNA_pinskdrev_sofa_fabric_guide|Пинскдрев]] in the same batch makes four. **Four unrelated sources — a designer, a bed manufacturer, a re-upholsterer and a sofa factory — name the same physical mechanism and split the same way on which fabrics fail it. This is now the best-corroborated furniture finding in the vault.**
>
> ⚠️ **And note the tension it creates internally**: Loose Furniture Selection Principles recommends рогожка on AESTHETIC grounds (texture, visible weave, avoiding flat monotone). **Both can be true. The page must not read as if the vault likes рогожка without qualification.**

## ⚠️ 3. Eco-leather — where it works, and the cost of putting it where it does not

- **For a kitchen or dining seat: he recommends it** — it wipes clean, which is the property that actually matters there.
- **⚠️ Service life before it starts peeling: 3–5 years.** A specific, falsifiable number and the vault did not have one. ⚠️ **Trade experience, not a test.**
- **⚠️⚠️ The failure case he gives is a living-room sofa in экокожа**: the owner found it uncomfortable to sit on, spent five years covering it with throws that constantly slid off, **and then it peeled underneath the throws anyway.** **→ Two costs, not one: it was unpleasant for the whole period AND it still failed on schedule.**

> **→ ⚠️ Corroborates the vault's existing ARMOS line — «экокожа is unsuitable if you want 10–15 years» — and now puts a number on the actual figure (3–5).** Two sources, consistent.

## 4. His framing question

Before choosing, he says to settle **who and what will actually be on this piece, and for how long** — children, animals, food, daily use versus occasional — and to choose the fabric to that answer rather than to a category label.

> **→ This is the same shape as the vault's mattress conclusion (fit dominates price) arriving independently in upholstery. Worth stating on the page as the organising principle rather than as a bullet.**
