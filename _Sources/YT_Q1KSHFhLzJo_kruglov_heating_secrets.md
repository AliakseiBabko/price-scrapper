## Heating — False-Wall Riser Concealment: Economics and Bonus Details (extends existing riser-boxing content)

- **⚠️ A false wall built to conceal a vertical riser (with a radiator niche) doesn't cost meaningfully more than simply plastering that same wall plane normally** — since the false wall replaces (not adds to) the plastering work the wall would have needed anyway, the net material/labor cost is comparable to ordinary finishing, not a large premium. **Three concrete bonuses beyond concealment**: (1) a deeper windowsill reveal, wide enough to fit an outlet; (2) a wider usable zone in front of the false wall (storage, a narrow work surface) that wouldn't otherwise be usable space since a bare riser/radiator already occupies it; (3) a clean baseboard-to-ceiling junction — two baseboards butting cleanly into a flat wall corner, versus the ugly result of two baseboards trying to terminate against an exposed round pipe.
