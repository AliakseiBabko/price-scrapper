## Mistakes / Warnings, Design Concept, and Numeric Data

- **⚠️ A bed without a headboard reads as visually unfinished/"just moved in"** — headboard functions as the room's visual anchor/focal point AND protects the wall behind it from stains/wear, even in an otherwise minimalist room. Genuinely new for this channel's bedroom content; needs reconciling against `zZO7y7Rpbk4` (this same round)'s critique of OVERSIZED headboards — see that note and the wiki update for the reconciled synthesis.
- **⚠️ Glossy/semi-gloss wall paint highlights every surface imperfection and reads cheap/plastic-like in a bedroom — matte paint recommended instead**, described as more forgiving, more tactile-reading, more "status."
- **⚠️ Avoid pure/primary "toy-store" bright colors** (saturated red/yellow/blue) — even neutral-looking whites/greys/beiges should have a complex, hard-to-name undertone (a hint of warm, cool, green, or blue) rather than being a flat pure hue. **Named recommended complex neutrals for base tones**: grey-beige (серо-бежевый), warm stone (тёплый каменный), foggy/misty blue (туманно-голубой), olive (оливковый), slate grey (грифельно-серый), milk (молочный). **Named accents** (in muted/"dusty" versions, not pure saturated tones): deep blue (глубокий синий), dark green (тёмно-зелёный), burgundy (бордовый), graphite (графит).
- **⚠️ Furniture-quantity restraint**: even with expensive individual pieces, overcrowding a bedroom with every possible furniture type (bed + 2 chairs + stone coffee table + designer armchairs + pouf + vanity table + 2 nightstands + open shelving) removes the sense of spaciousness that actually signals "expensive." Recommends 1-2 statement pieces plus restraint, explicitly framing excess furniture as "a museum of expensive purchases" rather than a calm status space.
- **Visual "noise" from combining too many bold patterns/colors simultaneously** (bright wallpaper + patterned rug + bold throw + trendy pillows, all at once) — limit to one or two accent moments, not all of them together. Restates the general accent-limiting principle already in this vault, cross-reference only.
- **⚠️ Curtain fabric quality — avoid cheap synthetic fabrics** (viscose, polyester), described as having an unnatural sheen, poor drape, and looking "tired"/lifeless over time — especially bad in a bedroom where textile quality is closely tied to the sense of privacy/comfort. Prefer natural fibers: linen («лён», natural roughness/texture), cotton (softness/pliability), velvet (бархат, color depth, luxury feel) as premium alternatives.
- **⚠️ Curtain length, with a specific new numeric detail**: curtains should either just touch the floor or have a slight break/puddle at the floor — **the transcript states "напуск... примерно 13 см"** (a puddle of ~13cm), which is almost certainly ASR mis-transcription of "1-3 см" (1-3cm), the figure that matches every other source's puddle convention in this vault (all cite low-single-digit centimeters, never 13cm — a 13cm puddle would be an extreme, unstated-elsewhere outlier). **Recorded here as `ASR-uncertain`, treated as 1-3cm** per the pattern match against this project's existing curtain-length content, but flagged explicitly rather than silently corrected. Curtains that stop at the windowsill or just above the floor read as budget/rental-apartment cheap and visually shorten the wall.
