## ⚠️ Underfloor heating under a kitchen — dimension from the plinth, not the facade

**The best coordination detail in the source, and it is the kind that is invisible until someone stands barefoot at the hob.**

- **Technically the heated field must stop 5 cm short of the furniture and any abutment.**
- **⚠️ But the dimension must be taken from the ЦОКОЛЬ (the recessed plinth), NOT from the facade line — «важно давать размеры не от фасадной части, а именно от цоколя».**
- **The reason is the toe recess: «когда хозяйка подходит к плите и у неё босая нога заходит под кухню, чтобы пальцам не было холодно».** Dimensioning from the facade leaves exactly the strip your feet occupy unheated.
- The corollary he states: this must be accounted for **when producing the drawings**, not on site.

**And the alignment it enabled: the tile joint runs exactly into the kitchen's edge.** «Это может получиться только если это прямо надо контролировать.» **The failure it avoids: the kitchen overhanging by a few centimetres, or falling short and leaving «такая белая светящаяся полоска».** He also notes the parquet running level from the kitchen's edge.

**His stated coordination practice, which is how any of it happens: «я всегда скидываю ТЗ строителям, информацию о строителе скидываю мебельщикам, чтобы они там все общались».**

## ⚠️ Furniture dimensional tolerance — an incident, a norm, and the contract rule

**The strongest procurement content in the round, because it has a failure, a figure and a remedy.**

- **A bed arrived 12 cm LONGER than declared. Per the manufacturer's own norms he was permitted to deviate by up to 7 cm — and the 12 cm became grounds for RETURNING the furniture.**
- **⚠️ The panel's reaction is the useful part: 7 cm is itself absurd for exclusive work. «Эксклюзивный ремонт подразумевает размеры в размеры… это не 7 см, это может быть 5 мм.»**
- **⚠️ THE RULE: tolerances are set by the CONTRACT, and every manufacturer has its own norms. «Изучаем договор прежде чем подписывать.»** If the contract permits 7 cm, that is what you agreed to.
- **⚠️ And the proactive form: tell the supplier at order time — «у нас нет возможности устанавливать нечто больше чем 2–3 см; если вас их условия не устраивают, не рискуйте, идите в другое место».**
- **Do not plan furniture flush into a niche.** For their Italian sofa they asked the supplier for the exact size **five times**, he asked for re-measurement five times, and **the client came with a tape measure himself.** They left **~3 cm** of clearance and he regards that as already generous — «7 см — это какая-то вообще неведомая запас».
- **The mitigation of last resort: «обращаться к надёжным поставщикам… дизайнеры работают уже годами с одними и теми же людьми, они вам порекомендуют».**

## ⚠️ Paint выкрасы — the protocol, and why RAL/NCS requests are useless

**A worked sequence, and it materially strengthens this vault's existing paint-testing material.**

- **«Подскажи цвет по RAL/NCS» requests get a flat refusal: «Это полный бред. Вы думаете, что на кадре это классно, но у вас будет вообще по-другому с вероятностью 99,9%.»**
- **Their actual process: design project with 3D renders → colours selected from a palette → SEVERAL выкрасы made, of the chosen colour AND its neighbours → taken to the actual room WITH the clients → and the colours read completely differently there. One was perfect; one was remade half a tone warmer; one was redone entirely.**
- **⚠️ Make the выкрас on a LARGE sheet — A4 or even A3 — not a small square. And view it under daylight AND artificial light.**
- **The economic argument: on a 100 m²+ flat the alternative is buying all the paint, painting it, paying the labour, and only then discovering it does not suit.** «Проще купить баночку краски.»
- **A demonstration worth repeating to a client: a wall that reads white on camera is not white — hold a sheet of white paper against it and the tone, warmth and undertone appear.**

## ⚠️ Two-colour junctions, and the micro-pilaster

- **«Идеально стыковать два цвета практически невозможно — всегда будут какие-то наплывы.»**
- **⚠️ The solution: a micro-pilaster or наличник projecting out of the wall plane, so the colour wraps around it and there is no butt joint at all. «Когда краска заходит за эту пилястру, это всегда получается очень эстетично.»**
- **Gradient execution: the whole wall sprayed floor-to-ceiling with a sprayer; once the wall and paint had dried and gained strength, decorators taped a stencil and painted the lower portion in the second colour.**
- **Gypsum elements were made TO ORDER rather than bought as standard items — «это всегда гораздо практичнее, и казалось бы, не должно экономить денег, но по факту это экономит деньги».**
- **Skirting in cast stone («жидкий камень», he jokes Т-1000): does not fear knocks and does not fear moisture, so a mop will not chip it and it will not swell under wet cleaning.**

## ⚠️ Gypsum wall panels — a failure-mode list, and a delayed one

**The best technical passage in the source.**

- **The problem with 60 × 60 panels is the sheer number of vertical and horizontal joints, all of which must be handled competently.**
- **Requirements: glue the panels very reliably; ALSO screw them; ⚠️ AND THE SCREWS MUST BE STAINLESS. «Часто кто-то допускает ошибку — вкручивают саморезы обычные, потом они ржавеют, через гипс ржавчина легко проходит, и со временем появляются ржавые пятна на стене.»** A specific, checkable, delayed failure.
- Tight abutment of the gypsum wall to the load-bearing wall with full gluing, good joint finishing, and **a great deal of hand sanding.**
- **⚠️ THE DESIGN RULE: order a single whole VERTICAL panel where you can, because HORIZONTAL joints are the worst — they show most. Vertical joints get masked in the reeding and are easier to hide.**
- **⚠️ The practical compromise, with dimensions: instead of seven horizontal seams, order e.g. a 2 m plus a 1 m panel and put the single horizontal joint LOW, where least light falls.** If a 2.5 m panel cannot be carried in, do 2 m + 1 m.
- **Procurement point: ready-made gypsum solutions vary in thickness and «многие жалуются» — order from gypsum specialists who will pull the element to your size.**
- Quality evidence offered: **under ceiling and raking side light, not a single line is visible.**

**⚠️ And a small mounting detail that follows: sockets in a gypsum panel need cut flat pads for tight abutment.** Otherwise «за розетками отверстия есть торчащие рёбра, либо разбиваются, розетка топится туда, и мы видим сверху рёбра» — «некрасиво, неэстетично». The pads also make the connection more reliable.

## ⚠️ Cove-lighting niche — a ready-made profile, and the reflective-surface reason

**A third distinct consideration for cove lighting, alongside this vault's existing dust-trap and low-ceiling objections.**

- **They used ready-made cove profiles (brand ASR-garbled as «Р-gips», recorded `uncertain`) made specifically for закарнизные niches.**
- **Three stated advantages:** (1) **no need to paint the inner end face**, so nobody has to reach into a narrow gap — and «бывает ещё уже расстояние между потолком и стеной»; (2) **they arrive with a clip-on light-diffusing plastic profile**, making the LED easier to fit; (3) **⚠️ where there are REFLECTIVE surfaces nearby — black gloss tile, mirrors — a hand-painted niche will never be good enough, because you can SEE INTO the niche in the reflection.** «Применять именно такие профиля хорошо там, где у вас есть стены, в которых в отражении можно увидеть, что находится за закарнизной нишей.»
- **Installation is tied into the plasterboard framing** — the trades interlock the framing and the board comes to it, producing a finished construction.

**And the track-lighting equivalent: tracks have dedicated grooves for plasterboard leaving a layer for filler and paint; reinforcement is mandatory; and the framing under a track is assembled in a specific order — strips left around, the frame assembled, strips under the tracks — then re-sheeted, then reinforced, filled and sanded by the painters.**

## ⚠️ ГКЛ versus газобетон — a full comparison, prompted by a hostile comment

**A viewer complained that they use plasterboard everywhere, calling empty GKL walls «уровень Шутов» and GKL ceilings «это вообще СССР», and asserting that газобетон is only used to economise. The answer is the best material comparison of the round.**

- **⚠️ Plasterboard is chosen for SOUNDPROOFING: «если мы делаем шумоизоляцию во всей квартире, стены могут достигать 200 мм толщиной. Профессиональная шумоизоляция требует таких потерь помещения»** — and that is why people buy larger free-plan flats.
- **Where газобетон is used: where only a small demolition is done and the existing walls are already газобетон — they then extend in the same material.**
- **Cost and speed: «дороже и дольше делать стены из гипсокартона; газобетонные блоки делать гораздо быстрее».**
- **⚠️ THE LABOUR-AVAILABILITY ARGUMENT, which is the genuinely non-obvious one: masonry is easier to staff. «Коллеги, которые приезжают из других стран, со Средней Азии в том числе, они умеют делать кладку очень хорошо. С гипсокартоном работать могут не все.»**
- **⚠️ AND THE TOOLING FIGURES, which explain both the price and the availability gap: a good plasterboard brigade's full tool set can reach 300,000 RUB (≈$3,100) and more. For masonry the tooling can be bought for 10,000 RUB (≈$100) — or up to ~30,000 (≈$310) including laser levels and a decent level. A 10–30× asymmetry.**
- **His summary: «газобетон — это быстро; гипсокартон — это долго, сложно, дорого и ТИХО».**
- **Thickness, which reverses the space argument: two layers of plasterboard as a lining ≈ 5 cm; газобетон is a minimum of 8 cm PLUS plaster, another minimum 1 cm.** So thin partitions favour plasterboard. **Combined solutions are normal.**
- **He widens the field honestly rather than defending two options: acoustic boards (not plasterboard), brick and its varieties, пазогребень, silicate blocks. «Материалов для возведения перегородок достаточно большое [количество].»**
- **⚠️ An open question left open: the host raises that silicate blocks are said to suit bathrooms better because they take up moisture differently, while газобетон is not especially recommended for wet rooms. The contractor declines to resolve it — «у всех разные мнения, технологии, это всё нужно изучать; это не такой простой вопрос».** Recorded as unresolved.
- Client-preference anecdotes: one client claimed an allergy specifically to «гипрок» but not to gypsum; another insisted on plasterboard only.

## Concealment as a client demand, and its material basis

- Client "phobia" about anything protruding or any visible wire. **His answer is availability rather than taste: there is now a mass of finish material, electrical included, that permits interiors with no visible cabling** — e.g. the illuminated "droplets" **integrated into the wall itself, «мы можем избавиться не только от проводов, а даже от фигур в виде выпуклых плафонов»**.
- **Concealed-mount TV: fixings invisible, pressed to the perimeter, with a niche behind it carrying all the commutation, and a cable channel running diagonally to the connection point «чтобы не было всяких сопель в виде проводов».**
- **The economic argument: «если вы вкладываете большие деньги в ремонт… зачем вам вся эта красота, которую испортит внешний вид».** Beautiful cables exist — retro, textile — but are rare and cost money.
- **«Есть скрытого монтажа от сантехники до электрики и техники в том числе.»**
- **⚠️ And the order rule he cites from his own multi-million-view video, in its most compressed form: «вперёд ставьте то, чем вы постоянно пользуетесь; прячьте то, чем вы не пользуетесь».** He notes it seems obvious and people still do not do it. **A nice honest exemption: a mad scientist with cables he genuinely uses daily will have that disorder «и это будет квартира сумасшедшего учёного — это будет круто».**

## Windowsills and balcony steps

- **Materials: continue in the same parquet; porcelain stoneware (they will use the floor tile on the balcony step); quartzite, quartz agglomerate. The governing criterion is WEAR RESISTANCE, because a step is a traffic point.**
- **⚠️ A widespread misconception corrected, including the host's own: the "ears" (проушины) on classical windowsills are NOT there to mask a crooked wall. They are the SUPPORTS for the window's framing — in old interiors a window was always framed with an наличник, and the проушина is the base the наличник grows from «чтобы он не висел непонятно как». «Этот элемент по сути как бы не нужен»** in a modern interior.
- **⚠️ Projection and the radiator, with a mechanism: a small overhang makes the sill usable, but it must NOT fully cover the radiator. You may extend it to the MIDDLE of the radiator so heat still reaches the glass. If it must be wider, you MUST PERFORATE the sill so heat passes up the window — otherwise you get the dew point and moisture accumulating on the glass.**
- **⚠️ And a hard prerequisite for the minimalist flush (заподлицо) sill: the wall must be perfectly straight, «иначе будет морковка — будет торчать ваш камень».** In an ordinary developer-finish flat you probably cannot do it. **«Минимализм — это про очень грамотную работу строителей, где счёт идёт на миллиметры везде и во всём.»** *(The same conclusion this channel's cost roundtable reaches from the budget side — minimalism raises the minimum acceptable trade quality.)*

## Kids room transformability, and the final finishing pass

- Client asked for a room able to transform later: **one bed now, but built so it can be SEPARATED — like hotel twin singles.**
- **⚠️ And the electrical consequence, which is where transformability is actually bought: sockets everywhere — he counts four — «чтобы с ростом ребёнка можно было легко делать перепланировку».** Specified at rough stage.
- **⚠️ A QC practice worth recording: after the full clean is complete, a painter goes out ONE MORE TIME with decorative sealants and small artist's brushes and removes remaining minor defects «которые в принципе даже глазу незаметны», bringing it to an ideal state.** So the final punch-list pass happens **after** cleaning, not before.

## Zero-budget ceiling advice, and an honest correction

Answering a pensioner's question about cables on the ceiling: **a stretch ceiling is cheaper, and he would take it without hesitation. Classification given: cheap China; quality China category A (expensive); quality German; quality French.** **⚠️ But he immediately corrects the implication — «многие подумают, что мы так говорим: натяжные потолки однозначно дешёвое решение. Это разные решения»** — the category spans a wide price range. **Zero-budget alternative: varnish the bare concrete and route the cables neatly across the ceiling — make it a loft.** Or, depending on stage, put the conduits in the floor.
