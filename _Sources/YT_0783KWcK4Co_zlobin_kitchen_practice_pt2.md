  this is the *width of countertop* needed at each triangle point for

  triangle's own geometry.
- **⚠️ Corner-sink placement is a compromise for genuinely small kitchens
  only, not a default choice**: the source states a personal
  dislike/skepticism of corner sinks in general — in a small kitchen
  (his example: ~2x2.5m) there may be no other place to put the sink, so
  a corner placement (with a trash bin fitted underneath) becomes a
  forced necessity. **In a spacious kitchen, prefer a straight-run sink
  placement instead** — standing in a corner to work is called
  unnecessarily awkward when a longer straight run is available.
