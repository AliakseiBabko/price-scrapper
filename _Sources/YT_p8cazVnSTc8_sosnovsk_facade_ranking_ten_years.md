## Why this source matters

**The vault's facade coverage is broad and it is all INVENTORY** — what a front can be made of. **This is the first source that RANKS them and names, for each one, the single defect that actually generates complaints**, on the basis of a decade of one shop's returns. *«Я уже 3 года не снимал на эти темы видео»* — so it is a considered restatement rather than a routine upload.

**His stated six-criterion standard for any facade**: aesthetic · moisture-resistant · heat-resistant · mechanically strong · easy to clean · sensibly priced.

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE SINGLE MOST USEFUL NUMBER: emboss depth, 0.3 mm vs 1.3 mm

**On ЛДСП fronts — the volume seller in econom and even business class, and frequently mis-sold as "HPL plastic" or "thermostructured panels".** It is in fact **melamine decorative kraft paper impregnated with resins.**

> ***«Есть важный критерий — это ТОЛЩИНА ТИСНЕНИЯ. Эконом-класс ДСП — максимум 0,3 мм. А премиум — до 1,3 мм.»***

**The mechanism, and it is the whole argument:** cutting and edge-banding damages the surface invisibly. **On cheap ЛДСП the thin film breaks, moisture or steam gets in, and it swells** — *«превращается в бутерброд, и кромка вам падает в борщ»*. **On premium ЛДСП with deep emboss the damage does not reach the chipboard.**

- **⚠️⚠️ EGGER ALONE HAS SIX ЛДСП CATEGORIES**, differing in price and performance — including an **F/FW** class with thick coating, and a class with an **MDF base** that holds fasteners better, machines better and lasts longer.
- **⚠️⚠️ The price spread is 10–15×: *«ДСП за квадрат может стоить 1.000 руб, а может 15»*.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS THE THIRD ARRIVAL IN THIS ROUND AT THE SAME PRACTICAL POINT, AND THE ONLY ONE WITH A MEASURABLE PROXY.** [[_Sources/YT_Lfvs1XUz53s_sosnovsk_carcass_construction_and_edge_technology|The carcass source]] says look at lamination layers, not board thickness; [[_Sources/YT_24DWOdqalc4_sosnovsk_metr_pogonny_and_supply_contract|the погонный метр source]] says confirm the category. **This gives the number you can actually ask for: what is the emboss depth, and which category?**
>
> **→ AND IT IS THE ANSWER TO WHY TWO QUOTES DIFFER BY 3× FOR "THE SAME" ЛДСП KITCHEN.** *«Когда вы отправляете расчёты в [несколько] компаний»* — without naming the category you are not comparing like with like.

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. The UV-lacquer finding — a tested defect on a material the vault has never covered

**ALVIK, PerfectSense and Mattelux had ZERO mentions in `03_Kitchen/` before this round.** All three are the same construction: **an MDF board, a PAPER that carries the colour and pattern, and a UV lacquer over it.**

> ***«Идеальный материал — кроме УБОРКИ. Я делал тест: он ВПИТЫВАЕТ загрязнение. Я оставил чай, и он прямо в лак проникся, в эту текстуру, в бумагу — и фасад испорчен.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ TEA SOAKED THROUGH THE UV LACQUER INTO THE DECOR PAPER AND RUINED THE FRONT.** **That is a fully-tested, disqualifying defect on a currently fashionable premium product, reported by someone who installs three or four such kitchens a month.**
>
> ⚠️ **His own position is not a refusal** — he says with correct care it is an ideal front, and stresses that the panel maker's bonding technology (**PUR or laser**) must be followed. **→ Recorded as: a staining risk that belongs on the shortlist conversation, not as a ban.**

Here, plain TEA destroys a premium front.** **Different substrate, opposite result — so "stain resistance" is not a property of price bracket.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Plywood-based fronts, and the argument that there is no edge to fail

**Birch plywood faced with HPL or FENIX NTM** — *«вообще равных нет этому материалу по прочности и долговечности»*.

> ***«У нас НЕТ ЗДЕСЬ КРОМКИ с торца. А это значит, ничего не отвалится. Никакая технология — ни PUR, ни лазер, ни ЭВА — не даст вам стопроцентную гарантию, что со временем кромка всё-таки не начнёт с фасада сползать. А здесь кромки нет — проблем нет.»***

- **Any handle profile can be milled into the edge and it will not crumble, because it is plywood.**
- **Fastener retention**: *«саморез закручивается со скрипом»*, and you can assemble and disassemble repeatedly; **fronts do not sag, so they need no constant readjustment.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ FULLY CONSISTENT WITH [[_Sources/YT_-I97gsh-aek_sosnovsk_carcass_material_and_edge_crash_tests|HIS OWN CRASH TESTS]], where plywood scored 5/5 on every criterion including fastener retention, against ЛДСП's 0/5.** **Unlike his ЛДСП-carcass claim, this one his tests SUPPORT — which is why it carries more weight.**
> **→ And the deepest point in the source: ELIMINATING THE EDGE ELIMINATES THE FAILURE MODE, rather than choosing a better glue for it.**

## ⚠️ 5. Checkable questions this source hands you

- **⚠️⚠️ THE ANTI-FINGERPRINT TEST, which you can run in a showroom with a glass of water.** On matt acrylic plastics, ask whether the decor carries the **AF (anti-fingerprint) hydrophobic coating**: *«капельку капнуть — если она будет так собираться в одну кучку [и скатываться], то это современное покрытие»*. **Resists fingerprints and grease.**
- **⚠️ On gloss acrylic (Senosan): there is a lacquered layer over the gloss that prevents micro-swirls** — *«паутинка, которая на чёрном автомобиле»*. **Deep scratches still show.**
- **⚠️⚠️ On an HPL-faced front, ask about the КОМПЕНСАТОР.** HPL on MDF needs **a balancing counter-layer bonded to the reverse**; its 2000s reputation collapsed *«потому что многие не использовали компенсатор»* — **the plastic fell off, cracked and the panel bowed.** ⚠️ **A specific, askable construction detail behind a whole material's bad name.**
- **⚠️ EVA vs PUR is visible at the seam**: he shows both under macro — a thin seam on PUR against **a visible YELLOW seam on EVA**. **→ You can see the glue technology with your eyes on a sample.**
- **⚠️ On ПВХ-film fronts: ease of processing is why counterfeits are common** — *«нужен по сути лобзик, тумбочка, самый дешёвый кромочник, и всё — любой кустарь может обработать этот фасад у себя в гараже»*.

## ⚠️ 6. Three materials he excludes, and why the exclusions are useful

- **Ceramic fronts** — *«может себе позволить 0,001% всех людей»*.
- **Stone veneer** — a thin stone sheet on MDF; niche.
- **⚠️⚠️ MICROCEMENT / plaster fronts — excluded for a reason that is a genuine warning: *«тяжело согласовать цвет, потому что каждый новый фасад получается разной текстуры. Никто не знает [что получится] — даже тот человек, который занимается этим покрытием.»*** **→ A finish whose outcome cannot be specified in advance, by anyone. Directly relevant to any decorative-plaster decision in this flat.**

⚠️ **And one he adds for a disclosed reason:** the **aluminium-framed glass front** (витрина with internal lighting) is in the list because *«я провёл небольшой анализ своей Instagram-странички, и самое большое количество [реакций] набирают именно кухни с витринами»* — **an engagement metric, not a performance one, and he says so.** ⚠️ **The performance claim attached to it is real though: glass and aluminium *«не боится ни огня, ни воды, ни пара»*,** and he notes a premium Italian maker uses a glass-and-aluminium carcass on its most expensive kitchens.
