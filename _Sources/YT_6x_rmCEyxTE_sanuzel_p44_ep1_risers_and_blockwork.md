## Sequencing — the dependency that opens the whole job

**⚠️ The risers had to be re-welded BEFORE any wall could go up.** The existing towel
rail riser stood exactly where a new wall line runs: «мне как раз простенок будет
проходить здесь, поэтому скоро мне придётся сварщик и переварит эти стояки».

**The welder works to the software model, and this is the third video in a row where
that model drives the physical work:**

- **The linear-expansion compensator on the stack is retained** — «он должен быть, раз он здесь находится». Not removed for convenience.
- **Two stub-outs, hot and cold** — and his stated acceptance criteria are **position exactly as set in the design program beforehand, level with each other, and true in the vertical.** «Всё получилось просто идеально, всё чётко, ровно, на одной высоте, как мне нужно было.»
- **⚠️ Galvanised pipe: the weld seams must be treated with a dedicated zinc spray** — «цинк — это защитное покрытие трубы, поэтому продаётся специально цинковый спрей, как краска в баллончике». A specific, cheap step that is invisible once boxed in.

## Replacing the stack with low-noise pipe

**⚠️ And here he contradicts the manufacturer, a year before they contradicted him.**
He uses a **socket restorer («насадная муфта»)** to drop the bottom tee **without going
to the neighbour below**, and gives **two** reasons: it does not narrow the bore, and
**«все заужения будут создавать лишний шум — нам это не надо, ведь мы ставим бесшумную
канализацию не просто так».**

> In [[_Sources/YT_LDeNqQL7TLQ_sanuzel_plumbers_meetup_manufacturer_qa|the 2024 Q&A]] he put that same noise reasoning to Ostendorf's own specialist, **who declined it** and said the real issue is throughput, not noise. **So this is a genuine, dated, two-sided disagreement between the fitter and the maker — not a settled fact.** Both are recorded; neither is resolved here.

- **Work fast, because the risers cannot be shut off.** «Очень тяжело, чтобы водой не пользовались… всё равно надо делать быстро.» A real constraint on any stack work in an occupied building, and one that shapes how much preparation must be done before cutting.
- **Tees prepared in advance**, before anything is cut.
- **Lubricant is not optional** when entering a socket — without it the pipe will not go in.
- **The compensator goes on first, pushed fully home, lubricated separately.**
- **A safety clamp («страховочный хомут») goes on immediately** so the pipe cannot drop before permanent fixing follows later.
- **⚠️ Cutting length: measure the span, then add the socket depth.** He measures from the top of the temporary socket to the target point, gets **198**, and marks **198 + 6 cm**, saying «либо 5, там по-разному — посчитайте, сколько глубина раструба». ⚠️ **The two numbers cannot be reconciled from ASR** (198 mm as a span with a ~5–6 cm socket depth is the only coherent reading, but he also appears to call 198 the socket depth). **Carry only the rule, which is unambiguous and is the point: add YOUR system's socket depth to the measured span, and measure that depth rather than assuming it.**

## Blockwork — extending, and in two places explaining, the ПИК account

Corroborates [[_Sources/YT_z0hcMY5PYoc_sanuzel_pik_cabin_prep_works|the ПИК prep-works video]] on the no-adhesive/foam-gap principle, and adds the mechanics.

- **First course on fast-setting Litokol Plan Rapid — «буквально через полчаса она уже встаёт камнем».** The floor has level differences and **the first course must be dead level, so its bed thickness varies** (~15 mm here). He rejects the alternative explicitly: «кто-то делает подушку из стяжки… мне это неудобно, я буду укладывать сразу на раствор».
- **Floor and abutments ground and primed before laying** — stated as an afterthought he almost forgot to mention.
- **⚠️ Why the gap to the wall is 1–2 cm specifically**: not a tolerance — **it is the clearance a foam gun's nozzle needs to get deep into the joint.** And **foam from both sides**, because full depth is not reachable from one.
- **Subsequent courses on block adhesive, notched trowel, joint ~2–3 mm.**
- **⚠️ Mix the adhesive wetter than the instructions**, «ближе к жидкому» — because **aerated block absorbs water strongly**, so a semi-liquid mix stiffens to the right consistency once it is on the block. A practitioner's deviation with a stated mechanism.
- **Blocks must be staggered («перевязывать»)**: a whole block covers the joint beneath. **Half-lap ideally; one-third/two-thirds is acceptable.**
- **⚠️ Check the level from ONE face only, decided in advance** — «блоки играют по толщине, немного разные, поэтому показания уровня могут отличаться». Checking both faces produces contradictory readings from the block's own thickness variation, not from bad work.
- **⚠️ Vertical matters more than horizontal**: a horizontal error is correctable with adhesive and «вообще не критично»; a vertical error means **thicker plaster later**.
- **Door opening laid oversize, cut to final size afterwards** — corroborates the ПИК video, same reasoning.
- **⚠️ The lintel is made and reinforced, and this vault had nothing on it**: two blocks glued together with **adhesive foam** («за час-два этого хватает»), a **chase cut along it**, adhesive laid in, and **two metal studs bedded in as reinforcement** — explicitly to stop **diagonal cracking** from the opening corners. «Армировать всегда надо. Все это делают по-разному — кто-то снизу, кто-то сверху; я делаю вот так.» **The lintel goes in first, and only then is the opening cut.**

## ⚠️⚠️ The workaround for a load-bearing wall that must carry services

**«По всем СНиПам и нормам строительным данную стену несущую запрещено как-либо
повреждать, то есть резать.»** His answer is not to route around it but to **thicken
it with aerated block bonded to its face**, then chase the services into the added
skin:

> **«Важно не просто возвести рядом отдельную стену — важно именно приклеить к этой стене сам блок.»** The reason is specific: at the very bottom, cutting the chase for the sewer means cutting **almost all the way through** the added skin — so an unbonded skin would collapse. — Василий

- **Notched trowel stepped up to 10 mm**, adhesive applied **to the wall as well as the block**.
- **Substrate prep**: a white deposit («я не знаю, что это было») ground off with a **diamond cup wheel**, then primed — «чтобы он сильно не впитывался и адгезия… произошла максимальная».
- **⚠️ And note what is deliberately NOT done here: no level or plumb chasing.** «В данном случае нет никакой необходимости — мы просто повторяем плоскость вот этой стены, только уже более лёгким материалом, который разрешено будет резать.» **The skin's job is to be cuttable, not flat.**

**This is the direct answer to a problem this vault already records as a blocker** —
see the monolithic-wall-cannot-be-chased finding on
[[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Rough_Plumbing_Sequencing|Rough Plumbing Sequencing]].

## Closing the gap, and QC

- **The gap is left at the walls AND at the ceiling** — «газоблок и бетон по-разному расширяются, по-разному двигаются; они должны жить независимо друг от друга, поэтому никакой жёсткой привязки нет».
- **⚠️ Wet the gap with plain water before foaming** — and not soaking wet: «прямо на мокрый наносить не надо», let it draw in slightly first.
- **⚠️ Start at the ceiling**, because the can's liquid settles downward and runs out — so the least accessible, most important run is done while there is still propellant.
- Once cured the foam is trimmed. It **expands into a wedge («встаёт в распор»)** so the partition is rigid — «в жизни не упадёт» — **while the damper layer survives**, so «внешнее давление не будет передаваться друг на друга».
- **QC: a 3 m straightedge laid diagonally, corner to corner.** Result «практически идеально», and he notes this is still **черновая кладка** to be plastered.
