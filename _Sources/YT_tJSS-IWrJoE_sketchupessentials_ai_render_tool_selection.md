## ⚠️ The One Criterion Worth Keeping

**The selection axis for an image model used in rendering is NOT single-image quality — it is CONSISTENCY ACROSS EDITS.**

> The two front-runners are preferred *"because you can do things to these images and it will retain the actual image that you had in there before"* — move the camera, change a material — and *"generally they remember what was in the previous image."*

**His worked reason is the part that generalises, and it is a real requirement rather than a preference:**

> *"You can't have it forgetting that this fireplace was stone and making it out of brick the next time. You can't have that happening."*

- **→ For any iterative visual work, the property that matters is whether the tool holds everything you did NOT ask it to change.** A model that produces a better single image but re-invents the unmentioned details is useless for a sequence of views of one room.
- **⚠️ This is the visual-domain form of a principle this vault already holds in prompting** — [[_Sources/YT_la8Ml1fQfOg_urbandecoders_claude_for_architects|la8Ml1fQfOg]]'s rule to *tell the model what to change **and what to protect***. **Here the protection has to come from the tool rather than the prompt**, because a render has far more unmentioned detail than a prompt can enumerate.
