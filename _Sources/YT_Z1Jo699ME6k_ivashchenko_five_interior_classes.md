## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE METHODOLOGICAL FINDING — scope is a third axis, and this vault nearly had a trap

**The previous batch recorded [[_Sources/YT_uE8xWD07ThI_remonthochu_moscow_tiers_2025|РемонтХочу's]] Moscow tiers for 2025 at 20,000 / 30,000 / 45,000 RUB per m² — for ОТДЕЛКА, finishing only.**

**This source gives Moscow figures of 100,000 / 200,000 / 400,000 / 700,000 / 1,500,000+ RUB per m² — for the WHOLE INTERIOR.** He is explicit about what that includes:

> ***«Это полностью готовый интерьер, вообще полностью. То есть от бетона до включая кружки IKEA»*** — with the dishwasher, the washing machine, curtains, switches, sockets, textiles, rugs, decor and picture frames.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THESE ARE NOT COMPARABLE, AND THE DIFFERENCE IS NEITHER LOCATION NOR YEAR — IT IS SCOPE.** **A reader setting the two ladders side by side would conclude Moscow renovation quintupled in twelve months. It did not; they are measuring different things.**
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ STANDING RULE 2 REQUIRES LOCATION AND YEAR BEFORE TWO FIGURES MAY BE COMPARED. This pair satisfies both and is still incomparable.** **The two axes are necessary and not sufficient: a per-m² renovation figure also needs its SCOPE stated — finishing only, or turnkey including furnishing.**
>
> ⚠️ **And once scope IS stated the two reconcile rather than conflict: finishing at ~20,000 plus furniture, appliances, textiles and decor plausibly reaches ~100,000. Each is probably right about its own basis.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. The ladder — Moscow, projected for 2026, whole interior per m²

May include wall-hung sanitaryware, concealed mixers, recessed lights, shadow skirting, hidden doors. **Comparable to a 3–4★ chain hotel.** |

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Two unusually candid admissions

1. **⚠️⚠️ Class 2 can look identical to class 4.** *«Он может выглядеть абсолютно так же, как интерьер четвёртого класса на самом деле, но НА ОЩУПЬ будет другой.»* **→ The visible difference between a 200,000 and a 700,000 interior may be nil; the difference is tactile and in what is behind the surface.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ And what class 4 actually buys, said plainly by someone selling it:** *«Визуально она может даже примерно так же выглядеть, но людям, которые заказывают интерьеры такого класса, важно ЧУВСТВОВАТЬ, что эта мебель итальянская.»* **→ Part of the class-4 premium is the buyer's need to know the provenance, not a visible difference. A contractor conceding that is worth recording.**

> **→ Together these sharpen what [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Budget_Tiers_Cheap_Optimal_Premium|the tiers page]] already holds from РемонтХочу — that a tier describes SPEND rather than workmanship.** **This adds: above class 2, a growing share of the spend buys things that are invisible (class 3, comfort engineering) or known rather than seen (class 4, provenance).**

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. His method — set the budget FIRST, and have the builders price the design as it is drawn

**The failure he opens with:** an architect designs without regard to budget → you get beautiful renders and boast about them → the builders' and furniture-makers' estimates then arrive → **and worst, an interior built by whoever was cheapest starts falling apart after handover.**

**His counter-sequence:**

1. **Fix the interior BUDGET first.**
2. Use the class descriptions to know roughly what that budget looks like.
3. **⚠️⚠️ Take that sum TO the architect and check the project against it** — rather than receiving a design and discovering its cost afterwards.
4. **⚠️⚠️ Best of all: assemble the delivery team in advance so the people who will BUILD and FURNISH it price the project while it is being designed.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE SAME STRUCTURAL CLAIM AS [[_Sources/YT_uE8xWD07ThI_remonthochu_moscow_tiers_2025|РемонтХочу's]] "a fixed price is downstream of a finished design set", but INVERTED and arguably stronger.** **РемонтХочу says: finish the design so the price can be fixed. This says: fix the budget so the design can be constrained, and let the builders price it during design rather than after.** **Two Moscow contractors, opposite directions of travel, same underlying point — that design and price must be settled together rather than in sequence.**
>
> ⚠️ **He is selling design-and-build, which is exactly the arrangement step 4 describes. Flagged.**
