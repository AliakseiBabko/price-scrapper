## What held up

- **Electric towel rail, one year on: works, and the owners report no noticeable electricity cost** — «никаких излишних платежей за электричество здесь не возникает, потребление достаточно мало». His conclusion: «не стоит бояться» electric rails. Owner-reported rather than metered, so it is testimony, not measurement — but it is testimony from a year of use rather than a prediction.
- **The access hatch still opens easily, and behind it: no drips, no staining, no leaks — «чисто и сухо».** Dusty inside, because nobody looks in there often.
- **Wall tile intact, nothing cracked — and he attributes the internal corners specifically to using silicone grout there** «как раз для того, чтобы она не потрескалась». A technique with a one-year outcome attached to it, which is rarer than the technique itself.
- **Cast-marble («литьевой мрамор») shower tray, used daily for a year, «как новое»: it has not sagged or settled, and the sealant has not lifted.** Glass enclosure still slides easily and is easy to keep clean.
- **The wall-mounted mixer with an integral shelf gets used as a shelf during showering** — a small piece of validated ergonomics.
- **The second mixer works «одним пальчиком» after a year** — he credits the manufacturer rather than himself, twice, without naming a price.

## ⚠️ What failed — five findings, and none of them are about workmanship

**1. The WC pan flushes badly, and it is a selection failure, not an installation one.** Water runs, everything looks right, «но по факту плохо смывается» — the bowl retains solids because of its bowl form and casting. **The household's remedy is a bin for toilet paper**, which he regards as wrong in a flat («канализационная сеть справляется») and which smells.

> **His rule, given to all his clients as a result: before buying a pan, watch video reviews or look at one in the flesh — specifically at how the bowl and its chute actually work, not at how it looks.** — Василий

**2. ⚠️ The mirror broke the hygienic shower, and the mechanism is a rough-stage interlock meeting a late-stage purchase.** He normally **wires the hygienic shower's water supply to switch with the main lighting** — so the valve is only live when the room light is on. The owners then chose, **after the renovation was finished**, a mirror with many built-in lamps, which they now use as the room's mood lighting. **With only the mirror lit, the hygienic shower does not work.** «Не учли тот момент, что будет подобное зеркало.»

> **The generalisable failure: an item chosen after handover can silently disable an interlock decided at rough stage.** Nothing was installed badly; the two decisions were simply made months apart by different people.

**3. The underfloor-heating thermostat failed three times in one year.** The **heating cable itself is fine** — «проблема не с нагревательным кабелем, он сейчас исправно работает». Each replacement was bought **from the same shop as the original**, which he himself raises as the likely explanation: «возможно, из той же партии». **The purchasing lesson is his, not mine: replacing a failed component from the same source risks the same batch.**

**4. Storage was under-provisioned even though it was planned.** There is a large vanity, seasonal storage, and a small unit added into a corner — and the owners still say it is not enough. **His retrospective fix: run the false wall higher and form more niches in it, the way the shower niche was done**, or add a furniture unit now.

**5. The basin sits too high and is slightly uncomfortable to use.** He puts this down to fixture selection — «вопрос о выборе» — rather than to the vanity height as installed.

## Dimensions

- **Shower 90 × 90 cm, with a niche formed inside it.** Owner feedback: **a little tight**, and the niche is already full of bottles. **His judgement: 90 × 90 is the minimum size that is comfortable to use**, and the niche is what makes it work.
- ⚠️ A room dimension is spoken but the ASR is unrecoverable («помещение здесь 170 наш что 70») — **not used.**

## His own closing conclusions

1. **Don't be afraid to build false walls in order to get concealed niches** — «это действительно помогает в организации свободного пространства в санузле». Note that this is the recommendation of someone who has just been told the storage is still insufficient, so read it as *build more of them*, not as *they solved it*.
2. **Pay particular attention when buying the finish fixtures — consider their size, their appearance, and how convenient they will actually be to use.** Two of the year's five defects were purchases, not work.
3. **It looks new because it is looked after** — maintenance is part of the outcome.
