## Materials / Rules — Genuinely New Component Details

- **⚠️ "Duplicating" (дублирующие) shutoff valves installed downstream of a developer's own existing riser valves, specifically to avoid re-welding/re-soldering the riser connection**: rather than removing/replacing the developer-installed shutoff at the riser (which would require cutting into and re-welding/re-soldering the existing riser pipe — the material the original valve is fused to), this company adds its own additional, better-quality shutoff valves just downstream of the developer's valves, leaving the original riser connection completely untouched. The added valves are specifically chosen for **long-term easy operability** — they don't seize/gum up with mineral buildup over time the way the underlying developer valves might — while avoiding the extra invasive plumbing work re-welding would require. A genuinely new named installation technique for this store, distinct from the existing "Антроп"-brand light-operation valve detail already recorded from `Zl_fegEg7yY` (that entry was about *which valve product* to use; this one is about *where/how to add a valve without modifying existing risers*).
