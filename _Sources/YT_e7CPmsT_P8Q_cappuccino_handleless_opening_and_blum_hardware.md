## ⚠️⚠️ 1. FOUR WAYS TO OPEN A FRONT WITHOUT A HANDLE — and the deciding difference is what your fingers touch

### TIP-ON (mechanical push-to-open, spring)

**Pros**: a monolithic kitchen unencumbered by handles; smooth travel and fast response; **universal — works on hinged fronts, drawers AND lift mechanisms.**

**⚠️ Cons, and they are specific:**
- **A 4 mm gap between front and cabinet side is a technical necessity of the mechanism, and it is very visible from the side.** ⚠️ Their workaround is **overlay side panels (накладные боковые стойки)** that close the slot off.
- **⚠️ For mechanisms wider than 60 cm a SYNCHRONISER is required, which increases the cost.**
- **Breaks quickly if used roughly.**
- **⚠️⚠️ *"Частое соприкосновение с лицевой частью фасада приводит к загрязнению и истиранию лакокрасочного покрытия."*** **→ You press the FACE of the front every time, so the face gets dirty and the paint/lacquer abrades.**

> **⚠️⚠️ THAT LAST POINT IS THE DECIDING ONE, AND IT CORROBORATES THIS VAULT DIRECTLY.** [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]] already carries a section titled *"Handles — the mechanism behind the push-to-open objection."* **Here is a fabricator arriving at the same objection, and pairing it with the wear mechanism on the coating.** ⚠️ **And it interacts with the MDF finding in [[_Sources/YT_sGuFVS5Ze7I_cappuccino_painted_vs_laminated_mdf|the fronts note]]: painted MDF's lacquer wears through where it is repeatedly wiped in one place. Push-to-open puts the wear exactly there.**

### J-HANDLE (integrated milled groove)

A groove milled into the edge of an MDF front — **opens with no expensive mechanism at all.** Can take various shapes, can run the full width, **can be painted in two colours.**

- **⚠️⚠️ Used on MDF from 19 to 25 mm. At 19 mm the groove is shallow, and *"девушка с длинными ногтями чувствует определённый дискомфорт"*.** **→ A concrete ergonomic threshold. His recommendation is unambiguous: choose the 25 mm front, it costs more and gives incomparably better comfort.**
- ⚠️ **Unaesthetic on tall units (пеналы) and upper fronts.**
- **⚠️ Do not put a J-handle on the dish-drainer module**, because an **AVENTOS HF** usually goes there and such a front becomes awkward to open.
- **⚠️⚠️ And it cannot be done in laminated MDF at all** — see [[_Sources/YT_sGuFVS5Ze7I_cappuccino_painted_vs_laminated_mdf|the fronts note]] §Design. **The opening method constrains the front material.**

### GOLA PROFILE (aluminium channel)

Fixed to the carcass, giving a finger slot between fronts. Aluminium, stainless-look and other metal colours; **powder-coatable to any RAL.**

- **⚠️⚠️ Two real advantages over TIP-ON: no expensive mechanism and NO SYNCHRONISER even beyond 60 cm, and — the one he calls the main one — *"не пачкается лицевая часть фасада."*** **The face stays clean because you never touch it.**
- **⚠️ Cons are dimensional: the profile is 7 cm high and that space is unusable.**
- **⚠️⚠️ AND THE CONSEQUENCE THAT MATTERS MOST: *"если в вашем проекте есть посудомоечная машина, то минимальная высота столешницы у вас будет 92 сантиметра."*** **→ GOLA + DISHWASHER FORCES A WORKTOP HEIGHT OF AT LEAST 920 mm.**
  > **⚠️⚠️ This is directly checkable against our own model and against [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Layout_Sizing_and_Ergonomics|Layout, Cabinet Sizing and Ergonomics]].** **A handle choice propagates into worktop height, which propagates into splashback height, upper-cabinet position and ergonomics for the actual cook.** **Route it as a constraint, not a preference.**

### SERVO-DRIVE (Blum, electric)

Like TIP-ON but opening is by a **programmable servo drive**; usable on drawers, lift mechanisms, **and also on the fridge and the dishwasher.**

- **⚠️ The con is stated plainly: it needs electricity. *"Если его нет, то пользоваться кухней достаточно проблематично."*** ⚠️ **A single-point-of-failure note worth keeping for a flat where the kitchen must work during an outage.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. AVENTOS LIFT MECHANISMS — the selection rule is WHAT IS ABOVE THE CABINET

**This is the most directly usable table in the round.** All figures are his, from the Blum 2020 range.

| Mechanism | Fronts | Cabinet envelope | Max load | ⚠️ What is above it |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | ---: | :--- |
| **HF** | **two (bi-fold), one sweep** | 48–104 cm h × ≤180 cm w | **20 kg** (→ **27 kg** with a second power unit + arm) | **Ceiling OK** — with optional **103° / 83° limiters**, *"чаще всего для кухонь в потолок либо с антресолями, чтобы не повредить фасады"* |
| **HS** | one, opens **over the carcass** | 35–80 cm h × ≤180 cm w | 20 kg | **⚠️⚠️ NOTHING may be above it — explicitly NOT for ceiling-height kitchens or antresols** |
| **HL** | one, **vertical parallel lift** | 30–58 cm h × ≤180 cm w | 20 kg | **For cabinets that HAVE cabinets above**; ⚠️ **not recommended with antresols** |
| **HK top** (2020) | one, up | 20.5–60 cm h × ≤180 cm w | **18 kg** | **Needs very little space above → suits BOTH ceiling kitchens and antresols**; limiter **built in, 75–105°, screwdriver-adjustable** |
| **HK-S** | one, up | ≤60 cm h × ≤90 cm w | **18 kg** | **Antresol section, or the cabinet over the fridge**; max **107°**, optional 75°/100° limiters |
| **HK-XS** | one, up | 24–60 cm h × ≤90 cm w, **min depth 10 cm** | — | Smallest; ⚠️ **an excellent replacement for a GAS STRUT, with the difference that it fixes at ANY position** |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE SELECTION RULE IS NOT ABOUT THE DOOR, IT IS ABOUT THE CEILING.** HS needs clear space above and is therefore incompatible with the ceiling-height kitchen this vault covers at length; HF works there but only with limiters fitted; HK top works either way. **Choosing a ceiling-height kitchen silently removes HS from the option set.**

**Also worth keeping:**
- **HF is the best option for the dish-drainer cabinet**, and takes **asymmetric fronts**; kit includes **4 CLIP top hinges, two of them 120°**.
- **HS needs no hinges at all.**
- **All AVENTOS ship with soft-close as standard**; Servo-Drive can be added to any; **⚠️ TIP-ON can be fitted to HK top specifically, unlike the others.**
- **⚠️ A safety mechanism on HF Servo-Drive**: closing it on your fingers will not pinch, because **the internal hinges unclip**; re-seat by pressing in the centre.
- **Fronts may be wooden or aluminium-framed**; HK-S also takes **thin fronts**.
- ⚠️ *"Правильно рассчитывайте вес фасада"* — **the load limits are against the FRONT's weight, which a stone or thick front will approach.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. BLUM DRAWER RUNNERS — a load ladder, and two dimensional costs

| Runner | Load | Side height | ⚠️ Notes |
| :--- | ---: | :--- | :--- |
| **METABOX** | **25 kg** | 54–150 mm | ⚠️ **Budget. *"Работают достаточно громко и не так плавно доводят."* Cream and grey only. Use for drawers you rarely open.** |
| **TANDEM** | 30 / 50 kg | adjustable | Box from **any carcass material**; **undermount**; partial or full extension; TIP-ON possible (a no-soft-close version exists) |
| **TANDEMBOX antaro / plus** | 30 / 65 kg | 68–115 mm | **Soft-close only**, even when push-to-open; antaro straight-topped, plus rounded; ORGA-LINE inserts; 5 colours |
| **MOVENTO** | 40 / 60 kg | adjustable | **No non-soft-close version**; TIP-ON opens, **BLUMOTION** closes |
| **LEGRABOX** | **up to 70 kg** | **66–241 mm** | Top line. 4 colours. **PURE** = monolithic side; **FREE** = takes a ≤10 mm insert, usually glass. **Fixes to thin fronts.** |

**⚠️⚠️ Three dimensional costs, and they are the transferable part:**

1. **TANDEM loses ~43 mm of useful internal space** to its mounting geometry.
2. **⚠️ TANDEMBOX's rounded inner wall costs almost 10 cm of useful space.**
3. **⚠️⚠️ CARGO (pull-out bottle/spice unit): minimum front width 20 cm, *"при меньшем размере внутренняя часть будет просто бесполезной."* And at that 20 cm front — on Movento the useful interior is only 11.5 cm; on LEGRABOX, whose walls are just 10 mm, it is over 13 cm.**
   > **⚠️⚠️ This lands squarely on a figure this vault already holds** — [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]] records a *"pull-out spice/bottle rack, typical width 15–20 cm."* **Now the usable interior behind that width is known, and it is roughly half of it.** **A 200 mm cargo is a 115–130 mm shelf.**

**Width and stability limits:**
- **⚠️ TANDEM: he recommends no wider than 90 cm, and a SYNCHRONISER is mandatory.**
- **⚠️ MOVENTO's higher load allows fronts up to 140 cm, but a STABILISER must be fitted.**

**Serviceability and systems:**
- **TANDEM fixings come as screws or LOCKS — the locks let you unclip and fully remove a runner** if something falls behind the drawer. ⚠️ A real maintenance affordance.
- **⚠️ Tandem and Movento are the choice if the runner should match the carcass colour**, because the box is made of carcass material.
- **SPACE CORNER** for corner kitchens, shown on antaro with **SYNCROMOTION**.
- **SPACE TOWER**: 4 × Legrabox free at 177 mm + 1 × Legrabox pure at 90 mm, with **155° hinges** so drawers withdraw unobstructed; configured for a **60 cm wide × 2 m high** cabinet, extendable. ⚠️ He frames it with *"зона хранения запасов — основная стратегическая точка в кухонном треугольнике."*
- **⚠️⚠️ SPACE STEP** (a step in the plinth, for reaching upper cabinets): **minimum plinth height 15 cm, but he recommends 20–25 cm.**
  > **⚠️ This vault already records a toe-kick pull-out step as a technique** ([[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]]); **now it has a minimum dimension, and that dimension competes with the 920 mm worktop height the gola profile forces (§1). Plinth height, worktop height and opening method are one coupled decision.**
- A 2020 addition: **a fixator for a pull-out shelf** that locks it at full extension so it cannot slide back.
