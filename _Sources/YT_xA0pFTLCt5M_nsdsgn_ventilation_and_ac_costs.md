## ⚠️ The framing, which is the reason to care

«Вентиляция напрямую влияет на качество жизни, но очень часто о ней забывают и вспоминают в самый последний момент ремонта. **Но это может полностью изменить дизайн вашей квартиры, и что ещё важнее — очень сильно увеличивает бюджет на ремонт.**»

## System types, with the trade-off each carries

**1. Естественная (natural)**

- Open windows; **cross-ventilation is faster where the flat has openings on two opposite sides.**
- Modern buildings have **КИВ (клапан инфильтрации воздуха)** wall valves; windows can carry valves too.
- Extraction via **passive vent channels**, driven by the pressure difference at the two ends — «работает это обычно так себе, но зато бесплатно». A fan is often fitted to run on demand when cooking or showering.
- **⚠️ If the fan works badly or not at all: raised humidity, raised contamination, and «чаще всего это кончается плесенью».**
- **⚠️ In many buildings it is forbidden to connect a cooker hood into the passive ventilation channels — and many neighbours ignore the ban, «и это всё кончается тем, что вы чувствуете запах их пищи или, ещё хуже, никотина».** A concrete building-shared-system problem that no amount of work inside your own flat fixes.
- **The balance rule: the volume entering through КИВ equals the volume leaving through the channels.** Control is purely mechanical — open or closed, entering or not entering.
- **⚠️ And the reason people defeat it: air through КИВ arrives at street temperature and street contamination.** He has repeatedly seen valves and КИВ **simply bricked up** so nothing blows in.
- **Required by building norms in every flat, costs nothing, and never breaks.** Its minus is **street noise and dust, and cold in winter** — a real problem for allergy sufferers and anyone who wants quiet.

**2. Механическая (mechanical extract)**

- One mechanical system serving all the vent channels, so no fan is needed in each flat; air leaves and is replaced through КИВ and valves.
- **⚠️ The acceptance test, and it is the most quotable line in the source: when accepting a new flat, hold a sheet of paper against the ventilation grille. «Если прилип — то вентиляция отличная.»**
- Supply can be mechanised with **бризеры**, fitted onto an existing КИВ or a newly cut channel. The machine delivers cleaned, tempered street air and **the flow rate becomes controllable — but the volume is limited by physics and by the КИВ diameter, so a large flat needs several machines.**
- **⚠️ Бризер-based ventilation is the cheapest of the supply-and-extract options, and the cost is stated honestly: «надо быть готовым к тому, что у вас под окном появится довольно объёмная машина, которая при этом будет ещё очень шумная».**

**3. Принудительная приточно-вытяжная (forced supply and extract)**

- The most technically complex. **«Съест не только большую часть вашего бюджета, но ещё и солидную часть высоты ваших потолков.»**
- One or more units depending on the flat's volume; street air is cleaned, heated and distributed by ducts; dirty air is extracted and expelled.
- **Рекуперация**: the exhaust gives its heat to the incoming air, so **ventilation participates in heating and saves electricity. All modern energy-efficient buildings are equipped with recuperation.**
- On expensive objects clients often add **humidification**; ionisation blocks also exist.
- **⚠️ The use case that justifies it, quoted from a real client at a first meeting: «Я хочу, чтобы в моей квартире никогда не открывались окна, чтобы в неё попадал только чистый воздух.»**

## ⚠️ Ceiling space consumed — the numbers that close Round 1's loop

- **Ventilation ducts most often take 150–200 mm of ceiling void.**
- **If two ducts cross one above the other, you get a double drop.**
- **Ventilation machines take more still — a minimum of 300 mm.**
- **Duct cross-section grows with run length, and increases nearer the machine** — so the void requirement is not uniform across the flat.
- **⚠️ THE SEQUENCING TRAP, stated explicitly, and it is the exact complement to Round 1's ceiling source: ventilation ducts are installed FIRST on site. You must check how much ceiling void remains BELOW them — because the lighting arrives AFTER the ceilings are installed, and «очень часто этого пространства не хватает для установки лампочек или треков».**
- **Equipment is normally placed where ceiling height and extra noise matter less: bathrooms, storerooms, walk-in wardrobes**, with the ducts fanning out to the living rooms.
- **⚠️ A trick to avoid eating the ceiling in a habitable room: put the grille directly in the WALL and bring the duct to it across the ceiling of the ADJACENT room.**

## ⚠️ Diffusers — and independent corroboration of the combined slot

- **Ordinary grilles are what ventilation contractors offer by default on budget projects, and the consequence is stated bluntly: «такая решётка сразу же превратит ваш интерьер в подсобное помещение эконом-класса».**
- **Щелевые (slot) grilles are his preference**, available with different slot counts according to air volume — **including versions that are plastered flush into the wall so no joint shows and only the slots remain.**
- **⚠️ «Сейчас даже есть решение, когда щель вентиляции стыкуется с шинопроводом» — the slot diffuser docking with a track/busbar. This is the same combined slot Round 1's ceiling installer demonstrated in the manufacturer's showroom, described here independently from the designer's side.** Two sources, two roles, same product.
- **For classical interiors: perforation in the gypsum cornice instead of a grille** — «идеально подходит, когда любая современная деталь испортит всю стилистику».
- His summary: the diffuser choice follows from the interior, and the wrong grille can «убить весь интерьер».

## Ventilation versus air conditioning

- **Ventilation supplies fresh air. Conditioning cools, and sometimes heats.**
- **⚠️ «Самое большое заблуждение — думать, что из кондиционера поступает свежий воздух. Ему там просто неоткуда взяться.»**
- Expensive ventilation systems may carry conditioning, ionisation and humidification blocks alongside the ventilation block, which creates the impression of one machine.

## AC placement — real constraints, one with a real cost

- **⚠️ The outdoor unit rejects as much heat as the cold delivered inside.** So installing it on your own balcony creates «маленький филиал Дубая» — «останется только насыпать песочек и привязать верблюда».
- Hence outdoor units often go on the facade — **but this must be agreed with the управляющая компания.** Modern buildings frequently provide **baskets under the windows** or a dedicated **сплит-шахта** where all the outdoor units sit.
- Roof mounting requires a flat on the upper floors.
- **⚠️ A worked constraint with a real cost: a flat on the 2nd floor of a ~12-storey building required a very expensive outdoor unit to pump the whole freon line — «это прямо обошлось очень дорого, но другого варианта не было».** So **vertical position in the building is a cost driver, not just a logistics detail.**
- **⚠️ And a legal one: if the building is on a central street the facade is likely protected by law and you will simply not be permitted an outdoor unit on it.**

**Indoor unit types, ranked as he gives them:**

- **Wall-mounted** — the default; disliked for appearance, and the mitigations are to hide it in furniture or paint it.
- **Floor-ceiling units** — more elongated, wall- or under-ceiling-mounted; **he has encountered them «буквально один или два раза».**
- **Cassette** — the large centre-of-ceiling unit familiar from restaurants and offices; **«на порядок больше», an order of magnitude dearer than a wall unit, and really intended for industrial spaces.**
- **⚠️ The most aesthetic option: hide a ducted indoor unit above the ceiling and distribute the air via the vent channels.** Dearer and it eats ceiling void, **but it can be integrated with the ventilation system.**

## ⚠️ Process rules — when to start and who does what

- **«Ни в коем случае не надо ждать, когда окончится ваш дизайн-проект. Как только закончен первый этап и у вас есть утверждённая планировка — сразу же передавайте её проектировщикам вентиляции.»**
- **Ask your designer to curate the ventilation and AC project.** The stated reason is collision: **ventilation directly affects ceiling level changes, and intersects with lamps, diffusers and service hatches — «может в самый последний момент всё испортить».**
- **⚠️ There is no typical solution: «типового решения для системы вентиляции и кондиционирования не существует, и каждый раз под каждый интерьер нужно разрабатывать свою систему».**
- **The ventilation designer must be met ON SITE**, and must **also talk to the управляющая компания to obtain the building's engineering parameters.** Otherwise **«можно выйти за рамки возможностей или даже закона»**, and an expensive system will either work incorrectly or, worst case, **be dismantled.**
- **⚠️ The division of labour he states, and it corroborates the designer-as-generalist finding running through this channel: «дизайнер может только в общих чертах описать, какие бывают варианты. Проработкой должен обязательно заниматься профессионал. Дизайн и вентиляция хоть и связаны, но являются совершенно разными отдельными проектами.»**
- **And the reason the designer must still check every detail: «для проектировщика вентиляции самое главное, чтобы всё работало правильно, и он чаще всего не обращает внимания на всё, что связано с дизайном».**

## Maintenance and noise

- **⚠️ Annual technical servicing is required, and it has a design cost: access hatches in the stretch ceiling or plasterboard, and «это почти всегда портит дизайн и общее впечатление».**
- **Where aesthetics do not matter — a boiler room or laundry — use a demountable ceiling instead: Armstrong or грильято.**
- **⚠️ Noise as a diagnostic, stated as a flat rule: «если вы слышите шум вентиляции, это значит только одно — что вентиляция спроектирована неправильно».** Two mechanisms named: **air volume forced into a smaller diameter than the design requires**, and **excess bends.**
- **⚠️ Cross-talk between rooms, which is a privacy failure rather than a comfort one: one duct snaking into several rooms means the occupants hear each other** — «помните в детстве баночки с верёвочкой». **Real case: a neighbour complained that he could hear the flat's owner talking, through the kitchen extract.**

## ⚠️ Ventilation contractors — the failure modes, from the designer's side

**«Очень часто взаимодействие с вентиляционщиками похоже на военные действия.»**

- **⚠️ The root cause named: «в погоне за самыми бюджетными решениями заказчики часто находят специалистов, которые работали только с промышленными помещениями».** They promise it will be fine, «100 раз сделали эту вентиляцию» — **but they do not account for a private client's raised requirements and do not communicate with the designers or the builders.**
  **This is precisely the risk Round 1's builder described from the opposite side, having watched such a crew cut a duct on the kitchen island at final-finish stage — see [[YT_M0Wv4SOSUhs_nsdsgn_builder_qa_costs_and_estimates]]. Two independent accounts, two roles, same failure class.**
- **On a private project, especially a large one, there is a strict order of operations and «не получится один раз приехать, сделать работу и уехать».**
- **⚠️ Failure mode 1: the crew installs its equipment and leaves. Once the finishing works are done the diffusers must be fitted and commissioning performed — and the crew has vanished and cannot be found. New ones must be found: «это время, деньги, нервы».**
- **⚠️ Failure mode 2, the most concrete: they installed the air conditioning and forgot to connect the indoor unit's condensate drain into the sewer, then left. By the time they returned everything was closed up and tiled, and the drain is «где-то там, никто не знает где». Everything must be broken open and redone.**
- **Advice: find them by recommendation from builders, designers, or friends with positive experience.**
