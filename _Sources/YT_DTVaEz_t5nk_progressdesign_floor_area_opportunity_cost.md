## ⚠️⚠️ 1. THE METHOD — and it generalises well beyond his product

**His framing: the real comparison for transformer furniture is not against other furniture, it is against BUYING THE FLOOR AREA that conventional furniture would occupy.**

| Step | His figures |
| :--- | :--- |
| A separate sofa | **1.44 m²** |
| A separate stationary double bed | **2.8 m²** |
| **Both, permanently** | **4.24 m²** |
| A wall bed, folded, by day | **1.44 m² — the same as the sofa alone** |
| **Daytime area recovered** | **2.8 m²** |
| Moscow secondary-market rate | **325,000 RUB/m²** (≈ **$3,500**) |
| **Area valued at that rate** | **910,000 RUB** (≈ **$9,900**) |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE METHOD IS GENUINELY USEFUL AND THIS VAULT HAS NOTHING LIKE IT.** Every budget page here prices *what you buy*. **This prices what you would otherwise have had to buy, in the one unit a flat is actually sold in.** **It turns "is fitted furniture worth it" into an arithmetic instead of a taste argument, and the same method transfers to a walk-in closet, a boot room, a second bathroom — anything that consumes permanent floor area.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. THE ARITHMETIC AS PRESENTED IS A SALES ARGUMENT — three corrections before reuse

**Recorded because the method is worth keeping and the presentation is not.**

1. **⚠️⚠️ HE NEVER SUBTRACTS THE COST OF THE FURNITURE.** The 910,000 RUB is stated as *«почти миллион рублей, которые вы не тратите»* — but the transformer unit is not free. **[[_Sources/YT_jZAAwUZVb0I_progressdesign_spec_and_cost_structure|The same firm's own price walkthrough]] puts a bed-plus-sofa at 115,000 RUB, plus 10% assembly.** **The honest form of his own claim is a NET figure, and it still favours him — which makes the omission gratuitous rather than necessary.**
2. **⚠️⚠️ FLOOR AREA IS NOT PURCHASABLE IN 2.8 m² INCREMENTS.** You cannot buy 2.8 m²; you buy a differently-sized flat, in whatever steps the market offers. **The 910,000 is an OPPORTUNITY-COST VALUATION, not a saving that appears anywhere — no money moves.** **It is a legitimate way to value space and an illegitimate way to describe a cash saving, and he describes it as the latter.**
3. **⚠️ THE 2.8 m² IS RECOVERED BY DAY ONLY.** At night the bed occupies it again. **[[_Sources/YT_cZNP6TaUyCU_freedom_wallbed_installation_and_two_layouts|A competing maker states this limit plainly]]** — deployed, *«места больше нет»*. **His comparison is against furniture that occupies the area 24 hours; the fair statement is that the device converts a permanent cost into a nightly one.**

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ RECORDED AS: the METHOD is transferable and the WORKED EXAMPLE is not.** **Reuse the approach — value recovered floor area at the local per-m² rate, then subtract what the joinery costs — and re-derive the numbers.**

## ⚠️ 4. The 26 m² worked case

**A whole flat in 26 m², built around a 160×200 wall bed:**

- **Light palette chosen deliberately for the size** — cream sofa, beige carcass, white fronts.
- **⚠️ A PODIUM WITH NICHES AT THE HEAD of the bed** for a book or phone — *«не занимают лишнего места»*. **→ The bedside-table function without a bedside table's footprint; relevant because a wall bed leaves nowhere obvious for one.**
- Storage with **separate rails for long and short clothing**; **antresols on Push-to-Open**, no handles.
- **⚠️ A large mirror at the entrance**, for checking your appearance and to widen the room visually.
- The kitchen corner sits immediately behind the filming position — i.e. all of it inside 26 m².

> **→ ⚠️ Corroborates this vault's small-space material in direction rather than adding a principle. The podium-with-niches is the one detail worth carrying.**
