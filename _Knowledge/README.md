# `_Knowledge/` — Intermediate Knowledge Store

The accumulation layer between individual source notes (`_Sources/`) and the
reader-facing wiki pages in the numbered folders. Facts land here as they are
extracted, are reconciled across sources, and are then synthesised into the
room/systems pages.

## Layout

- **`store/`** — the main budgeting-and-technique store, split by kind:
  `Durable_Facts.md`, `Rules_Heuristics.md`, `Numeric_Data.md`,
  `Planning_Rules.md`, `Source_Index.md` (one entry per processed source),
  `Cross_Source_Comparison_Tables.md`, `Advertising_Promotional_Notes.md`,
  `Pending_Wiki_Page_Decisions.md`, `Change_Log.md`, and `_index.md` as the entry
  point. Open only the bucket a source actually touches.
- **`renovation_regulations_belarus_knowledge_store.md`** — a separate store with
  a **stricter evidence bar**: a jurisdictional fact must name its location in the
  source's own content (level 1), not merely via channel branding. Its synthesised
  counterpart is `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`.
- **`attribution_inventory_batch*.md`** — audit records from the 2026-08 inline-
  attribution pass.

## Why this is top-level

Same reasoning as `_Sources/`: this store accumulates facts for **every** topic in
the vault — plumbing, lighting, tiling, colour, legal — not just budgeting. It
lived under `11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/intermediate/store/`
until 2026-08-30 for historical reasons only.
