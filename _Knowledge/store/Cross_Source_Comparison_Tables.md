Per explicit user guidance: forcing all sources into one grand $/m² total
doesn't work well, since different sources measure different scopes
(labor-only vs. materials-bundled, construction-only vs. full-realization
with furniture). Instead, this section groups **comparable pieces of
information by topic** — the same stage, trade, or material category
across sources — so real overlaps and gaps are visible without pretending
incompatible totals are the same thing. Nothing here is averaged or
force-converted between currencies; figures are shown side by side with
their own units, and the reader draws the comparison.
