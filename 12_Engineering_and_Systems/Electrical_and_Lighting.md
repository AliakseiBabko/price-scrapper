# Electrical & Lighting - Do's and Don'ts

## Do's

| Rule | Applies To | Reason | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Plan furniture layout before final socket placement | All Rooms | Avoid sockets blocked by beds or cabinets | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_vid1_transcript_d04723c5.txt` |
| Install hidden in-wall HDMI channels and behind-TV sockets | Living Room, Bedrooms | Eliminates hanging TV wires between TV and console | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_tips_video_f23c504a.txt` |
| Use recessed curtain niche profiles with built-in LED channels | Living Room, Bedrooms | Hides curtain track mounts and provides sleek indirect lighting | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_tips_video_f23c504a.txt` |
| Use dedicated lines and individual breakers for high-power consumers | Kitchen, Laundry, Major Appliances | Prevents circuit overload and improves safety | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_tips_video_f23c504a.txt` |
| Procure lighting fixtures directly from overseas factory suppliers | All Rooms | Saves 2x-3x on lighting hardware compared to domestic retail markups | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_apartment_renovation_guide_360f4c7c.txt` |
| Plaster brick and block walls before chasing electrical cable slots | All Masonry Walls | Prevents hanging unanchored cables and avoids splitting electrical work into multiple messy phases | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_2_61e3a372.txt` |
| Leave generous extra length (1.5-2m) on cooktop hardwired power cable | Kitchen Work Zone | Avoids stretched diagonal wiring or hazardous splices if cooktop position shifts during kitchen assembly | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_3_a0e895b1.txt` |
| Position electrical outlets for motorized power-recliner sofas at floor level | Living Room | Prevents plug heads from pressing against sofa backrests and pushing sofa away from wall | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_3_a0e895b1.txt` |
| Project electrical back-boxes outward by planned acoustic insulation thickness on soundproofed walls | Acoustic Walls | Ensures electrical sockets finish perfectly flush with the finished soundproofed wall surface | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_4_21a6e3c1.txt` |
| Install a master cut-off switch near the main entrance door | Entrance, Apartment-wide | Single-button shutdown of apartment lighting upon exit | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_minsk_world_60m2_design_tour_26bc9c25.txt` |

| Install power outlets directly in window sills or window reveal surrounds | Living Room, Bedrooms | Provides convenient power access for laptops and seasonal LED window decorations | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_minsk_world_60m2_design_tour_26bc9c25.txt` |


## Don'ts

| Rule | Applies To | Risk | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Do not place standard sockets near wet areas without IP rating | Bathroom, WC, Kitchen Sink | Electrical hazard and short-circuit risk | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_vid1_transcript_d04723c5.txt` |
| Do not cut recessed electrical breaker niches into load-bearing monolithic walls | Hallway, Corridor | Violates structural building codes (use surface-mounted boxes in cabinets) | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_mistakes_video_21ade3f6.txt` |
| Do not install excessive breaker counts or cosmetic LED backlighting inside electrical panels | Hallway, Utility | Unnecessary expense for features that are rarely accessed | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_a8e90887.txt` |
| Do not pay labor premiums for purely decorative sub-screed cable patterns | All Floors | Cables are encased in screed; compliance with code without crossovers is sufficient | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_a8e90887.txt` |
| Do not puncture subfloor soundproofing membranes with mechanical anchors for electrical conduit clips | All Living Rooms | Ruptures acoustic insulation integrity (use specialized adhesive clips bonded to soundproofing layer) | `90_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_guide_mistakes_2_61e3a372.txt` |
