# HVAC — Humidification

Detail page for [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/HVAC_and_Ventilation|HVAC & Ventilation]].

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **⚠️⚠️ This page exists because a grep on 2026-09-14 found the word `увлажнение` in ZERO files across the whole of `12_Engineering_and_Systems/`.** The folder covered supply ventilation, breathers, air conditioning, condensate and heating in depth, **and said nothing about the humidity of the air any of it moves.**
>
> **⚠️ It is currently a ONE-SOURCE page**, and that source is a Moscow contractor who sells the installation. **Treat it as an agenda for a decision, not as a specification.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. The problem, with the only figures on offer

> ***«Зимой в квартирах влажность падает до 15% при норме 45–65.»***

Consequences he names:

- **Skin and mucous membranes** — the health argument.
- **⚠️⚠️ Wooden furniture cracks — *«гарантия снимается»*.** **A warranty consequence, not only a comfort one**, and it bears directly on any solid-wood or veneered specification in this flat.

> ⚠️ **The 45–65% band is a conventional indoor-comfort range and the vault holds it in a handful of source notes; the 15% winter figure is a Moscow observation from this contractor, not a measurement made here.** **→ The honest version for this project is: measure it in the flat over a winter before sizing anything.** **A hygrometer costs almost nothing and turns this whole page from assertion into data.**

## ⚠️ 2. The two routes

| | **Domestic units** | **Built-in systems** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cost** | cheap | *«дороже»* |
| **Effort** | ⚠️ **require regular maintenance and take floor space** | automatic — *«автоматически поддерживают влажность»* |
| **Types named** | — | **isothermal** or **adiabatic**; his firm uses **nozzle-type (форсуночные)** |

- **⚠️ He states no capacity figures, no water-treatment requirement and no maintenance interval for the built-in option** — all three of which are the questions that actually decide between them. **Recorded as a gap, not an omission to paper over.**
- ⚠️ **An adiabatic/nozzle system atomises water into the air stream, so the water it atomises ends up in the room.** **→ The water analysis on [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Water_Inlet_Node_Components|Water Inlet Node Components]] becomes a prerequisite rather than a nicety if this route is taken.** ⚠️ **That connection is this vault's inference, not his claim.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. What to do NOW even if the answer is "not yet"

**His practice is to reserve for it rather than decide it:** *«Мы всегда закладываем под них место в проекте и отдельные розетки»*, and in his closing rules — *«если вы сейчас не планируете делать увлажнение… всё равно заложите закладные трубы и дополнительные провода»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS THE ACTIONABLE PART, AND IT IS CHEAP: a dedicated socket, conduit and reserved space cost almost nothing at first fix and are unavailable afterwards.** **See [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Engineering_Design_Stage|Engineering Design Stage]] §3, where the same reserve pattern appears three times in one source.**

⚠️ **Market context, not a recommendation**: *«8 из 10 наших клиентов просят увлажнения»* — a Moscow bureau's client mix in 2026, and he calls it *«тренд 2026 года»*. **A trend statement from someone selling the trend.**

## ⚠️ Open questions before this becomes a decision

- **Measure the actual winter humidity in this flat** rather than adopting a Moscow figure.
- **Capacity, water quality requirement and maintenance interval** for a built-in system — none stated by this source.
- **Interaction with the supply ventilation already covered in [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Fresh_Air_Ventilation_and_Ducting|Fresh Air Ventilation and Ducting]]** — a system bringing in cold dry winter air is the thing creating the deficit, so the two are one problem.
- **A second source.** ⚠️ **One contractor who sells humidifiers is not enough to specify from.**
