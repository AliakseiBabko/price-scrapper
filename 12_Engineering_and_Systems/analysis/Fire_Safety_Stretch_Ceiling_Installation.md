## Three Housing Types Mentioned

Battery-powered, mains-wired (two-part: mounting platform + detector head — the type installed and covered below), and water/sprinkler-type (mentioned but not covered further in this source).

## ⚠️ Core Sequencing Fact: Platform Before Film

The mounting platform (закладная/площадка) **must be installed before the stretch-ceiling film is fitted**. Sequence demonstrated: the platform is fixed to the structural ceiling first, wiring is run to it, then the PVC/fabric film is stretched over the whole ceiling, and only afterward is the film cut open at the platform location to bring the detector through. This is the single most important stretch-ceiling-specific fact — get it backwards and there's no way to add the platform after the film is already installed without redoing the ceiling.

## Mounting Platform Spec

- ~135mm diameter, ~20mm center hole.
- Can be secured with 2 suspension points/hangers, but **4 hangers are recommended for rigidity** — fewer risks the platform flexing/sagging inward over time.

## Placement Clearance Rules

Stated as regulatory ("по регламенту"), matching the ceiling-detector clearances from the branded-hub source in [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Fire_Safety_Detector_Mechanism_and_Wiring|Detector Mechanism & Wiring]]:

- **Not less than 50cm from walls/corners.**
- **Not less than 50cm from light fixtures**, including track lighting.
- **Not less than 50cm between adjacent detectors themselves** — a rule this source states that the other fire-safety sources in this batch don't mention explicitly.

The source flags a real practical tension worth planning for: satisfying these clearances alongside a light-fixture layout can look aesthetically awkward, and recommends confirming the exact detector positions with the client in advance rather than treating the rule as flexible.

## Film-Penetration Technique

Once the film is stretched taut, a reinforcing ring (кольцо) is glued at each point where a wire/fixture needs to pass through. **35mm rings were used here; smaller works too, and even 50mm still works**, since the platform itself overlaps/covers the ring either way. A cross-shaped slit is then cut through the ring opening to pull the wiring through cleanly without further tearing the film.

## Detector Mounting & Wiring

- The detector head detaches from its base plate; the base plate screws into the center of the embedded platform (through the platform's window/opening where wires exit) using a single screw.
- Two wires connect to two screw-terminal contacts on the base — genuinely simple, hard to get wrong.
- **⚠️ Practical caution: the terminal screws are described as fragile/easily stripped** — if one strips on the first attempt, it can't be tightened a second time, so avoid over-torquing.
- Wire prep: insulation strips easily by hand/fingernail on this detector's wiring, no tool needed. When daisy-chaining multiple detectors, the installer doesn't cut the wire at each junction — just strips and loops it through in sequence.
- **Final verification**: once the detector head snaps onto its base, a blinking indicator confirms it's powered and functioning before the installer leaves the site.

## Assumptions / Uncertainties

No city/region stated. `single-account`, low promotional ratio — genuine job documentation, no brand/product push beyond the installer's own labor. ## Independent Corroboration Plus a Sprinkler-Specific Distinction (Andrey Frolov interview, added 2026-08-28, Round 5)

A second, independent professional installer (interviewed by Konstantin Kruglov, no commercial tie to either source) corroborates the core rule above from the *why*, not just the *how*: **a smoke detector must never be fully concealed behind the stretch-ceiling membrane** — it must sit flush with or only slightly recessed into the surface, fully exposed to the room, because real-fire detection speed is measured in seconds. A protective grille cover is sometimes installed at a client's explicit request, but this installer actively discourages it rather than treating it as a routine option — consistent with, and reinforcing, this page's own platform-before-film/clearance-rule content above. **Sprinkler/fire-suppression heads, distinct from a smoke detector, cannot be routed as permissively at all**: they require a specific manufacturer-made mounting collar/platform at a designated penetration point — not an ordinary wire-penetration reinforcing ring — through a named specialty-hardware supplier the installer didn't clearly identify on record. `single-account`, low promotional ratio.
