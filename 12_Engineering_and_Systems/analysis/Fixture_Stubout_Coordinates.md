# Plumbing — Fixture Stub-Out Coordinates

A complete, mechanism-explained rough-plumbing stub-out coordinate reference by fixture type, from a single dense source. Part of [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/Plumbing_and_Waterproofing|Plumbing & Waterproofing]] — complements [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Rough_Plumbing_Sequencing|Rough Plumbing Sequencing]] (technique/order) and [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Mounting_Heights_and_Positioning|Mounting Heights & Positioning]] (electrical-point heights).

> [!NOTE]
> `single-account`, one practitioner's own standard convention — not independently verified against an official plumbing code or a second source. All heights measured from finished-floor level unless stated otherwise; color convention hot=red, cold=blue, drain=grey. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_ssS7-TdXhu0_plumbing_stubout_coordinates_225|extraction note]]]

## Sink / Basin

- Drain: 50mm diameter, centered on the sink's centerline, at **500mm** from finished floor.
- Hot/cold supply: at **600mm**, offset **50mm left (hot) / 50mm right (cold)** from the same centerline. This 100mm total spacing is *not* a fixed mixer-fitting spacing — just two closely-grouped flexible-hose stub-outs, minimized to save space under the sink.

## Shower (built-in pan, integrated trap)

- No separate floor-drain stub-out needed — standard modern practice uses a pan with its own integrated trap.
- **Reference height is measured from the shower podium/platform, not the general bathroom floor.**
- Hot/cold supply: **1100mm above the podium**, offset **75mm left / 75mm right** from the pan's centerline — this **150mm center-to-center spacing is the standard shower-mixer fitting spacing**, distinct from the sink's non-fitting 100mm grouping above. Don't confuse the two.

## Bathtub

- Drain: 50mm diameter, on the centerline, at **30-100mm** from finished floor.
- Hot/cold supply: offset **75mm left / 75mm right**, at **800mm** — high enough that the mixer isn't crowding the rim, low enough that water doesn't fall far and splash onto the floor.

## Toilet + Hygienic Shower

- Thermostatic-mixed-water outlet: offset **300mm strictly to the RIGHT** of the toilet's centerline, at **800mm**.
- **Handedness mechanism**: most people wipe with the same hand they wash with (typically right-handed → right hand washes), so the sprayer/wand should sit in the LEFT hand — an outlet to the right of centerline lets the wand hang naturally to the seated user's left. **Mirror this to the left for a Muslim household**, where religious practice specifically requires the left hand for this task.

## Urinal (rare in residential units, included for completeness)

- Drain: 50mm diameter, at **100-400mm from floor, model-dependent** — no fixed height, take from the specific unit's own installation instructions.
- Cold supply: **100mm above the drain outlet**, referenced to the drain's own position — **never** independently measured from the floor.

## Washing Machine

- No fixed centerline — horizontal position is flexible depending on which side is more convenient for connection, influenced by adjacent fixtures.
- Drain: 32mm diameter, at **500mm**.
- Cold supply: **150mm above the drain**, same vertical axis.

## Kitchen Sink, Without an Adjacent Washing Machine

Includes a dishwasher connection by default.

- Sink drain: 50mm dia. at **200mm**.
- Sink cold supply: **150mm above** the drain.
- Sink hot supply: **75mm left** of the cold outlet, same horizontal axis.
- Dishwasher cold supply: another **75mm further left** (150mm total left of sink cold).
- Dishwasher drain: 32mm dia., at the intersection of the dishwasher's vertical axis and the sink-drain horizontal axis.

## Kitchen Sink, With an Adjacent Washing Machine

Different build-outward logic — start from the washer's own drain, not the sink's.

- Washer drain: 32mm dia. at **200mm**; washer cold supply **150mm above** it.
- Dishwasher cold supply: **75mm** from the washer's cold supply (same horizontal axis).
- Sink hot supply: another **75mm further** along the same axis.
- Sink cold supply: another **75mm further** along the same axis.
- Sink drain: 50mm dia., **150mm below** the sink's own cold outlet.
- Dishwasher drain: 32mm dia., centered exactly between the sink drain and the washer drain.

Net layout: three drain outlets (washer 32mm, dishwasher 32mm, sink 50mm) and four supply outlets (washer cold, dishwasher cold, sink hot, sink cold) arranged along one horizontal band.

## Kitchen Stub-Out Group Placement: Trash-Bin Clearance (added 2026-08-19)

> [!NOTE]
> A different, complementary aspect of kitchen stub-out planning — offset/placement, not the coordinate grid itself. Independent source. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_fSEPr5fpfPM_kitchen_stubouts_ac_fridge_niche_166|note]]]

**Offset the whole sink/dishwasher stub-out group left or right of the sink's own centerline — never center the group under the sink itself.** Purpose: leaves clear floor space directly under the sink cabinet for a floor-standing trash bin. This is a placement/positioning decision layered on top of the coordinate grid above, not a change to any of its numbers.

## Never Compress This Grid to Save Space

**Explicit warning, both kitchen configurations**: the hot/cold supply spacing (75mm gaps) could technically be tightened to 50mm without immediate issue, but the drain lines cannot. At 32-50mm diameter, the required chase width plus the mandatory drain slope means compressed spacing forces extra elbow/joint bends and incorrect slope angles — described as "guaranteed" to cause intermittent drainage problems. This grid is presented as the minimum spacing that avoids drain-line conflicts while still leaving room under the sink for water filters and other under-sink appliances.

## Gaps

- Not independently corroborated against a second source or an official plumbing code — treat as one practitioner's working standard, not a verified universal spec.
