# 🏷️ Bosch PIF63KHC1E Cooktop (Serie 6)

* **Category**: Induction Hob
* **Price**: ~2,168 rubles
* **Key Specifications**: Serie 6, 4 induction zones, 1 extra-large 28 cm circular expandable zone, DirectSelect control, steel trim.

---

## 🎯 Why It Was Selected

1. **Large Usable Circular Burner (28 cm)**:
   - **Reasoning**: Your main complaint about your previous 50 cm gas stove was that cookware crowded each other and large pans heated unevenly. The PIF63KHC1E features an expandable **28 cm circular burner** on the left. This perfectly matches the round base of large frying pans and stew pots, distributing heat evenly across the entire surface.
2. **Rejection of Marketing: Circular vs. FlexInduction**:
   - **Reasoning**: Manufacturers heavily market "FlexInduction" (rectangular flex zones). However, our technical audit revealed that FlexInduction relies on small oval coils that can cause uneven heating ("stripes") on large round pans. Standard round coils are technically superior and more reliable for classic cookware.
3. **Safety for Children (3 and 6 Years Old)**:
   - **Reasoning**: Induction technology only heats the ferrous bottom of the pot, leaving the glass surface relatively cool. There is no open flame, gas leakage risk, or danger of igniting kitchen towels. It also includes an instant **Child Lock** button.
4. **No "Smart Tax" (Manual Control over Wi-Fi)**:
   - **Reasoning**: We opted for a model without Home Connect (Wi-Fi syncing with the hood). Synchronizing appliances via Wi-Fi adds cost and wireless complexity for very little daily benefit. Manual activation is simple and robust.

## ⚠️ Concerns and Technical Risks

* **DirectSelect Slider Sensitivity**:
   - **Detail**: The DirectSelect panel has a printed slider bar. If liquid boils over from a pot and covers the control panel, the safety system will sound an alarm and shut off the entire cooktop. The panel must be kept dry and clean.
* **Coil Whistling (High Power)**:
   - **Detail**: At power level 9 or Boost mode, the electromagnetic coils can produce a high-pitched humming or whistling sound (especially when using lightweight or multi-ply pots). Heavy cast-iron or high-quality thick-bottomed pots minimize this.
* **Electrical Connection Requirement**:
   - **Detail**: The cooktop has a maximum power rating of 7.4 kW. It requires a dedicated 32A line or three-phase connection in your apartment.
