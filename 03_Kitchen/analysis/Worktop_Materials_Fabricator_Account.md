# Kitchen Worktops — A Fabricator's Own Account

**A systematic material-by-material comparison from one kitchen fabricator**, covering quartz agglomerate, porcelain stoneware, acrylic, HPL, glass, stainless steel, wood and ЛДСП.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **This page follows the precedent of [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Facade_Materials_Fabricator_Account|Kitchen Facades — A Fabricator's Own Account]]: a maker's account of a material is a different evidence class from a designer's specification of it, and this folder keeps the two apart.**
>
> **The companion page is [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]]**, which holds the designer-sourced material: the backsplash tier ladder, the overhang experiments, sink mounting, handles and the joint-count principle. **Read that page for what to specify; read this one for what the material does in the shop and under a hot pan.**

> [!WARNING]
> **⚠️ Source: CAPPUCCINO, a kitchen fabricator in Odessa and Kyiv, Ukraine, 2020.** **He sells every material he compares.** Treat each ranking as an opinion with a commercial interest attached and the **mechanisms** as the transferable part. ⚠️ **Nothing here was measured or tested** — these are trade statements.
>
> **⚠️ Dating**: 2020 is a hard caution for **prices, brands and availability**, and close to irrelevant for **what stains, what chips and what tolerates heat.** **No price and no brand verdict is routed.** ⚠️ **Rule 4: Ukrainian shop practice is not a Belarusian norm, and nothing here goes to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`.**

## 1. ⚠️⚠️ The structural facts — three that change how a quote is read

- **⚠️⚠️ ALL QUARTZ AGGLOMERATE IN THE WORLD IS MADE BY ONE PATENTED TECHNOLOGY (Breton, Italy).** *«Основные различия производителей сводятся лишь к цветовым решениям… и стоимости человеческих ресурсов.»* **→ Between quartz brands you are buying a colour range and a labour market, not a different material.** ⚠️ He adds that counterfeits below standard are sold at tempting prices — *«всегда требуйте сертификат»*.
- **⚠️⚠️ PRICE PER m² FALLS WITH SLAB YIELD.** *«Чем больше вероятность, что столешница по площади займёт сляб или его половину, тем ниже цена в расчёте за квадратный метр.»* **→ Nesting efficiency sets the effective unit price, so a layout that wastes half a slab is paid for twice.** Same principle as the `rectpack` entry in [`toolchain_gap_analysis_20260908.md`](../../_Inbox/planning/toolchain_gap_analysis_20260908.md).
- **⚠️⚠️ POR­CELAIN STONEWARE CARRIES A MANDATORY PERIMETER TRIM: *«нужно по периметру снять минимум по 5 сантиметров»*, to relieve stress and avoid cracking.** **→ A waste figure that is not a percentage but a geometric constant of 50 mm all round.** ⚠️ **Read with the waste-is-not-one-number finding in [[18_Digital_Toolchain/analysis/Quantity_Takeoff_and_Cost_Join|Quantity Take-Off and the Cost Join]] §2** — this is a third category again: neither a nesting percentage nor a cutting allowance, but a fixed edge sacrifice.

## 2. The materials, compared

### Quartz agglomerate

**94% quartz crumb + 5% resin + 1% pigment. Slabs ~3000 × 1400 mm, 12 or 20 mm.**

**For**: abrasion resistance close to granite; **non-porous — no bacteria or mould**, used in medical labs and food plants; **absorbs no dyes** (coffee, tea, bright juice leave nothing); resists acids and solvents; **needs no waxing, unlike granite and marble**; **colour stability at production** — *«можно не беспокоиться, что листы будут отличаться по оттенку»*, which natural stone cannot offer.

**⚠️ Against, and three are structural:**

- **⚠️⚠️ 52 kg/m² at 20 mm — *«требует усиленных опор… и специально изготовленного металлического каркаса на барной стойке длиной более 60 сантиметров»*.**
  > **→ A LOAD RULE WITH A THRESHOLD. [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]] already records that an unsupported пролёт without a закладная *«рано или поздно может легко сломаться»* — stated as a principle. This supplies the number: 600 mm.**
- **Cannot be restored** — damage means replacement. **A red-hot pan left in place can crack it by thermal shock.**
- **Cannot be bent** (contrast acrylic); **seams visible**, invisible only beyond ~1 m with some techniques; **tactilely cold**; **few wood-look and matt decors**; **cutting on it leaves fine scratches**; **UV fades it**.
- **⚠️ Measurement requires the cabinets already installed** — the slightest error and it will not fit, and trimming in place is very problematic.

### Porcelain stoneware (керамогранит)

**Quartz sand, clay, crushed granite, feldspar and pigments, pressed and fired above 1200 °C. 3–20 mm: 3–5 mm for facing FRONTS, 10–20 mm for worktops.**

> ⚠️ **The 3–5 mm band corroborates Шеврина's existing note** on thin large-format ceramic light enough to be fixed to MDF fronts, so worktop, splashback and fronts carry one pattern. ⚠️ **And he rebuts a common claim**: worktop-grade керамогранит is not ordinary tile — it differs in size, thickness, composition and properties, and needs more than a glass cutter and an angle grinder to work.

**⚠️⚠️ Where it beats quartz, head to head:**

| | Porcelain stoneware | Quartz |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Weight** | *«не очень тяжёлый»*, lower load on structure | **52 kg/m², reinforced supports** |
| **UV** | **does not fade** | **fades** |
| **Cracking** | *«прочнее камня, практически невозможно образовать трещину»* | **thermal shock can crack it** |
| **Decors** | **includes WOOD, rusty metal, full stone replication** | few wood-look decors |

**Also**: coloured through the full body, so wear barely changes the colour; near-zero water absorption; fire-resistant, no smoke or toxins; **dissipates static**; food-safe and recyclable. **⚠️ And it hosts a HIDDEN INDUCTION HOB, a wireless charging platform and embedded controls under the surface** — which no other material here does.

**⚠️ Against**: the 50 mm perimeter trim (§1); **fragile at the edges and around an undermount sink**; seams on textured finishes are made as a groove **that collects dirt**; **no shaped/profiled edge**; tactilely cold; **cannot be restored**; very high cost, in consumables and equipment wear as much as material.

> ⚠️ **A fabrication trick worth keeping, shared with HPL: on thin slabs a 45° mitre lets you present a worktop of ANY apparent thickness.**

### Acrylic (solid surface) — and a health refusal

**Fine mineral crumb + pigments + acrylic resin. Sheet 3600 × 760 mm, 6 or 12 mm.**

> **⚠️⚠️ TWO REFUSALS, STATED AS RULES:**
> 1. **6 mm acrylic must not be used for worktops** — prone to cracking.
> 2. **⚠️⚠️ LIQUID acrylic (4–6 mm) he refuses outright: *«при химическом отвердевании смолы выделяется огромное количество СТИРОЛА, который является токсичным и опасным для здоровья человека.»*** **→ A health objection to a whole product category, from the person who would otherwise sell it. New to this vault.**

**For**: a **truly seamless plane** — joints sanded until invisible; **sink and worktop from one piece**; **light-transmitting decors → back-lit worktops**; **best of these materials for curved and complex forms**; **warmer to the touch than stone**; **can be re-sanded repeatedly**.

**⚠️ Against**: shows cuts, dents and scratches; **acids and dyes must be wiped immediately**; **low heat resistance — a hot pan can leave a black mark and a temperature change can crack it**; fades under UV.

> **⚠️⚠️ Two consequences the vault did not hold:**
> - **Restoration is an on-site sanding, and *«ваша квартира запомнится тонким слоем пыли, и вам понадобится вызывать заново cleaning»*.** **The repairability that sells acrylic has a dust bill.** ⚠️ **This gives a second reason behind Шеврина's existing position — *«чаще люди ленятся… лучше предусмотреть материал понадёжнее»*.**
> - **⚠️⚠️ *«Без дополнительной подложки из более твёрдого материала акриловый камень со временем проседает, особенно в районе варочной поверхности.»*** **→ Acrylic SAGS without a substrate, worst at the hob.**

### HPL (пластик высокого давления)

**Invented in the USA, 1913. 0.8–20 mm; furniture uses 12 mm double-laminated; sheets to roughly 4200 × 1850 mm.**

> **⚠️⚠️ The property that matters, and it is the exact inverse of ЛДСП's failure: *«стопроцентная влагостойкость — даже если вы повредили верхний слой ламината, столешница не разбухнет и не вздуется.»***
>
> **⚠️ And the edge needs no treatment at all** — *«торец не требует дополнительной обработки, является полностью функциональным сразу же после распила»*. **No banding, no PVC, no glue line.** ⚠️ **The core is white or black and shows at every edge — factor it into the design.**

**Also**: the 45° mitre trick for any apparent thickness; many wood and stone decors; undermount sink possible; **does not glare in sun**; solvent-resistant; **does not crack, does not bend over large spans**; frost- and fire-resistant; **no halogens, sulphur compounds, heavy metals or asbestos**.

**⚠️ Against, and he is honest about the first**: **scratch resistance *«такая же, как и у столешницы из ДСП»***; **impact resistance poor — a falling pot can chip a piece out**; **weak at the edges and around an undermount sink**; heat can ruin the laminate; cannot be bent; **hob and sink cutouts impossible without special equipment**; **no on-site adjustment — hand work risks chipping and ruining the piece**; joints visible even with anchor connections.

> ⚠️ **A note that lands on an existing finding**: HPL *«легко перфорируют, но только на специальном оборудовании — особенно актуально, если кухня стоит под окном и нужно сделать отверстие для вентиляции батареи»*. **[[03_Kitchen/Kitchen_General|Kitchen General]] records a real case where cabinetry blocked the radiator and the window began streaming condensation. This is the shop-side answer: the vent hole is drillable, but not on site.**

### Glass, and stainless steel — both restricted to non-work zones

**Glass**: tempered, **thicker than 8 mm**, or triplex; assembly 5–20 mm with the top layer tempered. **Two clear glasses — ordinary reads slightly GREENISH, super-clear slightly BLUISH.** Sizes over 3 m; clear, painted any colour, or tinted bronze/graphite.

> **⚠️⚠️ THE WARNING WORTH MOST, AND IT IS A MECHANISM: *«ни в коем случае не используйте матовое покрытие лицом наружу. Матовый слой создаётся в результате химического травления, что создаёт МИКРОПОРЫ, в которые очень хорошо проникают жир и пыль, и оттереть его практически невозможно.»* He extends it to matt glass on a skinali.**
>
> **→ This supplies the mechanism behind the existing note that tempered glass *"shows fingerprints/grease heavily"* — and sharpens it into a rule: the problem is the ETCHED MATT finish, not glass.** ⚠️ **It also reconciles the disagreement on [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]], where Синчуков rates tempered glass as durable for a splashback: both are true — polished tempered glass is tough, etched matt glass is a grease trap.**

**Glass otherwise**: does not abrade or deform; unafraid of moisture; no bacteria; **hot pans straight down**; **the best gloss of any of these materials**. **⚠️ Against**: **fragile — even tempered will eventually crack**; tactilely cold; **⚠️ metal cookware produces an unpleasant squeak**; shows every mark; expensive; afraid of edge impacts.

**Stainless steel**: absorbs no odours or moisture; not stained by food; **unafraid of high temperature**; matt or gloss, flat or textured; cleans with anything. **⚠️ Against**: scratches appear; **a falling metal object dents it**; **⚠️ the seams oxidise and need re-polishing**.

> **⚠️⚠️ HIS TWO PLACEMENT VERDICTS, WHICH ARE THE USABLE OUTPUT: glass belongs on a bar counter or any zone where no work happens — specifically a second-tier bar top, *«чтобы создать эффект невесомости»*. Stainless steel is perfectly usable on the MAIN work zone.** **→ The pair is a display-surface / work-surface split, not a ranking.**

### ЛДСП and wood — the budget end

**⚠️⚠️ ЛДСП THICKNESS RULE, WITH A FUNCTIONAL REASON: *«толщина может быть 28 и 38 миллиметров. Я рекомендую 38 — это связано с габаритами техники, а именно с толщиной варочной поверхности. Если вы располагаете выдвижные ящики в районе варочной поверхности, то, чтобы содержимое ящика не соприкасалось с техникой, нужен минимальный зазор, который обеспечивает столешница 38 миллиметров.»***

> **→ A 38 mm worktop exists to create the clearance a drawer needs beneath a hob. New to this vault, and exactly the kind of dimension that silently breaks a drawer layout.**

**ЛДСП for**: a huge decor range; **3D embossing good enough that beyond ~1 m it is hard to tell from real wood**; **formats over 4 m with no joint**; easy to clean; **matching carcass board available**, which matters for portal kitchens.

> **⚠️⚠️ A LOGISTICS CONSTRAINT NOTHING ELSE HERE RECORDS: *«обязательно убедитесь, что такой размер возможно провести между пролётов этажей, потому что такая столешница в лифт точно не поместится.»*** **→ A >4 m jointless worktop goes up the stairwell or not at all. Check the flight before specifying it.**

**⚠️ ЛДСП against**: colour wears where frequently wiped; **weak heat resistance**; **⚠️ an undermount sink cannot be fitted at all** (⚠️ corroborates the existing sink-mounting ladder exactly); **⚠️ a mitred «еврозапил» joint must not be used beside the sink — it is the first node to swell**; **swells** wherever water reaches the substrate; **a wall upstand is mandatory and usually cannot be colour-matched**, adding a visible break line.

**Wood — three types**: **евробрус** (glued bars — ⚠️ shade may vary and the pieces show); **евробрус со шпоном** (veneered over); and **⚠️ a single solid piece, which he says is not usable for a kitchen worktop at all.**

**For**: natural and warm; individual sizes; **re-sandable**; moisture-resistant with the right lacquer. **⚠️ Against**: **very weak heat and fire resistance**; dries out or sags with humidity change; swells at unprotected joints; weak against acids and dyes; **fungi with frequent wetting**; shade varies along the length; *«как ни странно, есть только один декор»*.

> **⚠️⚠️ THE MAINTENANCE FINDING, WITH INTERVALS: protection is lacquer OR oil-wax, and both cost something. *«На лаке будут оставаться белые пятна после контакта с водой. Масло-воск нужно обновлять каждые полгода, потому что он стирается.»*** **→ Lacquer: permanent white water marks. Oil-wax: a six-monthly job for the life of the worktop.**
>
> ⚠️ **His verdict: wood on a bar counter, a windowsill, and surfaces that do not meet water. ЛДСП for the rest.**

## 3. ⚠️⚠️ Two things he talks clients OUT of — recorded as Perspectives, not corrections

**1. A same-material integrated sink in quartz.** *«Я обычно отговариваю от такого варианта, потому что в углах этой мойки скапливается грязь и достаточно проблематично оттуда убрать.»*

> **⚠️ [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Worktops_and_Backsplash|Worktops & Backsplash]] records the same-material quartz undermount sink as a newer premium capability, with a visible seam as its only stated drawback.** **A fabricator who makes them adds a cleaning objection and declines to sell it.** **Both can be true — the capability is real and the corners are real. Not resolved here.**

**2. A flush-mounted hob.** Quartz mills finely enough to allow it, *«но я так же не рекомендую такой способ монтажа, потому что в результате нагрева стеклокерамика расширяется, ей необходим зазор… этот зазор заливается термогерметиком, который со временем теряет свои первоначальные свойства и требует замены»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ A MECHANISM WITH A MAINTENANCE TAIL: the glass-ceramic expands when hot, the gap must be sealed, and the sealant degrades and needs replacing.** **A flush hob is not a one-off detail but a recurring maintenance item. Entirely new to this vault.**

## ⚠️⚠️ Five Destructive Tests on Four Countertops — and Only One Can Be Repaired (Сосновская фабрика, ~2020)

**The account above gives a material inventory. This applies the SAME insult to four materials and reports what happened.** Specimens: **DEKTON** (quartz, glass, porcelain), **quartz agglomerate** (94–97% natural quartz), **compact laminate** (65% paper, 30–35% cured resins) and **acrylic stone** (acrylic resin plus aluminium hydroxide).

### ⚠️⚠️ You cannot cut on any of them — and he says the showroom will tell you otherwise

> ***«Ни в коем случае не режем продукты без разделочной доски, ЧТО БЫ ВАМ НИ ГОВОРИЛИ ПРОДАВЦЫ В САЛОНЕ. На топовых столешницах в любом случае остаются микроповреждения.»***

**Most resistant: DEKTON, then quartz** — and even so, micro-damage remains. **⚠️ Quartz takes DEEP grooves from a knife.** Compact laminate and acrylic scratch badly. **→ A tested contradiction of a sales claim, by a seller.**

### ⚠️⚠️ The finding that should decide the material: only acrylic can be repaired

> **Acrylic**: *«Царапин нету, я в шоке — всё убрал… смотрите, как новый стал.»* **It also polished out the зелёнка stain.**
> **Quartz, DEKTON, compact laminate**: not restorable. ***«Если вы поцарапали, вам придётся жить с этими царапинами ДО КОНЦА ЭКСПЛУАТАЦИИ этого гарнитура.»***

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS REFRAMES THE COMPARISON. Acrylic loses every resistance test and wins the only one that is RECOVERABLE.** Quartz is harder *and* permanent in its damage; acrylic marks easily *and* can be made new again. **Over a 10–20 year life that is a different trade from the one a hardness table implies.**
>
> ⚠️⚠️ **Not in conflict with the styrene refusal recorded above**, which was a health objection to LIQUID acrylic during cure and a thickness objection to 6 mm sheet. **This is a repairability property of the cured material. → The question becomes WHICH acrylic, not whether.**

### ⚠️ Heat, staining and impact

- **⚠️⚠️ HEAT: a red-hot frying pan straight from the hob damaged NONE of the four**, acrylic included. ⚠️ **Single brief contact, single trial** — and heat at the hob stays a JOINT problem, per the flush-hob sealant finding.
- **⚠️⚠️ STAINING: every natural dye washed off all four** (beetroot, tea, coffee, blackcurrant, cherry, soy sauce, 8–9 hours). **ЗЕЛЁНКА AND IODINE ARE TERMINAL** — *«это смерть абсолютная»* — surviving five to ten minutes of scrubbing **including with acetone**. Compact laminate and quartz went permanently green; DEKTON nearly cleaned; acrylic only via polishing.
  **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIRD INDEPENDENT ARRIVAL IN THIS VAULT AT ЗЕЛЁНКА** — [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Facade_Materials_Fabricator_Account|the MDF head-to-head]] found the same on FRONTS. **Nothing resists it, so it is a household-practice finding, not a selection criterion: keep brilliant green and iodine off the worktop and wipe any spill at once.**
- **IMPACT**: mallet blows on face and exposed edge — all fine. A **sledgehammer from 1 m** — all survived; **from 2 m the DEKTON split**, ⚠️ **which on his own caveat is a THICKNESS result, that specimen being thinner.** **Quartz rated highest for impact.**

⚠️ **Prices RUB ~2020 — rule 2, ratios only: DEKTON is by far the dearest (~2.5–3× a quartz sheet); quartz sits in the middle; compact laminate and acrylic are cheapest and roughly equal.** ⚠️⚠️ **Both cheap options were quoted at 12 mm against 20–22 mm for the other two — so the fashionable ultra-thin slab and the budget option COINCIDE, and a thin worktop is not by itself evidence of a premium specification.**

> ⚠️ **`promotional_ratio: low`** — the headline result contradicts showroom patter and he destroys four expensive materials he sells. ⚠️⚠️ **Method limits are the ones already applied to the MDF head-to-head: single specimens, one trial each, no controls, and the DEKTON was thinner than its rivals. Treat the DIRECTION as evidence and the rankings as opinion.**
