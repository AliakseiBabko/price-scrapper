# Kitchen — Ordering a Kitchen Commercially

Detail page for [[03_Kitchen/Kitchen_Furniture|Kitchen Furniture & Layout]].

**How a kitchen is priced, what documents must exist before money moves, and what the contract has to say.** One Russian factory's account. **The owner will be buying a kitchen from someone, and this is the commercial half of that.**

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **⚠️⚠️ Every figure here is RUB, Russia, 2018–2021 — standing rule 2 blocks all of it and nothing is converted.** What transfers is **structure**: what the advertised unit contains, which documents to demand, and which contract terms decide whether you have a remedy.
>
> **⚠️⚠️ The legal section is RUSSIAN law, asserted by a furniture seller who is not a lawyer and cites no statute.** **Standing rule 4 makes `16_Legal_and_Regulations/` Belarus-only, so none of it is routed there. Treat every legal claim below as a QUESTION TO ASK a Belarusian supplier, never as a rule that applies here.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. «Метр погонный» — what the advertised number actually buys

**The unit every kitchen in this market is advertised in, and the vault had no coverage of it before 2026-09-14.**

> **A погонный метр is the indicative cost of a 1-metre length of kitchen with a MINIMAL set of elements** — standard depth and height, simplest finish, minimum equipment. **Method: sum every element, divide by the run length.**
>
> ***«Финальную стоимость гарнитура без детального просчёта всех её элементов вам не сможет сказать НИ ОДИН производитель… Корректнее говорить о МИНИМАЛЬНОЙ цене метра погонного — то есть "от".»***

**⚠️⚠️ What a quoted standard metre contains, against what a real metre contains** — he runs the comparison on his own kitchen:

| Quoted «стандартный» metre | The same metre of his own kitchen |
| :--- | :--- |
| Hinged doors top and bottom | A pull-out basket, a TandemBox, two more drawer boxes and one hidden box |
| **No drawer boxes, no hidden drawers** | ⚠️ **fitting-out worth roughly the price of the metre itself** |
| One bottom drawer on ball-bearing runners | — |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE ADVERTISED FIGURE PRICES AN EMPTY BOX.** **Compare quotes on the SPECIFICATION, never on the metre price.**

**⚠️⚠️ And a systematic distortion nobody corrects for:** *«Если берётся для расчёта УГЛОВАЯ кухня, расчёт получается изначально НЕКОРРЕКТНЫМ: по длине столешницы получается одна длина, но в этом углу НЕТ ФАСАДА… Но никто это не принимает в расчёты.»* **→ A corner kitchen quoted per linear metre is systematically over-quoted.**

**The base specification a метр погонный assumes** — i.e. the checklist of what you will be paying extra for: plain slab fronts, no milling or glass · **plastic countertop 38 mm**, no overlay or rounded edge · simple rail or bow handle · **Chinese hinges** · PVC plinth · **carcass of ЛДСП 16 mm** · uppers on an adjustable rail. Standard dimensions: uppers **720 mm** high and **300 mm** deep, lowers **860 mm** including front, plinth and countertop, side panels **560 mm** deep with a services gap behind.

- **⚠️ A substitution to confirm in writing:** *«Крепёж может быть заменён: евровинты, сделанные ради экономии, — на эксцентриковые стяжки, Rafix.»* ⚠️⚠️ **Note this runs OPPOSITE to [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Cabinet_Assembly_Technique|CAPPUCCINO's rule]] that visible faces should use minifix rather than confirmat.** **Both are true of different locations — which is exactly why the contract must name which fastener goes WHERE, not which is "better".**

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Two documents to demand BEFORE paying

**⚠️⚠️ THE ДЕТАЛИРОВКА — proof the carcass is genuinely custom.** A printed carcass specification: every part, **the full fastener schedule** (евровинт, эксцентрик, glued joints) and the hardware list.

> ***«Если вот такого листочка ты не увидел перед заказом кухни — либо ты купил МОДУЛЬНУЮ кухню, либо тебе будет её пилить ГАРАЖНИК.»***
>
> **Its second function is proof of process:** such a drawing is what feeds the CNC that drills and mills the parts, and *«в полевых условиях сделать такие фрезеровки вручную практически невозможно»*.
> **⚠️ The warning that makes it worth asking for:** *«даже в среднем-плюс сегменте, который говорит, что делает кухню на заказ, вам также продают МОДУЛЬНЫЕ гарнитуры»*.

**⚠️⚠️ THE ЭСКИЗ — proof of what you ordered.** The contract's subject clause reads *«изготовить и передать гарнитур СОГЛАСНО СПЕЦИФИКАЦИИ И ЭСКИЗУ»*, where the **спецификация** lists materials and hardware and the **эскиз is the drawing WITH DIMENSIONS.**

> *«Может получиться так, что вы заказали УГЛОВОЙ гарнитур, а вам привезут ПРЯМОЙ — и именно эта картинка с размерами является ДОКАЗАТЕЛЬСТВОМ того, что вы заказали.»*
>
> **Why it is needed:** several people act for the maker — the sales designer, the measurer, the fitter — *«каждый из этих лиц может накосячить»*, and you end up with **Chinese hardware instead of Italian**, *«и вроде бы вы помните… но ДОКАЗАТЬ ЭТО НЕ МОЖЕТЕ»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS PROJECT ALREADY PRODUCES DIMENSIONED DRAWINGS. The album IS the эскиз**, and that is a real advantage at the ordering stage rather than only a modelling exercise.

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. Six contract terms to fix before signing

1. **⚠️⚠️ ENUMERATE THE ADDITIONAL SERVICES** — assembly, appliance installation, appliance connection, carrying to the flat. **The failure he names: if the LIGHTING connection is not listed, the fitters may decline to connect it and be entirely within their rights.**
2. **⚠️ SPECIFIC DATES, NOT RANGES.** A range is acceptable only for the installation window (1–5 days) or interregional transport. **⚠️⚠️ Check for any clause letting the maker extend the deadline UNILATERALLY, or change the facade colour or shade unilaterally.**
3. **⚠️⚠️ PAY PARTIALLY, NEVER 100% UP FRONT** — he suggests 50–70%. *«Эти оставшиеся проценты будут вашей СТРАХОВКОЙ»* against delay and against the firm failing.
4. **⚠️⚠️ DO NOT SIGN THE АКТ ПРИЁМКИ-ПЕРЕДАЧИ IN A HURRY.** *«Монтажники могут подсунуть акт — "подпишите, потом разберёмся". Разбираться с вами НЕ БУДУТ, и будут полностью правы, потому что вы подписали акт.»* **→ Run the inspection first: [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Cabinet_Assembly_Technique|the delivery-day and post-installation checklists]].**
5. **A reasonable rectification period — 14 working days, not 35.**
6. **On cancellation the maker should retain only ACTUALLY INCURRED costs, and must be able to prove them** — not an arbitrary share of the price.

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Russian consumer law as he states it — flagged, not adopted

> ⚠️⚠️ **Rule 4: nothing below is routed to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`. Belarusian equivalents must be confirmed separately.**

- **⚠️⚠️ THE PENALTY RATE DEPENDS ON THE CONTRACT TYPE, and this is the sharpest disclosure.** A **sale contract** carries **0.5% per day** of delay; a **works-and-services contract** carries **3%**. *«Многие крупные компании делают договор СМЕШАННЫМ: продают вам кухню, а монтаж делают ОТДЕЛЬНЫМ договором — чтобы за кухню платить 0,5%, а не 3%. Практически 90% крупных компаний именно так и делают.»*
  **→ ⚠️⚠️ A DELIBERATE STRUCTURING CHOICE THAT MOVES THE BUYER'S REMEDY BY 6×, disclosed by an insider. Whatever the local equivalent, the QUESTION transfers: which contract type covers which part of the job?**
- **On late delivery** you may claim in writing, set a new deadline, cancel and recover the prepayment, or claim a per-day penalty — **which he says is owed even if the contract omits it.**
- **Warranty: a minimum of two years where the contract is silent.** The contract should state that furniture is made to the showroom samples and that materials meet GOST with hygiene certificates.
- **On defects, four remedies**: free rectification; a price reduction; free replacement against return of the original; or third-party rectification at the maker's cost.
- **⚠️⚠️ A REFUND REQUIRES A "SUBSTANTIAL" DEFECT, and his calibration is sobering:** either repair approaches the cost of a new set, or the defect makes the kitchen unusable. ***«Царапины, сколы, отошедшая кромка — ДАЖЕ ЕСЛИ ЦВЕТ ФАСАДА НЕ СООТВЕТСТВУЕТ ЗАКАЗАННОМУ — это всё НЕсущественный брак.»*** **→ A wrong facade colour does not get your money back. That is the argument for catching everything at the эскиз stage rather than at delivery.**
- **⚠️ A kitchen set cannot be returned for "it did not suit by form, colour or style"** — unlike a chair, table or sofa.
- **Escalation**: a written претензия in two copies (one retained, signed and stamped, or by registered post) → **no reply within 10 days** → consumer-protection authority plus a court claim.
- ⚠️ **One durable point from the COVID force-majeure section**: force majeure released the maker from **liability for breach** (penalties and interest), **not from the obligation itself**. **A distinction worth carrying into any contract reading.**
