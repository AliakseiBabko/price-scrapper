  4. **⚠️⚠️ THE ETCHED-MATT-GLASS MECHANISM, WHICH RESOLVES A STANDING DISAGREEMENT.** Etching creates **micropores** that grease and dust enter and which cannot be cleaned. **→ Polished tempered glass is durable, as Синчуков says; etched matt glass is a grease trap, as the tier ladder implies. The variable is the FINISH, not the material.**
  5. **⚠️⚠️ A HANDLE CHOICE PROPAGATES INTO WORKTOP HEIGHT.** A **gola profile plus a dishwasher forces a worktop at ≥920 mm**; a Blum **SPACE STEP needs a 15 cm plinth minimum** (20–25 recommended). **Plinth height, worktop height and opening method resolve together or not at all.** ⚠️ **And the opening method constrains the FRONT MATERIAL — a J-handle cannot be milled into laminated MDF at all.**
  6. **⚠️⚠️ THREE TOLERANCES AT THREE SCALES, FROM TWO UNRELATED SOURCES IN ONE BATCH** — the building at **±50 mm** (our own variance study), finishing at **~10 mm**, and fitted furniture at **3 mm per wall just to install**. **They do not reconcile, which is exactly why both sources insist on a post-handover measurement before anything is ordered.** ⚠️ **A run modelled hard against two walls is 6 mm shorter than the opening**, and **the fabricator names OUT-OF-SQUARE ROOM GEOMETRY as the dominant error term** — this project's own premise, arrived at from the furniture side.
  **Also**: **the MDF head-to-head ran FOUR PHYSICAL TESTS on camera** and ended in an honest **46–46 draw** from a vendor comparing two products it sells — ⚠️ **and the water result is about the SUBSTRATE not the finish, so any MDF front is moisture-limited and the coating is not the variable that fixes it.** **⚠️ Painted fronts fade, so a replacement after a year cannot be colour-matched — order a spare at manufacture.** **⚠️ Two quantity figures**: quartz at **52 kg/m²** needing a steel frame beyond **600 mm** of bar overhang, and porcelain stoneware's **mandatory 50 mm perimeter trim**, which is neither a percentage nor a cutting allowance. **⚠️ A logistics constraint**: a jointless worktop over 4 m **will not fit in a lift**. **No price transferred** (Ukraine 2020 / Russia 2025, rule 2) — only two structural ratios. **Nothing routed to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`.**

- **2026-09-14 — CAPPUCCINO, one further video: the acceptance-inspection checklist (16 facts).** `Cabinet_Assembly_Technique.md` extended; a Perspective routed to [[11_Budget_and_Planning/analysis/Contractor_Vetting_and_Selection|Contractor Vetting and Selection]]. **THREE THINGS WORTH CARRYING:**
  1. **⚠️⚠️ MISSING EDGE BANDING IS A HEALTH ITEM, NOT A COSMETIC ONE.** The **E1 emission class is stated for the EDGE-BANDED condition**, and an open ЛДСП edge is claimed to raise emission **tenfold**. → **Every unbanded edge counts, including the ones behind a plinth and against a wall.** ⚠️ **Unsourced figure from a fabricator, not a laboratory — the direction is not in doubt and the check costs nothing.** ⚠️⚠️ **And the largest single source of unbanded edges is CUT-OUTS MADE ON SITE** for the riser, the hood duct, the gas meter and water stub-outs — which is fixed upstream, free, at the drawing stage.
  2. **⚠️⚠️ UNEVEN FRONT GAPS TRACE BACK TO THE SAW, AND THIS PARTLY REVISES WHAT WE ALREADY RECORDED FROM THE SAME CHANNEL.** The chain is **uncalibrated saw → carcass not square → diagonals that do not match → gaps that cannot be adjusted out**, because the error is in the box, not in the hanging. **Elsewhere this presenter names out-of-square ROOM geometry as the dominant error term; here he says the room is the LESS frequent cause.** → **Both are real and they are different populations — and the operational consequence is that a bad gap is NOT automatically the flat's fault. Check the carcass diagonals.**
  3. **⚠️⚠️ A SOCKET LANDING ON A JOINT BETWEEN TWO MODULES CANNOT BE CUT CLEANLY IN EITHER ONE** — «приходится кромсать всю кухню». **The kitchen module grid and the socket layout are one problem, and both are open in this project.** **The same deadline the CNC section already establishes, arriving from the failure side.**
  **Also**: **genuine Blum carries a factory engraving — check it on delivery**, since hardware is the easiest line to substitute and nobody looks after installation day; **the dish-drainer cabinet needs ventilation AND the drainer needs decent stainless, and either alone still rusts** within «пара месяцев»; **16 mm board substituted for a promised 18 mm** is a spec verifiable with a ruler; **confirmat is fine where it cannot be seen, minifix on show faces**; **the kitchen should arrive as pre-assembled modules, with only tall units broken down for the lift**. **⚠️ Every front material has a DELAYED failure mode invisible at handover** — film delaminates, paint sinks into шагрень, glass unsticks, wood cracks — **which is the checklist's own limit and makes the warranty term a real selection criterion.** **⚠️ The «Вася» bus-factor argument is the most self-serving item in the source and was routed as a Perspective**, narrowed to the one usable question: who does each stage, and what happens if that person is unavailable — **worth asking of a firm too.** **No prices in this source.
