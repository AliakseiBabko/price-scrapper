Triage: [`cappuccino_channel_triage_20260914.md`](../../_Inbox/planning/cappuccino_channel_triage_20260914.md). **SIX THINGS WORTH CARRYING:**
  1. **⚠️⚠️ A SECOND FABRICATOR, ON THE TOPIC THE FIRST DID NOT COVER.** The vault's kitchen material coverage was almost entirely DESIGNERS SPECIFYING; this is a MAKER CUTTING. **A grep before processing found `HPL` at ZERO mentions on the 283-line worktops page, stainless at two, керамогранит at two** — so three of the five countertop videos were genuinely new ground. ⚠️ **The channel is ~85% marketing by video count (45 testimonials), and the technical core is about twenty videos; the owner's read on it was correct.**
  2. **⚠️⚠️ A HEALTH REFUSAL: LIQUID ACRYLIC RELEASES LARGE AMOUNTS OF STYRENE AS THE RESIN CURES**, and he will not use it for worktops — nor 6 mm sheet acrylic, which cracks. **An objection to a whole product category from the person who would otherwise sell it.**
  3. **⚠️⚠️ TWO FABRICATOR DISSENTS FROM WHAT THIS VAULT RECORDS AS DESIRABLE**, both routed as Perspectives: the **same-material integrated quartz sink** (*dirt collects in the corners and will not come out*), and the **flush-mounted hob** (*the glass-ceramic expands, the gap needs thermal sealant, and that sealant degrades and must be replaced*). **A flush hob is a recurring maintenance item, not a one-off detail.**
  4. **⚠️⚠️ THE ETCHED-MATT-GLASS MECHANISM, WHICH RESOLVES A STANDING DISAGREEMENT.** Etching creates **micropores** that grease and dust enter and which cannot be cleaned. **→ Polished tempered glass is durable, as Синчуков says; etched matt glass is a grease trap, as the tier ladder implies. The variable is the FINISH, not the material.**
  5. **⚠️⚠️ A HANDLE CHOICE PROPAGATES INTO WORKTOP HEIGHT.** A **gola profile plus a dishwasher forces a worktop at ≥920 mm**; a Blum **SPACE STEP needs a 15 cm plinth minimum** (20–25 recommended). **Plinth height, worktop height and opening method resolve together or not at all.** ⚠️ **And the opening method constrains the FRONT MATERIAL — a J-handle cannot be milled into laminated MDF at all.**
  6. **⚠️⚠️ THREE TOLERANCES AT THREE SCALES, FROM TWO UNRELATED SOURCES IN ONE BATCH** — the building at **±50 mm** (our own variance study), finishing at **~10 mm**, and fitted furniture at **3 mm per wall just to install**. **They do not reconcile, which is exactly why both sources insist on a post-handover measurement before anything is ordered.** ⚠️ **A run modelled hard against two walls is 6 mm shorter than the opening**, and **the fabricator names OUT-OF-SQUARE ROOM GEOMETRY as the dominant error term** — this project's own premise, arrived at from the furniture side.
  **Also**: **the MDF head-to-head ran FOUR PHYSICAL TESTS on camera** and ended in an honest **46–46 draw** from a vendor comparing two products it sells — ⚠️ **and the water result is about the SUBSTRATE not the finish, so any MDF front is moisture-limited and the coating is not the variable that fixes it.** **⚠️ Painted fronts fade, so a replacement after a year cannot be colour-matched — order a spare at manufacture.** **⚠️ Two quantity figures**: quartz at **52 kg/m²** needing a steel frame beyond **600 mm** of bar overhang, and porcelain stoneware's **mandatory 50 mm perimeter trim**, which is neither a percentage nor a cutting allowance. **⚠️ A logistics constraint**: a jointless worktop over 4 m **will not fit in a lift**. **No price transferred** (Ukraine 2020 / Russia 2025, rule 2) — only two structural ratios. **Nothing routed to `16_Legal_and_Regulations/`.**
