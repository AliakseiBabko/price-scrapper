# Kitchen — Carcass Construction and Edge Technology

Detail page for [[03_Kitchen/Kitchen_Furniture|Kitchen Furniture & Layout]].

**What the box is made of, how its edges are sealed, and which of those decides how long the kitchen lasts.** One Russian factory's account, **with on-camera destructive testing**, plus where it agrees and disagrees with the fabricator accounts already in this vault.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **⚠️⚠️ THE CENTRAL CLAIM ON THIS PAGE CONTRADICTS SOMETHING THE VAULT ALREADY RECORDED.** [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Cabinet_Assembly_Technique|The CAPPUCCINO acceptance checklist]] says to check the board thickness — 16 mm substituted for a promised 18. **This source says the board is not the binding constraint:** *«Можно взять самый дорогой ЛДСП, самый толстый, поклеить на него дерьмовую тонкую кромку на EVA клей — и кромка отвалится, каркас разбухнет, и из него вывалится вся присаженная фурнитура.»*
>
> **Both are probably right. Board thickness is a NECESSARY-not-sufficient check; the edge question is the one nobody asks.**

## ⚠️⚠️ 1. The tested result: ЛДСП fails on water whatever the edge

**Six specimens, 24 hours in warm water, filmed** (Сосновская фабрика, 2022):

| Specimen | Result |
| :--- | :--- |
| **ЛДСП, laser/Airtec edge** | ⚠️ **Destroyed within ~30 minutes** |
| **ЛДСП, EVA edge** | ⚠️ **Destroyed within ~30 minutes** |
| **ЛДСП, PUR edge** | ⚠️⚠️ **Also failed by 24 h** |
| **МДФ, facade-grade coating, PUR** | ✅ Intact, edge sound front and back |
| **Фанера 18 mm birch, HPL both faces** | ✅ Undamaged; some edge absorption that dried out |

> ***«ЛДСП — PUR, ЭВА, лазер — не держит влагу однозначно. Разбухает ЛЮБАЯ технология. Пропускается влага через саму ЛАМИНАЦИЮ по торцу.»***
>
> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE WATER ENTERS THROUGH THE LAMINATION AT THE EDGE, NOT PAST THE BANDING — a different failure path from the one every other source describes, and it means the edge technology cannot fix ЛДСП's water behaviour.**

**⚠️⚠️ And the result that inverts the obvious ranking: the laser edge — the seamless, most expensive-looking one — swelled MORE than the cheapest EVA.** *«Эстетики здесь на высоте, но эксплуатационные характеристики практически вообще отсутствуют.»* **Confirmed in a separate 3-hour immersion.**

> **→ A ZERO-VISIBLE-SEAM EDGE IS AN AESTHETIC PRODUCT, NOT A PROTECTIVE ONE.** **It is also the one a showroom pushes hardest.**

[source: [[_Sources/YT_-I97gsh-aek_sosnovsk_carcass_material_and_edge_crash_tests|YT_-I97gsh-aek]]]

## ⚠️⚠️ 2. Fastener retention — the mechanism behind a failure already in the vault

A cross-plate driven into each material and torn out on camera:

| Material | Result | Score |
| :--- | :--- | ---: |
| **ЛДСП** | *«Очень нестабильный материал, очень сильно крошится»* | **0 / 5** |
| **МДФ** | Denser, *«но такая же история — тоже крошится»* | **1 / 5** |
| **Фанера** | *«Я его выдрал и даже чуть погнул планку, но материал не крошится»* | **5 / 5** |

- **→ ⚠️⚠️ THIS IS WHY A HINGE TEARS OUT.** [[03_Kitchen/analysis/Cabinet_Assembly_Technique|CAPPUCCINO's argument against 16 mm board]] named exactly that failure. **Reproduced here on camera — and it happens to 18 mm ЛДСП too. Fastener retention is a property of the BOARD TYPE, not of its thickness.**
- **→ AND IT EXPLAINS TWO RULES ELSEWHERE**: that a front over 700 mm needs a **third hinge fitted from the start** — *«когда петля вывалится из ЛДСП, оно там всё разрушится, потом качественно присадить петлю практически нереально»*, with **two hinges near the top and one at the bottom**, not equidistant — and that **baskets hung on the back of a front pull the hardware out of the board**, which is a load problem in the ЛДСП, not in the hinge.
- ⚠️ **At high-load points, use expanding sleeves (спецвтулки) rather than screws driven straight into the board.**

[source: [[_Sources/YT_-I97gsh-aek_sosnovsk_carcass_material_and_edge_crash_tests|YT_-I97gsh-aek]], [[_Sources/YT_Lfvs1XUz53s_sosnovsk_carcass_construction_and_edge_technology|YT_Lfvs1XUz53s]]]

## ⚠️⚠️ 3. The three edge technologies — and what to ask

| | **ЭВА / PVA** | **PUR** | **Laser Line / Airtec** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Method** | Granules melted, roller-applied, open bath | Polyurethane, **closed applicator that never contacts air** | **No glue** — a two-layer edge fused by laser or hot air |
| **Seam** | Visible, *«жёлтый шов»* | *«Практически незаметный»* | **None — monolithic** |
| **Cure** | Seconds | **Five days** | n/a |
| **Heat rating / tested** | 70 °C / **detached at 250 °C** | 150 °C / **held at 250 °C** | held at 250 °C |
| **Water (tested)** | lifts ~0.5 mm at 3 h | ✅ **no swelling** | ⚠️ **worse than EVA** |
| **Peel adhesion (tested)** | comes off easily | ✅ **much the strongest** | ⚠️ breaks off easily |
| **Cost** | baseline | **~2× the glue cost** | — |

**Machine details that turn into questions for a supplier:** a closed PUR applicator (*«дай бог в России процентов пять станков»* ⚠️ unsourced, and he sells the machine); **two milling units for 0.8 and 2 mm edges**; ⚠️ **a radius/round unit, which many machines lack**; a polishing unit that removes the dynamic wave; a cleaning unit.

> **⚠️⚠️ THE LIMIT HE VOLUNTEERS AGAINST HIS OWN PITCH:** *«Если вас зальют соседи, и у вас полностью корпус на PUR клею, и фасады тоже — всё равно [плохо], потому что **СТЫКИ не обрабатываются**.»* **A flood defeats every edge technology, because no banding seals the panel-to-panel joints.**
>
> **⚠️ And the market reality: *«90% кухонь всё равно будут кромиться на ЭВА клей, потому что основные станки не оснащены PUR узлами»*. → PUR is a question to ask, not an assumption.**

**⚠️ Edge MATERIAL is a separate variable from the glue: ABS vs PVC.** ABS has better heat resistance, elasticity and fade resistance, **absorbs impact better** (fewer chips when a pan hits the edge), and costs about **4× PVC**. ⚠️ **He also makes a phthalate/health argument against PVC; it is flagged in the source note as loosely stated chemistry from someone who profits from ABS, and is not adopted here.** ⚠️ **A 1 mm edge was measured to protect the corner well against dropped steel balls.**

[source: [[_Sources/YT_-I97gsh-aek_sosnovsk_carcass_material_and_edge_crash_tests|YT_-I97gsh-aek]], [[_Sources/YT_Lfvs1XUz53s_sosnovsk_carcass_construction_and_edge_technology|YT_Lfvs1XUz53s]]]

## ⚠️⚠️ 4. Cut-outs — and a direct disagreement about the fix

**Both fabricators agree on the cause:** cut-outs for the riser, hood duct, gas meter and water stub-outs **must be made on CNC at the factory**, never on site with a jigsaw — *«много сколов и много брака»*. **Independent second arrival.**

**⚠️⚠️ They disagree on the remedy, and this lands on an item the vault flagged as a HEALTH finding.**

| | **CAPPUCCINO** | **Сосновская фабрика** |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The fix** | *«Вырезы должны быть обязательно ЗАКРОМКОВАНЫ»* | ⚠️⚠️ **Banding a cut-out is cosmetic.** *«Эта кромка — чистая эстетика. Она вообще не спасёт от влаги, потому что её клеят на обычный евроклей»* |
| **What he prescribes instead** | — | **СПЕЦПРОПИТКИ — impregnating sealants that close the joint entirely.** *«Оттуда не идёт эмиссия формальдегида»* |
| **Evidence** | asserted | ⚠️ *«Мы даже тестировали — деталь можно положить в воду, ЛДСП настолько прочно запечатывается, даже не разбухает»* — **no method, duration or control stated** |

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ SEALANT, NOT BANDING, IS WHAT HE SAYS STOPS BOTH THE WATER AND THE FORMALDEHYDE ON A SITE CUT-OUT.** **The CAPPUCCINO finding that an unbanded edge raises emission tenfold is not contradicted — the disagreement is about what an on-site remedy can actually achieve.**
>
> **→ OPEN ITEM, and it is a specification sentence either way: ask the fabricator what goes on a site cut-out — banding, impregnation, or both.**

[source: [[_Sources/YT_Lfvs1XUz53s_sosnovsk_carcass_construction_and_edge_technology|YT_Lfvs1XUz53s]]]

## ⚠️⚠️ 5. Carcass ventilation — an appliance-life variable nothing else here covers

> *«Многие ставят решётки в цоколь — и на этом всё. Особенно актуально, если у вас кухня до потолка. Никакого воздухообмена за бытовой техникой не происходит, и поэтому техника РАНЬШЕ ВРЕМЕНИ ВЫХОДИТ ИЗ СТРОЯ, перегревается… не отрабатывает положенный срок эксплуатации.»*

**His design: air in at the bottom, channels milled across the whole back perimeter, exhausting into a dedicated shaft** — *«и дышит здесь и холодильник, и духовка»*.

> **→ ⚠️⚠️ THE CARCASS DESIGN IS AN APPLIANCE-LIFE VARIABLE, and this is live here: a built-in appliance set and a kitchen-to-ceiling question are both open.** **A question to put to any fabricator: how does the equipment breathe?**

[source: [[_Sources/YT_Lfvs1XUz53s_sosnovsk_carcass_construction_and_edge_technology|YT_Lfvs1XUz53s]]]

## ⚠️ 6. Assembled vs glued, and where geometry actually comes from

**A glued carcass costs about 2× an assembled one** and has better initial geometry with no visible fasteners. **He argues against paying for it:**

> *«Классная геометрия будет В ЦЕХЕ. Но когда ты поставишь её на НЕРОВНЫЙ ПОЛ, любой клеёный каркас разъедется, как и сборный. Самое главное в геометрии — это как СБОРЩИК профессионально отрегулирует опоры и всю фурнитуру.»*

- **→ ⚠️⚠️ GEOMETRY IS SET BY THE FITTER'S ADJUSTMENT, NOT BY THE FACTORY** — this project's own out-of-square premise, reached from the manufacturing side, **and it agrees with the nine-year walkthrough's observation that the horizontals were *«идеально выровнены ПОСЛЕ МОНТАЖА»*.**
- **His gold standard: assembled, with professional concealed combined fasteners** (евровинт, minifix, Rafix). ⚠️ **Tall units are never glued in any case, so a "glued" order is in practice a combined product.**
- **⚠️ Rigidity: царги/рёбра прочности of ЛДСП or birch ply around the countertop perimeter**, *«чтобы придать жёсткость и сделать правильную геометрию нижнего каркаса… не позволят ей расшатываться и люфтить»*. **Back panels: ЛДСП where rigidity and appearance matter, HDF where lightness does.** **Services and sockets behind фальш-панели on magnets with access for inspection** — relevant where a gas boiler must be concealed.

[source: [[_Sources/YT_Lfvs1XUz53s_sosnovsk_carcass_construction_and_edge_technology|YT_Lfvs1XUz53s]]]

## Perspectives — carcass material

**⚠️⚠️ The same presenter contradicts himself across four years, and the later video is the one that loses.**

- **2026 (asserted):** ЛДСП after PUR banding resists moisture **better** than MDF, because PUR penetrates the coarser chip and fuses it; ЛДСП is *«вообще база, даже на премиальных гарнитурах»*; MDF carcass is *«в большей степени маркетинг»*.
- **2022 (filmed test):** ЛДСП scored **0/5 on moisture and 0/5 on fastener retention**; MDF with a facade coating passed 24 h intact.

> **→ WHERE THEY DISAGREE, PREFER THE TEST.** ⚠️ **The charitable reading is that the 2026 claim compares ЛДСП against MDF CARCASS board, not against the expensive facade-grade panel that actually passed — and he disqualifies that panel from the carcass category himself.** **→ On his own evidence the real comparison is ЛДСП against PLYWOOD, and plywood wins every criterion he scored.** ⚠️ **Plywood's cost is the reason it stays niche: roughly 5× ЛДСП per m² (ratio only — RUB 2026, rule 2).**
