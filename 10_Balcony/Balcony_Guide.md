# Balcony/Loggia — Glazing, Insulation, and Finishing Guide

General, sourced technique/reasoning for balcony/loggia glazing, structural
work, insulation, heating, and finishing — gathered mostly from Alexey
Zemskov/Zemstandart-Zemsproekt's own extensive glazing series, plus several
Petrishin-Stroi sources. Each section states the leading recommendation(s)
and *why*, so you can form a view without clicking through; the linked
`analysis/` pages carry the full multi-source breakdown, every number, and
every citation.

## Glazing & Structural Work

Full glazing planning (joint tolerances, spec floor, sash-count
minimization, frame alignment to avoid a cold bridge, anchor fixing),
support-masonry construction when raising a parapet, floor-width
reclamation techniques (removing a railing, thinning an interior wall,
relocating a loggia's entry door), and a complete customer-side
acceptance checklist. Also covers the legal risk of relocating a
central-heating radiator onto a loggia (illegal; one company's own
documented inspection-evasion practice) and a real case of client-driven
balcony scope-of-work.

## Insulation & Heating

Insulation conserves heat, it doesn't generate it — pointless without a
real heat source, and water/hydronic radiators are illegal on a
balcony/loggia (electric is the legal alternative). Material comparison
(пеноплекс vs. mineral wool vs. пенофол vs. a PIR board alternative),
thickness rules, a full heated-floor-over-insulation layer stack with a
named failure mode it avoids, a genuine two-source disagreement on
quartz-vinyl over a heated floor, and a real 2025 Moscow cost benchmark
(≈$130/m² for full loggia insulation). **⚠️ A real pyrometer-measured
3-loggia comparison directly bears on this project's own unheated plan**
— insulation + glazing alone, with no active heat source and the loggia
door kept closed, likely runs colder than the comfortable readings this
source shows, since those depended on an open door borrowing room heat;
see the linked page for the full caveat.

applicable to this project's own unheated plan, retained as reference.
