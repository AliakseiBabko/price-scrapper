# Kids Room — Common Mistakes

Part of [[05_Kids_Room/Kids_Room_Guide|Kids Room Guide]].

- **Over-packing a small room's vertical space to fit every function**
  can leave "no air" even when every functional requirement is
  technically met — a real design was explicitly discarded for this
  reason in favor of a more open, slightly less storage-dense layout.
  [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_t8k09YlBbtc_tregubov_room_son_daughter_design_project|YT_t8k09YlBbtc]]]
- **Corner wardrobes in a two-child room** — each triangular section is
  only ~50cm usable, workable for one child, not two; see
  [[05_Kids_Room/analysis/Materials_Furniture_and_Storage|Materials, Furniture, and Storage]].
- **A bed against a cold exterior wall**, and **a glass-paned (even
  frosted) interior door** into a kids' room — the latter because seeing
  movement/shadows pass by a translucent door was flagged as a real,
  if subtle, source of low-level stress for the room's occupant; prefer
  an opaque, painted door. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_t8k09YlBbtc_tregubov_room_son_daughter_design_project|YT_t8k09YlBbtc]]]
- **Long/narrow ("matchbox") room with furniture along both long
  walls** (bed on one, wardrobe on the other) exaggerates the tunnel
  effect — move the wardrobe to a short wall instead if one is
  available. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_lbaZ__wbLWo_atmosfera_svoboda_two_children_room_design|YT_lbaZ__wbLWo]]]
- **Building single-purpose deep toy-storage compartments with no
  future repurposing path** — plan ahead for what else that volume
  could hold later (bedding, a pull-out guest bed) rather than a
  storage design that only works for one age stage. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs_archwood_kids_room_project_zoning_outlets|YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs]]]
- **Assuming a themed bed fits any layout** — most themed beds (house-
  shaped, ship-shaped) are designed for along-the-wall placement only;
  check this before committing. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs_archwood_kids_room_project_zoning_outlets|YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs]]]
- **A loft/bunk-bed ladder instead of a storage staircase** — a
  staircase is the safer option for a child getting up at night (e.g.
  for the bathroom) and doubles as storage, preserving floor area a
  separate storage piece would otherwise use. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs_archwood_kids_room_project_zoning_outlets|YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs]]]
- **Skipping rough-in-stage wall reinforcement for a planned sports
  fixture** (pull-up bar, climbing wall) — coordinate plywood backing
  or chemical anchors with the foreman before the wall is closed up,
  not after. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs_archwood_kids_room_project_zoning_outlets|YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs]]]
- **A storage shelf/unit blocking outlets already planned above a
  future desk** — coordinate storage-unit placement against the
  90cm-height outlet plan from [[05_Kids_Room/analysis/Age_Staged_Planning|Age-Staged Planning]]
  before finalizing either. [source: [[11_Budget_and_Planning/_supporting/knowledge/sources/YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs_archwood_kids_room_project_zoning_outlets|YT_9ykrlQ5tpBs]]]
