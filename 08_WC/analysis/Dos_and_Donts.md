## ⚠️ Minimum WC Dimensions and the Stack-Distance Rule (Татьяна Безверхая, added 2026-09-02)

This folder's thinnest page gains its first dimensional set. Moscow-practice designer, 2024, 23-minute bathroom/WC guide; `promotional_ratio: medium` (one sponsored tile mid-roll, excluded). **The full bathroom block, the servicing-access rules and the RUSSIAN wet-zone limits are on [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Planning_and_Layout|Bathroom: Planning & Layout]]** — repeated here only where WC-specific. [source: [[_Sources/YT_T3b-IS4Rb0E_bezverkhaia_ideal_wc_ten_rules|T3b-IS4Rb0E]]]

- **⚠️ Minimum width of a WC containing only a toilet: 900 mm.** "Уже не делайте — некомфортно." **Where the WC instead sits in a niche between a vanity and a shower partition in a combined bathroom, 800 mm is attemptable because there is more room for shoulders — but no less.** Corroborates the 850–900 mm toilet-between-two-walls figure already on the Bathroom planning page.
- **⚠️ Clear space in front of the WC to a wall, a door or any other element: minimum 550 mm** — "ещё больше полуметра," or sitting is very uncomfortable. **Note this is bowl-front-to-obstruction, a different measurement basis from the ≥1200 mm back-wall-to-usable-path figure on the Bathroom page — do not conflate them.**
- **⚠️ From the soil stack or wall to the front plane of the WC installation: minimum 250 mm; 200 mm for ultra-thin installations, never less.**
- **⚠️ Wall-hung WC bowl projection: 550 mm; compact models 500 mm.** Together with the two figures above, this is what sizes the whole WC depth.
- **⚠️ Site the WC as close to the soil stack as possible — ideally with its back directly against it. Failing that, no more than two 90° bends in the soil pipe, or it will eventually block.** **See [[12_Engineering_and_Systems/analysis/Rough_Plumbing_Sequencing|Rough Plumbing Sequencing]], where this is reconciled against that page's stronger existing two-45°s-never-a-single-90° rule.**
- **⚠️ Forced extract ventilation is mandatory, and the extract must switch from its own separate key — not together with the light and not on a motion sensor**, "иначе она вас дико задолбает."
- **⚠️ Underfloor heating is always required, and in an apartment it can only be electric** (in a house, electric or hydronic). See [[07_Bathroom/analysis/Heated_Floor_and_Thermostat|Heated Floor & Thermostat]].
- **A place for dirty laundry has to be planned** — people remember the washing machine and forget the basket.

**⚠️ No code or standard is cited for any figure — these are her studio's working minima, not norms.** No prices anywhere in the source.
