## Do's

| Rule | Applies To | Reason | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Install access hatch for plumbing collectors | Collector Wall | Allows maintenance of valves and meters | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_vid1_transcript_d04723c5.txt` |
| Leave deliberate clearance between a ventilation duct/box and the adjacent wall (Konstantin Kruglov / Ontario, added 2026-08-28, Round 16) | WC, Bathroom | Reserves space for a future shelf-and-door storage module (e.g. bulk toilet-paper/tissue storage) planned at design stage, rather than being crowded out by the duct once installed | [[_Sources/YT_kHmUYEX1Lqw_kruglov_stylish_2room_apartment_review\|YT_kHmUYEX1Lqw]] |
| Check whether a developer's fully-boxed ventilation shaft/chase can be partially opened up (Nikita Kuznetsov, Ontario, added 2026-08-28, Round 16) | Bathroom, WC | A real project found real usable shelf space by demolishing part of a fully-enclosed developer vent-shaft box, once confirmed structurally safe to open — worth checking before assuming a boxed vent chase is fixed dead space | [[_Sources/YT_xkA8v-0jGqg_kruglov_modern_3room_apartment_review\|YT_xkA8v-0jGqg]] |
| Consider a toilet installation-frame integrated with surrounding furniture/shelving rather than fully tiled-in (Nikita Kuznetsov, Ontario, added 2026-08-28, Round 16) | Bathroom, WC | Recovers usable shelf space around the frame instead of a plain tiled box; a distinct trend from this store's existing upper-storage/lower-installation two-box framing | [[_Sources/YT_xkA8v-0jGqg_kruglov_modern_3room_apartment_review\|YT_xkA8v-0jGqg]] |
| Consider two separate sinks for a couple sharing one bathroom (Nikita Kuznetsov, Ontario, added 2026-08-28, Round 16) | Bathroom | Avoids a morning-routine bottleneck when both partners need the sink at the same time (e.g. both leaving for work) — an adult/couple-use-case parallel to this store's existing two-children-two-basins guidance | [[_Sources/YT_xkA8v-0jGqg_kruglov_modern_3room_apartment_review\|YT_xkA8v-0jGqg]] |
| Install reverse check valves on cold and hot water manifolds | Collector Wall | Prevents cross-pressure backflow between hot and cold supply lines | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_mistakes_video_21ade3f6.txt` |
| Choose wall-hung rimless toilet bowls with concealed installation frame | Toilet Zone | Simplifies floor cleaning and conceals flush mechanism | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_renovation_mistakes_video_21ade3f6.txt` |
| Build a WC to a minimum 1m length from the installation's front face | WC | 80-90cm covers seated use only; standing use needs the extra clearance | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260731_zems_b120_toilet_060b616c.txt` |
| Build a WC to a minimum 90cm post-plaster width (87cm finished) if no sink is present | WC | Accounts for door leaf + casing clearance on both sides | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260731_zems_b120_toilet_060b616c.txt` |
| Consider a wall-mounted urinal where a WC is too small for a second full toilet | Utility WC, Second WC | Needs only ~50cm width and doesn't require proximity to the stack | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260731_zems_b121_toilethack_60df4db3.txt` |
| Build two separate boxes (upper storage, lower installation) around a toilet frame | WC | A single uniform box wastes usable storage space above the frame's own required height | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260731_zems_b147_toiletmistake_33c63821.txt` |
| Move a wall (not the tile grid) in 10mm increments to remove a narrow cut piece | Bathroom, WC, Kitchen | Consumes exactly one grout joint per move; doesn't create a new cut piece in the adjoining room | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260731_zems_b151_tilenoporozhki_538187dc.txt` |

## Don'ts

| Rule | Applies To | Risk | Source |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Do not seal plumbing risers without access hatches | Risers | Prevents emergency repairs | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260727_vid1_transcript_d04723c5.txt` |
| Do not size a WC to seated-knee clearance alone | WC | People also stand to use the toilet; seated-only clearance is too short for comfortable standing use | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260731_zems_b120_toilet_060b616c.txt` |
| Do not specify a recessed toilet-paper holder without confirming wall depth | WC, Bathroom | Needs at least 8cm of chase depth; not always available on a shared wall | `_Archive/processed_sources/20260731_zems_b106_mirror_d60b55af.txt` |
